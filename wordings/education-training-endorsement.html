Education and Training Professional Indemnity Insurance — Endorsement

The Education and Training Professional Indemnity Insurance terms, in the form of an endorsement to the base Professional Indemnity Insurance wording.
Draft v1.0.0-DRAFTDownload .docxIntegrated wordingBase wordingSuggest a change
DRAFT — subject to legal review — not for use. This wording is not settled and must not be issued, quoted from, or bound until Wording Partner sign-off. It is provided as source material under CC BY 4.0, without warranty.

Application

This endorsement attaches to and forms part of the Meridian Professional Indemnity Insurance wording, version 1.4.0-DRAFT (document reference 2026/PI/0001), referred to here as the base wording. It changes the base wording only as set out below. All other terms, conditions, exclusions and limits of the base wording continue to apply.

Words defined in the base wording have the same meaning in this endorsement. Where this endorsement and the base wording conflict, this endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict.

The same terms are published as a single integrated wording, Education and Training Professional Indemnity Insurance, version 1.0.0-DRAFT. Both documents are generated from the same source and have the same effect.

Replaced: Professional services

The definition of Professional services in the base wording is deleted and replaced with the following.

Professional services

The following services provided for a fee, a grant or other funding:

(a) education and training by a school, higher education provider or registered training organisation, including teaching, assessment, the issue of qualifications and statements of attainment, and student support;

(b) tutoring and coaching;

(c) course and curriculum design;

(d) recruiting and advising students as an education agent; and

(e) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

A service in (a) is a professional service only while you hold the registration that the law requires to provide it.

Added: Failure to Educate

The following extension is added to the base wording, after the Subcontractors and Outsourcers extension.

Failure to Educate

We cover you for a claim by a student, or a person paying for a student, that you failed to provide the education, training or assessment that you promised or that the law required, including a claim arising from the cancellation of a qualification or statement of attainment that you issued.

Added: Re-assessment and Re-issue Costs

The following extension is added to the base wording, after the Failure to Educate extension.

Re-assessment and Re-issue Costs

If a regulator finds that your assessment of students did not meet the standards that apply, we will pay the reasonable costs, incurred with our consent, of re-assessing the affected students, providing additional training to them, and re-issuing their qualifications or statements of attainment.

The most we will pay under this extension in the policy period is the sub-limit shown in the schedule, which is part of the limit of liability.

Added: Education Regulators

The following extension is added to the base wording, after the Re-assessment and Re-issue Costs extension.

Education Regulators

We also pay defence costs under the Inquiry Costs extension for an audit, investigation or inquiry into your conduct by an education regulator, including ASQA, TEQSA, the Victorian Registration and Qualifications Authority, the Training Accreditation Council of Western Australia, and a state or territory school registration authority.

This extension does not cover the cost of a routine registration or re-registration audit.

Added: Teachers, Contractors and Volunteers

The following extension is added to the base wording, after the Education Regulators extension.

Teachers, Contractors and Volunteers

A teacher, trainer, assessor or tutor engaged by you as a contractor, and a volunteer acting for you, is an insured person for the professional services they provide on your behalf.

Added: Abuse

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Bodily Injury and Property Damage exclusion.

Abuse

Arising from the sexual, physical or psychological abuse, molestation or harassment of any person, or from any failure to prevent, detect, report or respond to it, whether the claim is for negligence, breach of a non-delegable duty or any other cause of action.

This exposure is designed to be covered under an abuse liability policy.

Added: Fee Refunds

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Trading Debts and Business Losses exclusion.

Fee Refunds

For the refund or re-crediting of tuition fees, student loan amounts or levies, including amounts payable under a tuition protection scheme, except to the extent that a court or tribunal orders them as compensation for your negligence.

Added: Research

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Fee Refunds exclusion.

Research

Arising from research, clinical trials or product development, other than research carried out by students as part of their course.

Education and Training Endorsements

The following endorsements apply to this policy only when the schedule shows that they apply.

Third-Party Delivery Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from training or assessment delivered on your behalf, or under your registration, by a third party that is not your employee, including under an auspicing, partnership or licensing arrangement.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • What percentage of your training or assessment is delivered by third parties under auspicing, partnership or licensing arrangements? qs.pi.et.third_party_delivery_pct

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.et.third_party_delivery_pct > 20: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because {qs.pi.et.third_party_delivery_pct}% of your training is delivered by third parties.

International Students Sub-limit

For all claims arising from services to international students, including services as an education agent, the most we will pay in total, including defence costs, is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement. The sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • What percentage of your students are international students? qs.pi.et.international_pct

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.et.international_pct > 30: Suggest to the underwriter (quote not held)

Underwriter responses (on a referral or suggestion): Attach; Attach with amended sub-limit; Do not attach

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because {qs.pi.et.international_pct}% of your students are international students.

VET Student Loans Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from your participation in the VET Student Loans program, including any requirement to repay or re-credit loan amounts.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Are you an approved VET Student Loans provider? qs.pi.et.vsl_approved

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.et.vsl_approved = yes: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because you are an approved VET Student Loans provider.

High-Risk Practical Training Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from practical training in high-risk work, including work that requires a high-risk work licence, working at heights, confined spaces, driving, aviation, maritime and firearms training.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Do you deliver practical training in high-risk work, such as high-risk work licences, working at heights, confined spaces, driving, aviation, maritime or firearms? qs.pi.et.high_risk_training

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.et.high_risk_training = yes: Apply automatically

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because you deliver high-risk practical training.

Increased Re-assessment Sub-limit

The sub-limit for the Re-assessment and Re-issue Costs extension is increased to the amount shown in the schedule for this endorsement.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • How many students were enrolled with you in the last 12 months? qs.pi.et.enrolments

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.et.enrolments > 1000: Offer as a priced option

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Offered because large enrolments raise the cost of re-assessing students.
