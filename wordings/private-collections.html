Private Collections Insurance

All-risks cover for personal collections of jewellery, watches, fine art, antiques, collectables, wine, musical instruments and other valuables in Australia, at agreed values for specified items.

About This Policy

This policy insures the jewellery, watches, art, antiques, collectables, wine, musical instruments and other valuables that you list in the schedule, for physical loss or damage from any cause that the policy does not exclude.

It is designed for collections that are worth more than a home contents policy will cover. You can hold it alongside your home contents policy, or on its own.

The schedule lists your specified items, each with its agreed value, and the sum insured for each category of your collection. Unspecified items in a category are covered up to the unspecified item limit shown in the schedule.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

Important Information

Who issues this policy. The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

Who this policy is for. This wording is designed for retail clients: individuals, joint owners, and trustees of family trusts and self-managed superannuation funds, insuring property kept for personal use. An issuer using it must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available, as the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) requires. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement. It is not for property held for sale or used in a business.

Your duty when you apply. This policy is a consumer insurance contract under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). You have a duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated. Answer every question in the proposal honestly and completely, including the values you give us for your specified items. If you do not take reasonable care, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

Standard cover. This policy insures valuables only. It is not a home building or home contents policy, and it does not replace one.

[WP REVIEW: confirm that a policy insuring only scheduled valuables, issued separately from a home contents policy, is not a home contents contract prescribed by the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017, so that standard cover, the s.35 notice of departures from standard cover and a Key Facts Sheet do not apply to it.]

Things you should know. The main limits on cover are:

  • unspecified items are covered only up to the unspecified item limit in the schedule, for any one item, and a pair or set counts as one item (see the How We Settle Claims provision and the Pairs and Sets provision);
  • fine art, antiques, collectables, wine and musical instruments are covered in Australia only, unless the E01 Worldwide Cover endorsement applies (see the Where Your Collection Is Covered clause);
  • we do not pay for wear and tear, gradual deterioration, or damage caused by cleaning, repair or restoration (see the Wear and Gradual Causes exclusion and the Work on Items exclusion);
  • we do not pay for theft from an unattended vehicle, breakage of fragile items in transit unless professionally packed, or theft or malicious damage while the premises are unoccupied for longer than the schedule allows (see the Unattended Vehicles exclusion, the Breakage in Transit exclusion and the Unoccupied Premises exclusion);
  • we do not pay for property held for sale or used in a business, or for loss arising from a defect in your title (see the Business Use exclusion and the Title and Confiscation exclusion).

Cooling-off period. You may cancel this policy within 21 days of the date it starts, and within 21 days of each renewal, by telling us in writing. If you have not made a claim, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover. After that, you may still cancel under the Cancellation condition.

Commission and fees. The schedule or the quote shows any commission or fee paid to your broker in connection with this policy.

Financial Claims Scheme. If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.

Schedule

(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

  • Your name, and the name of anyone else insured; for a trust or self-managed superannuation fund, the trustee and the name of the trust or fund
  • The premises, which is the address where your collection is normally kept
  • The policy period, with its start and end dates
  • The total sum insured, and the sum insured for each category
  • Each specified item, with its description, any serial number and its agreed value
  • The unspecified item limit for each category
  • The excess
  • The security at the premises on which the premium is based
  • The name of any vault where items are kept
  • The limits and periods of the extensions, where they differ from the defaults stated in them
  • Any interested party, such as a financier
  • The premium, including stamp duty, any emergency services levy, and GST
  • Any commission or fee paid to your broker
  • The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see the Governing Law condition)
  • The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables

Definitions

In this policy, the words below have these meanings wherever they appear, whatever their capitalisation. The singular includes the plural.

Agreed Value

For a specified item, the value shown for it in the schedule. It is agreed between you and us when the item is first specified, and changes only when we issue a new schedule.

Category

One of the following categories of property. The schedule shows a sum insured for each category you insure.

  • jewellery and watches: jewellery and watches kept at the premises or worn
  • jewellery and watches in a vault: jewellery and watches kept in a vault
  • fine art: paintings, drawings, prints, photographs, works on paper and sculpture
  • antiques and decorative arts: antiques, silver, ceramics, glass, clocks, rugs and furniture of artistic or historic value
  • collectables: coins, banknotes, stamps, medals, memorabilia, rare books and manuscripts
  • wine: wine and spirits kept in a cellar or wine storage unit
  • musical instruments: musical instruments and their cases and accessories
  • other valuables: handbags, luggage, pens, cameras, sunglasses and other personal valuables of high value
Category Sum Insured

The amount shown in the schedule for a category. It includes the agreed value of every specified item in that category.

Collection

Property in each category for which the schedule shows a category sum insured, that you or a member of your household own or are legally responsible for, and that is kept for personal use. It is made up of the specified items and the unspecified items.

Damage

Physical loss of, or physical damage to, property. It includes loss of property by theft, and the disappearance of property in circumstances that cannot be explained.

Endorsement

A written change to this policy that we issue and that is shown in the schedule. The available endorsements are listed in the Endorsement Library.

Event

One occurrence, or a series of occurrences arising from one original cause. All damage caused by the same person or group of people acting together is one event.

Excess

The amount you pay towards a claim, as the Excess provision sets out.

Household

You, your spouse or partner, and your and their children and other relatives who live with you at the premises. It does not include a tenant or boarder who pays you to live at the premises.

Jewellery and Watches

Articles of personal adornment made wholly or partly of precious metal, gemstones or pearls, and watches, including their straps, bracelets and cases.

Pair or Set

Two or more items that belong together and are worth more together than separately, such as a pair of earrings, a set of chairs, or a series of prints by one artist.

Policy Period

The period shown in the schedule, unless this policy is cancelled earlier.

Premises

The address in Australia shown in the schedule where your collection is normally kept.

Professional Valuation

A written valuation by a valuer qualified in the kind of item valued, who is not selling the item to you, that describes the item and states its replacement value at the date of the valuation.

Safe

A locked safe made for storing valuables, bolted to the floor or a wall of the premises, whose key or code is not left in or near it.

Specified Item

An item listed individually in the schedule with its description and agreed value. A pair or set may be listed as one specified item.

Total Sum Insured

The total of the category sums insured, shown in the schedule.

Unoccupied

The premises have not been lived in overnight by you or a member of your household for more than the number of consecutive days shown in the schedule for the Unoccupied Premises exclusion.

Unspecified Item

An item in your collection that is not a specified item.

Vault

A safe deposit box or strongroom at a bank, or at a business whose trade is the secure storage of valuables, named in the schedule.

We, Us, Our

The insurer named in the schedule.

You, Your

The person or people named in the schedule as the insured. Each of them is an insured. Where the schedule names a trustee, it means that trustee only in its capacity as trustee of the trust or fund shown in the schedule.

Your Collection

This section applies to every category for which the schedule shows a category sum insured.

Damage to Your Collection

If any part of your collection suffers damage during the policy period, in a place where the Where Your Collection Is Covered clause says it is covered, we will pay for the damage as set out in the How We Settle Claims provision.

This cover applies to damage from any cause, unless an exclusion in the What We Do Not Cover section applies. The most we pay is set out in The Most We Pay provision.

Where Your Collection Is Covered

Each category is covered in the places shown in this table.

CategoryWhere it is covered
Jewellery and watchesAnywhere in the world
Jewellery and watches in a vaultIn the vault, and anywhere in the world while out of the vault as the E02 Vault Storage endorsement allows
Other valuablesAnywhere in the world
Fine art; antiques and decorative arts; collectables; wine; musical instrumentsAt the premises; at any other address in Australia for up to the number of consecutive days shown in the schedule (default 60); and in transit between them within Australia

Where the E01 Worldwide Cover endorsement applies, every category is covered anywhere in the world.

Newly Acquired Items

If you acquire an item during the policy period that belongs in a category shown in the schedule, it is covered automatically from the time you become responsible for it, for up to the number of days shown in the schedule for this extension (default 60).

The most we pay under this extension for all newly acquired items in a category is the percentage of the category sum insured shown in the schedule (default 25%), and for any one item the amount shown in the schedule (default $25,000). This is in addition to the category sum insured.

To keep the item covered after that time, tell us about it before the time ends and pay any additional premium we ask for. We will then add it to the schedule.

Loss in Value After Repair

If a specified item of fine art, or of antiques and decorative arts, is repaired after damage covered by this policy, and the item is then worth less than its agreed value because of the damage, we also pay the difference between its agreed value and its value after repair.

The value after repair is agreed between you and us. If we cannot agree, it is set by a professional valuation obtained by us at our cost from a valuer you have agreed to, and you may not unreasonably withhold your agreement.

The most we pay for the repair and the loss in value together is the agreed value of the item.

Costs to Protect Your Collection

We pay the reasonable costs you incur to protect your collection from imminent damage covered by this policy, or to protect it from further damage after such damage. This includes the cost of packing, moving and storing items temporarily.

The most we pay for any one event is the amount shown in the schedule for this extension (default $10,000), in addition to the total sum insured.

How We Pay Claims

This applies to every claim under this policy.

How We Settle Claims

Specified items. If a specified item is lost, stolen or damaged beyond economic repair, we pay you its agreed value. With your agreement, we may replace it instead. If it can be repaired economically, we pay the reasonable cost of repair by a repairer or restorer that you and we agree on, and you may not unreasonably withhold your agreement. The Loss in Value After Repair extension may also apply.

Unspecified items. For an unspecified item, we may repair it, replace it with an item of the same kind and quality, or pay you what it would cost to do so. We choose the option that costs least, and we do not deduct for wear, tear or depreciation. The most we pay for any one unspecified item is the unspecified item limit shown in the schedule for its category (default $10,000).

No reduction for underinsurance. We do not reduce a claim because a category sum insured is less than the value of the items in that category. The limits in The Most We Pay provision still apply.

Pairs and Sets

If an item that is part of a pair or set suffers damage, we pay the reduction in the value of the pair or set caused by that damage.

If you prefer, you may give us the undamaged items in the pair or set, and we will then pay the value of the whole pair or set. For a specified item, that value is its agreed value. For unspecified items, the pair or set counts as one item and the unspecified item limit applies to it.

The Most We Pay

For any one event, after deducting the excess, the most we pay is:

  • for a specified item, its agreed value, together with any amount payable under the Loss in Value After Repair extension within the limit that extension sets;
  • for all items in a category, the category sum insured; and
  • for all categories together, the total sum insured.

The amounts payable under the Newly Acquired Items extension and the Costs to Protect Your Collection extension are in addition to these limits, up to the amounts those extensions set.

Recovered Property

If property is recovered after we have paid you for it, tell us. You may choose to buy it back from us for the amount we paid for it, less the reasonable cost of repairing any damage to it. If you do not, it becomes our property.

If property is recovered before we pay you, we pay the reasonable cost of repairing any damage to it, and of returning it to you, instead of its value.

Excess

You pay the excess shown in the schedule for each event. If one event causes damage to more than one item, only one excess is deducted.

GST and Input Tax Credits

Where you are entitled to an input tax credit for the GST on the cost of a repair or replacement, we pay the claim net of that credit. Each sum insured, agreed value and limit in this policy includes GST to the extent you are not entitled to an input tax credit for it.

Other Insurance

If damage covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy, including a home contents policy, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows.

Who We Pay

We pay you, or as you direct. Where an interested party is shown in the schedule, we pay as the E10 Interested Party endorsement requires. We may pay a repairer, restorer or supplier directly.

What We Do Not Cover

These exclusions apply to every category. Each one is subject to the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition.

Wear and Gradual Causes

We do not pay for damage caused by wear and tear, gradual deterioration, fading, rust, corrosion, mould, or a defect or quality in the item itself that causes it to deteriorate, or by moths, insects or vermin.

We do pay for the loss of a gemstone from its setting because the setting has worn, if the setting was checked by a jeweller within the number of months shown in the schedule (default 24) before the loss.

Work on Items

We do not pay for damage caused by cleaning, repairing, restoring, altering, framing or reframing an item, or by any other work on it. We do pay for damage from a fire or theft that happens while the work is being done.

Mechanical and Electrical Breakdown

We do not pay for the mechanical or electrical breakdown or failure of an item, including the overwinding of a watch or clock. We do pay for damage caused to the item by an external event, such as the item being dropped.

Musical Instrument Parts

We do not pay for the breaking of strings, reeds, skins or bow hair, or for scratching, chipping or denting of a musical instrument while it is being played, unless the instrument is also damaged in another way at the same time.

Breakage in Transit

We do not pay for breakage of glass, ceramics, sculpture or any other fragile item while it is being moved, unless it was packed and moved by a professional fine art packer or carrier, or carried by hand by you or a member of your household.

Unattended Vehicles

We do not pay for theft of jewellery and watches or other valuables from a vehicle while neither you nor a member of your household is in the vehicle or close enough to see it.

Temperature and Humidity

We do not pay for damage caused by changes in temperature or humidity, unless the change was itself caused by a fire, or by sudden damage to the premises. Wine spoiled by a failure of cellar temperature control is covered only where the E04 Wine Temperature Failure endorsement applies.

Unoccupied Premises

We do not pay for theft or malicious damage at the premises while the premises are unoccupied for more than the number of consecutive days shown in the schedule (default 60), unless the E07 Unoccupied Premises endorsement applies. This exclusion does not apply to items in a vault.

Business Use

We do not pay for damage to property while it is held for sale, offered for sale, held on consignment, or used in a business or profession. This exclusion does not apply to a musical instrument used professionally where the E05 Professional Use of Instruments endorsement applies.

Title and Confiscation

We do not pay for any loss arising from a defect in your title to an item, or from a claim to the item by a person who says they own it, or from the confiscation, seizure or destruction of an item by order of a government, customs or other public authority. We do not cover an item that was imported or exported illegally.

Deliberate Acts and Household Theft

We do not pay for damage caused deliberately by you, by a member of your household, or by a person acting with your authority, or for theft by a member of your household or by a person living at the premises. The Joint Insureds condition applies.

Electronic Data

We do not pay for loss of, damage to or corruption of electronic data, including digital art and digital assets, or for loss arising from unauthorised use of a computer system. Unauthorised use means use of a computer system by a person not permitted by its owner to use it. We do pay for physical damage to an item of your collection caused by a resulting fire or explosion.

War

We do not pay for any loss, damage or cost arising from war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities or warlike operations (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, or military or usurped power.

Nuclear

We do not pay for any loss, damage or cost arising from ionising radiation, contamination by radioactivity from nuclear fuel or waste, or nuclear weapons material.

Terrorism

We do not pay for any loss, damage or cost arising from an act of terrorism, meaning an act, including the use of force or violence or the threat of it, by any person or group, committed for political, religious, ideological or similar purposes, including the intention to influence a government or to put the public in fear. This exclusion does not apply to the extent the Terrorism Insurance Act 2003 (Cth) requires cover to be provided.

What You Must Do

If you do not do something this section asks, the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition applies: we may reduce a claim only by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced.

Taking Care of Your Collection

Take reasonable care to prevent damage to your collection. Keep the security shown in the schedule in working order, and switch on any alarm when the premises are empty.

Records and Valuations

Keep a professional valuation, or a receipt from the seller, for each specified item whose agreed value is equal to or more than the amount shown in the schedule (default $10,000), and a photograph of each specified item. Give them to us if we ask.

We recommend a new professional valuation at least every three years. If you give us one, we will update the agreed value when we next issue a schedule, and adjust the premium for the rest of the policy period.

Telling Us About Changes

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable if:

  • the premises change;
  • security shown in the schedule is removed, or is out of action for more than 7 days;
  • you sell or give away a specified item, so that we can remove it from the schedule and refund the premium for it;
  • an item is to be lent to a gallery, museum or exhibition; or
  • an item kept in a vault is to be kept elsewhere.

Telling Us About Claims

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after damage that may lead to a claim. Tell the police promptly about any theft, burglary or malicious damage, and give us the report number.

Give us the information and documents we reasonably ask for to assess the claim, including proof of ownership and value. Keep a damaged item, and do not have it repaired, until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

Recovery Rights

After we pay a claim, we may take over your rights to recover the amount from anyone else, and act in your name. Help us do so, at our cost. We will not exercise a right of recovery against a member of your household, or a guest, unless they caused the loss deliberately.

General Conditions

Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth)

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies the operation of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Where this policy is inconsistent with the Act, the Act prevails.

If this policy would otherwise let us refuse to pay a claim, in whole or in part, because of something you did or did not do after this policy was entered into, section 54 of the Act applies. We may not refuse to pay the claim only because of that act or omission. Our liability is reduced by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced. Where the act or omission could reasonably be regarded as capable of causing or contributing to the loss, we may refuse to pay only the part of the claim that the act or omission caused or contributed to. We may not refuse any part that you prove the act or omission did not cause. This applies to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation in this policy.

Agreed Values

We will not dispute the agreed value of a specified item when you make a claim, except where the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows because of a misrepresentation or fraud when the value was agreed.

Joint Insureds

Where more than one person is named in the schedule as the insured, this policy applies to each of them separately.

A fraud, deliberate act or failure to comply with this policy by one of them, or by a member of your household who is not named as an insured, does not affect the cover of any named insured who did not know of it, did not take part in it and did not consent to it.

Cancellation

You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. If you cancel within the cooling-off period in the Important Information section, we refund the premium as that section states. Otherwise we refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. If we have paid a total loss of a specified item, no refund applies to the premium for that item.

We may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you written notice as that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis. Renewal

Fraud

If a claim is made fraudulently, we may refuse to pay it, as s.56 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

  • the trade or economic sanctions laws of the United Kingdom, the United States of America or the European Union, to the extent they apply to us.

Privacy

Disputes and Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, or we have not responded within 30 calendar days, you may take the complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), a free and independent dispute resolution service: www.afca.org.au, 1800 931 678, info@afca.org.au.

Governing Law

This policy is governed by the law of the Australian state or territory in which the premises are located, as shown in the schedule at the start of the policy period.

Jurisdiction

Headings and References

Headings are for convenience only. A reference to legislation includes any legislation that amends or replaces it. A reference to a clause by its heading is a reference to that clause as it appears in this wording.

How to Make a Claim

  1. If an item is stolen, or deliberately damaged, tell the police as soon as you can and get the report number.
  2. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
  3. Tell us what happened, when and where, and which items are affected.
  4. Keep any damaged item, and do not have it repaired or restored, until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.
  5. Give us the valuation, receipt and photograph for each item you claim for.
  6. We will acknowledge your claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision as the General Insurance Code of Practice requires where the insurer subscribes to it.

E04 Wine Temperature Failure

We cover wine in your collection that is spoiled by a sudden failure of the temperature or humidity control of the cellar or wine storage unit at the premises, despite the Temperature and Humidity exclusion.

We do not pay for spoilage caused by a failure that you knew of and did not take reasonable steps to fix, or that lasted less than the number of consecutive hours shown in the schedule for this endorsement. The most we pay for any one event is the amount shown in the schedule for this endorsement.

Schedule variables: minimum hours of failure (default 24); limit any one event (default 25% of the wine category sum insured).

The musical instruments shown in the schedule for this endorsement are covered while used to earn an income, including at performances, rehearsals, recordings and lessons, and while in transit to and from them. The Business Use exclusion does not apply to them.

E07 Unoccupied Premises
