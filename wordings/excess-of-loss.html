Excess of Loss Insurance

Follow-form excess of loss cover for Australian businesses, above a named primary policy, with no automatic drop-down.

About This Policy

This is an excess of loss policy. It covers loss above the limits of the underlying insurance shown in the schedule, up to the limit of liability of this policy. It is not a standalone policy: it adopts the terms of the followed policy, except where this policy says otherwise.

This policy does not drop down. If the underlying insurance does not pay, for any reason, this policy still responds only above the attachment point. Drop-down cover for the insolvency of an underlying insurer, or for exhaustion of an underlying aggregate limit, is available only by endorsement.

This policy, together with your schedule and any endorsements shown in it, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read it together with the followed policy and the other underlying insurance, and tell us if anything in the schedule is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

This wording is written for businesses. The insurer or distributor that issues it is responsible for deciding whether any purchaser is a retail client under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) and, if so, for issuing a Product Disclosure Statement and Target Market Determination. Definitions

These words have the meanings below wherever they appear in this policy, the schedule or an endorsement. The singular includes the plural and the plural includes the singular. A word defined in the followed policy and not defined here has the meaning it has in the followed policy.

Attachment Point

The amount shown in the schedule as the attachment point. It is the total of the limits of the underlying insurance that apply to a loss, plus any gap excess shown in the schedule. It is fixed at the start of the policy period and does not change if any underlying insurance is reduced, cancelled, not renewed or not paid. The Attachment and Exhaustion clause says when it is reached.

Claim

Has the meaning given to it in the followed policy. Where the followed policy responds to a loss rather than a claim, a reference in this policy to a claim means that loss.

Defence Costs

Has the meaning given to it in the followed policy, or the nearest equivalent term in the followed policy for the costs of investigating, defending or settling a claim.

Endorsement

A clause in the endorsement library at the end of this policy whose code is shown in the schedule, read with the variables shown for it in the schedule.

Followed Policy

The policy named in the schedule as the followed policy, on the wording, version and terms in force at the start of the policy period. It is usually the primary policy. A renewal, replacement or change to it binds us only as the Changes to the Followed Policy clause states.

Gap Excess

Any amount shown in the schedule as a gap excess. It sits between the top of the underlying insurance and the attachment point, and you bear it.

Limit of Liability

The amount shown in the schedule as the limit of liability of this policy. Where this layer is shared between insurers, our limit of liability is our share of it.

Loss

Has the meaning given to it in the followed policy, including defence costs where the followed policy treats defence costs as part of loss.

Policy Period

The period shown in the schedule, unless this policy is cancelled earlier.

Retroactive Date

Where the followed policy is written on a claims-made basis, the retroactive date of the followed policy, unless the schedule shows a different retroactive date for this policy. Where the schedule shows a later date, this policy does not cover any claim arising from an act, error, omission or matter that first happened before that later date, even if the followed policy covers it.

Underlying Insurance

The followed policy and every other policy shown in the schedule as underlying insurance, each at the limit, attachment and terms shown in the schedule at the start of the policy period.

Underlying Insurer

An insurer that issued any of the underlying insurance.

Underlying Limit

For each policy of underlying insurance, its limit shown in the schedule. Where a policy of underlying insurance has both a limit for any one claim and an aggregate limit, the underlying limit is the limit that applies to the loss.

We, Us, Our

The insurer or insurers named in the schedule as insurers of this policy, each for its own share.

You, Your

Every person or entity that is an insured under the followed policy, including the named insured shown in the schedule.

What Is Covered

Follow-form Excess Layer

We will pay loss that is covered by the followed policy, to the extent that it exceeds the attachment point, up to the limit of liability.

Except where this policy states otherwise, this policy adopts the terms of the followed policy as they are at the start of the policy period, including its insuring clauses, definitions, exclusions, conditions, claims trigger, geographical limits and jurisdiction limits, subject to the definition of Retroactive Date. Where the followed policy has a sub-limit for a particular loss, this policy does not cover that loss unless the schedule shows that this policy sits excess of that sub-limit.

Where this policy and the followed policy differ, this policy applies. Where a term of the followed policy cannot sensibly apply to an excess layer (for example a premium, excess, notification address or limit), the corresponding term of this policy applies instead.

Attachment and Exhaustion

This policy responds to a loss only once the attachment point has been reached for that loss.

The attachment point is reached when the full underlying limit of each policy of underlying insurance that applies to the loss, and any gap excess, has been paid by any one or more of:

  • the underlying insurers;
  • you;
  • any other insurer, or any other person, paying on your behalf.

A payment counts towards the attachment point only if it is a payment of loss that would be covered by the underlying insurance under which it is counted, disregarding any deductible, excess or retention under that underlying insurance. It does not matter whether the payment was made by way of judgment, award or settlement, or in what order the payments were made.

A settlement for less than an underlying limit does not prevent the attachment point being reached, as long as the difference is paid by you or on your behalf. We are not bound by the amount or terms of any settlement between you and an underlying insurer for the purpose of deciding whether the loss is covered under this policy.

No Drop-down

This policy does not drop down to replace any underlying insurance, and does not pay any part of a loss below the attachment point. That applies whatever the reason the underlying insurance does not pay, including:

  • the insolvency, administration or liquidation of an underlying insurer, or its inability to pay;
  • the reduction or exhaustion of an aggregate underlying limit by other losses;
  • the cancellation, non-renewal, expiry, avoidance or reduction of any underlying insurance;
  • a refusal by an underlying insurer to pay all or part of a loss, for any reason.

In each of these cases you bear the amount below the attachment point that the underlying insurance does not pay. This clause does not apply to the extent that the E03 Drop-down for Underlying Aggregate Exhaustion endorsement or the E09 Drop-down for Underlying Insurer Insolvency endorsement applies.

Narrower Followed Policy

This policy covers only loss that the followed policy covers. Where any other underlying insurance is broader than the followed policy, this policy does not follow the broader terms. Where any other underlying insurance is narrower than the followed policy, a loss falling within the gap reaches the attachment point only if the underlying limit of the narrower policy is paid by you or on your behalf, as the Attachment and Exhaustion clause provides.

This clause does not apply to the differences in cover specified in the E01 Difference in Conditions endorsement where that endorsement applies.

Changes to the Followed Policy

This policy follows the followed policy as it is at the start of the policy period. A change to the followed policy or any other underlying insurance during the policy period, including an endorsement, a change of wording or version, or a change of limit, excess or retroactive date, binds us only if we agree to it in writing.

If the followed policy is renewed or replaced during the policy period on terms that are no wider than, and a limit that is not less than, the terms and limit shown in the schedule, this policy follows the renewal or replacement on the terms shown in the schedule.

Defence Costs

Defence costs are treated under this policy as they are under the followed policy. Where the followed policy pays defence costs within its limit, defence costs paid by us are within, and reduce, the limit of liability, unless the E04 Defence Costs in Addition to the Limit endorsement applies. Where the followed policy pays defence costs in addition to its limit, we pay defence costs in the proportion that our payment of loss bears to the total loss, in addition to the limit of liability.

We will pay defence costs as they are incurred once the attachment point has been reached. Our consent to defence costs is needed only where the followed policy requires the consent of the primary insurer, and we will not unreasonably withhold or delay it.

What Is Not Covered

Every exclusion in the followed policy applies to this policy. This policy also has the exclusions below, which apply whether or not the followed policy has a corresponding exclusion.

Amounts Below the Attachment Point

Any part of a loss that falls below the attachment point, including any deductible, excess or retention under any underlying insurance, any gap excess, and any amount that an underlying insurer does not pay.

Fines and Penalties

Any fine, penalty, punitive, exemplary or aggravated damages, or civil pecuniary penalty, unless the followed policy covers it and it is insurable under the law that governs this policy. We do not cover any amount that it is unlawful for us to pay, including a penalty that statute prohibits a person from insuring.

War, Terrorism and Nuclear

Any loss arising directly or indirectly from:

  • war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities or warlike operations (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, or military or usurped power;
  • an act of terrorism, meaning an act, or the threat of an act, that involves the use of force or violence, or harm to life, property or infrastructure, by any person or group, done or threatened for political, religious, ideological or similar purposes, including to influence a government or to put the public or any section of it in fear; or
  • ionising radiation or contamination by radioactivity from any nuclear fuel or nuclear waste, or the radioactive, toxic, explosive or other hazardous properties of any nuclear assembly or component.

The act of terrorism part of this exclusion does not apply to the extent that the followed policy covers loss arising from an act of terrorism and the schedule shows that this policy follows that cover.

How the Exclusions Apply

Where the followed policy has a write-back, carve-back or sub-limit for a matter excluded above, that write-back, carve-back or sub-limit does not extend this policy unless the schedule shows that this policy sits excess of it. The conduct of one insured is not imputed to another insured for the purpose of these exclusions, except as the followed policy provides.

How Much We Will Pay

Limit of Liability

The most we will pay for all loss under this policy during the policy period, including under any endorsement, is the limit of liability, unless the E02 Reinstatement of the Limit of Liability endorsement applies. Where the schedule also shows a limit for any one claim, the most we will pay for any one claim is that limit, and the limit of liability still applies to all claims together.

Claims that the followed policy treats as one claim, or as related, are one claim under this policy, subject to the limit of liability and the policy period that the followed policy assigns to them.

Shared Layer

Where this layer is shared between insurers, each insurer is liable only for its own share of each loss within the layer, and of the limit of liability, as shown in the schedule. We are not liable for the share of any other insurer, including if that insurer is insolvent or does not pay.

Order of Payment

This policy is liable only after the attachment point has been reached, and then only for the part of a loss above it, up to the limit of liability. Any insurance above this policy responds only after the limit of liability has been paid. Where an underlying limit is restored or reinstated after a loss, the attachment point for later losses includes the restored or reinstated amount.

Other Insurance

This policy is written as a true excess layer above the underlying insurance. It is excess of, and does not contribute with, the underlying insurance. Where another policy that is not shown in the schedule covers the same loss in the same layer, we will contribute to that loss with the other insurer in the proportion that our limit of liability bears to the total limits for that layer, and nothing in this provision limits s.45 or s.76 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).

Recoveries

Any amount recovered from a third party in respect of a loss paid under this policy and the underlying insurance, after deducting the reasonable costs of the recovery, is applied first to the layer above this policy, if any, then to this policy, then to the underlying insurance, in that order, and lastly to you. Each layer receives no more than it paid. This provision does not apply to the extent that a different order is required by law.

Your Obligations

This section sets out what you must do. The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition limits what we may do if you do not comply.

Maintain the Underlying Insurance

You must keep the underlying insurance in force throughout the policy period, at the limits and on the terms shown in the schedule, except for the reduction or exhaustion of an aggregate underlying limit by payment of loss.

If you do not, this policy still applies as if the underlying insurance had been kept in force, and the attachment point does not change. We are not liable for any loss that the underlying insurance would have paid had it been kept in force.

Tell Us About Claims

You must give us notice of any claim, and of any circumstance, within the time and in the manner required by the followed policy. A notice given to the insurer of the followed policy under its terms is notice to us, as long as we also receive a copy within a reasonable time.

You must also tell us as soon as reasonably practicable when you become aware that a loss, including reserves for defence costs, is likely to exceed half of the attachment point, or that an underlying limit is likely to be paid in full. Notification of circumstances that may give rise to a claim has the effect provided in s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).

Cooperate With Us

You must give us the information, documents and help that we reasonably ask for about any claim that may involve this policy. We may take part, at our own cost, in the investigation, defence and settlement of any such claim, and you must keep us informed of significant developments in it.

Settlement Consent

You must not settle a claim, or agree to any judgment, for an amount that is likely to involve this policy, without our written consent. We will not unreasonably withhold or delay our consent.

Tell Us About Changes

You must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable if any underlying insurance is cancelled, not renewed, changed, or reduced other than by payment of loss, or if an underlying insurer becomes insolvent or enters administration or liquidation.

How Excess Layer Cover Works

This guide explains this policy. It does not change its terms.

An excess layer sits above other insurance. Each layer pays only after the layers below it have been paid in full. For example, where the followed policy has a limit of $5 million and this policy has a limit of liability of $10 million in excess of $5 million:

  • for a loss of $8 million, the primary layer pays its full $5 million (less your primary excess, which you pay), and this policy pays $3 million;
  • for a loss of $20 million, the primary layer pays $5 million, this policy pays $10 million, and the remaining $5 million is yours unless there is a further excess layer.

If the primary insurer agrees to settle for $4 million and you pay the other $1 million, the $5 million primary layer is treated as paid in full and this policy pays from that point.

If the primary insurer cannot pay, for example because it is insolvent, this policy does not move down to replace it unless your schedule shows a drop-down endorsement.

E09 Drop-down for Underlying Insurer Insolvency

Where an underlying insurer named in the schedule for this endorsement becomes subject to an insolvency event during the policy period and, solely because of that event, does not pay loss that its policy covers, we will treat its unpaid share of its underlying limit as paid for the purpose of the Attachment and Exhaustion clause, and pay that loss as if this policy attached at that point, up to the drop-down sub-limit shown in the schedule, which is part of and not in addition to the limit of liability.

An insolvency event means the appointment of a liquidator, provisional liquidator, administrator, judicial manager or receiver to the underlying insurer, or the underlying insurer becoming unable to pay its debts as they fall due, or the equivalent under the law of its place of incorporation.

Once we pay, we are subrogated to your rights against the underlying insurer and its estate, including under the Financial Claims Scheme, to the extent of our payment.

Schedule variables: the underlying insurers to which it applies; the drop-down sub-limit.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)
