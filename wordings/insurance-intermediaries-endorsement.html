Insurance Intermediaries Professional Indemnity Insurance — Endorsement

The Insurance Intermediaries Professional Indemnity Insurance terms, in the form of an endorsement to the base Financial Services Professional Indemnity Insurance wording.
Draft v1.1.0-DRAFTDownload .docxIntegrated wordingBase wordingSuggest a change
DRAFT — subject to legal review — not for use. This wording is not settled and must not be issued, quoted from, or bound until Wording Partner sign-off. It is provided as source material under CC BY 4.0, without warranty.

Application

This endorsement attaches to and forms part of the Meridian Financial Services Professional Indemnity Insurance wording, version 1.1.0-DRAFT (document reference 2026/FS/0001), referred to here as the base wording. It changes the base wording only as set out below. All other terms, conditions, exclusions and limits of the base wording continue to apply.

Words defined in the base wording have the same meaning in this endorsement. Where this endorsement and the base wording conflict, this endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict.

The same terms are published as a single integrated wording, Insurance Intermediaries Professional Indemnity Insurance, version 1.1.0-DRAFT. Both documents are generated from the same source and have the same effect.

Replaced: Professional services

The definition of Professional services in the base wording is deleted and replaced with the following.

Professional services

The following services, to the extent that you are authorised to provide them under the Australian financial services licence shown in the schedule, or as an authorised representative of the licensee shown in the schedule:

(a) arranging general insurance and life insurance, and advising on it, as an insurance broker or agent;

(b) acting under a binding authority or binder from an insurer, including as an underwriting agency;

(c) handling and settling insurance claims on behalf of an insurer or an insured; and

(d) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

Added: Premium and Claims Money

The following extension is added to the base wording, after the AFCA Determinations extension.

Premium and Claims Money

The cover for fraud or dishonesty of your employees and representatives under the Claims Against You clause includes their misappropriation of premium, claims money or other money that you hold for a client or an insurer, where you are liable for it to a third party who did not take part in the conduct.

We also pay your own loss of that money where an employee caused it, as the First-Party Dishonesty extension provides. That extension covers employees only, so it does not pay your own loss caused by a representative who is not an employee.

Added: Insurer Insolvency

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Investment Performance exclusion.

Insurer Insolvency

Arising from the insolvency of an insurer, reinsurer, underwriting agency or premium funder with which you placed or arranged business.

This exclusion does not apply to a claim that you were negligent, or breached your duty, in choosing or monitoring the insurer, reinsurer, underwriting agency or premium funder, even if it later became insolvent.

[WP REVIEW: confirm that carving back negligent selection of an insurer is consistent with RG 126 and the intended scope of this exclusion.]

Insurance Intermediaries Endorsements

The following endorsements apply to this policy only when the schedule shows that they apply.

An endorsement does not apply to the extent that a law, or a requirement of ASIC, requiring you to hold this insurance does not permit the cover it removes to be excluded or limited.

Insurer Insolvency Buy-back

We will not apply the Insurer Insolvency exclusion where, when you placed the business, the insurer or reinsurer was authorised by APRA, or held a financial strength rating of at least A- from S&P Global Ratings or an equivalent rating from another recognised agency.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • In the last 12 months, did you place any business with an insurer that was not authorised by APRA and not rated at least A- (or equivalent)? qs.fs.ii.unrated_placements

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.fs.ii.unrated_placements = no: Offer as a priced option
  • When qs.fs.ii.unrated_placements = yes: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Offered because you place business with insurers and want cover if one becomes insolvent.

Exceeding Authority Sub-limit

For all claims arising from a claim by an insurer that you exceeded the authority given to you under a binding authority or binder, the most we will pay in total, including defence costs, is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement. The sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Do you write business under a binding authority or binder from an insurer? qs.fs.ii.binders_held
  • What gross written premium did you write under binding authorities or binders in the last 12 months, and what are your claims settlement authority limits? qs.fs.ii.binder_gwp

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.fs.ii.binders_held = yes: Apply automatically

Underwriter responses (on a referral or suggestion): Attach; Attach with amended sub-limit; Attach with special excess; Do not attach

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because you write business under a binding authority or binder.

Life Risk Insurance Advice Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from personal advice to a retail client about a life risk insurance product.

[WP REVIEW: confirm that excluding personal advice to retail clients on life risk insurance is a deliberate trade-off. It removes core regulated advice, and the endorsements preamble saves only the cover the law requires.]

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Do you give personal advice to retail clients on life risk insurance products? qs.fs.ii.life_risk_advice

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.fs.ii.life_risk_advice = yes: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because you give personal advice on life risk insurance.

Premium Funding

Arranging premium funding for your clients, including as an introducer or agent of a premium funder, is a professional service under this policy, to the extent that you are authorised to provide it under your licence or any credit licence or exemption that applies.

This endorsement does not affect the Insurer Insolvency exclusion, which continues to apply to the insolvency of a premium funder.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Do you arrange premium funding for clients? qs.fs.ii.premium_funding

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.fs.ii.premium_funding = yes: Offer as a priced option

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Offered because you arrange premium funding for clients.

Strata Insurance Remuneration Exclusion

We do not cover any claim for the repayment or refund of commission, fees or other remuneration that you or a related party received for arranging strata insurance, or any claim alleging that such remuneration was not disclosed or was excessive.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • What percentage of the premium you placed in the last 12 months was for strata insurance? qs.fs.ii.strata_gwp_pct

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.fs.ii.strata_gwp_pct > 20: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because {qs.fs.ii.strata_gwp_pct}% of your premium comes from strata insurance.
