Public and Products Liability Insurance

Broadform public and products liability cover for Australian businesses, written on an occurrence basis.

About This Policy

This is a broadform liability policy. It covers your legal liability to pay compensation when someone other than an employee is injured, or their property is damaged, because of your business or your products. It has two covers: the Public Liability clause and the Products Liability clause.

Both covers respond on an occurrence basis. What matters is when the injury or damage happens, not when the claim is made against you. A claim made years after the policy period ends is still covered if the injury or damage happened during the policy period.

This policy, together with your schedule and any endorsements shown in it, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

This wording is written for businesses. The insurer or distributor that issues it is responsible for deciding whether any purchaser is a retail client under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) and, if so, for issuing a Product Disclosure Statement and Target Market Determination. Schedule

(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

  • The named insured, ABN, and any subsidiaries named
  • The business, and its ANZSIC class or classes
  • The policy period, start and end dates
  • The limit of liability for public liability (any one occurrence) and the products aggregate limit for products liability
  • Sub-limits that differ from the defaults stated in the Extensions of Cover section
  • The excess for each cover, any worker-to-worker excess, and any special excess (for example for hot works or property in your care)
  • The geographical limits, if different from the default in the definition of Geographical Limits
  • The maximum watercraft length, if different from the default in the Aircraft and Watercraft exclusion
  • The rating basis and the declared figures (turnover, wages and products turnover) used for the Premium Adjustment condition
  • The premium, the deposit and minimum premium, and whether the premium is adjustable, including stamp duty and GST
  • The state or territory whose law governs this policy (see the Governing Law condition)
  • The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables (sub-limits, excesses, named parties, countries, retroactive dates)
  • Interested parties and principals to be noted

Definitions

These words have the meanings below wherever they appear in this policy, the schedule or an endorsement. The singular includes the plural and the plural includes the singular.

Abuse

Any actual, alleged or threatened sexual abuse, sexual harassment, sexual misconduct, molestation, grooming, physical abuse, or psychological or emotional abuse of any person, and any neglect of a person in your care that is connected with any of those acts.

Advertising Injury

Injury arising from defamation, infringement of copyright, title or slogan, misappropriation of advertising ideas or style of doing business, or invasion of privacy, committed in the course of advertising your own goods, services or business.

Business

The business described in the schedule. It includes: owning, occupying and maintaining the premises; activities incidental to the business, such as trade fairs, exhibitions, conferences, sponsorships and charitable activities; providing first aid, fire-fighting and emergency services; private work done by an employee for a director or officer with your consent; and social, sporting and welfare activities you organise for employees and their families.

Claim

A written or oral demand for compensation, or any proceeding (including a writ, summons, cross-claim, arbitration or other dispute resolution process) in which compensation is sought from you.

Communicable Disease

Any disease, illness or condition that can be transmitted from one person, animal or thing to a person by any means, including by a virus, bacterium, parasite, fungus or other organism, or any variation of one. It includes COVID-19 and any listed human disease under the Biosecurity Act 2015 (Cth).

Compensation

Money you are legally liable to pay to a claimant as damages, under a judgment, an award or a settlement we have agreed to, including the claimant's costs and expenses awarded against you.

Defence Costs

Reasonable legal costs and expenses incurred by us, or by you with our written consent, in investigating, defending or settling a claim. We will not unreasonably withhold or delay consent.

Employee

A person employed by you under a contract of service or apprenticeship, whether full-time, part-time, fixed-term, casual or seasonal. It also includes a person who is a worker of yours, or is deemed to be your worker, under any workers compensation law. It does not include a labour hire worker, a voluntary worker, a work experience student, an independent contractor or the employee of an independent contractor.

Endorsement

A written change to this policy that we issue and that is shown in the schedule. The available endorsements are listed in the Endorsement Library.

Excess

The amount shown in the schedule that you bear as the first part of each occurrence. The schedule may show a different excess for each cover, a worker-to-worker excess, and special excesses.

Geographical Limits

For public liability, anywhere in the world except the USA and Canada, but outside Australia and New Zealand only for business visits by your directors, officers and employees who do no manual work while there. For products liability, anywhere in the world except the USA and Canada. The schedule or an endorsement may change these limits.

Labour Hire Worker

A person supplied to you by a labour hire business or other third party to work in your business under your direction, who is not your employee.

Limit of Liability

The amount shown in the schedule as the most we will pay for compensation for any one occurrence under the Public Liability clause, and in total for all occurrences in the policy period under the Products Liability clause (the products aggregate).

Named Insured

The person or entity named in the schedule as the insured.

Occurrence

An event, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general conditions, that results in personal injury or property damage that you did not expect or intend. All personal injury and property damage attributable to one source or original cause is one occurrence, whenever it happens and however many people or properties are affected.

Personal Injury
  • bodily injury, sickness, disease, disability or death;
  • mental injury, nervous shock, mental anguish or emotional distress, whether or not it results from bodily injury;
  • false arrest, wrongful detention, false imprisonment or malicious prosecution;
  • wrongful entry into, wrongful eviction from, or other invasion of the right of private occupancy;
  • assault and battery not committed by you or at your direction, unless committed to protect people or property; and
  • advertising injury.
Policy Period

The period shown in the schedule.

Pollutant

Any solid, liquid, gaseous, biological, radiological or thermal irritant or contaminant, including smoke, vapour, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, waste, noise, vibration and electromagnetic fields. Waste includes material to be recycled, reconditioned or reclaimed.

Premises

Any land, building or structure that you own, lease, rent or occupy for the business, and any site where you are working temporarily for the business.

Products

Anything (including its packaging, container, labelling, instructions, warnings, design, formula or specification) that you or anyone acting for you or under your licence has manufactured, grown, extracted, produced, processed, assembled, constructed, erected, installed, repaired, serviced, treated, sold, supplied, distributed, imported or exported, after it has left your physical possession or control. It includes anything for which you are treated by law as the manufacturer, including as an importer under the Australian Consumer Law. It does not include land or buildings.

Property Damage

Physical loss of, damage to, or destruction of tangible property, including the loss of use of that property. It also includes loss of use of tangible property that is not physically lost, damaged or destroyed, if the loss of use is caused by an occurrence.

Retroactive Date

The date shown in the schedule as the retroactive date for an endorsement that is written on a claims-made basis (Endorsements E05, E17 and E19). That endorsement does not cover anything that happened, or products supplied, before that date.

Subsidiary

An entity that is a subsidiary of the named insured under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) at the start of the policy period, or that becomes one under the Newly Acquired Subsidiaries extension.

USA and Canada

The United States of America and Canada, and any territory or possession under the jurisdiction of either country.

Voluntary Worker

A person who works in your business without payment other than reimbursement of expenses, including a work experience student and a person doing community service work.

We, Us, Our

The insurer or insurers named in the schedule as the insurer. Where there is more than one, each is liable only for its own share, as set out in the Several Liability condition.

Worker-to-worker Claim

A claim for personal injury to a labour hire worker, or to the employee of a contractor or subcontractor of yours, where the injury arises from the business while that person is working on premises or a site where you are working.

You, Your
  • the named insured and its subsidiaries;
  • any past, present or future director, officer, partner or principal of an entity in the first point, while acting in that capacity;
  • any employee, labour hire worker or voluntary worker, while acting for the business;
  • any social, sports or welfare club or canteen run for employees, and its members while acting in that capacity;
  • any person or entity, including a principal, that you are required by a written contract entered into before the occurrence to include as an insured, but only for liability arising from the business and only to the extent the contract requires; and
  • if the named insured dies, its legal personal representative for liability arising from the business.

What Is Covered

Public Liability

We will pay the compensation you become legally liable to pay because of personal injury or property damage that:

  • happens during the policy period within the geographical limits;
  • is caused by an occurrence in connection with the business; and
  • is not caused by your products.

We will also pay defence costs and supplementary payments, as set out in the Defence Costs clause and the Supplementary Payments clause.

Products Liability

We will pay the compensation you become legally liable to pay because of personal injury or property damage that:

  • happens during the policy period within the geographical limits; and
  • is caused by an occurrence in connection with your products.

The most we will pay for compensation under this cover for all occurrences in the policy period is the products aggregate limit shown in the schedule. We will also pay defence costs and supplementary payments, as set out in the Defence Costs clause and the Supplementary Payments clause.

Defence Costs

We have the right, and the duty, to defend any claim against you that this policy may cover, even if the claim is groundless, false or fraudulent. We will choose the lawyers and manage the defence, and we will consult you about strategy.

We pay defence costs in addition to the limit of liability, except as set out in the Defence Costs and the Limit provision. We pay defence costs as they are incurred and do not wait until the claim is resolved.

Supplementary Payments

For a claim this policy covers, we will also pay, in addition to the limit of liability:

  • interest on a judgment against you that accrues after judgment and before we pay or offer to pay our share of it;
  • the cost of appeal bonds and bonds to release attachments, up to the limit of liability (we do not have to provide the bonds);
  • expenses you incur for first aid or emergency medical treatment given to others at the time of an occurrence;
  • reasonable costs of temporary measures to protect property damaged by an occurrence, to prevent further damage;
  • your reasonable expenses, with our consent, including loss of earnings of your directors and employees of up to $500 a day for each person (or the amount shown in the schedule), for attending court or hearings at our request;
  • the costs of legal representation, with our consent, at any coronial inquest or official inquiry into a death or personal injury that may result in a claim under this policy; and
  • your reasonable costs of preparing information we ask for in support of a claim, up to $25,000 for any one occurrence (or the amount shown in the schedule).

Extensions of Cover

The following extensions are included automatically. Each is subject to the terms of this policy and to the limit of liability, unless a different sub-limit is stated. A default sub-limit applies only if the schedule does not show a different amount. A sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability.

Tenants' Liability

We cover your liability for property damage to premises, or their fixtures and fittings, that you lease, rent or occupy for the business. The default sub-limit is $500,000 for any one occurrence. We do not cover wear and tear, gradual deterioration, or damage that existed before you took occupation. This extension applies despite the Property You Own or Control exclusion.

Property in Your Care, Custody or Control

We cover your liability for property damage to property of others that is in your physical or legal care, custody or control for the business. The default sub-limit is $250,000 for any one occurrence. We do not cover property you own, hire or lease, property in transit, or the part of any property you are working on that is damaged because of that work. This extension applies despite the Property You Own or Control exclusion.

Loading and Unloading Vehicles

We cover your liability arising from the loading or unloading of any vehicle in connection with the business, and from the delivery or collection of goods to or from it. This extension applies despite the Motor Vehicles exclusion. It does not cover liability arising from the vehicle being driven.

Vehicles as Tools of Trade

We cover your liability arising from the use of any vehicle as a tool of trade or plant, such as a crane, hoist, forklift, elevating work platform or excavator, while it is being used for its mechanical function at a work site and not while it is travelling. This extension applies despite the Motor Vehicles exclusion. It does not apply to any liability that a compulsory third party scheme or statutory motor accident scheme covers, or would cover if the vehicle were registered.

Completed Work

We cover your liability for personal injury or property damage caused by work you have completed and handed over, as part of the Products Liability clause. We do not pay the cost of repairing, replacing or redoing the work itself (see the Your Products and Work Themselves exclusion).

Advertising Injury

Personal injury includes advertising injury committed during the policy period in the course of advertising your goods, services or business. Advertising injury is subject to the public liability limit of liability for all advertising injury in the policy period combined.

Statutory Liability Defence Costs

We pay defence costs you incur, with our consent, to defend a prosecution brought under any Australian law for a breach of that law in connection with an occurrence that this policy covers or would cover but for the excess. The default sub-limit is $500,000 for all prosecutions in the policy period.

We do not pay any fine, penalty or penalty notice, or the cost of complying with any order. Work health and safety laws in New South Wales, Victoria, Queensland and other jurisdictions prohibit insurance against work health and safety penalties, and this extension does not cover them. [WP REVIEW: confirm the state-by-state position on insuring defence costs (as distinct from penalties) for WHS prosecutions, including WHS Act 2011 (NSW) s272A and the Victorian and Queensland provisions.]

Cross Liabilities

Where you consist of more than one party, each is treated as if it had a separate policy, so that one of you can claim against another. This does not increase the limit of liability. This extension does not cover personal injury to an employee of the party against whom the claim is made, which the Employers' Liability exclusion deals with.

Newly Acquired Subsidiaries

An entity that becomes a subsidiary during the policy period is covered automatically for 60 days from the date it becomes a subsidiary, or to the end of the policy period if sooner, if its annual turnover is $10,000,000 or less (or the amount shown in the schedule) and its business is similar to the business. To continue cover after 60 days, you must give us details within those 60 days and pay any additional premium we reasonably require. We do not cover occurrences that happened before the entity became a subsidiary.

Voluntary Workers

We cover your liability for personal injury to a voluntary worker while working in the business, as if that person were a member of the public. This extension applies despite the Employers' Liability exclusion.

First Aid and Medical Services

We cover your liability for personal injury arising from first aid or emergency medical treatment given by you or an employee, other than a medical practitioner acting in that capacity, in connection with the business. This extension applies despite the Professional Services exclusion.

Damage to Underground Services

We cover your liability for property damage to underground pipes, cables, mains and other services, if before starting work you took reasonable steps to find them, including making an enquiry through Before You Dig Australia or the relevant authority and following the information provided. The default sub-limit is $500,000 for any one occurrence. We do not pay the cost of repairing the service itself unless you are legally liable for it.

Liability for Contractors

We cover your liability for personal injury or property damage caused by a contractor or subcontractor working for you in connection with the business. We do not cover the contractor or subcontractor themselves unless they are an insured under the definition of You, Your.

Business Visits Overseas

We cover your liability arising from business visits outside Australia and New Zealand by your directors, officers and employees who do no manual work while there, including visits to the USA and Canada. For claims brought in the USA and Canada, the Defence Costs and the Limit provision applies.

Wrongful Arrest

We cover your liability for personal injury from false arrest, wrongful detention, false imprisonment or malicious prosecution, including the detention of a person suspected of theft. The default sub-limit is $250,000 for any one occurrence.

Employees' and Visitors' Property

We cover your liability for property damage to the personal property of employees, labour hire workers and visitors while on your premises. The default sub-limit is $100,000 for any one occurrence. This extension applies despite the Property You Own or Control exclusion.

Incidental Supply of Liquor

We cover your liability arising from the supply of liquor in the course of the business where it is incidental to the business, such as at functions, events, client entertainment or as a gift. Liability arising from the sale or supply of liquor under a licence that you hold as licensee of a venue is covered only by Endorsement E07 (see the Licensed Venues exclusion).

Injury to Labour Hire Workers

We cover your liability for personal injury to a labour hire worker, as if that person were a member of the public, except to the extent the Employers' Liability exclusion applies. A worker-to-worker excess applies to these claims if one is shown in the schedule (see the Excess provision).

What Is Not Covered

We do not cover any claim, compensation, defence costs or other amount described in the exclusions below. Where an exclusion states that it does not apply in a particular case, that exception (a carve-back) applies only to the extent the exclusion says. Where an extension or endorsement states that it applies despite an exclusion, the extension or endorsement prevails to the extent it says so. The How the Exclusions Apply exclusion shows which exclusions have carve-backs.

Employers' Liability

Personal injury to an employee arising out of or in the course of their employment by you.

This exclusion also applies to any liability for personal injury to any other person, to the extent that you are required by any workers compensation law to insure that liability, or would be required to but for any exemption you hold.

This exclusion does not apply to liability for personal injury to a labour hire worker, a voluntary worker, or the employee of a contractor or subcontractor, except to the extent you are required by a workers compensation law to insure it. A host business is not usually required to insure a labour hire worker under workers compensation law, because the labour hire business is the worker's employer.

Professional Services

Any act, error or omission in professional advice, design, specification, inspection, certification or other professional service that you give for a fee, or that would usually be given for a fee.

This exclusion does not apply to personal injury or property damage that is a direct physical result of that act, error or omission, or to advice given free of charge in connection with the sale of your products or the installation of them.

Cyber Event

Any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from access to or use of any computer system or electronic data by a person without authority, or from the corruption, deletion or unavailability of any computer system or electronic data, or from any breach of privacy or data protection law.

This exclusion does not apply to personal injury, or to property damage to tangible property other than electronic data, caused directly by such an event.

Directors' and Officers' Liability

Any act, error or omission of a director or officer in their capacity as a director or officer in managing an entity, as distinct from carrying on the business.

Property You Own or Control

Property damage to property you own, or that is in your care, custody or control. This exclusion does not apply to the extent the Tenants' Liability extension, the Property in Your Care, Custody or Control extension or the Employees' and Visitors' Property extension covers the damage.

Your Products and Work Themselves

  • Property damage to your products, if the damage is caused by a defect in them or by their nature;
  • the cost of repairing, replacing, reconditioning, modifying or redoing any of your products or work;
  • the cost of withdrawing, recalling, inspecting, repairing or replacing any of your products, or of any property of which they form part, because of a known or suspected defect (product recall is covered only by Endorsement E16); and
  • loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically damaged, arising from a delay in or failure to perform a contract by you, or from your products failing to meet the level of performance, quality or fitness you warranted or represented.

This exclusion does not apply to property damage to other property caused by your products or work.

Product Performance

Failure of your products to perform their intended function or to meet the level of performance, quality, fitness or durability warranted or represented by you, unless the failure causes personal injury or property damage to other property. Product errors and omissions are covered only by Endorsement E17.

Contractual Liability

Liability you assume under any contract or agreement, to the extent it is greater than the liability you would have had without the contract or agreement.

This exclusion does not apply to:

  • liability you would have had at law without the contract;
  • a term of a contract that relieves a party from liability, or that requires you to indemnify a party, where that term is void or unenforceable under the Australian Consumer Law (including as an unfair contract term) and you remain liable at law;
  • warranties or guarantees as to the fitness or quality of your products that are implied or imposed by law, including consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law, but only for resulting personal injury or property damage to other property;
  • a lease, licence or tenancy agreement for premises, other than any obligation to repair the premises;
  • an agreement with a railway, road, port or public authority for access, siding, crossing or the like; and
  • a contract with a principal that requires you to indemnify it for liability arising from the business, to the extent the principal is an insured under the definition of You, Your.

Deliberate Acts

Personal injury or property damage that you expected or intended. This exclusion does not apply to personal injury or property damage that results from reasonable force used to protect people or property. It applies only to the person who expected or intended the injury or damage, as set out in the Individual Treatment of Insureds condition.

Motor Vehicles

Personal injury or property damage arising from the ownership, use or operation by you or on your behalf of any vehicle that is registered, or is required by law to be registered, or that is being driven on a public road, or for which compulsory third party insurance or a statutory motor accident scheme provides cover or would if the vehicle were registered.

This exclusion does not apply to:

  • property damage caused by a vehicle on your premises or at a work site, or in a car park, other than property damage to the vehicle itself;
  • liability covered by the Loading and Unloading Vehicles extension or the Vehicles as Tools of Trade extension; or
  • personal injury where no compulsory third party scheme or statutory motor accident scheme provides cover, and the vehicle is not required to be registered.

Aircraft and Watercraft

Personal injury or property damage arising from the ownership, use or operation by you or on your behalf of any aircraft (including any remotely piloted aircraft or drone), hovercraft, or any watercraft longer than 8 metres (or the length shown in the schedule).

This exclusion does not apply to aircraft you do not own or operate on which your employees travel as passengers, or to watercraft at or below that length. Drones are covered only by Endorsement E09.

Aircraft Products

Personal injury or property damage arising from your products that are installed in or used in any aircraft, if the products affect the flight capability, navigation or safety of the aircraft, or are used in its ground control or air traffic control. [WP REVIEW: confirm the scope against current Australian market wordings and whether an aviation products write-back is needed.]

Pollution

Personal injury or property damage, and any cost of testing, monitoring, cleaning up, removing, containing or neutralising, arising from the discharge, dispersal, release, seepage or escape of any pollutant.

This exclusion does not apply if the discharge, dispersal, release, seepage or escape is caused by a sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected event that takes place in its entirety at a specific time and place during the policy period, outside the USA and Canada. Gradual pollution is covered only by Endorsement E05.

Asbestos

Personal injury, property damage or any cost arising from asbestos or any material containing asbestos in any form, including its mining, manufacture, processing, distribution, sale, installation, presence, removal, disposal or exposure to it.

Silica

Personal injury arising from inhaling, ingesting or being exposed to respirable crystalline silica, silica dust, or any material containing silica, including engineered stone. Cover may be written back by Endorsement E20. Since 1 July 2024, the manufacture, supply, processing and installation of engineered stone benchtops, panels and slabs has been prohibited in Australia under work health and safety laws.

PFAS

Personal injury, property damage or any cost arising from per- and poly-fluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), including perfluorooctane sulfonate (PFOS), perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA) and perfluorohexane sulfonate (PFHxS), or any product or material containing them, including firefighting foam. Cover may be written back by Endorsement E22.

Mould

Personal injury or property damage arising from mould, mildew, fungus or spores, or any toxin they produce, unless they result directly from property damage caused by a sudden and accidental event covered by this policy.

Communicable Disease

Personal injury, property damage or any cost arising from the actual or alleged transmission of, or failure to prevent the transmission of, any communicable disease. This exclusion does not apply to food-borne illness caused by your products that are food or beverages. Other cover may be written back by Endorsement E23.

Abuse

Any liability arising from abuse, including liability for failing to prevent, detect or report abuse, for negligent hiring, supervision or retention of any person, or for any vicarious liability for abuse. This exclusion applies whoever committed the abuse. Cover may be written back by Endorsement E19.

Licensed Venues

Liability arising from the sale or supply of liquor by you, or by anyone on your behalf, under a liquor licence for which you are the licensee or approved manager, including liability for the actions of intoxicated persons. Cover may be written back by Endorsement E07. This exclusion does not apply to the Incidental Supply of Liquor extension.

Vibration and Removal of Support

Property damage to land, buildings or structures caused by vibration, or by the removal, weakening or interference with the support of land, buildings or structures, in connection with excavation, piling, demolition or similar work by you or on your behalf. Cover may be written back by Endorsement E13.

Shoring and Underpinning

Personal injury or property damage arising from shoring, underpinning or the temporary support of any building or structure by you or on your behalf. Cover may be written back by Endorsement E14.

Work Outside Australia and New Zealand

Liability arising from manual or physical work performed outside Australia and New Zealand by you or on your behalf. Cover may be written back by Endorsement E04. This exclusion does not apply to the Business Visits Overseas extension.

USA and Canada

  • Any claim brought in a court in the USA and Canada, or to enforce a judgment or award of such a court; and
  • any liability arising from your products exported to the USA and Canada, by you or with your knowledge.

This exclusion does not apply to the Business Visits Overseas extension. Cover may be written back by Endorsement E10.

High-Hazard Products

Personal injury or property damage arising from your products that are:

  • therapeutic goods, medicines, medical devices or other goods regulated by the Therapeutic Goods Administration, unless written back by Endorsement E06;
  • tobacco, nicotine or vaping products;
  • firearms, ammunition, explosives or fireworks; or
  • radioactive materials.

Known Injury or Damage

Personal injury or property damage that began before the policy period and that you knew about, or ought reasonably to have known about, before the policy period began, including any continuation, change or resumption of it during the policy period. This exclusion does not apply if you told us about it before the policy period began and we agreed in writing to cover it.

Fines, Penalties and Punitive Damages

Fines, penalties, penalty notices, civil penalties, punitive, exemplary or aggravated damages, liquidated damages, and the multiple part of any multiplied damages award. New Zealand exemplary damages are covered only by Endorsement E18. This exclusion does not apply to defence costs under the Statutory Liability Defence Costs extension.

Financial Loss Without Injury or Damage

Financial loss that does not result from personal injury or property damage covered by this policy.

War and Terrorism

  • War, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities or warlike operations (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, military or usurped power, or confiscation by any government or public authority; and
  • any act of terrorism, meaning an act, or the threat of an act, involving the use of force or violence by any person or group for political, religious, ideological or similar purposes, including to influence a government or put the public in fear, and any action to control, prevent or suppress it.

The terrorism part of this exclusion does not apply to personal injury from an act of terrorism that occurs in Australia, to the extent the Terrorism and Cyclone Insurance Act 2003 (Cth) makes it ineffective. [WP REVIEW: confirm the TCIA position for a general liability policy, which is not an eligible insurance contract for property or business interruption.]

Nuclear

Ionising radiation from, or contamination by radioactivity from, any nuclear fuel, nuclear waste or the combustion of nuclear fuel; the radioactive, toxic, explosive or other hazardous properties of any nuclear installation, assembly or component; and any nuclear weapon.

How Much We Will Pay

Limit of Liability

The most we will pay for compensation for any one occurrence under the Public Liability clause is the public liability limit of liability shown in the schedule.

The most we will pay for compensation for all occurrences in the policy period under the Products Liability clause is the products aggregate limit shown in the schedule. Each occurrence under that cover is also subject to the products aggregate limit as a limit for any one occurrence.

The limit of liability applies however many people or entities are insured, claims are made or claimants there are.

Sub-limits

A sub-limit shown in an extension, an endorsement or the schedule is the most we will pay for the matter it applies to. It is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability. Where a sub-limit applies "in the aggregate", it is the most we will pay for all occurrences in the policy period.

One Occurrence, One Limit

All personal injury and property damage attributable to one source or original cause is one occurrence, under the definition of Occurrence. It is treated as having happened when the first personal injury or property damage happened. Only one limit of liability and one excess apply to it, even if it continues into later policy periods, and if it could fall under both covers we will treat it as falling under the one that gives you more cover.

Defence Costs and the Limit

We pay defence costs in addition to the limit of liability, with two exceptions:

  • if the compensation you are liable to pay for an occurrence exceeds the applicable limit of liability, we pay only the proportion of defence costs that the limit of liability bears to the total compensation paid to settle or satisfy the claim; and
  • for any claim brought in a court in the USA and Canada, or to enforce a judgment or award of such a court, defence costs are part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability.

Endorsement E03 changes this so that defence costs are within the limit of liability for all claims, if it is shown in the schedule.

Worked example. Limit of liability $10,000,000. A claim settles for $15,000,000 and defence costs are $900,000. We pay $10,000,000 of the compensation and two-thirds of the defence costs ($600,000), because the limit is two-thirds of the total compensation.

Excess

You bear the excess shown in the schedule for each occurrence. It applies to compensation, and not to defence costs unless the schedule says so.

If a worker-to-worker excess is shown in the schedule, it applies to each worker-to-worker claim instead of the standard excess. If a special excess is shown in the schedule or an endorsement for a particular type of occurrence, it applies to that type of occurrence instead of the standard excess. Where more than one excess could apply to one occurrence, only the highest applies.

Paying Out the Limit

At any time, we may pay you the amount of the limit of liability that remains for an occurrence (less any amount already paid), together with defence costs and supplementary payments incurred to that date. When we do, we will have no further liability for that occurrence, including for defence costs incurred after that date. We will consult you before doing this and will give you reasonable time to take over the defence.

Other Insurance

If another insurance policy covers you for the same liability, tell us as soon as you know of a claim. You may claim under this policy or the other policy. If we pay, we may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows, including under s.76 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Nothing in this provision limits s.45 of that Act. This provision does not apply where this policy is written as an excess layer under Endorsement E11, which sets out how that layer relates to the underlying insurance.

  • keep a suitable fire extinguisher at the work location; and
  • keep a person on fire watch during the work and for at least 30 minutes after it ends.

If you do not, we may reduce our liability for an occurrence arising from the hot works to the extent that the failure caused or contributed to the loss, as s.54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows. The special excess shown for this endorsement applies to each occurrence arising from hot works away from your premises.

Schedule variables: special excess.

E16 Product Recall Expenses

We will pay the recall expenses you incur in recalling your products from the market, if:

  • the recall is required by law, or is necessary because you or a regulator first discover during the policy period that the use of your products has caused or could cause personal injury or property damage; and
  • you tell us in writing within 7 days of deciding to recall.

Recall expenses means the reasonable costs of communicating the recall, transporting, storing and destroying recalled products, and extra staff costs, incurred within 12 months of the recall decision. The Your Products and Work Themselves exclusion does not apply to recall expenses covered by this endorsement.

We do not pay: the cost of repairing, replacing or refunding the products; loss of profit, market share or goodwill; recalls caused by the products reaching the end of their shelf life, by deterioration in normal use, or by a defect you knew of before the policy period; or recalls of products that are prohibited from sale. The most we will pay for all recalls in the policy period is the aggregate sub-limit shown for this endorsement, which is $500,000 if none is shown. The special excess shown for this endorsement applies to each recall.

Schedule variables: aggregate sub-limit (default $500,000); special excess.

E17 Product Errors and Omissions

We will pay the compensation you become legally liable to pay because your products fail to perform their intended function or to meet the level of performance, quality or fitness you warranted or represented, if:

  • the claim is first made against you during the policy period and you tell us about it during the policy period; and
  • the products were supplied after the retroactive date shown in the schedule for this endorsement.

The Product Performance exclusion and the Financial Loss Without Injury or Damage exclusion do not apply to compensation covered by this endorsement. We do not pay the cost of repairing, replacing or refunding the products themselves, or any claim arising from a circumstance you knew of before the policy period. The most we will pay for all claims in the policy period, including defence costs, is the aggregate sub-limit shown for this endorsement. The special excess shown applies to each claim.

Schedule variables: aggregate sub-limit; retroactive date; special excess.

E18 New Zealand Exemplary Damages

The Fines, Penalties and Punitive Damages exclusion does not apply to exemplary or punitive damages awarded in New Zealand for personal injury that is covered by the Accident Compensation Act 2001 (NZ), where you are liable for them and they arise from an occurrence covered by this policy. We do not pay exemplary damages to the extent they are uninsurable by law, or where the conduct giving rise to them was deliberate or reckless conduct by a director or officer of the named insured. The most we will pay for all such damages in the policy period, including defence costs, is the aggregate sub-limit shown for this endorsement.

Schedule variables: aggregate sub-limit (NZD or AUD).

E19 Abuse Write-back

The Abuse exclusion does not apply to a claim for abuse that is first made against you during the policy period and that you tell us about during the policy period, if:

  • the abuse first happened after the retroactive date shown in the schedule for this endorsement; and
  • at the time of the abuse you held the child-safe accreditation, or followed the National Principles for Child Safe Organisations, as you told us in your proposal, and you required working with children checks or equivalent screening for every person working with children or vulnerable people.

We do not cover the person who committed the abuse, or any person who knew of it and failed to report it as the law required. The most we will pay for all claims in the policy period, including defence costs, is the aggregate sub-limit shown for this endorsement, and the special excess shown applies to each claim. E20 Silica Write-back

The Silica exclusion does not apply to personal injury to a person other than an employee, labour hire worker or person working on the same site, if at the time of exposure you complied with the workplace exposure standard for respirable crystalline silica and the work health and safety laws that apply to silica dust control, including on-tool water suppression or extraction and air monitoring where required. It never applies to engineered stone work. The aggregate sub-limit and special excess shown for this endorsement apply.

The PFAS exclusion does not apply to personal injury or property damage caused by a sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected release of PFAS that takes place in its entirety at a specific time and place during the policy period in Australia, if you tell us about it in writing within 30 days. The aggregate sub-limit and special excess shown for this endorsement apply.

E23 Communicable Disease Write-back

The Communicable Disease exclusion does not apply to personal injury to a member of the public arising from the transmission of a communicable disease at your premises, if at the time you complied with any public health order and with the infection control measures you told us about in your proposal. We do not cover any claim arising from a pandemic or epidemic declared by the World Health Organization or by an Australian government.
