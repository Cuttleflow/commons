Geoscience and Mineral Professional Indemnity Insurance — Endorsement

The Geoscience and Mineral Professional Indemnity Insurance terms, in the form of an endorsement to the base Professional Indemnity Insurance wording.

DRAFT — subject to legal review — not for use. This wording is not settled and must not be issued, quoted from, or bound until Wording Partner sign-off. It is provided as source material under CC BY 4.0, without warranty.

Application

This endorsement attaches to and forms part of the Meridian Professional Indemnity Insurance wording, version 1.4.0-DRAFT (document reference 2026/PI/0001), referred to here as the base wording. It changes the base wording only as set out below. All other terms, conditions, exclusions and limits of the base wording continue to apply.

Words defined in the base wording have the same meaning in this endorsement. Where this endorsement and the base wording conflict, this endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict.

The same terms are published as a single integrated wording, Geoscience and Mineral Professional Indemnity Insurance, version 1.0.0-DRAFT. Both documents are generated from the same source and have the same effect.

Replaced: Professional services

The definition of Professional services in the base wording is deleted and replaced with the following.

Professional services

The following services provided to a third party for a fee:

(a) geological, mineral and exploration consulting;

(b) the estimation of mineral resources and ore reserves;

(c) the technical assessment and valuation of mineral assets;

(d) geophysics, geochemistry and hydrogeology;

(e) public reports, which means a report prepared for release to a securities exchange, for a disclosure document, or for any other public document about a mineral asset, and any contribution to such a report; and

(f) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

A public report is a professional service only if each competent person or specialist who signs it, or whose work it relies on, holds the professional membership that the reporting code applying to the report requires. A reporting code is the JORC Code, the VALMIN Code or an equivalent code recognised by the Committee for Mineral Reserves International Reporting Standards (CRIRSCO).

Added: Contributing Competent Persons

The following extension is added to the base wording, after the Subcontractors and Outsourcers extension.

Contributing Competent Persons

We cover you for a claim arising from your contribution to a public report signed by another competent person or specialist, to the extent that the claim arises from the part of the report that you prepared or for which you accepted responsibility.

Added: Securities Exchange Queries

The following extension is added to the base wording, after the Contributing Competent Persons extension.

Securities Exchange Queries

We also pay defence costs under the Inquiry Costs extension for a written query from a securities exchange, or a review by ASIC, about a public report that you prepared or contributed to.

Added: Guarantees of Grade, Tonnage or Price

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Contractual Liability exclusion.

Guarantees of Grade, Tonnage or Price

Arising from any guarantee or warranty that you give of the grade, tonnage, recovery or price of any mineral, or of the outcome of any exploration or mining project, except to the extent that you would have been liable without that guarantee or warranty.

Added: Excluded Geoscience Activities

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Non-Compensatory Payments exclusion.

Excluded Geoscience Activities

Arising from:

(a) the design or construction of dams more than 6 metres in height, including tailings storage facilities;

(b) oil and gas services, including the estimation of petroleum reserves; or

(c) nuclear engineering.

Geoscience and Mineral Endorsements

The following endorsements apply to this policy only when the schedule shows that they apply.

Public Report Excess

For each claim arising from a public report, the excess is the amount shown in the schedule for this endorsement. If no amount is shown, the excess is the greater of $10,000 and 0.25% of the amount raised in, or the value of the transaction connected with, the capital raising or transaction for which the public report was prepared, but not more than $250,000.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • What percentage of your fees in the last 12 months came from public reports, including contributions to them? qs.pi.gm.public_report_pct

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.gm.public_report_pct > 0: Apply automatically

Underwriter responses (on a referral or suggestion): Attach; Attach with special excess; Do not attach

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because you sign or contribute to public reports about mineral assets.

Petroleum Reserves Estimation

Paragraph (b) of the Excluded Geoscience Activities exclusion does not apply to the estimation and evaluation of petroleum reserves and resources, if the work is carried out under the Petroleum Resources Management System or an equivalent standard shown in the schedule.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • What percentage of your fees in the last 12 months came from the estimation of petroleum reserves or resources? qs.pi.gm.petroleum_pct

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.gm.petroleum_pct > 0: Offer as a priced option

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Offered because you estimate petroleum reserves.

North American Reporting Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from a report prepared under Canadian National Instrument 43-101 or United States Securities and Exchange Commission Regulation S-K subpart 1300, or any claim brought in a court of the United States of America or Canada.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Do you prepare reports under Canadian NI 43-101 or US SEC Regulation S-K subpart 1300, or for projects in the USA or Canada? qs.pi.gm.na_reports

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.gm.na_reports = yes: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because you prepare reports under North American reporting rules.

Tailings and Mine Closure Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from advice on, or the design, assessment or inspection of, a tailings storage facility, or the planning or costing of mine closure or rehabilitation.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • What percentage of your fees in the last 12 months came from tailings storage, mine closure or rehabilitation work? qs.pi.gm.tailings_pct

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.gm.tailings_pct > 0: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because {qs.pi.gm.tailings_pct}% of your fees come from tailings storage or mine closure work.

Mineral Asset Valuation Sub-limit

For all claims arising from the valuation of mineral assets, the most we will pay in total, including defence costs, is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement. The sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • What percentage of your fees in the last 12 months came from mineral asset valuations? qs.pi.gm.valmin_pct

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.gm.valmin_pct > 10: Suggest to the underwriter (quote not held)

Underwriter responses (on a referral or suggestion): Attach; Attach with amended sub-limit; Do not attach

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because {qs.pi.gm.valmin_pct}% of your fees come from mineral asset valuations.
