Nursery and Greenlife Package Insurance

Property including plant stock, deterioration of stock, business interruption, theft, machinery breakdown, transit and public and products liability for production nurseries, turf and flower growers and garden centres.

About This Policy

This policy insures a nursery business: its buildings, contents and equipment, shade structures, glasshouses and plant stock, its gross profit, and its legal liability to others. It is written for production nurseries growing under cover or outdoors, turf growers, flower growers and garden centres.

It has seven sections. Property and Public and Products Liability always apply. The other sections apply only if the schedule shows them.

Important Information

Who issues this policy. The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

Who this policy is for. This wording is written for nursery businesses that buy it for the business. Its sections are property, deterioration of stock, business interruption, theft, liability, machinery breakdown and transit covers, which are not among the kinds of insurance the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) and its regulations treat as retail when bought for a small business. Most buyers will therefore be wholesale clients, and this wording is not a Product Disclosure Statement. A dwelling and its contents, a registered vehicle of up to 2 tonnes carrying capacity, and personal accident and illness cover are retail kinds of insurance. They are not insured by this policy.

Who is insured. You are the person, partnership, company or trust named in the schedule. The Liability for Injury and Damage clause says who else is insured under Public and Products Liability.

Your duty when you apply. Before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated, you have the duty of disclosure in s.21 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). You must tell us every matter you know, or that a reasonable person in your circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we waive. If you do not comply, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

If this policy is a consumer insurance contract under that Act, you instead have a duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us, and the Act sets out what we may do if you do not.

Your nursery business matters. We set the cover, the premium and the endorsements by the main activity of the nursery business, the value of your property and plant stock and the share of each class, and the activities you tell us about. Tell us whether you sell or make potting mix or soil products, apply chemicals for others, do landscaping or installation work, export live plants, have retail customers or run open days, and whether an order or direction under a biosecurity law is in force for the situation. Tell us during the policy period when the nursery business changes, as the Tell Us About Changes obligation requires.

Flood, frost, pests and disease. Flood is not covered unless the E01 Flood Cover endorsement is shown in the schedule. Damage caused by bushfire is covered. Cover for bushfire and flood may not start while an emergency warning is in force for the situation, as the Emergency Warnings at the Start of Cover condition explains. Frost, drought, heatwave, pests and plant diseases are not insured events. Plant stock destroyed by order under a biosecurity law is covered only if the schedule shows the E05 Biosecurity Destruction endorsement.

What this policy does not replace. This policy does not provide workers compensation insurance, which each state and territory requires an employer to hold through its own scheme. It does not insure a dwelling, your vehicles on the road, land, or crops grown for their fruit, vegetables or other produce.

Cooling-off period. You may cancel this policy within 21 days of the date it starts, and within 21 days of each renewal, by telling us in writing. If you have not made a claim, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover. After that, you may still cancel under the Cancellation condition.

Commission and fees. The schedule or the quote shows any commission or fee paid to your broker in connection with this policy.

Financial Claims Scheme. If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.

Definitions

These words have the meanings below wherever they appear in this policy, with or without capital letters.

Biosecurity law

The Biosecurity Act 2015 (Cth), and any law of a state or territory about plant pests, plant diseases, weeds or biosecurity, and any order, direction, notice or permit made under them.

Breakdown

The sudden and accidental failure of a machine, a climate control system or electronic equipment to work, caused by a mechanical or electrical fault, that needs repair or replacement before it can work again.

Buildings

Buildings and structures at the situation used for the nursery business, including offices, sheds, potting and packing sheds, workshops, cool rooms, shops and display areas, with their fixtures, fittings and services, and fences, gates, walls, paths, paved areas, car parks, signs and water tanks. Buildings do not include glasshouses, shade structures, a dwelling or a building used as a home, or land.

Bushfire

A fire in grass, crops, scrub, bush, forest or other vegetation, including one that started at the situation.

Climate control system

The irrigation, misting, fogging, fertigation, heating, cooling, ventilation, lighting and shading systems at the situation that keep plant stock alive and growing, with their pumps, boilers, fans, motors, sensors, controllers and alarms.

Contents and equipment

Property used in the nursery business that you own or are responsible for, other than buildings, glasshouses, shade structures, plant stock, money and vehicles, including tools, benches, empty pots, trays and containers, potting and seeding machines, forklifts and other machinery, potting mix, soil, seed, fertiliser and chemicals, garden supplies held for sale, and office and shop equipment.

Damage

Sudden and accidental physical loss of, or physical damage to, property. Damaged has a corresponding meaning.

Employee

A person under a contract of service or apprenticeship with you, and a person you engage as a casual, seasonal or contract worker who works under your direction in the nursery business. It does not include a contractor carrying on their own business.

Endorsement

A written change to this policy that is shown in the schedule by its code, together with its variables.

Event

One incident, or a series of incidents arising from one cause, during the policy period. A bushfire, a storm or a flood is one event for all damage it causes within any 72 consecutive hours.

Excess

The amount you bear of each claim, shown in the schedule for the section or set by an endorsement.

Flood

The covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from the normal confines of any lake, river, creek or other natural watercourse, whether or not altered or modified, or any reservoir, canal or dam.

Glasshouse

A glasshouse, greenhouse, polytunnel or polyhouse at the situation whose roof or walls are wholly or mainly glass, polycarbonate, acrylic or plastic film, including its frame, glazing, coverings and benches, and the parts of a climate control system fixed to it.

Gross profit

The turnover of the nursery business, plus the value of closing stock, less the value of opening stock and less the cost of purchases of plant stock, seed, cuttings, growing media, pots and other goods for resale or for growing. Stock is valued as your accounts value it.

Indemnity period

The period beginning on the date of the damage and lasting no longer than the period shown in the schedule, during which the results of the nursery business are affected by the damage.

Insured event

An event listed in the Insured Events clause.

Nursery business

The main activity shown in the schedule, carried on at the situation, and these incidental activities: selling plant stock, potting mix, soil and garden supplies by wholesale and retail, at the situation and at markets, shows and field days; delivering plant stock and placing it where the customer asks; and the activities that the E08 Chemical Application for Others endorsement, the E09 Incidental Landscaping and Installation endorsement and the E10 Open Days and Workshops endorsement cover where the schedule shows them.

Occurrence

An event, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same conditions, that results in personal injury or property damage that you did not expect or intend. All personal injury and property damage arising from one source or original cause is one occurrence.

Personal injury

Bodily injury, death, sickness, disease, disability, shock, fright, mental anguish or mental injury, and false arrest, wrongful detention, malicious prosecution, defamation and invasion of privacy.

Plant stock

Live plants, seedlings, cuttings, tubestock, bulbs, cut flowers, turf and other greenlife that the nursery business grows, holds or buys for sale, at any stage of growth, with the pots, containers and growing media they are in. Plant stock does not include plants that form part of a garden, a display garden or a windbreak and are not for sale, or crops grown for their fruit, vegetables or other produce.

Plants in the open

Plant stock at the situation that is not under cover, including plant stock in containers on open ground, and turf and plants growing in the ground for sale.

Plants under cover

Plant stock inside a glasshouse, a shade structure or a building.

Policy period

The period shown in the schedule, or a shorter period if this policy is cancelled.

Pollutant

A solid, liquid, gaseous or thermal irritant or contaminant, including smoke, vapour, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, fertiliser, pesticide, herbicide, fungicide, effluent and waste.

Property damage

Physical damage to or destruction of tangible property, including the loss of its use, and the loss of use of tangible property that is not physically damaged.

Shade structure

A shade house, a hail netting structure or another structure at the situation whose roof or walls are wholly or mainly shade cloth, netting or mesh, including its frame, posts, cables and covering.

Situation

The premises shown in the schedule, and any other premises in Australia that you own or lease for the nursery business and tell us about.

Sum insured

The amount shown in the schedule for an item or section, which is the most we pay for that item or section for one event, unless this policy says otherwise.

Theft

Taking property dishonestly without the owner's consent, with the intention of permanently depriving the owner of it.

Turnover

The money paid or payable to you, less GST, for plant stock and goods sold and delivered and for services provided in the nursery business.

Vehicle

A vehicle registered, or required by law to be registered, for use on a public road, other than under a conditional registration scheme, and a trailer, caravan, aircraft, drone weighing more than 2 kilograms, or watercraft.

We, Us, Our

The insurer named in the schedule.

You, Your

The person, partnership, company or trust named in the schedule as the insured.

Your products

Anything grown, propagated, made, sold, supplied, delivered or installed by the nursery business, including plant stock, potting mix, soil, mulch and garden supplies, after it has left your possession or control.

Property

This section always applies.

Property Cover

We will pay for damage to buildings, contents and equipment, shade structures, glasshouses and plant stock at the situation, caused by an insured event during the policy period.

The most we pay for one event is the property sum insured shown in the schedule, plus the additional benefits in the Property Additional Benefits extension. The schedule shows each class of property's share of that sum insured: buildings, contents and equipment, shade structures, glasshouses, plants under cover and plants in the open. The share is the most we pay for that class in one event, unless the schedule shows a separate sum insured for an item.

Plant Stock Valuation

We pay for plant stock as follows:

  • plant stock ready for sale: the wholesale price you would have received for it at the time of the damage, less the costs of selling and delivering it that you do not incur;
  • plant stock not yet ready for sale: the cost of growing replacement plant stock to the stage of growth it had reached, including propagation material, growing media, containers, labour and a fair share of overheads, but not more than its wholesale price when ready for sale less the costs of growing it on that you do not incur; and
  • plant stock you bought for resale and have not grown on, including a garden centre's plant stock held for retail sale: what it would cost you to buy plant stock of the same kind and size.

Where damaged plant stock recovers, or can be sold at a reduced price, we pay the reduction in its value.

Buildings Underinsurance

This provision applies only to buildings, and only to a claim of more than $20,000. If, at the time of the damage, the buildings share of the property sum insured is less than 80% of the cost of rebuilding all the buildings, we pay only the proportion of the claim that the buildings share bears to 80% of that cost.

Worked example: the buildings share is $600,000; rebuilding all the buildings would cost $1,000,000; a fire causes $300,000 of damage to buildings. 80% of $1,000,000 is $800,000. We pay $600,000 divided by $800,000, times $300,000, which is $225,000, before the excess.

Property Exclusions

In addition to the General Exclusions section, under Property we do not pay for:

  • flood, unless the schedule shows the E01 Flood Cover endorsement;
  • storm, wind, rain and hail damage to plants in the open, unless the schedule shows the E03 Plants in the Open endorsement;
  • theft (see Theft);
  • breakdown (see Machinery Breakdown and Electronic Equipment);
  • death of or damage to plant stock caused by the breakdown or failure of a climate control system, or by the failure of a public supply of electricity, water or gas, unless an insured event at the situation caused the breakdown or failure (see Deterioration of Stock);
  • weather that is not an insured event, such as frost, cold, heatwave, sunburn and drought, and a lack or excess of light, water or nutrients, other than water escaping as the Insured Events clause describes;
  • pests, plant diseases, weeds, fungi, insects and vermin, and the cost of treating or destroying plant stock because of them;
  • damage to plant stock caused by applying chemicals, fertiliser, water or growing media to it, including using the wrong product, rate or method;
  • plant stock that dies or deteriorates through natural growth or ageing, or fails to grow;
  • vehicles, land, soil, dams, water in dams, and trees and plants that are not plant stock;
  • property while it is being carried by road (see Transit);
  • wear and tear, rust, corrosion, rot, mould and gradual deterioration;
  • erosion, subsidence, landslip, shrinkage or expansion of the ground, unless directly caused by an insured event; or
  • damage caused by water entering a building or a glasshouse through an opening that was not made by the storm or by an insured event.

Deterioration of Stock

This section applies if the schedule shows it.

Death and Deterioration of Plant Stock

We will pay for the death of, or damage to, plant stock at the situation caused during the policy period by:

  • the breakdown of a climate control system;
  • damage to a climate control system by an insured event; or
  • the failure of a public supply of electricity, water or gas to the situation for more than 4 consecutive hours, caused by something that happened away from the situation.

The most we pay for one event is the deterioration limit shown in the schedule. We value plant stock as the Plant Stock Valuation clause sets out. The E04 Deterioration of Stock Conditions endorsement sets conditions for this section.

Deterioration of Stock Exclusions

In addition to the General Exclusions section, under Deterioration of Stock we do not pay for:

  • the cost of repairing or replacing the climate control system itself (see Machinery Breakdown and Electronic Equipment);
  • a planned interruption of a public supply that you were told about at least 24 hours before it began, or a supply cut off because you did not pay for it;
  • frost, heatwave or other weather, where the climate control system worked as it was designed and set to work;
  • pests and plant diseases;
  • flood, unless the schedule shows the E01 Flood Cover endorsement; or
  • a breakdown caused by a fault that you knew of before the breakdown and did not repair.

Business Interruption

This section applies if the schedule shows it.

Loss of Gross Profit

If damage covered under Property interrupts the nursery business during the policy period, we will pay during the indemnity period:

  • the loss of gross profit caused by the reduction in turnover, as the How We Calculate Loss of Gross Profit provision sets out; and
  • the increased cost of working: the extra costs you reasonably incur to reduce the reduction in turnover, such as buying in plant stock to supply your customers, hiring glasshouse or shade house space, and moving to temporary premises, up to the loss of gross profit they avoid.

The most we pay for all claims arising from one event is the gross profit sum insured shown in the schedule. The indemnity period is 12 months unless the schedule shows otherwise.

How We Calculate Loss of Gross Profit

We apply the rate of gross profit to the amount by which the turnover during the indemnity period falls short of the turnover in the same period of the 12 months before the damage. The rate of gross profit is the gross profit as a percentage of turnover in the last financial year before the damage. Turnover earned at other premises for the nursery business during the indemnity period counts as turnover.

We adjust these figures for the trends of the nursery business, the seasons, and the other circumstances that would have affected it during the indemnity period if the damage had not happened, so that the result represents as nearly as possible what the nursery business would have earned. We deduct the costs that stop or reduce because of the damage.

The Buildings Underinsurance provision does not apply to this section. The gross profit sum insured is the most we pay, and we do not reduce a claim because it is less than the gross profit.

Business Interruption Exclusions

In addition to the General Exclusions section, under Business Interruption we do not pay for:

  • loss caused by flood, unless the schedule shows the E01 Flood Cover endorsement;
  • loss caused by drought, water restrictions, frost or other weather that is not an insured event, pests, plant diseases, an order or direction under a biosecurity law, quarantine, a fall in market prices, or the loss of a market or a contract;
  • loss after the indemnity period ends;
  • loss caused by damage that is not covered by Property; or
  • fines, penalties or damages you must pay for not completing a contract.

Theft

This section applies if the schedule shows it.

Theft of Property

We will pay for theft of contents and equipment and plant stock from the situation during the policy period, and for damage to buildings, glasshouses and shade structures caused during the theft or an attempted theft.

The most we pay for all theft in the policy period is $25,000, or the theft limit the schedule shows. The most we pay for any one item is $10,000 unless the schedule shows the item with its value.

The Property Basis of Settlement provision decides how we settle.

Theft Reporting

Report a theft to the police within 48 hours of discovering it, and give us the police report number. For an item of contents and equipment worth more than $5,000, keep a record of its serial or identification number and give it to us when you claim. The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies to these requirements.

Theft Exclusions

In addition to the General Exclusions section, under Theft we do not pay for:

  • theft by you, a member of your household or an employee, or with their knowledge or consent;
  • property that cannot be accounted for and that you cannot show was stolen, including shortages found at stocktake;
  • money, and vehicles;
  • property taken from an area open to customers during trading hours by a person who entered as a customer, unless they used force or violence; or
  • contents and equipment left in the open, other than fixed equipment, unless it was in a locked building, glasshouse or container.

Public and Products Liability

This section always applies.

Liability for Injury and Damage

We will pay the amount you become legally liable to pay as compensation for personal injury or property damage that happens during the policy period in Australia and is caused by an occurrence in connection with the nursery business, including by your products.

Who is insured under this section. You; each of your directors, partners and employees while acting for the nursery business; and each owner of the situation that you lease, but only for liability arising from your nursery business, are insured as if each were named in the schedule.

Limit. The most we pay for all compensation arising from one occurrence is the liability limit shown in the schedule. For personal injury and property damage caused by your products, the liability limit is also the most we pay for all occurrences in the policy period.

Defence costs. In addition to the limit, we pay the reasonable legal costs we incur or approve in defending or settling a claim covered by this section, and interest on a judgment up to the date we pay or offer to pay it.

Settlement. We may take over and conduct the defence or settlement of any claim in your name. We may at any time pay you the limit, less any amount already paid, and then we have no further obligation for that occurrence except for defence costs already incurred.

More than one insured. This section applies to each insured as if a separate policy had been issued to each, but the limit is not increased.

Nursery Activities Covered

This section covers liability arising from:

  • selling plant stock, potting mix, soil, mulch and garden supplies by wholesale and retail, subject to the E06 Potting Mix and Soil Products endorsement and the E07 Retail Visitors endorsement where the schedule shows them;
  • delivering plant stock and placing it where the customer asks at the customer's premises;
  • applying chemicals by ground equipment to your own plant stock and premises, by you or an employee;
  • selling at markets, shows and field days, and showing plant stock at them; and
  • the use of forklifts and other machinery that is not a vehicle as a tool of trade at the situation, or on a road while moving between parts of the situation, where the liability is not required by law to be insured under a compulsory third party scheme.

Fire Spreading from the Situation

This section covers your liability for personal injury or property damage caused by a fire that starts at the situation or in the activities of the nursery business and spreads, including a fire lit for burning off, a fire caused by a spark from machinery, and a fire caused by a fault in electrical equipment you own.

Where the law requires a permit to light a fire, or prohibits lighting it on a total fire ban day, you must hold the permit and comply with its conditions and that law. The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies to this requirement.

Property in Your Care

This section covers your liability for property damage to property of others in your care, custody or control in connection with the nursery business, including plant stock you grow or hold for others and a customer's vehicle while you load it, up to $100,000 for one occurrence, or the sub-limit the schedule shows. This sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, the liability limit.

It does not cover property you lease or rent, or property being worked on, other than as the E09 Incidental Landscaping and Installation endorsement provides.

Injury to Your Workers

We do not pay for personal injury to an employee that arises out of their employment, where you are required by law to insure that liability under a workers compensation scheme, or would be but for an exemption.

Contractual Liability

We do not pay for liability you agree to under a contract, except to the extent you would have been liable without the contract, or the liability is assumed under a lease of the situation and arises from the nursery business.

Property You Own

We do not pay for property damage to property you own, or, except as the Property in Your Care extension provides, to property in your care, custody or control.

Vehicles, Aircraft and Watercraft

We do not pay for liability arising from the ownership or use of a vehicle while it is on a public road, or for liability that is required by law to be insured under a compulsory third party scheme. We do not pay for liability arising from the ownership or use of an aircraft, a drone weighing more than 2 kilograms or a watercraft more than 8 metres long, or from aerial spraying.

Gradual Pollution

We do not pay for liability arising from the discharge, dispersal, release or escape of a pollutant, or the cost of removing or cleaning up a pollutant, unless the discharge, dispersal, release or escape is sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected, and happens at a specific time and place during the policy period. Spray drift from applying chemicals by ground equipment that happens at a specific time and place is sudden for the purposes of this exclusion.

Fines and Penalties

We do not pay fines, penalties, or punitive, exemplary or aggravated damages, or any amount that the law does not allow to be insured, including a penalty under a work health and safety law.

Advice for a Fee

We do not pay for liability arising from advice, design or a specification you give for a fee, other than advice incidental to selling your products, such as advice on choosing, planting and caring for plants.

Genetically Modified Organisms

We do not pay for liability arising from a genetically modified organism that you grow, sell or supply, other than as a licence or approval under the Gene Technology Act 2000 (Cth) allows.

Spread of Plant Pests and Diseases

We do not pay for liability arising from the spread of a pest, disease or weed that a biosecurity law requires to be notified, from your plant stock, your products or the situation, unless you did not know and could not reasonably have known that they carried it.

Prohibited Plants

We do not pay for liability arising from selling or supplying a plant whose sale or supply is prohibited by a biosecurity law, or by another law of the place where it was sold or delivered, unless you did not know and could not reasonably have known that it was prohibited.

Landscaping and Work for Others

We do not pay for liability arising from:

  • landscaping, planting, laying turf, installing irrigation or other work you do for others at their premises, other than delivering plant stock and placing it where the customer asks, unless the schedule shows the E09 Incidental Landscaping and Installation endorsement; or
  • applying chemicals for others for reward, unless the schedule shows the E08 Chemical Application for Others endorsement.

Own-Brand Growing Media

Open Days, Workshops and Events

How Claims Are Paid

These provisions apply to every section.

Excess

You pay the excess shown in the schedule for each claim, for the section that applies. An endorsement may set a special excess for the matter it describes; that excess replaces the section's excess for that matter.

One event, one excess. If one event gives rise to claims under more than one section of this policy, we deduct only the highest excess that applies. A hailstorm that damages glasshouses, shade structures and plant stock is one event.

No excess applies to the firefighting costs in the Property Additional Benefits extension.

One Loss, One Section

A loss is paid under one section only, even if more than one section could apply. Where it could be paid under more than one, we pay it under the section that gives you the higher amount, except that:

  • theft is paid under Theft; and
  • property being carried by road is paid under Transit where the schedule shows it.

Reinstatement of Sums Insured

After we pay a claim, the sum insured or limit is reinstated from the date of the damage for the rest of the policy period, without extra premium, except after a total loss of an item that is not replaced, and after a claim under Theft or the products limit in the Liability for Injury and Damage clause.

GST and Sums Insured

Where you are entitled to an input tax credit for the GST on the cost of a repair or replacement, we pay the claim net of that credit. Each sum insured and limit in this policy includes GST to the extent you are not entitled to an input tax credit for it.

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because you run open days, workshops or events for the public at the situation.
