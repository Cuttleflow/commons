Farm Pack Insurance

Farm property, livestock, farm interruption, theft and farm liability for grazing, mixed farming, cropping, dairy, horticulture and viticulture businesses and hobby farms, with the farmhouse, vehicles and personal accident insured under linked policies.

About This Policy

This policy insures a farm business: its farm buildings, farm contents, farm improvements, farm produce and farm machinery, its livestock, its farm income, and its legal liability to others. It is written for grazing, mixed farming, broadacre cropping, dairy, horticulture and viticulture enterprises, and for hobby farms and lifestyle blocks.

It has nine sections. Farm Property and Farm Liability always apply. The other sections apply only if the schedule shows them.

The farmhouse and other dwellings, cottages rented to employees or tenants, registered vehicles, accidental damage to high-value self-propelled machinery, and personal accident and illness cover for you are not part of this policy. They may be insured under separate policies, which the schedule may show as linked to this policy. Crop hail, multi-peril crop and livestock mortality insurance for stud animals are specialist covers and are not part of this policy unless an endorsement says so. This policy does not provide workers compensation insurance.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

Important Information

Who issues this policy. The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

Who this policy is for. This wording is written for farm businesses that buy it for the business. Its sections are farm property, livestock, business interruption, theft, liability, machinery breakdown, transit and tax audit covers, which are not among the kinds of insurance the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) and its regulations treat as retail when bought for a small business. Most buyers will therefore be wholesale clients, and this wording is not a Product Disclosure Statement. The farmhouse and its contents, a registered vehicle of up to 2 tonnes carrying capacity, and personal accident and illness cover are retail kinds of insurance. They are not insured by this policy: a linked policy that insures them has its own Product Disclosure Statement and Target Market Determination.

Who is insured. You are the person, partnership, company or trust named in the schedule. The Liability for Injury and Damage clause says who else is insured under Farm Liability.

Your duty when you apply. Before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated, you have the duty of disclosure in s.21 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). You must tell us every matter you know, or that a reasonable person in your circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we waive. If you do not comply, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

If this policy is a consumer insurance contract under that Act, you instead have a duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us, and the Act sets out what we may do if you do not.

Your enterprises matter. We set the cover, the premium and the endorsements by the enterprises the farm business carries on and the share of farm income each brings in. Tell us every enterprise, any contracting you do for others, whether you spray chemicals, take in other owners' livestock on agistment, have paying guests or visitors who ride horses, and whether you sell produce direct to the public. Tell us during the policy period when the farm business changes, as the Tell Us About Changes obligation requires.

Flood and bushfire. Flood is not covered unless the E01 Flood Cover endorsement is shown in the schedule. Damage caused by bushfire is covered, and a special excess may apply under the E11 Bushfire Special Excess endorsement. Cover for bushfire and flood may not start while an emergency warning is in force for the farm location, as the Emergency Warnings at the Start of Cover condition explains.

What this policy does not replace. This policy does not provide workers compensation insurance, which each state and territory requires an employer to hold through its own scheme. It does not insure the farmhouse, your vehicles on the road, crops while they are growing, land, pasture or water, or the cost of drought.

Cooling-off period. You may cancel this policy within 21 days of the date it starts, and within 21 days of each renewal, by telling us in writing. If you have not made a claim, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover. After that, you may still cancel under the Cancellation condition.

Commission and fees. The schedule or the quote shows any commission or fee paid to your broker in connection with this policy.

Financial Claims Scheme. If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.

Definitions

These words have the meanings below wherever they appear in this policy, with or without capital letters.

Agistment

Grazing or feeding livestock owned by someone else on land of the farm business, for a fee or other reward. Taking in livestock on agistment is part of the farm business only where the schedule shows the E13 Agistment of Other Owners' Livestock endorsement.

Bushfire

A fire in grass, crops, scrub, bush, forest or other vegetation, including one that started on the farm location.

Damage

Sudden and accidental physical loss of, or physical damage to, property. Damaged has a corresponding meaning.

Employee

A person under a contract of service or apprenticeship with you, and a person you engage as a casual, seasonal or contract farm worker who works under your direction. It does not include a contractor carrying on their own business.

Endorsement

A written change to this policy that is shown in the schedule by its code, together with its variables.

Event

One incident, or a series of incidents arising from one cause, during the policy period. A bushfire, a storm or a flood is one event for all damage it causes within any 72 consecutive hours.

Excess

The amount you bear of each claim, shown in the schedule for the section or set by an endorsement.

Fabric structure

A building or covering whose roof or walls are wholly or mainly fabric, plastic film, shade cloth or a similar flexible material, including a hay cover, a poly tunnel and a fabric shelter.

Farm buildings

Buildings and structures at the farm location used for the farm business, including machinery, hay, shearing and implement sheds, workshops, stables, dairies, silos, cool rooms, yards, loading ramps and their fixtures. It does not include a dwelling, a building used as a home, or land.

Farm business

The farming enterprises shown in the schedule, carried on at the farm location, and these incidental activities: selling your own farm produce at the farm gate, at markets and at shows and field days; taking part in shows and field days; and incidental contracting.

Farm contents

Property used in the farm business that you own or are responsible for, other than farm buildings, farm improvements, farm produce, farm machinery, livestock, money and vehicles, including tools, spare parts, fuel, chemicals, fertiliser, seed, stock feed, veterinary supplies, saddlery and office equipment.

Farm improvements

Fencing, gates, grids, water tanks, troughs, pumps, pipes, windmills, bores and their equipment, irrigation equipment, solar systems, power poles and lines owned by you, and other fixed structures at the farm location that are not farm buildings.

Farm income

The money the farm business earns from selling farm produce and livestock, from agistment and from incidental contracting, less what it spends buying livestock, produce and stock feed for resale or fattening.

Farm location

The land shown in the schedule, and any other land in Australia you own, lease or share-farm for the farm business and tell us about.

Farm machinery

Tractors, harvesters, sprayers, implements, motorcycles, all-terrain and side-by-side vehicles and other machinery used in the farm business, that is not registered for use on a public road, or is registered only under a farm machinery or conditional registration scheme.

Farm produce

Hay, silage, straw, grain, seed, fodder, wool, mohair, fruit, vegetables, grapes and other produce of the farm business that has been harvested or shorn and is in storage at the farm location, and stock feed you bought for the farm business.

Flood

The covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from the normal confines of any lake, river, creek or other natural watercourse, whether or not altered or modified, or any reservoir, canal or dam.

Incidental contracting

Work the farm business does for other farmers or landholders for reward with its own farm machinery and employees, such as harvesting, hay making, ploughing, sowing, fencing, livestock handling and ground spraying, where the income from it is no more than the share of gross farm turnover shown in the E05 Incidental Contracting endorsement.

Indemnity period

The period beginning on the date of the damage and lasting no longer than the period shown in the schedule, during which the results of the farm business are affected by the damage.

Insured event

An event listed in the Insured Events clause.

Linked policy

A policy issued by us to you for the farm business or the farm household, such as a home, landlord, motor, plant and equipment or personal accident policy, that the schedule shows as linked to this policy.

Livestock

Sheep, cattle, goats, alpacas, llamas, pigs, deer, horses and poultry kept for the farm business at the farm location, and working dogs. It does not include animals kept only as pets, or animals under specified cover in the E08 Stud and High-Value Animals endorsement except as that endorsement provides.

Occurrence

An event, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same conditions, that results in personal injury or property damage that you did not expect or intend. All personal injury and property damage arising from one source or original cause is one occurrence.

Personal injury

Bodily injury, death, sickness, disease, disability, shock, fright, mental anguish or mental injury, and false arrest, wrongful detention, malicious prosecution, defamation and invasion of privacy.

Policy period

The period shown in the schedule, or a shorter period if this policy is cancelled.

Pollutant

A solid, liquid, gaseous or thermal irritant or contaminant, including smoke, vapour, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, fertiliser, pesticide, herbicide, effluent, manure and waste.

Property damage

Physical damage to or destruction of tangible property, including the loss of its use, and the loss of use of tangible property that is not physically damaged.

Sum insured

The amount shown in the schedule for an item or section, which is the most we pay for that item or section for one event, unless this policy says otherwise.

Theft

Taking property dishonestly without the owner's consent, with the intention of permanently depriving the owner of it.

Vehicle

A vehicle registered, or required by law to be registered, for use on a public road, other than under a farm machinery or conditional registration scheme, and a trailer, caravan, aircraft, drone weighing more than 2 kilograms, or watercraft.

We, Us, Our

The insurer named in the schedule.

Working dog

A dog kept and trained to work livestock for the farm business.

You, Your

The person, partnership, company or trust named in the schedule as the insured.

Your products

Anything grown, raised, harvested, produced, sold, supplied or delivered by the farm business, including livestock and farm produce, after it has left your possession or control.

Farm Property

This section always applies.

Farm Property Cover

We will pay for damage to farm buildings, farm contents, farm improvements, farm produce and farm machinery at the farm location, caused by an insured event during the policy period. Farm machinery is also covered while it is anywhere in Australia for the farm business.

The most we pay for one event is the farm property sum insured shown in the schedule, plus the additional benefits in the Farm Property Additional Benefits extension. The schedule shows each class of farm property's share of that sum insured, and the share is the most we pay for that class in one event, unless the schedule shows a separate sum insured for an item.

Fencing and Farm Improvements

We pay for damage to fencing and other farm improvements by an insured event, including damage by livestock that are escaping a fire or a storm. Fencing is insured on a first loss basis: its share of the farm property sum insured is the most we pay for fencing in one event, and the Farm Buildings Underinsurance provision does not apply to it.

We also pay the reasonable cost of temporary fencing to keep livestock in while damaged fencing is repaired, up to $10,000 for one event.

Farm Produce and Seasonal Increase

Farm produce is insured at its market value at the farm location at the time of the damage, less the costs you would have incurred to sell it that you do not incur.

During the three months after the harvest or shearing of the farm business's main crop or clip, we increase the farm produce share of the farm property sum insured by 25% to allow for the larger quantity in storage. Spontaneous combustion of farm produce is covered as fire, subject to the E07 Hay and Fodder Storage endorsement where the schedule shows it.

Farm Buildings Underinsurance

This provision applies only to farm buildings, and only to a claim of more than $20,000. If, at the time of the damage, the farm buildings share of the farm property sum insured is less than 80% of the cost of rebuilding all the farm buildings, we pay only the proportion of the claim that the farm buildings share bears to 80% of that cost.

Worked example: the farm buildings share is $600,000; rebuilding all the farm buildings would cost $1,000,000; a fire causes $300,000 of damage to farm buildings. 80% of $1,000,000 is $800,000. We pay $600,000 divided by $800,000, times $300,000, which is $225,000, before the excess.

Farm Property Exclusions

In addition to the General Exclusions section, under Farm Property we do not pay for:

  • flood, unless the schedule shows the E01 Flood Cover endorsement;
  • theft (see Theft);
  • breakdown of machinery or electronic equipment (see Machinery Breakdown and Electronic Equipment);
  • livestock (see Livestock);
  • vehicles, and damage to farm machinery caused by its own operation, collision or overturning, or by the work it is doing;
  • crops, pasture and trees while growing, land, soil, roads, dams, levees and water in dams, except as the Farm Property Additional Benefits extension provides;
  • a fabric structure damaged by storm, unless the schedule shows the E02 Fabric Structures endorsement;
  • property while it is being carried by road (see Transit);
  • wear and tear, rust, corrosion, rot, mould, gradual deterioration, insects, vermin, and damage caused by livestock other than as the Fencing and Farm Improvements clause provides;
  • erosion, subsidence, landslip, shrinkage or expansion of the ground, unless directly caused by an insured event;
  • damage caused by water entering a farm building through an opening that was not made by the storm or by an insured event; or
  • farm produce damaged by heating, fermentation or moisture, other than spontaneous combustion.

Livestock

This section applies if the schedule shows it.

Death of Livestock

We will pay for the death of livestock at the farm location during the policy period caused by:

  • fire, including bushfire, lightning or explosion;
  • earthquake;
  • electrocution;
  • the collapse of a farm building or structure; or
  • impact by a vehicle or an aircraft, other than one you own or operate.

We also pay where a veterinary surgeon, or a person authorised by law to do so where no veterinary surgeon is available, destroys an animal for humane reasons because of an injury caused by one of these causes.

The most we pay for one event is the livestock sum insured shown in the schedule.

Livestock Valuation

We pay the market value of each animal at the farm gate at the time of its death, based on recent saleyard and over-the-hooks prices for animals of the same kind, age and condition in the region, less the costs of selling the animal that you do not incur. The most we pay for any one animal is $5,000, or the amount the schedule shows, unless the animal is specified under the E08 Stud and High-Value Animals endorsement.

The Farm Buildings Underinsurance provision does not apply to livestock.

Livestock Exclusions

In addition to the General Exclusions section, under Livestock we do not pay for death caused by or arising from:

  • disease, illness, parasites, infection, poisoning or contamination, other than by smoke from a fire;
  • drought, starvation, thirst, or exposure to heat or cold;
  • pregnancy, giving birth, castration, mulesing, surgery or any other procedure;
  • theft, straying, attack by an animal or accidental death, unless the schedule shows Livestock Extended Cover;
  • livestock while being carried by road (see Transit);
  • slaughter or destruction by order of a government or public authority, including under a biosecurity law; or
  • livestock you are holding on agistment, unless the schedule shows the E13 Agistment of Other Owners' Livestock endorsement.

Livestock Extended Cover

This section applies only if the schedule shows it and also shows Livestock. It adds to the causes covered by the Death of Livestock clause, within the same livestock sum insured.

Theft, Attack and Accidental Death

We will also pay, under the terms of Livestock, for:

  • theft of livestock from the farm location;
  • the death of livestock, or their destruction for humane reasons, after attack by dogs, foxes, dingoes or other wild animals; and
  • the accidental death of livestock from a cause not excluded, including drowning in a flood or a dam, smothering, falling, becoming trapped or entangled, being struck by a vehicle on a road next to the farm location, and lightning or storm.

Livestock Theft Limit

The most we pay for theft of livestock in the policy period is 25% of the livestock sum insured, or the theft limit the schedule shows.

We pay for theft only where you report it to the police within 7 days of discovering it and give us the police report number. You must also give us the records you keep under the National Livestock Identification System or your state's brands and identification law that show the number of animals before and after the theft. Where those records cannot show the theft, we pay only for the animals your other reasonable evidence shows were stolen. The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies to these requirements.

Working Dogs

We pay for the death or theft of a working dog from any cause, including being struck by a vehicle, snake bite and heat stroke while working, other than disease, old age, an act of yours, or a cause excluded by the General Exclusions section, up to $3,000 for any one dog and $10,000 in the policy period. We also pay reasonable veterinary costs to treat a working dog injured by such a cause, up to $1,500 for any one dog, within those limits.

Livestock Extended Cover Exclusions

Under Livestock Extended Cover, the Livestock Exclusions exclusion applies, other than its exclusion of theft, attack and accidental death, and other than to working dogs, which the Working Dogs clause covers on its own terms. We do not pay for:

  • livestock that cannot be accounted for and that you cannot show were stolen, including shortages found at mustering or counting;
  • theft by you, a member of your household, an employee, a sharefarmer or a person on agistment with you;
  • death from an attack by your own dogs, unless you did not know and could not reasonably have known that a dog was attacking livestock; or
  • an animal more than 12 years old, other than a working dog or a horse.

Farm Interruption

This section applies if the schedule shows it.

Loss of Farm Income

If damage covered under Farm Property or Livestock interrupts the farm business during the policy period, we will pay during the indemnity period:

  • the reduction in farm income caused by the damage;
  • the increased cost of working: the extra costs you reasonably incur to reduce that reduction, such as buying fodder, agisting livestock elsewhere, hiring machinery and buildings, and carting water, up to the reduction in farm income they avoid; and
  • the loss of agistment income where a bushfire damages pasture or fencing at the farm location that you were using for agistment.

Where a bushfire destroys pasture at the farm location, we pay the increased cost of working to feed and agist your livestock even though pasture is not insured property.

The most we pay for all claims arising from one event is the farm income sum insured shown in the schedule. The indemnity period is 12 months unless the schedule shows otherwise.

How We Calculate Loss of Farm Income

We compare the farm income during the indemnity period with the farm income the farm business would have earned during it if the damage had not happened. To work out what it would have earned, we use the farm income for the same period of the year before, adjusted for the seasonal conditions, prices, production and other circumstances that would have affected the farm business during the indemnity period, including drought and market prices.

We deduct the costs that stop or reduce because of the damage. We do not pay for any loss caused by drought, market prices or seasonal conditions; they are taken into account only to work out what the farm income would have been.

The Farm Buildings Underinsurance provision does not apply to this section. The farm income sum insured is the most we pay, and we do not reduce a claim because it is less than the farm income.

Farm Interruption Exclusions

In addition to the General Exclusions section, under Farm Interruption we do not pay for:

  • loss caused by flood, unless the schedule shows the E01 Flood Cover endorsement;
  • loss caused by drought, disease in livestock or plants, a biosecurity order, quarantine, a fall in market prices, or the loss of a market or a contract;
  • loss after the indemnity period ends;
  • loss caused by damage that is not covered by Farm Property or Livestock, other than the bushfire benefits in the Loss of Farm Income clause; or
  • fines, penalties or damages you must pay for not completing a contract.

Theft

This section applies if the schedule shows it.

Theft of Farm Property

We will pay for theft of farm contents, farm produce, farm machinery and farm improvements from the farm location during the policy period, and for damage caused during the theft or an attempted theft.

The most we pay for all theft in the policy period is $25,000, or the theft limit the schedule shows. The most we pay for any one item is $10,000 unless the schedule shows the item with its value.

The Farm Property Basis of Settlement provision decides how we settle.

Theft Reporting

Report a theft to the police within 48 hours of discovering it, and give us the police report number. For farm machinery worth more than $10,000, keep a record of its serial or identification numbers and give it to us when you claim. The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies to these requirements.

Theft Exclusions

In addition to the General Exclusions section, under Theft we do not pay for:

  • theft of livestock (see Livestock Extended Cover);
  • theft by you, a member of your household, an employee or a sharefarmer, or with their knowledge or consent;
  • property that cannot be accounted for and that you cannot show was stolen, including shortages found at stocktake;
  • fuel taken from a tank, drum or machine that was not locked;
  • money, and vehicles; or
  • property left in the open away from the farm buildings and farm improvements, other than farm machinery, farm produce and farm improvements themselves, unless it was in a locked building or container.

Farm Liability

This section always applies.

Liability for Injury and Damage

We will pay the amount you become legally liable to pay as compensation for personal injury or property damage that happens during the policy period in Australia and is caused by an occurrence in connection with the farm business, including by your products.

Who is insured under this section. You; each member of your household; each of your employees and sharefarmers while working in the farm business; and each owner of land you lease or share-farm, but only for liability arising from your farm business, are insured as if each were named in the schedule.

Limit. The most we pay for all compensation arising from one occurrence is the liability limit shown in the schedule. For personal injury and property damage caused by your products, the liability limit is also the most we pay for all occurrences in the policy period.

Defence costs. In addition to the limit, we pay the reasonable legal costs we incur or approve in defending or settling a claim covered by this section, and interest on a judgment up to the date we pay or offer to pay it.

Settlement. We may take over and conduct the defence or settlement of any claim in your name. We may at any time pay you the limit, less any amount already paid, and then we have no further obligation for that occurrence except for defence costs already incurred.

More than one insured. This section applies to each insured as if a separate policy had been issued to each, but the limit is not increased.

Straying Livestock

This section covers your liability for personal injury or property damage caused by livestock that stray from the farm location onto a road or a neighbour's land, including a collision with a vehicle on a road.

Fire Spreading from the Farm

This section covers your liability for personal injury or property damage caused by a fire that starts at the farm location or in the farm business's activities and spreads, including a fire lit for burning off, a fire caused by a spark from farm machinery, and a fire caused by a fault in electrical equipment you own.

Where the law requires a permit to light a fire, or prohibits lighting it on a total fire ban day, you must hold the permit and comply with its conditions and that law. The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies to this requirement.

Property in Your Care

This section covers your liability for property damage to property of others in your care, custody or control in connection with the farm business, including machinery you borrow or hire and livestock you hold, up to $100,000 for one occurrence and $10,000 for any one animal, or the sub-limits the schedule shows. This sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, the liability limit.

It does not cover property you lease or rent, other than machinery, or property being worked on, other than as the E05 Incidental Contracting endorsement provides.

Farm Activities Covered

This section covers liability arising from:

  • ground spraying of chemicals for the farm business by you or an employee, subject to the E06 Ground Spraying Conditions endorsement where the schedule shows it;
  • incidental contracting, subject to the E05 Incidental Contracting endorsement;
  • selling your own farm produce at the farm gate, at markets and at shows and field days, subject to the E12 Farm Gate Sales and Markets endorsement where the schedule shows it;
  • showing livestock and farm produce at shows and field days; and
  • the use of farm machinery as a tool of trade at the farm location, or while crossing or travelling on a road between parts of the farm location, where the liability is not required by law to be insured under a compulsory third party scheme.

Injury to Your Workers

How Claims Are Paid

These provisions apply to every section.

Excess

You pay the excess shown in the schedule for each claim, for the section that applies. An endorsement may set a special excess for the matter it describes; that excess replaces the section's excess for that matter.

One event, one excess. If one event gives rise to claims under more than one section of this policy, we deduct only the highest excess that applies. A bushfire that damages farm buildings, fencing, farm produce and livestock is one event.

Linked policies. If one event also gives rise to a claim under a linked policy, and the excess under that policy is deducted, we do not deduct an excess under this policy for that event, unless ours is higher, in which case we deduct only the difference.

No excess applies to Tax Audit, or to the firefighting costs in the Farm Property Additional Benefits extension.

One Loss, One Section

A loss is paid under one section only, even if more than one section could apply. Where it could be paid under more than one, we pay it under the section that gives you the higher amount, except that:

  • theft is paid under Theft, or for livestock under Livestock Extended Cover;
  • property and livestock being carried by road are paid under Transit where the schedule shows it; and
  • a loss covered by a linked policy is paid under that policy where it insures the property, such as a vehicle under a linked motor policy or self-propelled machinery under a linked plant and equipment policy, and this policy pays only what that policy does not pay for property this policy insures.

Reinstatement of Sums Insured

After we pay a claim, the sum insured or limit is reinstated from the date of the damage for the rest of the policy period, without extra premium, except after a total loss of an item that is not replaced, after a claim under Theft, Tax Audit or the products limit in the Liability for Injury and Damage clause, and except as the Livestock Theft Limit provision provides.

GST and Sums Insured

Where you are entitled to an input tax credit for the GST on the cost of a repair or replacement, we pay the claim net of that credit. Each sum insured and limit in this policy includes GST to the extent you are not entitled to an input tax credit for it.

Other Insurance

If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows. This does not apply where the schedule or an endorsement states that this policy is excess of a specified underlying policy, in which case this policy pays only after the limit of that specified policy is exhausted. We pay a claim for that property to you and the interested party as their interests appear, or as you and they direct in writing. An act or omission of yours does not reduce what we pay the interested party for its interest, unless the interested party knew of it and did not tell us.

Schedule variables: interested party; the property in which it has an interest.

E10 Lloyd's Underwriters

Where the insurer is one or more syndicates at Lloyd's, each syndicate is liable only for its own proportion of any claim, and not for another syndicate's proportion. You may serve proceedings on the Lloyd's General Representative in Australia shown in the schedule, who will accept service for the syndicates. Complaints may also be referred to Lloyd's Australia, and then to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority where its rules allow.

Schedule variables: the syndicates and their proportions; the Lloyd's General Representative in Australia.

E11 Bushfire Special Excess

The bushfire excess applies to each event of damage or death caused by bushfire, under every section, instead of the section's excess. The Excess provision still applies, so one bushfire event bears one bushfire excess.

Schedule variables: bushfire excess (default: $5,000).

E12 Farm Gate Sales and Markets

When you sell farm produce direct to the public, at the farm gate, at markets or at shows and field days, you must hold any registration or licence that the food safety law of the state or territory requires for the produce you sell, and label it as that law requires.

The special excess applies to each claim for personal injury caused by produce you sold direct to the public. If a condition of this endorsement was not met, the Insurance Contracts Act condition applies.

Schedule variables: special excess (default: $1,000).

E13 Agistment of Other Owners' Livestock

Taking in livestock on agistment is part of the farm business. The Livestock Exclusions exclusion does not exclude livestock you hold on agistment, and they are insured under Livestock for their owners' interest, within the livestock sum insured, where the agistment agreement makes you responsible for them.

The Property in Your Care extension applies to livestock on agistment up to the agistment sub-limit for one occurrence and the agistment sub-limit for any one animal, instead of its own sub-limits. You must have a written agistment agreement with each owner that states the number and kind of animals and who is responsible for them.

Schedule variables: agistment sub-limit for one occurrence (default: $100,000); agistment sub-limit for any one animal (default: $10,000).

E14 Northern Australia Cyclone

For a farm location north of the 26th parallel of south latitude, damage and death caused by a cyclone, and by storm surge or flood caused by a cyclone, is subject to the cyclone excess instead of the section's excess. We do not pay for damage by a cyclone that is caused during the first 72 hours after this policy is first entered into, if a cyclone watch or warning that includes the farm location was in force when it was, unless this policy replaces, without a break, cover for the same property.

Flood caused by a cyclone is covered only if the schedule shows the E01 Flood Cover endorsement.

Schedule variables: cyclone excess (default: $10,000).

E15 Firefighting Equipment

You have told us that during the fire danger season you keep at the farm location a firefighting unit with a pump and a tank holding at least the minimum tank capacity of water, in working order and ready to use.
