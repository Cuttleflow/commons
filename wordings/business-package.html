A modular package for Australian small and medium businesses: property, business interruption, crime, glass, liability, equipment, general property, transit, tax audit, cyber and management liability.

About This Policy

This is a modular business insurance package for small and medium enterprises. It has eleven sections. Four sections are written for each Situation (a location where the Business operates). Seven sections are written once for the whole Business.

A section provides cover only where the Schedule shows it as insured, and a section written for each Situation provides cover at a Situation only where the Schedule shows it as insured for that Situation. The Policy is made up of this wording, the Schedule and any Endorsement. Where the Schedule and this wording differ, the Schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

Who This Policy Is For

This Policy is designed for Australian small and medium businesses. It is not a consumer insurance contract: it is not entered into wholly or predominantly for personal, domestic or household purposes, and the duty of disclosure in the Duty of Disclosure condition applies.

Where this Policy is issued to a retail client within the meaning of s 761G of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), the issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a target market determination. This wording is not a Product Disclosure Statement. [WP REVIEW: confirm the retail/wholesale posture for the package roll-up, and whether the Cyber and Management Liability sections change it.]

Notice Under s 40 of the Insurance Contracts Act

Tax Audit, Management Liability and the liability cover in the Cyber section respond only to matters first made or notified during the Period of Insurance.

If, during the Period of Insurance, you give us written notice of facts that might give rise to a Claim or an Audit notice, s 40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) means we cannot refuse to pay a later Claim or Audit notice arising from those facts only because it was made after the Period of Insurance ended. The Notice of Circumstances condition sets out how to give that notice.

Schedule

(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The Schedule shows:

  • The named insured, ABN and any subsidiaries named
  • The Business, and its activities as ANZSIC 2006 classes
  • The Period of Insurance
  • Each Situation, and for each Situation the sections insured there, the property insured, each Sum insured, the Excess, and for Business Interruption the gross profit Sum insured, indemnity period and any waiting period
  • The sections insured for the whole Business, with each section limit and Excess
  • Sub-limits and other values that differ from the defaults in the Default Schedule Values
  • The Retroactive date for Cyber and Management Liability, and the pending and prior date
  • The declared figures used for rating, including turnover, employees and annual carryings
  • The premium for each section, with stamp duty, levies and GST
  • The State or Territory whose law governs this Policy (see the Governing Law and Jurisdiction condition)
  • The Endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
  • Interested parties

Definitions

These words have the meanings below wherever they appear in this Policy, the Schedule or an Endorsement, with a capital letter. Where a definition says it applies in one section only, it has that meaning in that section, and the word has its ordinary meaning elsewhere. The singular includes the plural.

Audit notice

A written notice from the Australian Taxation Office or a State or Territory revenue office that it will conduct an audit, review or investigation of the Business or of a director's tax affairs relating to the Business.

Breakdown

The actual breaking, distortion, burning out, or electrical or mechanical failure of a part of an item while in use or being tested after repair, requiring repair or replacement before the item can resume normal operation.

Building

The building at the Situation, including foundations, fixtures, fittings and fixed services (electrical, plumbing, heating, cooling, fire protection, lifts and escalators), to the extent you are responsible for insuring them.

Business

The business described in the Schedule.

Claim

In the Cyber section: a written demand for compensation, a civil proceeding, or a regulatory investigation, against you.

In the Management Liability section: a written demand for compensation, a civil, criminal, administrative or regulatory proceeding, or a formal investigation, against an insured for a Wrongful act.

The word is used with a capital letter only in those two sections and in the General Conditions section.

Computer system

Computer hardware, software, networks and connected devices owned by, leased to or operated for you, other than a system operated by a third-party service provider for many customers.

Contents

Furniture, furnishings, fixtures, fittings, equipment, tools and other tangible property at the Situation that you own or are responsible for, but not Stock, Money, glass, vehicles, or property more specifically insured under another section.

Cyber event

Unauthorised access, a malicious code attack, a denial-of-service attack, or a human error or system failure affecting your Computer system or Data.

Damage

Physical loss, destruction or damage. Damaged has a corresponding meaning.

Data

Information held electronically on your Computer system.

Discovery

In the Comprehensive Crime section: the time a Responsible person first becomes aware of facts that would cause a reasonable person to believe that a loss covered by that section has been or will be incurred, even if the amount or details are not yet known.

Dishonest act

A dishonest or fraudulent act committed with the intent to cause you a loss and to obtain a financial benefit, other than salary, fees, commissions or bonuses, for the Employee or another person.

Employee

A person working for you under a contract of employment (permanent, part-time, casual or seasonal), including volunteers, work experience students and labour hire workers under your direct supervision.

Employment claim

A Claim alleging wrongful dismissal, discrimination, harassment, bullying, adverse action, breach of an employment contract, or another employment-related Wrongful act.

Endorsement

A document issued by us that changes this Policy and is listed in the Schedule.

Excess

The amount you bear as the first part of each claim or insured loss, as shown in the Schedule for the relevant section.

Extended reporting period

The period after the Period of Insurance ends during which a Claim may be first made and notified, as the Management Liability Claims-made Conditions condition provides.

Extortion threat

A threat to damage, disclose, encrypt or withhold your Data or your Computer system unless a ransom is paid.

Final adjudication

A final judgment or determination by a court, tribunal or arbitrator after any appeal, or after the time to appeal has expired.

Insured event

An event that the relevant section covers.

Insured person

A past, present or future director, officer or Employee of the Business, while acting in that capacity.

Interested party

A mortgagee, financier, lessor or other party whose interest in insured property is noted in the Schedule.

Loss

In the Management Liability section: compensation, settlements, judgments and defence costs, and fines and penalties only as the Statutory Insurability exclusion permits. Loss does not include tax, the cost of complying with an order, or matters uninsurable at law.

Money

Current coins, banknotes, cheques, money orders, postal notes, unused postage stamps, gift cards and vouchers, belonging to you or held by you in trust.

Occurrence

An event, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same conditions, that results in Personal injury or Property damage neither expected nor intended by you. All Personal injury and Property damage from one source or original cause is one Occurrence.

Period of Insurance

The period shown in the Schedule.

Personal injury

Bodily injury, death, illness, disease or shock, and mental injury arising from them, and also false arrest, wrongful detention, malicious prosecution, defamation and invasion of privacy.

Premises

The Building and land at a Situation.

Property damage

Physical damage to or destruction of tangible property, including resulting loss of use, and loss of use of tangible property that is not physically damaged.

Responsible person

A director, partner, principal, chief financial officer or office manager of the Business, or, where the Business has none of these, its most senior officer.

Retroactive date

The date shown in the Schedule as the retroactive date for the Cyber section or the Management Liability section. Where no date is shown, it is the inception date of the first policy we issued to you that included that section, without a break in cover.

Schedule

The schedule we issue for this Policy, as amended by any Endorsement.

Single loss

All loss resulting from the same, similar or related acts or events, or involving the same person or persons, whenever they occurred.

Situation

Each location shown in the Schedule.

Stock

Merchandise, raw materials, goods in process of manufacture, and finished goods that you own or are responsible for, at the Situation.

Sum insured

The amount shown in the Schedule for an item of property or for a section.

Theft

Taking property without the owner's consent with the intention of permanently depriving the owner of it.

Third party

A person other than you, a director or an Employee, acting without your collusion.

Unauthorised access

Access to or use of your Computer system by a person without your authority, or by an authorised person beyond the authority given, including an Employee who exceeds their authority.

Unoccupied

The Premises have not been attended by you or an Employee for more than the number of consecutive days shown in the Schedule.

We, us, our

The insurer named in the Schedule.

Wrongful act

An actual or alleged breach of duty, breach of trust, neglect, error, misstatement, misleading statement, omission, or breach of warranty of authority by an insured in that capacity.

You, your

The person or entity named in the Schedule as the insured, and any subsidiary named in the Schedule.

Your products

Goods that you sell, supply, manufacture, install, repair, service or deliver, including their packaging and instructions.

General Exclusions

These exclusions apply to every section unless the exclusion or a section says otherwise. The Exclusion Applicability Table shows which exclusions apply to which section; where it shows that an exclusion does not apply to a section, it does not apply to that section.

War

Loss, Damage, liability or cost caused by war, invasion, hostilities, civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection or military or usurped power. For the Cyber section, the War and State Cyber Operations exclusion applies instead of this exclusion.

Terrorism

Loss, Damage, liability or cost caused by an act of terrorism. For the Property Damage section and the Business Interruption section, this exclusion does not apply to the extent the Terrorism Insurance Act 2003 (Cth) provides that it has no effect for a declared terrorist incident.

Nuclear

Loss, Damage, liability or cost caused by nuclear reaction, nuclear radiation or radioactive contamination.

Asbestos

Loss, Damage, liability or cost arising from asbestos in any form.

Mould

Mould, mildew or fungi, unless they result directly from an Insured event under the Property Damage section and are discovered within the number of hours after that event shown in the Schedule.

Pollution

Pollution or contamination, unless sudden, accidental and unintended and resulting directly from an Insured event. The Pollution (Liability) exclusion applies to the Public and Products Liability section instead of this exclusion.

Electronic Data

Loss of, or Damage to, electronic data or software, except as covered under the Property Damage section (rewriting of records), the Business Interruption section (to the extent the interruption follows Damage covered under Property Damage), the Equipment Damage section (data restoration) and the Cyber section.

Cyber

Loss, Damage, liability or cost caused by a Cyber event. This exclusion does not apply to:

  • physical Damage to insured property caused by fire or explosion resulting from a Cyber event;
  • the Computer Fraud clause or the Social Engineering Fraud clause;
  • the Public and Products Liability section;
  • the Cyber section; or
  • the Management Liability section.

Gradual Causes

Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, oxidation, corrosion or inherent vice. This exclusion does not apply to resulting Damage from a sudden Insured event.

Deliberate Acts

Loss or Damage that you deliberately cause or allow. For the Comprehensive Crime section, this exclusion applies to the acts of you and your directors only, not to a Dishonest act of an Employee covered under the Employee Dishonesty clause. For the Management Liability section, the Conduct exclusion applies instead of this exclusion.

Unoccupied Premises

Loss or Damage at Premises that are Unoccupied when the loss occurs, unless we have agreed in writing to continue cover. This exclusion does not apply where the Premises are partly occupied, or to the Public and Products Liability section, the Tax Audit section, the Cyber section or the Management Liability section.

Known Defects

A defect existing before the Period of Insurance started that you knew about, or that a reasonable person in your circumstances would have known about.

Confiscation

Confiscation, nationalisation or requisition by a public authority, except destruction ordered to prevent the spread of fire.

Consequential Loss

Consequential loss of any kind, except as covered under the Business Interruption section, the Public and Products Liability section, the Cyber section and the Management Liability section.

General Conditions

These conditions apply to every section.

The Insurance Contracts Act

Nothing in this Policy excludes, restricts or modifies the operation of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Where this Policy is inconsistent with that Act, the Act applies. In particular, where a condition of this Policy would allow us to refuse a claim because of an act or omission after the Policy was entered into, s 54 of that Act applies: we may not refuse to pay the claim only because of that act or omission, and our liability is reduced only by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced, unless the Act allows otherwise.

Duty of Disclosure

Before you enter into, renew, extend, vary or reinstate this Policy, you have a duty under s 21 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) to tell us everything you know, or that a reasonable person in the circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know in the ordinary course of our business, or that we tell you we do not need to know.

If you do not comply with this duty, our remedies are limited to those in ss 26 to 28 of that Act. We may avoid this Policy only where the non-disclosure or misrepresentation was fraudulent.

For the Management Liability section, as between us and each Insured person, we assess the knowledge and disclosure of each Insured person separately. We will not reduce our liability to an Insured person under s 28(3) of that Act because of another person's non-disclosure or misrepresentation that the Insured person did not know about.

Premium

You must pay the premium by the date shown on our invoice. If the premium is not paid, we may cancel this Policy under the Cancellation condition.

Cancellation

You may cancel this Policy at any time by telling us in writing. We may cancel this Policy only on a ground permitted by s 60 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), by giving you written notice under s 59 of that Act. Unless that Act requires a shorter period, cancellation by us takes effect 30 days after we give notice. On cancellation by either party we refund the premium for the unexpired Period of Insurance on a pro rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. Cancellation does not affect any claim, Claim, Audit notice or circumstance notified to us before cancellation takes effect.

Making a Claim

When you become aware of loss, Damage, a Claim, an Audit notice or a circumstance that may give rise to a claim under this Policy, you must:

  • tell us as soon as reasonably practicable, and report theft, malicious damage or a Cyber event involving a crime to the police;
  • take reasonable steps to prevent further loss;
  • give us the information and assistance we reasonably ask for;
  • not admit liability, settle or make any payment for a claim against you under the Public and Products Liability section, the Cyber section or the Management Liability section without our written agreement, which we will not unreasonably withhold; and
  • keep damaged property for our inspection where it is reasonable to do so.

The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies to each of these requirements.

Excess

You bear the Excess for each claim or insured loss. If one event gives rise to a claim under more than one section, you bear only the highest applicable Excess.

Limits and More Than One Section

Each section limit and Sum insured is the most we pay under that section, as stated in the section. Defence costs under the Public and Products Liability section are payable in addition to the limit. Section limits do not share an overall aggregate. If one loss or Claim is covered under more than one section, we pay it under one section only: the section that gives you the greater amount. That section's limit, Excess and terms then apply.

Other Insurance

If another policy covers the same loss, you must tell us. We will contribute rateably with the other insurer, subject to the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), including s 45. Between the Comprehensive Crime section and the Cyber section, the Limits and More Than One Section condition applies, and a loss of funds is paid under Comprehensive Crime where that section is insured.

Subrogation and Interested Parties

After we pay a claim, we may take action in your name to recover the amount we paid. You must not give up any right of recovery without our agreement, except under a contract made before the loss in the ordinary course of the Business. We waive our rights against your directors, officers and Employees, except in cases of dishonesty or fraud. An Interested party noted in the Schedule is told before we cancel, and its interest is not prejudiced by your act or omission that it did not know about.

Notice of Circumstances

During the Period of Insurance, you may give us written notice of facts that might give rise to a Claim under the Cyber section or the Management Liability section, or an Audit notice under the Tax Audit section. The notice should describe the facts, the persons involved and the potential loss as fully as you can. A Claim or Audit notice later arising from those facts is treated as first made during the Period of Insurance in which the notice was given, as s 40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) provides.

Reinstatement of Sums Insured

After a claim under the Property Damage section, the Glass section, the Equipment Damage section, the General Property section or the Goods in Transit section, the Sum insured is reinstated from the date of loss unless we paid a total loss. We may charge an additional premium for the reinstatement. The section limits of Comprehensive Crime, Tax Audit, Cyber and Management Liability are aggregate limits and are not reinstated.

GST

Amounts in this Policy include GST. If you are entitled to an input tax credit for the premium or for the cost of a repair or replacement, we reduce our payment by that input tax credit, as the A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth) allows. You must tell us your entitlement to input tax credits when you make a claim.

Sanctions

We do not provide cover, pay any claim or provide any benefit to the extent that doing so would expose us to a sanction, prohibition or restriction under the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011 (Cth), the Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 (Cth), United Nations resolutions, or the trade or economic sanctions laws or regulations of the European Union, the United Kingdom or the United States of America.

Fraudulent Claims

If a claim is fraudulent, we may refuse to pay it, as s 56 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Policy is governed by the law of the State or Territory shown in the Schedule as the governing-law jurisdiction at inception. Each party submits to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of that State or Territory. [WP REVIEW: add a service-of-suit clause where the insurer is not APRA-authorised.]

Underinsurance (Property)

If, at the time of Damage, the Sum insured for an item is less than the underinsurance percentage shown in the Schedule of that item's full insurable value, we pay only the proportion of the loss that the Sum insured bears to that percentage of full value. This clause applies to each item separately. We will not apply it to a loss of less than the threshold shown in the Schedule.

Worked example, underinsurance at 80%: Contents insured for $500,000 (Schedule); full insurable value $800,000; Damage $200,000. 80% of $800,000 is $640,000. We pay $500,000 ÷ $640,000 × $200,000 = $156,250, before the Excess.

Business Interruption

Written for each Situation.

Interruption of the Business

If Damage covered under the Property Damage section at a Situation, or Damage to property in the vicinity of the Premises that prevents access, interrupts the Business during the Period of Insurance, we will pay, during the indemnity period shown in the Schedule:

  • loss of gross profit (reduction in turnover less expenses that cease);
  • increased cost of working; and
  • loss of rent receivable, as shown in the Schedule.

Business Interruption Waiting Period

If the Schedule shows a waiting period, we pay only where the interruption lasts longer than the waiting period. Once it does, we pay from the start of the interruption. Any Excess applies in addition, as the Excess condition provides.

Underinsurance (Gross Profit)

If the gross profit Sum insured is less than the underinsurance percentage shown in the Schedule of the annual gross profit (adjusted for the indemnity period where it exceeds 12 months), we pay only the proportion of the loss that the Sum insured bears to that percentage.

Business Interruption Additional Benefits

Each up to the sub-limit shown in the Schedule (defaults in the Default Schedule Values):

  • prevention of access;
  • Damage at customers' or suppliers' premises;
  • failure of electricity, gas, water or telecommunications supply caused by physical Damage;
  • claims preparation costs;
  • loss of attraction; and
  • closure ordered by a government or council authority.

Business Interruption Exclusions

In addition to the General Exclusions section, we do not cover under the Business Interruption section:

  • loss that is not a direct consequence of Damage covered under the Property Damage section;
  • loss after the indemnity period;
  • fines or contractual penalties for late delivery; and
  • loss of market or permanent loss of customers that would have occurred regardless of the Damage.

Comprehensive Crime

Written once for the Business. We will pay your direct financial loss, first discovered during the Period of Insurance, under the insuring clauses below.

Theft

Theft of Contents or Stock from the Premises following forcible and violent entry or exit, or hold-up, including Damage to the Building caused by the theft or attempt.

Money

Loss of Money by Theft, hold-up, accidental loss or destruction, in the custody locations and up to the custody sub-limits shown in the Schedule.

Employee Dishonesty

A Dishonest act of an Employee, acting alone or with others.

Forgery

Forgery or fraudulent alteration of a cheque, payment instruction or other financial instrument drawn on your account.

Computer Fraud

A fraudulent electronic transfer of your funds by a Third party from your accounts without your authority.

Social Engineering Fraud

Where the Schedule shows it as operative, a transfer of your funds by an Employee acting in good faith on a fraudulent instruction from a Third party impersonating a customer, supplier, director or Employee, up to the social engineering sub-limit shown in the Schedule.

Crime Limits and Aggregation

The section limit shown in the Schedule is the most we pay for all loss discovered during the Period of Insurance. The custody sub-limits under the Money clause and the sub-limit under the Social Engineering Fraud clause are part of, not in addition to, the section limit. A Single loss is treated as one loss, subject to one Excess, and is treated as discovered in the Period of Insurance in which Discovery first occurred.

Discovery and Extended Discovery

You must notify us of a loss within 60 days after Discovery, and in any event within the extended discovery period. If this section is cancelled or not renewed, and not replaced by other crime cover, you have an extended discovery period of 90 days after the Period of Insurance ends to discover loss sustained before it ended. The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies.

Social Engineering Verification

For the Social Engineering Fraud clause, we expect you to verify a request to change payment details by contacting the requester using contact details you held before the request. This is not a condition of cover. If you did not verify a request, we will not refuse the claim on that ground; we may reduce our payment only to the extent that failing to verify caused or contributed to the loss, as The Insurance Contracts Act condition and s 54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allow.

Crime Valuation and Recoveries

We value Money at its face value and securities at their market value at the close of business on the day of Discovery, converted into Australian dollars at the Reserve Bank of Australia rate on that day. Other property is valued as the Settling Property Damage provision provides. Amounts recovered after a loss are applied first to reimburse you for loss above the limit or within the Excess, then to us, then to you for any uninsured loss.

Comprehensive Crime Exclusions

In addition to the General Exclusions section (other than the Deliberate Acts exclusion, which applies as it states), we do not cover under the Comprehensive Crime section:

  • loss discovered before the Period of Insurance or reported under an earlier policy;
  • loss caused by an Employee after a Responsible person knew of a previous Dishonest act by that Employee;
  • inventory or profit-and-loss shortages proved only by comparing records;
  • loss from an unattended vehicle unless locked and the property concealed from view;
  • indirect or consequential loss;
  • costs of investigating a loss, except as shown in the Schedule;
  • loss of cryptocurrency or other digital assets; and
  • a transfer under the Social Engineering Fraud clause where the Schedule does not show that clause as operative.

Public and Products Liability

Written once for the Business.

We also pay defence costs we approve. Defence costs are paid in addition to the limit.
