Design and Construct Professional Indemnity Insurance — Endorsement

The Design and Construct Professional Indemnity Insurance terms, in the form of an endorsement to the base Professional Indemnity Insurance wording.
Draft v1.0.0-DRAFTDownload .docxIntegrated wordingBase wordingSuggest a change
DRAFT — subject to legal review — not for use. This wording is not settled and must not be issued, quoted from, or bound until Wording Partner sign-off. It is provided as source material under CC BY 4.0, without warranty.

Application

This endorsement attaches to and forms part of the Meridian Professional Indemnity Insurance wording, version 1.5.0-DRAFT (document reference 2026/PI/0001), referred to here as the base wording. It changes the base wording only as set out below. All other terms, conditions, exclusions and limits of the base wording continue to apply.

Words defined in the base wording have the same meaning in this endorsement. Where this endorsement and the base wording conflict, this endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict.

The same terms are published as a single integrated wording, Design and Construct Professional Indemnity Insurance, version 1.0.0-DRAFT. Both documents are generated from the same source and have the same effect.

Replaced: Professional services

The definition of Professional services in the base wording is deleted and replaced with the following.

Professional services

The following services, when you provide them in connection with a construction contract:

(a) the design of construction work, including architectural, structural, civil, geotechnical, mechanical, electrical, hydraulic and fire services design, and the design of temporary works;

(b) the preparation, review or certification of drawings, specifications, shop drawings and other design documentation, to the extent a law permits you to certify;

(c) design management, including the engagement and co-ordination of design consultants;

(d) value engineering, and advice on the selection of materials, products and construction methods;

(e) the management and administration of the construction contract, including the inspection of construction work for its conformity with the design; and

(f) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

A service in (a) or (b) is a professional service only if it is performed by a design consultant, or by or under the direct control and supervision of a person who holds a tertiary or vocational qualification in the design discipline concerned. The Licences, Registrations and Accreditations condition also applies.

Construction work itself is not a professional service. The Construction Workmanship exclusion sets out how a claim that arises from both is treated.

Added: Construction contract

The following definition is added to the base wording, after the definition of Confidential information.

Construction contract

A contract under which you carry out construction work, or arrange for others to carry it out, and are responsible for all or part of the design of that work, however the contract describes the arrangement (for example design and construct, design and build, novated design or early contractor involvement). It includes a subcontract under which you take design responsibility for part of the work.

Added: Construction work

The following definition is added to the base wording, after the definition of Construction contract.

Construction work

Building, civil and engineering work, including the installation of building services, fit-out, alteration, renovation, repair, maintenance and demolition, and the supply and installation of materials and goods as part of that work.

Added: Design consultant

The following definition is added to the base wording, after the definition of Defence costs.

Design consultant

A person, other than an employee, whom you engage to perform any part of the professional services, including an architect, engineer, building designer or draftsperson.

Added: Design Rectification Costs

The following extension is added to the base wording, after the Mitigation Costs extension.

Design Rectification Costs

If, during the policy period, you discover an error in the professional services that would reasonably be expected to give rise to a claim covered by this policy if it were not corrected, we will pay the reasonable and necessary costs you incur, with our prior written consent, to correct the error and so avoid or reduce that claim. These costs are:

(a) the cost of redesigning the affected part of the work; and

(b) the cost of removing, replacing or rectifying construction work that has been built to the design containing the error, but only to the extent that the cost is less than the amount we would reasonably expect to pay for the claim if the error were not corrected.

We do not pay:

(i) any cost that would have been incurred if the design had been correct, including the cost of any improvement or upgrade;

(ii) the cost of correcting construction work that does not conform to the design; or

(iii) your own overheads or profit, or the time of your employees, other than time spent on redesign, which we pay at cost.

Before you incur the costs, you must tell us about the error as the Tell Us About Circumstances obligation requires and obtain our consent, which we will not unreasonably withhold or delay. These costs are paid under this extension and not under the Mitigation Costs extension.

The most we will pay under this extension during the policy period is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for design rectification costs or, if none is shown, one quarter of the limit of liability. The sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability. The excess applies to each error as if it were a claim.

Replaced: Subcontractors and Outsourcers

The Subcontractors and Outsourcers extension in the base wording is deleted and replaced with the following.

Design Consultants and Subcontractors

We will cover you for any claim arising from professional services performed on your behalf by a design consultant, or by any other subcontractor or outsourcer, as if you had performed them yourself, provided the work falls within your business activity. This cover does not depend on whether the design consultant holds its own professional indemnity insurance.

This extension covers your liability only. It does not cover the liability of the design consultant, subcontractor or outsourcer unless the schedule names that person as an insured.

If we pay a claim under this extension, we may recover what we have paid from the design consultant, subcontractor or outsourcer, as the Preserve Our Recovery Rights obligation provides.

Added: NSW Registered Practitioners

The following extension is added to the base wording, after the Design Consultants and Subcontractors extension.

NSW Registered Practitioners

This extension applies to you, or to any insured person, who is registered under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 (NSW) as a design practitioner, principal design practitioner, professional engineer or building practitioner.

For a claim arising from work that the Act requires that person to be registered to carry out, the retroactive date is the date that person was first registered under the Act, if that date is earlier than the retroactive date shown in the schedule. That date applies to the claim in place of the date shown in the schedule, as the definition of Retroactive date provides.

This extension does not affect the Prior Known Circumstances exclusion.

[WP REVIEW: confirm the insurance terms that will apply to registered building practitioners when the requirement commences, and whether they permit a prior-knowledge exclusion to apply to cover from first registration.]

Added: Construction Workmanship

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Products exclusion.

Construction Workmanship

Arising from construction work performed by you or on your behalf, including the quality of the workmanship, the materials or goods used or supplied, and any failure to build in accordance with the design.

This exclusion does not apply to the extent that the claim arises from an error in the professional services. Where a claim arises partly from an error in the professional services and partly from construction work, we cover the part of the loss and defence costs that is fairly attributable to the error in the professional services, determined as the Allocation provision provides.

Added: Your Own Rectification Costs

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Construction Workmanship exclusion.

Your Own Rectification Costs

For any cost that you incur yourself, as distinct from a liability you have to a third party, to re-perform or correct the professional services, or to remove, replace, repair or complete construction work, including under an obligation in a construction contract to rectify defects, except as the Design Rectification Costs extension provides.

This exclusion does not prevent cover for a claim by a third party, including the principal under a construction contract, for the cost of rectifying construction work that was built in accordance with a design containing an error in the professional services.

Added: Delay and Cost Overruns

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Your Own Rectification Costs exclusion.

Delay and Cost Overruns

Arising from:

(a) a failure to complete construction work by a date, or within a period, required by a construction contract; or

(b) the cost of construction work exceeding a price, budget or estimate,

except to the extent that the claim arises directly from an error in the professional services.

Added: Liquidated Damages and Contractual Penalties

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Contractual Liability exclusion.

Liquidated Damages and Contractual Penalties

For any liquidated damages, or any penalty payable under a contract, except to the extent that you would have been liable for the amount in the absence of the contract.

Added: Excluded Projects

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Non-Compensatory Payments exclusion.

Excluded Projects

Arising from professional services for:

(a) a dam more than the height shown in the schedule for this purpose, or 6 metres if no height is shown;

(b) a tunnel, or underground work other than basements, footings, piling and trenches for services;

(c) a bridge with a span longer than the length shown in the schedule for this purpose, or 10 metres if no length is shown;

(d) a wharf, jetty, sea wall, breakwater or other marine or offshore structure;

(e) an oil, gas, petrochemical or chemical processing facility; or

(f) a mine or a mineral processing plant,

unless the schedule shows that project as covered.

Added: Single Project Cover

The following section is added to the base wording, after the General Conditions section.

Single Project Cover

This section applies only if the schedule shows the basis of cover as single project. Where it applies, it changes this policy as set out below and prevails over any other provision of this policy to the extent of any inconsistency.

Project Covered

This policy covers only the professional services you provide for the project shown in the schedule, under the construction contract for that project, on or after the retroactive date. Your business activity is limited accordingly. Professional services for any other construction contract are not covered.

Construction Period and Reporting Period

The schedule shows the construction period and the reporting period. The construction period starts on the start date shown in the schedule. The reporting period starts when the construction period shown in the schedule ends and runs for the number of years shown in the schedule. The policy period is the construction period and the reporting period together, and claims must be first made and notified to us within it.

If the project will not reach practical completion (as the construction contract defines it or, if it does not, completion of the work apart from minor defects and omissions) by the end of the construction period shown in the schedule, you must tell us before that date. We may extend the construction period, and the policy period with it, on terms and for an additional premium that we state in writing. If no extension is agreed, the policy period does not change, and professional services performed after the construction period shown in the schedule has ended are not covered.

Limit of Liability for a Single Project

The limit of liability is the most we will pay for all claims and defence costs combined during the whole policy period, including the reporting period. The Automatic Reinstatement provision does not apply.

Provisions That Do Not Apply to a Single Project

The Continuous Cover extension, the Run-Off Cover extension and the Newly Acquired Subsidiaries extension do not apply, and the run-off extension endorsement in the Endorsement Library is not available. The Joint Ventures extension applies only to a joint venture that is a party to the construction contract for the project.

Premium and Cancellation for a Single Project

The premium is for the whole policy period and is fully earned on the start date shown in the schedule.

You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. If you cancel before the start date, we refund the premium in full, less any non-refundable government charges. If you cancel on or after the start date, no premium is refunded.

We may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you written notice as that Act requires. If we cancel, we refund the premium for the unexpired part of the policy period on a pro-rata basis.

Cancellation does not affect your rights for any claim or circumstance that you notified to us before the cancellation took effect. This clause applies in place of the Cancellation condition.

[WP REVIEW: confirm that a premium fully earned on cancellation by the insured after the start date is consistent with the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) and the unfair contract terms provisions for a small business insured.]

Design and Construct Endorsements

The following endorsements apply to this policy only when the schedule shows that they apply.

An endorsement does not apply to the extent that a law requiring you to hold this insurance does not permit the cover it removes or limits to be excluded or limited.

Combustible Cladding Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from the use, or proposed use, in a building of external wall cladding that is combustible or that does not comply with the National Construction Code, including aluminium composite panels with a polyethylene core and expanded polystyrene products. This applies to any design, specification, selection, supply, installation, certification or approval of that cladding by you or on your behalf, and to any advice about it.

[WP REVIEW: confirm whether any state law requiring a building practitioner to hold insurance limits the use of this exclusion.]

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Are you asking for the combustible cladding exclusion to be removed? qs.pi.dc.cladding_exclusion_removal

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.dc.cladding_exclusion_removal = no: Apply automatically
  • When qs.pi.dc.cladding_exclusion_removal = yes: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Underwriter responses (on a referral or suggestion): Attach; Attach with premium loading; Do not attach; Decline risk

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies to every design and construct policy unless an underwriter agrees to remove it.

Apartment Buildings Sub-limit

For all claims arising from professional services for a Class 2 building under the National Construction Code, or for any building of more than three storeys, the most we will pay in total, including defence costs, is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement. The sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability.

[WP REVIEW: confirm whether this sub-limit may apply to a registered building practitioner once the insurance requirement under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 (NSW) commences.]

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • What percentage of your revenue from construction contracts in the last 12 months came from Class 2 (apartment) buildings? For single-project cover, enter 100 if the project is a Class 2 building and 0 if it is not. qs.pi.dc.class2_pct
  • What is the greatest number of storeys of any building designed under your construction contracts in the last three years? For single-project cover, enter the number of storeys of the project. qs.pi.dc.max_storeys

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.dc.class2_pct > 0 or qs.pi.dc.max_storeys > 3: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Underwriter responses (on a referral or suggestion): Attach; Attach with amended sub-limit; Attach with premium loading; Do not attach; Decline risk

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because {qs.pi.dc.class2_pct}% of your work with design responsibility is for Class 2 buildings, or the buildings you design are over three storeys.
