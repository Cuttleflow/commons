Architects and Engineers Professional Indemnity Insurance — Endorsement

The Architects and Engineers Professional Indemnity Insurance terms, in the form of an endorsement to the base Professional Indemnity Insurance wording.

DRAFT — subject to legal review — not for use. This wording is not settled and must not be issued, quoted from, or bound until Wording Partner sign-off. It is provided as source material under CC BY 4.0, without warranty.

Application

This endorsement attaches to and forms part of the Meridian Professional Indemnity Insurance wording, version 1.3.0-DRAFT (document reference 2026/PI/0001), referred to here as the base wording. It changes the base wording only as set out below. All other terms, conditions, exclusions and limits of the base wording continue to apply.

Words defined in the base wording have the same meaning in this endorsement. Where this endorsement and the base wording conflict, this endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict.

The same terms are published as a single integrated wording, Architects and Engineers Professional Indemnity Insurance, version 1.0.0-DRAFT. Both documents are generated from the same source and have the same effect.

Replaced: Professional services

The definition of Professional services in the base wording is deleted and replaced with the following.

Professional services

The following services provided to a third party for a fee:

(a) architecture;

(b) town planning;

(c) landscape design;

(d) interior design;

(e) drafting;

(f) quantity surveying;

(g) land surveying;

(h) civil engineering;

(i) structural engineering;

(j) mechanical engineering;

(k) electrical engineering;

(l) project management; and

(m) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

Where a law requires a person who provides a service to be registered, licensed or endorsed (for example, as an architect, a registered professional engineer, or a registered design practitioner or professional engineer for regulated work), the service is a professional service only if it is provided by a person who holds that registration, licence or endorsement, or under the direct control and supervision of such a person to the extent that law permits.

Any other service is a professional service only if it is provided by, or under the direct control and supervision of, a person holding a tertiary qualification in engineering, architecture, surveying or quantity surveying.

Added: Registered Designs and Plagiarism

The following extension is added to the base wording, after the Subcontractors and Outsourcers extension.

Registered Designs and Plagiarism

The cover under the Claims Against You clause includes your liability for any actual or alleged unintentional infringement of a registered design, or plagiarism, in the course of providing your professional services.

This extension does not cover infringement of a patent: see the Patent Infringement exclusion.

Added: NSW Registered Practitioners

The following extension is added to the base wording, after the Registered Designs and Plagiarism extension.

NSW Registered Practitioners

This extension applies to you, or to any insured person, who is registered under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 (NSW) as a design practitioner, principal design practitioner or professional engineer.

For a claim arising from work that the Act requires you to be registered to carry out, the definition of Retroactive date is replaced by the date you were first registered under that Act, if that date is earlier than the retroactive date shown in the schedule.

the Prior Known Circumstances exclusion continues to apply.

Added: Construction Work

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Products exclusion.

Construction Work

Arising from physical construction, installation, fabrication or demolition work carried out by you or on your behalf, or from defects in raw materials or goods you supplied.

This exclusion does not apply to design, contract administration, site inspection, site observation or certification services that you provide as part of your professional services.

Added: Patent Infringement

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Construction Work exclusion.

Patent Infringement

Arising from any actual or alleged infringement of a patent.

Added: Project Management Activities

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Patent Infringement exclusion.

Project Management Activities

Arising from your project management services, to the extent the claim arises from:

(a) a failure to arrange or maintain insurance, or performance or other bonds;

(b) a failure to obtain finance;

(c) financial, insurance or tax advice;

(d) the insolvency of any party to the project;

(e) construction costs exceeding any estimate, unless the estimate was prepared by a qualified quantity surveyor; or

(f) bodily injury to an employee of any party arising out of their employment, unless the injury arises directly from an error in a design covered by this policy.

Added: Liquidated Damages and Contractual Penalties

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Contractual Liability exclusion.

Liquidated Damages and Contractual Penalties

For any liquidated damages, or any penalty payable under a contract.

Added: Excluded Engineering Activities

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Non-Compensatory Payments exclusion.

Excluded Engineering Activities

Arising from:

(a) the design or construction of dams more than 6 metres in height;

(b) geological estimates prepared for a prospectus or other disclosure document;

(c) mineral extraction;

(d) oil and gas services;

(e) marine surveys;

(f) nuclear engineering;

(g) chemical engineering; or

(h) pre-purchase building inspections.

Added: Automatic Reinstatement

The following provision is added to the base wording, after the Limit of Liability provision.

Automatic Reinstatement

Despite the Limit of Liability provision, if payments under this policy reduce or exhaust the limit of liability, it is reinstated once during the policy period, in full and without additional premium.

The reinstated limit of liability:

(a) is available only for a claim that does not arise from the same, similar or related acts, errors or omissions as a claim already paid under this policy; and

(b) does not increase the most we will pay for any one claim, which remains the limit of liability.

The most we will pay for all claims during the policy period is twice the limit of liability.

Architects and Engineers Endorsements

The following endorsements apply to this policy only when the schedule shows that they apply.

An endorsement does not apply to the extent that a law or Ministerial Order requiring you to hold this insurance does not permit the cover it removes to be excluded.

Bridges and Tunnels Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from the design or construction of a bridge or tunnel, or any part of one.

Combustible Cladding Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from the use, or proposed use, in a building of external wall cladding that is combustible or that does not comply with the National Construction Code, including aluminium composite panels with a polyethylene core and expanded polystyrene products. This applies to any design, specification, selection, certification, inspection or approval of that cladding, and to any advice about it.

Mining Equipment Warranties Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from any express or implied warranty or guarantee of the safety or performance of mining equipment.
