Carriers Combined Insurance

Goods in transit cover for road transport operators carrying other people's goods in Australia: load cover paid whatever your legal liability, in Essentials, Standard and Comprehensive tiers, with optional legal liability on approved terms of carriage.

About This Policy

This policy is for road transport operators who carry goods for other people. It insures loss of or damage to the goods while they are in your care during a transit within Australia. The core cover, the Basic Load section, pays for loss or damage caused by a listed event whether or not you are legally liable to the owner of the goods. That lets you settle with your customer without first arguing about who was at fault.

It is built in tiers, with options. The schedule shows which sections apply.

SectionWhat it coversBasis
Basic LoadLoss of or damage to goods in transit caused by fire, collision, overturning and the other events listed in itLoss or damage that happens during the policy period
Standard PerilsTheft and non-delivery, loading and unloading accidents, rainwater and shedding of loadAs above
ComprehensiveAccidental loss of or damage to all goods, from any cause not excludedAs above
Nominated ClientsAccidental loss or damage cover for the clients or commodities named in the scheduleAs above
Refrigeration BreakdownDeterioration of refrigerated goods caused by a refrigeration unit failingAs above
Legal LiabilityYour legal liability as a carrier for loss of, damage to or delay of goods, measured by your approved terms of carriage, and defence costsLoss, damage or delay that happens during the policy period

The three tiers are Essentials (Basic Load alone), Standard (Basic Load and Standard Perils) and Comprehensive (all three). Additional Benefits applies to every policy.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. The Order of Precedence condition says which part applies where they differ. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

Important Information

Who issues this policy. The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

Who this policy is for. This wording is designed for road transport operators, couriers and removalists who carry goods for others in the course of business, and for distribution to wholesale clients within the meaning of s.761G of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). It is not a Product Disclosure Statement, and no Target Market Determination has been prepared for it. An insurer that offers it to retail clients must first prepare the documents the law requires for retail distribution.

Which law applies. This policy insures goods carried by road, with sea legs only where they are incidental to a road transit. The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) applies to it. [WP REVIEW: confirm that a carrier's goods in transit policy whose only sea legs are incidental Bass Strait or island crossings is outside the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) under s.9(1)(d) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).]

Your duty of disclosure. Before you enter into, renew, extend, vary or reinstate this policy, s.21 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) requires you to tell us every matter you know, or that a reasonable person in your circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we waive. If you do not comply, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows. If the non-disclosure is fraudulent, we may avoid the policy from its beginning.

Financial Claims Scheme. If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.

Schedule

(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

  • Your name, ABN and address
  • The policy period, with its start and end dates
  • The sections that apply, and so the tier: Essentials, Standard or Comprehensive
  • The rating basis: your estimated gross freight earnings for the policy period, or the number of powered units on the per-vehicle basis
  • The goods carried, described by kind, and the main operating radius
  • The limit any one vehicle, and the accumulation limit if it differs from the Accumulation at a Depot extension
  • The nominated clients or commodities, if Nominated Clients applies
  • Your approved terms of carriage, if Legal Liability applies, and the limit for that section
  • The excess, and any special excess shown for an endorsement
  • Whether the premium is adjustable, the adjustment rate and the minimum premium (see the Premium Adjustment provision)
  • The premium, including any stamp duty and GST
  • Any commission or fee paid to your broker
  • The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see the Governing Law condition)
  • The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables

Definitions

These words have the meanings below wherever they appear in this policy, in the singular or the plural.

Accumulation Limit

The most we pay for all loss or damage from one event at a depot or other place where goods from more than one vehicle are gathered, as set by the Accumulation at a Depot extension or shown in the schedule.

Approved Terms

The terms and conditions of carriage shown in the schedule, or later agreed by us in writing, that limit or exclude your liability to the owner or consignor of goods, and that you incorporate into each contract of carriage by a consignment note, a written agreement or a standing notice the customer has accepted.

Bulk Dangerous Goods

Dangerous goods carried in a receptacle with a capacity of more than 500 litres or a net mass of more than 500 kilograms, including a tank, an intermediate bulk container or loose in the body of the vehicle.

Consignment

Goods carried under one consignment note or one contract of carriage.

Conveying Vehicle

A motor vehicle, trailer or semi-trailer that you own, lease, hire or operate, or that a subcontractor operates for you, while it carries goods in an insured transit, including a rail wagon or a ferry that carries the vehicle on a leg of the transit.

Dangerous Goods

Goods that are dangerous goods under the Australian Code for the Transport of Dangerous Goods by Road and Rail, as in force when the goods are carried.

Defence Costs

The reasonable legal fees, costs and expenses incurred with our written consent to investigate, defend or settle a claim against you under Legal Liability. Defence costs do not include your own staff costs or overheads.

Depot

A building, yard or compound that you own, lease or use, at which goods are held in the course of a transit, including a cross-dock.

Endorsement

A clause from the endorsement library that applies to this policy because its code is shown in the schedule.

Event

One occurrence, or a series of occurrences from one originating cause.

Excess

The amount shown in the schedule that you bear for each claim, as the Excess provision sets out.

Goods

Goods, merchandise and materials of the kinds described in the schedule that you carry for reward for other people, including their packing, and including goods you carry as a subcontractor for another carrier. Goods do not include your own property, the property of your employees, or excluded goods.

Excluded Goods

The goods listed in the Excluded Goods exclusion.

Gross Freight Earnings

The total income you earn from carriage during the policy period, whether as principal, as a subcontractor or through subcontractors, including fees, charges and commissions for carriage, but excluding GST and the cost of permits, pilot vehicles and escorts.

Insured Transit

The carriage of goods by road in Australia, and any sea or rail leg incidental to it, from the time cover starts to the time it ends under the When Cover Starts and Ends clause.

Limit Any One Vehicle

The most we pay for all loss or damage to goods on one conveying vehicle, including a trailer or semi-trailer coupled to it, from one event, as shown in the schedule.

Livestock

Live animals, including cattle, sheep, pigs, goats, horses and poultry.

Nominated Client

A customer, principal carrier or commodity named in the schedule for Nominated Clients.

Policy Period

The period of insurance shown in the schedule.

Powered Unit

A prime mover, rigid truck, van or other self-propelled vehicle used to carry or tow goods.

Refrigerated Goods

Goods that must be kept within a stated temperature range during carriage, and that you carry in a refrigerated body, trailer or container.

Subcontractor

A carrier that you engage to carry goods, or part of a transit, that you have contracted to carry.

Temporary Storage

The holding of goods at a depot, or in a conveying vehicle parked at a depot, in the ordinary course of a transit, for up to 30 days in total.

We, Us and Our

The insurer named in the schedule.

You and Your

The named insured shown in the schedule, and any subsidiary or related entity named in the schedule.

The Insured Transit

This part says when goods are in transit and covered. It applies to every section.

When Cover Starts and Ends

Cover starts when the goods are handed to you or your subcontractor for carriage, or are loaded onto a conveying vehicle, whichever happens first, at the consignor's premises or another place named in the contract of carriage. The conveying vehicle must leave within 72 hours of the goods being handed over, unless the delay is caused by an event outside your control.

Cover continues during the ordinary course of the transit, including:

  • temporary storage;
  • transfers between conveying vehicles, and at depots; and
  • any sea or rail leg that is incidental to a road transit within Australia.

Cover ends at the first of:

  • delivery of the goods to the consignee, or to the place the consignee or the consignor directs;
  • delivery to any other place where the goods leave the care of you and your subcontractors;
  • the end of temporary storage, if the goods are still held at a depot; and
  • 72 hours after the goods arrive at the delivery address, if they have not been unloaded and the delay is within your control.

Goods Held at a Depot

Goods in temporary storage are covered on the same terms as goods on a conveying vehicle, while they are at a depot. Theft of goods held at a depot is covered under Standard Perils only if the depot is locked and secured, or attended, when the theft happens, or entry is gained by force or violence. Storage beyond 30 days is covered only if endorsement E09 applies.

Goods Carried by Subcontractors

Goods carried by a subcontractor are covered as if you carried them, where you remain the contracting carrier to the owner or consignor. We keep our right to recover from a subcontractor that holds its own carriers' insurance for the goods, or that is liable to you under its contract with you. We do not seek recovery from an owner-driver subcontractor that carries solely under your contract and holds no carriers' insurance of its own.

Basic Load

This section always applies.

Basic Load Cover

We pay for loss of or damage to goods in an insured transit during the policy period that is caused by:

  • fire, lightning or explosion;
  • the conveying vehicle colliding with another vehicle or an external object, other than a kerb or an uneven road surface;
  • the conveying vehicle overturning, jack-knifing or being derailed;
  • collapse of a bridge, culvert, building or other structure onto or under the conveying vehicle;
  • flood, storm, earthquake or landslide;
  • impact by an aircraft or by an article dropped from one;
  • theft of the whole conveying vehicle with the goods, or theft by armed hold-up or hijacking;
  • malicious damage by a person other than you, your employee or your subcontractor; and
  • for a sea leg, the stranding, sinking, grounding or capsizing of the vessel, the vessel colliding with another vessel or object, and jettison or washing overboard.

We pay whether or not you are legally liable to the owner of the goods, and whatever the terms of carriage say. Where the goods are refrigerated goods, we also pay for their deterioration from a failure of refrigeration that one of these events causes. Where the goods are livestock, we pay for the death or injury of an animal that one of these events causes only if endorsement E01 applies.

Standard Perils

This section applies if the schedule shows it. It adds to Basic Load Cover.

Standard Perils Cover

We pay for loss of or damage to goods in an insured transit during the policy period that is caused by:

  • theft of goods or of part of a load, and non-delivery of a whole package or consignment that we are satisfied was stolen;
  • an accident while the goods are being loaded onto or unloaded from the conveying vehicle by you, your employee or your subcontractor, including by a forklift, crane or tailgate lifter;
  • rainwater or other water entering the conveying vehicle or its covering; and
  • goods falling from the conveying vehicle while it is moving (shedding of load).

Theft From an Unattended Vehicle

For theft of goods from a conveying vehicle left unattended, we pay under Standard Perils Cover only if:

  • the vehicle's doors and load compartment were locked and its keys removed, or entry was gained by force or violence; and
  • for a detached trailer or semi-trailer left outside a locked compound, a kingpin lock or wheel clamp was fitted.

The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies to this condition: if the security was not in place but did not cause or contribute to the theft, we pay the claim.

Comprehensive

This section applies if the schedule shows it. It applies only together with Standard Perils.

Comprehensive Cover

We pay for accidental loss of or damage to goods in an insured transit during the policy period, from any cause that this policy does not exclude.

Where this section applies, Nominated Clients is not needed and does not apply.

Nominated Clients

This section applies if the schedule shows it and Comprehensive does not apply.

Nominated Clients Cover

For goods carried for a nominated client, or goods of a nominated commodity, we pay for accidental loss of or damage to the goods in an insured transit during the policy period, from any cause that this policy does not exclude, as if Comprehensive applied to them. Endorsement E07 records the nominated clients and commodities and any special excess for them.

Refrigeration Breakdown

This section applies if the schedule shows it.

Refrigeration Breakdown Cover

We pay for deterioration of refrigerated goods in an insured transit during the policy period caused by the breakdown or failure of the refrigeration unit of the conveying vehicle, or by an error in operating it, that keeps the goods outside their required temperature range for more than four consecutive hours. Endorsement E02 sets the conditions for carrying refrigerated goods.

Refrigeration Section Exclusions

Under Refrigeration Breakdown we do not pay for deterioration caused by:

  • the goods not being at the required temperature when they were loaded;
  • the unit running out of fuel, unless the shortage was caused by an event that Basic Load Cover covers; or
  • wear and tear of the refrigeration unit where it has not been serviced at the intervals its manufacturer recommends, to the extent that the lack of service caused the breakdown.

Legal Liability

This section applies if the schedule shows it. It covers your legal liability as a carrier. It is separate from the other sections, which pay for the goods whatever your liability is.

Carrier's Legal Liability

We pay the amount you become legally liable to pay to the owner or consignor of goods for loss of, damage to or delay in delivery of goods in an insured transit during the policy period, to the extent a payment under another section of this policy does not meet it, up to the limit for this section shown in the schedule.

The measure of cover is your liability under your approved terms. The Measure of Liability provision says how we measure it where the approved terms were not used.

Liability as a Subcontractor

When you carry goods as a subcontractor for another carrier, Carrier's Legal Liability applies to your liability to that carrier, provided the principal carrier's terms limit your liability at least as well as your approved terms would, and extend their protection to you.

Defence Costs

We also pay defence costs for a claim covered by Carrier's Legal Liability. Defence costs are paid in addition to the limit for this section, up to an amount equal to that limit.

Measure of Liability

Where your approved terms were incorporated into the contract of carriage, we pay your liability as limited by them.

Where you did not incorporate your approved terms, because of an oversight or mistake by you or your employee, we pay as if they had been incorporated.

Where you chose not to use your approved terms, or agreed to terms that increase your liability beyond them, we pay only what you would have been liable to pay under your approved terms.

Where a law that cannot be excluded by contract, including the Australian Consumer Law, makes a term of your approved terms void or unenforceable, we pay your liability as that law leaves it, up to the limit for this section. [WP REVIEW: confirm that following liability where the unfair contract terms regime voids a limitation in the approved terms is the intended position, and how it interacts with s.54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).]

Legal Liability Exclusions

Under Legal Liability we do not pay for:

  • liability you assume under a contract or indemnity beyond your approved terms, except liability you would have had without it;
  • fines, penalties, infringement notices, and punitive, aggravated or exemplary damages;
  • personal injury, or damage to property other than goods in an insured transit;
  • liability for delay where the goods suffered no physical loss or damage and the delay was not caused by an event this policy covers; or
  • liability arising from your insolvency or the insolvency of a subcontractor.

Additional Benefits

These benefits apply to every policy. Each is paid in addition to the limit any one vehicle, up to the amount shown in the schedule, or if none is shown, the amount stated here, for any one event. They are paid only after loss or damage that a section of this policy covers.

Onforwarding

The reasonable extra cost of unloading, storing, reloading and forwarding the goods to their destination after an insured event interrupts an insured transit, up to $50,000.

Debris Removal and Clean-Up

The reasonable cost of removing debris of the goods, and of cleaning up the site and the road, that you incur after an insured event, or that an authority requires you to pay, up to $50,000. This does not include the cost of cleaning up pollution or contamination other than from the goods themselves.

Packaging and Containers

Loss of or damage to packaging, pallets, tarpaulins, gates, chains, straps and shipping containers used to carry the goods, whether you own them or are responsible for them, up to $50,000.

Carrier's Equipment

Loss of or damage to loading equipment, tools and accessories you carry on the conveying vehicle for use with the goods, up to $10,000.

Resecuring a Load

The reasonable cost of resecuring or reloading goods that have shifted on the conveying vehicle after an insured event, to prevent further loss, up to $10,000.

Fumigation and Quarantine

The reasonable cost of fumigation, quarantine or decontamination of the goods that an authority requires after an insured event, up to $25,000.

Additional Expenses

The extra costs the owner of the goods incurs, and that you are obliged to reimburse or choose to reimburse with our consent, because loss of or damage to the goods from an insured event delays their delivery, up to $50,000 for any one event and $100,000 in total in the policy period. This benefit does not pay for loss of market or loss of profit.

General Average and Salvage

Your share of any general average or salvage charge on goods in an insured transit, during a sea leg.

Driver's Personal Effects

Loss of or damage to the personal effects of the driver of the conveying vehicle from an insured event, up to $1,000.

Accumulation at a Depot

Where goods from more than one conveying vehicle are gathered at a depot, or more than one consignment is on one conveying vehicle beyond your control, the most we pay for all loss or damage from one event is the accumulation limit shown in the schedule. If none is shown, it is twice the limit any one vehicle, or $1,000,000, whichever is less. Endorsement E06 can increase it.

How We Pay

This part says how we value a loss and what we pay.

Basis of Valuation

We pay for the goods at the lowest of:

  • their invoice value to the consignee, plus freight and charges paid or payable, where the goods were sold;
  • the cost of replacing them with goods of the same kind and quality at the place and time of the loss; and
  • the cost of repairing or reconditioning them, where they can be repaired, including the cost of relabelling or repacking.

Where only part of a set is lost or damaged, we pay the proportion of the value of the set that the lost or damaged part represents.

Limits

The most we pay for loss of or damage to goods on one conveying vehicle from one event, under all sections other than Legal Liability, is the limit any one vehicle. The Accumulation at a Depot extension sets the most we pay for one event at a depot. Under Legal Liability, the most we pay is the limit for that section shown in the schedule, plus defence costs as the Defence Costs clause allows. The additional benefits are paid in addition to these limits.

One Section for Each Loss

We pay any one loss under one section only. Where more than one section could respond to the same loss of the same goods, we pay under the section that gives you the most cover, and only one excess applies. A payment for the goods under any section reduces any amount we pay under Legal Liability for the same goods.

Excess

For each claim you bear the excess shown in the schedule for the section that responds, for each event. Where endorsement E02 or another endorsement shows a special excess for a kind of loss, that excess applies instead. Where more than one excess applies to the same event, only the highest is deducted. Defence costs are not subject to the excess.

Premium Adjustment

On the gross freight earnings basis, the premium shown in the schedule is a deposit, worked out on your estimate of gross freight earnings for the policy period. Within 30 days after the policy period ends, you must tell us your actual gross freight earnings for the period. We then adjust the premium at the rate shown in the schedule. If the adjusted premium is more than the deposit, you pay us the difference. If it is less, we refund the difference, but we keep at least the minimum premium shown in the schedule.

On the per-vehicle basis, the premium is worked out on the number of powered units shown in the schedule and is not adjusted, unless you add powered units during the policy period, when the Tell Us About Changes obligation applies.

Who We Pay

We pay you, or with your agreement the owner of the goods directly. A payment to the owner of the goods is a payment to you under this policy.

Salvage and Recoveries

When we pay for goods as a total loss, we may take the salvage. Any recovery from a third party is applied first to our payment and then to your excess, after deducting the cost of making the recovery.

GST

Where you are entitled to an input tax credit for the GST on a repair, replacement or other payment, we reduce our payment by the amount of that credit, as A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth) allows. You must tell us your entitlement to input tax credits when you make a claim.

Other Insurance

If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by a cargo policy held by the owner of the goods, or by another policy, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows. Tell us about any other insurance you know of when you claim.

What We Do Not Cover

These exclusions apply to every section unless a section or an endorsement says otherwise.

Excluded Goods

We do not pay for loss of or damage to, or liability for:

  • cash, bank notes, coins, cheques, securities, negotiable instruments, bullion, precious stones and jewellery;
  • explosives (Class 1 dangerous goods) and radioactive material (Class 7 dangerous goods);
  • bulk dangerous goods;
  • livestock, unless endorsement E01 applies;
  • household furniture and personal effects carried in a household removal, unless endorsement E03 applies;
  • cigarettes and tobacco products, where the consignment is worth more than $50,000;
  • motor vehicles, boats and caravans carried on their own wheels or driven, rather than loaded on the conveying vehicle; and
  • goods carried outside Australia, other than on a sea leg incidental to a transit within it.

Inherent Vice and Ordinary Loss

We do not pay for loss or damage caused by the inherent vice or nature of the goods, ordinary leakage, ordinary loss of weight or volume, ordinary wear and tear, insects or vermin, or gradual deterioration, unless an event that a section covers causes it.

Insufficient Packing

We do not pay for loss or damage caused by insufficient or unsuitable packing of the goods, where you or your employee packed them or should reasonably have seen the defect before accepting them. This exclusion does not apply to packing by the consignor that you could not reasonably inspect.

Delay and Loss of Market

We do not pay for loss of market, loss of profit or other consequential loss, or for loss or damage caused by delay, even if an event this policy covers causes the delay. This exclusion does not apply to the Additional Expenses extension, the Onforwarding extension, or to delay liability under Legal Liability.

Dishonesty

We do not pay for loss or damage caused by a dishonest or fraudulent act of you or your employee, or of a subcontractor or its employee, acting alone or with others. Theft by a person who gains possession of the goods by deception, such as by impersonating a consignee or another carrier, is covered under Standard Perils Cover where that section applies. [WP REVIEW: confirm the treatment of release of goods to an impostor (fictitious pick-up) as theft under the Standard Perils section rather than as excluded dishonesty.]

Unroadworthy Vehicles and Unlicensed Drivers

We do not pay for loss or damage caused by the conveying vehicle being unroadworthy or overloaded beyond its legal mass limits, or by its being driven by a person who did not hold a licence for that class of vehicle or was under the influence of alcohol or drugs beyond the legal limit. This exclusion applies only where the condition caused or contributed to the loss or damage, and not where it arose without your knowledge or the knowledge of your management.

Breakdown of the Conveying Vehicle

We do not pay for loss or damage caused by mechanical or electrical breakdown of the conveying vehicle, except fire or collision that results from it, and except under Refrigeration Breakdown.

Fines and Penalties

We do not pay any fine, penalty, infringement notice or civil penalty imposed on you, your employee or a subcontractor, including under the Heavy Vehicle National Law or road transport laws. Cover for regulatory penalties is outside this policy.

War

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities or warlike operations (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, military or usurped power, or confiscation by a government or public authority. Seizure or detention by customs or quarantine authorities is not confiscation for this purpose where the Fumigation and Quarantine extension applies.

Nuclear

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from ionising radiation, or contamination by radioactivity, from any nuclear fuel or nuclear waste, or from the radioactive, toxic, explosive or other hazardous properties of any nuclear assembly or component.

Terrorism

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from an act of terrorism, meaning an act, including the use of force or violence or the threat of it, by any person or group, committed for political, religious, ideological or similar purposes, including the intention to influence a government or to put the public in fear. This exclusion has no effect to the extent the Terrorism Insurance Act 2003 (Cth) requires cover to be provided.

Cyber

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from any access to or use of a computer system, software, network or electronic data without the authority of its owner, malicious or harmful code, or a denial of service attack. This exclusion does not apply to physical loss of or damage to goods that a fire, collision or other event listed in Basic Load Cover causes, or to theft of goods under Standard Perils Cover.

Your Obligations

These obligations apply to every section. The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies to each of them.

Tell Us About Loss

You must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable about any loss of or damage to goods, or any demand or claim from an owner or consignor, that may give rise to a claim under this policy. Theft, hijacking and malicious damage must be reported to the police. You must give us the information and help we reasonably ask for, and keep damaged goods available for inspection until we have had a reasonable chance to inspect them.

Reduce the Loss

You must take reasonable steps to prevent further loss or damage after an event, including salvaging, protecting and resecuring the goods. We pay the reasonable cost of doing so under the Onforwarding extension and the Resecuring a Load extension.

Use Your Terms of Carriage

Where Legal Liability applies, you must use your approved terms in each contract of carriage, by including or referring to them on the consignment note or in the written agreement with your customer. The Measure of Liability provision says what we pay where you do not.

Admissions

You must not admit liability for, offer to settle, or settle any claim by an owner or consignor under Legal Liability without our written consent, which we do not unreasonably withhold. Paying an owner for goods under another section, with our agreement, is not an admission of liability.

Rights Against Others

You must not give up or limit any right to recover from another person, including a subcontractor, a depot operator or another carrier, beyond what your approved terms or the subcontract already provide, without our written consent.

Records

You must keep records of your gross freight earnings, your consignment notes and your subcontract arrangements for the policy period, and let us inspect them on reasonable notice, for up to two years after the policy period ends.

Tell Us About Changes

You must tell us within 30 days if, during the policy period, you start to carry livestock, bulk dangerous goods, refrigerated goods or household removals, you start to operate outside the main operating radius shown in the schedule, or, on the per-vehicle basis, you add a powered unit. We may change the premium or terms for the rest of the policy period, as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

General Conditions

These conditions apply to every section.

Insurance Contracts Act

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies the operation of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Where this policy is inconsistent with the Act, the Act prevails.

If this policy would otherwise let us refuse to pay a claim, in whole or in part, because of something you or another insured did or did not do after this policy was entered into, section 54 of the Act applies. We may not refuse to pay the claim only because of that act or omission. Our liability is reduced by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced. Where the act or omission could reasonably be regarded as capable of causing or contributing to the loss, we may refuse to pay only the part of the claim that the act or omission caused or contributed to. We may not refuse any part that you prove the act or omission did not cause. This applies to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation in this policy, not only to the claims conditions.

Order of Precedence

Where parts of your contract of insurance differ, they apply in this order: an endorsement shown in the schedule, then the schedule, then this wording.

Our Rights of Recovery

After we pay a claim, we may take over your rights to recover from anyone else, in your name, and you must cooperate with us. The Goods Carried by Subcontractors clause sets out when we do not seek recovery from a subcontractor. We will not seek recovery from your employee, unless the loss arose from the employee's serious or wilful misconduct.

Cancellation

You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. On the gross freight earnings basis, the refund is worked out on the premium adjusted to your actual gross freight earnings to the date of cancellation, subject to the minimum premium.

We may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you written notice as that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis.

Cancellation does not affect a claim for loss or damage that happened before it took effect.

Fraud

If a claim is made fraudulently, we may refuse to pay it, as s.56 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

Sanctions

We do not provide cover, pay any claim or provide any benefit under this policy to the extent that doing so would expose us to any sanction, prohibition or restriction under:

  • the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011 (Cth), the Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 (Cth), or regulations made under either Act; or

How to Make a Claim

  1. Make the vehicle, the goods and the site safe, and take reasonable steps to prevent further loss (see the Reduce the Loss obligation).
  2. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
  3. Tell the police about any theft, hijacking or malicious damage, and keep the report number.
  4. Tell us what happened and when, and give us the consignment note, the delivery docket, photographs, the invoice for the goods and any survey report.
  5. For a claim by the owner of the goods, send us the demand as soon as it is received, and do not admit liability (see the Admissions obligation).
  6. We will acknowledge the claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision as the General Insurance Code of Practice requires where the insurer subscribes to it.

E01 Livestock

Livestock is not excluded goods. Under Basic Load Cover we pay for the death of an animal, or its injury that requires it to be destroyed for humane reasons, caused by an event listed in that clause, at the value shown in the schedule for each animal or, if none is shown, its market value at the destination.

We also pay the reasonable cost of agisting, mustering and recovering livestock after an insured event, up to the amount shown in the schedule for each animal and for each event.

We do not pay for death or injury from disease, suffocation, crushing or trampling that an insured event did not cause, or from overloading of the deck.

Schedule variables: value for each animal; agistment and mustering limit for each animal (default $2,000) and for each event (default $50,000).

Goods are covered while the conveying vehicle, or the goods, cross Bass Strait or travel by sea to Kangaroo Island, the Whitsunday islands or another Australian island named in the schedule, as part of an insured transit. The goods must be stowed in or on the conveying vehicle, or in a container, and not on deck unless the vehicle itself is carried on deck.
