Freight Services Liability Insurance

Liability cover for freight forwarders, customs brokers, carriers and logistics operators, measured by their standard trading conditions, with cargo liability, errors and omissions, fines and penalties, and optional customs duty and public liability sections.

About This Policy

This policy is for businesses that arrange, carry, store or clear other people's goods: freight forwarders, non-vessel operating common carriers, customs brokers, carriers, warehouse operators and third-party logistics providers. The market also calls this cover freight forwarders' liability, logistics operators' liability, carriers' liability and customs brokers' liability.

It covers your legal liability arising from your freight services. It does not insure the goods themselves. The owner of the goods insures them under its own marine cargo or transit policy.

It has five sections. Cargo Liability, Errors and Omissions, and Fines and Penalties always apply. Customs Duty Liability and Public Liability apply only if the schedule shows them as operative.

Your standard trading conditions set the measure of cover. This policy pays the liability you would have under the standard trading conditions named in the schedule, as they take effect subject to any law that the parties cannot exclude by contract. The Measure of Cover provision explains how this works, including when your conditions did not form part of a contract.

Three sections are claims-made. Errors and Omissions, Fines and Penalties and Customs Duty Liability cover claims first made against you during the policy period that you tell us about during the policy period, or within the time the Extended Reporting Period extension allows. They do not cover a claim made after that time, unless you told us during the policy period about facts that might give rise to it. You have that right under section 40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), and the Notification of Circumstances clause sets it out. These sections also do not cover errors committed before the retroactive date shown in the schedule.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Where an endorsement differs from either, the endorsement applies. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

Definitions

These words have the meanings below wherever they appear in this policy, the schedule or an endorsement. The singular includes the plural and the plural includes the singular.

Authority

A government department, agency or regulator, in Australia or in another country through which goods move in the course of the freight services, that has power over customs, border control, biosecurity, ports, shipping, aviation, road transport or dangerous goods. It includes the Australian Border Force and the Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry, and any successor to either.

Claim
  • A written demand for compensation made against you; or
  • a writ, summons, cross-claim, arbitration or other proceeding in which compensation is sought from you.

For Fines and Penalties and Customs Duty Liability, a claim is a written notice from an authority that imposes, or states an intention to impose, a fine or penalty on you, or that demands duty, tax or charges from you. It is first made when you first receive it.

Compensation

Money you are legally liable to pay to a claimant as damages, under a judgment, an award or a settlement we have agreed to, including the claimant's costs and expenses awarded against you.

Control Group

The named insured's, or the relevant insured entity's, chief executive officer, chief financial officer, general counsel, head of risk or insurance, and directors, any person performing an equivalent role, and any person who is the nominee for a customs broker licence held by the named insured.

Customer

A person or entity for whom you provide the freight services, and any person who owns the goods, has an interest in them or holds a bill of lading or other document of title for them.

Customs Entry

An import declaration, export declaration, request for amendment or refund, or other document or electronic communication that you lodge with an authority as customs broker or agent for a customer.

Defence Costs

Reasonable legal costs and expenses, including the fees of surveyors and other experts, incurred by us, or by you with our written consent, in investigating, defending, settling or appealing a claim. For a notice from an authority, they include the reasonable costs of responding to it, objecting to it or seeking review of it. We will not unreasonably withhold or delay consent.

Endorsement

A written change to this policy that we issue and that is shown in the schedule. The available endorsements are listed in the Endorsement Library.

Error

An actual or alleged negligent act, error, omission, misstatement or breach of duty in providing the freight services, by you or by a person for whose conduct you are legally responsible, including a subcontractor.

Excess

The amount shown in the schedule that you bear as the first part of each occurrence or claim. The schedule may show a different excess for each section, and special excesses. The Excess provision says how it applies.

Excluded Goods

The following goods, unless the schedule shows them as insured:

  • money, including cash, coins, bank notes, cheques, credit and debit cards, and stored-value cards;
  • bullion, precious metals, precious stones and jewellery;
  • securities, bonds, negotiable instruments, stamps and other documents of value, other than the shipping and customs documents for the goods;
  • works of art, antiques and collectors' items;
  • live animals and human remains;
  • explosives (Class 1 dangerous goods) and radioactive material (Class 7 dangerous goods); and
  • household goods and personal effects being moved for a private individual.

The schedule may add goods to this list.

Final Adjudication

A judgment, decision or ruling of a court, tribunal or arbitrator of competent jurisdiction, after all appeals or reviews have been decided or the time to appeal or seek review has passed without one being filed, that finally determines the relevant conduct. A written admission of the conduct by the insured concerned has the same effect. A consent order, settlement or compromise that does not include a determination or admission of the conduct is not a final adjudication.

Freight Services

The services described in the schedule that you provide in the course of your business, from your premises in Australia or in any other country shown in the schedule, as:

  • a freight forwarder, arranging the carriage of goods by sea, air, road, rail or post, as agent or as principal;
  • a non-vessel operating common carrier or contracting carrier, issuing your own bills of lading, air waybills or consignment notes;
  • a carrier, carrying goods in vehicles you operate;
  • a warehouse or depot operator, receiving, storing and dispatching goods;
  • a third-party logistics provider, including packing, consolidation, pick and pack, labelling and distribution; or
  • a customs broker or agent, lodging customs entries and arranging clearances, permits and inspections.

They include services that are incidental to these, such as preparing documents, arranging inspections, collecting amounts on delivery and arranging insurance of goods as agent for a customer, and services that subcontractors perform for you.

Goods

Property belonging to someone other than you, including its packaging, that you have agreed to carry, store or handle in the course of the freight services, or that is in the care, custody or control of you or a subcontractor in the course of them.

Gross Receipts

All amounts you charge for the freight services, including freight, fees, commission and the amounts you pay to subcontractors and carriers. Gross receipts do not include goods and services tax, or duty, taxes and other charges that you pay for customers and recover from them without a margin.

Named Insured

The person or entity named in the schedule as the insured.

Occurrence

An event, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general conditions, that results in physical loss of or damage to goods, delay in delivering goods, personal injury or property damage that the insured claiming cover did not expect or intend. How occurrences are counted is set out in the Counting Occurrences and Claims provision.

Personal Injury
  • Bodily injury, sickness, disease, disability or death; and
  • mental injury, nervous shock, mental anguish or emotional distress, whether or not it results from bodily injury.
Policy Period

The period shown in the schedule.

Pollutant

Any solid, liquid, gaseous, biological, radiological or thermal irritant or contaminant, including smoke, vapour, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals and waste. Waste includes material to be recycled, reconditioned or reclaimed.

Property Damage

Physical loss of, damage to or destruction of tangible property, including the loss of use of that property. It also includes loss of use of tangible property that is not physically lost, damaged or destroyed, if the loss of use is caused by an occurrence.

Retroactive Date

The date shown in the schedule as the retroactive date. If no date is shown, it is the date this policy first started or, if this policy renews or replaces an earlier policy that we issued to the named insured without a break in cover, the date the earliest of those policies started.

Standard Trading Conditions

The terms and conditions on which you provide the freight services that are named in the schedule, in the version in force at the start of the policy period, and any later version we agree to in writing.

Subcontractor

A carrier, shipping line, airline, warehouse or depot operator, freight forwarder, customs broker, agent or other person or entity, other than your employee, that you engage to perform any part of the freight services.

Subsidiary

An entity that is a subsidiary of the named insured under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) at the start of the policy period, or that becomes one under the Newly Acquired Subsidiaries extension.

We, Us, Our

The insurer named in the schedule.

You, Your

What Is Covered

Cargo Liability, Errors and Omissions, and Fines and Penalties always apply. Customs Duty Liability and Public Liability apply only if the schedule shows them as operative. The Which Section Responds provision says which section covers each kind of loss.

Cargo Liability

We will pay the compensation you become legally liable to pay for physical loss of or damage to goods, and for delay in delivering goods, that:

  • happens during the policy period, wherever the goods are; and
  • is caused by an occurrence in the course of the freight services.

This includes your legal liability:

  • as agent, bailee, carrier, contracting carrier under a bill of lading, air waybill or consignment note you issue, warehouse operator or customs broker;
  • for the acts and omissions of your subcontractors;
  • for loss of goods by theft, including theft by your employee or a subcontractor; and
  • for loss of goods delivered to the wrong person, or released to a person who obtained them by deception.

Liability for delay is covered only to the extent your standard trading conditions, or a law that overrides them, make you liable for it.

The Measure of Cover provision sets the most we pay. We also pay defence costs and supplementary payments, as set out in the Defence Costs clause and the Supplementary Payments clause.

Errors and Omissions

If a claim is first made against you during the policy period, and you tell us about it during the policy period or within the time allowed by the Extended Reporting Period extension, we will pay the compensation you become legally liable to pay for financial loss caused by an error committed on or after the retroactive date.

This section covers financial loss that is not physical loss of or damage to goods and is not caused by delay in delivering goods. Examples are loss caused by:

  • sending goods to the wrong destination, so that they must be re-forwarded;
  • an error in a document, including a bill of lading, air waybill, certificate of origin, permit, or a document presented under a letter of credit;
  • failing to follow a customer's written instructions;
  • an error in a customs entry, including a wrong tariff classification, value or origin, or failing to claim a concession, refund or drawback the customer was entitled to; and
  • failing to arrange insurance of goods when a customer instructed you in writing to arrange it, or arranging it on terms other than those instructed.

For a failure to arrange insurance, we pay no more than the insurance you were instructed to arrange would have paid.

The Measure of Cover provision sets the most we pay. We also pay defence costs, as set out in the Defence Costs clause.

Fines and Penalties

If an authority first gives you notice of a fine or penalty during the policy period, and you tell us about it during the policy period or within the time allowed by the Extended Reporting Period extension, we will pay:

  • the fine or penalty imposed on you, including an infringement notice or administrative penalty, because of an error committed on or after the retroactive date; and
  • defence costs.

We do not pay:

  • a fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured, including a penalty under work health and safety law in a state or territory whose law prohibits insurance of it (including New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland), and any criminal fine;
  • a fine or penalty imposed for conduct that you, or a member of your control group, knew was a breach of the law when it took place, as established by final adjudication;
  • a fine or penalty for paying duty, tax or other charges late, where you held the money to pay them on time;
  • a fine or penalty for a road traffic, parking or speeding offence committed in driving a vehicle;
  • duty, tax or other charges, which are covered only under Customs Duty Liability if it is operative; or
  • the cost of complying with any order, condition or direction.

Customs Duty Liability

This section applies only if the schedule shows it as operative.

If an authority first makes a demand on you during the policy period, and you tell us about it during the policy period or within the time allowed by the Extended Reporting Period extension, we will pay:

  • duty, goods and services tax, and other taxes and charges on goods, and interest charged on them, that you become legally liable to pay in your own right because of an error in a customs entry lodged on or after the retroactive date; and
  • defence costs.

We pay only the amount you cannot recover from the owner of the goods, or from anyone else, after taking reasonable steps to recover it.

We do not pay:

  • an amount you agreed to pay under a guarantee, security or other contract, unless you would have been liable for it without that contract;
  • an amount arising from a customs entry that you knew was wrong when you lodged it; or
  • a fine or penalty, which is covered only under Fines and Penalties.

A claim by a customer for duty it paid, or could not recover, because of your error is covered under Errors and Omissions, not under this section.

Public Liability

This section applies only if the schedule shows it as operative.

We will pay the compensation you become legally liable to pay because of personal injury, or property damage to property other than goods, that:

  • happens during the policy period; and
  • is caused by an occurrence in connection with the freight services.

We also pay defence costs and supplementary payments, as set out in the Defence Costs clause and the Supplementary Payments clause.

Notification of Circumstances

This clause applies to Errors and Omissions, Fines and Penalties and Customs Duty Liability.

If during the policy period you become aware of facts that might give rise to a claim, and you tell us about them in writing during the policy period, any claim that later arises from those facts is treated as a claim first made during this policy period. This clause implements section 40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). If that section gives you greater protection than this clause, it applies instead.

Your notice should include, as far as you are able: why you think a claim might arise; details of the error involved; who the claimant or authority might be; and an estimate of the amount.

Defence Costs

For a claim this policy may cover, we have the right to take over and conduct the defence or settlement of the claim in your name. For a claim under Cargo Liability or Public Liability, we also have the duty to defend it, even if it is groundless, false or fraudulent. We will choose the lawyers and manage the defence, and we will consult you about strategy.

We pay defence costs as they are incurred and do not wait until the claim is resolved. The Limits provision says whether defence costs are in addition to, or part of, the limit.

We will not settle a claim under Errors and Omissions, Fines and Penalties or Customs Duty Liability without your consent, which you will not unreasonably withhold. If you do not consent to a settlement that we recommend and the claimant or authority would accept, the most we pay for that claim is the amount of that settlement and the defence costs incurred up to the date you refused.

Supplementary Payments

For a claim covered under Cargo Liability or Public Liability, we also pay, in addition to the limit:

  • interest on a judgment against you that accrues after judgment and before we pay or offer to pay our share of it;
  • the cost of appeal bonds and bonds to release attachments, up to the limit (we do not have to provide the bonds); and
  • your reasonable expenses, with our consent, including loss of earnings of your directors and employees for attending court or hearings at our request, up to $500 a day for each person or the amount shown in the schedule.

Extensions of Cover

The following extensions are included automatically. Each is subject to the terms of this policy and to the limit of the section it extends. A sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, that limit. The default sub-limits are in the Sub-limits provision; a different amount shown in the schedule replaces the default.

Costs of Preventing or Reducing Loss

If, during the policy period, something happens in the course of the freight services that has caused, or is about to cause, a loss for which you would be legally liable under Cargo Liability or Errors and Omissions, we pay the reasonable costs you incur to prevent or reduce that loss. Examples are re-packing or re-conditioning goods, moving them to another conveyance or warehouse, re-forwarding goods sent to the wrong destination, and survey fees.

You must ask for our consent before incurring the costs, unless it is not practicable to do so in the time available. We do not pay more than the amount we would have paid for the loss the costs were incurred to prevent or reduce. The most we pay is the sub-limit for this extension.

This extension applies despite the Your Fees and Charges exclusion.

Removal and Disposal of Damaged Goods

If goods suffer physical loss or damage for which you are legally liable and which Cargo Liability covers, we pay the reasonable costs you incur, or that an authority or the owner of the goods requires you to pay, to remove, destroy or dispose of the damaged goods. The most we pay is the sub-limit for this extension.

Cargo Pollution Clean-up

If goods escape, spill or leak from their packaging or container during the policy period, because of an occurrence that Cargo Liability covers, we pay the costs you are legally liable to pay, or that an authority requires you to pay, to clean up, remove, contain or neutralise the goods and any pollutant released from them. The most we pay for any one occurrence, and for all occurrences in the policy period, is the sub-limit for this extension.

This extension applies despite the Pollution exclusion.

Continuous Cover

This extension applies to Errors and Omissions, Fines and Penalties and Customs Duty Liability.

If a claim is first made against you during the policy period, and:

  • it arises from facts that you first became aware of while insured under an earlier policy that we issued to the named insured;
  • you did not tell us about those facts under that earlier policy, through oversight and not fraud; and
  • you have been insured by us without a break in cover since then,

we will cover the claim under this policy, despite the Known Claims and Circumstances exclusion. The most we pay is the lower of the limit under this policy and the limit that applied under the earlier policy, and the terms of the earlier policy apply where they are narrower than this policy.

Extended Reporting Period

This extension applies to Errors and Omissions, Fines and Penalties and Customs Duty Liability.

If this policy is cancelled or is not renewed, for any reason other than non-payment of premium or fraud, and you do not take out other insurance that covers the same claims, you have an automatic extended reporting period of 90 days, starting when the policy period ends. During it you may tell us about claims first made against you during those 90 days that arise from errors committed on or after the retroactive date and before the policy period ended.

Instead of the automatic period, you may buy an extended reporting period of 12 months on the same basis. To do so, ask us within those 90 days and pay the premium shown in the schedule for it, or if none is shown, the premium we reasonably set by reference to the last annual premium.

A claim notified during an extended reporting period is treated as first made during the policy period. An extended reporting period does not increase or reinstate any limit.

Newly Acquired Subsidiaries

An entity that becomes a subsidiary during the policy period is covered automatically for 60 days from the date it becomes a subsidiary, or to the end of the policy period if sooner, if its business is freight services of a kind shown in the schedule and its annual gross receipts are not more than 20% of yours, or the percentage shown in the schedule. To continue cover after 60 days, you must give us details within those 60 days and pay any additional premium we reasonably require.

We do not cover the new subsidiary for an occurrence that happened, or an error committed, before it became a subsidiary.

Measure of Cover

This provision sets the most we pay under Cargo Liability and Errors and Omissions for a claim by a customer, or by anyone who claims through a customer or through the contract under which you provided the freight services.

The measure. We pay the compensation you are legally liable to pay, but not more than you would have been liable to pay if your standard trading conditions had formed part of the contract, as they take effect subject to any law that the parties cannot exclude by contract. This includes their limits of liability, exclusions and time limits.

Where your conditions did not form part of the contract. If your standard trading conditions did not form part of the contract, for example because you did not refer to them or give them to the customer before the contract was made, we still pay the claim, but only up to the amount in the measure above. We do not refuse the claim because the conditions did not apply.

Where a law overrides your conditions. If a law that the parties cannot exclude by contract makes you liable for more than your standard trading conditions allow, the measure includes that greater liability. Examples are:

  • the amended Hague Rules as they have effect under the Carriage of Goods by Sea Act 1991 (Cth), or another sea carriage regime that applies by law;
  • the Montreal Convention or another convention given effect by the Civil Aviation (Carriers' Liability) Act 1959 (Cth);
  • a convention or law that governs international road or rail carriage in another country;
  • the unfair contract terms provisions of the Australian Consumer Law or the Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001 (Cth), where a court or tribunal decides that a term of your standard trading conditions is unfair and void; and
  • the consumer guarantees in the Australian Consumer Law.

Contracts on other terms. If you agreed in writing to terms that make you liable for more than your standard trading conditions, such as a customer's own contract, a higher limit per kilogram or an indemnity, we cover the extra liability only if the contract is named in the schedule under Endorsement E01, and only as far as that endorsement allows.

Declared value. If a customer declared a value for the goods, or paid you for a higher limit of liability, under your standard trading conditions, the measure includes that higher liability, up to the Cargo Liability limit, if the declared value or higher limit is recorded in your documents for the consignment.

Which Section Responds

Each kind of loss is covered under one section only:

  • physical loss of or damage to goods, including loss by theft, delivery to the wrong person or release to a person who obtained the goods by deception, and liability for delay in delivering goods, is covered only under Cargo Liability;
  • personal injury, and property damage to property other than goods, is covered only under Public Liability, if it is operative;
  • other financial loss caused by an error is covered only under Errors and Omissions;
  • a fine or penalty imposed on you is covered only under Fines and Penalties; and
  • duty, taxes and charges you become liable to pay in your own right are covered only under Customs Duty Liability, if it is operative.

If one occurrence or one error causes losses of more than one kind, each section pays its part up to its own limit, and only one excess applies, as the Excess provision says. If a section is not operative, the loss it would have covered is not covered under another section.

Counting Occurrences and Claims

Occurrences. All physical loss of or damage to goods, delay, personal injury and property damage attributable to one source or original cause is one occurrence, whenever it happens and however many consignments, customers or people are affected. If the first loss, damage, delay or injury from an occurrence happens during the policy period, the whole occurrence is treated as happening in that policy period, and only the limit, excess and terms of that policy period apply to it.

Claims. All claims arising from the same error, or from errors that have the same source or original cause, are one claim. It is treated as first made when the earliest of them was first made, and only the limit, excess and terms of the policy period in which it was first made apply to it.

[WP REVIEW: confirm that aggregating thefts or misdeliveries by the same person, over time, as one occurrence is the intended position for this class.]

Excess

You bear the excess shown in the schedule for each occurrence under Cargo Liability and Public Liability, and for each claim under the other sections. It applies to compensation, fines, duty and the costs we pay under the extensions. It does not apply to defence costs unless the schedule says so.

If a special excess is shown in the schedule or an endorsement for a particular kind of occurrence or claim, it applies instead of the standard excess. If one occurrence or one error results in payments under more than one section, or more than one excess could apply, you bear only one excess: the highest that applies.

You may settle a claim within the excess without our consent, but a settlement you make does not bind us on any later claim.

Allocation

Schedule variables: the warehouses and depots it applies to; any agreed exception.

E04 Customs Broker Licence Investigation Costs

We pay the reasonable costs you incur, with our consent, to respond to an investigation, inquiry, show-cause notice or disciplinary proceeding by the Australian Border Force, the Comptroller-General of Customs or the National Customs Brokers Licensing Advisory Committee about a customs broker licence held by you or by your nominee. The investigation must first be notified to you during the policy period and must arise from customs broking you performed on or after the retroactive date.

The most we pay for all investigations in the policy period is $50,000 or the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement. These costs are part of, and reduce, the Errors and Omissions limit.

We do not pay a fine, a penalty or the cost of complying with any condition, order or direction.

Schedule variables: sub-limit (default $50,000, as shown in the schedule).

E05 Containers and Equipment

The Property You Own, Hire or Lease exclusion does not apply to containers, trailers, flat racks, pallets and other equipment that a carrier or lessor provides and that you take into your possession under an equipment interchange agreement or a similar arrangement. We cover your legal liability for physical loss of or damage to that equipment as if it were goods, up to the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement for any one occurrence. The sub-limit is part of the Cargo Liability limit.

We do not cover wear and tear, gradual deterioration, mechanical breakdown, or detention, demurrage, per diem or hire charges.

Schedule variables: sub-limit any one occurrence; any special excess.

E06 Duty on Goods in Your Custody

We pay the duty, goods and services tax and other charges that you become legally liable to pay to the Commonwealth because goods under customs control were lost, stolen or destroyed during the policy period while in your custody, including in a warehouse or depot you operate under a licence granted under the Customs Act 1901 (Cth). We pay only the amount you cannot recover from the owner of the goods, or from anyone else, after taking reasonable steps to recover it.

The most we pay for any one occurrence, and for all occurrences in the policy period, is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement. It is part of the Cargo Liability limit. This endorsement adds this cover to Cargo Liability, despite the Which Section Responds provision. It does not cover any fine or penalty.

E07 Release Fraud Sub-limit

The most we pay under Cargo Liability for loss of goods released to a person who obtained them by deception is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement, for any one occurrence and for all occurrences in the policy period. Deception includes impersonating a customer, carrier or driver, using false or altered documents or release instructions, and using a computer system or electronic communication to deceive you or a subcontractor.

The sub-limit does not apply if, before the goods were released, you or your subcontractor followed the verification procedure shown in the schedule for this endorsement. If no procedure is shown, the sub-limit applies to every such loss.

Schedule variables: sub-limit any one occurrence and in the aggregate; verification procedure.

E08 USA and Canada Operations

The USA and Canada exclusion does not apply to the freight services you provide from the offices, warehouses and depots in the United States of America or Canada named in the schedule for this endorsement.

If the schedule shows it for this endorsement, the exclusion also does not apply to claims under Public Liability brought in a court in the United States of America or Canada that arise from those freight services. Defence costs for those claims are part of the limit, as the Limits provision provides.

Schedule variables: the locations; whether Public Liability claims brought in United States or Canadian courts are covered; any special excess.
