Marine Cargo Insurance

An annual open transit policy for Australian importers, exporters and distributors, built on the Institute Cargo Clauses (A), (B) and (C), with inland transit cover within Australia.

About This Policy

This is an annual open policy for goods in transit. It insures goods you send and receive during the policy period, without a separate declaration for each transit. It is built on the Institute Cargo Clauses, which are the standard cargo terms of the London and Australian markets. This wording does not repeat those clauses. It names the clauses that apply and adds what they do not say: the transits insured, Australian law, the basis of valuation, the limit any one conveyance, the goods that are excluded, cover for transits within Australia, and how the premium is adjusted.

It has two sections. The schedule shows which apply.

SectionWhat it coversBasis
TransitLoss of or damage to goods in transit: on the Institute Cargo Clauses for import and export transits, and on [[ref:mcg.cover.inland-transitheading]] within AustraliaTransits that start during the policy period
War and StrikesWar, strikes, riot, civil commotion and terrorism risks, on the Institute War and Strikes ClausesTransits that start during the policy period

The schedule shows the kinds of transit insured (import, export and inland), the cover level for the Transit section, (A), (B) or (C), the limit any one conveyance and the excess.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal, the Institute clauses named in it and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. The How the Parts of This Policy Fit Together condition says which part applies where they differ. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

Who is insured. You are the business named in the schedule. A person who holds a certificate of insurance issued under this policy is insured for the transit the certificate describes, as the Certificates of Insurance extension sets out.

Your duty when you apply. This policy contains two kinds of insurance, and the law sets a different duty for each.

  • Import and export transits are marine insurance, governed by the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth). Before the policy is entered into, you must disclose to us every circumstance you know that is material to the risk: every circumstance that would influence a prudent insurer in fixing the premium or deciding whether to take the risk. You do not need to disclose a circumstance that reduces the risk, that we know or are presumed to know, or that we waive. If you do not disclose a material circumstance, or you misrepresent one, that Act allows us to avoid the contract.
  • Inland transits are governed by the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated, you have the duty of disclosure in s.21 of that Act: you must tell us every matter you know, or that a reasonable person in your circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we waive. If you do not comply, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

Definitions

The most we pay for goods accumulated at any one place in the course of transit, as the Accumulation extension sets out.

Annual Sendings

The total value, on the basis in the Basis of Valuation provision, of all goods carried in transits insured by this policy that start during the policy period.

Conveyance

A vessel, aircraft, road vehicle, railway wagon or container in which goods are carried. For goods sent by post or courier, the postal or courier service is the conveyance.

Endorsement

A written change to this policy that we issue and that is shown in the schedule. The available endorsements are listed in the Endorsement Library.

Excess

The amount you pay towards each claim, as shown in the schedule.

Excluded Goods

The goods listed in the Excluded Goods exclusion, unless the schedule names them as goods insured.

Export Transit

A transit of goods that begins in Australia and ends outside Australia.

Goods

Goods, merchandise and materials of the kinds described in the schedule, including their packing, in which you have an insurable interest at the time of the loss or damage.

Import Transit

A transit of goods that begins outside Australia and ends in Australia.

Inland Transit

A transit of goods that begins and ends in Australia, by road, by rail, by sea between Australian ports, by air within Australia, or by post or courier within Australia.

Institute Clauses

The clauses published by the Lloyd's Market Association and the International Underwriting Association of London, through their Joint Cargo Committee, that are named by number and date in the Institute Clauses That Apply clause, the War and Strikes Section clause or an endorsement.

Limit Any One Conveyance

The most we pay for loss of or damage to goods carried on or in any one conveyance, or held at any one place in the course of transit, as shown in the schedule.

Own Vehicle

Policy Period

The period shown in the schedule, unless this policy is cancelled earlier.

Professional Carrier

Theft-Attractive Goods

Transit

The carriage of goods insured by this policy, for the period of cover set out in the When Transit Starts and Ends clause.

We, Us, Our

What We Cover

This part sets out the Transit section and the War and Strikes section. Each section applies only if the schedule shows it.

Transit Section

We insure the goods against loss or damage during transit, for the kinds of transit shown in the schedule:

  • for import transits and export transits, on the terms of the Institute Clauses that the Institute Clauses That Apply clause applies to the transit and to the cover level shown in the schedule; and
  • for inland transits, on the terms of the Inland Transit Cover clause at the cover level shown in the schedule.

Every transit of a kind shown in the schedule that starts during the policy period is insured automatically. You do not need to declare each transit to us. You tell us your annual sendings as the Premium and Annual Sendings provision requires.

For any one conveyance, or any one place in the course of transit, we pay up to the limit any one conveyance, less the excess. The Accumulation extension applies where goods accumulate at a place through circumstances beyond your control. General average and salvage charges are paid in addition to the limit any one conveyance where the Institute Clauses provide for them, up to the proportion that the insured value of the goods bears to their contributory value.

Inland Transit Cover

For inland transits, we insure the goods at the cover level shown in the schedule.

Cover (A). All risks of loss of or damage to the goods.

Cover (B). Loss of or damage to the goods reasonably attributable to:

  • fire or explosion;
  • the conveyance overturning or being derailed, or colliding with or striking any external object other than water;
  • collapse of a bridge, building or other structure onto the conveyance;
  • earthquake, landslide, lightning, storm or flood;
  • the stranding, grounding, sinking or capsizing of a vessel or ferry carrying the conveyance or the goods;
  • entry of sea, lake or river water into the conveyance, container or place of storage;
  • the total loss of any package lost overboard or dropped while being loaded onto or unloaded from the conveyance; and
  • theft of an entire package, or non-delivery of an entire package, while in the custody of a professional carrier.

Cover (C). Loss of or damage to the goods reasonably attributable to:

  • fire or explosion;
  • the conveyance overturning or being derailed, or colliding with or striking any external object other than water;
  • collapse of a bridge onto the conveyance; and
  • the stranding, grounding, sinking or capsizing of a vessel or ferry carrying the conveyance or the goods.

Exclusions for inland transits. The exclusions of the Institute Cargo Clauses (A) apply to inland transits under Cover (A), and the exclusions of the Institute Cargo Clauses (B) apply under Covers (B) and (C), as if they were written here. For this purpose, those exclusions are read as follows:

  • a reference to a vessel, craft, carrying vessel or carrying conveyance includes the conveyance used for the inland transit;
  • the exclusions for unseaworthiness and for war do not apply to inland transits, and loss or damage caused by war is excluded under the War on Land exclusion; and
  • the exclusion for strikes, riots, civil commotions and terrorism applies, except to the extent that the War and Strikes section covers them.

We also pay the cost of extinguishing a fire, removing debris of the goods, and reloading and forwarding the goods to their destination after an insured event, where the cost is reasonably incurred, up to 10% of the limit any one conveyance in addition to it.

When Transit Starts and Ends

Import and export transits. The Transit Clause, the Termination of Contract of Carriage Clause and the Change of Voyage Clause of the Institute Clauses that apply set when cover starts and ends. Cover for an import transit ends, at the latest, when the goods are delivered to the final warehouse or place of storage in Australia named in the contract of carriage, or at the end of the period after discharge that the Transit Clause allows.

Inland transits. Cover starts when the goods are first moved in the warehouse, premises or place of storage at the start of the transit, for the purpose of loading them onto the conveyance for the transit. It continues during the ordinary course of transit, including loading, unloading, transhipment and storage in the ordinary course of transit. It ends at the first of:

  • completion of unloading at the place of delivery named in the contract of carriage, or chosen by you;
  • 7 days after the conveyance arrives at the town or locality of that place of delivery; and
  • when you or the consignee choose to use the goods, or to store them, other than in the ordinary course of transit.

Storage in the course of an inland transit for more than 7 days at any one place is not insured unless the E09 Storage in Transit endorsement applies.

Transits that start before the policy period ends. A transit that starts during the policy period stays insured until it ends, even if it ends after the policy period, provided it continues without delay within your control.

Extensions

The following extensions apply to the Transit section. They are subject to the limit any one conveyance unless they say otherwise, and to the other terms of this policy.

Accumulation

If, through circumstances beyond your control, goods insured by this policy accumulate at any one place in the course of transit to a value above the limit any one conveyance, we cover them up to twice the limit any one conveyance, or up to the accumulation limit shown in the schedule if one is shown. Examples are goods held at a port or depot after a delay, a missed connection or a carrier's failure.

You must tell us as soon as you know of the accumulation. We may charge an additional premium on the value above the limit any one conveyance, at the rate shown in the schedule.

Errors and Omissions

An unintentional error or omission by you in describing the goods, the conveyance or the route of a transit does not prejudice your cover, provided you tell us as soon as you discover it and pay any additional premium we reasonably charge. This extension does not insure excluded goods, or a transit of a kind not shown in the schedule.

Certificates of Insurance

Where you sell goods on terms that require you to insure them for the buyer, such as CIF or CIP under Incoterms 2020, we will issue a certificate of insurance for the transit at your request.

A certificate is evidence of cover under this policy for the transit it describes. The person named in it, or to whom it is endorsed, may claim under this policy for that transit as if they were insured, subject to the terms of this policy and of the certificate.

A certificate may show a cover level higher than the schedule shows, for example cover (A) where the sale is on CIP terms, only if you ask us before the transit starts and we agree. We may charge an additional premium for the higher cover level.

Packing Materials and Labels

Where only labels, capsules, wrappers or packing are lost or damaged, we pay the reasonable cost of replacing them and repacking or relabelling the goods, and not for loss of value of the goods themselves.

How We Pay Claims

This part applies to claims under both sections.

Basis of Valuation

Goods are valued as follows, both for the annual sendings and for claims, unless the schedule states another basis.

TransitValue
Import transitsThe invoice value plus freight and the cost of insurance, plus 10%
Export transitsThe invoice value plus freight and the cost of insurance, plus 10%, or the value a certificate of insurance shows where the sale contract requires a certificate
Inland transits of goods sold or boughtThe invoice value plus freight, plus 10%
Inland transits of goods you own that are not sold or bought, such as transfers between your premisesThe cost of the goods to you plus freight

The value is the agreed value of the goods for the Institute Clauses. Where an invoice is in a foreign currency, it is converted to Australian dollars at the rate on the date of the invoice. Import duty and taxes are not part of the value unless the E07 Import Duty and Taxes endorsement applies.

For a partial loss, we pay the agreed value of the goods lost, or the proportion of the agreed value that the reduction in sound value bears to the sound value, or the reasonable cost of repair or reconditioning, whichever is less.

Excess

We deduct the excess shown in the schedule from each claim for loss or damage from one event, or to goods on one conveyance. If an endorsement shows a special excess for the loss or damage, that excess replaces the excess shown in the schedule.

The excess does not apply to general average or salvage charges, or to a total loss of all the goods on a conveyance.

Premium and Annual Sendings

Unless the schedule says the premium is not adjustable, the premium is a deposit calculated on your estimate of the annual sendings for each kind of transit.

Within 60 days after the end of the policy period, tell us the actual annual sendings for each kind of transit. We will adjust the premium at the rates shown in the schedule. If the adjusted premium is higher than the deposit, you pay the difference. If it is lower, we refund the difference, but we keep at least the minimum premium shown in the schedule.

If you do not tell us the actual annual sendings within that time, we may ask to see your records under the Records of Sendings obligation, and adjust the premium on them.

GST on Claims

Where you are entitled to an input tax credit for the GST on the cost of replacing or repairing goods, we pay the claim net of that credit. Each limit in this policy includes GST to the extent you are not entitled to an input tax credit for it.

Exclusions

These exclusions apply in addition to the exclusions in the Institute Clauses that apply.

Excluded Goods

This policy does not insure the following goods unless the schedule names them as goods insured:

  • livestock and other live animals;
  • bulk cargo carried loose in the hold of a vessel or in a bulk tanker, silo or hopper;
  • cash, bank notes, coins, securities, negotiable instruments, credit and debit cards, tickets and vouchers;
  • bullion, precious metals, precious stones and jewellery;
  • works of art, antiques and collectibles;
  • household goods and personal effects being moved;
  • motor vehicles, mobile plant, aircraft and boats, while travelling under their own power;
  • explosives, fireworks and ammunition;
  • tobacco and vaping products; and
  • goods carried on the deck of a vessel other than in a closed container.

Transits Outside Australia

This policy does not insure a transit that neither begins nor ends in Australia, unless the schedule says it does.

War on Land

This policy does not insure loss or damage during an inland transit caused by war, civil war, revolution, rebellion, insurrection or civil strife arising from them, or any hostile act by or against a belligerent power, or by capture, seizure, arrest, restraint or detainment arising from them.

Delay and Loss of Market

This policy does not insure loss of market, loss of profit, or any loss caused by delay, even if the delay is caused by a risk insured, except general average and salvage charges.

Your Obligations

The Marine Insurance Act and Insurance Contracts Act condition sets out how the law limits what we may do if you do not meet these obligations.

Telling Us About a Loss

Tell us, or the surveyor or agent shown in the schedule or on the certificate, as soon as reasonably possible after you know of loss or damage. Keep the damaged goods and their packing until we, or our surveyor, have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect them, unless keeping them would be unsafe or unlawful.

Where loss or damage is apparent when the goods are delivered, note it on the delivery receipt before you sign it.

Preserving Rights Against Carriers

You must take reasonable steps to preserve the rights of recovery against a carrier, bailee or other person who may be responsible for loss or damage, including:

  • making a written claim on them within the time their contract of carriage or the law allows;
  • not giving a clean receipt for goods that are damaged or short; and
  • not agreeing to release them from liability or to limit their liability beyond the terms of their usual contract of carriage, without our agreement.

This obligation applies to all transits, and is in addition to the Duty of Assured Clause in the Institute Clauses for import and export transits.

Minimising Loss

For inland transits, you must take reasonable steps to avoid or minimise loss or damage, as the Duty of Assured Clause in the Institute Clauses requires for import and export transits. We pay the reasonable costs you incur in doing so, in addition to the claim, as that clause provides.

Records of Sendings

Keep records of each transit, such as invoices, bills of lading, air waybills and consignment notes, for at least two years after the end of the policy period. Let us inspect them on reasonable notice.

Telling Us About Changes

Tell us before you:

  • carry goods of a kind not described in the schedule;
  • carry goods by a kind of transit or conveyance not shown in the schedule;
  • send or receive goods to or from a country or area that you have not told us about, where the area is subject to sanctions or is listed as a war risk area by the Joint War Committee of the Lloyd's Market Association; or
  • expect the annual sendings for any kind of transit to exceed your estimate by more than 25%.

We may change the premium or terms for the change, or tell you that the change will not be insured, by notice to you. A change you tell us about is insured on the terms we agree.

General Conditions

These conditions apply to the whole policy.

Marine Insurance Act and Insurance Contracts Act

Import and export transits are marine insurance, governed by the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth). Inland transits, and any other part of this policy to which the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) does not apply, are governed by the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).

To the extent the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) applies, nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies any right you have under it. Where you fail to do something this policy requires, or do something it does not allow, after the policy is entered into, s.54 of that Act applies. We will not refuse a claim only because of that act or omission. We may reduce our payment by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced. We may refuse the claim only where the act or omission could reasonably be regarded as capable of causing or contributing to the loss, and then only as that section allows.

Cancellation

You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us in writing.

We may cancel this policy:

  • for inland transits, only on a ground allowed by the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), by giving you written notice as s.59 of that Act requires; and
  • for import and export transits, by giving you 30 days' written notice. The War and Strikes section may be cancelled on shorter notice as the War and Strikes Section clause sets out.

Cancellation does not affect a transit that started before it took effect. That transit stays insured until it ends under the When Transit Starts and Ends clause.

When this policy is cancelled, we adjust the premium under the Premium and Annual Sendings provision on the annual sendings for transits that started before cancellation, and we keep at least the proportion of the minimum premium for the time the policy was in force.

Fraudulent Claims

If a claim is made fraudulently, we may refuse to pay it. For inland transits, s.56 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) applies. For import and export transits, the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) applies.

Sanctions

We will not provide cover, pay any claim or provide any benefit under this policy to the extent that doing so would expose us, or any of our related bodies corporate, to any sanction, prohibition or restriction under the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011 (Cth), the Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 (Cth), or the trade or economic sanctions laws or regulations of the United Nations, the European Union, the United Kingdom or the United States of America. This condition applies in addition to the Sanction Limitation and Exclusion Clause named in the Institute Clauses That Apply clause.

GST

The premium includes GST where it applies, under A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth). Insurance of the international transport of goods may be GST-free; the schedule shows the GST charged. Claims are paid as the GST on Claims provision sets out. Tell us your entitlement to input tax credits when you make a claim.

Privacy

We collect, use and disclose personal information about you, your employees and others named in the proposal or a claim to assess the proposal, administer this policy and manage claims, in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and our privacy policy. Our privacy policy explains how a person can access and correct their information and how to complain about a privacy matter.

Complaints and Disputes

If you are unhappy with our service or a decision, contact us at the details in the schedule. We will acknowledge the complaint within one business day and give our decision within 30 calendar days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, or we have not responded within 30 calendar days, you may be able to take the complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), a free and independent dispute resolution service, if it is within AFCA's rules: www.afca.org.au, 1800 931 678, info@afca.org.au.

Headings and References

How to Make a Claim

  1. Take reasonable steps to protect the goods from further loss or damage (see the Minimising Loss obligation).
  2. Contact your broker, or contact us or the surveyor or agent shown in the schedule or on the certificate, as soon as you can.
  3. Make a written claim on the carrier, and keep the delivery receipt, the contract of carriage and the packing (see the Preserving Rights Against Carriers obligation).
  4. Tell the police about any theft.
  5. Give us the invoice, the packing list, the bill of lading, air waybill or consignment note, any survey report, and photographs of the damage.
  6. We will acknowledge your claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision.

E01 Temperature-Controlled Goods

This endorsement applies to goods that must be kept within a temperature range during transit, as described in the schedule for this endorsement.

We cover loss of or damage to those goods caused by a change in temperature that results from the breakdown of the refrigerating machinery or the insulation of the conveyance or container, where the breakdown lasts for at least the period shown in the schedule for this endorsement. This cover applies whatever the cover level shown in the schedule, and despite the exclusions in the Institute Clauses for inherent vice and for delay, to the extent of the change in temperature only.

It is a condition of this cover that:

  • the goods are in sound condition and at the required temperature when the transit starts;
  • the refrigerating machinery is set to the required temperature, and you keep, or ask the carrier to keep, a temperature record for the transit; and
  • the goods are carried by a professional carrier, or in your own vehicle fitted with a working temperature alarm.

We do not cover loss or damage caused by the goods being packed or loaded at the wrong temperature, by the machinery being switched off or set wrongly by you or your employees, or by a failure to supply power that lasts less than the period shown.

Schedule variables: goods and temperature range; minimum breakdown period (default 24 hours); special excess.

E02 Used Goods

For goods that are used, second-hand or reconditioned, the Transit section covers only:

  • for import and export transits by sea, the risks covered by the Institute Cargo Clauses (C), together with theft or non-delivery of an entire package;
  • for import and export transits by air, the risks covered by the Institute Cargo Clauses (Air), except that we do not cover rust, oxidation, scratching, denting, chipping or bending unless the conveyance is involved in an accident; and
  • for inland transits, the risks of Cover (C) in the Inland Transit Cover clause, together with theft or non-delivery of an entire package.

Schedule variables: none.

E03 Own Vehicle Carriage

For goods carried in your own vehicle:

  • we do not cover theft of goods from a vehicle left unattended, unless the vehicle is locked with all windows and doors closed and the goods are in a closed, locked compartment or under a locked cover, or the vehicle is in a locked building or a locked and fenced compound;
  • we do not cover theft from a vehicle left unattended overnight in the open, unless the schedule for this endorsement says otherwise;
  • goods must be secured against movement during the transit; and
  • the driver must hold a current licence for the vehicle.

The excess shown in the schedule for this endorsement applies to each claim for goods carried in your own vehicle.

Schedule variables: special excess (default the excess shown in the schedule); whether overnight parking in the open is allowed.

E04 Dangerous Goods

The goods include dangerous goods of the classes described in the schedule for this endorsement.

It is a condition of the cover for those goods that they are classified, packed, marked, labelled, documented and carried as the Australian Code for the Transport of Dangerous Goods by Road and Rail requires for inland transits, the International Maritime Dangerous Goods Code requires for transits by sea, and the IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations require for transits by air.

We do not cover liability of any kind, including for pollution or contamination, or the cost of clean-up, under this endorsement.

Schedule variables: dangerous goods classes; special excess.

E07 Import Duty and Taxes

For import transits, the value of the goods in the Basis of Valuation provision includes the import duty, GST and other taxes payable on their entry into Australia, where you pay them and cannot recover them after the loss.

We pay that part of the value only where the duty or tax has been paid or is payable and is not refunded or remitted because of the loss.

Schedule variables: none.

E08 Seller's Contingent Interest

For export transits of goods you sell on terms under which the buyer arranges the insurance, such as FOB, FCA or CFR under Incoterms 2020, we cover your interest in the goods if they are lost or damaged by a risk this policy insures and the buyer does not pay for them, or rejects them, because of the loss or damage.

We pay only to the extent you cannot recover from the buyer or the buyer's insurer. You must not tell the buyer or anyone else that this cover exists, and you must take reasonable steps to recover from the buyer, with our help if you ask for it.

Schedule variables: sub-limit any one conveyance.

E09 Storage in Transit

For inland transits, storage of the goods in the course of transit at the places shown in the schedule for this endorsement is insured for up to the number of days shown, despite the 7 day limit in the When Transit Starts and Ends clause.

The limit any one conveyance applies to each place of storage. It is a condition of this cover that each place of storage is a closed building, locked when unattended.

E10 High-Risk Areas

The transits to or from the countries or areas shown in the schedule for this endorsement are insured on the terms and at the additional premium shown for them. Any other transit to or from an area listed as a war risk area by the Joint War Committee of the Lloyd's Market Association, or to or from a country subject to sanctions under the laws named in the Sanctions condition, is insured only if you tell us before it starts and we agree.

This endorsement does not change the Sanctions condition.

Schedule variables: countries or areas; terms and additional premium for each.
