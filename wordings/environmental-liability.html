Environmental Liability Insurance

Contractors pollution liability and site pollution legal liability for Australian businesses: gradual pollution, clean-up costs and natural resource damage, on a claims-made basis.

About This Policy

This is an environmental liability policy. It has two sections.

The Contractors Pollution Liability clause covers claims and clean-up costs arising from pollution caused by the work you carry out at other people's sites.

The Site Pollution Legal Liability clause covers claims and clean-up costs arising from pollution on, at, under or migrating from the locations shown in the schedule. It applies only if the schedule shows it.

This policy is written on a claims-made and notified basis. It covers claims first made against you, and pollution conditions first discovered by you, during the policy period and notified to us during the policy period or any extended reporting period, for pollution conditions that began after the retroactive date.

This policy covers gradual pollution and the clean-up costs that environmental law requires, which public liability policies usually exclude. The Relationship With Public Liability Insurance clause explains how it works together with your public and products liability policy.

This policy, together with your schedule and any endorsements shown in it, forms your contract of insurance with us. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

This wording is written for businesses. It is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement. What Is Covered

Contractors Pollution Liability

We will pay loss that you become legally liable to pay because of personal injury, property damage, natural resource damage or clean-up costs resulting from a pollution condition that:

  • is caused by covered operations;
  • began on or after the retroactive date; and
  • results in a claim first made against you during the policy period and notified to us during the policy period or any extended reporting period, as the Claims Made and Notified clause sets out.

Site Pollution Legal Liability

This section applies only if the schedule shows it.

We will pay loss that you become legally liable to pay because of personal injury, property damage, natural resource damage or clean-up costs resulting from a pollution condition on, at, under or migrating from a covered location that began on or after the retroactive date, where:

  • a claim is first made against you during the policy period; or
  • you first discover the pollution condition during the policy period and environmental law requires clean-up costs for it,

and in either case you notify us during the policy period or any extended reporting period. A pollution condition that began before the retroactive date is covered only if the E01 Pre-existing Conditions endorsement applies.

Emergency Response Costs

We will pay emergency response costs for a pollution condition that would be covered by a section of this policy that applies, up to the sub-limit shown in the schedule, which is part of and not in addition to the limit of liability. You must tell us within seven days after you incur them.

Defence Costs

We have the right and duty to defend any claim covered by this policy, even if it is groundless. We pay defence costs as they are incurred. Defence costs are part of, and reduce, the limit of liability and the aggregate.

Claims Made and Notified

This policy covers only claims first made against you, and pollution conditions first discovered by you, during the policy period, and notified to us in writing during the policy period or within the extended reporting period under the Extended Reporting Period clause.

If, during the policy period, you become aware of facts that might give rise to a claim and you notify us of them in writing as soon as reasonably practicable and before the policy period ends, a claim later made arising from those facts is treated as made during the policy period. This has the effect provided in s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).

All claims and all clean-up costs arising from the same pollution condition, or from related pollution conditions, are treated as one claim first made when the first of them was made or the pollution condition was first discovered, as the Related Pollution Conditions provision sets out.

Extended Reporting Period

If this policy is cancelled or not renewed, for any reason other than non-payment of premium, and you have not replaced it with similar insurance, you have an automatic extended reporting period of 90 days after the end of the policy period. During it you may notify claims first made against you during that period, and pollution conditions first discovered during it, that arise from covered operations completed, or pollution conditions that began, before the end of the policy period.

You may buy a longer extended reporting period of up to three years by asking us in writing within 30 days after the end of the policy period and paying the additional premium shown in the schedule for it. An extended reporting period does not increase or reinstate the limit of liability.

Relationship With Public Liability Insurance

Most public liability policies, including the Meridian Public and Products Liability wording, exclude pollution unless it is caused by a sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected event, and exclude clean-up of your own site and gradual pollution.

This policy is written to fill that gap. It applies as primary insurance for gradual pollution conditions, for clean-up costs and for natural resource damage. For personal injury and property damage caused by a sudden and accidental pollution condition that your public liability insurance also covers, this policy applies in excess of that insurance, as the Other Insurance provision sets out.

How We Pay

Limit of Liability

The most we will pay for all loss arising from any one pollution condition, under all sections together, including defence costs, emergency response costs and any sub-limit, is the limit of liability for each pollution condition shown in the schedule.

The most we will pay for all loss in the policy period and any extended reporting period, under all sections together, is the aggregate limit of liability shown in the schedule. Each sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, these limits.

Related Pollution Conditions

All pollution conditions that arise from the same, similar or related causes, or that are continuous or repeated releases from the same source, are one pollution condition, however many claims are made or locations affected.

One pollution condition that gives rise to claims or clean-up costs over more than one policy period is treated as first made or first discovered in the policy period in which the first claim was made or the pollution condition was first discovered, and the limit of liability, excess and terms of that policy period apply to all of it.

Excess

You bear the excess for each pollution condition, which applies to all loss together, including defence costs. Where one pollution condition is covered under more than one section, you bear only the highest applicable excess once.

Other Insurance

For personal injury and property damage caused by a sudden and accidental pollution condition that is also covered by your public liability insurance, this policy is excess of that insurance and pays only after its limits have been paid.

In every other case, this policy is primary. If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by another environmental or pollution liability policy, we contribute with the other insurer in the proportion that our limit of liability bears to the total limits for that loss.

How Environmental Liability Cover Works

This guide explains this policy. It does not change its terms.

Public liability policies usually cover pollution only when it is sudden and accidental, and do not pay to clean up contamination that environmental law requires. This policy covers those gaps for the work you do at other people's sites and, if the schedule shows it, for your own sites.

For example:

  • a drainage contractor's pipe joint fails slowly over three months and contaminates a neighbour's soil; the neighbour sues and the environment protection authority issues a clean-up notice. Both the claim and the clean-up costs are covered under Contractors Pollution Liability;
  • a truck operated by your earthmoving business leaks hydraulic fluid into a creek at a job site; you call in a spill response contractor straight away. Those emergency response costs are covered without our prior consent, up to the sub-limit;
  • an old underground fuel tank at your own depot is found to have leaked before you bought the site. That is a pre-existing condition, covered only if Endorsement E01 is shown.

The policy is claims-made: tell us as soon as you know of a claim or a pollution condition, during the policy period.

E04 Project Term

For the project named in the schedule for this endorsement, the Contractors Pollution Liability clause applies with its own limit of liability, aggregate and policy period, as shown for the project, and claims arising from the project do not reduce the limits for your other covered operations. The project's policy period ends on the earlier of the date shown for it and three years after its start.

Schedule variables: the project and job site; the project limit and aggregate; the project policy period; the project premium.

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Offered because a single project is a large share of your turnover, or a contract requires project-specific cover.

E05 Asbestos Abatement

The Asbestos exclusion does not apply to property damage, natural resource damage or clean-up costs arising from the release of asbestos fibres during asbestos removal or abatement work that you carry out as covered operations, under a licence required by work health and safety law, at a job site. It still applies to personal injury. The most we will pay under this endorsement is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for it, which is part of and not in addition to the limit of liability.

Schedule variables: the sub-limit; the licence class held.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

E06 Prior Acts

The retroactive date for the Contractors Pollution Liability clause is the date shown in the schedule for this endorsement, which is the start of your continuous prior contractors pollution cover. The Known Conditions exclusion and the Prior Claims and Circumstances exclusion still apply.

Schedule variables: the retroactive date; the prior insurer.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

E07 Business Interruption

We will pay your loss of gross profit, and reasonable extra expense, while a covered location is wholly or partly closed because clean-up costs covered under the Site Pollution Legal Liability clause are being incurred, after the waiting period shown in the schedule and for no longer than the indemnity period shown. Once the waiting period is exceeded, loss is paid from the start of the closure. The most we will pay under this endorsement is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for it, which is part of and not in addition to the limit of liability.

Schedule variables: the covered locations; the sub-limit; the waiting period; the indemnity period.
