About This Policy

This policy, together with your schedule and any endorsements, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read all documents together and let us know if anything is incorrect.

Words shown in bold have special meanings defined in the Definitions section.

This wording implements the notification-of-circumstances protection required by s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) — see the Notification of Circumstances clause. Before you enter into this contract, your insurer or its distributor must give you written notice of the effect of s.40(3) as required by s.40(1) of that Act.

Version and Document History

This document is version-controlled. The version and date shown on the cover page and in the footer of each page govern this text. The version stated in your schedule as current at inception applies for the life of your policy period, unless your schedule or an endorsement states otherwise.

Schedule

(Completed separately for each policy — contains your specific details)

The schedule shows:

  • Named insured and any subsidiary
  • Professional services covered
  • Policy period — start and end dates
  • Retroactive date
  • Limit of liability — the most we will pay for all claims combined
  • Sub-limits — lower limits for specific cover types
  • Excess — the amount you pay first on each claim
  • Premium — including stamp duty and GST
  • Court attendance daily rate, if different from the policy default of $500 (see the Court Attendance extension)
  • Newly acquired subsidiary revenue threshold, if different from the policy default of $5,000,000 (see the Newly Acquired Subsidiaries extension)
  • Endorsements that change the standard terms, and any endorsement schedule (for example, the premium for a run-off extension offered and agreed under Endorsement E01)

Definitions

When the following words appear in bold in this policy, they have the meanings set out below.

Business activity

Your professional services, performed in the ordinary course of your business.

Claim

(a) any written demand for compensation made against you;

(b) any writ, summons, cross-claim, or other legal proceeding issued against you;

(c) any formal investigation or inquiry by a regulator that could lead to a finding of personal civil liability against you;

(d) any written assertion of a right against you under a statute that could lead to your civil liability; or

(e) any formal complaint or proceeding before a professional disciplinary body.

Two or more claims arising from the same, similar, or related act(s), error(s), or omission(s) are treated as a single claim first made when the earliest was first made.

Company

The entity named as the named insured in the schedule, and any subsidiary.

Computer system

Any computer, network, hardware, software, data storage, or communications system — including cloud services — owned, leased, or operated on your behalf.

Confidential information

Information that is not publicly available and that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including information protected by law.

Defence costs

The reasonable legal costs and expenses incurred with our prior written consent in investigating, defending, or settling a claim. We will not unreasonably withhold or delay consent.

Employee

Any natural person who works for you under a contract of employment, including permanent, fixed-term, and casual employees. Does not include independent contractors.

Endorsement

A document attached to this policy that changes its terms.

Excess

The amount shown in the schedule that you must pay as the first part of each claim, applied to compensation and defence costs combined unless the schedule states otherwise.

Final adjudication

A judgment, verdict, or ruling by a court, tribunal, or arbitrator of competent jurisdiction, after the exhaustion of all appeals or the expiry of the time to appeal without an appeal being filed, that finally determines the relevant conduct. A consent order, or a settlement, compromise, or admission of fact, that does not include a judicial or arbitral determination of the relevant conduct, is not a final adjudication for the purposes of this policy.

Geographical limits

The territory shown in the schedule.

Insured person

(a) any past, present, or future partner, principal, director, officer, or member of the company;

(b) any employee of the company; and

(c) any person who was a partner, principal, director, officer, member, or employee of a predecessor practice that the company has acquired, merged with, or succeeded.

Joint venture

Any incorporated or unincorporated arrangement between you and one or more other parties to carry out a specific business activity, in which you hold: (a) if incorporated, more than 50% of the voting rights; or (b) if unincorporated, an interest (however described) of 50% or more in its profits, losses, or capital.

Limit of liability

The maximum amount shown in the schedule we will pay for all claims and defence costs combined during the policy period, unless a specific sub-limit applies.

Loss

The amount you become legally liable to pay as compensation to a third party, including the claimant's legal costs awarded against you. Does not include fines, penalties, punitive or exemplary damages, taxes, or amounts not insurable at law.

Named insured

The entity shown as the named insured in the schedule.

Policy period

The period shown in the schedule, from the start date to the end date, both inclusive.

Pollution

Any release of, or exposure to, a substance or agent that contaminates or harms the environment, in any form (solid, liquid, gas, biological, radiological, thermal, or energy-based, including noise, vibration, and electromagnetic fields), whether actual, alleged, or threatened.

Predecessor practice

Any business you have acquired, merged with, or succeeded, whose professional services are the same as or similar to your business activity.

Professional services

The following services provided for a fee, a grant or other funding:

(a) education and training by a school, higher education provider or registered training organisation, including teaching, assessment, the issue of qualifications and statements of attainment, and student support;

(b) tutoring and coaching;

(c) course and curriculum design;

(d) recruiting and advising students as an education agent; and

(e) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

A service in (a) is a professional service only while you hold the registration that the law requires to provide it.

Retroactive date

The date shown in the schedule. We only cover claims arising from acts, errors, or omissions on or after this date.

Schedule

The document titled "Schedule" issued with this policy, as amended by any endorsement.

Subsidiary

Any entity in which the named insured holds more than 50% of the voting rights, or over which the named insured has effective management control, at the time the relevant act, error, or omission occurs.

We / us / our

The insurer named in the schedule.

You / your

(a) the company; and (b) any insured person.

What Is Covered

Claims Against You

If a claim is first made against you during the policy period, and the claim arises from your business activity performed on or after the retroactive date within the geographical limits, we will pay:

(a) any loss you become legally liable to pay; and

(b) your defence costs,

for any actual or alleged:

  • (i) negligence, breach of duty, or failure to exercise reasonable care and skill
  • (ii) negligent misstatement, misrepresentation, or misleading advice
  • (iii) breach of confidence or misuse of confidential information
  • (iv) infringement of copyright, trademark, or moral rights, or passing off
  • (v) unintentional defamation
  • (vi) breach of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) or any state or territory fair trading legislation, in connection with your professional services
  • (vii) dishonesty of your employees (where you are liable to a third party for your employee's conduct)
  • (viii) any other civil liability arising from your professional services

unless excluded under the What Is Not Covered section.

Notification of Circumstances

If during the policy period you become aware of any fact, matter, or circumstance that might reasonably be expected to give rise to a claim against you, and you tell us about it in writing during the policy period, then any claim that later arises from those facts will be treated as a claim first made during this policy period. This clause implements s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).

Your written notice must include: (a) the reasons why you think a claim might arise; (b) the details of the act, error, or omission involved; (c) the identity of any potential claimant (if known); and (d) an estimate of the potential value (if possible).

Avoiding a Potential Claim

This extension applies where: (a) your client disputes a fee or other amount you claim they owe for your professional services, and has given you specific reasons for that dispute that, if accepted, would found a claim against you under the Claims Against You clause; (b) your client has threatened, in writing or otherwise on the record, to bring such a claim for an amount exceeding the disputed fee; and (c) we reasonably consider that paying you the disputed amount is likely to avoid that claim being made.

Before acting under this extension, we will give you reasonable notice of our intention and consider any objection you raise. If we proceed, we may pay you the disputed amount (above the excess), and you agree not to pursue your client for that amount.

This extension does not limit your right to make a claim, and does not affect the trigger for a claim under the Claims Against You clause, if a claim is later made despite our payment under this extension. If a claim is still made, our total payment including what we have already paid under this extension will not exceed the limit of liability. If you later recover the debt, you must repay us less your reasonable collection expenses.

Extensions of Cover

The following extensions are included automatically and are subject to the limit of liability unless a separate sub-limit is stated.

Loss of Documents and Data

If during the policy period any document or electronic data necessary for your business activity — whether in tangible or electronic form — is lost, corrupted, damaged, or destroyed while in your care, custody, or control, we will pay the reasonable cost of restoring or replacing it, with our prior written consent. This extension responds regardless of whether the cause is a Cyber Event as described in the Cyber Event exclusion: that exclusion applies to your liability to third parties, and does not affect your own first-party cost of restoring your own documents or data under this extension.

First-Party Dishonesty

If during the policy period you discover that an employee has caused you a direct financial loss through dishonest or fraudulent conduct — intending to cause you loss and to obtain a personal financial benefit beyond their normal compensation — we will pay your direct financial loss. We will deduct any amounts the employee owes you or any property of the employee that you hold.

Statutory Liability

We will pay insurable fines, penalties, and defence costs arising from a prosecution or formal regulatory proceeding brought against you during the policy period under any Australian statute, in connection with your business activity.

We do not pay:

(a) any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured or indemnified, including a penalty under a work health and safety law that prohibits insurance or indemnity for it; or

(b) any amount arising from deliberate or reckless conduct.

Inquiry Costs

We will pay your defence costs in responding to a formal investigation or inquiry by a regulator (including ASIC, OAIC, ACCC, or a state or territory equivalent) into your conduct in connection with your business activity, provided the investigation is first notified to us during the policy period.

This extension applies to a formal investigation or inquiry that has not yet become a claim within paragraph (c) of the definition of Claim. If the investigation or inquiry becomes such a claim, cover for it continues under the Claims Against You clause instead of this extension, and the trigger, limit, and excess applicable to a claim apply from that point.

Continuous Cover

If a claim is made against you during the policy period and: (a) it arises from an act, error, or omission you should have notified under a previous PI policy; (b) you did not know about it at the time; (c) you had continuous PI cover in the interim; and (d) this policy provides equivalent or narrower cover than the previous policy — we will cover the claim as if it had been properly notified under the previous policy, subject to the terms of this policy, except that our liability will not exceed the lower of the limit of liability under this policy and the limit of liability that applied under the previous policy at the time of the act, error, or omission.

Mitigation Costs

We will pay the reasonable costs you incur, with our prior written consent, to prevent or reduce a loss that would otherwise be covered by this policy.

Public Relations Costs

Following a claim covered by this policy, we will pay the reasonable costs of engaging a public relations consultant to protect or restore your professional reputation.

Court Attendance

If any insured person or employee attends court, a tribunal, or an arbitration as a witness in connection with a claim covered by this policy, we will pay the amount shown in the schedule for each day or part-day of attendance required by us (or $500 for each day or part-day if no amount is shown in the schedule).

Newly Acquired Subsidiaries

If during the policy period you acquire or create a new subsidiary, it is automatically covered for 60 days — provided its annual revenue does not exceed the amount shown in the schedule for this purpose (or $5,000,000 if no amount is shown) and its business activity is the same as or similar to yours. You must tell us within 60 days; we may adjust the premium or terms.

Joint Ventures

We will cover your liability arising from a claim in connection with a joint venture in which you hold an interest of 50% or more, but only to the extent that the liability arises from your own acts, errors, or omissions, or your own proportionate share of the joint venture's liability under the arrangement governing it. We do not cover liability arising solely from the acts, errors, or omissions of another joint venture participant, except to the extent you are liable for them at law and that liability arises from your business activity.

Predecessor Practices

We will cover claims arising from the business activity of any predecessor practice, as if the predecessor practice's work had been performed by you.

Run-Off Cover

If you permanently cease to practise during the policy period (for any reason other than merger or acquisition), this policy automatically continues, without additional premium, to cover claims first made against you and notified to us before the end of the policy period. All terms, conditions, exclusions, and the limit of liability continue to apply. This automatic cover ends at the end of the policy period and is not extended by this extension beyond that date, except as set out below.

You may ask us for a quotation to extend cover under this policy for claims first made after the end of the policy period, for a period of up to 84 months after the end of the policy period (or such other maximum period as is shown in the schedule). We will respond to a request made under this paragraph within 30 days of receiving it. We are not obliged to offer terms for an extension, and may decline to offer an extension or may offer one on conditions we consider appropriate. If we agree to offer an extension, it is implemented under Endorsement E01, for the period, on payment of the premium, and subject to the terms shown in the schedule or endorsement schedule for that endorsement.

Nothing in this extension affects your rights, or our obligations, under the Notification of Circumstances clause or s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). A fact, matter, or circumstance you notify to us in accordance with the Notification of Circumstances clause before the end of the policy period remains covered as a claim first made during the policy period, whether or not you request, or we offer or agree to, an extension of run-off cover under this clause.

Subcontractors and Outsourcers

We will cover you for any claim arising from work performed on your behalf by a subcontractor or outsourcer, provided the work falls within your business activity.

Failure to Educate

We cover you for a claim by a student, or a person paying for a student, that you failed to provide the education, training or assessment that you promised or that the law required, including a claim arising from the cancellation of a qualification or statement of attainment that you issued.

Re-assessment and Re-issue Costs

If a regulator finds that your assessment of students did not meet the standards that apply, we will pay the reasonable costs, incurred with our consent, of re-assessing the affected students, providing additional training to them, and re-issuing their qualifications or statements of attainment.

The most we will pay under this extension in the policy period is the sub-limit shown in the schedule, which is part of the limit of liability.

Education Regulators

We also pay defence costs under the Inquiry Costs extension for an audit, investigation or inquiry into your conduct by an education regulator, including ASQA, TEQSA, the Victorian Registration and Qualifications Authority, the Training Accreditation Council of Western Australia, and a state or territory school registration authority.

This extension does not cover the cost of a routine registration or re-registration audit.

Teachers, Contractors and Volunteers

A teacher, trainer, assessor or tutor engaged by you as a contractor, and a volunteer acting for you, is an insured person for the professional services they provide on your behalf.

What Is Not Covered

We do not cover any claim, loss, or liability:

Bodily Injury and Property Damage

Arising from the death, bodily injury, illness, or disease of any person, or from physical loss of or damage to tangible property — unless the liability arises directly from a breach of your professional duty of care in performing your business activity.

Abuse

Arising from the sexual, physical or psychological abuse, molestation or harassment of any person, or from any failure to prevent, detect, report or respond to it, whether the claim is for negligence, breach of a non-delegable duty or any other cause of action.

This exposure is designed to be covered under an abuse liability policy.

Employer's Liability

Arising from your obligations or conduct as an employer, including wrongful dismissal, discrimination, harassment, or breach of employment law.

This exposure is designed to be covered under a management liability or employment practices liability policy.

Directors' and Officers' Liability

Arising from an insured person's personal liability as a director or officer of any entity other than the company, or from the management of the company (as distinct from the performance of professional services for clients).

This exposure is designed to be covered under a directors' and officers' liability policy.

Cyber Event

Arising from: (a) unauthorised access to or interference with your computer system or data; (b) ransomware, malware, or denial-of-service attacks; (c) failure of your computer system's security; or (d) loss, theft, or unauthorised disclosure of data held electronically — unless the liability arises directly from a breach of your professional duty of care performing business activity for a client.

This exposure is designed to be covered under a cyber insurance policy.

Financial Services

Arising from any activity regulated by ASIC, APRA, or any equivalent regulator, including investment advice, financial product advice, credit activities, funds management, or dealing in securities.

This exclusion does not apply to a service that is shown in the schedule as a professional service and that you are licensed or authorised to provide, including as an authorised representative or credit representative, under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) or the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (Cth).

Products

Arising from the design, manufacture, supply, distribution, sale, or recall of any tangible product.

This exposure is designed to be covered under a products liability policy.

Contractual Liability

For any liability you assume under a contract that is greater than the liability you would have at law without the contract.

Deliberate and Dishonest Acts

Arising from any act, error, or omission you deliberately, dishonestly, fraudulently, criminally, or maliciously commit, condone, or ignore — but only to the extent that a Final adjudication establishes that you personally committed, condoned, or participated in that conduct. Until Final adjudication, we will advance defence costs under the Defence Costs provision. If Final adjudication then establishes that this exclusion applies, you must repay the defence costs advanced in connection with that conduct, in accordance with the Defence Costs provision.

However: we will still cover any insured person who did not personally commit, condone, or participate in the conduct — this protection applies separately to each insured person, and a Final adjudication establishing the conduct against one insured person does not, of itself, establish that conduct against another insured person. This exclusion does not apply to the First-Party Dishonesty extension or to the cover for dishonesty of your employees under the Claims Against You clause.

Prior Known Circumstances

Arising from any fact, matter, or circumstance: (a) that you knew about, or ought reasonably to have known about, before the start of this policy period (or before we first agreed to insure you, if earlier); and (b) that you knew, or ought reasonably to have known, might lead to a claim against you.

War, Terrorism, and Sanctions

Arising from: (a) war (whether or not declared), invasion, hostilities, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, civil unrest, or the use or usurpation of military power, or any similar belligerent act; (b) terrorism as defined by relevant Australian or foreign law; or (c) trade or economic sanctions imposed by Australia, the UN, EU, UK, or USA. We will not make any payment that would expose us to enforcement action.

Nuclear

Arising from nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination, however caused.

Asbestos

Arising from or in connection with asbestos in any form, including its mining, manufacture, distribution, installation, presence, removal, or disposal.

Pollution

Arising from pollution — unless the pollution is sudden, accidental, unintended, and unexpected, and results directly from your business activity.

Trading Debts and Business Losses

For: (a) fees, charges, or debts owed to you; (b) your lost profit, mark-up, or overheads; (c) any trading loss or loss of any client, contract, or business opportunity; or (d) your liability for GST or any other tax.

Fee Refunds

For the refund or re-crediting of tuition fees, student loan amounts or levies, including amounts payable under a tuition protection scheme, except to the extent that a court or tribunal orders them as compensation for your negligence.

Research

Arising from research, clinical trials or product development, other than research carried out by students as part of their course.

Related Party Claims

For any claim brought by: (a) any entity that controls you, is controlled by you, or is under common control with you; (b) any person or entity holding a financial, executive, or managerial interest of 15% or more in you; or (c) any entity in which you hold such an interest. However: this exclusion does not apply to a claim based on your liability to an independent third party arising directly from the performance of your business activity for that third party.

Non-Compensatory Payments

For fines, penalties, punitive damages, exemplary damages, aggravated damages, or multiple damages — except where cover for fines or penalties is specifically provided under the Statutory Liability extension.

How Much We Will Pay

Limit of Liability

The most we will pay for all claims and defence costs combined during the policy period is the limit of liability shown in your schedule. This is an aggregate limit — it applies to all claims combined, regardless of how many are made or how many insured persons are involved.

Automatic Reinstatement

Despite the Limit of Liability provision, if payments under this policy reduce or exhaust the limit of liability, it is reinstated once during the policy period, in full and without additional premium.

The reinstated limit of liability:

(a) is available only for a claim that does not arise from the same, similar or related acts, errors or omissions as a claim already paid under this policy; and

(b) does not increase the most we will pay for any one claim, which remains the limit of liability.

The most we will pay for all claims during the policy period is twice the limit of liability.

Sub-Limits

Where a sub-limit is shown — in this wording or the schedule — it is the most we will pay for that type of cover. Sub-limits are included within — not in addition to — the limit of liability, unless the schedule states otherwise.

Defence Costs

Defence costs are included within the limit of liability and reduce the amount available to pay compensation, unless the schedule states that defence costs are payable in addition to the limit. We will advance defence costs as they are incurred, without waiting for the final outcome of a claim. If a claim is later found not to be covered, you must repay any defence costs advanced for the uncovered portion, subject to the Allocation provision where the claim was only partly covered.

Where a claim involves conduct excluded under the Deliberate and Dishonest Acts exclusion, repayment of defence costs advanced in connection with that conduct is required only after a Final adjudication establishes that the exclusion applies, consistent with the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition.

Excess

You must pay the excess shown in the schedule as the first part of each claim, applied to loss and defence costs combined unless the schedule states otherwise. The excess applies even where a claim involves only defence costs.

Other Insurance

If you have other insurance that covers the same loss, notify us promptly. Consistent with s.45 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), you may claim under this policy or your other policy. We may seek contribution from the other insurer in proportion to each policy's liability.

Paying Out the Limit

At any time during a claim, we may pay you the limit of liability (or whatever remains after earlier payments) and our obligation to defend or pay further amounts will end. We will give you reasonable notice and the opportunity to take over the defence at your own cost before doing so.

Allocation

Where a claim involves both: (a) amounts covered and amounts not covered under this policy; or (b) an insured and a person or entity who is not insured under this policy — we and you must use reasonable endeavours to agree a fair and reasonable allocation of loss and defence costs between the covered and uncovered amounts, parties, or causes of action, having regard to their relative legal and financial exposures. An allocation agreed or determined under this provision does not itself determine whether conduct is excluded under the Deliberate and Dishonest Acts exclusion; that question is determined only by Final adjudication, and advancement continues in the meantime on the terms of the Defence Costs provision.

If we and you cannot agree an allocation within a reasonable time, the dispute will be determined by Senior Counsel — mutually agreed between us and you or, failing agreement within 21 days, nominated by the President of the Bar Association or Bar Council of the State or Territory whose law governs this policy under the Governing Law condition. The Senior Counsel's determination of a fair and reasonable allocation is binding on us and you.

Pending an allocation being agreed or determined, we will advance defence costs on a fair and reasonable interim allocation, subject to the Defence Costs provision.

Your Obligations

These obligations apply subject to the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition and the Individual Treatment of Insured Persons obligation.

Tell Us About Claims

You must tell us in writing as soon as reasonably practicable after you become aware that a claim has been made against you. Tell us: (a) who is making the claim and what they allege; (b) when you first became aware; (c) details of the act, error, or omission involved; and (d) your estimate of the potential value if you can.

Tell Us About Circumstances

Tell us during the policy period about any fact, matter, or circumstance you first become aware of that might reasonably be expected to give rise to a claim. This protects you — see the Notification of Circumstances clause.

Examples: a client has expressed serious dissatisfaction and may seek compensation; you have discovered a significant error in advice you gave; a regulator has contacted you about a formal investigation; you have received a complaint through a professional body.

Do Not Admit Liability

Do not admit liability, make any offer or promise to pay, or agree to settle any claim without our written consent. We will not unreasonably withhold or delay consent.

Cooperate With Us

Give us all information, documents, and assistance we reasonably need to handle the claim. Attend any meetings, examinations, or proceedings we reasonably require.

Mitigate Your Loss

Take all reasonable steps to prevent or reduce any loss that might be covered by this policy.

Preserve Our Recovery Rights

Do not do anything after a claim is made that might prejudice our right to recover from any third party. If we pay a claim, we may take action in your name to recover what we have paid.

Tell Us About Other Insurance

Tell us about any other insurance that might cover the same loss.

Tell Us About Changes

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable about any material change to your business that might affect this policy — for example, new types of professional services, a merger, or a new subsidiary.

Under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), we cannot refuse your claim solely because you failed to tell us about a change unless the change caused or contributed to the loss or prejudiced our interests — see also the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition.

Individual Treatment of Insured Persons

Each insured person's cover is assessed individually. A failure by one insured person to comply with the Tell Us About Claims obligation, the Tell Us About Circumstances obligation, the Do Not Admit Liability obligation, the Cooperate With Us obligation, the Mitigate Your Loss obligation or the Preserve Our Recovery Rights obligation, or any knowledge, act, error, omission, or non-disclosure of one insured person, does not affect the cover of another insured person who did not know of, participate in, or contribute to that failure, knowledge, act, error, omission, or non-disclosure. This section does not affect the Deliberate and Dishonest Acts exclusion or its Final adjudication and non-imputation mechanics, which continue to apply according to their terms.

General Conditions

Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth)

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts, or modifies the operation of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) (the ICA). Where this policy is inconsistent with the ICA, the ICA prevails.

Without limiting the above, s.54 of the ICA applies to any act or omission by you connected with a claim after it arises, including under the Tell Us About Claims obligation, the Do Not Admit Liability obligation, the Cooperate With Us obligation, the Mitigate Your Loss obligation and the Preserve Our Recovery Rights obligation. We may not refuse to pay a claim, or reduce our liability under it, by reason only of such an act or omission, except to the extent (if any) that we are prejudiced by it, or as the ICA otherwise permits in the case of fraud.

Your Duty of Disclosure

Before you enter into, renew, extend, or vary this policy, you have a duty to tell us anything you know (or could reasonably be expected to know) that would be relevant to our decision to insure you and on what terms. This is a non-consumer contract; the duty described in this condition is owed to us by the company, as the party proposing this contract.

You do not need to tell us about matters: (a) that reduce the risk; (b) that are common knowledge; (c) that we know or should know as an insurer; or (d) that we have said we do not need to know.

If the company does not tell us something it should have, we may, to the extent permitted by the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), reduce our liability for a claim proportionately or, where the non-disclosure was fraudulent, avoid this policy. We will not avoid this policy, or reduce the cover of an insured person, because of a non-disclosure by another insured person, unless that insured person seeking cover personally knew of the relevant matter and knew it should have been disclosed. As between us and each insured person, this duty and its consequences are assessed individually — see also the Deliberate and Dishonest Acts exclusion and the Individual Treatment of Insured Persons obligation.

Licences, Registrations and Accreditations

Where a law, or an accreditation scheme under which you provide a service, requires the person who provides that service to hold a licence, registration, accreditation or endorsement, the service is a professional service under this policy only if it is provided by a person who holds it, or under the direct control and supervision of such a person to the extent that the law or scheme permits.

Compulsory Insurance Requirements

This condition applies where a law, or a requirement made under a law by a regulator or licensing body, requires you to hold professional indemnity insurance with minimum terms in order to provide your professional services. Each such requirement is a compulsory insurance requirement.

For a claim to which a compulsory insurance requirement applies, this policy is to be read as providing at least the cover, limit of liability, excess terms, reinstatement and run-off that the requirement prescribes. Where this policy would otherwise provide less, we will pay what the requirement obliges an insurer to pay.

If we pay an amount only because of this condition, and we would not otherwise have been liable to pay it under this policy, you must repay that amount to us, to the extent that the law and the requirement allow us to recover it.

Professional Standards Schemes

If you are a member of a scheme approved under professional standards legislation in a state or territory of Australia, and the scheme limits your occupational liability, this policy is intended to meet the insurance standard that the scheme sets for its members.

For a claim to which the scheme applies, if a term of this policy is inconsistent with that insurance standard, the standard applies to the extent of the inconsistency.

This condition does not increase the limit of liability. You are responsible for choosing a limit of liability that is at least the amount the scheme requires.

Cancellation

By you: Cancel at any time by telling us in writing. We will refund the premium for the unexpired policy period, less any amount paid or reserved for claims.

By us: We may only cancel this policy on grounds permitted by the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) s.60. Where we are entitled to cancel, we will give you written notice as required by s.59, and will refund the premium for the unexpired portion proportionally.

Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws of the state or territory of Australia in which the named insured has its principal place of business, as shown in the schedule.

Jurisdiction

You and we submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the State or Territory whose law governs this policy under the Governing Law condition, and the courts that hear appeals from those courts.

GST
