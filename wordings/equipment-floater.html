Equipment Floater Insurance

Portable tools and equipment used in a business, insured anywhere in Australia on site, in vehicles and in transit: specified items and a blanket for unspecified tools, with optional hired-in equipment, replacement hire costs and worldwide use.

About This Policy

This policy insures the portable tools and equipment you use in your business, wherever they are in Australia: at your premises, in your vehicle, on a site or in transit between them. It is for tools and equipment that are carried by hand or in a vehicle. It does not insure self-propelled or registrable plant, such as excavators, loaders, forklifts or trailers; a plant and equipment policy insures those.

The schedule shows which sections apply.

SectionWhat it coversBasis

Hired-In EquipmentPortable equipment you hire or borrow from othersAs above

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. The Order of Precedence condition says which part applies where they differ. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

Important Information

Who issues this policy. The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

Who this policy is for. This wording is designed for businesses, sole traders and professionals whose tools and equipment are used wholly or predominantly for business purposes, and for distribution to wholesale clients within the meaning of s.761G of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). Before the policy is issued, you state that the insured property is used wholly or predominantly for business purposes. It is not a Product Disclosure Statement, and no Target Market Determination has been prepared for it. An insurer that offers it to retail clients, including for property used wholly or predominantly for personal, domestic or household purposes, must first prepare the documents the law requires for retail distribution. [WP REVIEW: confirm that a business-use statement given before issue keeps a sole trader's tool cover outside the personal and domestic property category in reg 7.1.17 of the Corporations Regulations 2001 (Cth).]

Your duty of disclosure. Before you enter into, renew, extend, vary or reinstate this policy, s.21 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) requires you to tell us every matter you know, or that a reasonable person in your circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we waive. If you do not comply, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows. If the non-disclosure is fraudulent, we may avoid the policy from its beginning.

Financial Claims Scheme. If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.

Schedule

(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

  • Your name, ABN and address
  • The policy period, with its start and end dates
  • The sections that apply
  • Each specified item, with its description, serial number where it has one, and its sum insured
  • The blanket sum insured for unspecified tools, and the most we pay for any one unspecified item
  • The total sum insured
  • The sum insured for hired-in equipment and the replacement hire costs limit, if those sections apply
  • The excess, and any special excess shown for an endorsement
  • The premium, including any stamp duty and GST
  • Any commission or fee paid to your broker
  • The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see the Governing Law condition)
  • The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables

Definitions

These words have the meanings below wherever they appear in this policy, in the singular or the plural.

Accidental Loss or Damage

Sudden and unforeseen physical loss or damage, including theft, that you did not intend or expect.

Business Use

Use in carrying on your business, trade or profession, including travel to and from places of work and storage when not in use.

Endorsement

A clause from the endorsement library that applies to this policy because its code is shown in the schedule.

Event

One occurrence, or a series of occurrences from one originating cause.

Excess

The amount shown in the schedule that you bear for each claim, as the Excess provision sets out.

Hired-In Equipment

Portable equipment that you hire, lease or borrow from another person under a written or oral agreement, for which you are responsible, and that would be insured property if you owned it.

Insured Property

Specified items and unspecified tools that you own, or for which you are legally responsible, used wholly or predominantly for business use. Insured property does not include excluded property.

Excluded Property

The property listed in the Excluded Property exclusion.

Policy Period

The period of insurance shown in the schedule.

Portable Equipment

Tools, instruments and equipment designed to be carried by hand or in a vehicle, that are not self-propelled and are not registrable for use on a public road.

Specified Item

An item of portable equipment described in the schedule with its own sum insured.

Unspecified Tools

Portable equipment that is not a specified item, insured on the blanket sum insured shown in the schedule.

Unattended

Left where neither you, your employee nor a person you have authorised can see and is close enough to prevent its loss or damage.

We, Us and Our

The insurer named in the schedule.

You and Your

The named insured shown in the schedule.

Portable Equipment

This section always applies.

Portable Equipment Cover

We pay for accidental loss of or damage to insured property that happens during the policy period anywhere in Australia, including while it is in a vehicle, on a site, at your premises or at your home, or in transit between them.

We pay for a specified item up to its sum insured, and for unspecified tools up to the blanket sum insured, but not more than the amount shown in the schedule for any one unspecified item.

Newly Acquired Equipment

Portable equipment you acquire during the policy period is insured property for 30 days after you acquire it, up to the amount shown in the schedule, or if none is shown, 20% of the total sum insured or $10,000, whichever is less. You must tell us about it within those 30 days for it to remain insured after them.

Removal of Debris

We pay the reasonable cost of removing the debris of insured property after loss or damage this section covers, up to 10% of the amount we pay for that loss or damage, in addition to the sum insured.

Locks and Keys

If keys or access cards to a vehicle or a storage container holding insured property are stolen, we pay the reasonable cost of replacing the locks and keys, up to $1,000 for any one event, in addition to the sum insured.

Theft From an Unattended Vehicle

For theft of insured property from an unattended vehicle, we pay only if the vehicle was locked and its windows closed, and entry was gained by force or violence, or the property was in a locked tool box or rack fixed to the vehicle and was removed by force or violence. The special excess in the Excess provision applies to each such claim.

The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies to this condition: if the security was not in place but did not cause or contribute to the theft, we pay the claim.

Portable Equipment Exclusions

Under Portable Equipment we do not pay for:

  • mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdown or failure, unless it results from an external accidental event;
  • wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion, or damage from cleaning, repairing or adjusting;
  • scratching, chipping or denting that does not stop the item working;
  • loss of or damage to consumable parts, bits, blades and batteries, unless the item they are part of is lost or damaged at the same time;
  • loss of data or software, or the cost of recreating them;
  • unexplained disappearance, or a shortage found only on taking stock; or
  • theft of insured property left unattended in the open overnight, unless endorsement E01 applies.

Hired-In Equipment

This section applies if the schedule shows it.

Hired-In Equipment Cover

We pay for accidental loss of or damage to hired-in equipment that happens during the policy period anywhere in Australia, up to the sum insured for this section shown in the schedule for any one item and in total for any one event. We pay the lesser of the cost of repair, the cost of replacement and the amount the hire agreement makes you liable for, together with the hire charges you are obliged to keep paying while the item cannot be used, up to 4 weeks.

The exclusions in the Portable Equipment Exclusions exclusion apply to this section as if hired-in equipment were insured property.

Replacement Hire Costs

This section applies if the schedule shows it.

Replacement Hire Costs Cover

After accidental loss of or damage to insured property that Portable Equipment Cover covers, we pay the reasonable cost of hiring a similar item while the lost or damaged item is repaired or replaced, from the time of the loss or damage until the repaired or replacement item is available, up to the limit for this section shown in the schedule for any one event, or if none is shown, $2,500, and for no more than 4 weeks.

Worldwide Use

This section applies if the schedule shows it.

Worldwide Use Cover

Portable Equipment Cover extends to insured property that you take outside Australia for business use for no more than 90 consecutive days on any one trip, including while in transit to and from Australia as accompanied baggage or by courier. We do not pay for loss or damage in a country to which the Australian Government's Smartraveller service advised against all travel at the time.

How We Pay

This part says how we value a loss and what we pay.

Basis of Settlement

We may repair the item, replace it with a new item of the same kind and capacity, or pay you the cost of doing so. Where the item was less than 5 years old at the time of the loss, we pay on that basis without deduction for age. Where it was 5 years old or more, we pay its market value at the time of the loss, unless the schedule shows that the item is insured for its agreed value.

We pay no more than the sum insured for a specified item, or the amount shown in the schedule for any one unspecified item. We do not apply average or reduce a payment because the sum insured is less than the value of the property.

Excess

For each claim you bear the excess shown in the schedule, for each event. For theft from an unattended vehicle, the special excess shown in the schedule applies in addition, or if none is shown, $500. Where an endorsement shows a special excess, it applies instead of the excess for the kind of loss it names. Where more than one excess applies to the same event, only the highest is deducted, except the theft from vehicles special excess, which applies in addition.

Who We Pay

We pay you, or with your agreement the repairer, supplier or owner of hired-in equipment directly.

Salvage and Recoveries

When we replace an item or pay its full value, the damaged or recovered item becomes ours. If stolen property is recovered after we have paid, you may buy it back from us for the amount we paid, less any cost of repairing damage it suffered.

GST

Where you are entitled to an input tax credit for the GST on a repair, replacement or other payment, we reduce our payment by the amount of that credit, as A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth) allows. You must tell us your entitlement to input tax credits when you make a claim.

Other Insurance

If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by another policy, including the general property section of a business package policy, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows. Tell us about any other insurance when you claim.

What We Do Not Cover

These exclusions apply to every section unless a section or an endorsement says otherwise.

Excluded Property

We do not pay for loss of or damage to:

  • motor vehicles, trailers, caravans, watercraft and aircraft, including drones and unmanned aircraft, and their parts and accessories while fitted;
  • self-propelled plant, or plant registrable for use on a public road;
  • stock in trade, materials and goods held for sale or for installation in a customer's property;
  • money, cards, jewellery and watches;
  • mobile phones, unless the schedule shows them as specified items; and
  • property used wholly or predominantly for personal, domestic or household purposes.

Dishonesty

We do not pay for loss or damage caused by a dishonest or fraudulent act of you or your employee, acting alone or with others, or theft by a person you have lent or hired the property to.

Confiscation

We do not pay for loss or damage caused by confiscation, seizure or detention of insured property by a government or public authority.

Consequential Loss

We do not pay for loss of income, loss of use, delay, penalties or any other consequential loss, except under Replacement Hire Costs and the hire charges the Hired-In Equipment Cover clause pays.

War

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities or warlike operations (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, or military or usurped power.

Nuclear

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from ionising radiation, or contamination by radioactivity, from any nuclear fuel or nuclear waste, or from the radioactive, toxic, explosive or other hazardous properties of any nuclear assembly or component.

Terrorism

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from an act of terrorism, meaning an act, including the use of force or violence or the threat of it, by any person or group, committed for political, religious, ideological or similar purposes, including the intention to influence a government or to put the public in fear. This exclusion has no effect to the extent the Terrorism Insurance Act 2003 (Cth) requires cover to be provided.

Cyber

We do not pay for any loss, damage or cost arising from any access to or use of a computer system, software, network or electronic data without the authority of its owner, malicious or harmful code, or a denial of service attack. This exclusion does not apply to theft of, or physical damage to, the device itself.

Your Obligations

These obligations apply to every section. The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies to each of them.

Tell Us About Loss

You must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable about any loss or damage that may give rise to a claim. Theft and malicious damage must be reported to the police within 48 hours of your discovering it. You must give us the information and help we reasonably ask for, including proof of ownership and value, and keep damaged items available for inspection until we have had a reasonable chance to inspect them.

Reasonable Precautions

You must take reasonable precautions to prevent loss of or damage to insured property, including keeping it secure when it is not in use, maintaining it as its manufacturer recommends, and not using it for a purpose for which it was not designed.

Records

You must keep a record of your specified items, with serial numbers where they have them, and proof of purchase or valuation where you have it.

Tell Us About Changes

You must tell us within 30 days if, during the policy period, the property starts to be used wholly or predominantly for personal, domestic or household purposes, or you start to leave insured property in the open overnight. We may change the premium or terms for the rest of the policy period, as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

General Conditions

These conditions apply to every section.

Insurance Contracts Act

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies the operation of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Where this policy is inconsistent with the Act, the Act prevails.

If this policy would otherwise let us refuse to pay a claim, in whole or in part, because of something you did or did not do after this policy was entered into, section 54 of the Act applies. We may not refuse to pay the claim only because of that act or omission. Our liability is reduced by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced. Where the act or omission could reasonably be regarded as capable of causing or contributing to the loss, we may refuse to pay only the part of the claim that the act or omission caused or contributed to. We may not refuse any part that you prove the act or omission did not cause. This applies to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation in this policy, not only to the claims conditions.

Order of Precedence

Where parts of your contract of insurance differ, they apply in this order: an endorsement shown in the schedule, then the schedule, then this wording.

Our Rights of Recovery

After we pay a claim, we may take over your rights to recover from anyone else, in your name, and you must cooperate with us. We will not seek recovery from your employee, unless the loss arose from the employee's serious or wilful misconduct, or from a principal contractor or hirer you were required, by a written contract made before the loss, to release.

Cancellation

You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. No refund applies to the premium for an item we have paid as a total loss.

We may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you written notice as that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis.

Cancellation does not affect a claim for loss or damage that happened before it took effect.

Fraud

If a claim is made fraudulently, we may refuse to pay it, as s.56 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

Sanctions

We do not provide cover, pay any claim or provide any benefit under this policy to the extent that doing so would expose us to any sanction, prohibition or restriction under:

  • the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011 (Cth), the Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 (Cth), or regulations made under either Act; or
  • the trade or economic sanctions laws of the United Kingdom, the United States of America or the European Union, to the extent they apply to us.

[WP REVIEW: confirm that naming the UK, US and EU regimes is appropriate for Australian capacity; this is a deliberate library-wide scope choice.]

Privacy

We collect, use and disclose personal information as the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) allows, and as our privacy policy describes. Our privacy policy is available from us on request.

Disputes and Complaints

If you have a complaint, contact us first. We will acknowledge it within one business day and give you our response within 30 calendar days. If you are not satisfied with our response, or we do not respond within that time, you may refer the complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), if it falls within AFCA's rules: www.afca.org.au, 1800 931 678, info@afca.org.au.

Governing Law

This policy is governed by the law of the Australian state or territory shown in the schedule. If none is shown, it is governed by the law of the state or territory of your address shown in the schedule at the start of the policy period.

Jurisdiction

You and we submit to the jurisdiction of the courts of the state or territory whose law governs this policy, and of any court that may hear appeals from them.

Headings and References

Headings are for convenience only. A reference to legislation includes any legislation that amends or replaces it. A reference to a clause by its heading is a reference to that clause as it appears in this wording.

How to Make a Claim

  1. Take reasonable steps to prevent further loss or damage.
  2. Report any theft or malicious damage to the police within 48 hours, and keep the report number.
  3. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
  4. Tell us what happened and when, and give us proof of ownership and value, serial numbers, photographs and repair quotes.
  5. Keep damaged items until we have had a reasonable chance to inspect them.
  6. We will acknowledge the claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision as the General Insurance Code of Practice requires where the insurer subscribes to it.

Endorsement Library

An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables shown for it in the schedule. Where an endorsement and the rest of this policy conflict, the endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. If a variable an endorsement needs is not shown in the schedule, the default stated in the endorsement applies; if it has no default, the endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the schedule.

E01 Open Site Storage

The Portable Equipment Exclusions exclusion does not exclude theft of insured property left in the open overnight on a construction or work site, where the site is fenced and locked, or the property is chained or locked to a fixed structure. The special excess shown in the schedule applies to each such claim.

Schedule variables: special excess (default $1,000).

E02 Employees' Tools

Tools owned by your employees and used in your business are insured property while they are at your premises, on a site or in your vehicle, up to the amount shown in the schedule for each employee, and within the blanket sum insured for unspecified tools.

Schedule variables: amount for each employee (default $2,000).

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because you want your employees' tools insured.

E03 Overnight Vehicle Storage

Insured property kept overnight in a vehicle is covered for theft only where the vehicle is locked, has an engaged alarm or immobiliser, and is parked at your premises, at your home or in a locked garage or compound, or the property is in a locked tool box or rack fixed to the vehicle. The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies to these conditions.

Schedule variables: none.
