Aircrew Loss of Licence Insurance

Annual, reviewable loss of licence cover for commercial pilots: a lump sum when CASA permanently cancels or refuses a Class 1 medical certificate, and a monthly benefit while it is suspended, built on the Meridian Personal Accident and Sickness base wording.

About This Policy

This policy pays benefits to a pilot whose Class 1 medical certificate is cancelled, suspended or not renewed because of an injury or sickness, so that they cannot exercise the privileges of their licence. It pays a lump sum for permanent loss of licence, and, if the schedule shows it, a monthly benefit while a temporary loss of licence lasts.

It is a benefit policy, not an indemnity policy. The lump sum and the monthly benefit are fixed amounts shown in the schedule. They do not depend on the income you lose.

It is an annual policy. Each renewal is a new contract, and we may review the premium, the benefits and the terms at each renewal (see the Renewal condition). It is not life insurance, workers compensation or private health insurance, and it does not replace any of them. It does not pay medical expenses.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

This wording is the Meridian Personal Accident and Sickness base wording with the changes needed for loss of licence cover. It is a complete wording in its own right.

Important Information

Who issues this policy. The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

Who this policy is for. This wording is written for commercial pilots, and for cabin crew if Endorsement E17 applies. A pilot who buys it for themselves is a retail client under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). The issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement. Where an airline or a pilots' association buys cover for its members, the issuer must decide whether each member is a retail client.

Annual and reviewable. This policy is for 12 months. It is general insurance, and not life insurance, because it is annual and we may review the premium, the benefits and the terms at each renewal. We are not obliged to renew it.

Terms that differ from standard cover. The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) sets standard cover for sickness and accident contracts. This policy differs from standard cover. In particular: - it pays only when CASA acts on your Class 1 medical certificate because of an injury or sickness, not for loss of licence for any other reason (see the Loss of Licence for Other Reasons exclusion); - it does not cover a pre-existing condition in the first 24 months (see the Pre-existing Conditions exclusion); - it does not cover a condition that first shows itself in the first 90 days (see the Initial Period exclusion); - it does not cover loss of licence caused by alcohol or drug misuse, or a failed CASA drug or alcohol test (see the Alcohol and Drugs exclusion); - it does not cover normal pregnancy (see the Pregnancy exclusion), intentional self-injury, war or nuclear risks; - the monthly benefit starts only after the waiting period and stops at the end of the benefit period; - no benefit is payable for a loss of licence that starts after your 65th birthday (see the Age Limits provision).

Definitions

In this policy, the words below have these meanings wherever they appear, whatever their capitalisation. The singular includes the plural.

Accident

A sudden, unexpected and identifiable event that happens to you during the policy period and within the scope of cover.

Benefit Period

The longest period for which we pay the monthly benefit for any one temporary loss of licence, shown in the schedule as 12 or 24 months. The benefit period starts on the first day after the waiting period ends.

Capital Sum

The lump sum shown in the schedule for permanent loss of licence.

CASA

The Civil Aviation Safety Authority, or a designated aviation medical examiner or other person acting under a delegation from it.

Class 1 Medical Certificate

A class 1 medical certificate issued under Part 67 of the Civil Aviation Safety Regulations 1998 (Cth), as in force when CASA makes its decision.

Endorsement

A written change to this policy that we issue and that is shown in the schedule. The available endorsements are listed in the Endorsement Library.

Hazardous Pursuit

Any activity listed in the Hazardous Pursuits exclusion.

Injury

A bodily injury caused solely and directly by an accident, and not by a sickness or a gradual process. Injury includes a resulting medical condition that first shows itself within 12 months of the accident.

Insured Person

The person named in the schedule as the insured person, who holds a licence and a current Class 1 medical certificate when the policy starts.

Licence

The air transport pilot licence or commercial pilot licence issued to the insured person under Part 61 of the Civil Aviation Safety Regulations 1998 (Cth) and shown in the schedule, with the ratings they hold.

Medical Practitioner

A person registered to practise medicine in Australia, or a person with an equivalent qualification and registration in the country where you are treated, who is not you, a member of your family, your business partner or your employee.

Monthly Benefit

The amount shown in the schedule as the monthly benefit, paid for each month of temporary loss of licence after the waiting period, and pro rata for part of a month.

Permanent Loss of Licence

A decision by CASA, because of an injury or sickness, to cancel your Class 1 medical certificate or to refuse to issue or renew it, that you cannot reverse by any review or appeal available to you, where a medical practitioner we accept certifies that you will not meet the medical standard for a Class 1 medical certificate again. A decision to impose a condition on your certificate that stops you exercising the privileges of your licence in commercial operations is treated as a cancellation.

Policy Period

The period shown in the schedule.

Pre-existing Condition

A condition, illness or injury:

  • for which you received treatment, medication or advice from a medical practitioner in the 12 months before the date you were first insured under this policy; or
  • whose symptoms you were aware of, or a reasonable person in your circumstances would have been aware of, in the 3 months before that date.

A condition that you told us about before we insured you, and that we accepted in writing and show in the schedule, is not a pre-existing condition.

Sickness

An illness or disease that first shows itself during the policy period. Sickness does not include an injury.

Temporary Loss of Licence

A period during which, because of an injury or sickness, CASA has suspended your Class 1 medical certificate, or you do not meet the medical standard for it and a designated aviation medical examiner has told you not to exercise the privileges of your licence, so that you cannot fly in commercial operations.

Waiting Period

The number of days, shown in the schedule (default 30 days), at the start of a temporary loss of licence for which we do not pay the monthly benefit.

We, Us, Our

The insurer named in the schedule.

You, Your

The insured person. The insured person is also the policyholder.

What Is Covered

Permanent Loss of Licence

If you suffer an injury, or a sickness first shows itself, during the policy period, and it results in permanent loss of licence within 24 months, we will pay you the capital sum. We pay it once. When we have paid it, this policy ends, and we refund the premium for the monthly benefit for the unexpired policy period.

We may ask for the CASA decision, your medical certificate history and the reports of the medical practitioners who examined you, and may ask you to see a medical practitioner we choose, at our cost.

Temporary Loss of Licence

If the schedule shows the monthly benefit, and you suffer an injury, or a sickness first shows itself, during the policy period that results in temporary loss of licence, we will pay you the monthly benefit after the waiting period, for as long as the temporary loss of licence continues, up to the benefit period.

The monthly benefit stops on the earliest of: the day CASA reinstates your Class 1 medical certificate or you again meet the medical standard; the end of the benefit period; and the day we pay the capital sum for permanent loss of licence.

How We Pay Benefits

Recurrence

If you return to flying and, within 6 months, you have a further temporary loss of licence from the same or a related injury or sickness, we treat it as a continuation of the earlier one. No new waiting period applies, and the benefit period already used counts towards the benefit period.

Monthly Benefit and Capital Sum

If a temporary loss of licence becomes a permanent loss of licence, we pay the capital sum in full, and we do not deduct the monthly benefits already paid for the same injury or sickness.

Other Insurance

If a loss of licence covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. The benefits of this policy are fixed amounts and are paid in full.

Age Limits

We do not pay any benefit for a loss of licence that starts after your 65th birthday. We do not pay the monthly benefit for any period after your 65th birthday. We will not renew this policy after your 64th birthday.

Where You Are Covered

You are covered for an injury or sickness anywhere in the world. A loss of licence is covered only if it is a decision about your Class 1 medical certificate by CASA, unless Endorsement E16 applies.

Who We Pay

We pay every benefit to you, except that the Event 1 benefit and the benefits that follow it are paid to your legal personal representative. If you are unable to manage your affairs, we may pay a person legally entitled to act for you. Payment to that person discharges our liability for the amount paid.

What Is Not Covered

The exclusions below apply to every benefit under this policy, unless an exclusion says otherwise.

Intentional Self-Injury

We do not pay any benefit for your intentional self-injury, suicide or attempted suicide.

War

We do not pay any benefit for an injury or sickness caused by war, invasion, act of a foreign enemy, hostilities (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution or insurrection.

This exclusion does not apply to an act of terrorism, unless you took part in it.

Nuclear

We do not pay any benefit for an injury or sickness caused by ionising radiation, or contamination by radioactivity, from any nuclear fuel, nuclear waste or nuclear weapon.

Alcohol and Drugs

We do not pay any benefit for a loss of licence caused by:

  • the misuse of alcohol or a drug, or your dependence on alcohol or a drug; or
  • a positive result in a CASA drug and alcohol test, or your refusal to take one.

We do pay for a loss of licence caused by a drug a medical practitioner prescribed for you and that you took as prescribed. It is for us to show that the exclusion applies.

Criminal and Malicious Acts

We do not pay any benefit for an injury to the extent that it was caused or contributed to by you committing, or attempting to commit, a criminal offence punishable by imprisonment, or by you acting maliciously.

Acting maliciously means acting with the intention of harming another person or damaging another person's property.

Professional Sport

We do not pay any benefit for an injury that happens while you are taking part in, training for, coaching or officiating in any sport for which you receive, or are entitled to receive, a fee, sponsorship or prize money that is more than your reasonable expenses.

Hazardous Pursuits

We do not pay any benefit for an injury that happens while you are taking part in:

  • parachuting, skydiving, hang-gliding, paragliding, BASE jumping or ballooning;
  • a race, rally or speed trial in or on any motor vehicle or motorcycle, other than a road vehicle event that tests regularity and is not timed for speed;
  • scuba diving below 30 metres, or scuba diving without a qualification from a recognised training body unless under the supervision of a qualified instructor;
  • mountaineering above 4,000 metres; or
  • rodeo, bull riding, or horse racing (including harness racing and steeplechase).

This exclusion does not apply to a pursuit shown in the schedule under Endorsement E05.

Military Service

We do not pay any benefit for an injury that happens while you are serving full time, or on operational deployment as a reservist, in the armed forces of any country or of an international organisation.

Pre-existing Conditions

We do not pay any benefit for a loss of licence caused by a pre-existing condition, unless we accepted it in writing or Endorsement E08 applies to it.

This exclusion stops applying to a pre-existing condition once you have been insured with us continuously under this policy, and the policies it renews, for 24 months, unless Endorsement E07 excludes the condition.

This exclusion is subject to s.47 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): we will not rely on it for a condition that you were not aware of, and that a reasonable person in your circumstances could not have been expected to be aware of, when you were first insured.

Loss of Licence for Other Reasons

We do not pay any benefit for a loss of licence that is not caused by an injury or sickness, including a licence or certificate cancelled, suspended or not renewed because of a breach of aviation law, a proficiency check or flight review you did not pass, an administrative or disciplinary decision, a failure to apply for renewal in time, the loss of a type rating, or your employer's decision to stop you flying.

Initial Period

We do not pay any benefit for a sickness that first shows itself in the first 90 days of this policy. This exclusion does not apply on renewal, or to a sickness that results from an injury.

Pregnancy

We do not pay the monthly benefit for a temporary loss of licence caused by a normal pregnancy or childbirth. We pay for a temporary loss of licence caused by a complication that a medical practitioner certifies is not a normal part of pregnancy or childbirth, as a sickness.

[WP REVIEW: confirm this exclusion is supportable under the Sex Discrimination Act 1984 (Cth) s.41, given CASA's approach to pregnancy and class 1 medical certification.]

Elective Treatment

We do not pay any benefit for disablement caused by cosmetic or elective surgery or treatment, unless it is needed because of an injury or sickness covered by this policy.

Your Obligations

Each obligation is subject to the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition.

Telling Us About a Claim

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after CASA, or a designated aviation medical examiner, tells you that your Class 1 medical certificate may be suspended, cancelled or not renewed, or that you should not fly.

Give us the information and documents we reasonably need to assess the claim, including the CASA decision and correspondence, your medical certificate history and your medical reports, at your own cost. For the monthly benefit, give us evidence each month that the temporary loss of licence is continuing when we reasonably ask for it.

Medical Care and Examination

Get treatment from a medical practitioner as soon as reasonably practicable after an injury or the start of a sickness, and follow their reasonable advice.

You must attend an examination by a medical practitioner or other health professional we choose, as often as we reasonably require while a claim is open. We pay for it. If you die, we may ask for a post-mortem examination at our expense, where the law allows it.

Tell Us About Changes

Tell us during the policy period, as soon as reasonably practicable, if:

  • you change your occupation, or your duties change so that a higher occupation class would apply; or
  • you start taking part regularly in a hazardous pursuit, or in any activity listed in the proposal that you did not tell us about.

We will tell you whether the change affects your cover or premium.

General Conditions

Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth)

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies the operation of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Where this policy is inconsistent with the Act, the Act prevails.

If this policy would otherwise let us refuse to pay a claim, in whole or in part, because of something you did or did not do after this policy was entered into, section 54 of the Act applies. We may not refuse to pay the claim only because of that act or omission. Our liability is reduced by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced. Where the act or omission could reasonably be regarded as capable of causing or contributing to the loss, we may refuse to pay only the part of the claim that the act or omission caused or contributed to. We may not refuse any part that you prove the act or omission did not cause. This applies to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation in this policy, not only to the claims conditions.

Cancellation

You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired period of insurance on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. If you cancel within the cooling-off period, the refund is as the Important Information section states.

Cancellation does not affect a claim for an injury that happened, or a sickness that first showed itself, before cancellation.

E17 Cabin Crew Fitness to Fly

This policy applies to the cabin crew member named in the schedule as if they held a licence. Permanent loss of licence and temporary loss of licence mean a decision by their employer's aviation medical adviser, because of an injury or sickness, that they are permanently, or for the time being, not fit to work as cabin crew under the employer's fitness to fly standard shown in the schedule.

Schedule variables: employer; fitness to fly standard; capital sum and monthly benefit.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Which licence do you hold? qs.pa.lol.licence_type

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pa.lol.licence_type == cabin_crew: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Underwriter responses (on a referral or suggestion): Attach; Attach with premium loading; Decline risk

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Referred because cabin crew fitness to fly cover needs an underwriter's terms.
