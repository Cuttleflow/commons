Risk, Safety and Claims Consultants Professional Indemnity Insurance — Endorsement

The Risk, Safety and Claims Consultants Professional Indemnity Insurance terms, in the form of an endorsement to the base Professional Indemnity Insurance wording.

Application

This endorsement attaches to and forms part of the Meridian Professional Indemnity Insurance wording, version 1.4.0-DRAFT (document reference 2026/PI/0001), referred to here as the base wording. It changes the base wording only as set out below. All other terms, conditions, exclusions and limits of the base wording continue to apply.

Words defined in the base wording have the same meaning in this endorsement. Where this endorsement and the base wording conflict, this endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict.

The same terms are published as a single integrated wording, Risk, Safety and Claims Consultants Professional Indemnity Insurance, version 1.0.0-DRAFT. Both documents are generated from the same source and have the same effect.

Replaced: Professional services

The definition of Professional services in the base wording is deleted and replaced with the following.

Professional services

The following services provided to a third party for a fee:

(a) work health and safety consulting, including psychosocial hazard assessment;

(b) risk management consulting;

(c) fire safety consulting, other than fire safety engineering and the design or certification of fire safety systems;

(d) loss assessing and loss adjusting;

(e) claims management services, other than deciding or settling insurance claims, unless that service is shown in the schedule;

(f) workplace investigations;

(g) investigation and surveillance services, provided by a person who holds the licence the law requires;

(h) non-clinical injury management and return-to-work coordination;

(i) training in any of these services; and

(j) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

Added: Workplace Investigation Reports

The following extension is added to the base wording, after the Subcontractors and Outsourcers extension.

Workplace Investigation Reports

The cover for defamation under the Claims Against You clause includes a claim by a person who was the subject of, or a witness in, a workplace investigation you conducted, arising from statements in a report you gave to your client, unless a Final adjudication establishes that you published the statements with malice.

Added: Clinical Services

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Bodily Injury and Property Damage exclusion.

Clinical Services

Arising from the diagnosis, treatment or clinical assessment of any person by a medical practitioner, nurse, psychologist or other registered health practitioner, including fitness-for-work medical assessments.

This exposure is designed to be covered under an allied health or medical indemnity policy.

Added: Fire Safety System Design and Certification

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Products exclusion.

Fire Safety System Design and Certification

Arising from the design, specification, installation, commissioning or certification of a fire safety system, or the preparation of a fire safety performance solution under the National Construction Code.

Risk, Safety and Claims Endorsements

The following endorsements apply to this policy only when the schedule shows that they apply.

An endorsement does not apply to the extent that a law requiring you to hold this insurance does not permit the cover it removes to be excluded or limited.

Claims Handling Authorisation

Deciding and settling insurance claims on behalf of an insurer is a professional service under this policy while you are authorised to provide it under the Australian financial services licence, or as an authorised representative of the licensee, shown in the schedule.

For a claim arising from that service, the Financial Services exclusion does not apply.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Do you decide or settle insurance claims on behalf of insurers? qs.pi.rs.claims_decisions
  • If so, do you hold an AFS licence authorising claims handling and settling, or act as an authorised representative of a licensee? Give the number. qs.pi.rs.afsl_or_ar

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.rs.claims_decisions = yes and qs.pi.rs.afsl_or_ar = yes: Apply automatically
  • When qs.pi.rs.claims_decisions = yes and qs.pi.rs.afsl_or_ar = no: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because you decide insurance claims on behalf of insurers.

Surveillance Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from covert surveillance of a person, including any claim for trespass, invasion of privacy or breach of a surveillance devices law.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • What percentage of your fees in the last 12 months came from covert surveillance? qs.pi.rs.surveillance_pct

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.rs.surveillance_pct > 25: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected
  • When qs.pi.rs.surveillance_pct > 0: Suggest to the underwriter (quote not held)

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because {qs.pi.rs.surveillance_pct}% of your fees come from surveillance.

Psychosocial Hazards Sub-limit

For all claims arising from the assessment of, or advice on, psychosocial hazards, the most we will pay in total, including defence costs, is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement. The sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • What percentage of your fees in the last 12 months came from assessing or advising on psychosocial hazards? qs.pi.rs.psychosocial_pct

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.rs.psychosocial_pct > 20: Suggest to the underwriter (quote not held)

Underwriter responses (on a referral or suggestion): Attach; Attach with amended sub-limit; Do not attach

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because {qs.pi.rs.psychosocial_pct}% of your fees come from psychosocial hazard work.

Fire Safety Engineering Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from fire safety engineering advice, including fire modelling, egress analysis and the assessment of fire safety performance.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Do you provide fire safety engineering advice, including fire modelling or egress analysis? qs.pi.rs.fire_engineering

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.rs.fire_engineering = yes: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because you provide fire safety engineering advice.

Expert Witness

Preparing expert reports for legal proceedings and giving expert evidence are professional services under this policy.

We also pay, under the Inquiry Costs extension, your defence costs in responding to a complaint to a professional body arising from your expert evidence.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Do you prepare expert reports for legal proceedings or give expert evidence? qs.pi.rs.expert_witness

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.rs.expert_witness = yes: Offer as a priced option

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Offered because you give expert evidence.
