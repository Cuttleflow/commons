About This Template

This is a template for Part A of a combined Product Disclosure Statement and policy wording. It becomes a Product Disclosure Statement only when an issuer completes the bracketed items, adapts it to its own product and distribution, has it approved by its compliance function and its lawyers, and prepares and gives it under Part 7.9 of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). Cuttleflow is not an issuer of insurance and does not issue this product.

The issuer must also make its own target market determination for the product under Part 7.8A of the Corporations Act. A target market determination depends on the issuer's eligibility rules, channels and data, so it is not part of this template.

Everything below this section, to the end of the notes, is the template text.

2. Is This Product Right for You?

Private Motor Insurance is for individuals who insure a car used mainly for private purposes, including travel to and from work. We have made a Target Market Determination (TMD) for this product, which describes the consumers it is designed for. It is available at [URL] or by calling us.

This product is not suitable if your vehicle is a motorcycle, a light commercial vehicle or a heavy vehicle, if it is used for hire, fare or reward (including rideshare or platform delivery) or mainly for business, or if you need cover outside Australia.

The information in this PDS is general only. It does not take into account your objectives, financial situation or needs. Consider whether the product is right for you before you buy it.

Options you can add (an extra premium may apply):

4. Significant Benefits

  • Settlement: if your vehicle is written off, we pay its market value immediately before the loss, up to the sum insured, or the agreed value if you chose that option, less the excess and any unpaid premium for that vehicle.
  • Repairs: you may choose your own repairer. If you use a repairer we recommend, we guarantee the repairs for as long as you own the vehicle. We do not use second-hand or non-genuine parts without your consent, unless your vehicle is more than five years old and genuine parts are not reasonably available.
  • Replacement vehicle: a car you buy during the period of insurance to replace your insured car is covered on the same terms for the first 14 days after you take possession of it, if you tell us about it within those 14 days.
  • Other drivers: anyone driving your vehicle with your permission is covered, subject to the exclusions and excesses.
  • No claim bonus: your rating improves each year you do not make an at-fault claim. It affects your premium only, not your cover.

5. Significant Risks

The cover may not be enough. If the sum insured or agreed value is less than the cost of replacing your vehicle, you bear the difference. Check the sum insured each year.

You pay an excess on most claims (section 8).

Exclusions apply. We do not pay for loss, damage or liability:

  • while the vehicle is driven by someone without a licence appropriate for it, or whose licence is suspended or cancelled, unless the person claiming cover did not know and could not reasonably have known, or the vehicle was driven without your consent;
  • while the driver's ability is impaired by alcohol or a drug, the driver is over the legal alcohol limit, or the driver refuses a lawful test, unless the person claiming cover did not know and could not reasonably have known of the driver's state, or the vehicle was driven without your consent;
  • caused by an unsafe or unroadworthy condition you knew about or ought reasonably to have known about;
  • while the vehicle is used for racing, pace-making, speed or reliability testing, rallying or any competition, or on a racetrack;
  • caused by a load or towed weight above the vehicle's rated or registered capacity;
  • while the vehicle is used for hire, fare or reward, including rideshare and platform delivery, or for a use other than its permitted use that you knew of or consented to;
  • that you, or someone with your consent, caused deliberately;
  • outside Australia; or
  • from war, nuclear risks or terrorism.

For your vehicle, we do not pay for mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdown, wear and tear, rust or corrosion, tyre damage by punctures, cuts or bursts unless other insured damage happens at the same time, loss of value after a repair, loss of use (except the additional benefits), or loss of your vehicle to a buyer who does not pay for it.

For your liability, we do not pay for damage to property you own or are in charge of, liability from carrying dangerous goods, liability you take on under a contract, or fines, penalties or punitive damages.

This is not CTP insurance. Injury to people is covered by the compulsory third party scheme in your State or Territory. Our supplementary bodily injury cover applies only where that scheme does not respond.

We do not pay where doing so would expose us to a sanction under Australian law, United Nations resolutions, or the sanctions laws of the European Union, the United Kingdom or the United States.

If you do not meet your duty to take reasonable care (section 11), or a condition of the policy, we may be able to refuse or reduce a claim, as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

Part B sets out the exclusions in full, under What Is Not Covered for Your Car, What Is Not Covered for Your Legal Liability and General Exclusions.

6. How This Policy Differs From Standard Cover

The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) and the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017 (Cth) set a standard cover for motor vehicle insurance taken out by an individual. Standard cover pays an amount sufficient to indemnify you for the destruction, theft or accidental damage of your vehicle in Australia, and covers liability for damage to other people's property caused by the use of the vehicle up to at least $5 million, subject only to the exclusions the Regulations allow.

7. Your Premium

Your premium is shown in your quote and Schedule. It is made up of our base premium and the government charges that apply: stamp duty in your State or Territory, GST, and any other levy that applies.

The base premium is calculated from information about you, your drivers and your vehicle, including:

  • the type, make, model, age and value of the vehicle, and the sum insured or agreed value;
  • the cover level and the value basis you choose;
  • where the vehicle is kept overnight;
  • how the vehicle is used;
  • the age and driving experience of the youngest driver;
  • your claims history and no claim bonus rating;
  • the basic excess you choose (a higher excess lowers the premium);
  • the third party property damage limit you choose, which cannot be set below the minimum amount that standard cover requires (section 6); and
  • any options you add.

A minimum premium of $[ ] applies. [Describe any discounts.]

8. Excesses

An excess is the amount you pay towards each claim. Your Schedule shows the excesses that apply.

ExcessWhen it appliesStandard amount
Basic excessEvery claim, unless no excess applies$750, or the amount you choose [list the options offered]
Age excessIn addition, where the driver was under 25$1,000 for a driver under 21; $600 for a driver aged 21 to 24
Inexperienced driver excessIn addition, where the driver had held a licence for that class of vehicle for less than 2 years$500
Unlisted driver excessIn addition, where your Schedule names drivers and the driver was not one of them$800
Voluntary excessIn addition, where you chose a higher excess for a lower premiumAs shown in your Schedule

No excess applies to a claim for windscreen or window glass only, or where your driver was not at fault and you give us the name and address of the person responsible. We decide whether your driver was not at fault reasonably, on the evidence available to us. If you disagree, you may ask us to review the decision or make a complaint (section 19). If more than one insured vehicle is damaged in the same event, you pay one basic excess (the highest that applies), plus any additional excesses for each driver.

Example: your basic excess is $750. Your 20-year-old son, who has held his licence for 18 months, is driving and damages your car in a collision that is his fault. You pay $750 + $1,000 (age) + $500 (inexperienced driver) = $2,250, and we pay the rest of the covered repair cost.

9. No Claim Bonus

Your no claim bonus rating is shown in your Schedule. It improves by one step at each renewal after a period of insurance with no at-fault claim, up to the highest rating. It falls by 2 steps for each at-fault claim. A claim does not count as at fault where your driver was not at fault, as we decide reasonably on the evidence available, and you give us the name and address of the person responsible, or where the claim is for glass only, or for theft, storm, hail or flood.

10. Paying by Instalments

If an instalment is unpaid for one month or more, we may refuse a claim for an event after the instalment fell due, and we may cancel the policy. We will tell you in writing before we do either, as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) requires.

11. Your Duty to Take Reasonable Care

Before you enter into, renew, extend, vary or reinstate this insurance, you have a duty under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us. A misrepresentation is a false answer, an answer that is only partly true, or an answer that does not fairly reflect the truth.

Answer every question honestly, accurately and as fully as you can. If you are answering for another person who will be covered, we will treat your answers as theirs.

The duty applies until we agree to insure you. If you do not meet it, we may cancel your policy or reduce the amount we pay for a claim, or both. If the misrepresentation was fraudulent, we may cancel the policy from its start (avoid it) and refuse to pay any claim.

12. Cooling-off Period

You may cancel the policy within 21 days of the day it was issued by telling us in writing. We refund the premium in full, less any government charges we cannot recover, unless you have made a claim. This cooling-off right does not apply once you have made a claim.

13. Cancelling Your Policy

You may cancel your policy, or remove a vehicle from it, at any time by telling us in writing. We refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis, less any government charges we cannot recover. If we have paid a total loss of your vehicle, no premium is refunded for that vehicle. [State any cancellation fee, or that there is none.]

We may cancel your policy only on a ground the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, for example if you do not meet your duty to take reasonable care, if you make a fraudulent claim or if you do not pay the premium. We give you at least 30 days' written notice and refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis.

14. Telling Us About Changes

Tell us during the period of insurance if you change your car, if the address where it is kept overnight changes, if it is modified, if someone not shown in your Schedule starts to drive it regularly, or if you start to use it for business, rideshare or delivery. We may change the premium or terms because of a change you tell us about, but only by giving you written notice of the new premium or terms. They apply only from the date stated in the notice, which is not earlier than 14 days after we send it. If you do not accept them, you may cancel the policy, we refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis from the date of cancellation, and the new premium or terms do not apply to you.

15. Renewal

Before your policy expires we will send you a renewal offer, or tell you that we will not offer renewal, at least 14 days before the expiry date. Check that your details and the sum insured are still correct. [State whether the policy renews automatically if the premium is paid by direct debit, and how to stop it.]

16. Making a Claim

Contact us as soon as you can on [claims phone], [online claims URL] or [email]. We will ask what happened, the details of each vehicle and person involved and any witnesses, and for a police report number for theft or malicious damage. Do not admit fault or agree to pay anyone without our consent, except as the law requires. Take reasonable steps to prevent further damage, and let us inspect the vehicle before repairs start, except emergency repairs needed to make it safe.

If you do not meet one of these obligations, we will not refuse your claim for that reason alone. Under s 54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) our liability is reduced only by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced. Where what you did or did not do could reasonably be regarded as capable of causing or contributing to the loss, we may refuse only the part of the claim that it caused or contributed to, and we may not refuse any part that you prove it did not cause.

Handling your claim is a financial service, and we must handle it efficiently, honestly and fairly. If we need more information, we will tell you what we need and why. We will tell you our decision in writing, with our reasons.

18. Financial Hardship and Extra Support

If you are experiencing financial hardship and owe us money (for example an excess, or an amount we have paid for which you are responsible), you may ask us for help. If you are experiencing vulnerability, for example because of family violence, illness, a disability or difficulty communicating in English, tell us and we will work with you to give you the support you need. Contact [hardship and support contact].

19. Complaints

If you are not happy with our product, our service or a decision we have made, contact us first on [complaints phone], [email] or [postal address]. We will acknowledge your complaint within one business day and give you a written response within 30 calendar days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, or we do not respond within 30 calendar days, you may take your complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), an independent external dispute resolution scheme. AFCA's service is free to you. Website www.afca.org.au; email info@afca.org.au; phone 1800 931 678; post GPO Box 3, Melbourne VIC 3001. Time limits may apply to complaints to AFCA.
