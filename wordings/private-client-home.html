Private Client Home Insurance

Accidental damage cover for high-value homes in Australia, with contents, valuables, worldwide personal liability, and family protection and cyber cover.

About This Policy

This policy insures a private home of high value, the contents and valuables of the household that lives in it, the household's legal liability, and the household against the cost of a home invasion, a car-jacking, identity fraud and a cyber event.

The schedule shows which of the property sections apply and their sums insured. Personal Liability and Family Protection and Cyber apply to every policy.

SectionWhat it coversBasis
Home BuildingDamage to the building from any sudden and accidental causeDamage during the policy period
ContentsDamage to your contents from any sudden and accidental cause, at the home and anywhere in the worldDamage during the policy period
ValuablesDamage to jewellery, watches, art, antiques, collectables, wine, musical instruments and other valuables, in each category the schedule showsDamage during the policy period
Personal LiabilityYour legal liability for injury or property damage, anywhere in the worldOccurrence during the policy period
Family Protection and CyberThe cost of a home invasion, a car-jacking, identity fraud or a cyber eventEvent during the policy period

The extensions are part of the section they appear in. Further cover, and the terms that apply to particular homes, are added by endorsement.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

Important Information

Who issues this policy. The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

Who this policy is for. This wording is designed for retail clients who own a home of high value in Australia and live in it, or use it as a second home. It is sold through insurance brokers. An issuer using it must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available, as the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) requires. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.

Your duty when you apply. This policy is a consumer insurance contract under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). You have a duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated. Answer every question in the proposal honestly and completely. If you do not take reasonable care, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

Terms that differ from standard cover. The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) and the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017 set standard cover for home building and home contents contracts. In most respects this policy gives more cover than standard cover. It gives less cover than standard cover in each of the ways listed here. They are listed so that you are clearly informed of each of them before this policy is entered into.

  • flood is covered, but we do not pay for damage by storm or flood in the first 72 hours after this policy first starts, except as the Storm and Flood in the First 72 Hours exclusion allows;
  • we do not pay for damage by erosion, subsidence or landslip except as the Earth Movement exclusion allows, or for damage by action of the sea (see the Action of the Sea exclusion);
  • we do not pay to fix a defect, or for damage from gradual causes, insects, vermin or tree roots (see the Defects and Gradual Causes exclusion);
  • cover is limited while the home is unoccupied for more than 100 days (see the Unoccupied Home exclusion);
  • we do not pay for the matters listed in the Other Property Exclusions exclusion, including theft or deliberate damage by a member of your household, land, loss of market value, an order of a government or public authority, damage while property is being worked on, breakdown, and damage by an animal kept at the home;
  • we do not pay for the matters listed in the Valuables Exclusions exclusion for valuables;
  • we do not pay for loss, damage or liability caused deliberately by you, by a member of your household or by a person acting with your authority, or arising from illegal activity by you or a member of your household that you knew of or had reason to suspect (see the Deliberate Acts exclusion and the Illegal Activity exclusion);
  • we do not pay for loss of, damage to or corruption of electronic data, or for loss from unauthorised use of a computer system, except as the Family Protection and Cyber section provides (see the Electronic Data and Computer Systems exclusion);
  • we do not pay for loss, damage, liability or cost arising from terrorism, war or nuclear risks (see the Terrorism exclusion, the War exclusion and the Nuclear exclusion);
  • we pay no more than the limits in the Limits for Some Contents provision for some contents, and no more than the category sums insured and item limits in The Most We Pay for Valuables provision for valuables;
  • the extensions to each section are limited as each states;
  • the excesses shown in the schedule apply to each claim under the property sections (see the Excess provision).

If you have not made a claim, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover. Commission and fees. Your broker may be paid a commission by us, or may charge you a fee instead of or as well as a commission. The schedule or the quote shows any commission or fee paid to your broker in connection with this policy.

Financial Claims Scheme. If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.

Schedule

(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

  • Your name, and the name of anyone else insured; where the insured is a company or the trustee of a trust, the people who live in the home (see the E13 Company or Trust as Insured endorsement)
  • The address of the home, and whether it is your main residence or a second home
  • The policy period, with its start and end dates
  • The sections that apply, with the building sum insured, the extended replacement percentage, the contents sum insured, each valuables category sum insured, and the unspecified item limit for each category
  • Each specified item, with its category, description and agreed value
  • The limit of liability, and the family protection and cyber limits where they differ from the defaults
  • The basic excess, and any special excess for flood, storm, bushfire or cyclone
  • The limits of the extensions, where they differ from the defaults stated in them
  • Any interested party, such as a financier
  • The premium, including stamp duty, any emergency services levy, and GST
  • Any commission paid to your broker, or any fee your broker charges you
  • The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see the Governing Law condition)
  • The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables

Definitions

In this policy, the words below have these meanings wherever they appear, whatever their capitalisation. The singular includes the plural.

Action of the Sea

The action of the sea, high water, tidal wave or storm surge, and any rise in the level of the sea. It does not include a tsunami.

Agreed Value

For a specified item, the value shown for it in the schedule. It is agreed between you and us when the item is first specified, and changes only when we issue a new schedule.

Building

The home, and on the same land:

  • garages, carports, sheds, pool houses, guest houses and other outbuildings;
  • fixed fixtures and fittings, including kitchen and bathroom fittings, built-in furniture, fixed floor coverings, fixed heating and cooling, fixed light fittings, home automation and security systems, solar panels and home batteries, and a fixed electric vehicle charger;
  • fences, gates, retaining walls, paths, driveways and fixed outdoor surfaces;
  • fixed swimming pools, spas, saunas, tennis courts, decks, pergolas, jetties, pontoons and boat ramps;
  • water tanks, septic systems, and services, including water, gas, electrical and telephone connections, that you own.

It does not include the land, trees, plants and lawns (except as the Landscaping extension provides), or any structure used mainly for a business. Where the home is a lot in a strata scheme, it does not include any part that the body corporate must insure.

Car-Jacking

The theft or attempted theft of a vehicle, by force or the threat of force, while a member of your household is in or entering the vehicle.

Category

One of the following categories of valuables. The schedule shows a sum insured for each category you insure under the Valuables section.

  • jewellery and watches: jewellery and watches kept at the home or worn
  • jewellery and watches in a vault: jewellery and watches kept in a vault
  • fine art: paintings, drawings, prints, photographs, works on paper and sculpture
  • antiques and decorative arts: antiques, silver, ceramics, glass, clocks, rugs and furniture of artistic or historic value
  • collectables: coins, banknotes, stamps, medals, memorabilia, rare books and manuscripts
  • wine: wine and spirits kept in a cellar or wine storage unit
  • musical instruments: musical instruments and their cases and accessories
  • other valuables: handbags, luggage, pens, cameras, sunglasses and other personal valuables of high value
Contents

Household goods and personal effects that you or a member of your household own, or for which you are legally responsible, including furniture, furnishings, carpets and floor coverings that are not fixed, curtains and blinds, clothing, electrical appliances and electronics, jewellery and watches, art, wine, tools and garden equipment, and sporting equipment.

It does not include money other than as the Limits for Some Contents provision provides; vehicles, trailers, caravans, aircraft and drones (other than ride-on mowers, mobility scooters, toys and e-bikes not needing registration); watercraft longer than 4 metres; animals; plants in the ground; property held for sale or used mainly for a business; or property of a tenant or boarder.

An item in a category for which the schedule shows a sum insured under the Valuables section is insured only under that section and is not contents.

Cyber Event

Any of the following, happening during the policy period:

  • access to, or use of, a computer, phone, tablet or other device, or an online account, used by a member of your household for personal purposes, by a person who is not permitted to access or use it;
  • malicious software on such a device or account;
  • a threat to damage such a device or account, or to publish the household's personal information, unless money or property is handed over;
  • a deception, made by email, message, telephone or website, that leads a member of your household to transfer money or to disclose a password or account details.
Damage

Physical loss of, or physical damage to, property. Damaged has a corresponding meaning.

Domestic Worker

A person employed or engaged by you or a member of your household to work at the home in domestic duties, such as a cleaner, housekeeper, nanny, gardener, driver or carer, who is not employed in a business of yours.

Endorsement

A written change to this policy that we issue and that is shown in the schedule. The available endorsements are listed in the Endorsement Library.

Event

One occurrence, or a series of occurrences arising from one original cause. All damage from storm, flood or earthquake within any 72 hour period is one event.

Excess

The amount you pay towards a claim, as the Excess provision sets out.

Flood

The covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from the normal confines of a lake (whether or not it has been altered or modified), a river (whether or not it has been altered or modified), a creek (whether or not it has been altered or modified), another natural watercourse (whether or not it has been altered or modified), a reservoir, a canal or a dam.

Home

The private residence at the address shown in the schedule.

Home Invasion

Entry into the home, or any building on the same land, by a person without your permission, by force or the threat of force, while a member of your household is there.

Household

You, your spouse or partner, and your and their children and other relatives who live with you at the home. Where the E13 Company or Trust as Insured endorsement applies, it means the people named for it in the schedule and their spouses, partners, children and relatives who live with them at the home. It does not include a tenant or boarder who pays to live at the home, or a domestic worker.

Personal Injury

Bodily injury, death, sickness, disease, disability, shock, fright, mental anguish or mental injury.

Policy Period

The period shown in the schedule, unless this policy is cancelled earlier.

Professional Valuation

A written valuation by a valuer qualified in the kind of property valued, who is not selling it to you, that describes the property and states its replacement value or rebuilding cost at the date of the valuation. For the building, it includes a written estimate of the rebuilding cost by a quantity surveyor, a registered builder, or a risk consultant appointed by us.

Property Damage

Physical damage to or destruction of tangible property that you do not own, including the loss of its use.

Rebuilding Cost

The cost, at the time of repair or rebuilding, of repairing or rebuilding the building as the How We Settle Building Claims provision sets out, including the costs that provision says are included.

Specified Item

An item of valuables listed individually in the schedule with its category, description and agreed value. A pair or set may be listed as one specified item.

Storm

A violent wind, with or without rain, hail or snow, and including a cyclone or tornado.

Sum Insured

The amount shown in the schedule for the building, the contents or a category of valuables.

Unoccupied

No member of your household, and no one else with your permission, has lived in the home or stayed overnight in it. A domestic worker who stays overnight at the home with your permission is someone with your permission.

Unspecified Item

An item of valuables that is not a specified item.

Valuables

Property in a category for which the schedule shows a sum insured under the Valuables section, that you or a member of your household own or for which you are legally responsible, kept for personal use and not held for sale or used mainly for a business.

Vault

A safe deposit box or locked vault at a bank, or at a commercial safe custody facility, named in the schedule for the E10 Vault Storage endorsement.

We, Us, Our

The insurer named in the schedule.

You, Your

The person, people, company or trustee named in the schedule as the insured. Each of them is an insured.

Home Building

This section applies if the schedule shows it. The schedule shows the building sum insured, the extended replacement percentage and the basic excess.

Damage to the Building

If the building suffers damage during the policy period from any sudden and accidental cause, including fire, storm, flood, earthquake, escape of liquid, theft, malicious damage and impact, we will pay to repair or rebuild it, as set out in the How We Settle Building Claims provision.

This cover applies to damage from any sudden and accidental cause unless an exclusion in the Property Exclusions section or the General Exclusions section applies.

The most we will pay for any one event is the building sum insured together with any amount payable under the Extended Replacement provision, plus the amounts the extensions to this section say are payable in addition.

How We Settle Building Claims

Repair or rebuild. We pay the cost of repairing or rebuilding the damaged part of the building to a condition substantially the same as, but not better or more extensive than, its condition when new, using materials and methods of a quality equal to the original. Where the original materials or methods are no longer available, we use the nearest equivalent of equal quality. We do not deduct for wear, tear or depreciation.

Costs included. The cost of repairing or rebuilding includes the reasonable cost of demolishing and removing debris, the fees of architects, engineers, surveyors and other professionals needed to repair or rebuild, and the extra cost of complying with the building laws in force at the time of repair for the damaged part of the building.

Matching materials. If we cannot find materials that match the undamaged parts, we pay to replace the materials in the room or area where the damage happened, and in any adjoining area that can be seen together with it, so that the result reasonably matches.

How we arrange the work. You may choose the builder, as long as the builder is licensed for the work and the quote is reasonable. We may instead offer a builder we appoint, who guarantees the work for at least six years. We pay the builder directly or reimburse you as the work is done.

Cash settlement. You may choose to be paid instead of having the building repaired or rebuilt, as the Cash Settlement provision sets out.

No under-insurance penalty. We do not reduce a claim because the building sum insured is less than the full rebuilding cost. The Extended Replacement provision sets out what we pay above the building sum insured.

Extended Replacement

If the rebuilding cost after one event is more than the building sum insured, we pay the rebuilding cost up to the building sum insured plus the extended replacement percentage shown in the schedule (default 25%, or 50% where the E01 Extended Replacement 50% endorsement applies) of the building sum insured.

This applies if, when this policy started or was last renewed, the building sum insured was not less than the rebuilding cost stated in the most recent professional valuation you gave us, or in an estimate made with a rebuilding cost calculator we provided, and you told us of any building works since then that increased the rebuilding cost by more than 10%.

Where the E09 Building Valuation to Follow endorsement applies, extended replacement applies only as that endorsement states.

Cash Settlement

When we pay cash. If you ask us to, or if the law does not allow the building to be repaired or rebuilt, we pay you the amount set out below instead of repairing or rebuilding. We will not pay cash instead of repairing or rebuilding without your agreement, unless the law does not allow the repair or rebuilding.

How we work out the amount. The amount is the reasonable cost of repairing or rebuilding the damaged part of the building at the date we make the offer, as the How We Settle Building Claims provision sets out, worked out from a written scope of works and at least two quotes from licensed builders, or an estimate by a quantity surveyor. It includes the costs that provision says are included, and any amount payable under the Extended Replacement provision. We do not reduce it by any discount or rate that a builder we appoint would have given us.

What we give you. With the offer we give you in writing the scope of works, the quotes or estimate we relied on, how we worked out the amount, and any amount we deducted and why. You may give us your own quote or estimate, and we will consider it and tell you in writing whether we change the offer and why. You have at least 20 business days to consider the offer before it is withdrawn.

After you accept. Once we have paid the agreed amount, we have no further liability for the damage it settled, other than for temporary accommodation already payable under the Temporary Accommodation extension up to the date of payment.

Temporary Accommodation

If damage covered by the Home Building section makes the home unfit to live in, we pay the reasonable cost of temporary accommodation of a similar standard for your household, your domestic workers who live at the home and your pets, and the reasonable cost of moving and storing your contents, until the home is fit to live in again.

The most we pay for any one event is 20% of the building sum insured, for up to 24 months, or the higher limit and period shown in the schedule. These limits are in addition to the building sum insured.

If the home is your second home, we pay instead the rent you lose, or the reasonable cost of comparable accommodation for the periods your household had planned to stay, within the same limits.

Emergency Repairs

After damage covered by this section, we pay the reasonable cost of emergency repairs, temporary fencing, boarding up, security guards and other measures needed to prevent further damage or to make the property safe, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $25,000) for any one event. This amount is in addition to the building sum insured. Keep the invoices and photographs of the damage.

Keys and Locks

If a key, remote control or access code to an external door, gate, window lock or alarm of the home is stolen, or a lock is damaged, we pay the reasonable cost to replace or re-key the affected locks and to reprogram the remote controls and alarm, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $5,000) for any one event. No excess applies. This amount is in addition to the building sum insured.

Landscaping

We pay the reasonable cost to replace trees, shrubs, plants, hedges and lawns at the home damaged by any sudden and accidental cause other than drought, frost, disease, insects or neglect, and to remove a fallen tree or branch that has damaged the building or blocks access to it, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $50,000) for any one event, and $10,000 for any one tree. This amount is in addition to the building sum insured.

Fire and Emergency Services Charges

We pay any charge a fire or emergency service lawfully makes for attending the home because of damage covered by this section, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $25,000) for any one event. This amount is in addition to the building sum insured.

Mortgage Discharge Costs

If the building is destroyed and we pay the building sum insured, we pay the reasonable legal costs of discharging a mortgage over the home, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $10,000).

Motor Burnout

We pay to repair or replace an electric motor in the building or in your contents, including a motor in a pool pump, air conditioner, gate, lift, refrigerator or washing machine, that burns out during the policy period, if the motor is less than 15 years old. The most we pay for any one motor is the limit shown in the schedule (default $25,000), and the basic excess applies. We do not pay for a motor while the manufacturer's guarantee covers it.

Contents

This section applies if the schedule shows it. The schedule shows the contents sum insured and the basic excess.

Damage to Contents

If your contents suffer damage during the policy period from any sudden and accidental cause, we will repair or replace them, as set out in the How We Settle Contents Claims provision.

The cover applies at the home, and anywhere in the world while the contents are with a member of your household or are being moved by a professional removalist, unless an exclusion in the Property Exclusions section or the General Exclusions section applies.

The most we will pay for any one event is the contents sum insured, subject to the Limits for Some Contents provision, plus the amounts the extensions to this section say are payable in addition.

How We Settle Contents Claims

We pay the cost of repairing the damaged item, or of replacing it with a new item of the same kind and quality, whichever is less, or we pay you that amount. We do not deduct for wear, tear or depreciation. You may choose the repairer or supplier, as long as the cost is reasonable.

If an item that is part of a pair or set is damaged, we pay the reduction in the value of the pair or set caused by that damage, as the Pairs and Sets provision sets out.

We do not reduce a claim because the contents sum insured is less than the replacement value of all your contents.

Contents in Storage and When Moving

We cover your contents for damage from any sudden and accidental cause while they are:

  • at another home you are moving to, for 60 days after you start moving;
  • at a residence where a dependent child of your household lives while studying, anywhere in the world, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $25,000);
  • in a locked room at a bank or commercial storage facility in Australia, up to 20% of the contents sum insured.

Spoiled Food and Medicine

We pay for food and medicine in a fridge, freezer or cool room at the home that spoils because the equipment breaks down, or because the electricity supply fails for more than 12 hours, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $5,000) for any one event. No excess applies.

Guests' and Domestic Workers' Property

We cover property at the home owned by a visitor or a domestic worker, for damage from any sudden and accidental cause, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $10,000) for any one event. The Other Insurance provision applies.

Accommodation Where Only Contents Are Insured

If the Home Building section does not apply and damage from a sudden and accidental cause makes the home unfit to live in, we pay the reasonable cost of temporary accommodation of a similar standard for your household, and of moving and storing your contents, until the home is fit to live in again or your tenancy ends, up to 20% of the contents sum insured, for up to 24 months. If you rent the home, we pay only the amount by which the temporary accommodation costs more than the rent you no longer have to pay.

Valuables

This section applies to each category for which the schedule shows a sum insured. The schedule shows each category sum insured, the unspecified item limit for each category, each specified item and the basic excess.

Damage to Valuables

If any of your valuables suffer damage during the policy period, in a place where the Where Valuables Are Covered clause says they are covered, we will pay for the damage as set out in the How We Settle Valuables Claims provision.

This cover applies to damage from any cause, including theft and disappearance that cannot be explained, unless an exclusion in the Property Exclusions section or the General Exclusions section applies. The most we pay is set out in The Most We Pay for Valuables provision.

Where Valuables Are Covered

Each category is covered in the places shown in this table.

CategoryWhere it is covered

Jewellery and watches in a vaultIn the vault, and anywhere in the world while out of the vault as the E10 Vault Storage endorsement allows
Other valuablesAnywhere in the world

The schedule may show wider places of cover for a category.

How We Settle Valuables Claims

Specified items. If a specified item is lost, stolen or damaged beyond economic repair, we pay you its agreed value. With your agreement, we may replace it instead. If it can be repaired economically, we pay the reasonable cost of repair by a repairer or restorer that you and we agree on, and you may not unreasonably withhold your agreement. The Loss in Value After Repair extension may also apply.

Unspecified items. For an unspecified item, we may repair it, replace it with an item of the same kind and quality, or pay you what it would cost to do so. We choose the option that costs least, and we do not deduct for wear, tear or depreciation. The most we pay for any one unspecified item is the unspecified item limit shown in the schedule for its category (default $25,000).

No reduction for underinsurance. We do not reduce a claim because a category sum insured is less than the value of the items in that category. The limits in The Most We Pay for Valuables provision still apply.

The Most We Pay for Valuables

For any one event, after deducting the excess, the most we pay is:

  • for a specified item, its agreed value, together with any amount payable under the Loss in Value After Repair extension within the limit that extension sets;
  • for an unspecified item, the unspecified item limit for its category; and
  • for all items in a category, the category sum insured.

The amounts payable under the New Acquisitions extension are in addition to these limits, up to the amount that extension sets.

Pairs and Sets

If an item that is part of a pair or set suffers damage, we pay the reduction in the value of the pair or set caused by that damage.

If you prefer, you may give us the undamaged items in the pair or set, and we will then pay the value of the whole pair or set. For a specified item, that value is its agreed value. For unspecified valuables, the pair or set counts as one item and the unspecified item limit applies to it. For contents, the limits in the Limits for Some Contents provision apply to it.

Loss in Value After Repair

If a specified item of fine art, or of antiques and decorative arts, is repaired after damage covered by this policy, and the item is then worth less than its agreed value because of the damage, we also pay the difference between its agreed value and its value after repair.

The value after repair is agreed between you and us. If we cannot agree, it is set by a professional valuation obtained by us at our cost from a valuer you have agreed to, and you may not unreasonably withhold your agreement.

The most we pay for the repair and the loss in value together is the agreed value of the item.

New Acquisitions

Valuables you acquire during the policy period in a category you insure are covered from when you acquire them for 60 days, up to 25% of that category sum insured, in addition to it. Tell us about them within 60 days if you want them to stay insured, and we will charge any additional premium from the date you acquired them.

Property Exclusions

These exclusions apply to the Home Building, Contents and Valuables sections.

Storm and Flood in the First 72 Hours

We do not pay for damage by storm or flood that happens in the first 72 hours after this policy first starts. This exclusion does not apply if this policy started immediately after another policy covering the home for storm and flood expired without a break, or if you bought the home within 72 hours before this policy started.

Defects and Gradual Causes

We do not pay for:

  • the cost of fixing a defect in the design, workmanship or materials of the building;
  • wear and tear, rust, corrosion, rot, mould, mildew or gradual deterioration;
  • damage that happens gradually over time, including from water or moisture seeping, leaking or condensing over a period, unless you did not know of it and could not reasonably have known of it;
  • damage caused by insects, vermin or tree roots.

We do pay for damage to other parts of the building, and to contents and valuables, that results suddenly and accidentally from a defect or from one of these causes, for example a ceiling that collapses because a pipe bursts.

Earth Movement

We do not pay for damage caused by erosion, subsidence, settling, shrinkage, expansion, landslide, landslip, mudslide or other movement of the earth. We do pay for such damage if it happens within 72 hours of, and as a direct result of, an earthquake, a storm, a flood, the escape of liquid from a pipe or tank, or an explosion.

Action of the Sea

We do not pay for damage caused by action of the sea, unless the schedule shows that it is covered.

Unoccupied Home

If the home has been unoccupied for more than 100 consecutive days, we pay for damage that happens after that only if it is caused by fire, lightning, explosion, earthquake or impact, unless the E07 Unoccupied Home endorsement applies. Cover is restored when a member of your household returns to live in or stay at the home.

Other Property Exclusions

Under the Home Building, Contents and Valuables sections, we do not pay for:

  • theft by, or damage caused deliberately by, a member of your household, or by a person at the home with your permission other than a tradesperson or a domestic worker;
  • land, or the cost of stabilising or improving land;
  • loss of market value, other than as the Loss in Value After Repair extension provides;
  • damage to a retaining wall that is not built to the standard of the building laws in force when it was built;
  • damage by water entering the building through an opening made for building works;
  • damage caused by a lawful order or action of a government or public authority, other than to prevent the spread of fire or other damage;
  • damage to property while it is being worked on, cleaned, restored or repaired, if the damage results from the work, other than damage by fire or theft;
  • mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdown, other than as the Motor Burnout extension provides, or damage that results suddenly and accidentally from it;
  • damage by an animal kept at the home, other than damage that is sudden and is not caused by chewing, scratching, pecking or soiling.

The Joint Insureds condition applies to the first exclusion in this list.

Valuables Exclusions

Under the Valuables section, we do not pay for:

  • breakage of a fragile item while it is being moved, unless it was packed by a professional packer, or the breakage is caused by fire, theft, or an accident to the vehicle carrying it;
  • theft of an item left in an unattended vehicle, unless the vehicle was locked and the item was out of sight;
  • wine or spirits spoiled by a change in temperature, unless the change is caused by sudden damage to the cellar or its equipment from an external cause;
  • wear of strings, reeds, skins or bows of musical instruments;
  • an item that is confiscated, or that you must give up because another person proves a better title to it.

Personal Liability

This section applies to every policy. The schedule shows the limit of liability.

Personal Liability

We will pay the amount a member of your household becomes legally liable to pay as compensation for personal injury or property damage that happens during the policy period anywhere in the world, caused by an occurrence:

  • at the home, in connection with your ownership or occupation of it; or
  • anywhere else, in connection with the personal activities of a member of your household.

Where you are a company or the trustee of a trust, we also pay the amount you become legally liable to pay as owner of the home.

Domestic workers. We pay the amount you become legally liable to pay for personal injury to a domestic worker arising from their work at the home, where no workers compensation law requires you to insure that liability.

Limit. The most we pay for all compensation arising from one occurrence is the limit of liability shown in the schedule (default $20,000,000). All personal injury and property damage arising from one source or original cause is one occurrence.

Defence costs. In addition to the limit, we pay the reasonable legal costs we incur or approve in defending or settling a claim covered by this section, and interest on a judgment up to the date we pay or offer to pay it.

Settlement. We may take over and conduct the defence or settlement of any claim in your name. We may at any time pay you the limit, less any amount already paid, and then we have no further obligation for that occurrence except for defence costs already incurred.

Courts outside Australia. For a claim brought in a court in the United States of America or Canada, defence costs are included within the limit, not in addition to it.

Injury to the Household

We do not pay for personal injury to a member of your household, or damage to property owned by, or in the care or control of, a member of your household.

Workers Compensation

We do not pay for personal injury to a person employed by you that arises out of their employment, where you are required by law to insure that liability under a workers compensation scheme, or would be but for an exemption.

Contractual Liability

We do not pay for liability you agreed to under a contract, to the extent it is more than the liability you would have had without the contract.

Vehicles, Aircraft and Watercraft

We do not pay for liability arising from the use of a vehicle that is registered or required to be registered, of an aircraft or a drone weighing more than 2 kilograms, or of a watercraft longer than 8 metres. This exclusion does not apply to a ride-on mower, mobility scooter, or e-bike that does not need registration.

Pollution

We do not pay for liability arising from the discharge, dispersal or escape of pollutants, unless it is sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected, and happens at a specific time and place during the policy period.

Asbestos

We do not pay for liability arising from asbestos, or from any material containing asbestos.

Business and Letting

We do not pay for liability arising from a business, trade or profession of a member of your household, or from letting the home or any part of it, unless the E08 Short-Stay Letting endorsement applies to that letting.

Fines and Penalties

We do not pay fines, penalties, or punitive, exemplary or aggravated damages.

The most we pay for any one home invasion or car-jacking is the family protection limit shown in the schedule (default $50,000). No excess applies.

Identity Fraud and Cyber Events

Identity fraud. If a member of your household discovers during the policy period that a person has used their personal information without permission to obtain money, credit, goods or services, we pay the reasonable cost of restoring their identity and credit record, including legal costs, the cost of replacing identity documents, and their lost wages for time off work to do so.

Cyber events. If a member of your household discovers a cyber event during the policy period, we pay:

  • the reasonable cost of an information technology specialist to remove malicious software, and to restore data and software on the devices and accounts affected, to their condition before the cyber event;
  • the reasonable cost of a specialist to respond to a threat of the kind described in the definition of cyber event, and to remove the household's personal information from websites where it was published without permission;
  • money taken from a personal bank account, credit card or digital wallet of a member of your household by an unauthorised electronic transfer, or transferred because of a deception, that the bank or other financial institution does not repay.

Limit. The most we pay under this clause for all identity fraud and cyber events discovered in one policy period is the cyber limit shown in the schedule (default $50,000), and $25,000 of that for money taken or transferred. No excess applies.

E12 Item Valuations to Follow

For each specified item listed in the schedule for this endorsement, you have not yet given us a professional valuation or a receipt dated within the last three years. Until you do, the most we pay for that item is the lower of its agreed value and the amount shown in the schedule for this endorsement.

Give us the valuation or receipt within the number of days after this policy starts shown in the schedule for this endorsement. When you do, this endorsement stops applying to that item.

Schedule variables: items; amount payable until valuation (default $10,000); days to give the valuation (default 60).

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Is there any item worth $10,000 or more without a professional valuation, or a receipt, dated within the last three years? qs.hnw.valuables.valuations_outstanding
  • What is the value of the most valuable single item, or pair or set? qs.hnw.valuables.highest_item_value

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.hnw.valuables.valuations_outstanding == yes and qs.hnw.valuables.highest_item_value > 50000: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected
  • When qs.hnw.valuables.valuations_outstanding == yes: Apply automatically

Underwriter responses (on a referral or suggestion): Attach; Attach with amended sub-limit; Do not attach; Decline risk

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because some items worth $10,000 or more have no valuation or receipt less than three years old; above $50,000 an item it is referred.

E13 Company or Trust as Insured

You are a company, or the trustee of a trust, that owns the home. The people named in the schedule for this endorsement live in the home, and they and their households are the household for this policy. They are insured for their own contents, valuables and personal liability as if each of them were named as an insured, and the Joint Insureds condition applies to them.

E15 Lloyd's Underwriters

This policy is underwritten by the Lloyd's underwriters named in the schedule. Each underwriter is liable only for its own share, and not for the share of any other underwriter.

The underwriters agree that any dispute about this policy may be heard by a court of competent jurisdiction in Australia, and that the Lloyd's General Representative in Australia named in the schedule may accept service of process for them. Complaints may also be made to Lloyd's Australia at the details shown in the schedule, as well as under the Complaints and Disputes condition.
