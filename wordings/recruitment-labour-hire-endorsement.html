Recruitment and Labour Hire Professional Indemnity Insurance — Endorsement

The Recruitment and Labour Hire Professional Indemnity Insurance terms, in the form of an endorsement to the base Professional Indemnity Insurance wording.

Application

This endorsement attaches to and forms part of the Meridian Professional Indemnity Insurance wording, version 1.4.0-DRAFT (document reference 2026/PI/0001), referred to here as the base wording. It changes the base wording only as set out below. All other terms, conditions, exclusions and limits of the base wording continue to apply.

Words defined in the base wording have the same meaning in this endorsement. Where this endorsement and the base wording conflict, this endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict.

The same terms are published as a single integrated wording, Recruitment and Labour Hire Professional Indemnity Insurance, version 1.0.0-DRAFT. Both documents are generated from the same source and have the same effect.

Replaced: Professional services

The definition of Professional services in the base wording is deleted and replaced with the following.

Professional services

The following services provided to a third party for a fee:

(a) permanent, contract and temporary recruitment, including candidate search, screening, reference and background checking, and placement;

(b) executive search;

(c) the supply of workers to host businesses (labour hire), including selecting, inducting and placing those workers, but not the work the workers perform for the host;

(d) psychometric and skills testing;

(e) outplacement and career transition services;

(f) human resources consulting; and

(g) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

Added: Wrongful Placement

The following extension is added to the base wording, after the Subcontractors and Outsourcers extension.

Wrongful Placement

We cover you for a claim by a client or host business that a candidate or worker you placed did not hold the qualifications, licences, experience, right to work or character that you represented, or that your checks should have revealed, where the claim arises from your negligence in selecting, checking or placing that person.

We will not apply the Employer's Liability exclusion to a claim under this extension.

Added: Wages, Superannuation and Employer Payments

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Employer's Liability exclusion.

Wages, Superannuation and Employer Payments

For any amount that you owe as an employer, including wages, allowances, leave entitlements, superannuation guarantee contributions and charges, payroll tax and workers compensation premiums, or any amount ordered to be paid to a worker to meet a same job, same pay or equal remuneration order.

Added: Work Performed by On-hired Workers

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Wages, Superannuation and Employer Payments exclusion.

Work Performed by On-hired Workers

Arising from the work performed by a worker you supply to a host business, while the worker is working under the direction or supervision of the host.

This exclusion does not apply to a claim arising from your negligence in selecting, checking, inducting or placing the worker, and is subject to the On-hired Workers endorsement if it is shown in the schedule.

Added: Migration Advice

The following exclusion is added to the base wording, after the Financial Services exclusion.

Migration Advice

Arising from immigration assistance or migration advice, including advice on visa eligibility or sponsorship.

Recruitment and Labour Hire Endorsements

The following endorsements apply to this policy only when the schedule shows that they apply.

An endorsement does not apply to the extent that a law requiring you to hold this insurance does not permit the cover it removes to be excluded or limited.

On-hired Workers

We will not apply the Work Performed by On-hired Workers exclusion where the worker was supplied under a written agreement with the host that places the supervision and direction of the worker's day-to-day work with the host. For a claim arising from that work, we cover your liability to the host or a third party for the worker's acts, errors or omissions in performing professional or technical work, except to the extent the host's own supervision caused or contributed to the loss.

This endorsement does not affect the Bodily Injury and Property Damage exclusion or the Employer's Liability exclusion.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • What percentage of your fees in the last 12 months came from supplying workers on hire? qs.pi.rl.onhire_pct
  • Do you supply all on-hired workers under a written agreement with the host that places day-to-day supervision with the host? qs.pi.rl.written_onhire_agreements

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.rl.onhire_pct > 0 and qs.pi.rl.written_onhire_agreements = yes: Offer as a priced option
  • When qs.pi.rl.onhire_pct > 0 and qs.pi.rl.written_onhire_agreements = no: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Offered because you supply workers to hosts under written on-hire agreements.

Payroll Administration

Payroll administration for clients, including calculating and paying wages, superannuation contributions and tax withholding on a client's behalf, is a professional service under this policy.

For all claims arising from payroll administration, the most we will pay in total, including defence costs, is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement, which is part of the limit of liability. The Wages, Superannuation and Employer Payments exclusion continues to apply to amounts you owe as an employer of your own workers.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • For how many clients do you run payroll, including paying wages or superannuation on their behalf? qs.pi.rl.payroll_clients

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.rl.payroll_clients > 0: Offer as a priced option

Underwriter responses (on a referral or suggestion): Attach; Attach with amended sub-limit; Do not attach

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Offered because you run payroll for {qs.pi.rl.payroll_clients} clients.

High-Risk Placements Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from the placement or supply of workers into roles in mining, construction, heavy industry, aviation, maritime, healthcare or security, or into any other role shown in the schedule for this endorsement.

This endorsement does not apply to a claim that a placed worker did not hold a licence or qualification that you represented they held.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • What percentage of your placements in the last 12 months were in mining, construction, heavy industry, aviation, maritime, healthcare or security roles? qs.pi.rl.high_risk_pct

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.rl.high_risk_pct > 20: Refer to the underwriter, with the endorsement pre-selected

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because {qs.pi.rl.high_risk_pct}% of your placements are in high-risk roles.

Labour Hire Licence Condition

Labour hire services in a state or territory are professional services under this policy only while you hold the labour hire licence that the law of that state or territory requires. The licence numbers are shown in the schedule.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • In which states and territories do you supply labour hire workers? qs.pi.rl.labour_hire_states
  • What are your labour hire licence numbers in each state or territory that requires one? qs.pi.rl.licence_numbers

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.rl.labour_hire_states is not empty: Apply automatically

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because you provide labour hire in a state or territory that requires a licence.

Migration Advice

We will not apply the Migration Advice exclusion to immigration assistance given by a registered migration agent named in the schedule, while that agent is registered.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Do you, or anyone acting for you, give migration advice or immigration assistance? qs.pi.rl.migration_advice
  • If so, what is the name and registration number (MARN) of each registered migration agent who gives it? qs.pi.rl.marn

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.rl.migration_advice = yes: Offer as a priced option

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Offered because a registered migration agent advises your clients or candidates.

Automated Screening Exclusion

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from a decision about a candidate that was made by an automated system, including an artificial intelligence tool, without review by a person before the decision was communicated.

Wording note — platform rule (not part of the policy)

Questions

  • Do you use automated tools, including artificial intelligence, to screen or rank candidates? qs.pi.rl.ai_screening

Default rules (the first rule that matches applies; agencies may set their own actions and thresholds)

  • When qs.pi.rl.ai_screening = yes: Suggest to the underwriter (quote not held)

Reason shown on the quote and schedule: Applies because you use automated tools to screen candidates.
