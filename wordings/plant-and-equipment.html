Plant and Equipment Insurance

Owned, financed, hired-in and hired-out mobile plant and equipment in Australia: all-risks material damage, with optional hired-in plant, machinery breakdown and mobile plant liability.

About This Policy

This policy insures mobile plant and equipment, such as excavators, loaders, cranes, telehandlers, skid steers, generators, compressors and their attachments, whether the plant is owned, financed, hired in or hired out. It has four sections. The schedule shows which apply.

SectionWhat it coversBasis
Material DamageAccidental loss of or damage to insured plant, including theft, transit, storm and floodLoss or damage during the policy period
Hired-In PlantYour liability for loss of or damage to plant you hire from others, and continuing hire chargesLoss or damage during the policy period
Machinery BreakdownSudden breakdown of specified plantBreakdown during the policy period
Mobile Plant LiabilityLiability to third parties for personal injury and property damage arising from insured plant, including its use as a tool of tradeOccurrence during the policy period

Material Damage applies whenever the schedule shows an item of insured plant. Hired-In Plant, Machinery Breakdown and Mobile Plant Liability apply only if the schedule shows them as included.

All risks. Material Damage covers accidental loss or damage from any cause that is not excluded. It does not list the events that are covered. Where we rely on an exclusion, we must show that it applies.

Other policies. Road risk for registered plant driven as transport on a public road belongs under a commercial motor policy (see the Road Use as Transport exclusion). Plant at a construction site may also be insured under a contract works policy; where both respond, the Other Insurance provision applies.

This policy, together with the schedule, the proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

It is not a Product Disclosure Statement, and no Target Market Determination has been prepared for it. An insurer that offers it to retail clients must first prepare the documents the law requires for retail distribution.

Schedule

(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

  • The named insured and its ABN, its business and its address
  • The policy period
  • The plant schedule: for each item of specified plant, its description, serial or registration number and year; its basis of settlement (market value or agreed value); its sum insured or agreed value; its excess; any financier; and whether it is hired out, and if so on dry hire or wet hire
  • The unspecified plant sum insured and the most we pay for any one item of unspecified plant
  • The limits of the extensions to Material Damage where they differ from the defaults stated in them, and whether the hire costs benefit applies
  • For Hired-In Plant: the limit for any one item, the limit for any one event, the continuing hire charges limit and the excess
  • For Machinery Breakdown: the items of specified plant that have breakdown cover and the excess
  • For Mobile Plant Liability: the limit for any one occurrence, any sub-limits that differ from the defaults stated in this wording and the excess
  • The geographical limits, if they differ from the default in the definition
  • The premium, with stamp duty, any emergency services or fire services levy, and GST
  • Any commission or fee paid to your broker
  • The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see the Governing Law condition)
  • The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables

Definitions

In this policy, the words below have these meanings wherever they appear, whatever their capitalisation. The singular includes the plural.

Accidental Loss or Damage

Sudden, unintended and unexpected physical loss or damage, including loss by theft. It does not include loss or damage that is deliberate on the part of the named insured, or gradual deterioration.

Agreed Value

The value shown in the schedule for an item of specified plant, agreed between the named insured and us at the start of the policy period or at renewal.

Attachment

A bucket, blade, hammer, auger, fork, grapple, coupler, ripper or other work tool or accessory designed to be fitted to an item of plant, whether or not it is fitted at the time of the loss.

Breakdown

The actual breaking, distortion, burning out, or mechanical, electrical, hydraulic or electronic failure of any part of an item of plant, while in ordinary use or while being tested after repair, that makes its repair or replacement necessary before it can resume work.

Continuing Hire Charges

The hire charges the named insured remains legally liable to pay under a hire agreement for hired-in plant while that plant cannot be used because of loss or damage covered by Loss of or Damage to Hired-In Plant, until it is repaired, replaced or settled.

Cyber Event

Any access to or use of a computer system, software, network or electronic data without the authority of its owner; malicious or harmful code in any of them; a denial of service attack on any of them; or an operator error in using any of them.

Damage Waiver

A term of a hire agreement under which the named insured, as owner of hired-out plant, gives up or limits its right to recover from the hirer for loss of or damage to that plant, usually in return for a charge to the hirer.

Defence Costs

The reasonable legal and other costs incurred with our written consent, which we do not unreasonably withhold, to investigate, defend or settle a third-party claim under Mobile Plant Liability.

Dry Hire

Hiring an item of plant out to another person without an operator supplied by the named insured.

Endorsement

A written change to this policy, identified by its code in the schedule. The text of each endorsement is in the Endorsement Library.

Event

One occurrence, or a series of occurrences arising from one source or original cause. All loss or damage caused by storm, flood, earthquake, tsunami, bushfire or cyclone within any period of 72 consecutive hours is one event.

Excess

The amount shown in the schedule, or in this policy, that the named insured bears as the first part of each claim, as the Excess provision sets out.

Financier

A lender, lessor or other secured party with a financial interest in an item of specified plant, named against that item in the schedule.

Geographical Limits

Australia, including its territorial waters, or any other area shown in the schedule.

Hire Agreement

A written agreement under which plant is hired from or to the named insured, including the terms and conditions of the owner or hirer that form part of it.

Hired-In Plant

Mobile plant and equipment, and its attachments, that the named insured hires or leases from another person under a hire agreement for a period of no more than 12 months, while it is in the named insured's care, custody or control. Plant on a longer lease, or that the named insured must insure as owner under the lease, is insured plant when it is shown in the schedule.

Insured

For Mobile Plant Liability, each person and entity described in the Who Is Insured clause. For the other sections, the named insured.

Insured Plant

Each item of specified plant; unspecified plant, within the unspecified plant sum insured; and newly acquired plant under the Newly Acquired Plant extension. Insured plant includes the attachments, accessories, GPS and telematics equipment and signwriting on an item, and their value is part of its sum insured.

Market Value

The reasonable cost of buying an item of plant of the same make, model, age, specification and condition in the Australian market immediately before the loss or damage. Prices from recognised industry publications and auction results may be used as evidence of market value.

Named Insured

The person or entity shown in the schedule as the named insured.

Occurrence

An event, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same conditions, that results in personal injury or property damage neither expected nor intended by the insured. All personal injury and property damage arising from one source or original cause is one occurrence.

Personal Injury

Bodily injury, death, sickness, disease, disability, shock, fright, mental anguish or mental injury, of a person who is not an insured.

Policy Period

The period shown in the schedule, unless the policy is cancelled earlier.

Pollutant

Any solid, liquid, gaseous or thermal irritant or contaminant, including smoke, vapour, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals and waste.

Property Damage

Physical loss of, damage to or destruction of tangible property that belongs to someone other than an insured, including the loss of use of that property that results.

Schedule

The schedule issued with this policy, and any later schedule we issue to replace it.

Specified Plant

Each item of mobile plant and equipment described individually in the schedule.

Third-Party Claim

A written demand, or a civil proceeding, made against an insured for compensation for personal injury or property damage.

Tool of Trade

The operation of an item of plant to perform the work it is designed to do, such as digging, lifting, loading, drilling, compacting, spraying or generating, at any location. It does not include driving the plant as transport on a public road.

Total Loss

An item of insured plant is a total loss when it is damaged beyond economic repair, or when it is stolen and not recovered within 30 days of the theft being reported to the police.

Unspecified Plant

Mobile plant and equipment, and attachments, owned by the named insured or for which it is responsible as lessee, that is not specified plant, up to the most we pay for any one item shown in the schedule (default $25,000 for any one item).

Wear Parts

Tyres, tracks, track pads, batteries, cutting edges, teeth, drill bits, augers, bushes, hoses, belts, chains, screens, filters, and similar parts that wear out in ordinary use and are replaced from time to time.

Wet Hire

Hiring an item of plant out to another person with an operator supplied by the named insured.

We, Us, Our

The insurer named in the schedule.

You, Your

The named insured.

Material Damage

This section applies to every item of insured plant. The schedule shows the plant schedule, the unspecified plant sum insured and the excesses.

Damage to Insured Plant

We will pay for accidental loss or damage to insured plant that happens during the policy period within the geographical limits, from any cause that is not excluded, including while the plant is:

  • at your premises or at any work site;
  • in transit by road, rail or inland waterway anywhere within the geographical limits, including while being loaded and unloaded;
  • temporarily stored anywhere within the geographical limits; and
  • being operated, tested, maintained or left unattended.

We pay as set out in the Basis of Settlement provision. The extensions to this section are paid in addition to the sum insured of the item, unless an extension says otherwise.

Basis of Settlement

Market value. Unless the schedule shows agreed value for an item, we will repair the item or pay the reasonable cost of repairing it, up to its market value immediately before the loss. For a total loss, we pay the market value of the item, up to its sum insured. We make no deduction for depreciation on parts used in a repair, except for wear parts, which we settle less an allowance for the wear they had already had.

Agreed value. Where the schedule shows agreed value for an item, we will repair it or pay the reasonable cost of repairing it, up to the agreed value. For a total loss, we pay the agreed value.

New plant replacement. If an item of specified plant is a total loss within 12 months of its first registration or first use as new, we will replace it with a new item of the same make, model and specification, or the nearest equivalent, up to 120% of its sum insured.

Salvage and excess. For a total loss, we deduct the excess, and the Salvage provision applies.

No average. If the sum insured of an item is less than its value, we do not reduce the amount we pay for a partial loss in proportion.

Recovery Costs

We will pay the reasonable costs of recovering an item of insured plant that is bogged, sunk, overturned or stranded during the policy period, whether or not it is damaged, up to $25,000 for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule. Where the item is damaged by accidental loss or damage covered by Damage to Insured Plant, the recovery costs are part of that claim and this limit does not apply to them.

Expediting Costs

We will pay the reasonable extra costs of overtime, night and holiday work and express freight to speed up the repair of insured plant after loss or damage covered by Damage to Insured Plant, up to 50% of the cost of the repair, and no more than $50,000 for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule.

Newly Acquired Plant

Mobile plant and equipment that the named insured buys, or takes on a lease for which it is responsible as owner, during the policy period is insured plant for 30 days from the date it takes responsibility for it, up to $500,000 for any one item, or the amount shown in the schedule. The named insured must tell us about the item within those 30 days and pay any additional premium we reasonably charge, for cover to continue after them.

Attachments and Accessories

An attachment is insured with the item of specified plant it is declared with, whether or not it is fitted to that item at the time of the loss. An attachment not declared with an item is unspecified plant.

Appreciation in Value

If the market value of an item of specified plant has risen since the start of the policy period, we will treat its sum insured as increased by the amount of that rise, up to 15% of its sum insured, or the percentage shown in the schedule. This extension does not apply to an item insured for agreed value.

Hire Costs Following Insured Damage

If the schedule shows that this extension applies and an item of specified plant cannot be used because of loss or damage covered by Damage to Insured Plant, we will pay the reasonable cost of hiring a substitute item while the item is being repaired or replaced, up to $5,000 a week for up to 26 weeks for each item, or the amounts shown in the schedule. We do not pay for the first 72 hours after the loss or damage.

Employees' Tools and Effects

We will pay for accidental loss or damage to the tools and personal effects of the named insured's employees while on or in an item of insured plant, up to $5,000 for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule, where the loss or damage happens at the same time as loss or damage to that item covered by Damage to Insured Plant, or by theft of that item.

Re-Erection and Repositioning Costs

We will pay the reasonable costs of dismantling, re-erecting or repositioning insured plant that is necessary to repair damage covered by Damage to Insured Plant, up to $25,000 for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule.

Removal of Debris

We will pay the reasonable costs of removing debris of insured plant, and of demolishing and making safe damaged insured plant, after loss or damage covered by Damage to Insured Plant, up to $100,000 or 20% of the amount we pay for that loss or damage, whichever is greater, for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule.

Lock and Key Re-Coding

If the keys, or the electronic key or code, of an item of insured plant are stolen during the policy period, we will pay the reasonable cost of replacing them and of re-coding or replacing the locks and immobiliser, up to $10,000 for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule.

Fire Brigade and Extinguishment Charges

We will pay the charges a fire or emergency service lawfully imposes for attending a fire or other incident involving insured plant, and the cost of replacing fire extinguishing agents used to protect insured plant, up to $50,000 for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule.

Unspecified Plant

Unspecified plant is insured up to the unspecified plant sum insured shown in the schedule for any one event (default $350,000), and up to the most we pay for any one item shown in the schedule (default $25,000). Unspecified plant is settled on market value. The exclusion headed Theft from Unattended Sites applies to it.

Hired-Out Plant: Dry Hire and Wet Hire

The schedule shows whether each item of specified plant is hired out, and on what basis.

Wet hire. While an item is on wet hire, Material Damage continues to apply to it without change.

Dry hire. While an item is on dry hire, Material Damage continues to apply to it if the named insured:

  • hires it out under a written hire agreement that makes the hirer responsible for loss of or damage to it while on hire;
  • obtains and keeps a record of the hirer's identity, licence and address before the plant leaves its control; and
  • does not give up its right to recover from the hirer, except under a damage waiver whose terms the named insured has told us about.

If the named insured does not do these things, the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition applies to our response. Our right to recover from the hirer is preserved, except to the extent of a damage waiver the named insured has told us about.

Mechanical and Electrical Breakdown

Under Material Damage, we do not pay for breakdown of an item of plant, unless the breakdown is itself caused by accidental loss or damage covered by that section. We do pay for accidental loss or damage to other parts of the item, or to other insured plant, that a breakdown causes. Breakdown can be insured under Machinery Breakdown.

Wear and Tear

We do not pay for wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion, cavitation, erosion or an inherent defect. We do pay for sudden accidental loss or damage to other insured plant, or to other parts of the item, that results from one of these.

Wear Parts

We do not pay for loss or damage confined to wear parts, unless other parts of the item suffer accidental loss or damage covered by Damage to Insured Plant at the same time.

Overload and Testing

We do not pay for damage caused by deliberately loading or operating plant beyond the manufacturer's rated capacity, or by testing it beyond its design limits, unless the overload was reasonably necessary in an emergency to protect life or property.

Waterborne and Airborne Plant

We do not pay for loss or damage to plant while it is waterborne, except while on a licensed vehicle ferry or punt crossing inland waters, or while it is airborne, except while carried as cargo by a licensed carrier. Endorsement E11 changes this exclusion where it applies.

Underground and Tunnelling

We do not pay for loss or damage to plant while it is underground, in a tunnel, or in an unsupported excavation deeper than 3 metres. Endorsement E01 changes this exclusion where it applies.

Unexplained Shortage

We do not pay for unexplained disappearance, or for a shortage found only when stock or records are checked, where there is no evidence of theft or of another cause of loss.

Theft from Unattended Sites

We do not pay for theft of unspecified plant left at an unattended site, unless the plant was immobilised, locked in a vehicle or secure container, kept in a locked compound, or secured in an equivalent way. This exclusion does not apply to specified plant; the Reasonable Precautions obligation applies to all insured plant.

Consequential Loss

We do not pay for loss of use, loss of income or profit, delay, penalties under any contract, or any other consequential loss, except as the Hire Costs Following Insured Damage extension provides.

Hired-In Plant

This section applies only if the schedule shows it as included. The schedule shows its limits and excess.

Loss of or Damage to Hired-In Plant

If hired-in plant suffers accidental loss or damage during the policy period within the geographical limits while in the named insured's care, custody or control, we will pay:

  • the amount the named insured is legally liable to pay to the owner for the loss or damage, including liability it has taken on under the hire agreement;
  • continuing hire charges, up to the continuing hire charges limit shown in the schedule; and
  • the owner's reasonable recovery, salvage and towing costs that the named insured is liable to pay.

Hired-In Plant Limits

The most we pay under Hired-In Plant is the limit for any one item, and the limit for any one event, shown in the schedule (defaults $500,000 for any one item and $1,000,000 for any one event). Continuing hire charges are paid within these limits, up to $5,000 a week, or the amount shown in the schedule.

If the named insured hires in an item whose replacement value is more than the limit for any one item, it must tell us before the hire starts. We may agree to increase the limit for that item, for an additional premium.

Hired-In Plant: What Is Not Covered

Under Hired-In Plant, we do not pay for:

  • breakdown or wear of hired-in plant that the named insured did not cause;
  • hire charges for any period after the hired-in plant is repaired, replaced or settled;
  • liability under a hire agreement that makes the named insured responsible for loss or damage whatever its cause, to a standard beyond that of a reasonable bailee, unless the named insured told us about the agreement before the loss; or
  • anything excluded by the exclusions to Material Damage, read as if hired-in plant were insured plant.

Machinery Breakdown

This section applies only if the schedule shows it as included, and only to the items of specified plant that the schedule shows as having breakdown cover.

Breakdown of Specified Plant

We will pay for the sudden and unforeseen breakdown, during the policy period and within the geographical limits, of an item of specified plant that the schedule shows as having breakdown cover.

Breakdown Settlement

We pay the reasonable cost of repairing the item, or of replacing the damaged parts with parts of equivalent specification, up to the sum insured of the item, less:

  • an allowance for wear parts, and for parts with a limited working life, for the wear they had already had; and
  • the excess for Machinery Breakdown shown in the schedule.

We pay for loss of oils, coolants and refrigerants only where it results from a breakdown we pay for.

Machinery Breakdown: What Is Not Covered

Under Machinery Breakdown, we do not pay for:

  • gradual deterioration, or a fault that existed at the start of the policy period and that the named insured knew about or could reasonably have been expected to know about;
  • breakdown caused by a failure to maintain the item as its manufacturer's maintenance schedule requires;
  • breakdown caused by interfering with, or removing, a safety device;
  • wear parts and consumables themselves; or
  • repairs that the manufacturer or supplier is responsible for under a warranty or recall. If they refuse or delay, we will pay the claim and recover from them in the named insured's name.

Mobile Plant Liability

This section applies only if the schedule shows it as included. It is designed for plant that is not registered for use on a public road, so that no compulsory third party or motor policy responds, and for the tool of trade risk of registered plant.

Liability to Third Parties

We will pay the amounts an insured becomes legally liable to pay as compensation for:

  • personal injury; and
  • property damage,

that happens during the policy period within the geographical limits and arises from an occurrence in connection with the ownership, operation or use of insured plant, including its use as a tool of trade.

The most we pay for any one occurrence is the limit shown in the schedule for Mobile Plant Liability (default $20,000,000). The sub-limits in the extensions to this section are part of that limit.

Defence Costs

We will pay defence costs for a third-party claim covered by Liability to Third Parties. Defence costs are paid in addition to the limit. If the amount needed to settle a third-party claim is more than the limit, we pay the defence costs incurred before we offer or pay the limit, and only in the proportion that the limit bears to the amount of the settlement or judgment.

We may take over the defence and settlement of any third-party claim in the insured's name.

Who Is Insured

For Mobile Plant Liability, each of the following is an insured:

  • the named insured;
  • any person operating insured plant with the named insured's permission; and
  • any principal of the named insured, but only for its vicarious liability for the named insured's operation of insured plant.

Each insured is treated as if it had a separate policy, except that the limit is not increased by the number of insureds. An exclusion that applies because of one insured's knowledge or conduct applies only to that insured.

Underground Services

We pay for liability for property damage to underground pipes, cables, conduits and other services, including the loss that the owner of the services suffers as a result, only if, before the work began, the insured:

  • obtained current plans of the services, for example through Before You Dig Australia;
  • confirmed the location of the marked services by sight, or by potholing; and
  • followed any conditions that the owner of the services imposed.

The most we pay for any one occurrence under this extension is $2,000,000, or the amount shown in the schedule.

Vibration and Removal of Support

We pay for liability for property damage caused by vibration, or by the weakening or removal of support of land or buildings, if a competent person assessed the work method as suitable before the work began. The most we pay for any one occurrence under this extension is $2,000,000, or the amount shown in the schedule. We do not pay the cost of strengthening or underpinning land or buildings unless it is needed to repair property damage we pay for.

Goods Lifted

We pay for liability for property damage to goods being lifted, lowered or carried by insured plant, if a competent person planned and rigged the lift and it was within the plant's rated capacity. The most we pay for any one occurrence under this extension is $500,000, or the amount shown in the schedule. We do not pay for damage to property owned by an insured.

Sudden Pollution

We pay for liability for personal injury or property damage caused by the discharge, dispersal, release or escape of a pollutant that is sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected, and happens at a specific time and place during the policy period. The most we pay for any one occurrence, and in total for the policy period, under this extension is $1,000,000.

Injury to Workers and Injury Covered by CTP

Under Mobile Plant Liability, we do not pay for:

  • personal injury to any person that is covered, or would be covered but for a failure to insure or register, by compulsory third party motor insurance or a statutory motor accident scheme; or
  • personal injury to an employee of an insured arising from their employment, or for which workers' compensation insurance, or a workers' compensation scheme, responds or is required by law to respond.

Road Use as Transport

Under Mobile Plant Liability, we do not pay for liability arising from the use of insured plant as a registered vehicle driven as transport on a public road. A commercial motor policy is the place to insure that risk. This exclusion does not apply to the use of registered plant as a tool of trade. Endorsement E07 changes this exclusion where it applies.

Own Property

Under Mobile Plant Liability, we do not pay for property damage to property owned by, leased to, or in the physical or legal control of an insured, except as the Goods Lifted extension and Hired-In Plant provide.

Aircraft and Watercraft

Under Mobile Plant Liability, we do not pay for liability arising from the ownership, operation or use of any aircraft, watercraft or hovercraft.

Pollution

Under Mobile Plant Liability, we do not pay for liability arising from the discharge, dispersal, release or escape of a pollutant, except as the Sudden Pollution extension provides.

Contractual Liability

Under Mobile Plant Liability, we do not pay for liability that an insured takes on under a contract or agreement, to the extent it is greater than the liability the insured would have had without it. This exclusion does not apply to the usual terms of a hire agreement, a site access agreement or a construction contract.

Fines and Penalties

Under Mobile Plant Liability, we do not pay fines, penalties, or aggravated, punitive or exemplary damages, or the costs of defending a prosecution, unless the schedule or an endorsement says otherwise.

Asbestos

Under Mobile Plant Liability, we do not pay for liability arising from asbestos or any material that contains asbestos.

Payment Provisions for All Sections

These provisions apply to every section.

Limits

The most we pay for an item of specified plant under Material Damage is its sum insured or agreed value, adjusted as the Basis of Settlement provision and the Appreciation in Value extension allow, plus the amounts the extensions to that section say are payable in addition. The most we pay for unspecified plant is set by the Unspecified Plant extension. The limits for the other sections are in the Hired-In Plant Limits provision, the Breakdown Settlement provision and the Liability to Third Parties clause.

Excess

For each claim, the named insured bears:

  • under Material Damage, the excess shown in the schedule for each item that suffers loss or damage, for each event;
  • under Hired-In Plant, the excess for that section shown in the schedule, for each event;
  • under Machinery Breakdown, the excess for that section shown in the schedule, for each breakdown; and
  • under Mobile Plant Liability, the excess for that section shown in the schedule, for each occurrence. Defence costs are not subject to the excess.

The schedule or an endorsement may show additional excesses. Where more than one excess applies to the same item for the same event, only the highest is deducted.

One Section for Each Loss

We pay any one loss, breakdown or liability under one section only. Where Material Damage and Machinery Breakdown could both respond to damage to the same item from the same cause, we pay under Material Damage, and only one excess applies.

Who We Pay

We pay the named insured, or the person the named insured directs. Where the schedule shows a financier for an item, the Financier Interests condition applies to payments for that item. Under Mobile Plant Liability, we may pay the third party, or its lawyers, directly.

GST

Where the named insured is entitled to an input tax credit for the GST on a repair, replacement or other payment, we reduce our payment by the amount of that credit, as A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth) allows. The named insured must tell us its entitlement to input tax credits when it makes a claim.

Other Insurance

If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy, including a contract works policy covering the same plant at a construction site, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows. Tell us about the other insurance when you claim. This does not apply where the schedule or an endorsement states that this policy is excess of a specified underlying policy, in which case this policy pays only after the limit of that specified policy is exhausted.

Salvage

If we pay a total loss for an item, the item belongs to us, subject to the interest of any financier. Before we dispose of it, we will offer it to the named insured at its salvage value.

Exclusions for All Sections

These exclusions apply to every section.

War

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities or warlike operations (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, military or usurped power, or confiscation by a government or public authority.

Nuclear

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from ionising radiation, or contamination by radioactivity, from any nuclear fuel or nuclear waste, or from the radioactive, toxic, explosive or other hazardous properties of any nuclear assembly or component.

Terrorism

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from an act of terrorism, meaning an act, including the use of force or violence or the threat of it, by any person or group, committed for political, religious, ideological or similar purposes, including the intention to influence a government or to put the public in fear. This exclusion has no effect to the extent the Terrorism Insurance Act 2003 (Cth) requires cover to be provided.

Cyber Events

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from a cyber event. This exclusion does not apply to physical loss of or damage to insured plant that a cyber event directly causes, or to personal injury or property damage under Mobile Plant Liability that a cyber event directly causes. Endorsement E12 changes this exclusion where it applies.

Your Obligations

These obligations apply to every section. The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition governs how we respond if one is not met.

Telling Us About Claims

The insured must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable about any loss, theft, breakdown or occurrence that may give rise to a claim, and send us any third-party claim as soon as it is received. Theft and malicious damage must be reported to the police. The insured must give us the information and help we reasonably ask for, and keep damaged plant and parts available for inspection until we have had a reasonable chance to inspect them.

Admissions

An insured must not admit liability for, offer to settle, or settle any third-party claim without our written consent, which we do not unreasonably withhold. Giving first aid, or taking reasonable emergency measures to protect people or property, is not an admission.

Reasonable Precautions

The insured must take reasonable precautions to prevent loss, damage, breakdown and injury, including:

  • maintaining insured plant as its manufacturer's maintenance schedule requires, and keeping maintenance records;
  • using operators who hold any licence the law requires, or whose competence can be verified;
  • complying with high risk work licensing and work health and safety laws; and
  • securing plant when it is left unattended, by immobilising it, removing its keys, or keeping it in a locked compound where reasonably practicable.

Telling Us About Changes

The named insured must tell us within 30 days if, during the policy period, it starts to hire plant out on dry hire, starts operations underground, in tunnels, on or over water, or in demolition, or starts operating outside the geographical limits. We may change the premium or terms for the rest of the policy period, as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

Recovery Rights and Subrogation

After we pay a claim, we may take over the insured's rights to recover from anyone else, in the insured's name, and the insured must cooperate with us. We will not seek to recover from:

  • a hirer, principal or other party that the insured was required, by a written contract made before the loss, to hold harmless or release, if the contract or damage waiver was disclosed to us or is usual for the insured's business; or
  • an employee of the insured, unless the loss arose from the employee's serious or wilful misconduct.

General Conditions

These conditions apply to every section.

Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth)

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies the operation of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Where this policy is inconsistent with the Act, the Act prevails.

If this policy would otherwise let us refuse to pay a claim, in whole or in part, because of something an insured did or did not do after this policy was entered into, section 54 of the Act applies. We may not refuse to pay the claim only because of that act or omission. Our liability is reduced by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced. Where the act or omission could reasonably be regarded as capable of causing or contributing to the loss, we may refuse to pay only the part of the claim that the act or omission caused or contributed to. We may not refuse any part that the insured proves the act or omission did not cause. This applies to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation in this policy, not only to the claims conditions.

Inspection

We may inspect insured plant, and the named insured's maintenance and hire records, at any reasonable time, after giving reasonable notice.

Cancellation

The named insured may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. If we have paid a total loss of an item, no refund applies to the premium for that item.

We may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving the named insured written notice as that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis.

Cancellation does not affect a claim for loss, damage, breakdown or an occurrence that happened before it took effect.

Financier Interests

Where the schedule shows a financier for an item, we pay the financier first, to the extent of its interest, and that payment discharges us to that extent. A financier's interest is not affected by an act or omission of the named insured that the financier did not know about, if the financier tells us as soon as it becomes aware of it and pays any additional premium we reasonably require.

Fraud

If a claim is made fraudulently, we may refuse to pay it, as s.56 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows. A fraudulent claim by one insured does not affect the cover of any other insured who was not a party to the fraud.

Sanctions

We do not provide cover, pay any claim or provide any benefit under this policy to the extent that doing so would expose us to any sanction, prohibition or restriction under:

  • the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011 (Cth), the Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 (Cth), or regulations made under either Act; or
  • the trade or economic sanctions laws of the United Kingdom, the United States of America or the European Union, to the extent they apply to us.

Privacy

We collect, use and disclose personal information as the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) allows, and as our privacy policy describes. Our privacy policy is available from us on request.

Disputes and Complaints

If you have a complaint, contact us first. We will acknowledge it within one business day and give you our response within 30 calendar days. If you are not satisfied with our response, or we do not respond within that time, you may refer the complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), if it falls within AFCA's rules: www.afca.org.au, 1800 931 678, info@afca.org.au.

Governing Law

This policy is governed by the law of the Australian state or territory shown in the schedule. If none is shown, it is governed by the law of the state or territory of the named insured's address shown in the schedule at the start of the policy period.

Jurisdiction

Each insured and we submit to the jurisdiction of the courts of the state or territory whose law governs this policy, and of any court that may hear appeals from them.

Headings and References

Headings are for convenience only. A reference to legislation includes any legislation that amends or replaces it. A reference to a clause by its heading is a reference to that clause as it appears in this wording.

How to Make a Claim

  1. Make the plant and the site safe, and take reasonable steps to prevent further loss or damage.
  2. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
  3. Tell the police about any theft or malicious damage, and keep the report number.
  4. Tell us what happened and when, and give us photographs, repair quotes, invoices, maintenance records and any hire agreement that applies.
  5. For a third-party claim, send us the demand or court documents as soon as they are received, and do not admit liability (see the Admissions obligation).
  6. We will acknowledge the claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision as the General Insurance Code of Practice requires where the insurer subscribes to it.

E02 Cranes

This endorsement applies to every crane that is insured plant, and to any item of plant used to lift a suspended load.

Lifting conditions. Each lift must be:

  • made by an operator who holds the high risk work licence that the law requires for that crane;
  • planned by a competent person using the crane's load chart for the radius, configuration and ground conditions; and
  • made with outriggers or stabilisers deployed on ground or mats assessed as able to bear the load, and within the wind limits set by the crane's manufacturer.

A dual or tandem lift, in which more than one item of plant shares a load, must also follow a written lift plan approved by a competent person before the lift.

The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition governs our response where a lift did not meet these conditions.

Overturning. Accidental loss or damage to a crane caused by its overturning, or by the collapse of its boom or jib, is covered under Damage to Insured Plant, and the crane excess shown in the schedule applies to it.

Goods lifted. The limit in the Goods Lifted extension is increased to the amount shown in the schedule for this endorsement.

Schedule variables: cranes covered, with their rated capacity; crane excess (default twice the item's excess); goods lifted limit (default $1,000,000 for any one occurrence).

E12 Remote and Autonomous Plant

This endorsement applies to the items of specified plant named in the schedule for it that are operated by remote control, or by a guidance or autonomy system.

Technology failure. The Mechanical and Electrical Breakdown exclusion and the Cyber Events exclusion do not apply to that loss or damage, except where it is caused by a deliberate and malicious cyber event.

Operating conditions. A named item must run only software and firmware approved by its manufacturer, within a geofenced work area where the system provides one, and under the supervision of a person able to stop it.

We do not pay for the cost of repairing or replacing the remote control, guidance or autonomy system itself, unless it is damaged by accidental loss or damage covered by Damage to Insured Plant.
