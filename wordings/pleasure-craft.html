Pleasure Craft Insurance

Cover for a boat or personal watercraft owned by individuals for private recreation in Australia: accidental damage to the boat, its motors and trailer, and legal liability from using it.

About This Policy

This policy insures a boat or personal watercraft owned by individuals and used for private recreation, and the legal liability of the people who use it with the owner's permission.

SectionWhat it coversBasis
Boat CoverAccidental damage to the boat, its motors, equipment, tender and trailer, and the personal effects on boardDamage during the policy period
Legal LiabilityLegal liability for personal injury and property damage arising from owning or using the boat, and the cost of removing its wreckOccurrence during the policy period

The extensions are part of the section they appear in. Racing beyond club racing, lay-up periods, water-skier liability, named cyclone terms, older boats, extended navigation and liveaboard use are dealt with by endorsement.

This policy is designed for a pleasure craft as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) describes one: a boat used only for recreation or sport, not for reward, and owned by one or more individuals. It is not designed for a boat owned by a company, trust or partnership, or used for hire, charter or any business.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in the Definitions section.

Important Information

Who issues this policy. The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

Who this policy is for. This wording is designed for retail clients. An issuer using it must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available, as the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) requires. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.

Which law applies. Because the boat is a pleasure craft owned by individuals, the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) applies to this policy and the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) does not (s.9A of the Insurance Contracts Act). [WP REVIEW: confirm the s.9A treatment, and how a boat owned jointly by an individual and a company, or by a self-managed superannuation fund trustee, should be handled; the platform refers both.]

Your duty when you apply. This policy is a consumer insurance contract under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). You have a duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated. Answer every question in the proposal honestly and completely. If you do not take reasonable care, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

Terms you should know about. Pleasure craft insurance has no standard cover under the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017. We draw your attention to these terms, which limit or reduce the cover and which you may not expect:

  • the boat is covered only within the navigation limits shown in the schedule, and cover is suspended once the boat clears Australian customs for an overseas destination (see the Outside the Navigation Limits exclusion);
  • racing is covered only as club racing within 50 nautical miles of the start, unless the E01 Racing endorsement applies, and racing damage to sails, spars and rigging is limited (see the Racing and Speed exclusion);
  • we do not pay for loss, damage or liability while the boat is operated faster than the maximum speed shown in the schedule (see the Racing and Speed exclusion);
  • a higher excess applies to theft of a personal watercraft or of a boat stored ashore, and a further amount applies if it was not secured, to a trailerable boat that sinks after being left afloat unattended, and to a claim while an operator under 25 was in control (see the Excesses provision);
  • we do not pay for damage by a named cyclone to a boat on a swing mooring in northern waters during the cyclone season, unless the E05 Swing Mooring in Northern Waters endorsement applies, and a named cyclone excess applies in northern waters under the E04 Named Cyclone Excess endorsement;
  • we do not pay for damage caused by the failure of a swing mooring that was not inspected in the 12 months before the damage, or by the failure of standing rigging more than 10 years old (see the Moorings and Standing Rigging exclusion);
  • we do not pay for wear and tear, osmosis, corrosion, marine growth or mechanical breakdown, or for damage to a motor from water entering it or from overheating, except as the Wear, Deterioration and Breakdown exclusion and the Damage to Motors exclusion allow;
  • we do not pay for loss, damage or liability while the boat is operated by a person who is not licensed as the law requires or is affected by alcohol or drugs, or while it is used for hire or reward (see the Licensing, Alcohol and Drugs exclusion and the Hire, Charter and Business Use exclusion);
  • on a market value basis we pay the market value of the boat on a total loss, which may be less than the sum insured (see the How We Settle Boat Claims provision);
  • personal effects are covered only up to the limits in the Personal Effects extension.

Cooling-off period. You may cancel this policy within 21 days of the date it starts, and within 21 days of each renewal, by telling us in writing. If you have not made a claim, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover. After that, you may still cancel under the Cancellation condition.

Commission and fees. The schedule or the quote shows any commission or fee paid to your broker in connection with this policy.

Financial Claims Scheme. If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.

Schedule

(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

  • Your name, and the name of anyone else insured
  • The boat: its type, make and model, year built, length, hull material, motors, registration number and hull identification number
  • Where the boat is normally kept, and how (for example on its trailer at home, in a marina berth or on a swing mooring), and the state or territory in which it is kept
  • The policy period, with its start and end dates
  • The sum insured, and whether the boat is insured for its market value or an agreed value
  • The navigation limits, and the maximum speed for the boat
  • The basic excess, the theft excess and the other excesses that apply
  • The limit of liability
  • The limits of the extensions, where they differ from the defaults stated in them
  • Any interested party, such as a financier
  • The premium, including stamp duty, any emergency services levy, and GST
  • Any commission or fee paid to your broker
  • The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see the Governing Law condition)
  • The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables

Definitions

In this policy, the words below have these meanings wherever they appear, whatever their capitalisation. The singular includes the plural.

Agreed Value

The amount shown in the schedule as the agreed value of the boat. It applies only where the schedule shows that the boat is insured for an agreed value.

Boat

The vessel described in the schedule, including its hull, motors, fixtures and fittings, equipment, tender and trailer. Where the schedule describes a personal watercraft, the boat is that personal watercraft.

Cyclone Season

The period from 1 November in one year to 30 April in the next.

Damage

Physical loss of, or physical damage to, property, including loss by theft.

Endorsement

A change to this policy listed in the Endorsement Library and shown by its code in the schedule.

Equipment

Property that belongs to the boat and is normally kept with it for its use and navigation, such as sails, covers, canopies, anchors, safety equipment, and fixed navigation, communication and fish-finding electronics. Equipment does not include personal effects.

Excess

The amount you pay towards a claim, as the Excesses provision sets out.

Household

You, and the members of your family and other people who normally live with you.

Marina Berth

A pen, berth, pile mooring or dry-stack rack at a marina or boating facility that is managed by its operator and has controlled access.

Market Value

The amount it would cost to buy a boat of the same make, model, age and condition in Australia immediately before the damage happened.

Named Cyclone

A tropical cyclone that the Bureau of Meteorology has named, from the time the Bureau first issues a warning for it until the time it cancels the last warning.

Navigation Limits

The waters in which the boat is covered, as shown in the schedule. If the schedule does not show them, they are: for a personal watercraft, the inland waters of Australia and the Australian coastal waters in which the law of the state or territory allows it to be operated; for any other boat, the inland waters of Australia and waters within 250 nautical miles of the coast of the Australian mainland and Tasmania.

Northern Waters

Any place, on water or on land, north of latitude 26 degrees south.

Occurrence

An event, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same conditions, that results in personal injury or property damage that the person legally liable did not expect or intend.

Operator

The person in control of the boat at the time.

Personal Effects

Clothing, personal belongings, fishing and diving gear, water-sport equipment and portable electronic devices belonging to a member of your household, while on board the boat or being carried to or from it. Personal effects do not include money, documents, jewellery or watches.

Personal Injury

Bodily injury, death, illness or disease, and any resulting shock, mental injury or disability.

Personal Watercraft

A powered vessel designed to be operated by a person standing, sitting astride or kneeling on it, such as a jet ski.

Policy Period

The period shown in the schedule.

Property Damage

Physical damage to, or destruction of, tangible property belonging to someone other than a person insured under the Legal Liability section, including the loss of use of that property.

Racing

Taking part in a race, regatta, time trial or speed trial, from the preparatory signal until the boat finishes or retires.

Sum Insured

The amount shown in the schedule as the sum insured for the boat, including its motors, equipment, tender and trailer.

Swing Mooring

A mooring buoy attached to a block, chain or anchor on the seabed or riverbed, other than a marina berth.

Tender

A dinghy or other small boat, with its motor, carried on or towed by the boat and used to get to and from it.

Trailer

The road trailer used to carry the boat.

We, Us, Our

The insurer named in the schedule.

You, Your

The person or people named in the schedule as the insured. Each of them is an insured.

Boat Cover

This section applies to every policy. The schedule shows the sum insured, whether the boat is insured for its market value or an agreed value, and the excesses.

Damage to the Boat

If the boat suffers accidental damage during the policy period, we will pay to repair it, replace it or pay its value, as set out in the How We Settle Boat Claims provision.

The boat is covered:

  • while it is afloat within the navigation limits;
  • while it is out of the water anywhere in Australia, including while it is stored, being repaired, or being lifted, launched or retrieved; and
  • while it is being carried on its trailer, or by a carrier, by road anywhere in Australia.

The most we will pay for any one event is the sum insured, plus the amounts the extensions to this section say are payable in addition.

How We Settle Boat Claims

Repair. For damage that can be repaired, we pay the reasonable cost of repairing the boat to the condition it was in immediately before the damage, using materials and methods that are reasonably available. We may arrange the repair ourselves, choose the repairer, or pay you the reasonable cost of the repair. If you want a repairer we did not choose, we pay what our quote would have cost.

Sails and canvas. For sails, covers, canopies, cushions and upholstery more than three years old, we deduct an amount for wear from the cost of replacement, at the rate shown in the schedule (default 10% for each full year of age beyond three years, and no more than 50% in all).

Total loss. If the boat is stolen and not recovered within 14 days of you telling the police, or if it would cost more to repair it than we would pay for a total loss, we treat it as a total loss and pay:

  • where the schedule shows an agreed value, the agreed value;
  • otherwise, the market value of the boat, up to the sum insured.

After we pay a total loss, the boat becomes our property, and this section ends for it.

New for old. If the boat is a total loss within two years after you bought it new from a dealer, and you were its first registered owner, we pay the cost of a new boat of the same make and model, or the nearest equivalent, up to the sum insured, even where the schedule shows the market value basis.

Salvage. In addition to the sum insured, we pay the reasonable cost of salvaging the boat after damage covered by this section, and any salvage award you must pay, up to the percentage of the sum insured shown in the schedule (default 25%).

No under-insurance penalty. We do not reduce a claim because the sum insured is less than the value of the boat. The most we pay for a total loss is the amount set out above.

Personal Effects

We cover accidental damage to personal effects while they are on board the boat or being carried to or from it, up to the limits shown in the schedule (default $1,500 for any one item and $10,000 for all personal effects in any one event). This amount is in addition to the sum insured.

We do not pay under this extension for personal effects left on board while the boat is unattended, unless they are in a locked cabin or compartment, or for portable electronic devices damaged by being dropped.

Emergency Expenses

If the boat cannot be used safely because of damage covered by this section that happens more than 50 kilometres from where it is normally kept, we pay the reasonable cost of emergency repairs to make it safe, of moving it to the nearest place of repair or safe haven, and of accommodation and travel home for the people on board, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $5,000) for any one event. This amount is in addition to the sum insured.

Recovery and Road Transport

After damage covered by this section, we pay the reasonable cost of recovering the boat from the water and transporting it by road to the nearest repairer we agree to, and of returning it after repair, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $2,500) for any one event. This amount is in addition to the sum insured.

Replacement Trailer and Tender

If the trailer or tender is stolen or destroyed and is not recovered, and you need to use the boat before we have settled the claim, we pay the reasonable cost of hiring a replacement trailer or tender for up to 14 days, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $500) for any one event. This amount is in addition to the sum insured.

Personal Accident Benefit

If a person dies within 12 months of an injury caused solely by an accident during the policy period while they are on board the boat, boarding or leaving it, or being towed by it under the E03 Water-Skier and Tow-Sport Liability endorsement, we pay the death benefit shown in the schedule (default $25,000 for each person, and $100,000 in all for any one accident) to that person's estate.

We do not pay this benefit for death caused by illness or disease, by an injury the person caused deliberately to themselves, or while the person was affected by alcohol above the legal limit or by a drug not taken as prescribed. This benefit is not paid for an operator who is not licensed as the law requires.

What Boat Cover Does Not Include

These exclusions apply to the Boat Cover section.

Wear, Deterioration and Breakdown

We do not pay for:

  • wear and tear, gradual deterioration, corrosion, electrolysis, osmosis, delamination, rot, mould or marine growth;
  • mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdown or failure;
  • the cost of fixing a defect in the design, construction, workmanship or materials of the boat;
  • damage caused by insects, vermin or birds, by their nesting, or by their droppings.

We do pay for other damage to the boat that results suddenly and accidentally from one of these causes, for example a hull that is holed when a corroded fitting fails. We do not pay to fix the part that wore out, failed or was defective.

Damage to Motors

We do not pay for damage to a motor caused by water entering it through its air intake or exhaust, by a lack of oil, lubrication or coolant, or by overheating, unless the damage results from the boat sinking, capsizing, colliding with something, running aground, catching fire, or being stolen.

We do not pay for the loss of an outboard motor that falls into the water because it was not fastened to the boat in the way its manufacturer specifies.

Unseaworthiness and Maintenance

We do not pay for damage caused by the boat being unseaworthy when it was taken out, or by a failure to maintain the boat in a reasonable state of repair, where you knew, or could reasonably have been expected to know, of the condition that caused the damage. The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies.

Moorings and Standing Rigging

We do not pay for:

  • damage caused by the failure of a swing mooring that had not been inspected by a qualified mooring contractor in the 12 months before the damage;
  • dismasting, or damage to the masts, spars, sails or rigging, caused by the failure of standing rigging that was more than 10 years old at the time, unless the E07 Standing Rigging endorsement applies;
  • damage by a named cyclone to the boat while it is on a swing mooring in northern waters during the cyclone season, unless the E05 Swing Mooring in Northern Waters endorsement applies.

Other Boat Exclusions

Under the Boat Cover section, we do not pay for:

  • theft of the boat, or damage caused deliberately to it, by a member of your household or by a person using the boat with your permission;
  • loss of use, loss of value, or any other loss that is not damage to the boat;
  • damage to property while it is being worked on, cleaned, repaired or restored, if the damage results from the work;
  • damage to sails, spars or rigging while racing, unless it is caused by the boat sinking, running aground, catching fire or colliding with something;
  • damage to the boat while it is being used as a permanent or temporary residence, unless the E09 Liveaboard Use endorsement applies.

The Joint Insureds condition applies to the first exclusion in this list.

Legal Liability

This section applies to every policy. The schedule shows the limit of liability.

Liability Arising From the Boat

We will pay the amount you, or a person operating the boat with your permission, become legally liable to pay as compensation for personal injury or property damage that happens during the policy period in Australia, caused by an occurrence arising from the ownership or use of the boat within the navigation limits.

Limit. The most we pay for all compensation arising from one occurrence is the limit of liability shown in the schedule (default $10,000,000). All personal injury and property damage arising from one source or original cause is one occurrence.

Pollution. For liability arising from the sudden and accidental escape of fuel, oil or another pollutant from the boat, the most we pay for one occurrence is the pollution limit shown in the schedule (default $500,000). This amount is part of, not in addition to, the limit of liability.

Defence costs. In addition to the limit, we pay the reasonable legal costs we incur or approve in defending or settling a claim covered by this section, and interest on a judgment up to the date we pay or offer to pay it.

Settlement. We may take over and conduct the defence or settlement of any claim in the name of the person insured. We may at any time pay the limit, less any amount already paid, and then we have no further obligation for that occurrence except for defence costs already incurred.

Wreck Removal

If the boat sinks or is wrecked because of damage covered by the Boat Cover section, we pay the cost you are legally required to pay to raise, remove, light, mark or destroy the wreck. This cost is part of, not in addition to, the limit of liability.

Injury to People Insured and Workers

We do not pay for liability for personal injury to you, to a member of your household, or to a person operating the boat, or for personal injury to a person employed by you or a member of your household that arises from that employment.

Property in Your Care

We do not pay for liability for damage to property owned by, or in the care or control of, you, a member of your household, or the operator of the boat, other than damage to a marina berth, jetty or mooring you are using.

Towing and Road Use

We do not pay for liability:

  • for personal injury to a person being towed by the boat on water skis, a wakeboard, a kneeboard, an inflatable or any similar thing, or caused by that person, unless the E03 Water-Skier and Tow-Sport Liability endorsement applies;
  • arising from towing a person or object in the air, such as a parasail or kite;
  • arising from the boat or the trailer while it is attached to, or being towed by, a motor vehicle on land.

Contractual Liability

We do not pay for liability that arises only because of a contract or agreement, unless the person insured would have been liable without it.

Gradual Pollution

We do not pay for liability arising from the discharge, dispersal or escape of pollutants, unless it is sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected, and happens at a specific time and place during the policy period.

Fines and Penalties

We do not pay fines, penalties, or punitive, exemplary or aggravated damages.

How Claims Are Paid

This applies to every section.

Excesses

Basic excess. You pay the basic excess shown in the schedule for each claim under the Boat Cover section. If one event gives rise to more than one claim under that section, only one basic excess is deducted. No basic excess applies to damage to the boat while it is in its marina berth, other than damage by a named cyclone.

Theft excess. For theft of a personal watercraft, or of the boat while it is out of the water, the theft excess shown in the schedule (default $1,000) applies in place of the basic excess. Where the stolen property was not secured, a further amount equal to the theft excess applies. Secured means kept in a locked building, in a marina berth, or with a wheel clamp, hitch lock, personal watercraft lock or other anti-theft device fitted and locked.

Unattended afloat. For damage to a boat that is normally kept on its trailer and that sinks, or is damaged by weather, after being left afloat and unattended for more than 24 hours, an additional excess applies, as shown in the schedule (default $2,000).

Young operator. Where the operator was under 25 when the damage happened, an additional excess applies, as shown in the schedule (default $500).

Other excesses. Any excess an endorsement shows applies as that endorsement states. No excess applies to the Legal Liability section.

GST and Claims

Where you are entitled to an input tax credit for the GST on the cost of a repair or replacement, we pay the claim net of that credit. The sum insured and each limit in this policy include GST to the extent you are not entitled to an input tax credit for it.

Other Insurance

If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows.

Who We Pay

We pay you, or as you direct. Where an interested party is shown in the schedule, we pay as the schedule requires. We may pay a repairer, salvor or supplier directly.

General Exclusions

These exclusions apply to every section.

Outside the Navigation Limits

We do not pay for loss, damage or liability arising while the boat is afloat outside the navigation limits, unless the E08 Extended Navigation Limits endorsement applies. Cover under this policy is suspended from the time the boat clears Australian customs for an overseas destination until it returns to Australian waters.

Racing and Speed

We do not pay for loss, damage or liability arising while:

  • the boat is racing, other than club racing by a sailing boat in a race organised by a yacht club or sailing association within 50 nautical miles of the start, unless the E01 Racing endorsement applies;
  • a powered boat or personal watercraft is racing, or taking part in a speed trial or a record attempt;
  • the boat is being operated faster than the maximum speed shown in the schedule (default 50 knots, or 65 knots for a personal watercraft).

Licensing, Alcohol and Drugs

We do not pay for loss, damage or liability arising while the boat is being operated, with your permission, by a person:

  • who does not hold the licence the law of the place requires for that boat, unless the law allows them to operate it under supervision and they are supervised as the law requires; or
  • whose blood alcohol concentration is above the limit the law of the place sets for the operator of a vessel, or who is under the influence of a drug that is not taken as prescribed.

This exclusion does not apply where the boat was stolen or used without your permission. The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies.

Hire, Charter and Business Use

We do not pay for loss, damage or liability arising while the boat is used for hire, charter or reward, to carry passengers for payment, or for any business purpose. Sharing the running costs of a trip with friends is not use for reward.

Deliberate Acts

We do not pay for any loss, damage or liability caused deliberately by you, by a member of your household, or by a person acting with your authority. The Joint Insureds condition applies.

Confiscation and Seizure

We do not pay for loss of or damage to the boat caused by its confiscation, seizure, detention or destruction by order of a government or public authority, other than to prevent the spread of fire or pollution.

War

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities or warlike operations (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, or military or usurped power.

Terrorism

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from an act of terrorism, meaning an act, including the use of force or violence or the threat of it, by any person or group, committed for political, religious, ideological or similar purposes, including the intention to influence a government or to put the public in fear.

Nuclear

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from ionising radiation, contamination by radioactivity from nuclear fuel or waste, or nuclear weapons material.

Electronic Data

We do not pay for loss of, damage to or corruption of electronic data, or for loss arising from unauthorised use of a computer system, other than physical damage to the boat by a resulting fire or explosion. Unauthorised use means use of a computer system by a person not permitted by its owner to use it.

Your Obligations

These apply to you and to anyone claiming under this policy.

Telling Us About a Claim

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after damage or an occurrence that may lead to a claim. Tell the police promptly about any theft or malicious damage, and report an accident to the maritime authority of the state or territory where the law requires it.

Give us the information and documents we reasonably ask for to assess the claim, including proof of ownership or value, and allow us to inspect the boat before it is repaired, unless emergency repairs are needed under the Emergency Expenses extension.

Care of the Boat

Take reasonable steps to keep the boat in a seaworthy condition and a reasonable state of repair, to secure it and its motors and equipment against theft, and to prevent further damage after an event. Keep damaged property and evidence until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

If a named cyclone warning is issued for the place where the boat is kept, take the steps a reasonable owner would take to protect it, where it is safe to do so.

Admissions and Co-operation

Do not admit liability for, or agree to settle, a claim under the Legal Liability section without our written agreement, which we will not unreasonably withhold. Co-operate with us in the defence of the claim.

Telling Us About Changes

Tell us during the policy period, as soon as reasonably practicable, if:

  • you sell the boat, or someone other than you becomes its owner or part-owner;
  • you change where or how the boat is kept, including moving it to a swing mooring, or into northern waters during the cyclone season;
  • the boat or its motors are modified, or replaced, in a way that increases its speed or power;
  • the boat will be used for hire, charter, reward or any business purpose, or lived on;
  • a person under 25 will operate the boat regularly.

We will tell you whether the change affects your cover or premium. The Insurance Contracts Act condition applies.

Recovery From Others

After we pay a claim, we may take over your rights to recover the amount from anyone else, and act in your name. Help us do so, at our cost. We will not exercise a right of recovery against a member of your household, or a person operating the boat with your permission, unless they caused the loss deliberately.

General Conditions

Insurance Contracts Act

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies the operation of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Where this policy is inconsistent with the Act, the Act prevails.

If this policy would otherwise let us refuse to pay a claim, in whole or in part, because of something you did or did not do after this policy was entered into, s.54 of the Act applies. We may not refuse to pay the claim only because of that act or omission. Our liability is reduced by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced. Where the act or omission could reasonably be regarded as capable of causing or contributing to the loss, we may refuse to pay only the part of the claim that the act or omission caused or contributed to. We may not refuse any part that you prove the act or omission did not cause. This applies to every condition, exclusion, excess, limit and obligation in this policy, not only to the claims conditions.

Joint Insureds

Where more than one person is named in the schedule as the insured, this policy applies to each of them separately.

A fraud, deliberate act or failure to comply with this policy by one of them, or by a member of your household who is not named as an insured, does not affect the cover of any named insured who did not know of it, did not take part in it and did not consent to it.

Sale of the Boat

Cover for the boat ends when you sell it or otherwise transfer your interest in it, unless we agree in writing to continue it for the new owner. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges.

Cancellation

You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. If you cancel within the cooling-off period in the Important Information section, we refund the premium as that section states. Otherwise we refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. If we have paid a total loss of the boat, no refund applies.

We may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you the written notice that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis. [WP REVIEW: confirm the notice an insurer must give to cancel a pleasure craft contract under s.59 and s.60 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).]

Cancellation does not affect a claim for damage, or an occurrence, that happened before cancellation.

Renewal

Each renewal is a new contract. Before this policy ends, we will tell you whether we offer to renew it and on what terms, as s.58 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) requires. We are not obliged to offer renewal.

Fraudulent Claims

If a claim is made fraudulently, we may refuse to pay it, as s.56 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

Sanctions

We do not provide cover, pay any claim or provide any benefit under this policy to the extent that doing so would expose us to any sanction, prohibition or restriction under:

  • the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011 (Cth), the Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 (Cth), or regulations made under either Act; or
  • the trade or economic sanctions laws of the United Kingdom, the United States of America or the European Union, to the extent they apply to us.

We collect, use and disclose personal information about you, your household and the people who operate the boat to assess the proposal, administer this policy and manage claims, in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and our privacy policy. Disputes and Complaints

If you are unhappy with our service or a decision, contact us at the details in the schedule. We will acknowledge the complaint within one business day and give our decision within 30 calendar days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, or we have not responded within 30 calendar days, you may take the complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), a free and independent dispute resolution service: www.afca.org.au, 1800 931 678, info@afca.org.au.

Governing Law

This policy is governed by the law of the Australian state or territory shown in the schedule at the start of the policy period as the place where the boat is normally kept.

Jurisdiction

You and we submit to the jurisdiction of the courts of the state or territory whose law governs this policy, and of any court that may hear appeals from them.

Headings and References

Headings are for convenience only. A reference to legislation includes any legislation that amends or replaces it. A reference to a clause by its heading is a reference to that clause as it appears in this wording.

How to Make a Claim

  1. Make sure everyone is safe, then take reasonable steps to protect the boat from further damage (see the Emergency Expenses extension).
  2. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
  3. Tell the police about any theft or malicious damage, and get the report number. Report an accident to the maritime authority where the law requires it.
  4. Tell us what happened and when, and give us photographs, receipts, quotes and invoices, and the names of any other vessels and people involved.
  5. Do not admit liability to anyone, or agree to pay them.
  6. We will acknowledge your claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision as the General Insurance Code of Practice requires where the insurer subscribes to it.
