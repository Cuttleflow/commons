# Contributing to the Cuttleflow Commons

Thank you for helping improve these wordings. There are two ways to take part.

## 1. Comment on a wording
Each wording page has a comment thread at the bottom, backed by GitHub Discussions.
Sign in with a **GitHub account** and leave your comment. Use comments to raise a
question, note something unclear, or discuss a clause. Please say which wording and
clause you mean (e.g. "Cyber 4.1").

If you would rather not create a GitHub account, use the **form** — *Raise a point or
propose a change* — which is the same route as below. Submitting still needs an
account; if that is a problem, email your point to the maintainers and we will file it
for you, quoting you.

## 2. Propose a change to the wording text
To propose specific new wording:
- **Raise an issue** using *Suggest a change* → "Propose a change", describing the
  clause and the change; or
- **Open a pull request** editing the wording source directly, so reviewers see the
  exact edit as a line-by-line comparison.

This route uses GitHub and needs a free GitHub account.

## Contributor terms — public-domain dedication
So the library stays open and free of new third-party rights, **by proposing a change
you agree that your contribution is dedicated to the public domain** (you do not assert
copyright over it) and that Cuttleflow may publish it under the library's CC BY 4.0
licence. Do not submit text you do not have the right to contribute — in particular,
do not paste text from another insurer's or market body's copyrighted wording.

## How changes are accepted
Comments and proposals do not change a published wording on their own. A maintainer
(and, where relevant, the Wording Partner) reviews each proposal. If accepted, it is
merged and released as a **new tagged version**, with the history preserved. Every
wording stays *DRAFT — SUBJECT TO LEGAL REVIEW* until Wording Partner sign-off.

## Please be constructive
Keep comments about the wording, not the people. Off-topic, promotional, or abusive
comments may be removed.
