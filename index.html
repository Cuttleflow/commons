Australian policy wordings, in the open.

The library holds 63 policy wordings, from professional indemnity, D&O and cyber to liability, property, motor, accident, travel and home. Each is drafted from first principles against the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), and every one is a working draft, subject to legal review.

Read any wording, adapt it under CC BY 4.0, and suggest changes in the open. Wordings for 14 professions, from accountants to veterinary practices, are built on the core professional indemnity and financial services forms. An agency can launch any wording here as a product on Cuttleflow Go.

Browse the wordings Why we publish these

A free GitHub account lets you comment on any wording, or edit the text yourself and send us the change — see how contributions work. No account? Send your point through our contact page and we will file it for you.

How a change happens

01

Say something

Comment on the wording page, or raise an issue in the repository. Both are public and both are read.

02

Or edit the words yourself

With a free GitHub account you can open the wording, change the text directly in your browser, and send the change to us. GitHub shows exactly which words you added or removed.

03

Review, then release

Nothing published changes on its own. A change is merged after review and Wording Partner sign-off, then tagged as a new version.
