How a change happens

01

Say something

Comment on the wording page, or raise an issue in the repository. Both are public and both are read.

02

Or edit the words yourself

With a free GitHub account you can open the wording, change the text directly in your browser, and send the change to us. GitHub shows exactly which words you added or removed.

03

Review, then release

Nothing published changes on its own. A change is merged after review and Wording Partner sign-off, then tagged as a new version.
