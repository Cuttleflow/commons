We pay the reasonable cost of repairing the item, or of replacing the damaged parts with parts of equivalent specification, up to the sum insured of the item, less:

- an allowance for wear parts, and for parts with a limited working life, for the wear they had already had; and
- the excess for [[ref:peq.section.breakdown|heading]] shown in the schedule.

We pay for loss of oils, coolants and refrigerants only where it results from a breakdown we pay for.
