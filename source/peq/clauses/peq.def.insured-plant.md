heading: Insured Plant
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Each item of specified plant; unspecified plant, within the unspecified plant sum insured; and newly acquired plant under [[ref:peq.ext.newly-acquired-plant]]. Insured plant includes the attachments, accessories, GPS and telematics equipment and signwriting on an item, and their value is part of its sum insured.
