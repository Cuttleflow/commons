**Market value.** Unless the schedule shows agreed value for an item, we will repair the item or pay the reasonable cost of repairing it, up to its market value immediately before the loss. For a total loss, we pay the market value of the item, up to its sum insured. We make no deduction for depreciation on parts used in a repair, except for wear parts, which we settle less an allowance for the wear they had already had.

**Agreed value.** Where the schedule shows agreed value for an item, we will repair it or pay the reasonable cost of repairing it, up to the agreed value. For a total loss, we pay the agreed value.

**New plant replacement.** If an item of specified plant is a total loss within 12 months of its first registration or first use as new, we will replace it with a new item of the same make, model and specification, or the nearest equivalent, up to 120% of its sum insured.

**Salvage and excess.** For a total loss, we deduct the excess, and [[ref:peq.pay.salvage]] applies.

**No average.** If the sum insured of an item is less than its value, we do not reduce the amount we pay for a partial loss in proportion.
