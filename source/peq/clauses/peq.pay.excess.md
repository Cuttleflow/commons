For each claim, the named insured bears:

- under [[ref:peq.section.material-damage|heading]], the excess shown in the schedule for each item that suffers loss or damage, for each event;
- under [[ref:peq.section.hired-in|heading]], the excess for that section shown in the schedule, for each event;
- under [[ref:peq.section.breakdown|heading]], the excess for that section shown in the schedule, for each breakdown; and
- under [[ref:peq.section.liability|heading]], the excess for that section shown in the schedule, for each occurrence. Defence costs are not subject to the excess.

The schedule or an endorsement may show additional excesses. Where more than one excess applies to the same item for the same event, only the highest is deducted.
