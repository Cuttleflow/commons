---
id: peq.def.unspecified-plant
type: definition
heading: Unspecified Plant
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Mobile plant and equipment, and attachments, owned by the named insured or for which it is responsible as lessee, that is not specified plant, up to the most we pay for any one item shown in the schedule (default $25,000 for any one item).
