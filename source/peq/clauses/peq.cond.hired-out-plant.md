The schedule shows whether each item of specified plant is hired out, and on what basis.

**Wet hire.** While an item is on wet hire, [[ref:peq.section.material-damage|heading]] continues to apply to it without change.

**Dry hire.** While an item is on dry hire, [[ref:peq.section.material-damage|heading]] continues to apply to it if the named insured:

- hires it out under a written hire agreement that makes the hirer responsible for loss of or damage to it while on hire;
- obtains and keeps a record of the hirer's identity, licence and address before the plant leaves its control; and
- does not give up its right to recover from the hirer, except under a damage waiver whose terms the named insured has told us about.

If the named insured does not do these things, [[ref:peq.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to our response. Our right to recover from the hirer is preserved, except to the extent of a damage waiver the named insured has told us about.
