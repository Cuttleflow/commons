The most we pay under [[ref:peq.section.hired-in|heading]] is the limit for any one item, and the limit for any one event, shown in the schedule (defaults $500,000 for any one item and $1,000,000 for any one event). Continuing hire charges are paid within these limits, up to $5,000 a week, or the amount shown in the schedule.

If the named insured hires in an item whose replacement value is more than the limit for any one item, it must tell us before the hire starts. We may agree to increase the limit for that item, for an additional premium.
