---
id: peq.excl.waterborne-airborne
type: exclusion
heading: Waterborne and Airborne Plant
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss or damage to plant while it is waterborne, except while on a licensed vehicle ferry or punt crossing inland waters, or while it is airborne, except while carried as cargo by a licensed carrier. Endorsement E11 changes this exclusion where it applies.
