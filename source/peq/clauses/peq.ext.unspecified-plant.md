heading: Unspecified Plant
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Unspecified plant is insured up to the unspecified plant sum insured shown in the schedule for any one event (default $350,000), and up to the most we pay for any one item shown in the schedule (default $25,000). Unspecified plant is settled on market value. The exclusion headed [[ref:peq.excl.unattended-theft|heading]] applies to it.
