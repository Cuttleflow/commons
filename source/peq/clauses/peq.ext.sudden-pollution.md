heading: Sudden Pollution
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We pay for liability for personal injury or property damage caused by the discharge, dispersal, release or escape of a pollutant that is sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected, and happens at a specific time and place during the policy period. The most we pay for any one occurrence, and in total for the policy period, under this extension is $1,000,000.
