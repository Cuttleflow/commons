heading: Expediting Costs
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We will pay the reasonable extra costs of overtime, night and holiday work and express freight to speed up the repair of insured plant after loss or damage covered by [[ref:peq.cover.material-damage|heading]], up to 50% of the cost of the repair, and no more than $50,000 for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule.
