For [[ref:peq.section.liability|heading]], each of the following is an insured:

- the named insured;
- any person operating insured plant with the named insured's permission; and
- any principal of the named insured, but only for its vicarious liability for the named insured's operation of insured plant.

Each insured is treated as if it had a separate policy, except that the limit is not increased by the number of insureds. An exclusion that applies because of one insured's knowledge or conduct applies only to that insured.
