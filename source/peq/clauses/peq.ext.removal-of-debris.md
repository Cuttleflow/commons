heading: Removal of Debris
since: 1.1.0
status: draft

We will pay the reasonable costs of removing debris of insured plant, and of demolishing and making safe damaged insured plant, after loss or damage covered by [[ref:peq.cover.material-damage|heading]], up to $100,000 or 20% of the amount we pay for that loss or damage, whichever is greater, for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule.
