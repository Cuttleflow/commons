---
id: peq.obl.reasonable-precautions
type: obligation
heading: Reasonable Precautions
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

The insured must take reasonable precautions to prevent loss, damage, breakdown and injury, including:

- maintaining insured plant as its manufacturer's maintenance schedule requires, and keeping maintenance records;
- using operators who hold any licence the law requires, or whose competence can be verified;
- complying with high risk work licensing and work health and safety laws; and
- securing plant when it is left unattended, by immobilising it, removing its keys, or keeping it in a locked compound where reasonably practicable.
