id: peq.excl.liability-road-use
type: exclusion
heading: Road Use as Transport
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Under [[ref:peq.section.liability|heading]], we do not pay for liability arising from the use of insured plant as a registered vehicle driven as transport on a public road. A commercial motor policy is the place to insure that risk. This exclusion does not apply to the use of registered plant as a tool of trade. Endorsement E07 changes this exclusion where it applies.
