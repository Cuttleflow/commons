type: obligation
heading: Telling Us About Claims
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

The insured must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable about any loss, theft, breakdown or occurrence that may give rise to a claim, and send us any third-party claim as soon as it is received. Theft and malicious damage must be reported to the police. The insured must give us the information and help we reasonably ask for, and keep damaged plant and parts available for inspection until we have had a reasonable chance to inspect them.
