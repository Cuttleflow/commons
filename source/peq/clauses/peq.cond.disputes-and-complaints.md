If you have a complaint, contact us first. We will acknowledge it within one business day and give you our response within 30 calendar days. If you are not satisfied with our response, or we do not respond within that time, you may refer the complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), if it falls within AFCA's rules: www.afca.org.au, 1800 931 678, info@afca.org.au.
