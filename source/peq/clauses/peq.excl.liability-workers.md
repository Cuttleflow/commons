heading: Injury to Workers and Injury Covered by CTP
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Under [[ref:peq.section.liability|heading]], we do not pay for:

- personal injury to any person that is covered, or would be covered but for a failure to insure or register, by compulsory third party motor insurance or a statutory motor accident scheme; or
- personal injury to an employee of an insured arising from their employment, or for which workers' compensation insurance, or a workers' compensation scheme, responds or is required by law to respond.
