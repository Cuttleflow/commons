We will pay the amounts an insured becomes legally liable to pay as compensation for:

- personal injury; and
- property damage,

that happens during the policy period within the geographical limits and arises from an occurrence in connection with the ownership, operation or use of insured plant, including its use as a tool of trade.

The most we pay for any one occurrence is the limit shown in the schedule for [[ref:peq.section.liability|heading]] (default $20,000,000). The sub-limits in the extensions to this section are part of that limit.
