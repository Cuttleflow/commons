---
id: peq.ext.lock-and-key
type: extension
heading: Lock and Key Re-Coding
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If the keys, or the electronic key or code, of an item of insured plant are stolen during the policy period, we will pay the reasonable cost of replacing them and of re-coding or replacing the locks and immobiliser, up to $10,000 for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule.
