The most we pay for an item of specified plant under [[ref:peq.section.material-damage|heading]] is its sum insured or agreed value, adjusted as [[ref:peq.pay.basis-of-settlement]] and [[ref:peq.ext.appreciation]] allow, plus the amounts the extensions to that section say are payable in addition. The most we pay for unspecified plant is set by [[ref:peq.ext.unspecified-plant]]. The limits for the other sections are in [[ref:peq.pay.hired-in-limits]], [[ref:peq.pay.breakdown-settlement]] and [[ref:peq.cover.liability]].
