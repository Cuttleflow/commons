---
id: peq.ext.appreciation
type: extension
heading: Appreciation in Value
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If the market value of an item of specified plant has risen since the start of the policy period, we will treat its sum insured as increased by the amount of that rise, up to 15% of its sum insured, or the percentage shown in the schedule. This extension does not apply to an item insured for agreed value.
