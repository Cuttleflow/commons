---
id: peq.excl.cyber
type: exclusion
heading: Cyber Events
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from a cyber event. This exclusion does not apply to physical loss of or damage to insured plant that a cyber event directly causes, or to personal injury or property damage under [[ref:peq.section.liability|heading]] that a cyber event directly causes. Endorsement E12 changes this exclusion where it applies.
