---
id: peq.excl.wear-and-tear
type: exclusion
heading: Wear and Tear
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion, cavitation, erosion or an inherent defect. We do pay for sudden accidental loss or damage to other insured plant, or to other parts of the item, that results from one of these.
