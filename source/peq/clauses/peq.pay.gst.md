Where the named insured is entitled to an input tax credit for the GST on a repair, replacement or other payment, we reduce our payment by the amount of that credit, as A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth) allows. The named insured must tell us its entitlement to input tax credits when it makes a claim.
