heading: Continuing Hire Charges
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

The hire charges the named insured remains legally liable to pay under a hire agreement for hired-in plant while that plant cannot be used because of loss or damage covered by [[ref:peq.cover.hired-in|heading]], until it is repaired, replaced or settled.
