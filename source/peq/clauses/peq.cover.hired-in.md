---
id: peq.cover.hired-in
type: cover
heading: Loss of or Damage to Hired-In Plant
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If hired-in plant suffers accidental loss or damage during the policy period within the geographical limits while in the named insured's care, custody or control, we will pay:

- the amount the named insured is legally liable to pay to the owner for the loss or damage, including liability it has taken on under the hire agreement;
- continuing hire charges, up to the continuing hire charges limit shown in the schedule; and
- the owner's reasonable recovery, salvage and towing costs that the named insured is liable to pay.
