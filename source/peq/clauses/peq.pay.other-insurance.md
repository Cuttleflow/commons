heading: Other Insurance
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy, including a contract works policy covering the same plant at a construction site, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows. Tell us about the other insurance when you claim. This does not apply where the schedule or an endorsement states that this policy is excess of a specified underlying policy, in which case this policy pays only after the limit of that specified policy is exhausted.
