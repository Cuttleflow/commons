heading: Own Property
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Under [[ref:peq.section.liability|heading]], we do not pay for property damage to property owned by, leased to, or in the physical or legal control of an insured, except as [[ref:peq.ext.goods-lifted]] and [[ref:peq.section.hired-in|heading]] provide.
