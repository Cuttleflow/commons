heading: Recovery Costs
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We will pay the reasonable costs of recovering an item of insured plant that is bogged, sunk, overturned or stranded during the policy period, whether or not it is damaged, up to $25,000 for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule. Where the item is damaged by accidental loss or damage covered by [[ref:peq.cover.material-damage|heading]], the recovery costs are part of that claim and this limit does not apply to them.
