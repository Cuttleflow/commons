heading: Contractual Liability
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Under [[ref:peq.section.liability|heading]], we do not pay for liability that an insured takes on under a contract or agreement, to the extent it is greater than the liability the insured would have had without it. This exclusion does not apply to the usual terms of a hire agreement, a site access agreement or a construction contract.
