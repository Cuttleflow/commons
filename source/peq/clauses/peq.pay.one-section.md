heading: One Section for Each Loss
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We pay any one loss, breakdown or liability under one section only. Where [[ref:peq.section.material-damage|heading]] and [[ref:peq.section.breakdown|heading]] could both respond to damage to the same item from the same cause, we pay under [[ref:peq.section.material-damage|heading]], and only one excess applies.
