---
id: peq.ext.hire-costs
type: extension
heading: Hire Costs Following Insured Damage
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If the schedule shows that this extension applies and an item of specified plant cannot be used because of loss or damage covered by [[ref:peq.cover.material-damage|heading]], we will pay the reasonable cost of hiring a substitute item while the item is being repaired or replaced, up to $5,000 a week for up to 26 weeks for each item, or the amounts shown in the schedule. We do not pay for the first 72 hours after the loss or damage.
