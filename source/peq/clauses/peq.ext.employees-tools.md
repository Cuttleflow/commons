---
id: peq.ext.employees-tools
type: extension
heading: Employees' Tools and Effects
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We will pay for accidental loss or damage to the tools and personal effects of the named insured's employees while on or in an item of insured plant, up to $5,000 for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule, where the loss or damage happens at the same time as loss or damage to that item covered by [[ref:peq.cover.material-damage|heading]], or by theft of that item.
