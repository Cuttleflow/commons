id: peq.cover.defence-costs
type: cover
heading: Defence Costs
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We will pay defence costs for a third-party claim covered by [[ref:peq.cover.liability|heading]]. Defence costs are paid in addition to the limit. If the amount needed to settle a third-party claim is more than the limit, we pay the defence costs incurred before we offer or pay the limit, and only in the proportion that the limit bears to the amount of the settlement or judgment.

We may take over the defence and settlement of any third-party claim in the insured's name.
