This policy insures mobile plant and equipment, such as excavators, loaders, cranes, telehandlers, skid steers, generators, compressors and their attachments, whether the plant is owned, financed, hired in or hired out. It has four sections. The schedule shows which apply.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Material Damage | Accidental loss of or damage to insured plant, including theft, transit, storm and flood | Loss or damage during the policy period |
| Hired-In Plant | Your liability for loss of or damage to plant you hire from others, and continuing hire charges | Loss or damage during the policy period |
| Machinery Breakdown | Sudden breakdown of specified plant | Breakdown during the policy period |
| Mobile Plant Liability | Liability to third parties for personal injury and property damage arising from insured plant, including its use as a tool of trade | Occurrence during the policy period |

[[ref:peq.section.material-damage|heading]] applies whenever the schedule shows an item of insured plant. [[ref:peq.section.hired-in|heading]], [[ref:peq.section.breakdown|heading]] and [[ref:peq.section.liability|heading]] apply only if the schedule shows them as included.

**All risks.** [[ref:peq.section.material-damage|heading]] covers accidental loss or damage from any cause that is not excluded. It does not list the events that are covered. Where we rely on an exclusion, we must show that it applies.

**Other policies.** Road risk for registered plant driven as transport on a public road belongs under a commercial motor policy (see [[ref:peq.excl.liability-road-use]]). Plant at a construction site may also be insured under a contract works policy; where both respond, [[ref:peq.pay.other-insurance]] applies.

This policy, together with the schedule, the proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:peq.section.definitions]].
