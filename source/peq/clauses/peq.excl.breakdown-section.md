heading: 'Machinery Breakdown: What Is Not Covered'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Under [[ref:peq.section.breakdown|heading]], we do not pay for:

- gradual deterioration, or a fault that existed at the start of the policy period and that the named insured knew about or could reasonably have been expected to know about;
- breakdown caused by a failure to maintain the item as its manufacturer's maintenance schedule requires;
- breakdown caused by interfering with, or removing, a safety device;
- wear parts and consumables themselves; or
- repairs that the manufacturer or supplier is responsible for under a warranty or recall. If they refuse or delay, we will pay the claim and recover from them in the named insured's name.
