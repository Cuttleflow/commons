heading: Mechanical and Electrical Breakdown
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Under [[ref:peq.section.material-damage|heading]], we do not pay for breakdown of an item of plant, unless the breakdown is itself caused by accidental loss or damage covered by that section. We do pay for accidental loss or damage to other parts of the item, or to other insured plant, that a breakdown causes. Breakdown can be insured under [[ref:peq.section.breakdown|heading]].
