type: obligation
heading: Telling Us About Changes
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

The named insured must tell us within 30 days if, during the policy period, it starts to hire plant out on dry hire, starts operations underground, in tunnels, on or over water, or in demolition, or starts operating outside the geographical limits. We may change the premium or terms for the rest of the policy period, as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.
