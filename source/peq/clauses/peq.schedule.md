(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The named insured and its ABN, its business and its address
- The policy period
- The plant schedule: for each item of specified plant, its description, serial or registration number and year; its basis of settlement (market value or agreed value); its sum insured or agreed value; its excess; any financier; and whether it is hired out, and if so on dry hire or wet hire
- The unspecified plant sum insured and the most we pay for any one item of unspecified plant
- The limits of the extensions to [[ref:peq.section.material-damage|heading]] where they differ from the defaults stated in them, and whether the hire costs benefit applies
- For [[ref:peq.section.hired-in|heading]]: the limit for any one item, the limit for any one event, the continuing hire charges limit and the excess
- For [[ref:peq.section.breakdown|heading]]: the items of specified plant that have breakdown cover and the excess
- For [[ref:peq.section.liability|heading]]: the limit for any one occurrence, any sub-limits that differ from the defaults stated in this wording and the excess
- The geographical limits, if they differ from the default in the definition
- The premium, with stamp duty, any emergency services or fire services levy, and GST
- Any commission or fee paid to your broker
- The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:peq.cond.governing-law]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
