---
id: peq.cond.financier-interests
type: condition
heading: Financier Interests
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Where the schedule shows a financier for an item, we pay the financier first, to the extent of its interest, and that payment discharges us to that extent. A financier's interest is not affected by an act or omission of the named insured that the financier did not know about, if the financier tells us as soon as it becomes aware of it and pays any additional premium we reasonably require.
