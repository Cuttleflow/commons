id: peq.def.hired-in-plant
type: definition
heading: Hired-In Plant
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Mobile plant and equipment, and its attachments, that the named insured hires or leases from another person under a hire agreement for a period of no more than 12 months, while it is in the named insured's care, custody or control. Plant on a longer lease, or that the named insured must insure as owner under the lease, is insured plant when it is shown in the schedule.
