---
id: peq.excl.unattended-theft
type: exclusion
heading: Theft from Unattended Sites
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for theft of unspecified plant left at an unattended site, unless the plant was immobilised, locked in a vehicle or secure container, kept in a locked compound, or secured in an equivalent way. This exclusion does not apply to specified plant; [[ref:peq.obl.reasonable-precautions]] applies to all insured plant.
