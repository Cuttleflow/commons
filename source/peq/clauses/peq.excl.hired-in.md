heading: 'Hired-In Plant: What Is Not Covered'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Under [[ref:peq.section.hired-in|heading]], we do not pay for:

- breakdown or wear of hired-in plant that the named insured did not cause;
- hire charges for any period after the hired-in plant is repaired, replaced or settled;
- liability under a hire agreement that makes the named insured responsible for loss or damage whatever its cause, to a standard beyond that of a reasonable bailee, unless the named insured told us about the agreement before the loss; or
- anything excluded by the exclusions to [[ref:peq.section.material-damage|heading]], read as if hired-in plant were insured plant.
