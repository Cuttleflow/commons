This is the first Commons release of the Meridian Plant and Equipment wording. It is converted from v1.0.0 (2026/PEQ/0001, July 2026). The cover and the settled positions of v1.0.0 are kept. The changes below are drafting remediation, and none reduces cover except where stated.

- Converted to single-source clauses with no clause numbers. Cross-references are by heading.
- Endorsements E01 to E12 were listed by title only in v1.0.0. Each now has full operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger. E05 is retitled Agreed Value: All Items, E06 Hired-Out Plant: Plant Hire Businesses, and E10 Unspecified Plant: Increased Item Limit.
- Terms that decided cover but were not defined are now defined: attachment, continuing hire charges, cyber event, damage waiver, defence costs, event, financier, geographical limits, hire agreement, hired-in plant, named insured, personal injury, pollutant, property damage, schedule, specified plant, third-party claim, unspecified plant and wear parts. Accidental loss or damage now says that it includes theft.
- Event: storm, flood, earthquake, tsunami, bushfire and cyclone damage within 72 hours is one event.
- In the definition of market value, prices are now those of the Australian market, and industry publications and auction results may be used as evidence.
- Hire costs following insured damage: a 72-hour waiting period is stated. Recovery costs: the limit applies where there is no damage; recovery of damaged plant is part of the damage claim.
- Unspecified plant has its own clause, with its total and item defaults carried over from v1.0.0.
- Hired-out plant: a failure to meet the dry hire terms is now dealt with under the Insurance Contracts Act condition, not as a bar to cover.
- Machinery Breakdown: a warranty or recall repair is paid and recovered only if the manufacturer refuses or delays.
- Mobile Plant Liability: defence costs are tied to third-party claims. Defence costs remain in addition to the limit, as in v1.0.0; where a settlement exceeds the limit they are paid in proportion. The asbestos exclusion stays in this section only. The road use exclusion now refers to endorsement E07.
- New payment provisions: One Section for Each Loss (Material Damage pays before Machinery Breakdown, with one excess), Who We Pay, and Salvage. Where several excesses apply to one item for one event, only the highest is deducted.
- Added an Insurance Contracts Act condition (s.54 applies to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation), a standalone sanctions condition naming the Australian regimes and, to the extent they apply to us, the UK, US and EU regimes, a jurisdiction condition, and a headings and references condition.
- Governing law is fixed to the state or territory shown in the schedule, or of the named insured's address at the start of the policy period, instead of its principal place of business, which could change during the policy.
- Fraud now cites s.56 of the Insurance Contracts Act and protects innocent insureds. Cancellation is restated, with no refund for an item paid as a total loss.
- New obligation: Telling Us About Changes, for dry hire, underground, wet works, demolition and work outside the geographical limits started during the policy period.
- Licence changed from CC BY-SA 4.0 to CC BY 4.0, in line with the rest of the Commons.
- Three WP REVIEW tags: registered plant with a carrying capacity of 2 tonnes or less as a retail motor product, mobile plant as eligible property under the Terrorism Insurance Act 2003 (Cth), and the library-wide sanctions scope.
