heading: Towing and Road Use
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability:

- for personal injury to a person being towed by the boat on water skis, a wakeboard, a kneeboard, an inflatable or any similar thing, or caused by that person, unless [[ref:mpc.end.e03]] applies;
- arising from towing a person or object in the air, such as a parasail or kite;
- arising from the boat or the trailer while it is attached to, or being towed by, a motor vehicle on land.
