---
id: mpc.excl.navigation
type: exclusion
heading: Outside the Navigation Limits
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss, damage or liability arising while the boat is afloat outside the navigation limits, unless [[ref:mpc.end.e08]] applies. Cover under this policy is suspended from the time the boat clears Australian customs for an overseas destination until it returns to Australian waters.
