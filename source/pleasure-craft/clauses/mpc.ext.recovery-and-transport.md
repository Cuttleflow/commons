heading: Recovery and Road Transport
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

After damage covered by this section, we pay the reasonable cost of recovering the boat from the water and transporting it by road to the nearest repairer we agree to, and of returning it after repair, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $2,500) for any one event. This amount is in addition to the sum insured.
