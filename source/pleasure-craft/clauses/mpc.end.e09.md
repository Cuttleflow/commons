The boat is covered while it is used as a residence by the people named in the schedule for this endorsement. The exclusion of a boat used as a residence, in [[ref:mpc.excl.boat-other|heading]], does not apply to that use. The limits in [[ref:mpc.ext.personal-effects]] are replaced by the limits shown in the schedule for this endorsement.

Schedule variables: residents; personal effects limits.
