---
id: mpc.excl.moorings-and-rigging
type: exclusion
heading: Moorings and Standing Rigging
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for:

- damage caused by the failure of a swing mooring that had not been inspected by a qualified mooring contractor in the 12 months before the damage;
- dismasting, or damage to the masts, spars, sails or rigging, caused by the failure of standing rigging that was more than 10 years old at the time, unless [[ref:mpc.end.e07]] applies;
- damage by a named cyclone to the boat while it is on a swing mooring in northern waters during the cyclone season, unless [[ref:mpc.end.e05]] applies.
