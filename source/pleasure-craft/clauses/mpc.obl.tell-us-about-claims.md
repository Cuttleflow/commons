---
id: mpc.obl.tell-us-about-claims
type: obligation
heading: Telling Us About a Claim
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after damage or an occurrence that may lead to a claim. Tell the police promptly about any theft or malicious damage, and report an accident to the maritime authority of the state or territory where the law requires it.

Give us the information and documents we reasonably ask for to assess the claim, including proof of ownership or value, and allow us to inspect the boat before it is repaired, unless emergency repairs are needed under [[ref:mpc.ext.emergency-expenses]].
