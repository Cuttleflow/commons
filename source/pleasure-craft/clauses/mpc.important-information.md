**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

**Who this policy is for.** This wording is designed for retail clients. An issuer using it must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available, as the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) requires. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.

**Which law applies.** Because the boat is a pleasure craft owned by individuals, the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) applies to this policy and the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) does not (s.9A of the Insurance Contracts Act). **Your duty when you apply.** This policy is a consumer insurance contract under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). You have a duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated. Answer every question in the proposal honestly and completely. If you do not take reasonable care, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

**Terms you should know about.** Pleasure craft insurance has no standard cover under the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017. We draw your attention to these terms, which limit or reduce the cover and which you may not expect:

- the boat is covered only within the navigation limits shown in the schedule, and cover is suspended once the boat clears Australian customs for an overseas destination (see [[ref:mpc.excl.navigation]]);
- racing is covered only as club racing within 50 nautical miles of the start, unless [[ref:mpc.end.e01]] applies, and racing damage to sails, spars and rigging is limited (see [[ref:mpc.excl.racing-and-speed]]);
- we do not pay for loss, damage or liability while the boat is operated faster than the maximum speed shown in the schedule (see [[ref:mpc.excl.racing-and-speed]]);
- a higher excess applies to theft of a personal watercraft or of a boat stored ashore, and a further amount applies if it was not secured, to a trailerable boat that sinks after being left afloat unattended, and to a claim while an operator under 25 was in control (see [[ref:mpc.pay.excess]]);
- we do not pay for damage by a named cyclone to a boat on a swing mooring in northern waters during the cyclone season, unless [[ref:mpc.end.e05]] applies, and a named cyclone excess applies in northern waters under [[ref:mpc.end.e04]];
- we do not pay for damage caused by the failure of a swing mooring that was not inspected in the 12 months before the damage, or by the failure of standing rigging more than 10 years old (see [[ref:mpc.excl.moorings-and-rigging]]);
- we do not pay for wear and tear, osmosis, corrosion, marine growth or mechanical breakdown, or for damage to a motor from water entering it or from overheating, except as [[ref:mpc.excl.wear-and-breakdown]] and [[ref:mpc.excl.motor-damage]] allow;
- we do not pay for loss, damage or liability while the boat is operated by a person who is not licensed as the law requires or is affected by alcohol or drugs, or while it is used for hire or reward (see [[ref:mpc.excl.operation]] and [[ref:mpc.excl.hire-and-reward]]);
- on a market value basis we pay the market value of the boat on a total loss, which may be less than the sum insured (see [[ref:mpc.pay.boat-settlement]]);
- personal effects are covered only up to the limits in [[ref:mpc.ext.personal-effects]].

**Cooling-off period.** You may cancel this policy within 21 days of the date it starts, and within 21 days of each renewal, by telling us in writing. If you have not made a claim, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover. After that, you may still cancel under [[ref:mpc.cond.cancellation]].

**Commission and fees.** The schedule or the quote shows any commission or fee paid to your broker in connection with this policy.

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
