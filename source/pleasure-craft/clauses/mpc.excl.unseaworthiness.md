---
id: mpc.excl.unseaworthiness
type: exclusion
heading: Unseaworthiness and Maintenance
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for damage caused by the boat being unseaworthy when it was taken out, or by a failure to maintain the boat in a reasonable state of repair, where you knew, or could reasonably have been expected to know, of the condition that caused the damage. [[ref:mpc.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies.
