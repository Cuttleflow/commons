You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. If you cancel within the cooling-off period in [[ref:mpc.important-information]], we refund the premium as that section states. Otherwise we refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. If we have paid a total loss of the boat, no refund applies.

We may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you the written notice that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis. [WP REVIEW: confirm the notice an insurer must give to cancel a pleasure craft contract under s.59 and s.60 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).]

Cancellation does not affect a claim for damage, or an occurrence, that happened before cancellation.
