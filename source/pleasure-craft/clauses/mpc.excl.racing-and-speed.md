---
id: mpc.excl.racing-and-speed
type: exclusion
heading: Racing and Speed
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss, damage or liability arising while:

- the boat is racing, other than club racing by a sailing boat in a race organised by a yacht club or sailing association within 50 nautical miles of the start, unless [[ref:mpc.end.e01]] applies;
- a powered boat or personal watercraft is racing, or taking part in a speed trial or a record attempt;
- the boat is being operated faster than the maximum speed shown in the schedule (default 50 knots, or 65 knots for a personal watercraft).
