heading: Personal Accident Benefit
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If a person dies within 12 months of an injury caused solely by an accident during the policy period while they are on board the boat, boarding or leaving it, or being towed by it under [[ref:mpc.end.e03]], we pay the death benefit shown in the schedule (default $25,000 for each person, and $100,000 in all for any one accident) to that person's estate.

We do not pay this benefit for death caused by illness or disease, by an injury the person caused deliberately to themselves, or while the person was affected by alcohol above the legal limit or by a drug not taken as prescribed. This benefit is not paid for an operator who is not licensed as the law requires.
