This is the first release of the Meridian Pleasure Craft wording (2026/MPC/0001). It was drafted for the Commons for a Cuttleflow Go template (MPC-AU-GO), on the structure of the Meridian Home and Contents wording. No other insurer's or market body's wording was used.

- Written for a pleasure craft as s.9A of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) describes one: recreational use, not for reward, owned by individuals. A boat owned by a company, trust or partnership is outside the design.
- The Boat section on an accidental damage basis, afloat within the navigation limits, ashore anywhere in Australia and on its trailer. Market value or agreed value as the schedule shows; new for old within two years for a first owner; salvage in addition to the sum insured; no under-insurance penalty.
- Extensions for personal effects, emergency expenses, recovery and road transport, a replacement trailer or tender, and a personal accident death benefit.
- Legal Liability of $10m by default, with $20m available, for the owner and anyone using the boat with permission, a pollution limit of $500,000 inside the limit, and wreck removal inside the limit.
- Theft security, unattended afloat and young operator terms are drafted as excesses, not exclusions. No basic excess applies in a marina berth, except for named cyclone damage.
- Named cyclone terms for northern waters: a separate excess (E04), and cover on a swing mooring by endorsement only (E05).
- Endorsements E01 to E11, each with full operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger.
- Four WP REVIEW tags: the s.9A treatment of mixed ownership, the s.37 notice of unusual terms, the insurer's cancellation notice, and the library-wide sanctions scope.
