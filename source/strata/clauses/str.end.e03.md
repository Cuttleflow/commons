[[ref:str.excl.major-works]] does not apply to the works named in the schedule for this endorsement, for the period shown.

The works themselves, and materials on site for them, are not covered: they must be insured by the contractor under a contract works policy, and you must keep evidence of that policy. Damage to the rest of the building caused by the works is covered, subject to the special excess shown in the schedule for this endorsement, less any amount recovered from the contractor's insurance.

Schedule variables: description and contract value of the works; works period; special excess.
