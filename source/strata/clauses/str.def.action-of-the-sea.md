heading: Action of the Sea
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The action of the sea, high water, tidal wave or storm surge, and any rise in the level of the sea, including a tsunami. It does not include a tsunami caused by an earthquake to the extent [[ref:str.excl.earth-movement]] does not exclude it.
