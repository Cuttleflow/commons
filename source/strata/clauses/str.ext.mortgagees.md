id: str.ext.mortgagees
type: extension
heading: Interests of Lot Owners and Financiers
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The interest of each lot owner, and of any financier of a lot owner, in the building is covered. An act or omission of a lot owner or occupier, without your knowledge or consent, does not reduce your cover or the cover for any other lot owner. [[ref:str.cond.lot-owner-acts]] applies.
