heading: Damage to the Building and Common Contents
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If the building or the common contents suffer damage during the policy period, we will pay to repair, rebuild or replace the damaged property, as set out in [[ref:str.pay.basis-of-settlement]].

The most we will pay for any one event is the building sum insured for the building and the common contents sum insured for the common contents, plus the amounts the extensions to this section say are payable in addition.
