---
id: str.ext.social-engineering
type: extension
heading: Social Engineering Fraud
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We will pay you for the loss of funds that you suffer through social engineering fraud, first discovered during the policy period, up to $10,000 for any one loss and in total during the policy period, or the amount shown in the schedule. This amount is within the limit of [[ref:str.section.fidelity]].

You do not need to have followed any particular verification procedure for this extension to respond. If a reasonable verification step was available and was not taken, we may take that into account only under [[ref:str.cond.insurance-contracts-act]], and never to refuse the claim outright.
