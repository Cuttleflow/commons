---
id: str.excl.major-works
type: exclusion
heading: Major Works
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for damage to any part of the building on which major works are being done, or caused by major works, unless we have agreed in writing to cover the works. [[ref:str.end.e03]] sets out the terms on which we cover them. Damage to the rest of the building by an insured cause is still covered.
