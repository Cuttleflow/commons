This cover applies only if the schedule shows it.

If an event that causes damage covered by [[ref:str.section.property]] is declared a catastrophe or disaster by the Insurance Council of Australia or a government, or causes widespread damage in the area of the building, and as a result the cost of repairing or rebuilding increases because of demand for labour and materials, we pay:

- the extra cost of repairing or rebuilding above the building sum insured;
- the extra cost of temporary accommodation and loss of rent above the limit in [[ref:str.ext.loss-of-rent]], and for up to a further 12 months;
- the extra cost of storing lot owners' contents while repairs are made.

The most we pay under this cover for any one event is 15% of the building sum insured, or the percentage shown in the schedule, in addition to the building sum insured.
