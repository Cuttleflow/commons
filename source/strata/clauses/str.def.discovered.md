For [[ref:str.section.fidelity]] only: when a member of the committee, or the strata manager acting for you, first becomes aware of facts that would cause a reasonable person to assume that a loss covered by that section has happened or will happen, even if the amount or details are not yet known. If no such person becomes aware because the person responsible for the loss is the only one who knows, the loss is discovered when any lot owner first becomes so aware.
