heading: Office Bearers' Claims Not Covered
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Under [[ref:str.section.office-bearers]] we do not pay for a claim:

- arising from a dishonest or fraudulent act, or a deliberate breach of the law, by the office bearer, or from the office bearer gaining a profit or advantage they were not entitled to, but only if this is established by final adjudication. Until then we pay defence costs as [[ref:str.cover.office-bearers]] provides;
- for personal injury or property damage, which [[ref:str.section.liability]] covers;
- arising from facts or circumstances the office bearer knew of before the policy period, and that a reasonable person in their position would have known could lead to a claim;
- for a wrongful act committed before the retroactive date;
- for a fine, penalty or other amount that the law does not allow to be insured;
- arising from the office bearer's acts as the strata manager or as a professional adviser to the body corporate.

A claim by the body corporate or a lot owner against an office bearer is covered.
