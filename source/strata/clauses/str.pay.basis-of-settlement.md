**Reinstatement and replacement.** We pay the cost of repairing or rebuilding damaged property to a condition substantially the same as, but not better or more extensive than, its condition when new, using current materials and methods. Where a lost item of common contents cannot be repaired, we pay the cost of replacing it with a new item of the same kind and quality. We do not deduct for wear, tear or depreciation.

**Costs included.** The cost of repairing or rebuilding includes the reasonable cost of demolishing and removing debris, and the fees of architects, engineers, surveyors and other professionals needed to repair or rebuild, within the sum insured.

**Building law.** The cost of repairing or rebuilding includes the extra cost of complying with the building laws in force at the time of repair, for the damaged part of the building. It does not include the cost of bringing any part of the building that is not damaged into compliance, or of complying with an order or notice served on you before the damage happened.

**When you repair.** We pay the reasonable cost as it is incurred. You may rebuild on another site, or in a different way, if the cost to us is no greater.

**When you do not repair.** If you decide under the strata legislation not to repair or rebuild, or the law does not allow it, we pay what it would have cost to repair or rebuild at the date of the damage, up to the sum insured.

**No under-insurance penalty.** We do not reduce a claim because the sum insured is less than the full value of the building.

**Sum insured not reduced.** The sum insured is not reduced by a claim we pay under this section.
