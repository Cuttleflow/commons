We pay the body corporate, or as it directs. Where an interested party is shown in the schedule, we pay as the schedule and the strata legislation require. Benefits under [[ref:str.section.voluntary-workers]] are paid to the voluntary worker, and payments under [[ref:str.section.office-bearers]] on behalf of the office bearer.
