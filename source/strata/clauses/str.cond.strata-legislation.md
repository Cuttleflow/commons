This policy is intended to provide the insurance the strata legislation requires the body corporate to hold. If the strata legislation requires cover, a limit or a term that this policy does not give, this policy is read as giving it, to the extent the legislation requires.

In particular, if the strata legislation requires a public liability limit higher than the limit shown in the schedule, the higher limit applies. If the strata legislation of South Australia applies, the limit of [[ref:str.section.fidelity]] is at least the amount that legislation requires.
