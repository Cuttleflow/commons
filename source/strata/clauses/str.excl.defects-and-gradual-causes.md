We do not pay for:

- the cost of fixing a defect, including the cost of removing, replacing or making good any cladding, waterproofing or other part of the building that is defective;
- wear and tear, rust, corrosion, rot, mould, mildew or gradual deterioration;
- damage that happens gradually over time, including from water or moisture seeping, leaking or condensing over a period, unless you did not know of it and could not reasonably have known of it;
- damage caused by insects, vermin or tree roots.

We do pay for damage to other parts of the building that results suddenly and accidentally from a defect, or from one of these causes, for example a fire caused by faulty wiring, or a ceiling that collapses because a pipe bursts. Where we have agreed to cover a known defect, [[ref:str.end.e02]] sets out what applies to it.
