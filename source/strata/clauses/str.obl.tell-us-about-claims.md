---
id: str.obl.tell-us-about-claims
type: obligation
heading: Tell Us About Claims
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after damage, an occurrence, a breakdown or a loss that may lead to a claim, and tell the police promptly about any theft, malicious damage or dishonest act.

Give us the information and documents we reasonably ask for to assess the claim, at your own cost, and allow us to inspect the damaged property before it is repaired, unless emergency repairs are needed under [[ref:str.ext.emergency-repairs]].
