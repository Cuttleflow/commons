This policy insures a strata scheme. It is bought by the body corporate (in some states called the owners corporation, strata company or community corporation) that the strata legislation requires to insure the building and common property, and to hold public liability insurance.

It has seven sections. The sections that apply are shown in the schedule.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Buildings and Common Contents | Damage to the building and common contents, with loss of rent and temporary accommodation | Damage during the policy period |
| Public Liability | Your legal liability for injury or property damage connected with the scheme | Occurrence during the policy period |
| Voluntary Workers | Fixed benefits for people who work for the scheme without pay | Injury during the policy period |
| Fidelity Guarantee | Loss of the scheme's funds through dishonesty, and social engineering fraud | Loss discovered during the policy period |
| Office Bearers' Liability | Claims against office bearers for their decisions | Claims first made during the policy period |
| Machinery Breakdown | Breakdown of lifts, pumps and other machinery on common property | Breakdown during the policy period |
| Government Audit, Appeal and Legal Expenses | Government audit costs, safety appeal costs and legal defence costs | Matters first notified during the policy period |

The [[ref:str.cover.catastrophe|heading]] cover, the flood cover in [[ref:str.end.e01]] and the [[ref:str.section.machinery|heading]] section apply only if the schedule shows them.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:str.section.definitions]].

The strata legislation of the state or territory where the building is located sets what this policy must cover. Where it requires more than this wording gives, [[ref:str.cond.strata-legislation]] applies.
