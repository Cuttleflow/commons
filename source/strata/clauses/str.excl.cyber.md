---
id: str.excl.cyber
type: exclusion
heading: Cyber Events
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss of, damage to or corruption of electronic data, or for loss arising from unauthorised use of a computer system, other than physical damage to the building by a resulting fire or explosion, and other than as [[ref:str.ext.social-engineering]] provides. Unauthorised use means use of a computer system by a person not permitted by its owner to use it.
