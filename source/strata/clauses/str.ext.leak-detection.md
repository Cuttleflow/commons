heading: Finding the Source of a Leak
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Where liquid has escaped and caused damage covered by this section, we pay the reasonable cost of finding the source of the escape and of making good the property damaged in finding it, up to $10,000 for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule. This amount is in addition to the building sum insured. We do not pay to repair the pipe, fitting or appliance from which the liquid escaped unless it was itself damaged by an insured cause.
