id: str.ext.lot-owners-fixtures
type: extension
heading: Lot Owners' Fixtures and Improvements
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We cover damage to lot owners' fixtures and improvements as if they were part of the building, up to $250,000 for each lot, or the amount shown in the schedule, for any one event. This amount is within the building sum insured.

Where a lot owner has their own insurance for the same fixtures and improvements, this policy pays first, subject to [[ref:str.pay.other-insurance]].
