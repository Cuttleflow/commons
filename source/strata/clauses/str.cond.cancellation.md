You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. If we have paid a total loss of the insured property, no refund applies to that item. If you cancel within the cooling-off period, [[ref:str.important-information]] says what we refund. The strata legislation requires the body corporate to keep the building insured, so arrange replacement cover before you cancel.

We may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you written notice as that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis.

Cancellation does not affect a claim for damage, an occurrence or a loss that happened, or a claim that was first made, before cancellation.
