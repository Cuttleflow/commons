(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The body corporate's name, the strata plan or scheme number and the address of the building
- The scheme type: residential, or commercial and mixed use
- The policy period, with its start and end dates
- The sections that apply, with the sum insured or limit and the excess for each
- The building sum insured and the date of the latest valuation, and the common contents sum insured
- Whether the catastrophe cover and the flood cover apply
- The limits of the extensions, where they differ from the defaults stated in them
- The retroactive date and the fidelity continuity date, if any
- The number of lots
- Any interested party, such as a financier
- The premium, including stamp duty, any emergency services or fire services levy, and GST
- Any commission or fee paid to your broker or strata manager
- The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:str.cond.governing-law]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
