heading: Floating Floors
since: 1.0.0

---

Floating floors, whether laid by the developer or by a lot owner, are part of the building. Where a floating floor is damaged, we pay to repair or replace the damaged area, and the reasonable cost of replacing undamaged flooring in the same room or connected area so that the flooring matches, as far as is reasonable.
