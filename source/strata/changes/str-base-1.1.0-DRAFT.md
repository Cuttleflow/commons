Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. Changes from version 1.0.0:

- Personal Injury (definition): the closing words "other than as a result of a wrongful act" are removed. They could be read to take an ordinary slip-and-fall claim out of Public Liability whenever the claim said a committee had neglected the common property.
- Public Liability: new paragraph on claims against office bearers. A claim for bodily injury, sickness, mental injury or property damage is paid under Public Liability even if it alleges an office bearer's neglect or error. A claim for false arrest, wrongful detention, malicious prosecution, defamation or invasion of privacy that results from a wrongful act is paid under Office Bearers' Liability, where that section is shown.
- Office Bearers' Claims Not Covered: the exclusion for personal injury and property damage says it applies even if the claim alleges a wrongful act. New exclusion for claims, proceedings and investigations that were pending or had started, or were notified under an earlier policy, before the retroactive date. A sentence applies the severability paragraph to every exclusion that turns on what an office bearer knew or did.
- Retroactive Date (definition): if the schedule shows none, the date is now the start of the first policy we issued to the body corporate that included the section, without a break in cover. Before, there was no retroactive date at all.
- Office Bearers' Liability, advancing defence costs: also repayable where a final adjudication or a court's finding establishes conduct that the policy or the law does not allow to be covered.
- Fidelity Losses Not Covered: the exclusion for loss discovered more than 12 months after the policy period ends or the section is cancelled is removed. Fidelity cover responds only to loss first discovered during the policy period, and the exclusion implied an extended discovery period that the policy never granted.
- Breakdown: new definition for the Machinery Breakdown section.
- Control Group: new definition. The legal expenses exclusion for matters known before the policy period now turns on what a member of the control group knew.
- Important Information: new paragraph, Who this policy is for. Some buyers may be retail clients, in which case a Product Disclosure Statement and Target Market Determination are needed.
- Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) (previously headed Insurance Contracts Act): replaced with a full statement that section 54 applies to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation.
- Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), sections 199A and 199B: new condition for office bearers where the body corporate is a company. Wording Partner review tag added.
- Sanctions: replaced with the library-wide wording naming the Australian regimes and, to the extent they apply to us, the UK, US and EU regimes. United Nations sanctions are no longer named separately. Wording Partner review tag added.
- Cancellation: restated. You may cancel at any time for a pro-rata refund, and no refund applies to insured property for which we have paid a total loss. We may cancel only as the Insurance Contracts Act allows.
- Other Insurance: replaced. We do not refuse or reduce a payment because of other insurance and may seek contribution. This policy is excess only of a specified underlying policy named in the schedule or an endorsement. Cover for lot owners' own insurance on the building is unchanged.
