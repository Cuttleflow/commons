This is the first release of the Meridian Strata wording. It is a new wording, drafted for the Commons for residential, commercial and mixed-use strata schemes in every Australian state and territory. It is not derived from an earlier Meridian wording.

- Seven sections: Buildings and Common Contents, Public Liability, Voluntary Workers, Fidelity Guarantee, Office Bearers' Liability, Machinery Breakdown, and Government Audit, Appeal and Legal Expenses. The schedule shows which apply.
- Buildings and common contents are insured for reinstatement and replacement, with no under-insurance penalty and no reduction of the sum insured after a claim. The cost of complying with building law applies to the damaged part only.
- Extensions include loss of rent and temporary accommodation (default 15% of the building sum insured, 12 months), lot owners' fixtures and improvements (default $250,000 a lot), floating floors, emergency repairs, finding the source of a leak, fire brigade charges, and keys and locks. Optional catastrophe cover adds up to 15% for demand surge.
- Flood is excluded unless Endorsement E01 applies. Defects are not themselves covered, but resulting sudden damage is. Known defects are recorded and covered on agreed terms by Endorsement E02, and combustible cladding by E04, so a defect leads to a referral, not an automatic decline.
- Public liability insures the body corporate, lot owners as owners of the common property, office bearers, voluntary workers and employees, with defence costs in addition to the limit.
- Fidelity guarantee is discovery-based, with single-loss and cross-period rules, a fidelity continuity date, and social engineering fraud (default $10,000) that does not depend on following a verification procedure.
- Office bearers' liability is claims-made, with a retroactive date, related-claims aggregation, defence costs advanced, severability, and the conduct exclusion applying only on final adjudication. Claims by the body corporate or a lot owner are covered.
- General conditions: an Insurance Contracts Act savings clause applying s.54, a conformity clause so that the strata legislation's requirements prevail, protection of the body corporate and other lot owners from one lot owner's act or fraud, no recovery against lot owners and occupiers except for deliberate or dishonest acts, a standalone sanctions condition, and governing law fixed to the state or territory where the building is located.
- The endorsement library has 11 endorsements, each with operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger.
