---
id: cr.excl.directors-and-officers-dishonesty
type: exclusion
heading: Directors' and Officers' Dishonesty
legacy_number: '4.6'
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

A dishonest act committed by a director, officer, or partner of yours who is also a beneficial owner of more than 15% of the named insured's issued shares or equity — unless the act is committed in collusion with an employee and the employee's involvement is independently established.
