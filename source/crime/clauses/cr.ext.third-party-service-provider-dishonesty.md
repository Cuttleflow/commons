id: cr.ext.third-party-service-provider-dishonesty
type: extension
heading: Third-Party Service Provider Dishonesty
legacy_number: '3.5'
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We will cover loss caused by a dishonest or fraudulent act committed by an employee of a third-party service provider while performing services for you under a written contract — provided the act is committed with manifest intent and directed at you. This extension does not apply to losses caused by general negligence, incompetence, or breach of contract by the third-party service provider.
