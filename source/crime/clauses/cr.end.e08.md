This policy is written in excess of the underlying insurance named in the schedule for this endorsement, up to the limit of liability shown in the schedule. We pay only after the underlying limits have been exhausted by payment of loss that this policy would cover.

This policy follows the terms of the underlying insurance except where this policy states otherwise. Where the underlying insurance does not cover a loss that this policy covers, this policy does not drop down.

You must keep the underlying insurance in force. If it is not, this policy applies as if the underlying insurance remained in force with its limits intact.

Schedule variables: underlying insurance; underlying limit; limit of liability.
