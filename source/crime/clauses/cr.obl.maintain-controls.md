Maintain reasonable financial controls and internal procedures throughout the policy period, including segregation of duties, dual authorisation for significant payments, reconciliation procedures, and access controls.

*The obligations in this [[ref:cr.section.obligations]] are subject to [[ref:cr.cond.insurance-contracts-act-1984-cth-general]] — General): we will not refuse to pay a loss by reason only of a post-contractual act or omission except to the extent s 54 of the Act permits.*
