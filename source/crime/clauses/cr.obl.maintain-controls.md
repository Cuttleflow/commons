Maintain reasonable financial controls and internal procedures throughout the policy period, including segregation of duties, reconciliation procedures and access controls, and dual authorisation for significant payments.

Dual authorisation and other verification steps are not a condition of cover under [[ref:cr.cover.social-engineering-fraud]] or [[ref:cr.cover.funds-transfer-fraud]]. A failure to keep them in place affects a payment only as [[ref:cr.cover.social-engineering-fraud]] and the endorsements for increased sub-limits say.

*The obligations in [[ref:cr.section.obligations]] are subject to [[ref:cr.cond.insurance-contracts-act-1984-cth-general]]: we will not refuse to pay a loss by reason only of a post-contractual act or omission except to the extent section 54 of the Act permits.*
