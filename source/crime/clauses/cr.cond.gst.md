Amounts payable under this policy are calculated without regard to any GST that you are entitled to recover as an input tax credit. If a payment under this policy is consideration for a taxable supply, we will pay the GST on it in addition. Under [[ref:cr.ext.clients-property]], if you pay a client an amount that we indemnify and you incur GST on it that you cannot recover as an input tax credit, we also pay that GST.

[WP REVIEW: the earlier text cited s 48 of A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth). Confirm that Division 78 governs the GST treatment of insurance settlements, and confirm the position on irrecoverable GST for Client's Property.]
