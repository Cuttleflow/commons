heading: Newly Acquired Subsidiaries
legacy_number: '3.7'

We automatically cover an entity that the named insured acquires or forms during the policy period and that becomes a subsidiary, from the date it becomes a subsidiary, for 90 days, if:

- its total assets do not exceed 25% of the named insured's consolidated total assets at that date; and
- the named insured tells us about it in writing within the 90 days.

We cover only a loss from a criminal act that first occurs on or after the date the entity became a subsidiary. For that entity, the retroactive date is the date it became a subsidiary. We do not cover a criminal act that occurred before that date, even if you discover the loss during the policy period.

After the 90 days, the entity is covered only if we agree in writing, and we may charge an additional premium and set terms. If we do not agree, cover for the entity ends at the end of the 90 days. A loss from a criminal act that occurred during the 90 days remains covered, if you discover it during the policy period.

An entity above the asset limit is covered only if we agree in writing before the 90 days end.
