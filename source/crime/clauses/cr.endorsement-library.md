An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables (sub-limits, excesses, periods and named parties) shown for it in the schedule. Where an endorsement and the rest of this policy conflict, the endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. If a variable an endorsement needs is not shown in the schedule, the endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the schedule. These endorsements are for use with version 2.0.x of this wording.

| Code | Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Increased Social Engineering Sub-limit | Increases the social engineering sub-limit |
| E02 | Third-Party Service Provider: Increased Sub-limit and Broadened Scope | Increases the sub-limit and names the service providers covered |
| E03 | Client Property: Increased Sub-limit | Increases the client property sub-limit |
| E04 | Vendor Dishonesty | Covers dishonest acts of suppliers working at your premises |
| E05 | Impersonation of Client at Premises | Covers delivery of money or property to an impostor at your premises |
| E06 | Cryptocurrency and Digital Asset Theft | Includes named digital assets as money and securities |
| E07 | Wage and Payroll Fraud: Expanded | Expands cover for fictitious employees and timesheet manipulation |
| E08 | Excess Layer | Writes this policy in excess of the underlying insurance named |
| E09 | Financial Institution: Fiduciary Funds | Covers client money held in trust |
| E10 | Invoice Manipulation Fraud | Covers payment of altered or false supplier invoices |
