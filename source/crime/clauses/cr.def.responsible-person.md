---
id: cr.def.responsible-person
type: definition
heading: Responsible person
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

means any director, officer, partner, principal, risk manager, compliance officer, internal auditor, chief financial officer, or head of finance of the named insured; or, where the named insured has no natural person holding any of those roles, its most senior officer or manager responsible for its financial affairs at the relevant time.
