Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts, or modifies the operation of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). In particular, s 54 of the Act applies to any act or omission by you after the criminal act giving rise to a loss (including in connection with the obligations in [[ref:cr.section.obligations]]) — we may not refuse to pay a loss by reason only of such an act or omission except to the extent the Act permits. Where any provision of this policy is inconsistent with the Act, the Act prevails.

*This policy operates on a discovery basis, not a claims-made basis. The circumstance-notification mechanism in s 40(3) of the Act (designed for claims-made covers) has no direct work to do here — the Discovery trigger in [[ref:cr.cond.discovery-trigger]] and the prompt-notification obligation in [[ref:cr.obl.notify-us-promptly]] serve the equivalent function for this policy.*
