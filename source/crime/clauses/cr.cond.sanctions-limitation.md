We are not liable to provide any cover, or to pay any claim or benefit, under this policy to the extent that doing so would expose us or our reinsurers to enforcement action under: (a) the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011 (Cth) and the Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011 (Cth); (b) the Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 (Cth) and regulations made under it; or (c) the applicable trade or economic sanctions laws of the United Nations, the United States, the United Kingdom, or the European Union. This clause is a limitation on payment, not an exclusion of a peril, and applies regardless of when a loss otherwise covered by this policy is discovered. See also [[ref:cr.excl.war-terrorism-and-nuclear-risks]].
