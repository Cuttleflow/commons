We will pay your loss caused by a dishonest or fraudulent act committed by an employee, acting alone or in collusion with others, provided: (a) the act was committed with manifest intent; and (b) the loss is first discovered during the policy period.

This covers: theft of money, securities, or tangible property; fraudulent alteration of your accounts or records; fraudulent electronic funds transfers; deliberate destruction of property or data to conceal a dishonest act; embezzlement and misappropriation; inventory theft; and payroll fraud (fictitious employees, inflated hours, unauthorised bonuses).

**Discovery after termination:** If you discover an employee's dishonesty after their employment ends, the loss is covered provided it is discovered during the policy period and the dishonest act occurred during the employee's employment.
