id: cr.cover.funds-transfer-fraud

heading: Funds Transfer Fraud
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We will pay your loss caused by a fraudulent funds transfer instruction issued by a third party who, without your authority, has gained access to your communications or banking systems and impersonates you or an authorised representative. The fraudulent instruction must result in the transfer of your money or securities from your account to an account not controlled by you. The loss must be first discovered during the policy period.
