heading: Subsidiary
since: 2.0.0
changed: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

means any entity in which the named insured holds more than 50% of the voting rights, or over which the named insured has effective management control, at the start of the policy period, and any entity that becomes a subsidiary during the policy period and is covered under [[ref:cr.ext.newly-acquired-subsidiaries]]. An entity is a subsidiary only for criminal acts that first occur while it is a subsidiary.
