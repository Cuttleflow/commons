This is a commercial (non-consumer) contract of insurance. Before entering into, varying, or renewing this policy, you have a duty under sections 21 and 21A of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) to disclose to us every matter that you know, or that a reasonable person in the circumstances could be expected to know, is relevant to our decision to accept the risk and on what terms. For the named insured and each subsidiary, what it knows is what a member of its control group knows (see [[ref:cr.def.control-group]]).

Each insured is the named insured or a subsidiary. Disclosure is assessed separately for each insured. The non-disclosure or misrepresentation of one insured does not affect the cover of another insured that did not know of it and was not party to it. A person who is not a member of the control group, including an employee whose dishonesty causes a loss, does not by that person's knowledge or conduct cause the non-disclosure or misrepresentation of an insured.

If you fail to comply with this duty, we may be entitled under s 28 of the Act to reduce our liability for a claim proportionately or, in the case of fraudulent non-disclosure or misrepresentation by the insured seeking cover, to avoid the policy from inception in relation to that insured. We will not exercise the s 28(3) proportionate-reduction remedy against an insured that did not know of, and was not party to, the relevant non-disclosure or misrepresentation.
