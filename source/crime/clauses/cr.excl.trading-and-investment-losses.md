---
id: cr.excl.trading-and-investment-losses
type: exclusion
heading: Trading and Investment Losses
legacy_number: '4.2'
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Loss arising from trading in shares, securities, commodities, currencies, derivatives, or other financial instruments — whether authorised or unauthorised — except to the extent the trading was conducted by an employee with manifest intent and the loss is covered under [[ref:cr.cover.employee-dishonesty]].
