This document is version-controlled. The version and date shown on the cover page and in the footer of each page govern this text. The version stated in your schedule as current at inception applies for the life of your policy period, unless your schedule or an endorsement states otherwise.

This is the first release of the Meridian Comprehensive Crime wording built from clause source in the Commons. It replaces version 1.2.0 (July 2026). Changes from version 1.2.0:

- Clause numbers are removed. Each clause is identified by its heading, and every cross-reference is a link to the clause it names.
- The endorsement library has 10 endorsements, each with operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger. Version 1.2.0 listed titles only.
- The kidnap, ransom and extortion endorsement (E05 in version 1.2.0) is removed, as it was from the Management Liability wording. The remaining endorsements are renumbered E01 to E10.
- Each endorsement trigger is published for the Cuttleflow Go template. Social engineering fraud cover is referred to an underwriter where the insured does not have an independent audit, call-back verification of changed payment details, or dual authorisation of payments.
- The wording remains DRAFT and is subject to legal review.
