---
id: cr.excl.government-and-regulatory-action
type: exclusion
heading: Government and Regulatory Action
legacy_number: '4.7'
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Loss arising from a fine, penalty, tax, levy, or confiscation order imposed by any government or regulatory body — except forensic investigation and legal costs covered under [[ref:cr.section.extensions]].
