The sub-limit in [[ref:cr.ext.third-party-service-provider-dishonesty]] is increased to the amount shown in the schedule for this endorsement, and the extension applies to each service provider named in the schedule for this endorsement, whether or not it is a third-party service provider under a written contract that meets the definition. The increased sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability of the clause it extends.

We do not cover loss caused by a service provider that is a subsidiary of the named insured, or loss that the service provider's own insurance or the written contract requires the service provider to bear.

Schedule variables: sub-limit; named service providers; excess.
