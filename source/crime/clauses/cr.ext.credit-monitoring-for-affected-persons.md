If a criminal act covered under [[ref:cr.cover.employee-dishonesty]] or [[ref:cr.cover.computer-crime]] involves the theft of personal information of your customers, employees, or other persons, and you provide credit monitoring or identity theft protection services to those persons, we will contribute to the reasonable costs.
