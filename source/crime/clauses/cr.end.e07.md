The payroll fraud cover in the [[ref:cr.cover.employee-dishonesty]] clause is expanded to cover loss caused by an employee who creates a fictitious employee, manipulates timesheets, inflates hours or rates, or authorises payments of wages or allowances that are not owed, whether or not the employee gains personally from each payment.

We do not cover wages or entitlements that you are legally required to pay to a genuine employee.

The sub-limit and excess shown in the schedule for this endorsement apply. The sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability of the clause it extends.

Schedule variables: sub-limit; excess.
