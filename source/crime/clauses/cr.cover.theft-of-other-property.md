heading: Theft of Other Property
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We will pay your loss of property caused by theft by a third party from: (a) your premises; or (b) while in transit by you, your employees, or a professional carrier. The loss is measured at the lesser of: (i) the replacement cost of equivalent property; or (ii) the actual cash value at the time of loss. The loss must be first discovered during the policy period.
