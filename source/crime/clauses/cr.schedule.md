(Completed separately for each policy, with your specific details)

The schedule shows:

- Who is insured (the named insured and any subsidiary)
- The policy period (start and end dates)
- The retroactive date (losses occurring on or after this date; see [[ref:cr.def.retroactive-date]])
- The limit of liability, per loss and aggregate, by insuring clause, and any overall aggregate
- The excess, per loss, by insuring clause
- The discovery period after non-renewal or cancellation (minimum 90 days; see [[ref:cr.cond.discovery-period]])
- Any endorsements
- The premium (including stamp duty and GST)
- The governing law (the Australian state or territory), fixed as at the start of the policy period (see [[ref:cr.cond.governing-law]])
