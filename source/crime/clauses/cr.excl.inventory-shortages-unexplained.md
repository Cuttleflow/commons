type: exclusion
heading: Inventory Shortages — Unexplained
legacy_number: '4.3'
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Loss consisting of unexplained shortages in inventory, stock, or accounts that cannot be attributed to a specific criminal act. General stock shrinkage or bookkeeping errors are not covered. However, inventory shortages that are demonstrated by forensic investigation to result from employee theft are covered under [[ref:cr.cover.employee-dishonesty]].
