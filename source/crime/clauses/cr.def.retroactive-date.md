type: definition
heading: Retroactive date
since: 2.0.0
changed: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

means the date shown in the schedule. We only cover losses arising from criminal acts that first occur on or after this date. For a subsidiary covered under [[ref:cr.ext.newly-acquired-subsidiaries]], the retroactive date is the date it became a subsidiary.
