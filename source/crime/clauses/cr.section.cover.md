We cover the insuring clauses shown as included in the schedule. Each insuring clause has its own limit of liability and excess, shown in the schedule. The employee dishonesty and theft of money and securities clauses are always included. The computer crime, funds transfer fraud and social engineering fraud clauses are included only where the schedule shows them. The forgery and alteration clause and the theft of other property clause are included where the schedule shows them and carry no separate premium in the Go template.
