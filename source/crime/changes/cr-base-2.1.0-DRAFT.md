Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026.

- Social Engineering Fraud, E01 Increased Social Engineering Sub-limit and E10 Invoice Manipulation Fraud: verification is now treated one way. It is not a condition of cover and does not reduce a covered payment. In E01 and E10 it limits only the increased sub-limit: where the verification procedure was not followed, cover applies at the sub-limit that would apply without the endorsement.
- Maintain Controls: states that dual authorisation and other verification steps are not a condition of cover for social engineering or funds transfer fraud.
- Newly Acquired Subsidiaries: now has a size limit (total assets not above 25% of the named insured's consolidated total assets), a duty to notify within 90 days, cover only for criminal acts on or after the date the entity became a subsidiary, and a stated outcome after 90 days. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Subsidiary and Retroactive Date: an entity is a subsidiary only from the date it became one, and for a newly acquired subsidiary the retroactive date is that date.
- Duty of Disclosure: the insureds are now stated to be the named insured and each subsidiary. Disclosure is assessed separately for each insured, and company knowledge is the knowledge of the control group.
- Control group: a new definition.
- War, Terrorism, and Nuclear Risks: war, terrorism and nuclear risks are now defined.
- GST: the reference to section 48 of the GST Act is removed. The gross-up for GST on third-party claims is limited to Client's Property.
- Other Insurance: replaced with the library's standard text and one crime priority rule. Crime insurance is primary for loss of money, securities or property through social engineering fraud or funds transfer fraud, including computer-enabled theft of them, and a cyber policy pays only the excess over it.
- Sanctions Limitation, Cancellation and Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): replaced with the library's standard text. The Insurance Contracts Act clause is now headed Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).
- Endorsements E01 to E10 and Maintain Controls: repeated words in cross-references are corrected.
