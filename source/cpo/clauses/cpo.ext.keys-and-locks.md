since: 1.0.0

If a key, card or access device for the building that you hold is stolen, we pay the reasonable cost of replacing it and of changing or recoding the locks or access system it opens, up to $5,000 for any one event, or the amount shown in the schedule. This amount is in addition to the building sum insured.
