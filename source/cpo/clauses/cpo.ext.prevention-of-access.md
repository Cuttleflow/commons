heading: Prevention of Access
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We pay the rent you lose under [[ref:cpo.cover.loss-of-rent]] where tenants cannot get to the building, or a public authority prevents access to it, because of damage to property within 1 kilometre of the building that would be covered by this policy if it were the building. The most we pay under this extension for any one event is 10% of the loss of rent sum insured, within that sum insured.
