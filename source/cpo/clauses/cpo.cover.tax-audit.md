heading: Professional Fees for a Tax Audit
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If, during the policy period, the Australian Taxation Office or a state revenue office first notifies you of an audit, review or investigation of your tax affairs relating to the income from the building, we will pay the reasonable fees of an accountant, tax agent or lawyer you appoint, with our consent, to deal with it.

The most we pay for all audits first notified during the policy period is $10,000, or the limit shown in the schedule.
