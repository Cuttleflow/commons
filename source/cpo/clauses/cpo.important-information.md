**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

**Who this policy is for.** This wording is written for owners of commercial investment property who buy it as a business. Most will be wholesale clients under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). Where a building includes a home or residential tenancy, the buyer may be a retail client. Where a buyer is a retail client, the issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.

[WP REVIEW: confirm whether a policy over a mixed-use building with residential tenancies is a home building contract under reg 7.1.12 of the Corporations Regulations 2001 (Cth), and so a retail product for an individual owner.]

**Who is insured.** You are the owner named in the schedule. Tenants are not insured under this policy.

**Your duty when you apply.** Before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated, you have the duty of disclosure in s.21 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). You must tell us every matter you know, or that a reasonable person in your circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we waive. If you do not comply, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

If this policy is a consumer insurance contract under that Act, you instead have a duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us, and the Act sets out what we may do if you do not.

**Your tenants matter.** The use each tenant makes of the building is central to our decision. Tell us each tenancy's occupation and its share of the floor area, every vacant area, and any tenant who cooks with deep fryers, spray paints, does hot work, stores flammable goods or trades late with a liquor licence. Tell us during the policy period when a tenancy changes, as [[ref:cpo.obl.tell-us-about-changes]] requires.

**Defects and building works.** Tell us about any defect you know of in the building, any order or notice about the building from a council, a building regulator or a fire authority, any combustible cladding, and any building works under way or planned. We consider these matters individually; telling us does not by itself mean we will refuse cover.

**Under-insurance.** [[ref:cpo.pay.average]] reduces a property claim if the building sum insured is less than 80% of the building's full reinstatement value. Keep the sum insured up to date.

**Cooling-off period.** You may cancel this policy within 21 days of the date it starts, and within 21 days of each renewal, by telling us in writing. If you have not made a claim, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover. After that, you may still cancel under [[ref:cpo.cond.cancellation]].

**Commission and fees.** The schedule or the quote shows any commission or fee paid to your broker in connection with this policy.

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
