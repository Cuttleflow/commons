---
id: cpo.excl.glass
type: exclusion
heading: Glass Exclusions
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for:

- glass that a tenant must insure or replace under its lease, unless the tenant's insurance does not respond, in which case we pay as if this exclusion did not apply;
- glass that was cracked, chipped or scratched before the breakage;
- breakage during building works, or while the glass is being installed, removed or worked on;
- glass in a part of the building that has been unoccupied for more than 90 consecutive days.
