heading: Glass Breakage
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We will pay for the breakage during the policy period of fixed glass in the building, including mirrors, glass shower screens and glass in doors, windows, skylights and display cases that form part of the building. We also pay the reasonable cost of boarding up, of replacing signwriting, film or alarm tape on the glass, and of repairing the frames, up to $5,000 for any one event.

The most we pay for any one event under this section is the cost of replacing the glass.
