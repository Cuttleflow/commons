This policy insures the owner of a commercial investment property. It covers the owner's building and the owner's interest in it as a landlord. It does not insure the tenants' own property or the tenants' businesses, which the tenants insure themselves.

It has six sections. The sections that apply are shown in the schedule.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Property | Damage to the building and to your contents in it | Damage during the policy period |
| Loss of Rent | Rent you lose because covered damage makes a tenancy unusable | Damage during the policy period |
| Theft | Theft of your property from the building after forcible entry | Theft during the policy period |
| Glass | Breakage of fixed glass in the building | Breakage during the policy period |
| Property Owners' Liability | Your legal liability as owner of the building | Occurrence during the policy period |
| Tax Audit | Professional fees for a tax audit of your property income | Audits first notified during the policy period |

The flood cover in [[ref:cpo.end.e01]] applies only if the schedule shows it.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:cpo.section.definitions]].
