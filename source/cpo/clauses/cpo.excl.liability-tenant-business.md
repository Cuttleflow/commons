---
id: cpo.excl.liability-tenant-business
type: exclusion
heading: Tenants' Businesses
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability arising from a business, trade or activity carried on by a tenant, or from goods a tenant sells or supplies. This exclusion does not apply to your liability as owner of the building for its condition, or for the common areas.
