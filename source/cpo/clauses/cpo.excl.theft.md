---
id: cpo.excl.theft
type: exclusion
heading: Theft Exclusions
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for:

- theft by you, a tenant, or a person employed by or acting for either of you;
- theft from a tenancy, or from a common area open to the public at the time;
- property that belongs to a tenant;
- loss discovered only by taking an inventory.
