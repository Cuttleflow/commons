---
id: cpo.excl.tax-audit
type: exclusion
heading: Tax Audit Exclusions
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for:

- tax, interest, penalties or fines;
- fees for preparing a return or other work you would have needed in any case;
- an audit that results from fraud, a false statement or a deliberate failure to lodge by you or someone acting for you;
- an audit of which you were notified before the policy period, or which you knew was likely when this policy was entered into.
