id: cpo.excl.property-other
type: exclusion
heading: Property Not Covered and Other Causes
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for:

- land, or the cost of stabilising or improving land;
- property that belongs to a tenant, including a tenant's fit-out, stock and plant;
- money;
- vehicles requiring registration, aircraft and watercraft;
- electronic data, or its loss, corruption or reconstruction;
- loss of market value, or any loss of rent or extra expense except as [[ref:cpo.section.loss-of-rent]] provides;
- damage caused by the deliberate act of you, or of a person acting with your authority;
- damage caused by a lawful order or action of a government or public authority, other than to prevent the spread of fire or other damage;
- damage to property while it is being worked on, if the damage results from that work, but we do pay for resulting fire or explosion.
