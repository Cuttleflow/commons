---
id: cpo.excl.building-works
type: exclusion
heading: Building Works
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

While building works are in progress, we do not pay for damage to the part of the building where the works are being done, or to the works themselves, unless we agreed in writing to cover them, for example by [[ref:cpo.end.e03]]. We do pay for damage to the rest of the building.
