---
id: cpo.excl.loss-of-rent
type: exclusion
heading: Loss of Rent Exclusions
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for:

- rent a tenant fails to pay for any reason other than damage covered by this policy, including the tenant's insolvency or default;
- rent for any period after a lease ends, unless the lease would have been renewed or a new lease had been agreed in writing before the damage;
- loss caused by a delay in repairing or rebuilding because of a lack of funds, or because you changed or improved the building;
- penalties, fines or damages you must pay under a lease or any other contract.
