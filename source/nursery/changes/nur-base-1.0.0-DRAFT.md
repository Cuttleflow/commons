This is the first release of the Meridian Nursery and Garden Package wording. It is a new wording, drafted for the Commons for Australian production nurseries, turf growers, flower growers and garden centres. Its structure follows the Commons Farm Pack wording, with plant stock insured as two classes of property. Its liability section follows the Farm Pack and Public and Products Liability wordings, and its general conditions follow the Farm Pack wording.

- Seven sections: Property and Public and Products Liability, which always apply, and Deterioration of Stock, Business Interruption, Theft, Machinery Breakdown and Electronic Equipment and Transit, which apply if the schedule shows them.
- Property is one sum insured shared between six classes: buildings, contents and equipment, shade structures, glasshouses, plants under cover and plants in the open. Plant stock ready for sale is valued at its wholesale price; plant stock not yet ready at the cost of growing it to the same stage, capped at its wholesale price; bought-in stock at its replacement cost.
- Storm and hail damage to plants in the open is covered by E03, which applies whenever plant stock is held in the open. Accidental breakage of glazing is covered by E02. Film coverings over 4 years old and shade cloth over 5 years old are settled at market value.
- Deterioration of Stock covers plant stock killed or damaged by the breakdown of a climate control system or a public supply failure of more than 4 hours, with the alarm, backup power and servicing conditions in E04.
- Frost, drought, heatwave, pests and plant diseases are not insured events. Plant stock destroyed by order under a biosecurity law is covered only by E05, a broker option, net of any compensation.
- Liability covers retail customers, delivery and placement, ground spraying of your own stock and fire spreading from the situation. Landscaping (E09), chemical application for others (E08), open days (E10) and own-brand growing media (E06) are covered only by endorsement. Selling a prohibited plant is excluded unless you could not reasonably have known.
- Flood is excluded unless E01 is attached. An emergency warning in force at the start of cover suspends bushfire and flood cover for 72 hours.
- Endorsements E01 to E10 each carry an Addendum B trigger read from 38 qs.nur platform questions.
- General conditions: an Insurance Contracts Act savings clause applying s.54 to every condition, including endorsement conditions; a standalone sanctions condition; and governing law fixed to the main state or territory at the start of the policy period.
