We may repair, rebuild or replace the damaged property, or pay you what it would cost us to do so.

| Property | How we settle |
|---|---|
| Buildings | The cost of repairing or rebuilding to a condition substantially the same as, but not better than, when new, including the extra cost of complying with current building laws |
| Glasshouses | The cost of repairing or rebuilding to a condition substantially the same as, but not better than, when new. For a plastic film covering more than 4 years old, its market value at the time of the damage, allowing for its age |
| Shade structures | The cost of repairing or rebuilding the frame to a condition substantially the same as, but not better than, when new. For shade cloth, netting or mesh more than 5 years old, its market value at the time of the damage, allowing for its age |
| Contents and equipment | The cost of repair, or of replacement with new property of the same kind and capacity. For an item more than 10 years old, its market value at the time of the damage |
| Plant stock | As [[ref:nur.prp.plant-stock]] sets out |

If you do not repair or rebuild a building, a glasshouse or a shade structure within a reasonable time, we pay its market value at the time of the damage.
