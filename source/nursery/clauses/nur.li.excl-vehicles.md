---
id: nur.li.excl-vehicles
type: exclusion
heading: Vehicles, Aircraft and Watercraft
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability arising from the ownership or use of a vehicle while it is on a public road, or for liability that is required by law to be insured under a compulsory third party scheme. We do not pay for liability arising from the ownership or use of an aircraft, a drone weighing more than 2 kilograms or a watercraft more than 8 metres long, or from aerial spraying.
