[[ref:nur.li.excl-events]] is changed so that [[ref:nur.section.liability|heading]] covers your liability arising from open days, workshops, classes, garden tours and similar events you organise for the public at the situation.

This endorsement does not cover an event with more people at one time than the largest attendance shown in the schedule, or amusement rides, inflatable devices, animal rides, petting zoos, fireworks, or the sale or supply of alcohol. Food that is not pre-packaged must be prepared and sold by a business that holds the registration or licence the law requires. If a condition of this endorsement was not met, [[ref:nur.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies.

Schedule variables: largest attendance (default: 300 people at one time).
