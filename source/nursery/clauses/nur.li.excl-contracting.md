id: nur.li.excl-contracting
type: exclusion
heading: Landscaping and Work for Others
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability arising from:

- landscaping, planting, laying turf, installing irrigation or other work you do for others at their premises, other than delivering plant stock and placing it where the customer asks, unless the schedule shows [[ref:nur.end.e09]]; or
- applying chemicals for others for reward, unless the schedule shows [[ref:nur.end.e08]].
