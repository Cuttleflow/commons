In addition to [[ref:nur.section.general-exclusions]], under [[ref:nur.section.property|heading]] we do not pay for:

- flood, unless the schedule shows [[ref:nur.end.e01]];
- storm, wind, rain and hail damage to plants in the open, unless the schedule shows [[ref:nur.end.e03]];
- theft (see [[ref:nur.section.theft|heading]]);
- breakdown (see [[ref:nur.section.machinery|heading]]);
- death of or damage to plant stock caused by the breakdown or failure of a climate control system, or by the failure of a public supply of electricity, water or gas, unless an insured event at the situation caused the breakdown or failure (see [[ref:nur.section.deterioration|heading]]);
- weather that is not an insured event, such as frost, cold, heatwave, sunburn and drought, and a lack or excess of light, water or nutrients, other than water escaping as [[ref:nur.prp.insured-events]] describes;
- pests, plant diseases, weeds, fungi, insects and vermin, and the cost of treating or destroying plant stock because of them;
- damage to plant stock caused by applying chemicals, fertiliser, water or growing media to it, including using the wrong product, rate or method;
- plant stock that dies or deteriorates through natural growth or ageing, or fails to grow;
- vehicles, land, soil, dams, water in dams, and trees and plants that are not plant stock;
- property while it is being carried by road (see [[ref:nur.section.transit|heading]]);
- wear and tear, rust, corrosion, rot, mould and gradual deterioration;
- erosion, subsidence, landslip, shrinkage or expansion of the ground, unless directly caused by an insured event; or
- damage caused by water entering a building or a glasshouse through an opening that was not made by the storm or by an insured event.
