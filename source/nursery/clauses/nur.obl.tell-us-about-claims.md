heading: Tell Us About Claims
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after damage, an occurrence or a loss that may lead to a claim, and tell the police promptly about any theft or malicious damage.

Give us the information and documents we reasonably ask for to assess the claim, at your own cost, including sales, stock and propagation records, the records of a climate control system and its alarms, and the spray records the law requires you to keep. Allow us to inspect damaged property and plant stock before it is repaired or disposed of, unless that is not safe or practicable, the plant stock must be destroyed under a biosecurity law, or repairs are needed urgently to prevent further damage.
