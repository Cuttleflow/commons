---
id: nur.excl.government-orders
type: exclusion
heading: Government and Biosecurity Orders
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for any loss, damage or cost arising from the seizure, destruction, quarantine or restriction of property or plant stock by order of a government or public authority, including an order or direction under a biosecurity law, other than destruction ordered to stop the spread of a fire, and except as [[ref:nur.end.e05]] provides where the schedule shows it.
