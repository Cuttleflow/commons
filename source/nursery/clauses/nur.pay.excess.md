You pay the excess shown in the schedule for each claim, for the section that applies. An endorsement may set a special excess for the matter it describes; that excess replaces the section's excess for that matter.

**One event, one excess.** If one event gives rise to claims under more than one section of this policy, we deduct only the highest excess that applies. A hailstorm that damages glasshouses, shade structures and plant stock is one event.

No excess applies to the firefighting costs in [[ref:nur.prp.additional-benefits]].
