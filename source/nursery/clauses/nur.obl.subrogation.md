heading: Recovery from Others
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

After we pay a claim, we may take over your rights to recover the amount from anyone else, and act in your name. Help us do so, at our cost.

Where a lease of the situation that you made before the loss releases another party from liability, that does not affect your cover, and we will not exercise any right of recovery the lease has released.
