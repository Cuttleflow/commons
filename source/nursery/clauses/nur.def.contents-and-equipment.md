heading: Contents and equipment
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Property used in the nursery business that you own or are responsible for, other than buildings, glasshouses, shade structures, plant stock, money and vehicles, including tools, benches, empty pots, trays and containers, potting and seeding machines, forklifts and other machinery, potting mix, soil, seed, fertiliser and chemicals, garden supplies held for sale, and office and shop equipment.
