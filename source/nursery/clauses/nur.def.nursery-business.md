The main activity shown in the schedule, carried on at the situation, and these incidental activities: selling plant stock, potting mix, soil and garden supplies by wholesale and retail, at the situation and at markets, shows and field days; delivering plant stock and placing it where the customer asks; and the activities that [[ref:nur.end.e08]], [[ref:nur.end.e09]] and [[ref:nur.end.e10]] cover where the schedule shows them.
