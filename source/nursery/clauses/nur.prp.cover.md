We will pay for damage to buildings, contents and equipment, shade structures, glasshouses and plant stock at the situation, caused by an insured event during the policy period.

The most we pay for one event is the property sum insured shown in the schedule, plus the additional benefits in [[ref:nur.prp.additional-benefits]]. The schedule shows each class of property's share of that sum insured: buildings, contents and equipment, shade structures, glasshouses, plants under cover and plants in the open. The share is the most we pay for that class in one event, unless the schedule shows a separate sum insured for an item.
