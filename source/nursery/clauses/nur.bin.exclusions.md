In addition to [[ref:nur.section.general-exclusions]], under [[ref:nur.section.interruption|heading]] we do not pay for:

- loss caused by flood, unless the schedule shows [[ref:nur.end.e01]];
- loss caused by drought, water restrictions, frost or other weather that is not an insured event, pests, plant diseases, an order or direction under a biosecurity law, quarantine, a fall in market prices, or the loss of a market or a contract;
- loss after the indemnity period ends;
- loss caused by damage that is not covered by [[ref:nur.section.property|heading]]; or
- fines, penalties or damages you must pay for not completing a contract.
