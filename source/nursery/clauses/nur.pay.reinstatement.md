heading: Reinstatement of Sums Insured
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

After we pay a claim, the sum insured or limit is reinstated from the date of the damage for the rest of the policy period, without extra premium, except after a total loss of an item that is not replaced, and after a claim under [[ref:nur.section.theft|heading]] or the products limit in [[ref:nur.cover.liability]].
