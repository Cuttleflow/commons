If damage covered under [[ref:nur.section.property|heading]] interrupts the nursery business during the policy period, we will pay during the indemnity period:

- the loss of gross profit caused by the reduction in turnover, as [[ref:nur.bin.calculation]] sets out; and
- the increased cost of working: the extra costs you reasonably incur to reduce the reduction in turnover, such as buying in plant stock to supply your customers, hiring glasshouse or shade house space, and moving to temporary premises, up to the loss of gross profit they avoid.

The most we pay for all claims arising from one event is the gross profit sum insured shown in the schedule. The indemnity period is 12 months unless the schedule shows otherwise.
