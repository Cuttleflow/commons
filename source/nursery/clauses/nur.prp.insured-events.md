| Insured event | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Fire | Fire, including bushfire, lightning and explosion |
| Storm | Storm, wind, rain, hail and snow. Flood is not covered unless the schedule shows [[ref:nur.end.e01]]. Storm damage to plants in the open is covered only as [[ref:nur.end.e03]] provides |
| Earthquake | Earthquake, tsunami and volcanic eruption |
| Impact | Impact by a vehicle, an aircraft or something dropped from it, a falling tree or branch, or an animal that is not yours |
| Malicious damage | Malicious damage and vandalism by someone other than you or an employee, other than theft (see [[ref:nur.section.theft|heading]]) |
| Riot | Riot, civil commotion and the actions of people taking part in industrial action |
| Escape of water | Water or liquid escaping from a tank, pipe, irrigation system or fixed apparatus because it bursts, leaks or overflows, including the cost of replacing water you lose, up to $2,000 |
| Fusion | The burning out of an electric motor of up to 10 years old by an electric current, including the motor of a pump, a fan or a climate control system |
