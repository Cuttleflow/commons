---
id: nur.cond.emergency-warnings
type: condition
heading: Emergency Warnings at the Start of Cover
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If, when this policy or an increase in a sum insured is first entered into, an emergency warning, a watch and act warning or an evacuation order for a bushfire or a flood is in force for the area that includes the situation, we do not pay for damage by that bushfire or flood caused during the first 72 hours of the cover or the increase.

This condition does not apply where this policy replaces, without a break, a policy that covered the same property for the same events, or where the situation was bought or leased within the previous 72 hours and the cover started when you took on the risk.
