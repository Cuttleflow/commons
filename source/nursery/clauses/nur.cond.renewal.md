Each renewal is a new contract. Before this policy ends, we will tell you whether we offer to renew it and on what terms, as s.58 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) requires. We are not obliged to offer renewal. At each renewal, give us the current main activity of the nursery business, the sums insured and each class of property's share, and any of the changes that [[ref:nur.obl.tell-us-about-changes]] lists.
