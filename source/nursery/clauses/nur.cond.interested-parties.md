heading: Interested Parties
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Where the schedule shows a financier or another interested party, its interest is noted on this policy. Noting an interest does not make the interested party an insured and does not increase any sum insured. When we pay a claim for property in which an interested party has an interest, we pay the interested party to the extent of its interest and pay you, or as you direct, for the rest.
