since: 1.0.0

We will pay for theft of contents and equipment and plant stock from the situation during the policy period, and for damage to buildings, glasshouses and shade structures caused during the theft or an attempted theft.

The most we pay for all theft in the policy period is $25,000, or the theft limit the schedule shows. The most we pay for any one item is $10,000 unless the schedule shows the item with its value.

[[ref:nur.prp.basis-of-settlement]] decides how we settle.
