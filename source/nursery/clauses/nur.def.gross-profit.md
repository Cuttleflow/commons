---
id: nur.def.gross-profit
type: definition
heading: Gross profit
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The turnover of the nursery business, plus the value of closing stock, less the value of opening stock and less the cost of purchases of plant stock, seed, cuttings, growing media, pots and other goods for resale or for growing. Stock is valued as your accounts value it.
