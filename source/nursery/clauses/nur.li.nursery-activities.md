This section covers liability arising from:

- selling plant stock, potting mix, soil, mulch and garden supplies by wholesale and retail, subject to [[ref:nur.end.e06]] and [[ref:nur.end.e07]] where the schedule shows them;
- delivering plant stock and placing it where the customer asks at the customer's premises;
- applying chemicals by ground equipment to your own plant stock and premises, by you or an employee;
- selling at markets, shows and field days, and showing plant stock at them; and
- the use of forklifts and other machinery that is not a vehicle as a tool of trade at the situation, or on a road while moving between parts of the situation, where the liability is not required by law to be insured under a compulsory third party scheme.
