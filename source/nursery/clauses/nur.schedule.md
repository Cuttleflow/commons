(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- Your name and ABN, and your main state or territory of operation
- The situation, and the main activity of the nursery business
- The policy period, with its start and end dates
- The sections that apply, with the sum insured or limit and the excess for each
- The property sum insured, and the share of it for each class of property: buildings, contents and equipment, shade structures, glasshouses, plants under cover and plants in the open
- The deterioration limit under [[ref:nur.section.deterioration|heading]]
- The gross profit sum insured and the indemnity period
- The liability limit and the care, custody or control sub-limit
- The limits of [[ref:nur.section.theft|heading]], [[ref:nur.section.machinery|heading]] and [[ref:nur.section.transit|heading]]
- The limits of the additional benefits, where they differ from the defaults stated in them
- Any interested party, such as a financier
- The premium, including stamp duty, any emergency services levy, and GST
- Any commission or fee paid to your broker
- The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:nur.cond.governing-law]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
