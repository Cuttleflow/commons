This section covers your liability for personal injury or property damage caused by a fire that starts at the situation or in the activities of the nursery business and spreads, including a fire lit for burning off, a fire caused by a spark from machinery, and a fire caused by a fault in electrical equipment you own.

Where the law requires a permit to light a fire, or prohibits lighting it on a total fire ban day, you must hold the permit and comply with its conditions and that law. [[ref:nur.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to this requirement.
