heading: Prohibited Plants
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability arising from selling or supplying a plant whose sale or supply is prohibited by a biosecurity law, or by another law of the place where it was sold or delivered, unless you did not know and could not reasonably have known that it was prohibited.
