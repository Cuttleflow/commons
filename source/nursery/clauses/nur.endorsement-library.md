An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables shown for it in the schedule. Where an endorsement and the rest of this policy conflict, the endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. If a variable an endorsement needs is not shown in the schedule, the default stated in the endorsement applies; if it has no default, the endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the schedule.

We attach the endorsements that apply to the nursery business and the activities you tell us about. Each endorsement says what it changes. An endorsement that sets a condition does not let us refuse a claim because the condition was not met, except as [[ref:nur.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] allows.

| Code | Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Flood Cover | Covers flood under property and business interruption, with a flood sub-limit and a flood excess |
| E02 | Glasshouse Glazing | Covers accidental breakage of glazing and tearing of film coverings, with a sub-limit and a special excess |
| E03 | Plants in the Open | Covers storm and hail damage to plants in the open, with a sub-limit and a special excess |
| E04 | Deterioration of Stock Conditions | Sets alarm, backup power and servicing conditions for deterioration of stock cover |
| E05 | Biosecurity Destruction | Covers plant stock destroyed by order under a biosecurity law, with a sub-limit |
| E06 | Potting Mix and Soil Products | Sets labelling and records conditions for growing media you sell, and says whether own-brand products are covered |
| E07 | Retail Visitors | Sets conditions for areas open to customers, and a special excess for injury to customers |
| E08 | Chemical Application for Others | Covers applying chemicals for others by ground equipment, with conditions and a special excess |
| E09 | Incidental Landscaping and Installation | Covers incidental landscaping and installation work, with limits on the work and a special excess |
| E10 | Open Days and Workshops | Covers open days, workshops and events you organise, with conditions |
