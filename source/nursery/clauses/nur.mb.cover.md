We will pay for the repair or replacement of fixed machinery, climate control systems and electronic equipment at the situation that suffer breakdown during the policy period.

They include boilers and heaters, fans and evaporative coolers, misting, fogging and irrigation pumps and controllers, fertigation units, potting, seeding and transplanting machines, conveyors, cool rooms, generators, electric motors, computers and point-of-sale equipment.

We also pay for the deterioration of cut flowers and plant stock in a cool room caused by a breakdown we cover, up to $10,000 for one event.

The most we pay for one event is $50,000, or the machinery sum insured the schedule shows.
