heading: Theft Reporting
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Report a theft to the police within 48 hours of discovering it, and give us the police report number. For an item of contents and equipment worth more than $5,000, keep a record of its serial or identification number and give it to us when you claim. [[ref:nur.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to these requirements.
