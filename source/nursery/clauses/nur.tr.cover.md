We will pay for damage to plant stock and contents and equipment while being carried by road anywhere in Australia, including loading and unloading, in your vehicle or in a carrier's vehicle, caused during the policy period by:

- fire or explosion;
- collision or overturning of the vehicle;
- flood, storm or the collapse of a bridge or culvert; or
- theft of the whole load with the vehicle.

We value plant stock as [[ref:nur.prp.plant-stock]] sets out. The most we pay for any one load is $25,000, or the transit limit the schedule shows.
