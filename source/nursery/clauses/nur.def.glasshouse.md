id: nur.def.glasshouse
type: definition
heading: Glasshouse
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A glasshouse, greenhouse, polytunnel or polyhouse at the situation whose roof or walls are wholly or mainly glass, polycarbonate, acrylic or plastic film, including its frame, glazing, coverings and benches, and the parts of a climate control system fixed to it.
