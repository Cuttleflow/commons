We also pay the following, in addition to the property sum insured, up to the limits shown unless the schedule shows a different limit. Each benefit other than firefighting costs applies only after damage caused by an insured event.

| Benefit | What we pay | Limit for one event |
|---|---|---|
| Firefighting costs | The cost of fighting a fire that threatens or damages property at the situation, including water, fire retardant and hire of equipment. We pay firefighting costs even if no insured property is damaged | $25,000 |
| Removal of debris | The cost of removing debris, demolishing damaged property, and disposing of damaged plant stock and growing media | 10% of the property sum insured, up to $250,000 |
| Professional fees | Fees of architects, engineers and surveyors to repair or rebuild buildings and glasshouses | 10% of the claim for buildings and glasshouses |
| Temporary protection | The cost of temporary repairs and coverings to protect damaged property and plant stock, including a temporary covering for a glasshouse or a shade structure | $10,000 |
| Property temporarily removed | Damage to contents and equipment and plant stock you own while temporarily at a market, a show, a field day or other premises in Australia, other than while being carried by road | $10,000 |
| Newly acquired property | Buildings, glasshouses, shade structures and contents and equipment you acquire during the policy period, for 60 days or until you tell us, whichever is sooner | $150,000 |
| Rewards | A reward for information that leads to the conviction of a person who maliciously damaged insured property | $5,000 |
