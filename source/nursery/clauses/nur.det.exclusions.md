In addition to [[ref:nur.section.general-exclusions]], under [[ref:nur.section.deterioration|heading]] we do not pay for:

- the cost of repairing or replacing the climate control system itself (see [[ref:nur.section.machinery|heading]]);
- a planned interruption of a public supply that you were told about at least 24 hours before it began, or a supply cut off because you did not pay for it;
- frost, heatwave or other weather, where the climate control system worked as it was designed and set to work;
- pests and plant diseases;
- flood, unless the schedule shows [[ref:nur.end.e01]]; or
- a breakdown caused by a fault that you knew of before the breakdown and did not repair.
