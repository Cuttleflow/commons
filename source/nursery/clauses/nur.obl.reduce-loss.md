---
id: nur.obl.reduce-loss
type: obligation
heading: Prevent Further Loss
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Take reasonable steps to prevent further damage or loss, including restarting or repairing a climate control system, moving plant stock to shelter, and covering a damaged glasshouse or shade structure, where it is safe to do so. Keep damaged property and evidence, and photograph damaged plant stock before you dispose of it, so that we have a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. We pay the reasonable cost of doing so as part of the claim.
