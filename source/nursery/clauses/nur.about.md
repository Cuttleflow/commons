This policy insures a nursery business: its buildings, contents and equipment, shade structures, glasshouses and plant stock, its gross profit, and its legal liability to others. It is written for production nurseries growing under cover or outdoors, turf growers, flower growers and garden centres.

It has seven sections. [[ref:nur.section.property|heading]] and [[ref:nur.section.liability|heading]] always apply. The other sections apply only if the schedule shows them.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| [[ref:nur.section.property|heading]] | Damage to buildings, contents and equipment, shade structures, glasshouses and plant stock at the situation, caused by an insured event | Damage during the policy period |
| [[ref:nur.section.deterioration|heading]] | Death of or damage to plant stock caused by the breakdown of a climate control system or the failure of a public supply of electricity, water or gas | Event during the policy period |
| [[ref:nur.section.interruption|heading]] | Loss of gross profit and extra costs after damage covered by [[ref:nur.section.property|heading]] | Damage during the policy period |
| [[ref:nur.section.theft|heading]] | Theft of contents and equipment and plant stock from the situation | Theft during the policy period |
| [[ref:nur.section.liability|heading]] | Your legal liability for personal injury and property damage caused by the nursery business or your products | Occurrence during the policy period |
| [[ref:nur.section.machinery|heading]] | Breakdown of fixed machinery, climate control systems and electronic equipment | Breakdown during the policy period |
| [[ref:nur.section.transit|heading]] | Damage to plant stock and contents and equipment carried by road in Australia | Damage during the policy period |

A dwelling at the situation, registered vehicles, crops grown for their fruit, vegetables or other produce, and personal accident and illness cover for you are not part of this policy. Flood is not covered unless an endorsement says so. This policy does not provide workers compensation insurance.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:nur.section.definitions]].
