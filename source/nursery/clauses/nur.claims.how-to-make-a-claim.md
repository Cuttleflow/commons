heading: How to Make a Claim
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

1. Make people and property safe, restart or repair climate control systems, and protect plant stock, where it is safe to do so.
2. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
3. Tell the police about any theft or malicious damage, and get a report number.
4. Tell us what happened and when, and give us photographs, receipts, quotes, invoices and, for plant stock, your stock records and the price lists or invoices that show its value.
5. Do not dispose of damaged plant stock or property until we agree, unless that is needed for plant health, biosecurity or safety. If it is, photograph it first.
6. For a claim against you, send us the demand or court documents as soon as you receive them, and do not admit liability.
7. We will acknowledge your claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision as the General Insurance Code of Practice requires where the insurer subscribes to it.
