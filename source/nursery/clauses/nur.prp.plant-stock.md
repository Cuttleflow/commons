We pay for plant stock as follows:

- plant stock ready for sale: the wholesale price you would have received for it at the time of the damage, less the costs of selling and delivering it that you do not incur;
- plant stock not yet ready for sale: the cost of growing replacement plant stock to the stage of growth it had reached, including propagation material, growing media, containers, labour and a fair share of overheads, but not more than its wholesale price when ready for sale less the costs of growing it on that you do not incur; and
- plant stock you bought for resale and have not grown on, including a garden centre's plant stock held for retail sale: what it would cost you to buy plant stock of the same kind and size.

Where damaged plant stock recovers, or can be sold at a reduced price, we pay the reduction in its value.
