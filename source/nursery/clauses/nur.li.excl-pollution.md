---
id: nur.li.excl-pollution
type: exclusion
heading: Gradual Pollution
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability arising from the discharge, dispersal, release or escape of a pollutant, or the cost of removing or cleaning up a pollutant, unless the discharge, dispersal, release or escape is sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected, and happens at a specific time and place during the policy period. Spray drift from applying chemicals by ground equipment that happens at a specific time and place is sudden for the purposes of this exclusion.
