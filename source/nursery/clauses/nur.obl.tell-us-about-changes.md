---

Tell us during the policy period, as soon as reasonably practicable, if:

- the main activity of the nursery business changes;
- you start applying chemicals for others, landscaping or installation work, making or bagging potting mix or soil under your own brand, exporting live plants, or running open days, workshops or events for the public;
- an order or direction under a biosecurity law is made for the situation or your plant stock;
- you acquire or lease other premises, or stop using any of the situation; or
- a building or a glasshouse is left unused for more than 90 days.

We will tell you whether the change affects your cover or premium.
