This is the first release of the Meridian Farm Pack wording. It is a new wording, drafted for the Commons for Australian farm businesses: grazing, mixed farming, broadacre cropping, dairy, horticulture and viticulture, and hobby farms and lifestyle blocks. Its farm property, interruption, theft, machinery and transit sections follow the Commons Business Package wording, adapted for farms. Its liability section follows the Commons Public and Products Liability and Tradies Package wordings. Its general conditions follow the Tradies Package wording.

- Nine sections: Farm Property and Farm Liability, which always apply, and Livestock, Livestock Extended Cover, Farm Interruption, Theft, Machinery Breakdown and Electronic Equipment, Transit and Tax Audit, which apply if the schedule shows them.
- The farmhouse, cottages rented to workers or tenants, registered vehicles, accidental damage to self-propelled machinery and personal accident are insured under linked policies on their own wordings. One excess applies to one event across sections, and across a linked policy issued by the same insurer.
- Farm property covers farm buildings, farm contents, fencing and farm improvements, farm produce in storage and farm machinery for insured events, including bushfire and fusion of electric motors. Fencing is insured on a first loss basis. Farm produce gets a 25% seasonal increase for three months after harvest or shearing. Firefighting costs of $25,000 are paid even where no insured property is damaged, with no excess.
- Underinsurance applies only to farm buildings, at 80%, and only to claims over $20,000.
- Livestock is insured for death by fire, lightning, explosion, earthquake, electrocution, building collapse and impact. Livestock Extended Cover adds theft, attack by dogs or wild animals, accidental death including drowning, and working dogs.
- Farm Interruption pays loss of farm income and increased cost of working, including fodder and agistment when a bushfire destroys pasture, and lost agistment income.
- Farm Liability covers straying livestock, fire spreading from the farm, ground spraying, incidental contracting, farm gate sales and property in your care, including livestock. Pollution is covered only when sudden; spray drift at a specific time and place is sudden.
- Flood is excluded unless E01 is attached. An emergency warning in force at the start of cover suspends bushfire and flood cover for 72 hours.
- Endorsements E01 to E15 each carry an Addendum B trigger read from 47 qs.frm platform questions.
- General conditions: an Insurance Contracts Act savings clause applying s.54 to every condition, including endorsement conditions; a standalone sanctions condition; and governing law fixed to the main state or territory at the start of the policy period.
