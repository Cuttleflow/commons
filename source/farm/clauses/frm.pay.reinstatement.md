heading: Reinstatement of Sums Insured
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

After we pay a claim, the sum insured or limit is reinstated from the date of the damage for the rest of the policy period, without extra premium, except after a total loss of an item that is not replaced, after a claim under [[ref:frm.section.theft|heading]], [[ref:frm.section.tax-audit|heading]] or the products limit in [[ref:frm.cover.liability]], and except as [[ref:frm.lx.theft-limit]] provides.
