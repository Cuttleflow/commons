heading: One Loss, One Section
since: 1.0.0

---

A loss is paid under one section only, even if more than one section could apply. Where it could be paid under more than one, we pay it under the section that gives you the higher amount, except that:

- theft is paid under [[ref:frm.section.theft|heading]], or for livestock under [[ref:frm.section.livestock-extended|heading]];
- property and livestock being carried by road are paid under [[ref:frm.section.transit|heading]] where the schedule shows it; and
- a loss covered by a linked policy is paid under that policy where it insures the property, such as a vehicle under a linked motor policy or self-propelled machinery under a linked plant and equipment policy, and this policy pays only what that policy does not pay for property this policy insures.
