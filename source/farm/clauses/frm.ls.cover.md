We will pay for the death of livestock at the farm location during the policy period caused by:

- fire, including bushfire, lightning or explosion;
- earthquake;
- electrocution;
- the collapse of a farm building or structure; or
- impact by a vehicle or an aircraft, other than one you own or operate.

We also pay where a veterinary surgeon, or a person authorised by law to do so where no veterinary surgeon is available, destroys an animal for humane reasons because of an injury caused by one of these causes.

The most we pay for one event is the livestock sum insured shown in the schedule.
