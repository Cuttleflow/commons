We pay for the death or theft of a working dog from any cause, including being struck by a vehicle, snake bite and heat stroke while working, other than disease, old age, an act of yours, or a cause excluded by [[ref:frm.section.general-exclusions]], up to $3,000 for any one dog and $10,000 in the policy period. We also pay reasonable veterinary costs to treat a working dog injured by such a cause, up to $1,500 for any one dog, within those limits.
