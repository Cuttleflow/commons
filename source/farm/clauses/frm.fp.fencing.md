We pay for damage to fencing and other farm improvements by an insured event, including damage by livestock that are escaping a fire or a storm. Fencing is insured on a first loss basis: its share of the farm property sum insured is the most we pay for fencing in one event, and [[ref:frm.fp.underinsurance]] does not apply to it.

We also pay the reasonable cost of temporary fencing to keep livestock in while damaged fencing is repaired, up to $10,000 for one event.
