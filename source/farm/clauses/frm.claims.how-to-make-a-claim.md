since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

1. Make people, livestock and property safe, and prevent further damage where it is safe to do so.
2. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
3. Tell the police about any theft or malicious damage, and get a report number. For stolen livestock, do this within 7 days of discovering the theft.
4. Tell us what happened and when, and give us photographs, receipts, quotes, invoices and, for livestock, your head counts and identification records.
5. Do not dispose of dead livestock or damaged property until we agree, unless that is needed for animal welfare, biosecurity or safety. If it is, photograph it first.
6. For a claim against you, send us the demand or court documents as soon as you receive them, and do not admit liability.
7. We will acknowledge your claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision as the General Insurance Code of Practice requires where the insurer subscribes to it.
