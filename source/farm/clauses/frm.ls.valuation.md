We pay the market value of each animal at the farm gate at the time of its death, based on recent saleyard and over-the-hooks prices for animals of the same kind, age and condition in the region, less the costs of selling the animal that you do not incur. The most we pay for any one animal is $5,000, or the amount the schedule shows, unless the animal is specified under [[ref:frm.end.e08]].

[[ref:frm.fp.underinsurance]] does not apply to livestock.
