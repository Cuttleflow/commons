In addition to [[ref:frm.section.general-exclusions]], under [[ref:frm.section.livestock|heading]] we do not pay for death caused by or arising from:

- disease, illness, parasites, infection, poisoning or contamination, other than by smoke from a fire;
- drought, starvation, thirst, or exposure to heat or cold;
- pregnancy, giving birth, castration, mulesing, surgery or any other procedure;
- theft, straying, attack by an animal or accidental death, unless the schedule shows [[ref:frm.section.livestock-extended|heading]];
- livestock while being carried by road (see [[ref:frm.section.transit|heading]]);
- slaughter or destruction by order of a government or public authority, including under a biosecurity law; or
- livestock you are holding on agistment, unless the schedule shows [[ref:frm.end.e13]].
