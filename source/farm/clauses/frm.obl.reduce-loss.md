---
id: frm.obl.reduce-loss
type: obligation
heading: Prevent Further Loss
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Take reasonable steps to prevent further damage or loss, including moving livestock and machinery out of the path of a fire or a flood where it is safe to do so, and keep damaged property and evidence until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. We pay the reasonable cost of doing so as part of the claim.
