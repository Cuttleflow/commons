In addition to [[ref:frm.section.general-exclusions]], under [[ref:frm.section.farm-property|heading]] we do not pay for:

- flood, unless the schedule shows [[ref:frm.end.e01]];
- theft (see [[ref:frm.section.theft|heading]]);
- breakdown of machinery or electronic equipment (see [[ref:frm.section.machinery|heading]]);
- livestock (see [[ref:frm.section.livestock|heading]]);
- vehicles, and damage to farm machinery caused by its own operation, collision or overturning, or by the work it is doing;
- crops, pasture and trees while growing, land, soil, roads, dams, levees and water in dams, except as [[ref:frm.fp.additional-benefits]] provides;
- a fabric structure damaged by storm, unless the schedule shows [[ref:frm.end.e02]];
- property while it is being carried by road (see [[ref:frm.section.transit|heading]]);
- wear and tear, rust, corrosion, rot, mould, gradual deterioration, insects, vermin, and damage caused by livestock other than as [[ref:frm.fp.fencing]] provides;
- erosion, subsidence, landslip, shrinkage or expansion of the ground, unless directly caused by an insured event;
- damage caused by water entering a farm building through an opening that was not made by the storm or by an insured event; or
- farm produce damaged by heating, fermentation or moisture, other than spontaneous combustion.
