since: 1.0.0

---

The most we pay for theft of livestock in the policy period is 25% of the livestock sum insured, or the theft limit the schedule shows.

We pay for theft only where you report it to the police within 7 days of discovering it and give us the police report number. You must also give us the records you keep under the National Livestock Identification System or your state's brands and identification law that show the number of animals before and after the theft. Where those records cannot show the theft, we pay only for the animals your other reasonable evidence shows were stolen. [[ref:frm.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to these requirements.
