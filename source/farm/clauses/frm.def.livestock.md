---
id: frm.def.livestock
type: definition
heading: Livestock
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Sheep, cattle, goats, alpacas, llamas, pigs, deer, horses and poultry kept for the farm business at the farm location, and working dogs. It does not include animals kept only as pets, or animals under specified cover in [[ref:frm.end.e08]] except as that endorsement provides.
