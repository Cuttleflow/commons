since: 1.0.0

---

Tell us during the policy period, as soon as reasonably practicable, if:

- the farm business starts a new enterprise, or an enterprise's share of farm income changes by more than 20 percentage points;
- you start incidental contracting, aerial spraying, taking in livestock on agistment, having paying guests, or allowing visitors to ride horses;
- you acquire or lease more land, or stop farming any of the farm location; or
- a farm building is left unused for more than 90 days.

We will tell you whether the change affects your cover or premium.
