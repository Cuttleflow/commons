We also pay the following after damage covered by [[ref:frm.fp.cover]], in addition to the farm property sum insured, up to the limits shown unless the schedule shows a different limit:

| Benefit | What we pay | Limit for one event |
|---|---|---|
| Firefighting costs | The cost of fighting a fire that threatens or damages farm property, including water, fire retardant, hire of equipment and aircraft, and your own and your employees' labour at a reasonable rate. We pay firefighting costs even if no insured property is damaged | $25,000 |
| Removal of debris | The cost of removing debris, demolishing damaged property and disposing of dead livestock | 10% of the farm property sum insured, up to $250,000 |
| Professional fees | Fees of architects, engineers and surveyors to repair or rebuild farm buildings | 10% of the claim for farm buildings |
| Pasture and firebreaks | The cost of resowing pasture and restoring firebreaks and fire trails destroyed by a bushfire that also damaged insured property | $10,000 |
| Temporary protection | The cost of temporary repairs and protection of damaged property | $10,000 |
| Newly acquired property | Farm buildings, farm contents and farm machinery you acquire during the policy period, for 60 days or until you tell us, whichever is sooner | $150,000 |
| Rewards | A reward for information that leads to the conviction of a person who maliciously damaged insured property | $5,000 |
