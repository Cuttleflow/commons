heading: Theft of Farm Property
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We will pay for theft of farm contents, farm produce, farm machinery and farm improvements from the farm location during the policy period, and for damage caused during the theft or an attempted theft.

The most we pay for all theft in the policy period is $25,000, or the theft limit the schedule shows. The most we pay for any one item is $10,000 unless the schedule shows the item with its value.

[[ref:frm.fp.basis-of-settlement]] decides how we settle.
