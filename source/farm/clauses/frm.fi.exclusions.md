In addition to [[ref:frm.section.general-exclusions]], under [[ref:frm.section.farm-interruption|heading]] we do not pay for:

- loss caused by flood, unless the schedule shows [[ref:frm.end.e01]];
- loss caused by drought, disease in livestock or plants, a biosecurity order, quarantine, a fall in market prices, or the loss of a market or a contract;
- loss after the indemnity period ends;
- loss caused by damage that is not covered by [[ref:frm.section.farm-property|heading]] or [[ref:frm.section.livestock|heading]], other than the bushfire benefits in [[ref:frm.fi.cover]]; or
- fines, penalties or damages you must pay for not completing a contract.
