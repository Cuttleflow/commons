This policy insures a farm business: its farm buildings, farm contents, farm improvements, farm produce and farm machinery, its livestock, its farm income, and its legal liability to others. It is written for grazing, mixed farming, broadacre cropping, dairy, horticulture and viticulture enterprises, and for hobby farms and lifestyle blocks.

It has nine sections. [[ref:frm.section.farm-property|heading]] and [[ref:frm.section.liability|heading]] always apply. The other sections apply only if the schedule shows them.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| [[ref:frm.section.farm-property|heading]] | Damage to farm buildings, farm contents, farm improvements including fencing, farm produce in storage and farm machinery, caused by an insured event | Damage during the policy period |
| [[ref:frm.section.livestock|heading]] | Death of livestock caused by fire, lightning, explosion and the other listed causes | Death during the policy period |
| [[ref:frm.section.livestock-extended|heading]] | Theft of livestock, attack by dogs or wild animals, accidental death, and working dogs | Loss during the policy period |
| [[ref:frm.section.farm-interruption|heading]] | Loss of farm income and extra costs after insured damage, including lost agistment income | Damage during the policy period |
| [[ref:frm.section.theft|heading]] | Theft of farm contents, farm produce, farm machinery and farm improvements from the farm location | Theft during the policy period |
| [[ref:frm.section.liability|heading]] | Your legal liability for personal injury and property damage caused by the farm business or your products, including straying livestock and fire spreading from the farm | Occurrence during the policy period |
| [[ref:frm.section.machinery|heading]] | Breakdown of fixed farm machinery and electronic equipment, and spoilage of produce in cold storage | Breakdown during the policy period |
| [[ref:frm.section.transit|heading]] | Damage to farm produce, farm machinery and livestock carried by road in Australia | Damage during the policy period |
| [[ref:frm.section.tax-audit|heading]] | Professional fees for a tax audit of the farm business | Audits first notified during the policy period |

The farmhouse and other dwellings, cottages rented to employees or tenants, registered vehicles, accidental damage to high-value self-propelled machinery, and personal accident and illness cover for you are not part of this policy. They may be insured under separate policies, which the schedule may show as linked to this policy. Crop hail, multi-peril crop and livestock mortality insurance for stud animals are specialist covers and are not part of this policy unless an endorsement says so. This policy does not provide workers compensation insurance.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:frm.section.definitions]].
