We may repair, rebuild or replace the damaged property, or pay you what it would cost us to do so.

| Property | How we settle |
|---|---|
| Farm buildings | The cost of repairing or rebuilding to a condition substantially the same as, but not better than, when new, including the extra cost of complying with current building laws |
| Farm improvements, including fencing | The cost of repairing or replacing with similar materials to a condition substantially the same as, but not better than, when new |
| Farm contents | The cost of repair, or of replacement with new property of the same kind and capacity. For an item more than 10 years old, its market value at the time of the damage |
| Farm machinery | The cost of repair, or the market value of the machinery at the time of the damage if it is destroyed or its repair would cost more than its market value |
| Farm produce | As [[ref:frm.fp.produce]] sets out |

If you do not repair or rebuild a farm building within a reasonable time, we pay the market value of the building at the time of the damage.
