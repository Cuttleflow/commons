since: 1.0.0

---

Farm produce is insured at its market value at the farm location at the time of the damage, less the costs you would have incurred to sell it that you do not incur.

During the three months after the harvest or shearing of the farm business's main crop or clip, we increase the farm produce share of the farm property sum insured by 25% to allow for the larger quantity in storage. Spontaneous combustion of farm produce is covered as fire, subject to [[ref:frm.end.e07]] where the schedule shows it.
