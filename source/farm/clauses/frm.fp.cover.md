heading: Farm Property Cover
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We will pay for damage to farm buildings, farm contents, farm improvements, farm produce and farm machinery at the farm location, caused by an insured event during the policy period. Farm machinery is also covered while it is anywhere in Australia for the farm business.

The most we pay for one event is the farm property sum insured shown in the schedule, plus the additional benefits in [[ref:frm.fp.additional-benefits]]. The schedule shows each class of farm property's share of that sum insured, and the share is the most we pay for that class in one event, unless the schedule shows a separate sum insured for an item.
