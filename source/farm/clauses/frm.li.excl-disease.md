heading: Spread of Notifiable Disease
since: 1.0.0
status: draft

We do not pay for liability arising from the spread of a disease or pest that a biosecurity law of the Commonwealth, a state or a territory requires to be notified, from your livestock, crops or property, unless you did not know and could not reasonably have known that the livestock, crops or property carried it.
