Each renewal is a new contract. Before this policy ends, we will tell you whether we offer to renew it and on what terms, as s.58 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) requires. We are not obliged to offer renewal. At each renewal, give us the current enterprises and their shares of farm income, the farm area, the sums insured, and any change to contracting, spraying, agistment or visitors.
