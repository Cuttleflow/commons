An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables shown for it in the schedule. Where an endorsement and the rest of this policy conflict, the endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. If a variable an endorsement needs is not shown in the schedule, the default stated in the endorsement applies; if it has no default, the endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the schedule.

We attach the endorsements that apply to the enterprises and activities you tell us about. Each endorsement says what it changes. An endorsement that sets a condition does not let us refuse a claim because the condition was not met, except as [[ref:frm.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] allows.

| Code | Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Flood Cover | Covers flood under farm property, livestock and farm interruption, with a flood excess |
| E02 | Fabric Structures | Covers storm damage to fabric structures, with a sub-limit and a special excess |
| E03 | Farm Stays and Paying Guests | Covers liability to paying guests, with conditions |
| E04 | Horse Riding and Equestrian Activities | Covers liability for riding by visitors and for events you organise, with conditions |
| E05 | Incidental Contracting | Sets the contracting share and the terms of contracting cover |
| E06 | Ground Spraying Conditions | Sets conditions for spraying chemicals and a special excess for spray drift |
| E07 | Hay and Fodder Storage | Sets storage conditions and a special excess for spontaneous combustion |
| E08 | Stud and High-Value Animals | Insures specified animals at agreed values |
| E09 | Interested Party | Notes a financier's or another party's interest |
| E10 | Lloyd's Underwriters | Adds Lloyd's several liability, service of suit and complaints terms |
| E11 | Bushfire Special Excess | Sets a special excess for bushfire damage in high bushfire exposure areas |
| E12 | Farm Gate Sales and Markets | Sets food safety conditions for produce sold direct to the public |
| E13 | Agistment of Other Owners' Livestock | Covers livestock you take in on agistment |
| E14 | Northern Australia Cyclone | Sets cyclone terms for a farm location north of the 26th parallel |
| E15 | Firefighting Equipment | Records the firefighting equipment you keep during the fire danger season |
