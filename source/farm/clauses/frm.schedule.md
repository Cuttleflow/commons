(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- Your name and ABN, and your main state or territory of operation
- The farm location, its area in hectares, and the enterprises of the farm business with each one's share of farm income
- The policy period, with its start and end dates
- The sections that apply, with the sum insured or limit and the excess for each
- The farm property sum insured, and each class of farm property's share of it
- The livestock sum insured and the most we pay for any one animal, and the theft limit under [[ref:frm.section.livestock-extended|heading]]
- The farm income sum insured and the indemnity period
- The liability limit and the care, custody or control sub-limits
- The limits of [[ref:frm.section.theft|heading]], [[ref:frm.section.machinery|heading]], [[ref:frm.section.transit|heading]] and [[ref:frm.section.tax-audit|heading]]
- The limits of the additional benefits, where they differ from the defaults stated in them
- Any linked policy, by its policy number and the insurer that issued it
- Any interested party, such as a financier
- The premium, including stamp duty, any emergency services levy, and GST
- Any commission or fee paid to your broker
- The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:frm.cond.governing-law]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
