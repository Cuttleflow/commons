heading: Tell Us About Claims
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after damage, an occurrence or a loss that may lead to a claim, and tell the police promptly about any theft or malicious damage.

Give us the information and documents we reasonably ask for to assess the claim, at your own cost, including farm income records, livestock and production records, and records you keep under the National Livestock Identification System. Allow us to inspect the damaged property before it is repaired, and dead livestock before they are disposed of, unless that is not safe or practicable or repairs are needed urgently to prevent further damage.
