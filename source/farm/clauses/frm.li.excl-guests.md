type: exclusion
heading: Paying Guests and Riding
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability arising from:

- providing accommodation, meals, tours or activities to paying guests or visitors, unless the schedule shows [[ref:frm.end.e03]]; or
- horse riding by anyone other than you, your household and your employees, or from equestrian events, rodeos, campdrafts or races you organise, unless the schedule shows [[ref:frm.end.e04]].
