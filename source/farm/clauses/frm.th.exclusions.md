In addition to [[ref:frm.section.general-exclusions]], under [[ref:frm.section.theft|heading]] we do not pay for:

- theft of livestock (see [[ref:frm.section.livestock-extended|heading]]);
- theft by you, a member of your household, an employee or a sharefarmer, or with their knowledge or consent;
- property that cannot be accounted for and that you cannot show was stolen, including shortages found at stocktake;
- fuel taken from a tank, drum or machine that was not locked;
- money, and vehicles; or
- property left in the open away from the farm buildings and farm improvements, other than farm machinery, farm produce and farm improvements themselves, unless it was in a locked building or container.
