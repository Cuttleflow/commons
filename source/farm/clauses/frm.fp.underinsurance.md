This provision applies only to farm buildings, and only to a claim of more than $20,000. If, at the time of the damage, the farm buildings share of the farm property sum insured is less than 80% of the cost of rebuilding all the farm buildings, we pay only the proportion of the claim that the farm buildings share bears to 80% of that cost.

Worked example: the farm buildings share is $600,000; rebuilding all the farm buildings would cost $1,000,000; a fire causes $300,000 of damage to farm buildings. 80% of $1,000,000 is $800,000. We pay $600,000 divided by $800,000, times $300,000, which is $225,000, before the excess.
