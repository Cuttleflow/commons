We will pay for the repair or replacement of fixed farm machinery and electronic equipment at the farm location that suffers breakdown during the policy period. Breakdown means the sudden and accidental failure of the machine or equipment to work, caused by a mechanical or electrical fault, that needs repair or replacement before it can work again.

Fixed farm machinery and electronic equipment includes pumps, irrigation pivots and controllers, milking machines, milk vats, refrigeration, cool rooms, grain dryers, generators, electric motors, computers, and the electronic controllers of fixed installations.

We also pay for the deterioration of milk in a vat, or of farm produce in a cool room, caused by a breakdown we cover, up to $10,000 for one event.

The most we pay for one event is $50,000, or the machinery sum insured the schedule shows.
