We will pay for damage to farm produce, farm contents and farm machinery, and the death of livestock, while being carried by road anywhere in Australia, including loading and unloading, in your vehicle or in a carrier's vehicle, caused during the policy period by:

- fire or explosion;
- collision or overturning of the vehicle;
- flood, storm or the collapse of a bridge or culvert; or
- theft of the whole load with the vehicle.

For livestock, we pay their market value as [[ref:frm.ls.valuation]] sets out, including an animal destroyed for humane reasons because of an injury caused by one of these events, and an animal that dies while being unloaded at a saleyard or an abattoir.

The most we pay for any one load is $50,000, or the transit limit the schedule shows.
