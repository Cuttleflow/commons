In addition to [[ref:frm.section.general-exclusions]], under [[ref:frm.section.transit|heading]] we do not pay for:

- livestock that die from stress, suffocation, heat, crushing or trampling, overcrowding, disease or exhaustion, other than as a result of an event covered by [[ref:frm.tr.cover]];
- property carried for others for reward;
- damage caused by poor packing, loading or securing of the load;
- breakdown or failure of refrigeration; or
- delay, or loss of market.
