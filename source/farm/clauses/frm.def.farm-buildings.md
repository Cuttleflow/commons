---
id: frm.def.farm-buildings
type: definition
heading: Farm buildings
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Buildings and structures at the farm location used for the farm business, including machinery, hay, shearing and implement sheds, workshops, stables, dairies, silos, cool rooms, yards, loading ramps and their fixtures. It does not include a dwelling, a building used as a home, or land.
