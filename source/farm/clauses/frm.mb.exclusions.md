In addition to [[ref:frm.section.general-exclusions]], under [[ref:frm.section.machinery|heading]] we do not pay for:

- farm machinery that moves under its own power, and vehicles;
- wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion and scale, or the cost of routine maintenance, adjustment or overhaul;
- belts, tyres, hoses, blades, cutting edges, filters, fuses, lamps, batteries, lubricants and other parts that wear out or need regular replacement;
- breakdown during testing, or of equipment more than 20 years old;
- a fault you knew of before the breakdown and did not repair; or
- the cost of a repair that a manufacturer, a supplier or a repairer must pay for under a guarantee or a maintenance agreement.
