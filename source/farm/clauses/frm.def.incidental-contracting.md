---
id: frm.def.incidental-contracting
type: definition
heading: Incidental contracting
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Work the farm business does for other farmers or landholders for reward with its own farm machinery and employees, such as harvesting, hay making, ploughing, sowing, fencing, livestock handling and ground spraying, where the income from it is no more than the share of gross farm turnover shown in [[ref:frm.end.e05]].
