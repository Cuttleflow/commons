Under [[ref:frm.section.livestock-extended|heading]], [[ref:frm.ls.exclusions]] applies, other than its exclusion of theft, attack and accidental death, and other than to working dogs, which [[ref:frm.lx.working-dogs]] covers on its own terms. We do not pay for:

- livestock that cannot be accounted for and that you cannot show were stolen, including shortages found at mustering or counting;
- theft by you, a member of your household, an employee, a sharefarmer or a person on agistment with you;
- death from an attack by your own dogs, unless you did not know and could not reasonably have known that a dog was attacking livestock; or
- an animal more than 12 years old, other than a working dog or a horse.
