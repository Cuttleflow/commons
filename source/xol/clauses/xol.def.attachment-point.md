type: definition
heading: Attachment Point
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

The amount shown in the schedule as the attachment point. It is the total of the limits of the underlying insurance that apply to a loss, plus any gap excess shown in the schedule. It is fixed at the start of the policy period and does not change if any underlying insurance is reduced, cancelled, not renewed or not paid. [[ref:xol.cover.attachment-and-exhaustion]] says when it is reached.
