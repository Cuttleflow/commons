This is an excess of loss policy. It covers loss above the limits of the underlying insurance shown in the schedule, up to the limit of liability of this policy. It is not a standalone policy: it adopts the terms of the followed policy, except where this policy says otherwise.

This policy does not drop down. If the underlying insurance does not pay, for any reason, this policy still responds only above the attachment point. Drop-down cover for the insolvency of an underlying insurer, or for exhaustion of an underlying aggregate limit, is available only by endorsement.

This policy, together with your schedule and any endorsements shown in it, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read it together with the followed policy and the other underlying insurance, and tell us if anything in the schedule is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:xol.section.definitions]].

This wording is written for businesses. The insurer or distributor that issues it is responsible for deciding whether any purchaser is a retail client under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) and, if so, for issuing a Product Disclosure Statement and Target Market Determination.
