heading: Followed Policy
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

The policy named in the schedule as the followed policy, on the wording, version and terms in force at the start of the policy period. It is usually the primary policy. A renewal, replacement or change to it binds us only as [[ref:xol.cover.changes-to-the-followed-policy]] states.
