We will pay loss that is covered by the followed policy, to the extent that it exceeds the attachment point, up to the limit of liability.

Except where this policy states otherwise, this policy adopts the terms of the followed policy as they are at the start of the policy period, including its insuring clauses, definitions, exclusions, conditions, claims trigger, geographical limits and jurisdiction limits, subject to [[ref:xol.def.retroactive-date]]. Where the followed policy has a sub-limit for a particular loss, this policy does not cover that loss unless the schedule shows that this policy sits excess of that sub-limit.

Where this policy and the followed policy differ, this policy applies. This does not apply to a term of the followed policy that protects an insured from avoidance or reduction of cover, or from the imputation of another insured's knowledge or conduct. This policy always follows those terms, including a non-avoidance or severability promise, and [[ref:xol.cond.your-duty-of-disclosure]] and [[ref:xol.excl.how-the-exclusions-apply]] are read subject to them. Where a term of the followed policy cannot sensibly apply to an excess layer (for example a premium, excess, notification address or limit), the corresponding term of this policy applies instead.
