heading: Retroactive Date
since: 2.0.0
changed: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Where the followed policy is written on a claims-made basis, the retroactive date of the followed policy, unless the schedule shows a different retroactive date for this policy.

Where the schedule shows a later date, this policy does not cover any claim arising from an act, error, omission or matter that first happened before that later date, even if the followed policy covers it.

Where the schedule shows an earlier date than the retroactive date of the followed policy, the earlier date does not widen this policy. This policy covers only loss that the followed policy covers, so the retroactive date of the followed policy still applies.
