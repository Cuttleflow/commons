heading: Fines and Penalties
since: 2.0.0
changed: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Any fine, penalty, punitive, exemplary or aggravated damages, or civil pecuniary penalty, unless the followed policy covers it and the law allows it to be insured.

We do not pay any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured, whatever law governs this policy. In particular, we do not pay a penalty imposed under work health and safety law in a State or Territory whose law prohibits insurance of it (including New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland), and we do not pay a criminal fine. We do not cover any amount that it is unlawful for us to pay, including a penalty that statute prohibits a person from insuring, or a liability that [[ref:xol.cond.corporations-act-199]] describes.
