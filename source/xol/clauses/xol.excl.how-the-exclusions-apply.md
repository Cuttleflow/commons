type: exclusion
heading: How the Exclusions Apply
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Where the followed policy has a write-back, carve-back or sub-limit for a matter excluded above, that write-back, carve-back or sub-limit does not extend this policy unless the schedule shows that this policy sits excess of it. The conduct of one insured is not imputed to another insured for the purpose of these exclusions, except as the followed policy provides.
