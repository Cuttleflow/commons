If you are unhappy with a decision, please contact us. We will follow our internal dispute resolution process and try to resolve the matter within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied, you can refer the matter to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA): www.afca.org.au, info@afca.org.au, 1800 931 678. AFCA provides a free and independent dispute resolution service. Time limits may apply, and AFCA's rules decide whether it can hear a complaint about this policy.
