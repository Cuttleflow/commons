(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The named insured and ABN
- The policy period, start and end dates
- The followed policy: insurer, policy number, wording and version, period, and limit
- The underlying insurance: each policy in the tower below this policy, by insurer, policy number, layer limit, attachment and expiry date
- The attachment point, and any gap excess you bear
- The limit of liability of this policy, in the aggregate, and any limit for any one claim
- Our share of this layer, where this layer is shared between insurers, and the other insurers on the layer
- The retroactive date, where the followed policy is written on a claims-made basis and this policy states a different date
- The defence costs cap, where it differs from the cap in [[ref:xol.cover.defence-costs]]
- The premium, the minimum premium, stamp duty and GST
- The state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:xol.cond.governing-law]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
