This guide explains this policy. It does not change its terms.

An excess layer sits above other insurance. Each layer pays only after the layers below it have been paid in full. For example, where the followed policy has a limit of $5 million and this policy has a limit of liability of $10 million in excess of $5 million:

- for a loss of $8 million, the primary layer pays its full $5 million (less your primary excess, which you pay), and this policy pays $3 million;
- for a loss of $20 million, the primary layer pays $5 million, this policy pays $10 million, and the remaining $5 million is yours unless there is a further excess layer.

If the primary insurer agrees to settle for $4 million and you pay the other $1 million, the $5 million primary layer is treated as paid in full and this policy pays from that point.

If the primary insurer cannot pay, for example because it is insolvent, this policy does not move down to replace it unless your schedule shows a drop-down endorsement.
