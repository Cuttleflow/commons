The most we will pay for all loss under this policy during the policy period, including under any endorsement, is the limit of liability, unless [[ref:xol.end.e02]] applies. Where the schedule also shows a limit for any one claim, the most we will pay for any one claim is that limit, and the limit of liability still applies to all claims together.

Claims that the followed policy treats as one claim, or as related, are one claim under this policy, subject to the limit of liability and the policy period that the followed policy assigns to them.
