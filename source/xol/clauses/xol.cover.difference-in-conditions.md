heading: Narrower Followed Policy
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

This policy covers only loss that the followed policy covers. Where any other underlying insurance is broader than the followed policy, this policy does not follow the broader terms. Where any other underlying insurance is narrower than the followed policy, a loss falling within the gap reaches the attachment point only if the underlying limit of the narrower policy is paid by you or on your behalf, as [[ref:xol.cover.attachment-and-exhaustion]] provides.

This clause does not apply to the differences in cover specified in [[ref:xol.end.e01]] where that endorsement applies.
