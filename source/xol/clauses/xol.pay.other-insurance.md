This policy is written as a true excess layer above the underlying insurance. It is excess of, and does not contribute with, the underlying insurance. Where another policy that is not shown in the schedule covers the same loss in the same layer, we will contribute to that loss with the other insurer in the proportion that our limit of liability bears to the total limits for that layer, and nothing in this provision limits s.45 or s.76 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).
