This policy is written as a true excess layer above the underlying insurance named in the schedule. It is excess of, and does not contribute with, that underlying insurance, and pays only after the attachment point is reached.

If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy that is not shown in the schedule, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows. Where that other policy covers the same loss in the same layer, we contribute with the other insurer in the proportion that our limit of liability bears to the total limits for that layer.
