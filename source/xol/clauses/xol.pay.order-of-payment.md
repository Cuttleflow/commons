This policy is liable only after the attachment point has been reached, and then only for the part of a loss above it, up to the limit of liability. Any insurance above this policy responds only after the limit of liability has been paid. Where an underlying limit is restored or reinstated after a loss, the attachment point for later losses includes the restored or reinstated amount.
