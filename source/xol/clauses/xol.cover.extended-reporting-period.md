The limit of liability applies to claims first made during the policy period, or, where the followed policy responds to a loss rather than a claim, to loss first discovered during the policy period.

If the followed policy gives an extended reporting period or discovery period because it is cancelled or not renewed, and this policy is also cancelled or not renewed at the same time and you have not replaced it with other excess insurance, this policy follows that period for the same length of time. A claim first made in that period is treated as first made during the policy period. The limit of liability is not reinstated or increased: it applies once across the policy period and that period together.

In any other case, this policy does not cover a claim first made after the policy period ends, even if the followed policy covers it in an extended reporting period or discovery period.
