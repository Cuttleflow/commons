By you: you may cancel this policy at any time by telling us in writing. We will refund the premium for the unexpired policy period, less any non-refundable government charges, unless the premium is a minimum premium shown in the schedule as fully earned.

By us: we may cancel this policy only on grounds permitted by s.60 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), by giving you written notice as required by s.59 of that Act. We will refund the premium for the unexpired policy period proportionally.

The cancellation or expiry of the followed policy does not by itself cancel this policy.
