heading: Defence Costs

Defence costs are treated under this policy as they are under the followed policy. Where the followed policy pays defence costs within its limit, defence costs paid by us are within, and reduce, the limit of liability, unless [[ref:xol.end.e04]] applies. Where the followed policy pays defence costs in addition to its limit, we pay defence costs in the proportion that our payment of loss bears to the total loss, in addition to the limit of liability.

We will pay defence costs as they are incurred once the attachment point has been reached. Our consent to defence costs is needed only where the followed policy requires the consent of the primary insurer, and we will not unreasonably withhold or delay it.
