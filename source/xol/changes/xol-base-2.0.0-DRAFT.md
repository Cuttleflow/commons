This is the first Commons release of the Meridian Excess of Loss wording. It replaces version 1.0.0 (July 2026), which was published outside the Commons and was never converted to the Commons clause source. Coverage changes from version 1.0.0:

- Drop-down is no longer automatic. Version 1.0.0 dropped down for underlying insurer insolvency, aggregate exhaustion and cancellation of the underlying insurance. In this version the layer responds only above the attachment point. Drop-down for aggregate exhaustion is Endorsement E03, and drop-down for insolvency of an underlying insurer is the new Endorsement E09. Both refer to an underwriter. Drop-down on cancellation of the underlying insurance is removed.
- Exhaustion is now by payment. The attachment point is reached when the underlying limits are paid by the underlying insurers, by you, or by others on your behalf. The version 1.0.0 test of an underlying insurer having "confirmed it is liable to pay" is removed. A settlement below an underlying limit no longer stops this policy attaching if you pay the difference.
- The conflict between version 1.0.0's attachment clause (no drop-down for aggregate erosion) and its drop-down clause (drop-down for aggregate exhaustion) is removed.
- The layer now follows a single named Followed Policy as at the start of the policy period. Later changes to the underlying insurance bind the insurer only if agreed in writing. Renewal or replacement on terms no wider and at a limit not lower is followed automatically.
- Version 1.0.0's exclusion for underlying insurance not maintained is replaced by an obligation to maintain it. If it is not maintained, the policy still applies as if it had been, with the attachment point unchanged.
- Version 1.0.0's rule that this policy sits separately excess of each of several underlying policies is replaced by a single attachment point across the tower, and a Narrower Followed Policy clause.
- New provisions: Defence Costs (following the followed policy, with pro rata sharing where defence costs are in addition), Shared Layer, Order of Payment, Recoveries (top-down), Changes to the Followed Policy, and How the Exclusions Apply.
- The War, Terrorism and Nuclear exclusion keeps the version 1.0.0 position and now defines an act of terrorism. Terrorism cover in the followed policy is followed only where the schedule says so. Sanctions moves out of the exclusion into a standalone condition naming the regimes.
- The Fines and Penalties exclusion now also excludes any amount it is unlawful to insure.
- New general conditions: an express Insurance Contracts Act savings clause applying s.54 to every obligation, Jurisdiction, Assignment, and Headings and References. Governing law is fixed by the schedule rather than following the principal place of business. The duty of disclosure now states the non-consumer duty in s.21 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).
- The endorsement library now has nine endorsements, each with operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger. E09 Drop-down for Underlying Insurer Insolvency is new. Endorsement codes E01 to E08 keep their version 1.0.0 meanings.
- The licence is now CC BY 4.0, in line with the rest of the Commons (version 1.0.0 was CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Clause numbers are removed. Each clause is identified by its heading.
