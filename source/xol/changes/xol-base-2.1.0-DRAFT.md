Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026.

- Your Duty of Disclosure: now states that this is a business contract and cites section 21 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Company knowledge is the knowledge of its control group. Disclosure is assessed separately for each insured, and where the followed policy protects an insured from avoidance, reduction of cover or imputation of another insured's knowledge, this policy gives the same protection.
- Follow-form Excess Layer: the rule that this policy applies where it and the followed policy differ no longer overrides the followed policy's non-avoidance, severability or non-imputation terms.
- Control Group: a new definition.
- Fines and Penalties: insurability no longer depends only on the law that governs this policy. The clause names the work health and safety penalties that cannot be insured in the States and Territories that prohibit it, and criminal fines, and refers to the Corporations Act limits.
- Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), sections 199A and 199B: a new condition with the library's standard text.
- Defence Costs: where the followed policy pays defence costs in addition to its limit, our share is now capped at 50% of the limit of liability unless the schedule shows a different amount. Default set for underwriter confirmation. Defence costs in civil penalty and criminal proceedings are advanced and repayable on final adjudication.
- Retroactive Date: now states both cases. A later date in the schedule narrows cover. An earlier date does not widen it.
- Extended Reporting and Run-off: a new clause. This policy follows the followed policy's extended reporting period only where this policy also ends at the same time and is not replaced. In any other case, a claim first made after this policy ends is not covered. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), Sanctions, Other Insurance and Cancellation: replaced with the library's standard text.
- Governing Law: if the schedule shows no state or territory, the law of the followed policy applies, not the law of the principal place of business.
- About This Policy: the statement about retail clients now follows the library's standard text.
- Schedule: now shows the defence costs cap.
