This is the first release of the Meridian Inbound Visitor Travel wording. It is built on the Meridian Travel base wording version 1.1.0-DRAFT. It differs from the base in these ways:

- Who Is Covered: visitors who live outside Australia, are not Australian citizens or permanent residents, are not Medicare-eligible, hold a visa for the trip and are no older than 75. Home and the new Home Country definition point to the visitor's home country.
- When You Are Covered: the stay in Australia, up to 365 days, with a 72-hour wait for sickness when the policy is bought in Australia.
- Medical and Hospital Costs in Australia replaces the overseas medical section, with a fourth plan, Medical Only. Plan is redefined.
- What the Law Does Not Allow Us to Pay is restated for visitors who are not Medicare-eligible, with a WP REVIEW tag on rule 17 of the Private Health Insurance (Health Insurance Business) Rules 2018 (Cth). About This Policy and Important Information say the policy is not overseas visitors or overseas student health cover and does not meet a visa health insurance condition.
- Business or Work allows work permitted by a working holiday visa, and manual work under E17.
- Endorsements E04, E05, E06 and E15 are removed. New endorsements: E16 Lloyd's Underwriters, E17 Working Holiday Manual Work and E18 Extension of Stay.
