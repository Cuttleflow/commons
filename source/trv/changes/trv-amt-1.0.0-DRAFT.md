This is the first release of the Meridian Annual Multi-Trip Travel wording. It is built on the Meridian Travel base wording version 1.1.0-DRAFT. It differs from the base in these ways:

- Annual cover. When You Are Covered covers every trip that starts in the policy period up to the maximum trip length (15, 30, 45 or 60 days), and trips within Australia more than 250 kilometres from home with a flight or a prepaid night. Trip is redefined, and Maximum Trip Length and Medical Screening are new definitions.
- Age limit. Insured persons must be no older than 74 when the policy is bought and at each renewal. Endorsement E15 is removed.
- Medical screening. Pre-existing Medical Conditions now accepts conditions only after medical screening, and sets out what happens when a condition starts or changes during the year. Endorsement E01 is replaced by E17 Screened Medical Conditions and E18 Conditions Not Accepted After Screening.
- Endorsements E04 Annual Multi-Trip and E05 Travel Within Australia are removed because the cover is now part of the wording. E16 Lloyd's Underwriters is added.
- Cancelling This Policy refunds the unexpired part of the policy period.

For underwriter and Wording Partner review before release: the obligation to tell us about a new or changed condition before booking a further trip (one WP REVIEW tag), and whether a trip planned to be longer than the maximum trip length should be covered for its first days rather than not at all.
