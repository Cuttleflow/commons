This is the first release of the Meridian Travel wording on the Commons. It is derived from the Meridian Travel Insurance Policy Wording v1.0.0 (July 2026), which was published outside the Commons. Changes from that wording:

- New Important Information section: the duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation (s.20B) for this consumer contract, a summary of the terms that differ from standard cover under s.35, the 21-day cooling-off period and the Financial Claims Scheme notice. The previous text, which said an undeclared condition would lead to a denied claim and cancellation, is replaced by the remedies the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.
- Domestic trips (Endorsement E05) no longer include medical expenses for Medicare-eligible travellers. Paying a medical expense for which a Medicare benefit is payable, or the gap, is prohibited for a general insurer under the Health Insurance Act 1973 (Cth) and the Private Health Insurance Act 2007 (Cth). New Medicare and Health Insurance Laws provision.
- The plan limits are unchanged and now sit in each section's table. The statement that some benefits may be reduced for travellers over 70 is removed, because it did not say which benefits or by how much; the age limit is 80 (85 by Endorsement E15).
- Cruise cover and natural disaster accommodation are given limits (previously "Included" with no limit), and the accidental death benefit for a dependent child is limited to $10,000.
- Cutting short shares the cancellation limit, as the plan table always showed.
- New definitions: Close Relative, Dependent Child, DFAT Advice, Known Event, Natural Disaster, Plan, Portable Electronics, Travel Companion, Travel Provider, Unattended and others. The pre-existing condition definition is unchanged in substance, and the exclusion is now subject to s.47.
- New provisions on limits and excess (one excess per event), other insurance (s.45 and s.76) and who we pay. New exclusions: elective and deferrable treatment, and business travel (written back by Endorsement E07). The war exclusion has a 14-day grace period. Pregnancy cover extends to week 36 by Endorsement E09.
- New general conditions: an express Insurance Contracts Act savings clause applying s.54 to every obligation, cancellation refunds stated before and after departure, fraud under s.56, a standalone sanctions condition naming the regimes, GST for overseas travel, a fixed governing law (the state where the policyholder lived when the policy was bought), and jurisdiction.
- The endorsement library has 15 endorsements, each with operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger.
- The licence is CC BY 4.0 (previously CC BY-SA 4.0), in line with the rest of the Commons.
- Clause numbers are removed. Each clause is identified by its heading.
