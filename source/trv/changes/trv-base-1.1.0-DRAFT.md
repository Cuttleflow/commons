Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026.

- Important Information: it no longer says the wording is part of the Product Disclosure Statement. It now says the wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement, that the Product Disclosure Statement is given with it and the Target Market Determination is made available separately, and adds a statement of who the policy is for and a note for issuers.
- Important Information, terms that differ from standard cover: the summary now lists every exclusion and the age limit that differ from standard cover, including known events, illegal acts, self-injury and intoxication, elective and deferrable treatment, business travel, war, nuclear, biological and chemical events, and the age 80 limit. The open Wording Partner question now asks for the list to be checked against the prescribed events.
- Business Travel: manual work of any kind is now clearly excluded. Endorsement E07 writes back only meetings, conferences and trade shows, and Endorsement E06 writes back only the casual work it names.
- Known Events: the exclusion now applies only to claims under the cancellation, cutting short and delay benefits, and no longer to every section.
- Illegal Acts: the exclusion now applies only to the extent that a criminal offence committed by an insured person caused or contributed to the loss.
- Medical and Evacuation Expenses and Epidemic or Pandemic: the plans are now treated the same way in both clauses. Comprehensive and Top Cover pay overseas medical expenses for an epidemic or pandemic disease, and Essentials does not. A Wording Partner question is added.
- Medical and Evacuation Expenses: whether evacuation or repatriation is medically necessary is now decided by the treating medical practitioner together with our emergency assistance service, with an independent opinion if they disagree. If an insured person refuses a recommended repatriation, we may reduce payment only by the extent of the prejudice to us, instead of stopping all further medical cover.
- Additional Benefits: the write-back of adventure activities listed in the schedule is removed, because the schedule had no such list. Endorsement E03 remains the way to cover adventure activities. Hazardous Activities no longer refers to it.
- Cancellation, Cutting Short and Delay: the exclusion for amounts payable under a credit card or other scheme is removed. Other Insurance now deals with overlapping cover.
- E11 Cancel for Any Reason: the exclusion for changing your mind, and for work or business commitments, no longer applies to the payment, so the endorsement does what its title says.
- When You Are Covered: cover for a delayed return now runs for up to 90 days after the return date, or up to 12 months where the insured person is in hospital or certified unfit to travel. It previously ran for up to 12 months in every case.
- E15 Senior Traveller: the default overseas medical limit for travellers aged 81 to 85 is now $500,000, which is the Essentials limit, instead of $1,000,000.
- Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): replaced with the library's standard text. Section 54 now applies expressly to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation.
- Sanctions: replaced with the library's standard text and made a general condition that names the Australian, United Nations, United Kingdom, United States and European Union regimes. A Wording Partner question is added.
- Cancellation: replaced with the library's standard text. The insured may cancel at any time for a pro-rata refund less non-refundable government charges, and the refund no longer depends on whether a claim was made. The Wording Partner question on the refund terms is removed.
- Other Insurance: replaced with the library's standard text. We no longer reduce payment because of another policy or a credit card or other scheme, and may seek contribution as the law allows. The recovery paragraph is now its own clause, Recovery.
