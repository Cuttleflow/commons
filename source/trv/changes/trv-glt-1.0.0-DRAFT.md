This is the first release of the Meridian Group Leisure Travel wording. It is built on the Meridian Travel base wording version 1.1.0-DRAFT. It differs from the base in these ways:

- The policyholder is the school, tour operator or club that arranges the group trip. Who Is Covered names the group members and supervisors on the group list, and confirms that each may claim under s.48 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Important Information sets out both disclosure regimes, with a WP REVIEW tag.
- New definitions: Group List and Supervisor. Insured Person and Trip are redefined for the group trip.
- When You Are Covered covers one group trip of up to 60 days, within the state, interstate or overseas, as the itinerary shows.
- Business or Work writes back a supervisor's supervising duties, and team matches and training under E17.
- A Replacement Supervisor benefit is added.
- Endorsements E04, E05, E06 and E14 are removed. New endorsements: E16 Lloyd's Underwriters, E17 Touring Sports Team and E18 Host Family Liability.
