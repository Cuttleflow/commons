The insured persons are the group members and the supervisors named on the group list. The policyholder must give us the group list before the group trip starts, and tell us of any change before the person joins or leaves the group trip.

A group member may be of any age. A supervisor must be no older than 80 when the policy is bought (85 if Endorsement E15 applies). Each insured person must live in Australia and hold a Medicare card or a visa that allows them to return to Australia, unless the schedule shows that the group includes visiting students, in which case [[ref:trv.pay.medicare]] still applies to any insured person who is Medicare-eligible.

Every insured person has the same plan. A group member who leaves the group trip with the policyholder's permission is covered until they return home, if they travel directly.

Each insured person who is not the policyholder may claim under this policy as a third-party beneficiary, as s.48 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.
