This policy covers every trip an insured person starts during the policy period, if the trip is no longer than the maximum trip length shown in the schedule. A trip that starts during the policy period is covered until it ends, even if it ends after the policy period. Cancellation cover for a trip starts when the trip is booked, or when this policy starts if that is later. All other cover for a trip starts when the insured person leaves their home to start it, and ends when they return home.

A trip must start and end in Australia, unless Endorsement E06 applies. It may be:

- a trip outside Australia, to a country in the area shown in the schedule, including the countries passed through on the way; or
- a trip within Australia that is more than 250 kilometres from the insured person's home and includes a flight or at least one night of prepaid accommodation. For such a trip, [[ref:trv.cover.medical]] covers only emergency transport home that is not a Medicare benefit, as [[ref:trv.pay.medicare]] provides, and the other sections apply as written.

A trip that is planned to last longer than the maximum trip length is not covered. If an insured person's return is delayed by an event this policy covers, cover continues until they can reasonably return home, for up to 90 days after the planned return. If they are in hospital, or a medical practitioner certifies that they are not fit to travel, cover continues until they can reasonably return home, for up to 12 months.
