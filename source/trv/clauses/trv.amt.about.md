This policy covers you, and the other people named in the schedule, for every trip you start during the policy period that is no longer than the maximum trip length: for medical emergencies overseas, for cancelling or cutting short a trip, for lost or stolen luggage and money, for accidentally injuring someone or damaging their property, and for a rental vehicle excess. It is an annual policy, so you do not need to buy a policy for each trip. You choose one of three plans, and the plan sets the limits of each benefit.

This policy, together with your schedule, the answers you gave when you bought or renewed it (including the answers to the medical screening questions) and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:trv.section.definitions]].

This policy is not private health insurance and does not replace it. It cannot pay any medical expense in Australia for which a Medicare benefit is payable (see [[ref:trv.pay.medicare]]).

This wording is the Meridian Travel base wording with the changes needed for annual multi-trip cover. It is a complete wording in its own right.
