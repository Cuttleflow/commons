[[ref:trv.excl.business-travel]] does not apply to the manual work shown in the schedule that an insured person on a working holiday visa does during the trip, other than work at heights over 3 metres, underground, offshore, with explosives, or operating heavy machinery. Medical expenses for an injury at work are covered only to the extent that workers compensation does not pay them.

Schedule variables: kinds of manual work.
