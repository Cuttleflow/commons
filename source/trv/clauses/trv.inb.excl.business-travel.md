---
id: trv.inb.excl.business-travel
type: exclusion
heading: Business or Work
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay any claim arising from business or work you do on the trip, including manual work of any kind.

This exclusion does not apply to attending meetings, conferences and trade shows if Endorsement E07 applies, to work in hospitality, retail, tourism, office or farm work allowed by a working holiday visa, or to the manual work Endorsement E17 writes back.
