If your money is stolen or lost during the trip, we will pay, up to the limits for your plan:

| Benefit | Essentials | Comprehensive | Top Cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| Money carried on you or locked in your room safe | $250 | $750 | $1,500 |
| An emergency cash advance, arranged by our emergency assistance service, after your money or cards are stolen | Not included | $500 | $1,000 |

We do not pay for money that was unattended, money not reported to the police within 24 hours of discovering the loss (subject to [[ref:trv.cond.insurance-contracts-act]]), a loss from a currency exchange or devaluation, or cryptocurrency.
