(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The policyholder's name, home country and address in Australia, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:trv.cond.governing-law]])
- The insured persons, with their ages and home countries at the date of purchase
- The policy type: single, duo or family
- The dates the trip starts and ends, and the number of days in Australia
- The plan: Medical Only, Essentials, Comprehensive or Top Cover
- The excess
- Any pre-existing condition we have accepted, and any condition we have not accepted
- The 24-hour emergency assistance telephone number
- The premium, including the policy fee, stamp duty and GST
- The insurer and its licence
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
