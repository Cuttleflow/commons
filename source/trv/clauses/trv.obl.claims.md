---
id: trv.obl.claims
type: obligation
heading: Making a Claim
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable about any event that may lead to a claim, and give us the information and documents we reasonably need to assess it: original receipts, medical reports, a carrier's report of lost or damaged luggage, written confirmation of a delay, and for theft or loss of property or money, a police report obtained within 24 hours of discovering it or as soon as practicable after that.

You must take reasonable care of your belongings and take reasonable steps to prevent or reduce loss. These obligations are subject to [[ref:trv.cond.insurance-contracts-act]].
