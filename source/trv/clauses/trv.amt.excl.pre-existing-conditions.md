We do not pay any claim arising from a pre-existing condition of an insured person unless we have accepted it after medical screening and Endorsement E17 applies to it.

If, during the policy period, an insured person's condition first shows itself or changes (for example, they need new treatment or medication, or are admitted to hospital), trips already booked are covered as before. Before booking a further trip, you must tell us about the condition so that we can assess it by medical screening. If we do not accept it, it is a condition not accepted for trips booked after that, and Endorsement E18 applies to it.

This exclusion is subject to s.47 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): we will not rely on it for a condition the insured person was not aware of, and that a reasonable person in their circumstances could not have been expected to be aware of, when the policy was bought or renewed. It is also subject to [[ref:trv.cond.insurance-contracts-act]].
