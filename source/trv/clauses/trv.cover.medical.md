If you suffer an injury or sickness during the trip, we will pay the reasonable costs, incurred within 12 months, of the following, up to the limits for your plan:

| Benefit | Essentials | Comprehensive | Top Cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overseas medical, hospital and ambulance expenses | $500,000 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Emergency dental treatment to relieve sudden pain | $2,000 | $3,500 | $5,000 |
| Medical evacuation and repatriation to Australia, including a medical escort | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Return of remains to Australia, or a funeral or cremation overseas | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Economy travel and accommodation for one close relative to be with you if you are hospitalised for more than 3 days or are dangerously ill | Not included | $15,000 | $25,000 |
| Travel to rejoin your trip if you recover | Not included | $5,000 | $10,000 |

You, or someone with you, must call the 24-hour emergency assistance service before you go to hospital if you can, and as soon as practicable after a life-threatening emergency. Whether and when evacuation or repatriation is medically necessary, and how it is carried out, is decided by the treating medical practitioner together with our emergency assistance service. If they do not agree, we will arrange an opinion from an independent medical practitioner at our cost. If you refuse a repatriation that they recommend as medically necessary, we may reduce what we pay for further medical expenses by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced by your refusal.

Under Comprehensive and Top Cover, overseas medical expenses for an epidemic or pandemic disease you contract during the trip, including COVID-19, are covered as any other sickness. Under Essentials, we do not pay medical expenses for an epidemic or pandemic disease.

Medical expenses in Australia are covered only for an insured person who is not Medicare-eligible (see [[ref:trv.pay.medicare]]).
