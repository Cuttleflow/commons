**You** may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired period of insurance on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. If you cancel before the trip starts, the unexpired period is the whole trip. After the trip starts, it is the part of the trip, up to the return date shown in the schedule, that has not yet happened. If you cancel within the cooling-off period, the refund is as [[ref:trv.important-information]] states.

**We** may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you written notice as that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis.
