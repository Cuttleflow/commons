After the cooling-off period, you may cancel this policy at any time before the trip starts by telling us in writing, and we will refund the premium less any non-refundable government charges, unless you have made a claim. After the trip starts, you may cancel, and we will refund the premium for the unused part of the trip in proportion, unless you have made a claim.

We may cancel this policy only on a ground allowed by the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), by giving you written notice as s.59 of that Act requires.
