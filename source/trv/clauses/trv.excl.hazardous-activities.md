---
id: trv.excl.hazardous-activities
type: exclusion
heading: Hazardous Activities
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay any claim caused by an insured person taking part in professional sport, motor racing, mountaineering or rock climbing using ropes, skydiving, hang-gliding, paragliding, base jumping, bungee jumping, scuba diving deeper than 30 metres or without a qualified instructor or certification, snow sports (unless Endorsement E02 applies), or flying other than as a fare-paying passenger, unless Endorsement E03 applies or [[ref:trv.cover.extras]] writes the activity back.
