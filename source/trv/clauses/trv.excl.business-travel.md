heading: Business Travel
since: 1.0.0

---

We do not pay any claim arising from business or work you do on the trip, including manual work of any kind.

This exclusion does not apply to attending meetings, conferences and trade shows if Endorsement E07 applies, or to the casual work that Endorsement E06 allows. Endorsement E07 does not write back manual work.
