heading: Medical Screening
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The health questions we ask about each insured person's medical conditions when the policy is bought or renewed, or when you tell us about a new or changed condition, and our assessment of the answers. We tell you the outcome for each condition in writing: accepted, accepted for an additional premium, or not accepted.
