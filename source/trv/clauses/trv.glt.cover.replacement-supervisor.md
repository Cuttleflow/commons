heading: Replacement Supervisor
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If a supervisor cannot continue the group trip because they die, suffer a serious injury or sickness, or must return home because a close relative dies or suffers a serious injury or sickness, and the policyholder needs to send a replacement supervisor so that the group can continue, we will pay the reasonable economy travel and accommodation costs of the replacement supervisor to join the group, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $10,000 for each event).

This benefit applies under every plan, and is in addition to the limits of the other sections.
