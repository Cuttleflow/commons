If your luggage and personal effects are lost, stolen or accidentally damaged during the trip, we will pay, at our option, for repair, replacement with an item of similar quality, or the value of the item at the time of loss. For an item less than 12 months old with its receipt, we pay the replacement cost; for older items we make an allowance for wear and depreciation. The limits for your plan are:

| Benefit | Essentials | Comprehensive | Top Cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| Luggage and personal effects, in total | $3,000 | $10,000 | $15,000 |
| Any one item or set, including any one item of portable electronics | $500 | $1,500 | $3,000 |
| Essential purchases if your luggage is delayed for more than 12 hours on the way out | Not included | $750 | $1,500 |
| Replacing passports, visas and travel documents | $1,000 | $3,000 | $5,000 |

A pair or set is treated as one item. If part of a pair or set is lost or damaged, we pay for that part only, unless it cannot be replaced or repaired to match.

We do not pay for:

- an item left unattended in a public place or in a vehicle, or theft from an unlocked vehicle or unlocked room;
- portable electronics, jewellery or money placed in checked luggage;
- wear and tear, gradual deterioration, scratching, or mechanical or electrical breakdown;
- sporting equipment while in use, unless Endorsement E02, E03 or E14 applies to it;
- an item confiscated or detained by customs or another government authority; or
- an amount a carrier or accommodation provider pays or must pay.
