This policy covers a group of travellers, and the supervisors who travel with them, on the group trip shown in the schedule: for medical emergencies, for cancelling or cutting short the trip, for lost or stolen luggage and money, for accidentally injuring someone or damaging their property, and for a rental vehicle excess. It is bought by a school, tour operator, club or other organisation (the policyholder) that arranges the trip.

Every group member and supervisor on the group list has the same plan. The plan sets the limits of each benefit, and unless the schedule shows otherwise the limits apply to each insured person.

This policy, together with your schedule, the group list, the itinerary, the answers given when the policy was bought and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms the contract of insurance. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:trv.section.definitions]].

This policy is not private health insurance and does not replace it. It cannot pay any medical expense in Australia for which a Medicare benefit is payable (see [[ref:trv.pay.medicare]]).

This wording is the Meridian Travel base wording with the changes needed for group trips. It is a complete wording in its own right.
