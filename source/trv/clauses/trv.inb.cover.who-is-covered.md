The insured persons are the people named in the schedule, under one of these policy types:

- **single:** the policyholder;
- **duo:** the policyholder and one other adult; or
- **family:** the policyholder, their spouse or domestic partner, and their dependent children.

Each insured person must, when the policy is bought:

- live permanently outside Australia, in the home country shown for them in the schedule;
- not be an Australian citizen or permanent resident, and not be Medicare-eligible;
- hold a visa, or be about to be granted one, that allows them to be in Australia for the trip; and
- be no older than 75.

If an insured person becomes Medicare-eligible during the trip, their cover for medical expenses in Australia ends on that day, and we refund the premium for the rest of the trip for that cover.
