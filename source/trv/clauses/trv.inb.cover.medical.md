If you suffer an injury or sickness during the trip, we will pay the reasonable costs, incurred in Australia within 12 months, of the following, up to the limits for your plan:

| Benefit | Medical Only | Essentials | Comprehensive | Top Cover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medical, hospital, ambulance and prescribed medication expenses in Australia | $1,000,000 | $1,000,000 | $5,000,000 | $10,000,000 |
| Emergency dental treatment to relieve sudden pain | $1,000 | $1,000 | $2,000 | $3,000 |
| Medical evacuation to the nearest appropriate hospital in Australia, and return to your home country when medically necessary, including a medical escort | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Return of remains to your home country, or a funeral or cremation in Australia | $20,000 | $20,000 | $30,000 | $50,000 |
| Economy travel and accommodation for one close relative to come to Australia to be with you if you are hospitalised for more than 5 days or are dangerously ill | Not included | Not included | $15,000 | $25,000 |

You, or someone with you, must call the 24-hour emergency assistance service before you go to hospital if you can, and as soon as practicable after a life-threatening emergency. Whether and when a return to your home country is medically necessary, and how it is carried out, is decided by the treating medical practitioner together with our emergency assistance service. If they do not agree, we will arrange an opinion from an independent medical practitioner at our cost. If you refuse a return home that they recommend as medically necessary, we may reduce what we pay for further medical expenses by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced by your refusal.

We do not pay medical expenses incurred outside Australia. [[ref:trv.pay.medicare]] limits what we may pay.
