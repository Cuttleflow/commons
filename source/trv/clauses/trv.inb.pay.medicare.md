The Health Insurance Act 1973 (Cth) and the Private Health Insurance Act 2007 (Cth) do not allow us to pay any medical expense for which a Medicare benefit is payable, or any gap between the Medicare benefit and the fee charged, or any amount that would make this policy health insurance business under the Private Health Insurance Act 2007 (Cth).

Every insured person under this policy must be a person who is not Medicare-eligible and is temporarily in Australia. We pay medical and hospital costs in Australia only for such a person. We will not pay any benefit, or part of a benefit, to the extent that paying it would breach those Acts. The other benefits of this policy are not affected.
