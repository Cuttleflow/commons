---
id: trv.glt.excl.business-travel
type: exclusion
heading: Business or Work
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay any claim arising from business or work done on the trip, including manual work of any kind.

This exclusion does not apply to a supervisor carrying out supervising duties for the group, to playing or officiating in the matches and training shown in the itinerary if Endorsement E17 applies, or to attending meetings, conferences and trade shows if Endorsement E07 applies. Endorsement E07 does not write back manual work.
