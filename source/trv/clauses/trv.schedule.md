(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The policyholder's name and address, and the state or territory where they live, whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:trv.cond.governing-law]])
- The insured persons, with their ages at the date of purchase
- The policy type: single, duo or family
- The plan: Essentials, Comprehensive or Top Cover
- The departure and return dates, and the destination region
- The excess
- Any pre-existing condition we have accepted, and any condition we have not accepted
- The 24-hour emergency assistance telephone number
- The premium, including the policy fee, stamp duty and GST
- The insurer and its licence
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
