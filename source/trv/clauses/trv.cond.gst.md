The premium includes GST where it applies, under A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth). Insurance for travel outside Australia is GST-free to the extent the law allows. The accidental death and permanent disablement benefits are fixed amounts and are not adjusted for GST.
