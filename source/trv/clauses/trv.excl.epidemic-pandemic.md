We do not pay any claim under the cancellation and delay section caused by an epidemic or pandemic, or by government action taken because of one, unless Endorsement E10 applies.

This exclusion does not apply to the medical section under Comprehensive and Top Cover. Under Essentials, we do not pay medical expenses for an epidemic or pandemic disease. [[ref:trv.cover.medical]] sets out the position for each plan.
