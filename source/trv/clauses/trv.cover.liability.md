heading: Personal Liability
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If, during the trip, you become legally liable to pay compensation for bodily injury to another person, or loss of or damage to another person's property, caused by an accident, we will pay that compensation and the reasonable legal costs we approve, up to the limit for your plan for all claims arising from one event: Essentials $1,000,000; Comprehensive $5,000,000; Top Cover $10,000,000.

You must not admit liability, or offer or agree to pay anything, without our written consent. We may take over the defence and settlement of the claim in your name. This obligation is subject to [[ref:trv.cond.insurance-contracts-act]].

We do not pay for liability arising from your business or profession; for injury to, or property of, an insured person, a close relative or your travel companion; arising from owning or using a motor vehicle, aircraft or motorised watercraft; assumed under a contract, unless it would have existed without the contract; from a deliberate act; or for fines, penalties or punitive, exemplary or aggravated damages.
