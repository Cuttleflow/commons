The most we will pay for each benefit is the limit for your plan, as each section shows. A limit in a table is for each insured person unless it says otherwise. The total paid under cancellation and cutting short together is no more than the cancellation limit.

We deduct the excess shown in the schedule from each claim. Only one excess applies to all claims arising from one event, whichever sections they fall under. No excess applies to the emergency evacuation and repatriation benefits or the additional benefits for accidental death and permanent disablement.
