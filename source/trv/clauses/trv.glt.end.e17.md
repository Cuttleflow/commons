[[ref:trv.excl.hazardous-activities]] does not apply to playing, training or officiating in the organised matches, competitions and training sessions in the sport shown in the schedule that are part of the itinerary. It does not write back professional sport.

The limit for sports equipment owned by the team or a team member is the amount shown in the schedule (default $5,000 for each item and $20,000 for the team) in addition to the luggage limit. We do not pay for damage to sports equipment while it is being used.

Schedule variables: sport; team sports equipment limit.
