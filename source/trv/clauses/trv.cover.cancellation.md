If a reason below happens, we will pay, up to the limits for your plan:

| Benefit | Essentials | Comprehensive | Top Cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cancellation: non-refundable deposits and prepaid travel and accommodation you lose if you cannot start the trip | $5,000 | $15,000 | $30,000 |
| Cutting short: reasonable additional travel costs to return home early, and the unused part of prepaid costs (shares the cancellation limit) | $5,000 | $15,000 | $30,000 |
| Delay: meals and accommodation for each full 12 hours your scheduled transport is delayed, after the first 12 hours | Not included | $250 (up to $1,500) | $300 (up to $2,500) |
| Missed connection: additional travel and accommodation to reach a pre-booked event, cruise or tour you would otherwise miss | Not included | $2,000 | $3,000 |
| Accommodation after a natural disaster disrupts your trip | Not included | $3,000 | $5,000 |

The reasons are:

- you or your travel companion become seriously ill or injured, or die;
- a close relative in Australia becomes seriously ill or injured, or dies;
- a natural disaster at your destination, or an act of terrorism there within 7 days before you are due to arrive;
- your home is made uninhabitable by fire, flood or storm;
- you are called for jury service or subpoenaed as a witness;
- you are made redundant by your employer, other than voluntarily, after buying this policy;
- a travel provider becomes insolvent and you lose what you paid it, unless you booked through an agent who has also failed; or
- your scheduled transport is cancelled, delayed or diverted for a reason outside your control.

We do not pay under this section for a loss caused by:

- you changing your mind, or your work or business commitments;
- a known event, or DFAT advice that applied when you bought this policy;
- an amount that a travel provider refunds or must refund;
- a pre-existing condition, of you or of a close relative or travel companion, unless it is a condition we have accepted for an insured person;
- an epidemic or pandemic, unless Endorsement E10 applies; or
- the wedding, event or other occasion you were travelling to being cancelled, unless Endorsement E13 applies.
