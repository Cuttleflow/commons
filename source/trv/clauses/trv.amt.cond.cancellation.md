**You** may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired part of the policy period on a pro-rata basis, less the policy fee and any non-refundable government charges. If you cancel within the cooling-off period, the refund is as [[ref:trv.important-information]] states.

**We** may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you written notice as that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired part of the policy period on a pro-rata basis.
