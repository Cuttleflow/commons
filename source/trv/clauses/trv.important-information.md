**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so. This wording is part of the Product Disclosure Statement the insurer gives you, which also includes its Target Market Determination.

**Your duty when you buy this policy.** This is a consumer insurance contract. Under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) you have a duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us before the policy is entered into, extended, varied or reinstated. Answer every question we ask, including the health questions, honestly and completely, for yourself and for everyone you name. If you do not take reasonable care, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows. If the misrepresentation was fraudulent, we may also avoid the policy.

**Terms that differ from standard cover.** The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) sets standard cover for travel insurance. This policy differs from standard cover. In particular, it does not cover a pre-existing condition unless we accept it (see [[ref:trv.excl.pre-existing-conditions]]), travel against Australian Government advice (see [[ref:trv.excl.government-advice]]), known events (see [[ref:trv.excl.known-events]]), epidemic or pandemic disruption (see [[ref:trv.excl.epidemic-pandemic]]), or the hazardous activities listed in [[ref:trv.excl.hazardous-activities]].

**Cooling-off period.** You may cancel this policy within 21 days of buying it by telling us in writing. If you have not started your trip and have not made a claim, we will refund the full premium, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover. After that, you may still cancel under [[ref:trv.cond.cancellation]].

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
