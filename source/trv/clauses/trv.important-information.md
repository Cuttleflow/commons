**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement. The insurer gives you a Product Disclosure Statement with this wording, and makes its Target Market Determination available separately.

**Who this policy is for.** This wording is designed for retail clients. An issuer using it must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available, as the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) requires. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.

**Note for issuers.** Before this wording is used, the issuer must wrap it in a Product Disclosure Statement that contains the information the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) requires for a retail travel product, and must prepare and keep current a Target Market Determination for the product.

**Your duty when you buy this policy.** This is a consumer insurance contract. Under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) you have a duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us before the policy is entered into, extended, varied or reinstated. Answer every question we ask, including the health questions, honestly and completely, for yourself and for everyone you name. If you do not take reasonable care, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows. If the misrepresentation was fraudulent, we may also avoid the policy.

**Terms that differ from standard cover.** The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) sets standard cover for travel insurance. This policy differs from standard cover. In particular, it does not cover, or limits cover for:
- a pre-existing condition, unless we accept it (see [[ref:trv.excl.pre-existing-conditions]]);
- travel to a country or region for which DFAT advice was "do not travel" when you left for it (see [[ref:trv.excl.government-advice]]);
- a known event, for claims under the cancellation, cutting short and delay benefits (see [[ref:trv.excl.known-events]]);
- an epidemic or pandemic (see [[ref:trv.excl.epidemic-pandemic]]), including, under the Essentials plan, medical expenses for an epidemic or pandemic disease;
- the hazardous activities listed in [[ref:trv.excl.hazardous-activities]];
- loss caused by you committing a criminal offence (see [[ref:trv.excl.illegal-acts]]);
- intentional self-injury, or loss to which alcohol or a drug contributes (see [[ref:trv.excl.self-injury-intoxication]]);
- cosmetic, elective or deferrable treatment, and treatment you travelled to obtain (see [[ref:trv.excl.elective-treatment]]);
- business or work you do on the trip (see [[ref:trv.excl.business-travel]]);
- pregnancy and childbirth (see [[ref:trv.excl.pregnancy]]);
- war, and nuclear, biological or chemical events (see [[ref:trv.excl.war]] and [[ref:trv.excl.nuclear-biological-chemical]]); and
- travellers who are older than 80 when the policy is bought, or 85 if Endorsement E15 applies (see [[ref:trv.cover.who-is-covered]]).

**Cooling-off period.** You may cancel this policy within 21 days of buying it by telling us in writing. If you have not started your trip and have not made a claim, we will refund the full premium, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover. After that, you may still cancel under [[ref:trv.cond.cancellation]].

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
