---
id: trv.def.luggage-and-personal-effects
type: definition
heading: Luggage and Personal Effects
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Suitcases and bags and their contents, and clothing and articles an insured person wears or carries on the trip, including portable electronics, but not money, vehicles, watercraft, drones, or items sent unaccompanied as freight.
