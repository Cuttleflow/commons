This policy covers visitors to Australia who are not eligible for Medicare, while they are in Australia: for medical, hospital and ambulance costs if they are injured or fall sick, for returning them to their home country when that is medically necessary, and, under the Essentials, Comprehensive and Top Cover plans, for cancelling or cutting short the trip, for lost or stolen luggage and money, for accidentally injuring someone or damaging their property, and for a rental vehicle excess. You choose a plan, and the plan sets the limits of each benefit.

This policy is travel insurance. It is not overseas visitors health cover or overseas student health cover, it is not private health insurance, and it does not meet a visa condition that requires the holder to keep adequate health insurance. If your visa has such a condition, you need a policy from a registered private health insurer.

This policy, together with your schedule, the answers you gave when you bought it and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:trv.section.definitions]].

This wording is the Meridian Travel base wording with the changes needed for visitors to Australia. It is a complete wording in its own right.
