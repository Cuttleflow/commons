The insured persons are the people named in the schedule, under one of these policy types:

- **single:** the policyholder;
- **duo:** the policyholder and one other adult; or
- **family:** the policyholder, their spouse or domestic partner, and their dependent children.

Each insured person must live in Australia, hold a Medicare card or a visa that allows them to return to Australia, and be no older than 80 when the policy is bought (85 if Endorsement E15 applies). Every insured person has the same plan. Unless the schedule shows otherwise, the limits in each section apply to each insured person, except personal liability, which applies to each event.
