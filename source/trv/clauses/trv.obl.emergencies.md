heading: Emergencies
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

In a medical emergency, you or someone with you must contact the 24-hour emergency assistance service on the number shown in the schedule as soon as practicable. In a life-threatening emergency, call local emergency services first. These obligations are subject to [[ref:trv.cond.insurance-contracts-act]].
