(Completed separately for each policy. It contains the specific details of the group trip.)

The schedule shows:

- The policyholder's name, ABN and address, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:trv.cond.governing-law]])
- The kind of group (for example a school group, a tour group or a touring sports team), and the number of group members and supervisors
- The departure and return dates of the group trip, and its destinations (see [[ref:trv.cover.trip]])
- The plan: Essentials, Comprehensive or Top Cover
- The excess
- The limit for replacement supervisor costs, if different from the default in [[ref:trv.glt.cover.replacement-supervisor]]
- Any pre-existing condition we have accepted, and any condition we have not accepted
- The 24-hour emergency assistance telephone number
- The premium, including stamp duty and GST
- The insurer and its licence
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables

The group list and the itinerary form part of the schedule.
