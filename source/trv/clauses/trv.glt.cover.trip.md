heading: When You Are Covered
since: 1.0.0

---

Cancellation cover starts when the policy is bought. All other cover for each insured person starts when they leave their home, or the place shown in the itinerary, to start the group trip on the departure date, and ends when they return there or on the return date, whichever is earlier.

The group trip is the journey in the itinerary. It may be within the state or territory where the policyholder is, interstate, or overseas, and may last no longer than 60 days. It must start and end in Australia. For any part of the group trip within Australia, [[ref:trv.cover.medical]] covers only emergency transport home that is not a Medicare benefit, as [[ref:trv.pay.medicare]] provides, for an insured person who is Medicare-eligible.

If the group's return is delayed by an event this policy covers, cover continues until the group can reasonably return home, for up to 30 days after the return date. If an insured person is in hospital, or a medical practitioner certifies that they are not fit to travel, cover for them continues until they can reasonably return home, for up to 12 months after the return date.
