[[ref:trv.excl.hazardous-activities]] does not apply to recreational skiing and snowboarding on marked runs, and cross-country skiing on marked trails. Your own snow sports equipment is covered under [[ref:trv.cover.luggage]] while in use. We also pay up to $1,000 for unused prepaid ski passes and lessons if an injury or sickness covered by this policy stops you using them.

Schedule variables: none.
