Under Comprehensive and Top Cover, we will also pay, up to the limits for your plan:

| Benefit | Essentials | Comprehensive | Top Cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accidental death within 12 months of an injury during the trip, to the insured person's estate | Not included | $25,000 | $50,000 |
| Permanent loss of sight in an eye, or of the use of a hand or foot, or permanent total disablement, within 12 months of an injury during the trip | Not included | $25,000 | $50,000 |
| Extra boarding for your pet in Australia if your return is delayed by an event this policy covers | Not included | $500 | $1,000 |
| Cruise: a missed port of call because of your sickness, and each full day of cabin confinement on medical advice ($100 a day) | Not included | $1,500 | $3,000 |

Only one of the accidental death and permanent disablement benefits is payable for any one injury. For a dependent child, the accidental death benefit is limited to $10,000.

Under Comprehensive and Top Cover, [[ref:trv.excl.hazardous-activities]] does not apply to the adventure activities listed in the schedule, while you take part in them under the supervision of a licensed operator and with the safety equipment it requires.
