Cancellation cover starts when you buy this policy. All other cover starts on the start date shown in the schedule, when you leave your home in your home country for Australia (or, if you buy this policy while you are already in Australia, 72 hours after you buy it, except for an injury), and ends when you leave Australia or on the end date shown in the schedule, whichever is earlier.

The trip is your stay in Australia, including travel within Australia, for no more than 365 days. Travel to another country during the trip is not covered, except a return to your home country under [[ref:trv.cover.medical]].

If your departure from Australia is delayed by an event this policy covers, cover continues until you can reasonably leave, for up to 30 days after the end date. If you are in hospital, or a medical practitioner certifies that you are not fit to travel, cover continues until you can reasonably leave or are returned home, for up to 12 months after the end date.
