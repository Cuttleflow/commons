The plan shown in the schedule: Medical Only, Essentials, Comprehensive or Top Cover. The plan sets the limit of each benefit, as each section shows. Under Medical Only, only [[ref:trv.section.medical]], [[ref:trv.section.liability]] and [[ref:trv.section.payment]] apply, and no other section does.
