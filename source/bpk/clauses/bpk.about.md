This is a modular business insurance package for small and medium enterprises. It has eleven sections. Four sections are written for each Situation (a location where the Business operates). Seven sections are written once for the whole Business.

A section provides cover only where the Schedule shows it as insured, and a section written for each Situation provides cover at a Situation only where the Schedule shows it as insured for that Situation. The Policy is made up of this wording, the Schedule and any Endorsement. Where the Schedule and this wording differ, the Schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:bpk.section.definitions]].

| Section | Written for | What triggers cover | Limit |

| [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage|heading]] | Each Situation | Damage during the Period of Insurance | Sum insured for each item at each Situation |

| [[ref:bpk.section.crime|heading]] | The Business | Loss discovered during the Period of Insurance | Section limit, aggregate |
