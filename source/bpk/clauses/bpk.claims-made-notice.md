[[ref:bpk.section.tax-audit|heading]], [[ref:bpk.section.management-liability|heading]] and the liability cover in [[ref:bpk.section.cyber]] respond only to matters first made or notified during the Period of Insurance.

If, during the Period of Insurance, you give us written notice of facts that might give rise to a Claim or an Audit notice, s 40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) means we cannot refuse to pay a later Claim or Audit notice arising from those facts only because it was made after the Period of Insurance ended. [[ref:bpk.cond.notice-of-circumstances]] sets out how to give that notice.
