(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The Schedule shows:

- The named insured, ABN and any subsidiaries named
- The Business, and its activities as ANZSIC 2006 classes
- The Period of Insurance
- Each Situation, and for each Situation the sections insured there, the property insured, each Sum insured, the Excess, and for [[ref:bpk.section.business-interruption|heading]] the gross profit Sum insured, indemnity period and any waiting period
- The sections insured for the whole Business, with each section limit and Excess
- Sub-limits and other values that differ from the defaults in [[ref:bpk.appendix.default-schedule-values]]
- The Retroactive date for [[ref:bpk.section.cyber|heading]] and [[ref:bpk.section.management-liability|heading]], and the pending and prior date
- The declared figures used for rating, including turnover, employees and annual carryings
- The premium for each section, with stamp duty, levies and GST
- The State or Territory whose law governs this Policy (see [[ref:bpk.cond.governing-law-and-jurisdiction]])
- The Endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
- Interested parties
