heading: Interruption of the Business
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

If Damage covered under [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage]] at a Situation, or Damage to property in the vicinity of the Premises that prevents access, interrupts the Business during the Period of Insurance, we will pay, during the indemnity period shown in the Schedule:

- loss of gross profit (reduction in turnover less expenses that cease);
- increased cost of working; and
- loss of rent receivable, as shown in the Schedule.
