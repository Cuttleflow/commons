since: 2.1.0

You must notify us of a loss within 60 days after Discovery, and in any event within the extended discovery period. If this section is cancelled or not renewed, and not replaced by other crime cover, you have an extended discovery period of 90 days after the Period of Insurance ends to discover loss sustained before it ended. [[ref:bpk.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies.
