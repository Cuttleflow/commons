The section limit shown in the Schedule is the most we pay under this section for the Period of Insurance, including defence costs. Sub-limits shown in the Schedule are part of the section limit. All loss from the same, similar or related Cyber events, or from one campaign by the same person or group, is one event subject to one Excess, and is treated as discovered when the first of them was discovered.
