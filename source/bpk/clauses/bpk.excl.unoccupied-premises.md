---
id: bpk.excl.unoccupied-premises
type: exclusion
heading: Unoccupied Premises
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Loss or Damage at Premises that are Unoccupied when the loss occurs, unless we have agreed in writing to continue cover. This exclusion does not apply where the Premises are partly occupied, or to [[ref:bpk.section.liability]], [[ref:bpk.section.tax-audit]], [[ref:bpk.section.cyber]] or [[ref:bpk.section.management-liability]].
