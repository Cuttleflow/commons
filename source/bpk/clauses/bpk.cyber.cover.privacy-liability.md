heading: Privacy and Network Security Liability
since: 2.1.0

---

The compensation you become legally liable to pay, and defence costs, for a Claim first made against you during the Period of Insurance arising from a Cyber event, a breach of privacy law, or the statutory tort of serious invasion of privacy under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), committed on or after the Retroactive date.
