heading: Professional Fees for an Audit
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

If you first receive an Audit notice during the Period of Insurance, or a later Audit notice arises from facts notified under [[ref:bpk.cond.notice-of-circumstances]], we will pay the reasonable professional fees of your accountant or tax agent for responding to it, up to the section limit shown in the Schedule. The section limit is the most we pay for all Audit notices in the Period of Insurance.
