heading: Cyber Business Interruption
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

If a Cyber event first discovered during the Period of Insurance interrupts the Business for longer than the waiting period shown in the Schedule, we will pay your loss of gross profit and increased cost of working from the start of the interruption until the end of the indemnity period shown in the Schedule.
