since: 2.1.0

---

In [[ref:bpk.section.management-liability]]: compensation, settlements, judgments and defence costs, and fines and penalties only as [[ref:bpk.ml.statutory-insurability]] permits. Loss does not include tax, the cost of complying with an order, or matters uninsurable at law.
