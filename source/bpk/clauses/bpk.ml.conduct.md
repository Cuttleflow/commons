heading: Conduct
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not cover Loss arising from a deliberately dishonest or fraudulent act, or from an insured gaining a profit or advantage to which it was not legally entitled, but only where this is established by Final adjudication or written admission. Until then we advance defence costs under [[ref:bpk.ml.limits]]. The conduct of one Insured person is not imputed to another Insured person. The conduct of a director or chief financial officer is imputed to you for [[ref:bpk.ml.cover.company-liability]].
