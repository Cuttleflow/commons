heading: Glass Additional Benefits
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Each up to the sub-limit shown in the Schedule (defaults in [[ref:bpk.appendix.default-schedule-values]]):

- temporary boarding up;
- frame damage caused by the breakage;
- Damage to Stock in display windows caused by the breakage; and
- alarm reconnection.
