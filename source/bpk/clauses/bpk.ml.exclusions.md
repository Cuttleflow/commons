since: 2.1.0

We do not cover under [[ref:bpk.section.management-liability]] a Claim:

- for Personal injury or Property damage, except mental injury in an Employment claim and defence costs under [[ref:bpk.ml.cover.statutory-liability]];
- for professional services provided to others for a fee;
- by you or a related entity holding more than the shareholding percentage shown in the Schedule, except an Employment claim or a derivative action brought independently;
- arising from facts notified under an earlier policy, or proceedings pending before the pending and prior date shown in the Schedule;
- for the cost of a product recall or a liability covered under [[ref:bpk.section.liability]];
- for a Cyber event, except an Employment claim relating to employee data; or
- brought in the United States of America or Canada, or under their laws.

There is no exclusion for your insolvency.
