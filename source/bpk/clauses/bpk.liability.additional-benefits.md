Each up to the sub-limit shown in the Schedule (defaults in [[ref:bpk.appendix.default-schedule-values]]), within the limit:

- tenants' liability for damage to leased premises;
- property in your physical or legal control;
- statutory defence costs;
- wrongful arrest;
- emergency medical expenses; and
- damage to underground services where you followed the before-you-dig enquiry process.
