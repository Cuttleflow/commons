heading: Glass Breakage
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We will pay for accidental breakage, during the Period of Insurance, of fixed glass, mirrors, glass shelving and counter tops, illuminated signs shown in the Schedule, and lettering or ornamentation on broken glass at a Situation, up to the glass Sum insured for that Situation. We settle by replacing with glass of equivalent quality.
