We value Money at its face value and securities at their market value at the close of business on the day of Discovery, converted into Australian dollars at the Reserve Bank of Australia rate on that day. Other property is valued as [[ref:bpk.pd.basis-of-settlement]] provides. Amounts recovered after a loss are applied first to reimburse you for loss above the limit or within the Excess, then to us, then to you for any uninsured loss.
