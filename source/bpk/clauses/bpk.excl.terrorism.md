heading: Terrorism
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Loss, Damage, liability or cost caused by an act of terrorism. For [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage]] and [[ref:bpk.section.business-interruption]], this exclusion does not apply to the extent the Terrorism Insurance Act 2003 (Cth) provides that it has no effect for a declared terrorist incident.
