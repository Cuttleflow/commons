In [[ref:bpk.section.cyber]]: a written demand for compensation, a civil proceeding, or a regulatory investigation, against you.

In [[ref:bpk.section.management-liability]]: a written demand for compensation, a civil, criminal, administrative or regulatory proceeding, or a formal investigation, against an insured for a Wrongful act.

The word is used with a capital letter only in those two sections and in [[ref:bpk.section.general-conditions]].
