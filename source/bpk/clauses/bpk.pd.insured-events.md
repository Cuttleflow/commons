| Event | Description |
|---|---|
| Fire | Fire, explosion, lightning, thunderbolt |
| Storm and flood | Storm, tempest, rainwater, flood, high water |
| Earthquake | Earthquake, tsunami, subterranean fire, volcanic eruption |
| Impact | Impact by vehicles, aircraft, falling trees or other objects |
| Water damage | Bursting, leaking or overflowing of water pipes, tanks or apparatus |
| Riot | Riot, civil commotion, strikes, locked-out workers |
| Malicious damage | Malicious acts or vandalism, other than theft (see [[ref:bpk.section.crime]]) |
| Escape of liquid | Escape of oil or other liquid from a fixed heating or cooking installation |
| Sprinkler leakage | Accidental discharge from a sprinkler or fire suppression system |

[[ref:bpk.end.e01]] extends this clause to accidental damage, and [[ref:bpk.end.e02]] modifies the flood cover, where the Schedule shows them as operative.
