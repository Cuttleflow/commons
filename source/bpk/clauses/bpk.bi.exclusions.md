In addition to [[ref:bpk.section.general-exclusions]], we do not cover under [[ref:bpk.section.business-interruption]]:

- loss that is not a direct consequence of Damage covered under [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage]], other than loss that [[ref:bpk.bi.cover]] covers following Damage to property in the vicinity of the Premises, or that [[ref:bpk.bi.additional-benefits]] covers;
- loss after the indemnity period;
- fines or contractual penalties for late delivery; and
- loss of market or permanent loss of customers that would have occurred regardless of the Damage.
