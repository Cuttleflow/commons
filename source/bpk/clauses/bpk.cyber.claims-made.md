[[ref:bpk.cyber.cover.privacy-liability]] and [[ref:bpk.cyber.cover.regulatory]] cover a Claim first made against you during the Period of Insurance for acts on or after the Retroactive date. All Claims arising from the same, similar or related facts are one Claim, first made when the earliest was first made. [[ref:bpk.cond.notice-of-circumstances]] applies.
