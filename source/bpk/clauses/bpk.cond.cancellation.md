You may cancel this Policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired Period of Insurance on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. If we have paid a total loss of insured property, no refund applies to that item.

We may cancel this Policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you written notice as that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis.

Cancellation does not affect any claim, Claim, Audit notice or circumstance notified to us before cancellation takes effect.
