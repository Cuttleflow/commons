You may cancel this Policy at any time by telling us in writing. We may cancel this Policy only on a ground permitted by s 60 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), by giving you written notice under s 59 of that Act. Unless that Act requires a shorter period, cancellation by us takes effect 30 days after we give notice. On cancellation by either party we refund the premium for the unexpired Period of Insurance on a pro rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. Cancellation does not affect any claim, Claim, Audit notice or circumstance notified to us before cancellation takes effect.
