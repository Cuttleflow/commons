---

| Property | How we settle |
|---|---|
| Building | Cost of reinstatement: repair or rebuild to a condition substantially the same as new, including the extra cost to comply with current building laws |
| Contents | Replacement as new with equivalent property, or repair if repair costs less |
| Stock | The lesser of cost price, selling price less profit, or market value at the time of Damage |
| Tenant's improvements | Cost of reinstatement, to the extent you are obliged or choose to reinstate |
