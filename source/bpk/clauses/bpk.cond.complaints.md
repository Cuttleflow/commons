If you have a complaint, contact us or your broker first. We will acknowledge it within one business day where practicable and give you our final response within 30 calendar days, as ASIC Regulatory Guide 271 requires. If you are not satisfied, or we have not responded in that time, you may contact the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA): www.afca.org.au · 1800 931 678 · info@afca.org.au · GPO Box 3, Melbourne VIC 3001.
