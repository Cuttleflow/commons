since: 2.1.0

---

In addition to [[ref:bpk.section.general-exclusions]], we do not cover under [[ref:bpk.section.tax-audit]]:

- tax, duty, interest, penalties or fines;
- fees for preparing returns or work you would have needed in any case;
- an Audit notice received before the Period of Insurance, or arising from facts you knew before it that you did not disclose;
- an audit arising from fraud, deliberate evasion or failure to lodge returns; and
- a voluntary disclosure that is not made in response to an Audit notice.
