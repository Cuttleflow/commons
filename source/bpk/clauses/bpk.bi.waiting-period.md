heading: Business Interruption Waiting Period
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

If the Schedule shows a waiting period, we pay only where the interruption lasts longer than the waiting period. Once it does, we pay from the start of the interruption. Any Excess applies in addition, as [[ref:bpk.cond.excess]] provides.
