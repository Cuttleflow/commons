heading: Other Insurance
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

If another policy covers the same loss, you must tell us. We will contribute rateably with the other insurer, subject to the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), including s 45. Between [[ref:bpk.section.crime]] and [[ref:bpk.section.cyber]], [[ref:bpk.cond.limits-and-more-than-one-section]] applies, and a loss of funds is paid under [[ref:bpk.section.crime|heading]] where that section is insured.
