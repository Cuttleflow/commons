heading: Retroactive date
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

The date shown in the Schedule as the retroactive date for [[ref:bpk.section.cyber]] or [[ref:bpk.section.management-liability]]. Where no date is shown, it is the inception date of the first policy we issued to you that included that section, without a break in cover.
