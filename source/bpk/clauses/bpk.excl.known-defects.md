type: exclusion
heading: Known Defects
since: 2.1.0
changed: 2.2.0
status: draft
---

Loss or Damage caused by a Defect that a member of the Control group knew about before the Period of Insurance started, or that a reasonable person in that member's position would have known about. This exclusion does not apply to [[ref:bpk.section.liability]].
