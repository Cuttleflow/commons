heading: Limits and More Than One Section
since: 2.1.0
changed: 2.2.0
status: draft
---

Each section limit and Sum insured is the most we pay under that section, as stated in the section. Defence costs under [[ref:bpk.section.liability]] are payable in addition to the limit. Section limits do not share an overall aggregate.

Sections respond together to different parts of the same event. For example, a fire can be paid under [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage]] for the Damage and under [[ref:bpk.section.business-interruption]] for the interruption that follows. Each section pays on its own terms and up to its own limit.

Where the same part of a loss is covered under more than one section, we pay it once only, under one section, and we do not pay the same amount twice. For a loss of funds covered under both [[ref:bpk.section.crime]] and [[ref:bpk.section.cyber]], we pay it under [[ref:bpk.section.crime|heading]] where that section is insured. For any other overlap, we pay it under the section that gives you the greater amount. The limit and terms of the section that pays then apply to that part of the loss.
