heading: Limits and More Than One Section
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Each section limit and Sum insured is the most we pay under that section, as stated in the section. Defence costs under [[ref:bpk.section.liability]] are payable in addition to the limit. Section limits do not share an overall aggregate. If one loss or Claim is covered under more than one section, we pay it under one section only: the section that gives you the greater amount. That section's limit, Excess and terms then apply.
