heading: Social Engineering Verification
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

For [[ref:bpk.crime.cover.social-engineering-fraud]], we expect you to verify a request to change payment details by contacting the requester using contact details you held before the request. This is not a condition of cover. If you did not verify a request, we will not refuse the claim on that ground; we may reduce our payment only to the extent that failing to verify caused or contributed to the loss, as [[ref:bpk.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] and s 54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allow.
