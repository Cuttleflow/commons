---
id: bpk.excl.electronic-data
type: exclusion
heading: Electronic Data
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Loss of, or Damage to, electronic data or software, except as covered under [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage]] (rewriting of records), [[ref:bpk.section.business-interruption]] (to the extent the interruption follows Damage covered under [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage|heading]]), [[ref:bpk.section.equipment]] (data restoration) and [[ref:bpk.section.cyber]].
