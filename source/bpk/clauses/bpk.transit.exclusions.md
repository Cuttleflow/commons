since: 2.1.0

---

In addition to [[ref:bpk.section.general-exclusions]], we do not cover under [[ref:bpk.section.transit]]:

- goods carried for others for reward;
- livestock, plants, cash, jewellery and works of art;
- inadequate packing;
- temperature change, unless caused by a collision or overturning of the conveying vehicle; and
- goods in an unattended vehicle overnight, unless in a locked building or yard.
