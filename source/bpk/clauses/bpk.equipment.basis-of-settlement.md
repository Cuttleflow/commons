heading: Settling Equipment Damage
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We pay the cost of repair, or of replacement with equipment of equivalent specification, less an allowance for wear and tear for items older than the age shown in the Schedule. For electronic equipment we include the cost of reinstating data and programs from backup media up to the data restoration sub-limit.
