These benefits apply at each Situation where [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage|heading]] is insured. Each is paid up to the sub-limit shown in the Schedule (defaults in [[ref:bpk.appendix.default-schedule-values]]) and, unless [[ref:bpk.appendix.default-schedule-values|heading]] says otherwise, in addition to the Sum insured:

- temporary removal of property to other locations;
- debris removal;
- fire brigade charges;
- expediting costs;
- Employees' personal property;
- automatic additions for new property at existing Situations;
- capital additions;
- temporary protection costs;
- landscaping;
- rewriting of records;
- lock replacement following theft of keys;
- prevention of access following insured Damage within the distance shown in the Schedule; and
- loss minimisation costs.
