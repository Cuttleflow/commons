An Endorsement applies only where its code is shown in the Schedule. Each modifies only the clause it names, and is read together with the variables shown for it in the Schedule. Other terms of this Policy continue to apply. If a variable an Endorsement needs is not shown in the Schedule, the default in [[ref:bpk.appendix.default-schedule-values]] applies; if there is none, the Endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the Schedule.

| Code | Endorsement | Section | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| E01 | Accidental Damage | [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage|heading]] | Extends the insured events to accidental Damage, to a sub-limit |
| E02 | Flood | [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage|heading]] | Removes flood or limits it to a sub-limit |
| E03 | Seasonal Stock Increase | [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage|heading]] | Increases the Stock Sum insured in the months shown |
| E04 | Equipment Damage Consequential Loss | [[ref:bpk.section.equipment|heading]] | Covers loss of gross profit after Breakdown |
| E05 | Increased Tenants' Liability | [[ref:bpk.section.liability|heading]] | Increases the tenants' liability sub-limit |
| E06 | Increased Property in Physical or Legal Control | [[ref:bpk.section.liability|heading]] | Increases the property in control sub-limit |
| E07 | Product Recall Costs | [[ref:bpk.section.liability|heading]] | Pays the costs of recalling Your products, to an aggregate sub-limit |
| E08 | Minor Contract Works | [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage|heading]] | Covers property under construction up to a contract value |
| E09 | Interests of Lessors and Financiers | All sections | Notes lessors and financiers as Interested parties |
| E10 | Hot Works | [[ref:bpk.section.liability|heading]] | Writes back hot works away from your Premises, with conditions |
| E11 | Voluntary Workers' Personal Accident | All sections | Pays personal accident benefits for death or Disablement of unpaid voluntary workers |
| E12 | Specified Occupations | Sections shown | Applies conditions for the occupation shown |
