In addition to [[ref:bpk.section.general-exclusions]], we do not cover under [[ref:bpk.section.general-property]]:

- property left in an unattended vehicle, unless locked and concealed from view;
- mobile phones and laptop computers left unattended in a public place;
- cracking or scratching that does not stop the item working; and
- property lent or hired to others.
