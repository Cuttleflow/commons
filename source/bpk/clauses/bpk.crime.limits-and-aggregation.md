The section limit shown in the Schedule is the most we pay for all loss discovered during the Period of Insurance. The custody sub-limits under [[ref:bpk.crime.cover.money]] and the sub-limit under [[ref:bpk.crime.cover.social-engineering-fraud]] are part of, not in addition to, the section limit. A Single loss is treated as one loss, subject to one Excess, and is treated as discovered in the Period of Insurance in which Discovery first occurred.
