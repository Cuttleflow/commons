heading: Underinsurance (Gross Profit)
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

If the gross profit Sum insured is less than the underinsurance percentage shown in the Schedule of the annual gross profit (adjusted for the indemnity period where it exceeds 12 months), we pay only the proportion of the loss that the Sum insured bears to that percentage.
