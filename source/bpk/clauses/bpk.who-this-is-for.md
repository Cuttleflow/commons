This Policy is designed for Australian small and medium businesses. It is not a consumer insurance contract: it is not entered into wholly or predominantly for personal, domestic or household purposes, and the duty of disclosure in [[ref:bpk.cond.duty-of-disclosure]] applies.

Where this Policy is issued to a retail client within the meaning of s 761G of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), the issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a target market determination.
