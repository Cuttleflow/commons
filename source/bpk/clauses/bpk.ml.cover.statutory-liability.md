Defence costs, and fines or penalties to the extent [[ref:bpk.ml.statutory-insurability]] permits, for a proceeding under a statute listed in the Schedule. We do not pay any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured, as [[ref:bpk.ml.statutory-insurability]] says.
