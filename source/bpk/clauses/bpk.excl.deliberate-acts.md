heading: Deliberate Acts
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Loss or Damage that you deliberately cause or allow. For [[ref:bpk.section.crime]], this exclusion applies to the acts of you and your directors only, not to a Dishonest act of an Employee covered under [[ref:bpk.crime.cover.employee-dishonesty]]. For [[ref:bpk.section.management-liability]], [[ref:bpk.ml.conduct]] applies instead of this exclusion.
