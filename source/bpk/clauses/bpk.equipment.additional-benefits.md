Each up to the sub-limit shown in the Schedule (defaults in [[ref:bpk.appendix.default-schedule-values]]):

- expediting costs;
- deterioration of refrigerated Stock;
- hire of temporary replacement equipment;
- data restoration; and
- increased cost of working.
