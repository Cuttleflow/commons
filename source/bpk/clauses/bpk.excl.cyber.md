Loss, Damage, liability or cost caused by a Cyber event. This exclusion does not apply to:

- physical Damage to insured property caused by fire or explosion resulting from a Cyber event;
- [[ref:bpk.crime.cover.computer-fraud]] or [[ref:bpk.crime.cover.social-engineering-fraud]];
- [[ref:bpk.section.liability]];
- [[ref:bpk.section.cyber]]; or
- [[ref:bpk.section.management-liability]].
