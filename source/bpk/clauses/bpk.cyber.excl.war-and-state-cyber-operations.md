heading: War and State Cyber Operations
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
note: Contains a WP REVIEW tag for the Wording Partner.
---

Loss caused by war, or by a cyber operation carried out by or on behalf of a state that has a major detrimental impact on the functioning of a state. This does not apply to your Computer system if it is located outside the state affected and was not itself the target. We must prove that this exclusion applies; in deciding whether a cyber operation is attributable to a state, we may rely on objectively reasonable evidence, including attribution by the government of the state in which your Computer system is located.
