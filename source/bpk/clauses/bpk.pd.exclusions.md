In addition to [[ref:bpk.section.general-exclusions]], we do not cover under [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage]]:

- theft (see [[ref:bpk.section.crime]]);
- breakdown of machinery or electronic equipment (see [[ref:bpk.section.equipment]]);
- glass breakage (see [[ref:bpk.section.glass]]);
- motor vehicles, aircraft and watercraft;
- Money (see [[ref:bpk.section.crime]]);
- land, roads, dams and bridges;
- animals and growing plants, except the landscaping benefit;
- property under construction where the contract value exceeds the amount shown in the Schedule; and
- subsidence or landslip, unless directly caused by an Insured event.
