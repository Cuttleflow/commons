id: bpk.transit.cover
type: cover
heading: Damage to Goods in Transit
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We will pay for Damage to goods you own or are responsible for while in transit within Australia during the Period of Insurance, carried by your vehicles, a professional carrier, Australia Post or a courier, up to the limit any one conveyance shown in the Schedule. We settle at the lesser of cost price, selling price less profit, or replacement cost.
