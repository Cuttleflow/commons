heading: Reinstatement of Sums Insured
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

After a claim under [[ref:bpk.section.property-damage]], [[ref:bpk.section.glass]], [[ref:bpk.section.equipment]], [[ref:bpk.section.general-property]] or [[ref:bpk.section.transit]], the Sum insured is reinstated from the date of loss unless we paid a total loss. We may charge an additional premium for the reinstatement. The section limits of [[ref:bpk.section.crime|heading]], [[ref:bpk.section.tax-audit|heading]], [[ref:bpk.section.cyber|heading]] and [[ref:bpk.section.management-liability|heading]] are aggregate limits and are not reinstated.
