heading: Liability for Injury and Damage
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We will pay the compensation you become legally liable to pay for Personal injury or Property damage that happens during the Period of Insurance within the geographical limits shown in the Schedule, in connection with the Business, and for Personal injury or Property damage caused by Your products after they have left your possession. We also pay defence costs we approve. Defence costs are paid in addition to the limit.
