During the Period of Insurance, you may give us written notice of facts that might give rise to a Claim under [[ref:bpk.section.cyber]] or [[ref:bpk.section.management-liability]], or an Audit notice under [[ref:bpk.section.tax-audit]]. The notice should describe the facts, the persons involved and the potential loss as fully as you can. A Claim or Audit notice later arising from those facts is treated as first made during the Period of Insurance in which the notice was given, as s 40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) provides.
