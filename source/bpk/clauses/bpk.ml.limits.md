The section limit shown in the Schedule is the most we pay for all Claims in the Period of Insurance, including defence costs. Sub-limits shown in the Schedule are part of the section limit. We advance defence costs as they are incurred. If an insured is later found not entitled to them, they must be repaid. Where a Claim involves covered and uncovered matters or parties, we pay the share that fairly reflects the covered part, having regard to the relative legal and financial exposures.
