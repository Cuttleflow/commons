The section limit shown in the Schedule is the most we pay for all Claims in the Period of Insurance, including defence costs. Sub-limits shown in the Schedule are part of the section limit. We advance defence costs as they are incurred. If a Final adjudication, or a court's finding, later establishes that an insured is not entitled to them, the insured must repay them, as [[ref:bpk.ml.corporations-act-199]] and [[ref:bpk.ml.conduct]] provide. Where a Claim involves covered and uncovered matters or parties, we pay the share that fairly reflects the covered part, having regard to the relative legal and financial exposures.
