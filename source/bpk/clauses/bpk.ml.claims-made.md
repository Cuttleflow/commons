All Claims arising from the same, similar or related Wrongful acts are one Claim, first made when the earliest was first made. [[ref:bpk.cond.notice-of-circumstances]] applies. If we cancel or do not renew this section, you may buy an Extended reporting period of 12 months for Claims for Wrongful acts before cancellation or expiry, by paying the additional premium shown in the Schedule within 30 days. Retired Insured persons have the run-off period shown in the Schedule.
