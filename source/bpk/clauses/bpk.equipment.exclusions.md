In addition to [[ref:bpk.section.general-exclusions]], we do not cover under [[ref:bpk.section.equipment]]:

- consumable parts such as belts, tools, bulbs, filters and fuses, unless damaged with another part;
- loss covered by a manufacturer's warranty or maintenance contract;
- gradual deterioration ([[ref:bpk.excl.gradual-causes|heading]]);
- equipment not in working order at the start of the Period of Insurance; and
- consequential loss, unless [[ref:bpk.end.e04]] is operative.
