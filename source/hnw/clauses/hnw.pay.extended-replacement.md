If the rebuilding cost after one event is more than the building sum insured, we pay the rebuilding cost up to the building sum insured plus the extended replacement percentage shown in the schedule (default 25%, or 50% where [[ref:hnw.end.e01]] applies) of the building sum insured.

This applies if, when this policy started or was last renewed, the building sum insured was not less than the rebuilding cost stated in the most recent professional valuation you gave us, or in an estimate made with a rebuilding cost calculator we provided, and you told us of any building works since then that increased the rebuilding cost by more than 10%.

Where [[ref:hnw.end.e09]] applies, extended replacement applies only as that endorsement states.
