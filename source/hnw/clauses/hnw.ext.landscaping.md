We pay the reasonable cost to replace trees, shrubs, plants, hedges and lawns at the home damaged by any sudden and accidental cause other than drought, frost, disease, insects or neglect, and to remove a fallen tree or branch that has damaged the building or blocks access to it, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $50,000) for any one event, and $10,000 for any one tree. This amount is in addition to the building sum insured.
