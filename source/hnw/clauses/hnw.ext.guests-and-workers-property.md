heading: Guests' and Domestic Workers' Property
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We cover property at the home owned by a visitor or a domestic worker, for damage from any sudden and accidental cause, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $10,000) for any one event. [[ref:hnw.pay.other-insurance]] applies.
