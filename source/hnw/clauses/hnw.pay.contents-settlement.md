heading: How We Settle Contents Claims
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We pay the cost of repairing the damaged item, or of replacing it with a new item of the same kind and quality, whichever is less, or we pay you that amount. We do not deduct for wear, tear or depreciation. You may choose the repairer or supplier, as long as the cost is reasonable.

If an item that is part of a pair or set is damaged, we pay the reduction in the value of the pair or set caused by that damage, as [[ref:hnw.pay.pairs-and-sets]] sets out.

We do not reduce a claim because the contents sum insured is less than the replacement value of all your contents.
