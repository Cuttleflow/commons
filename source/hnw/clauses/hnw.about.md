This policy insures a private home of high value, the contents and valuables of the household that lives in it, the household's legal liability, and the household against the cost of a home invasion, a car-jacking, identity fraud and a cyber event.

The schedule shows which of the property sections apply and their sums insured. Personal Liability and Family Protection and Cyber apply to every policy.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Home Building | Damage to the building from any sudden and accidental cause | Damage during the policy period |
| Contents | Damage to your contents from any sudden and accidental cause, at the home and anywhere in the world | Damage during the policy period |
| Valuables | Damage to jewellery, watches, art, antiques, collectables, wine, musical instruments and other valuables, in each category the schedule shows | Damage during the policy period |
| Personal Liability | Your legal liability for injury or property damage, anywhere in the world | Occurrence during the policy period |
| Family Protection and Cyber | The cost of a home invasion, a car-jacking, identity fraud or a cyber event | Event during the policy period |

The extensions are part of the section they appear in. Further cover, and the terms that apply to particular homes, are added by endorsement.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies.
