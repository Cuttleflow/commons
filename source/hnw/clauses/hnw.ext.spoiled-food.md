heading: Spoiled Food and Medicine
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We pay for food and medicine in a fridge, freezer or cool room at the home that spoils because the equipment breaks down, or because the electricity supply fails for more than 12 hours, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $5,000) for any one event. No excess applies.
