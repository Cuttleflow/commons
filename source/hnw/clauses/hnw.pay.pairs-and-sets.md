heading: Pairs and Sets
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If an item that is part of a pair or set suffers damage, we pay the reduction in the value of the pair or set caused by that damage.

If you prefer, you may give us the undamaged items in the pair or set, and we will then pay the value of the whole pair or set. For a specified item, that value is its agreed value. For unspecified valuables, the pair or set counts as one item and the unspecified item limit applies to it. For contents, the limits in [[ref:hnw.pay.contents-limits]] apply to it.
