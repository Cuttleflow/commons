---
id: hnw.excl.cyber
type: exclusion
heading: Electronic Data and Computer Systems
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Except under [[ref:hnw.section.family-protection]], we do not pay for loss of, damage to or corruption of electronic data, or for loss arising from unauthorised use of a computer system, other than physical damage to the building, contents or valuables by a resulting fire or explosion. Unauthorised use means use of a computer system by a person not permitted by its owner to use it.
