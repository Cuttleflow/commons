since: 1.0.0

---

Tell us during the policy period, as soon as reasonably practicable, if:

- the home will be unoccupied for more than 100 consecutive days;
- you start or plan building works costing more than $250,000, or works that affect the structure of the building;
- you let the home, or any part of it, or start using it mainly for a business;
- you acquire valuables that you want to insure, beyond the cover [[ref:hnw.ext.new-acquisitions]] gives;
- you move.

We will tell you whether the change affects your cover or premium.
