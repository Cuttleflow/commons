For contents that are not valuables, the most we pay for any one event is:

| Contents | Limit |
|---|---|
| Any one item of jewellery or any one watch | $25,000 |
| All jewellery and watches together | $50,000 |
| Silverware, and items of gold or silver | $100,000 |
| Any one artwork, antique or collectable | $25,000 |
| Wine and spirits | $25,000 |
| Money, gift cards and negotiable instruments | $2,500 |
| Documents, including the cost of replacing identity documents | $10,000 |
| Business equipment used at the home | $50,000 |
| Contents in the open air at the home | $25,000 |
| Watercraft up to 4 metres long, and their accessories | $10,000 |

The schedule may show different limits. Valuables are insured under [[ref:hnw.section.valuables]] up to the limits in [[ref:hnw.pay.valuables-most-we-pay]], and not under these limits.
