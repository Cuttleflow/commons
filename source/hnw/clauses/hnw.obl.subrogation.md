type: obligation
heading: Recovery From Others
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

After we pay a claim, we may take over your rights to recover the amount from anyone else, and act in your name. Help us do so, at our cost. We will not exercise a right of recovery against a member of your household, a domestic worker or a guest, unless they caused the loss deliberately.
