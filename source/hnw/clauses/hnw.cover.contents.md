If your contents suffer damage during the policy period from any sudden and accidental cause, we will repair or replace them, as set out in [[ref:hnw.pay.contents-settlement]].

The cover applies at the home, and anywhere in the world while the contents are with a member of your household or are being moved by a professional removalist, unless an exclusion in [[ref:hnw.section.property-exclusions]] or [[ref:hnw.section.general-exclusions]] applies.

The most we will pay for any one event is the contents sum insured, subject to [[ref:hnw.pay.contents-limits]], plus the amounts the extensions to this section say are payable in addition.
