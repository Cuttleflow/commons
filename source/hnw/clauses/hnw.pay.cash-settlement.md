**When we pay cash.** If you ask us to, or if the law does not allow the building to be repaired or rebuilt, we pay you the amount set out below instead of repairing or rebuilding. We will not pay cash instead of repairing or rebuilding without your agreement, unless the law does not allow the repair or rebuilding.

**How we work out the amount.** The amount is the reasonable cost of repairing or rebuilding the damaged part of the building at the date we make the offer, as [[ref:hnw.pay.building-settlement]] sets out, worked out from a written scope of works and at least two quotes from licensed builders, or an estimate by a quantity surveyor. It includes the costs that provision says are included, and any amount payable under [[ref:hnw.pay.extended-replacement]]. We do not reduce it by any discount or rate that a builder we appoint would have given us.

**What we give you.** With the offer we give you in writing the scope of works, the quotes or estimate we relied on, how we worked out the amount, and any amount we deducted and why. You may give us your own quote or estimate, and we will consider it and tell you in writing whether we change the offer and why. You have at least 20 business days to consider the offer before it is withdrawn.

**After you accept.** Once we have paid the agreed amount, we have no further liability for the damage it settled, other than for temporary accommodation already payable under [[ref:hnw.ext.temporary-accommodation]] up to the date of payment.
