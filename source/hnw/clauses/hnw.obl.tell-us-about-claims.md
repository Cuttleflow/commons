heading: Tell Us About Claims
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after damage, an occurrence, a home invasion, a car-jacking, identity fraud or a cyber event that may lead to a claim, and tell the police promptly about any theft, malicious damage, home invasion or car-jacking.

Give us the information and documents we reasonably ask for to assess the claim, including proof of ownership or value for contents and valuables, and allow us to inspect the damaged property before it is repaired, unless emergency repairs are needed under [[ref:hnw.ext.emergency-repairs]].
