heading: Damage to the Building
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If the building suffers damage during the policy period from any sudden and accidental cause, including fire, storm, flood, earthquake, escape of liquid, theft, malicious damage and impact, we will pay to repair or rebuild it, as set out in [[ref:hnw.pay.building-settlement]].

This cover applies to damage from any sudden and accidental cause unless an exclusion in [[ref:hnw.section.property-exclusions]] or [[ref:hnw.section.general-exclusions]] applies.

The most we will pay for any one event is the building sum insured together with any amount payable under [[ref:hnw.pay.extended-replacement]], plus the amounts the extensions to this section say are payable in addition.
