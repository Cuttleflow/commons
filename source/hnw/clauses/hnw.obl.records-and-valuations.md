heading: Records and Valuations
since: 1.0.0

---

Keep a professional valuation, or a receipt from the seller, for each specified item whose agreed value is $10,000 or more, or the higher amount shown in the schedule, and a photograph of each specified item. Give them to us if we ask.

We recommend a new professional valuation of each specified item, and of the rebuilding cost of the building, at least every three years. If you give us one, we will update the agreed value or the building sum insured when we next issue a schedule, and adjust the premium for the rest of the policy period.
