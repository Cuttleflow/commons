heading: Temporary Accommodation
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If damage covered by the Home Building section makes the home unfit to live in, we pay the reasonable cost of temporary accommodation of a similar standard for your household, your domestic workers who live at the home and your pets, and the reasonable cost of moving and storing your contents, until the home is fit to live in again.

The most we pay for any one event is 20% of the building sum insured, for up to 24 months, or the higher limit and period shown in the schedule. These limits are in addition to the building sum insured.

If the home is your second home, we pay instead the rent you lose, or the reasonable cost of comparable accommodation for the periods your household had planned to stay, within the same limits.
