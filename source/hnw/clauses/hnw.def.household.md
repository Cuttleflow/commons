---
id: hnw.def.household
type: definition
heading: Household
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You, your spouse or partner, and your and their children and other relatives who live with you at the home. Where [[ref:hnw.end.e13]] applies, it means the people named for it in the schedule and their spouses, partners, children and relatives who live with them at the home. It does not include a tenant or boarder who pays to live at the home, or a domestic worker.
