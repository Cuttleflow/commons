We pay to repair or replace an electric motor in the building or in your contents, including a motor in a pool pump, air conditioner, gate, lift, refrigerator or washing machine, that burns out during the policy period, if the motor is less than 15 years old. The most we pay for any one motor is the limit shown in the schedule (default $25,000), and the basic excess applies. We do not pay for a motor while the manufacturer's guarantee covers it.
