Each category is covered in the places shown in this table.

| Category | Where it is covered |
|---|---|
| Jewellery and watches | Anywhere in the world |
| Jewellery and watches in a vault | In the vault, and anywhere in the world while out of the vault as [[ref:hnw.end.e10]] allows |
| Other valuables | Anywhere in the world |
| Fine art; antiques and decorative arts; collectables; wine; musical instruments | At the home; at any other address in Australia for up to 90 consecutive days; and in transit between them within Australia |

The schedule may show wider places of cover for a category.
