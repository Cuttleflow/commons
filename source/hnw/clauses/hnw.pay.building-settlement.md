**Repair or rebuild.** We pay the cost of repairing or rebuilding the damaged part of the building to a condition substantially the same as, but not better or more extensive than, its condition when new, using materials and methods of a quality equal to the original. Where the original materials or methods are no longer available, we use the nearest equivalent of equal quality. We do not deduct for wear, tear or depreciation.

**Costs included.** The cost of repairing or rebuilding includes the reasonable cost of demolishing and removing debris, the fees of architects, engineers, surveyors and other professionals needed to repair or rebuild, and the extra cost of complying with the building laws in force at the time of repair for the damaged part of the building.

**Matching materials.** If we cannot find materials that match the undamaged parts, we pay to replace the materials in the room or area where the damage happened, and in any adjoining area that can be seen together with it, so that the result reasonably matches.

**How we arrange the work.** You may choose the builder, as long as the builder is licensed for the work and the quote is reasonable. We may instead offer a builder we appoint, who guarantees the work for at least six years. We pay the builder directly or reimburse you as the work is done.

**Cash settlement.** You may choose to be paid instead of having the building repaired or rebuilt, as [[ref:hnw.pay.cash-settlement]] sets out.

**No under-insurance penalty.** We do not reduce a claim because the building sum insured is less than the full rebuilding cost. [[ref:hnw.pay.extended-replacement]] sets out what we pay above the building sum insured.
