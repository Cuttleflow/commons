heading: Damage to Valuables
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If any of your valuables suffer damage during the policy period, in a place where [[ref:hnw.cover.valuables-where-covered]] says they are covered, we will pay for the damage as set out in [[ref:hnw.pay.valuables-settlement]].

This cover applies to damage from any cause, including theft and disappearance that cannot be explained, unless an exclusion in [[ref:hnw.section.property-exclusions]] or [[ref:hnw.section.general-exclusions]] applies. The most we pay is set out in [[ref:hnw.pay.valuables-most-we-pay]].
