The building is listed on a heritage register. We pay the extra cost of repairing or rebuilding heritage features using the methods, materials and trades the heritage authority requires, up to the heritage limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement, in addition to the building sum insured and any extended replacement.

Schedule variables: heritage limit (default 20% of the building sum insured).
