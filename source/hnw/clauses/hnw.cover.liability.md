We will pay the amount a member of your household becomes legally liable to pay as compensation for personal injury or property damage that happens during the policy period anywhere in the world, caused by an occurrence:

- at the home, in connection with your ownership or occupation of it; or
- anywhere else, in connection with the personal activities of a member of your household.

Where you are a company or the trustee of a trust, we also pay the amount you become legally liable to pay as owner of the home.

**Domestic workers.** We pay the amount you become legally liable to pay for personal injury to a domestic worker arising from their work at the home, where no workers compensation law requires you to insure that liability.

**Limit.** The most we pay for all compensation arising from one occurrence is the limit of liability shown in the schedule (default $20,000,000). All personal injury and property damage arising from one source or original cause is one occurrence.

**Defence costs.** In addition to the limit, we pay the reasonable legal costs we incur or approve in defending or settling a claim covered by this section, and interest on a judgment up to the date we pay or offer to pay it.

**Settlement.** We may take over and conduct the defence or settlement of any claim in your name. We may at any time pay you the limit, less any amount already paid, and then we have no further obligation for that occurrence except for defence costs already incurred.

**Courts outside Australia.** For a claim brought in a court in the United States of America or Canada, defence costs are included within the limit, not in addition to it.
