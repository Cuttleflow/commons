heading: Accommodation Where Only Contents Are Insured
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If the Home Building section does not apply and damage from a sudden and accidental cause makes the home unfit to live in, we pay the reasonable cost of temporary accommodation of a similar standard for your household, and of moving and storing your contents, until the home is fit to live in again or your tenancy ends, up to 20% of the contents sum insured, for up to 24 months. If you rent the home, we pay only the amount by which the temporary accommodation costs more than the rent you no longer have to pay.
