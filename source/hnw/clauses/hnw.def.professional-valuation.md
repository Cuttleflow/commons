---
id: hnw.def.professional-valuation
type: definition
heading: Professional Valuation
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A written valuation by a valuer qualified in the kind of property valued, who is not selling it to you, that describes the property and states its replacement value or rebuilding cost at the date of the valuation. For the building, it includes a written estimate of the rebuilding cost by a quantity surveyor, a registered builder, or a risk consultant appointed by us.
