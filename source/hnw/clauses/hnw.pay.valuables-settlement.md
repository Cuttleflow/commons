**Specified items.** If a specified item is lost, stolen or damaged beyond economic repair, we pay you its agreed value. With your agreement, we may replace it instead. If it can be repaired economically, we pay the reasonable cost of repair by a repairer or restorer that you and we agree on, and you may not unreasonably withhold your agreement. [[ref:hnw.ext.loss-in-value]] may also apply.

**Unspecified items.** For an unspecified item, we may repair it, replace it with an item of the same kind and quality, or pay you what it would cost to do so. We choose the option that costs least, and we do not deduct for wear, tear or depreciation. The most we pay for any one unspecified item is the unspecified item limit shown in the schedule for its category (default $25,000).

**No reduction for underinsurance.** We do not reduce a claim because a category sum insured is less than the value of the items in that category. The limits in [[ref:hnw.pay.valuables-most-we-pay]] still apply.
