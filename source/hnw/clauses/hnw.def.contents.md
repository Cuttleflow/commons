Household goods and personal effects that you or a member of your household own, or for which you are legally responsible, including furniture, furnishings, carpets and floor coverings that are not fixed, curtains and blinds, clothing, electrical appliances and electronics, jewellery and watches, art, wine, tools and garden equipment, and sporting equipment.

It does not include money other than as [[ref:hnw.pay.contents-limits]] provides; vehicles, trailers, caravans, aircraft and drones (other than ride-on mowers, mobility scooters, toys and e-bikes not needing registration); watercraft longer than 4 metres; animals; plants in the ground; property held for sale or used mainly for a business; or property of a tenant or boarder.

An item in a category for which the schedule shows a sum insured under [[ref:hnw.section.valuables]] is insured only under that section and is not contents.
