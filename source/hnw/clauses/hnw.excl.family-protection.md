Under the Family Protection and Cyber section, we do not pay:

- a ransom or extortion payment, or the value of property handed over in response to a threat;
- for a home invasion, car-jacking, identity fraud or cyber event carried out by, or with the involvement of, a member of your household;
- for loss of money or data of a business, or of a device used mainly for a business;
- for money taken or transferred, unless it was reported to the bank or financial institution, and to the police, within 48 hours of its discovery.

[[ref:hnw.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to the last exclusion in this list.
