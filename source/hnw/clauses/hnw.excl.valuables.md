Under the Valuables section, we do not pay for:

- breakage of a fragile item while it is being moved, unless it was packed by a professional packer, or the breakage is caused by fire, theft, or an accident to the vehicle carrying it;
- theft of an item left in an unattended vehicle, unless the vehicle was locked and the item was out of sight;
- wine or spirits spoiled by a change in temperature, unless the change is caused by sudden damage to the cellar or its equipment from an external cause;
- wear of strings, reeds, skins or bows of musical instruments;
- an item that is confiscated, or that you must give up because another person proves a better title to it.
