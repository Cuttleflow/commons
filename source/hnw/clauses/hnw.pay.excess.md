You pay the basic excess shown in the schedule for each event that gives rise to a claim under the Home Building, Contents or Valuables sections. If one event gives rise to claims under more than one of those sections, only one basic excess is deducted.

The schedule may show a special excess for damage by flood, storm, bushfire or cyclone (see [[ref:hnw.end.e02]], [[ref:hnw.end.e03]] and [[ref:hnw.end.e04]]). Where a special excess applies to the damage, it replaces the basic excess. No excess applies to the Personal Liability or Family Protection and Cyber sections, or where an extension says that no excess applies.

If the building is destroyed and we pay the building sum insured, no excess applies to that claim.
