heading: The Most We Pay for Valuables
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For any one event, after deducting the excess, the most we pay is:

- for a specified item, its agreed value, together with any amount payable under [[ref:hnw.ext.loss-in-value]] within the limit that extension sets;
- for an unspecified item, the unspecified item limit for its category; and
- for all items in a category, the category sum insured.

The amounts payable under [[ref:hnw.ext.new-acquisitions]] are in addition to these limits, up to the amount that extension sets.
