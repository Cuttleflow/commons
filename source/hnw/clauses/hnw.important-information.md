**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

**Who this policy is for.** This wording is designed for retail clients who own a home of high value in Australia and live in it, or use it as a second home. It is sold through insurance brokers. An issuer using it must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available, as the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) requires. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.

**Your duty when you apply.** This policy is a consumer insurance contract under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). You have a duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated. Answer every question in the proposal honestly and completely. If you do not take reasonable care, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

**Terms that differ from standard cover.** The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) and the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017 set standard cover for home building and home contents contracts. In most respects this policy gives more cover than standard cover. It gives less cover than standard cover in each of the ways listed here. They are listed so that you are clearly informed of each of them before this policy is entered into.

- flood is covered, but we do not pay for damage by storm or flood in the first 72 hours after this policy first starts, except as [[ref:hnw.excl.first-72-hours]] allows;
- we do not pay for damage by erosion, subsidence or landslip except as [[ref:hnw.excl.earth-movement]] allows, or for damage by action of the sea (see [[ref:hnw.excl.action-of-the-sea]]);
- we do not pay to fix a defect, or for damage from gradual causes, insects, vermin or tree roots (see [[ref:hnw.excl.defects-and-gradual-causes]]);
- cover is limited while the home is unoccupied for more than 100 days (see [[ref:hnw.excl.unoccupied]]);
- we do not pay for the matters listed in [[ref:hnw.excl.property-other]], including theft or deliberate damage by a member of your household, land, loss of market value, an order of a government or public authority, damage while property is being worked on, breakdown, and damage by an animal kept at the home;
- we do not pay for the matters listed in [[ref:hnw.excl.valuables]] for valuables;
- we do not pay for loss, damage or liability caused deliberately by you, by a member of your household or by a person acting with your authority, or arising from illegal activity by you or a member of your household that you knew of or had reason to suspect (see [[ref:hnw.excl.deliberate-acts]] and [[ref:hnw.excl.illegal-use]]);
- we do not pay for loss of, damage to or corruption of electronic data, or for loss from unauthorised use of a computer system, except as [[ref:hnw.section.family-protection]] provides (see [[ref:hnw.excl.cyber]]);
- we do not pay for loss, damage, liability or cost arising from terrorism, war or nuclear risks (see [[ref:hnw.excl.terrorism]], [[ref:hnw.excl.war]] and [[ref:hnw.excl.nuclear]]);
- we pay no more than the limits in [[ref:hnw.pay.contents-limits]] for some contents, and no more than the category sums insured and item limits in [[ref:hnw.pay.valuables-most-we-pay]] for valuables;
- the extensions to each section are limited as each states;
- the excesses shown in the schedule apply to each claim under the property sections (see [[ref:hnw.pay.excess]]).

**Key Facts Sheet.** A Key Facts Sheet for the Home Building section and one for the Contents section are given with this policy, as the law requires.

**Cooling-off period.** You may cancel this policy within 21 days of the date it starts, and within 21 days of each renewal, by telling us in writing. If you have not made a claim, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover. After that, you may still cancel under [[ref:hnw.cond.cancellation]].

**Commission and fees.** Your broker may be paid a commission by us, or may charge you a fee instead of or as well as a commission. The schedule or the quote shows any commission or fee paid to your broker in connection with this policy.

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
