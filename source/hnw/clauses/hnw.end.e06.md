Building works described in the schedule for this endorsement may be carried out at the home. [[ref:hnw.excl.unoccupied]] does not apply while the works are under way and a builder licensed for them is responsible for the site. We do not pay for damage to the works themselves, to materials not yet fixed to the building, or for damage that results from the works. When the works are finished, tell us the new rebuilding cost.

Schedule variables: description and contract value of the works; expected completion date.
