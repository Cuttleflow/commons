heading: Home Invasion and Car-Jacking
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If a member of your household suffers a home invasion or a car-jacking during the policy period, we pay the reasonable cost of:

- counselling for members of your household by a registered psychologist or psychiatrist;
- temporary accommodation of a similar standard for your household for up to 30 days, if they choose not to stay at the home;
- replacing locks and improving the security of the home or vehicle on the advice of a security consultant;
- medical expenses not recoverable from Medicare, a health insurer or a compulsory third party scheme.

The most we pay for any one home invasion or car-jacking is the family protection limit shown in the schedule (default $50,000). No excess applies.
