Under the Home Building, Contents and Valuables sections, we do not pay for:

- theft by, or damage caused deliberately by, a member of your household, or by a person at the home with your permission other than a tradesperson or a domestic worker;
- land, or the cost of stabilising or improving land;
- loss of market value, other than as [[ref:hnw.ext.loss-in-value]] provides;
- damage to a retaining wall that is not built to the standard of the building laws in force when it was built;
- damage by water entering the building through an opening made for building works;
- damage caused by a lawful order or action of a government or public authority, other than to prevent the spread of fire or other damage;
- damage to property while it is being worked on, cleaned, restored or repaired, if the damage results from the work, other than damage by fire or theft;
- mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdown, other than as [[ref:hnw.ext.motor-burnout]] provides, or damage that results suddenly and accidentally from it;
- damage by an animal kept at the home, other than damage that is sudden and is not caused by chewing, scratching, pecking or soiling.

[[ref:hnw.cond.joint-insureds]] applies to the first exclusion in this list.
