This is the first release of the Meridian Expatriate Medical wording. It is built on the Meridian Corporate Travel base wording version 1.1.0-DRAFT, restated as an annual international medical plan. It differs from the base in these ways:

- Who is covered: assignees (directors, officers and employees living in Australia sent to work overseas for more than 3 months and up to 5 years) and their dependants in the host country, aged 74 or under at the start or renewal. New definitions: Area of Cover, Assignment, Dependant and Host Country. Home, Insured Person and Trip are redefined.
- International Medical Plan replaces the travel medical section, with Standard and Premier plans covering inpatient, cancer, outpatient, mental health, maternity (Premier), dental, evacuation and repatriation. Limits and Excess applies annual limits and an annual excess.
- The personal accident, disruption, luggage, business expenses, liability, kidnap and crisis, and rental vehicle sections are removed, with the definitions only they used.
- What the Law Does Not Allow Us to Pay is restated for assignees; Important Information explains why the policy is not health insurance, with a WP REVIEW tag on rule 15 of the Private Health Insurance (Health Insurance Business) Rules 2018 (Cth).
- War now excludes only active participation. Government Travel Advice and Nuclear, Biological and Chemical Events are restated for treatment, and E03 no longer refers to luggage. Pregnancy and Childbirth, and Treatment We Do Not Pay For, are restated for a medical plan. The epidemic, known events and manual work exclusions are removed.
- Declarations is restated for assignees and dependants.
- Endorsements E02 and E04 to E10 are removed. New endorsements: E11 Lloyd's Underwriters, E12 Home Leave in Australia (refers, with a WP REVIEW tag), E13 USA Travel and E14 Routine Dental and Optical.
