This is the first release of the Meridian Hostile Environment Travel wording. It is built on the Meridian Corporate Travel base wording version 1.1.0-DRAFT. It differs from the base in these ways:

- War and Terrorism replaces the war exclusion: war and terrorism are covered unless the insured person takes an active part, except war between major powers and nuclear, chemical or biological weapons. The Terrorism Insurance Act 2003 (Cth) does not apply to this class, so the wording sets its own position.
- Government Travel Advice covers approved destinations where DFAT advice is "reconsider your need to travel" or "do not travel".
- New definitions: Active Participation, Approved Destination and Hostile Environment Training.
- Security Precautions and Training is a new obligation: hostile environment training, a written security plan and Smartraveller registration before travel to an approved destination, applied through s.54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).
- The kidnap, extortion and crisis response section is unchanged, and is always referred to an underwriter in Go.
- New endorsements: E11 Lloyd's Underwriters, E12 Embedded Journalists and E13 Security Evacuation Limit Increase.
