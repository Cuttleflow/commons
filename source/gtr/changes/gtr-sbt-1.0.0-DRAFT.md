This is the first release of the Meridian Small Business Travel wording. It is built on the Meridian Corporate Travel base wording version 1.1.0-DRAFT. It differs from the base in these ways:

- Named travellers. Who Is Covered names up to 20 directors, partners, officers and employees, aged 74 or under at the start or renewal. Named Traveller is a new definition. Changes to Named Travellers replaces the trip declarations: the premium is per named traveller, adjusted pro rata when one is added or removed.
- Leisure travel. When You Are Covered covers business trips, leisure travel around them and leisure trips on their own, up to the leisure travel allowance (default 42 days a year for each named traveller), in the area shown (worldwide, worldwide excluding the Americas, or Australia only), up to a maximum trip duration of 30, 60 or 90 days. Trip is redefined.
- Important Information states that a small business is a retail client for this policy.
- Endorsements E02 Leisure Extension, E06 Long-Term Assignment and E09 Domestic Travel Only are removed because the leisure allowance, the maximum trip duration and the area now cover them. New endorsements: E11 Lloyd's Underwriters and E12 Business Equipment Increase.

For underwriter review before release: the leisure-only trips for every named traveller, which is wider than the base.
