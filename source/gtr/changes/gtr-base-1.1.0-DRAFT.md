Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026.

- Important Information: adds a statement of who the policy is for, written for businesses and professionals and recognising that a small business may be a retail client, and a note for issuers about the Product Disclosure Statement and Target Market Determination. The open Wording Partner question on retail use stays.
- Medical and Evacuation Expenses: the first benefit now pays medical expenses outside Australia and, for an insured person who is not Medicare-eligible, in Australia, which matches the Medicare provision, the 'When They Are Covered' clause and Endorsement E09. There is now a default limit of $5,000,000 for each insured person for each trip, and a default limit of $20,000,000 for all insured persons for one event, with proportional reduction. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Medical and Evacuation Expenses: whether evacuation or repatriation is medically necessary is now decided by the treating medical practitioner together with our emergency assistance service, with an independent opinion if they disagree. If an insured person refuses a recommended repatriation, we may reduce payment only by the extent of the prejudice to us.
- Limits and Excess: a limit for each insured person now applies for each trip unless the schedule shows that it applies for the whole policy period. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Personal Accident Benefits: the table now has a full scale for fingers, thumbs and toes (Events 12 to 15), and Event 16 (permanent partial disablement) uses the Fifth Edition of the American Medical Association Guides unless the schedule shows another edition. The old references to a scale 'we publish' and to the edition 'we use at the time' are removed. Default set for underwriter confirmation. A Wording Partner question is added on the edition.
- Personal Accident Benefits, Permanent and Permanent Total Disablement: an event that must be permanent now only needs to start within 12 months of the accident, and is assessed once 12 months have passed. This removes the conflict between the 12-month window and the 12-month test. There is a new definition of Permanent.
- Personal Accident Benefits: the weekly benefit is reduced only by workers compensation, transport accident compensation or other statutory income replacement payments, and not by any personal accident, sickness or income protection policy.
- Kidnap, Extortion and Crisis Response: the clause now applies only to kidnap, extortion and unlawful detention. Security evacuation and terrorism response are now their own clauses, Security Evacuation and Terrorism Response, that apply whether or not the schedule shows kidnap cover. This removes the contradiction. Extortion and Unlawful Detention are now defined. War, Nuclear, Biological and Chemical, and Travel Against Government Advice refer to the new clauses.
- Cancellation, Curtailment and Delay and Travel Against Government Advice: advice raised to 'do not travel' after booking now clearly gives cancellation cover where the insured person cancels before the trip begins, and the government advice exclusion applies if the insured person travels anyway. The exclusion for amounts payable under a credit card or other scheme is removed.
- Other Insurance: replaced with the library's standard text and now covers every section, including medical, personal accident and kidnap. We no longer reduce payment because of other insurance. It also sets one priority rule with the personal accident and sickness wordings: this policy pays first for an injury or sickness on a trip it covers, a personal accident policy that names it pays its weekly and additional benefits only after it, and capital benefits are paid in full by both. The recovery paragraph is now its own clause, Recovery. The open Wording Partner question is removed.
- New definitions: Control Group, Dependent Child, Natural Disaster, Travelling Companion, Extortion, Unlawful Detention and Permanent. Known Event now turns on what a member of the control group or the insured person knew, instead of what 'you' knew.
- Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): replaced with the library's standard text. Section 54 now applies expressly to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation.
- Sanctions: replaced with the library's standard text, naming the Australian, United Nations, United Kingdom, United States and European Union regimes. A Wording Partner question is added.
- Cancellation: replaced with the library's standard text. The policyholder may cancel at any time for a pro-rata refund less non-refundable government charges.
