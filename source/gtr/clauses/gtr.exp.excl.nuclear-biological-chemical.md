---
id: gtr.exp.excl.nuclear-biological-chemical
type: exclusion
heading: Nuclear, Biological and Chemical Events
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for treatment of an injury or sickness caused by ionising radiation or contamination by radioactivity from any nuclear fuel, nuclear waste or nuclear weapon, or by the use of a nuclear, biological or chemical weapon. This exclusion does not apply to treatment for exposure in medical diagnosis or treatment.
