This policy pays the medical, hospital, evacuation and repatriation costs of employees of an Australian organisation who are on assignment outside Australia, and of their dependants who live with them in the host country. It is an annual international medical plan: it covers planned and unplanned treatment in the area of cover, not only emergencies.

You, the organisation named in the schedule, are the policyholder. The people covered are the insured persons described in [[ref:gtr.cover.who-is-covered]]. Benefits are paid to the provider, to you or to the insured person, as [[ref:gtr.pay.who-we-pay]] states.

This policy is not workers compensation, private health insurance or life insurance, and it does not replace any of them. It pays only because the insured persons are Australian residents temporarily employed outside Australia, and their dependants, and it does not pay for treatment in Australia except as Endorsement E12 allows (see [[ref:gtr.pay.medicare]]).

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and declarations, and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:gtr.section.definitions]].

This wording is the Meridian Corporate Travel base wording restated as an international medical plan for assignees. It is a complete wording in its own right.
