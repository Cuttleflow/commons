This policy covers the people your organisation sends to countries and regions with high security risks, such as staff of aid and development agencies, journalists and project staff, for the medical, accident, disruption, property, liability and security events set out in it. Unlike the base wording, it covers injury from war and terrorism unless the insured person takes an active part, and it covers travel to approved destinations where DFAT advice is "reconsider your need to travel" or "do not travel".

You, the organisation named in the schedule, are the policyholder. The people covered are the insured persons described in [[ref:gtr.cover.who-is-covered]]. Some benefits are paid to you, and some to the insured person or their estate, as each section states.

This policy is not workers compensation, private health insurance or life insurance, and it does not replace any of them. It cannot pay any medical expense in Australia for which a Medicare benefit is payable (see [[ref:gtr.pay.medicare]]).

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and declarations, and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:gtr.section.definitions]].

This wording is the Meridian Corporate Travel base wording with the changes needed for travel to high-risk destinations. It is a complete wording in its own right.
