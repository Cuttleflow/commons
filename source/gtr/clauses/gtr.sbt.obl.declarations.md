heading: Changes to Named Travellers
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You may add or remove a named traveller at any time by telling us. Cover for an added named traveller starts when we confirm it in writing, and we charge additional premium for the rest of the policy period on a pro-rata basis. If you remove a named traveller, we refund the premium for that traveller for the rest of the policy period on a pro-rata basis, unless a claim has been paid for them.

If you have more than 20 named travellers, this policy no longer fits your business. Tell us, and we will offer you a policy on a different basis.
