heading: Emergencies
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

In a medical emergency, kidnap, extortion or security event, the insured person or someone with them must contact the 24-hour emergency assistance service on the number shown in the schedule as soon as practicable. In a life-threatening emergency, they should first call local emergency services. These obligations are subject to [[ref:gtr.cond.insurance-contracts-act]].
