This clause applies only if the schedule shows that kidnap, extortion and crisis response cover applies. The security evacuation and terrorism response benefits are in [[ref:gtr.cover.security-evacuation]] and [[ref:gtr.cover.terrorism-response]], and apply whether or not this clause applies.

If, during a trip, an insured person is the subject of a kidnap, an extortion or an unlawful detention, we will pay, up to the limit shown in the schedule for each event (default $250,000), for all of the following together:
- the fees and expenses of the crisis response consultant we appoint;
- a ransom or extortion payment made with our prior written consent, where paying it is lawful, including the loss of that payment in transit;
- the insured person's salary for the period of the kidnap, extortion or detention, and reasonable travel, accommodation and medical costs on their release; and
- counselling for the insured person and their immediate family, up to the sub-limit shown in the schedule (default $25,000 for each insured person).

You must keep this clause, and the fact that you hold it, confidential, unless disclosure is required by law or to us. If this obligation is breached, [[ref:gtr.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies.

We do not pay:
- for a kidnap, extortion or unlawful detention in a country that had a DFAT advice of "do not travel" when the trip began, unless we agreed in writing to cover the trip;
- for a payment to a person or body that is a listed terrorist organisation, or that would be an offence under the Criminal Code Act 1995 (Cth) or would breach [[ref:gtr.cond.sanctions]]; or
- for a kidnap, extortion or unlawful detention planned or carried out with the involvement of you, an insured person, or a member of their immediate family.
