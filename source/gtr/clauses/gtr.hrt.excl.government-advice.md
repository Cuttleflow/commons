---
id: gtr.hrt.excl.government-advice
type: exclusion
heading: Government Travel Advice
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay any claim arising in a country or region for which DFAT advice was "reconsider your need to travel" or "do not travel" when the trip, or that part of it, began, unless it is an approved destination for that insured person and those dates.

This exclusion does not apply to the benefit in [[ref:gtr.cover.security-evacuation]] for an insured person who was already there when the advice was raised, or to a claim for cancellation under [[ref:gtr.cover.disruption]] where the advice was raised after the trip was booked and the insured person cancelled because of it.
