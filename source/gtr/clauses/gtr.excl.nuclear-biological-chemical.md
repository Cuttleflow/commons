heading: Nuclear, Biological and Chemical
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay any claim caused by ionising radiation or contamination by radioactivity from any nuclear fuel, nuclear waste or nuclear weapon, or by the use of a biological or chemical weapon, except as [[ref:gtr.cover.terrorism-response]] provides, up to its sub-limit.
