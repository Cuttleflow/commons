This policy covers the named travellers of a small business on business trips, within Australia and overseas, and on leisure trips up to the limit in [[ref:gtr.cover.trip]], for the medical, accident, disruption, property, liability and security events set out in it. It is an annual policy: it covers every trip that starts during the policy period, and you do not need to declare trips.

You, the business named in the schedule, are the policyholder. The people covered are the insured persons described in [[ref:gtr.cover.who-is-covered]]. Some benefits are paid to you, and some to the insured person or their estate, as each section states.

This policy is not workers compensation, private health insurance or life insurance, and it does not replace any of them. It cannot pay any medical expense in Australia for which a Medicare benefit is payable (see [[ref:gtr.pay.medicare]]).

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:gtr.section.definitions]].

This wording is the Meridian Corporate Travel base wording with the changes needed for small businesses with named travellers. It is a complete wording in its own right.
