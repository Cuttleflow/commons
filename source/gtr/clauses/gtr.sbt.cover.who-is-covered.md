The following people are insured persons while they are on a trip:

- the named travellers: the directors, partners, officers and employees of the policyholder named in the schedule, up to 20 people;
- accompanying family members of a named traveller, if the schedule shows that they are included for that traveller; and
- contractors, consultants and guests travelling on your business at your cost, if Endorsement E08 applies.

Each named traveller must live in Australia and be no older than 74 when the policy starts or is renewed. You may add or remove a named traveller during the policy period under [[ref:gtr.obl.declarations]]. A person who replaces a named traveller who has left your business is covered from the day you tell us.
