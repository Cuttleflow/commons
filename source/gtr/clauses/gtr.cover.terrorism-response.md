heading: Terrorism Response
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We will pay the emergency medical and evacuation costs of an insured person injured in an act of terrorism at or near their location during a trip, up to the sub-limit shown in the schedule (default $250,000 for each event).

This benefit applies whether or not the schedule shows that kidnap, extortion and crisis response cover applies. [[ref:gtr.excl.war]] does not apply to this benefit.
