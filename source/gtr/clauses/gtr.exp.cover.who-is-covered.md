since: 1.0.0

The insured persons are, while they are on assignment:

- assignees: your directors, officers and employees who live in Australia and whom you have sent to work outside Australia for a period expected to last more than 3 months and no more than 5 years; and
- their dependants who live with them in the host country.

Each insured person must be named in the schedule or declared under [[ref:gtr.obl.declarations]], and must be no older than 74 when the policy starts or is renewed. An assignee who stops being employed by you, or whose assignment ends, stops being an insured person 30 days after that, or when they return to Australia, whichever is earlier. Their dependants stop being insured persons at the same time.
