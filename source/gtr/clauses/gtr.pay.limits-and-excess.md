The most we will pay for each section is the limit shown in the schedule, and each sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of the section in which it appears, unless this wording says otherwise. Where a limit is expressed for each insured person, it applies separately to each insured person. Unless the schedule shows that a limit applies for the whole policy period, a limit for each insured person applies for each trip.

We deduct the excess shown in the schedule for the section from each claim. Where one event gives rise to claims under more than one section for the same insured person, only the highest of the excesses applies. No excess applies to the medical and evacuation section, the personal accident section or the kidnap, extortion and crisis response section unless the schedule shows one.
