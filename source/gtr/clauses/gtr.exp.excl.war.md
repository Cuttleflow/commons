---
id: gtr.exp.excl.war
type: exclusion
heading: War
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for treatment of an injury caused by war, invasion, act of a foreign enemy, hostilities (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution or insurrection, if the insured person took an active part in it. This exclusion does not apply to an insured person injured in an act of terrorism, or in war or civil unrest they took no part in, or to evacuation from it.
