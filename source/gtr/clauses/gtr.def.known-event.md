---
id: gtr.def.known-event
type: definition
heading: Known Event
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

An event or circumstance that, at the time a trip was booked or this policy started (whichever is later), had been reported in the media, was the subject of a warning by a government or carrier, or that a member of the control group or the insured person knew, or a reasonable person in their position would have known, could disrupt the trip.
