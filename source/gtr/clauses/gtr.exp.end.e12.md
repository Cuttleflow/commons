During home leave in Australia of up to the number of days shown in the schedule in each policy period (default 30 days), we pay for treatment in Australia of an injury or sickness that first shows itself during the home leave, other than a cost for which a Medicare benefit is payable or any gap between a Medicare benefit and the fee charged. We pay for the continuation in Australia of treatment that started outside Australia only if the schedule shows it.
