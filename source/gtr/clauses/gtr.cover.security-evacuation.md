id: gtr.cover.security-evacuation
type: cover
heading: Security Evacuation
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If, during a trip, DFAT advice for the country or region where an insured person is is raised to "do not travel", or civil unrest, war, a natural disaster or terrorism makes it reasonably necessary to leave, we will pay the reasonable costs of moving the insured person to the nearest safe place or to their home, up to the sub-limit shown in the schedule (default $50,000 for each insured person).

This benefit applies whether or not the schedule shows that kidnap, extortion and crisis response cover applies. [[ref:gtr.excl.war]] does not apply to this benefit.
