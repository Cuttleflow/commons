heading: War
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0

---

We do not pay any claim caused by war, invasion, act of a foreign enemy, hostilities (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution or insurrection, except as [[ref:gtr.cover.security-evacuation]] and [[ref:gtr.cover.terrorism-response]] provide. This exclusion does not apply to an insured person who is injured in an act of terrorism they took no part in, or to an insured person in a country when war unexpectedly breaks out there, for 14 days after it breaks out.
