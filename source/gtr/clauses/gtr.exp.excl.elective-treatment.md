---
id: gtr.exp.excl.elective-treatment
type: exclusion
heading: Treatment We Do Not Pay For
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for cosmetic treatment, unless it is to restore appearance after an injury or cancer surgery during the policy period; weight-loss treatment or surgery; experimental treatment; treatment for alcohol or drug dependence beyond an initial stabilisation; routine health checks, vaccinations or travel medicine, unless the plan or Endorsement E14 includes them; or treatment an insured person travelled to the host country, or within the area of cover, to obtain.
