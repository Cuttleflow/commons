[[ref:gtr.excl.epidemic-pandemic]] does not apply to the travel disruption and business expenses sections where the insured person, their travelling companion or a member of their immediate family is diagnosed with the disease, or the insured person is denied boarding or placed in mandatory quarantine because they are suspected of having it. It does not cover border closures, travel bans, government quarantine of a whole population, or a change in DFAT advice because of the epidemic or pandemic.

Schedule variables: sub-limit for each insured person (default $10,000).
