Cover for each insured person starts when they leave their home or usual place of work to begin a trip that starts during the policy period, and ends when they return there.

A trip is covered for up to the maximum trip duration shown in the schedule (30, 60 or 90 consecutive days). If a trip ends after the policy period, cover continues until the trip ends, subject to the maximum trip duration. If the insured person's return is delayed by an event covered by this policy, or by the delay of scheduled transport, cover continues until they can reasonably return, for up to 30 days.

A trip may be a business trip, leisure travel immediately before or after a business trip, or a leisure trip on its own. Each named traveller is covered for leisure travel, of either kind, for up to the leisure travel allowance shown in the schedule in the policy period. Accompanying family members are covered only while travelling with the named traveller.

Cover applies in the area shown in the schedule, subject to [[ref:gtr.excl.government-advice]] and [[ref:gtr.cond.sanctions]]. A trip within Australia is covered if it is a business trip, or a leisure trip more than 250 kilometres from home with a flight or at least one night of prepaid accommodation. For a trip within Australia, the medical expenses benefit in [[ref:gtr.cover.medical]] does not apply to a Medicare-eligible insured person.
