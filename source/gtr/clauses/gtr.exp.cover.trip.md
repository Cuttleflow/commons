since: 1.0.0

Cover for each insured person starts when they leave Australia to start the assignment, or when the policy starts, whichever is later, and ends when the assignment ends and they return to Australia, or when the policy ends, whichever is earlier.

Cover applies in the host country and anywhere else in the area of cover shown in the schedule, including trips from the host country, subject to [[ref:gtr.excl.government-advice]] and [[ref:gtr.cond.sanctions]]. Treatment in the USA is covered only if the area of cover is worldwide or Endorsement E13 applies. Treatment in Australia is not covered, except as Endorsement E12 allows.
