We will pay, for each insured person and up to the sub-limits shown in the schedule:

- **money:** the loss of money by theft or accidental loss during a trip, including fraudulent use of a corporate card after its theft, less any amount the card issuer bears (default $5,000);
- **emergency business purchases:** essential items bought to complete the business purpose of a trip after the loss of luggage or business equipment covered by [[ref:gtr.cover.luggage]] (default $5,000);
- **event fees:** non-refundable conference, exhibition or event registration fees if the insured person cannot attend because of an insured disruption event under [[ref:gtr.cover.disruption]] (default $10,000); and
- **communication costs:** additional telephone and data charges incurred because of an event covered by this policy (default $1,000).

We do not pay for money that is not reported to the police within 24 hours of the loss being discovered, subject to [[ref:gtr.cond.insurance-contracts-act]], or for a shortage caused by error or a change in exchange rates.
