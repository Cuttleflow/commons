The premium is based on the assignees and dependants you declared for the policy period. You must tell us within 30 days when an assignee or dependant starts or ends an assignment, and give us their name, date of birth, host country and dates. We charge or refund premium for them on a pro-rata basis at the rates used to calculate the premium.

Failure to give a declaration does not affect a claim for an insured person who meets [[ref:gtr.cover.who-is-covered]], subject to [[ref:gtr.cond.insurance-contracts-act]].
