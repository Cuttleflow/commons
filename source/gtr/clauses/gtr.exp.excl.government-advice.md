---
id: gtr.exp.excl.government-advice
type: exclusion
heading: Government Travel Advice
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for treatment in a country or region for which DFAT advice was "do not travel" when the insured person went there, unless we agreed in writing before they went to cover it.

This exclusion does not apply to an insured person who was already there, including in the host country, when the advice was raised, for 30 days after it was raised or until they can reasonably leave, whichever is earlier, or to their evacuation from it.
