(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The policyholder's name, ABN and address, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:gtr.cond.governing-law]])
- The policy period, with its start and end dates
- The named travellers, with their ages, and whether accompanying family members are included for each
- The area covered: worldwide, worldwide excluding the Americas, or Australia only
- The maximum trip duration: 30, 60 or 90 consecutive days
- The leisure travel allowance (default 42 days for each named traveller in the policy period)
- The limit and excess for each section, and the sub-limits where they differ from the defaults stated in this wording
- The capital sum and weekly benefit for the personal accident section, and the accumulation limit (see [[ref:gtr.pay.accumulation]])
- Whether the kidnap, extortion and crisis response section applies
- The 24-hour emergency assistance telephone number
- The premium, including stamp duty and GST
- The insurer and its licence
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
