If an insured person needs treatment for an injury or sickness during the policy period, we will pay the reasonable and customary charges for the treatment, given or prescribed by a medical practitioner in the area of cover, up to the limits for the plan shown in the schedule, after the excess:

| Benefit | Standard | Premier |
|---|---|---|
| Overall annual limit for each insured person | $2,000,000 | $5,000,000 |
| Hospital accommodation, intensive care, surgery and specialist fees as an inpatient or day patient | Paid in full | Paid in full |
| Cancer treatment, including chemotherapy and radiotherapy | Paid in full | Paid in full |
| Outpatient consultations, diagnostic tests, prescribed medication and physiotherapy | $5,000 a year | $15,000 a year |
| Mental health treatment as an inpatient or outpatient | $10,000 a year | $25,000 a year |
| Maternity: pregnancy and childbirth, for an insured person covered for 10 months or more | Not included | $15,000 for each pregnancy |
| Emergency dental treatment after an injury | $2,000 a year | $5,000 a year |
| Routine dental and optical | Only under Endorsement E14 | $1,500 a year |
| Emergency evacuation to the nearest place where appropriate treatment is available, and repatriation to Australia, including a medical escort | Paid in full | Paid in full |
| Return of remains to Australia, or a funeral or cremation in the host country | $25,000 | $50,000 |

We must approve inpatient treatment, day surgery, and every evacuation or repatriation before it happens, except in an emergency, when the insured person or someone with them must contact the emergency assistance service as soon as practicable afterwards. If prior approval was not obtained, [[ref:gtr.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies and we may reduce our payment only by the extra cost that approval would have avoided.

Whether and when evacuation or repatriation is medically necessary, and the method of transport, is decided by the treating medical practitioner together with our emergency assistance service. If they do not agree, we will arrange an opinion from an independent medical practitioner at our cost.
