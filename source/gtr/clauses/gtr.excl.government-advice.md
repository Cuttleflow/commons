---
id: gtr.excl.government-advice
type: exclusion
heading: Travel Against Government Advice
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay any claim arising in a country or region for which DFAT advice was "do not travel" when the trip, or that part of it, began, unless we agreed in writing before the trip began to cover it.

This exclusion does not apply to:
- a claim for cancellation under [[ref:gtr.cover.disruption]] where the advice was raised after the trip was booked and before the trip, or that part of it, began, and the insured person cancelled because of it; or
- the benefit in [[ref:gtr.cover.security-evacuation]] for an insured person who was already there when the advice was raised.
