since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The Health Insurance Act 1973 (Cth) and the Private Health Insurance Act 2007 (Cth) do not allow us to pay any medical expense in Australia for which a Medicare benefit is payable, or any gap between the Medicare benefit and the fee charged, or any amount that would make this policy health insurance business.

We pay for treatment outside Australia only for an insured person who is on assignment, as an Australian resident temporarily employed outside Australia or as their dependant. We do not pay for treatment in Australia, except as Endorsement E12 allows, and then only for costs for which no Medicare benefit is payable. We will not pay any benefit, or part of a benefit, to the extent that paying it would breach those Acts.
