[[ref:gtr.excl.hazardous-activities]] does not apply to the activities named in the schedule for this endorsement, while the insured person takes part in them as an amateur, under the rules of the governing body where there is one, and holding any licence or qualification it requires. Sporting equipment named in the schedule is covered under [[ref:gtr.cover.luggage]] while in use, up to the sub-limit shown.

Schedule variables: named activities and conditions; sporting equipment sub-limit.
