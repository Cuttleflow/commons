Directors, officers and employees whose home is in a country named in the schedule for this endorsement are insured persons. For them, a trip starts when they leave their home in that country, and the medical and evacuation section does not cover medical expenses in that country. We will not provide cover in a country where we are not permitted to do so by law.

Schedule variables: countries; number of insured persons based in each.
