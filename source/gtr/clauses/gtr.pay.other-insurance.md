If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows. This applies to every section of this policy, including the medical and evacuation, personal accident and kidnap, extortion and crisis response sections. This does not apply where the schedule or an endorsement states that this policy is excess of a specified underlying policy, in which case this policy pays only after the limit of that specified policy is exhausted.

**Personal accident and sickness policies.** This policy pays first for an injury or sickness that happens on a trip covered by this policy. We do not reduce any benefit because of a personal accident or sickness policy, including a group personal accident and sickness policy issued to you. If the schedule of such a policy names this policy as a specified underlying policy, that policy pays its weekly benefits and additional benefits only after this policy has paid, and both policies pay their capital benefits in full.

You or the insured person must tell us about any other insurance that may cover the loss when you make a claim.
