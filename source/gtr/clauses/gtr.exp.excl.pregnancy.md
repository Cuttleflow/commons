We do not pay for pregnancy or childbirth, or for a pregnancy-related condition, except:

- under the Premier plan, as the maternity benefit in [[ref:gtr.cover.medical]] allows; and
- under either plan, for a complication of pregnancy that a medical practitioner certifies as unforeseen, such as ectopic pregnancy, miscarriage or pre-eclampsia.

We do not pay for fertility treatment, assisted reproduction or surrogacy.
