---
id: gtr.def.business-trip
type: definition
heading: Business Trip
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A journey an insured person makes on your business, with your authority, that is more than the distance from their home and usual place of work shown in the schedule (default 100 kilometres) or that involves a flight, together with any leisure travel allowed by [[ref:gtr.cover.trip]].
