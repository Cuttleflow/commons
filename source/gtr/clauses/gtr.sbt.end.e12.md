In [[ref:gtr.cover.luggage]], the limit for business equipment for each insured person is increased to the amount shown in the schedule for this endorsement (default $20,000), and the limit for any one item of business equipment to the amount shown (default $7,500). These increased limits are part of, and not in addition to, the luggage limit for each insured person, unless the schedule shows otherwise.

Schedule variables: business equipment limit; limit for any one item.
