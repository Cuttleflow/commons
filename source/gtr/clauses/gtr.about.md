This policy covers the people your organisation sends on business trips, within Australia and overseas, for the medical, accident, disruption, property, liability and security events set out in it. It is an annual policy: it covers every business trip that starts during the policy period, so you do not need to arrange insurance for each trip.

You, the organisation named in the schedule, are the policyholder. The people covered are the insured persons described in [[ref:gtr.cover.who-is-covered]]. Some benefits are paid to you, and some to the insured person or their estate, as each section states.

This policy is not workers compensation, private health insurance or life insurance, and it does not replace any of them. It cannot pay any medical expense in Australia for which a Medicare benefit is payable (see [[ref:gtr.pay.medicare]]).

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and declarations, and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:gtr.section.definitions]].
