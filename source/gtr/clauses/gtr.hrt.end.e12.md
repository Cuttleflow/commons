An insured person who is a journalist, camera operator or producer, and is embedded with an armed force named in the schedule with that force's accreditation, is not taking active participation by being embedded, and is covered while embedded, for the dates shown, subject to the sub-limits for this endorsement. They must not carry a weapon.

Schedule variables: armed force; dates; insured persons; sub-limits.
