id: gtr.def.permanent-total-disablement
type: definition
heading: Permanent Total Disablement
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Disablement from an injury that starts within 12 months of the accident, that has lasted, or in the opinion of a medical practitioner we appoint will last, at least 12 months from the date of the injury without a break, and that in the opinion of that medical practitioner will prevent the insured person from ever working again in any occupation for which they are reasonably suited by education, training or experience. We assess permanent total disablement once 12 months have passed since the injury.
