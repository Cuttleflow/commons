---
id: gtr.hrt.excl.war
type: exclusion
heading: War and Terrorism
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay any claim arising from the insured person's active participation in war, invasion, act of a foreign enemy, hostilities (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection or terrorism.

We do not pay any claim arising from war between any two or more of Australia, China, France, Russia, the United Kingdom and the United States, or from the use of a nuclear, chemical or biological weapon (see also [[ref:gtr.excl.nuclear-biological-chemical]]).

Otherwise this policy covers injury and loss caused by war and terrorism, including in an approved destination. [[ref:gtr.cover.security-evacuation]] and [[ref:gtr.cover.terrorism-response]] apply as written.
