heading: Security Precautions and Training
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Before an insured person travels to an approved destination, you must:

- make sure they have completed hostile environment training;
- prepare a written security plan for the trip, including communications, check-in times and evacuation routes; and
- register the trip with the Australian Government through Smartraveller, where available.

During the trip you must follow the security plan, and the advice of our security assistance service, as far as it is reasonable to do so.

If you do not, [[ref:gtr.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies: we may reduce a claim only by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced, and we will not refuse a claim because of a failure that could not reasonably be regarded as having caused or contributed to the loss.
