An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables (sub-limits, excesses, co-insurance, periods, named parties) shown for it in the schedule. Where an endorsement and the rest of this policy conflict, the endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. Endorsements do not increase the limit of liability unless they say so; a sub-limit in an endorsement is part of, not in addition to, the limit of liability. If a variable an endorsement needs is not shown in the schedule, the default stated in the endorsement applies; if it has no default, the endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the schedule. These endorsements are for use with version 1.4.x of this wording.

| Code | Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Extended Reporting Period | Extends the time to notify claims after the policy ends, for 12, 24, 36 or 72 months |
| E02 | Defence Costs in Addition to the Limit | Pays defence costs in addition to the limit of liability, to a cap |
| E03 | End-of-Life Software | Writes back the Unsupported Software exclusion for the systems named, with co-insurance |
| E04 | Increased Social Engineering Fraud Sub-limit | Increases the funds transfer and social engineering sub-limit where payments are verified by call-back |
| E05 | Increased Dependent Business Interruption Sub-limit | Increases the Dependent Business Interruption sub-limit, for all or named providers |
| E06 | Digital Asset Theft | Extends Funds Transfer Fraud to cryptocurrency and other digital assets you own |
| E07 | Technology Errors and Omissions | Covers claims for errors in the technology products and services you provide, to a sub-limit |
| E08 | Invoice Manipulation Fraud | Covers payments to you that a fraudster diverts by altering or issuing your invoices |
| E09 | Contingent Bodily Injury from Operational Technology | Writes back bodily injury caused by a cyber event affecting your operational technology, in excess of liability insurance |
| E10 | Excess Layer | Writes this policy in excess of the underlying insurance named |
| E11 | United States and Canada Jurisdiction | Writes back claims brought, or judgments enforced, in the United States and Canada |
| E12 | Aggregate Limit Reinstatement | Reinstates the limit of liability after it is exhausted |
| E13 | Non-IT Dependent Business Interruption | Extends Dependent Business Interruption to cyber events at named non-IT suppliers |
| E14 | Customer Business Interruption | Covers lost income when a cyber event at a named customer reduces its purchases |
| E15 | Generative AI Systems | Extends the policy to AI systems, data poisoning and prompt injection |
| E16 | Ransomware Exclusion | Removes cyber extortion and ransomware first-party cover where MFA or tested offline backups are missing |
