If you discover a cyber event or privacy breach during the policy period, we will pay the reasonable costs you incur, with our prior consent, for:

- IT forensics — engaging a specialist to determine the cause, scope, and impact of the event
- Legal advice — obtaining legal guidance on your notification obligations and regulatory exposure
- Notification — notifying affected individuals, regulators (including the OAIC), and any other parties as required by law
- Credit monitoring — providing credit monitoring and identity theft protection services to affected individuals for up to 12 months
- Public relations — engaging a consultant to protect or restore your reputation
- Call centre — setting up and operating a call centre or helpline to manage enquiries from affected individuals

You should use the incident response team named in your schedule. If you use a different provider, you need our prior written consent. We will not unreasonably withhold consent, but using our panel helps ensure a fast, coordinated response.
