This policy covers cyber events, dependent events and privacy breaches wherever in the world they occur.

The covers in [[ref:cy.section.third-party]] apply only to claims brought, and regulatory proceedings commenced, in Australia or New Zealand, and to proceedings to enforce in Australia or New Zealand a judgment of an Australian or New Zealand court, unless the schedule names another country.

This policy does not cover, and we will not pay in respect of, any claim brought within, or judgment entered or enforced from, a court of the United States of America or Canada or their territories, except to the extent that [[ref:cy.end.e11]] is shown in the schedule.

You and we each submit to the jurisdiction of the courts of the state or territory identified under [[ref:cy.cond.governing-law]] and any courts of appeal from them. This does not stop either of us from bringing proceedings in any other court that has jurisdiction.

**Service of suit.** If the insurer named in the schedule is not authorised to carry on insurance business in Australia, it will, at your request, submit to the jurisdiction of any court of competent jurisdiction in Australia, and will comply with the final decision of that court. It authorises the agent named in the schedule to accept service of process on its behalf in any proceedings you bring.
