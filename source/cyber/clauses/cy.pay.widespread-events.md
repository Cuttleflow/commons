heading: Widespread Events
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

If the schedule shows this clause as operative, our total liability for all losses, costs, expenses, and claims arising from a widespread event is limited to the widespread event sub-limit shown in the schedule, applied in place of — and not in addition to — the limit of liability in [[ref:cy.pay.limit-of-liability]].

If this clause is not shown as operative in the schedule, it does not apply, and [[ref:cy.pay.limit-of-liability]] applies without modification.
