heading: Reputational Harm — Income Loss
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

If a cyber event or privacy breach covered by this policy results in negative media coverage or public reporting that causes a measurable loss of customers or revenue, we will pay your resulting net income loss for up to 12 months from the date of the first public report.

You must demonstrate a direct causal link between the cyber event or privacy breach, the public reporting, and the loss of revenue.

Sub-limit: the amount shown in the schedule (or $250,000 in the aggregate if not stated).
