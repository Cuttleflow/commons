This document is version-controlled. The version and date shown on the cover page and in the footer of each page govern this text. The version stated in your schedule as current at inception applies for the life of your policy period, unless your schedule or an endorsement states otherwise.

This is the first release of the Meridian Cyber wording built from clause source in the Commons. It replaces version 1.3.0 (July 2026) and brings the endorsement library (2026/CY/0003 v1.0.0, written for version 1.1.0) into this document. Changes from version 1.3.0:

- Clause numbers are removed. Each clause is identified by its heading, and every cross-reference that used a section number is now a link to the clause it names. Jurisdiction and Service of Suit is now headed Territory and Jurisdiction, and Appendix A is now the guide How to Respond to a Cyber Incident.
- Territory: cover applies to cyber events, dependent events and privacy breaches wherever they occur, but the third-party covers apply only to claims brought, and regulatory proceedings commenced, in Australia or New Zealand, unless the schedule names another country. Claims in the United States and Canada stay excluded unless Endorsement E11 applies.
- Funds transfer fraud and social engineering fraud now share one sub-limit shown in the schedule.
- New definition of Funds, which states that cryptocurrency and other digital assets are not funds unless Endorsement E06 applies. Version 1.3.0 was silent on digital assets.
- New definitions of Ransomware, used by Endorsement E16, and Extended reporting period.
- New general condition, Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): nothing in the policy excludes, restricts or modifies the Act, and s.54 applies to every obligation and to the first-party discovery and notice time limits.
- About This Policy now records the s.40(1) notice of the effect of s.40(3).
- Wording Partner review tags are added on the social engineering verification paragraph, the Other Insurance clause, and reinsurance conformity of the war exclusion.
- The endorsement library is now part of this wording, with 16 endorsements, each with operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger. Every endorsement was checked against this version, with these results.
- E01 refers to the automatic extended reporting period by its heading, and covers dependent events.
- E03 and E06 reduce payment under s.54 for a missed control instead of stating a condition of cover.
- E04 keeps cover at the standard sub-limit when verification is not performed, so verification remains a limit on the increase, not a condition of cover.
- E06 is re-anchored to Funds Transfer Fraud. Version 1.0.0 anchored it to the social engineering cover, which requires an impersonation and so would rarely have responded to a theft of digital assets.
- E07 now has its own retroactive date and lifts the Professional Services exclusion for its cover.
- E08 is anchored to the first-party section; version 1.0.0 referred to a cyber crime section the wording does not have.
- E11 writes back the Territory and Jurisdiction condition, which replaced the jurisdictional limitation version 1.0.0 deleted.
- E13 and E14 each carry their own waiting period.
- New E16 Ransomware Exclusion removes cyber extortion cover and first-party ransomware loss (except incident response costs) where multi-factor authentication or tested offline backups are missing.
- The endorsement library's licence is now CC BY 4.0, in line with the rest of the Commons; version 1.0.0 was published under CC BY-SA 4.0.
