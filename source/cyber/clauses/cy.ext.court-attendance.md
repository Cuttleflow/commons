id: cy.ext.court-attendance
type: extension
heading: Court Attendance
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

If any insured person attends court, a tribunal, or an arbitration as a witness in connection with a claim covered by this policy, we will pay $500 per day or part-day of attendance required by us.

Sub-limit: $10,000 in the aggregate.
