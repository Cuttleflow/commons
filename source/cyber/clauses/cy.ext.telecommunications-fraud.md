---
id: cy.ext.telecommunications-fraud
type: extension
heading: Telecommunications Fraud
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

If a third party gains **unauthorised** access to your telephone or communications system during the policy period and makes calls or uses services at your expense, we will pay the charges you incur as a direct result.

Sub-limit: the amount shown in the schedule (or $100,000 in the aggregate if not stated).
