heading: Retroactive Date
since: 1.4.0
changed: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

This policy does not cover any cyber event or privacy breach that first occurred before the retroactive date, under any section of this policy, whether or not the relevant insuring clause expressly refers to the retroactive date.

For [[ref:cy.cover.media-liability]], the retroactive date applies to the wrongful act or the publication that gives rise to the claim, whichever first occurred. This policy does not cover a claim arising from a wrongful act or publication that first occurred before the retroactive date.

[[ref:cy.cover.privacy-liability]] and [[ref:cy.cover.network-security-liability]] refer to the retroactive date for clarity; this condition is the authoritative source of the restriction and applies to every section of this policy, including [[ref:cy.section.first-party]].
