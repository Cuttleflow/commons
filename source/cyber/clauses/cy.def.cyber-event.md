---
id: cy.def.cyber-event
type: definition
heading: Cyber event
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means: (a) any **unauthorised** access to, or **unauthorised** use of, your computer system; (b) any malware, ransomware, virus, trojan, worm, or other malicious code that affects your computer system; (c) any denial-of-service attack directed at your computer system; (d) any system failure of your computer system; or (e) any theft, loss, or unauthorised disclosure of data. A cyber event does not include the planned shutdown, maintenance, or upgrade of your computer system.
