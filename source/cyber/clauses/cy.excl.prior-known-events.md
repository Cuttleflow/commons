heading: Prior Known Events
since: 1.4.0
changed: 1.5.0

---

arising from any cyber event or privacy breach that:

(a) a member of the control group knew about before the start of this policy period; or
(b) first occurred before the retroactive date (see also [[ref:cy.cond.retroactive-date]]),

or from any wrongful act or publication, for [[ref:cy.cover.media-liability]], that first occurred before the retroactive date. What a member of the control group knows is the knowledge of the company. It is not treated as the knowledge of any other insured person who did not know it.
