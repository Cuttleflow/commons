If a regulator (including the OAIC, ASIC, ACMA, or any State, Territory or New Zealand regulator) imposes a fine or penalty on you in Australia or New Zealand arising from a cyber event, or from a privacy breach described in paragraph (a) or (c) of [[ref:cy.def.privacy-breach]], that occurs during the policy period, we will pay the fine or penalty, but only to the extent that the law allows it to be insured.

We do not pay any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured. In particular, we do not pay a penalty imposed under work health and safety law in a State or Territory whose law prohibits insurance of it (including New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland), and we do not pay a criminal fine. We do not pay a penalty imposed for failing to report a cyber incident or a ransomware payment.

We will also pay your defence costs in responding to the regulatory proceeding. We advance those costs as they are incurred. You must repay any costs we advanced if a final adjudication, or a court's finding, establishes conduct for which the law does not allow the fine or penalty to be insured.

This clause does not cover a fine or penalty for a failure to comply with a privacy or data protection law that does not involve a cyber event or an unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure or loss of information.
