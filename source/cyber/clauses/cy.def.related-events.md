heading: Related events
since: 1.5.0
changed: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

means cyber events, dependent events, cyber extortion threats, privacy breaches, wrongful acts or publications that arise from the same cause, or from a series of the same, similar or connected causes. Events that exploit the same vulnerability, or are attributable to the same threat actor or campaign, are related events.

Related events are treated as one for the purposes of [[ref:cy.def.claim]], [[ref:cy.def.insured-loss]], [[ref:cy.def.widespread-event]] and [[ref:cy.end.e12]]. [[ref:cy.pay.excess]] and [[ref:cy.pay.limit-of-liability]] apply to them as one.
