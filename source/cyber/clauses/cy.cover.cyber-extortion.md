If you receive a cyber extortion threat during the policy period, we will pay:

(a) the reasonable costs of investigating and responding to the threat; and
(b) any ransom payment, but only with our prior written consent.

We will consent to a ransom payment only after we have confirmed (so far as reasonably possible) that the payment does not breach any law, including [[ref:cy.cond.sanctions]] and the offences in the Criminal Code Act 1995 (Cth) about financing terrorism. We do not pay a ransom where paying it would be one of those offences. We may engage a specialist negotiator on your behalf.

We will not reimburse a ransom payment that you make without our prior written consent.

**Reporting a ransom payment.** The Cyber Security Act 2024 (Cth) may require you to report a ransomware payment to the government, within a short time after the payment is made. This includes a payment made on your behalf by us or by a negotiator. Tell us before you make or agree to any ransom payment, so that we can help you make the report in time. We will give you the help you reasonably ask for. We do not pay any penalty for failing to report a payment.
