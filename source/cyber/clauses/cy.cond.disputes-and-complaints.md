If you are not happy with a decision we have made, contact us first. We will follow our internal dispute resolution process.

If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you can refer the matter to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA): www.afca.org.au, info@afca.org.au, 1800 931 678. AFCA provides a free and independent service. Time limits may apply.
