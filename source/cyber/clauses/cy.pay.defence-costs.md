Defence costs are included within the limit of liability, unless [[ref:cy.end.e02]] is shown in the schedule. We will advance defence costs as they are incurred, before the outcome of a claim is determined. If it is later established that a claim is not covered, you must repay any defence costs we advanced for the uncovered portion.
