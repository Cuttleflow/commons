heading: Network Security Liability
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

If a claim is first made against you during the policy period for a cyber event that first occurs on or after the retroactive date, we will pay any amount you become legally liable to pay as compensation, and your defence costs, for your failure to prevent:

(i) **unauthorised** access to or use of your computer system;
(ii) a denial-of-service attack originating from or directed at your computer system;
(iii) the transmission of malware from your computer system to a third party's system; or
(iv) the use of your computer system to attack a third party (where you are an unwitting intermediary).
