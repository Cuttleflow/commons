If you discover a **dependent event** during the policy period and it directly causes a total or partial interruption to your business, we will pay your net income loss and extra expense as if the event had occurred at your own computer system.

**This is the only cover under this policy for business interruption caused by a dependent event.** [[ref:cy.cover.business-interruption]] does not respond to interruption caused by a dependent event, and a dependent event is not a cyber event for the purposes of that clause.

The waiting period applies separately to dependent business interruption losses, on the same threshold basis as [[ref:cy.cover.business-interruption]].

Interruption arising at a non-IT supplier, or at a named customer, can be added by [[ref:cy.end.e13]] or [[ref:cy.end.e14]].

Sub-limit: the amount shown in the schedule (or $500,000 in the aggregate if not stated).
