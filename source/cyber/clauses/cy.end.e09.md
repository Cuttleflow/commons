[[ref:cy.excl.bodily-injury-property-damage]] does not apply to claims against you for bodily injury to a third party directly caused by a cyber event that affects operational technology under your control. Operational technology means computer systems that monitor or control physical equipment, plant or processes.

Cover under this endorsement applies only in excess of, and contingent on, any valid and collectible general liability insurance available to you, and only to the extent that insurance does not respond because of a cyber exclusion.

This endorsement does not cover property damage, product liability, product recall, or bodily injury arising from professional services.
