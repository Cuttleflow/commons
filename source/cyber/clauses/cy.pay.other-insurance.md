If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows. This does not apply where the schedule or an endorsement states that this policy is excess of a specified underlying policy, in which case this policy pays only after the limit of that specified policy is exhausted. The crime priority rule below is the one other exception.

**Crime priority rule.** Crime insurance is primary for loss of money, securities or property through social engineering fraud or funds transfer fraud, including computer-enabled theft of them. If a loss under [[ref:cy.cover.funds-transfer-fraud]], [[ref:cy.cover.social-engineering]] or [[ref:cy.end.e08]] is also covered by a crime policy, the crime policy pays first. This policy then pays only the part of the loss that is above what the crime policy pays, up to the limit and sub-limit of this policy. This applies whether or not we wrote the crime policy. If the crime policy also says that it is excess, the two policies share the loss in proportion to their limits. If there is no crime policy that covers the loss, this policy responds in full.
