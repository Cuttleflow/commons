---
id: cy.excl.infrastructure
type: exclusion
heading: Infrastructure Failure
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

arising from the failure, interruption, or outage of:

(a) the public internet or a national telecommunications network;
(b) the electricity grid or other utility supply; or
(c) satellite or GPS systems,

that is not caused by a cyber event or dependent event specifically targeting your computer system or your service provider's systems.
