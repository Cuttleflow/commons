heading: Sanctions
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

We will not make any payment that would breach any trade or economic sanctions, embargo, or restriction imposed under the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011 (Cth), the Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 (Cth), or equivalent sanctions measures of the European Union, the United Kingdom, or the United States.
