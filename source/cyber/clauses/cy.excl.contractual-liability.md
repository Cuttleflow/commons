---
id: cy.excl.contractual-liability
type: exclusion
heading: Contractual Liability
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

for any liability you assume under a contract that is greater than the liability you would have at law without the contract — except for:

(a) liability under a confidentiality or non-disclosure agreement; and
(b) PCI-DSS assessments under your merchant services agreement (covered under [[ref:cy.ext.pci-dss]]).
