heading: Media Liability
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

If a claim is first made against you during the policy period arising from your digital media content, we will pay any amount you become legally liable to pay as compensation, and your defence costs, for:

(i) defamation (including libel and slander);
(ii) breach of privacy or intrusion upon seclusion;
(iii) infringement of copyright, trademark, or domain name; or
(iv) plagiarism.

This clause does not cover claims arising from advertising of products or services other than your own, or any content published by third parties on platforms you operate (unless you were aware of the content and failed to remove it after being notified).
