---
id: cy.obl.notify-claims
type: obligation
heading: Notify Us of Claims
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Tell us in writing as soon as reasonably practicable after a claim is made against you. Provide:

(a) the identity of the claimant and the nature of the allegations;
(b) details of the cyber event or privacy breach involved;
(c) when you first became aware of the claim; and
(d) your estimate of the potential value (if you can).
