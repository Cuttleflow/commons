(Completed separately for each policy — contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- Who is insured
- The policy period (start and end dates)
- The governing law state or territory (see [[ref:cy.cond.governing-law]])
- The retroactive date
- The limit of liability and any sub-limits
- The excess for each section
- The waiting period for business interruption cover
- The indemnity period for business interruption cover
- The widespread event sub-limit and threshold, if [[ref:cy.pay.widespread-events]] is operative
- Incident response team contact details
- The premium (including stamp duty and GST)
- Which sections are operative (marked "Included")
- Any endorsements, and the variables shown for each
