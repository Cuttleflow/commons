These insuring clauses cover the costs and losses you incur directly. They are not about claims by third parties — those are covered in [[ref:cy.section.third-party]]. All cover under this section is subject to [[ref:cy.cond.retroactive-date]].

**One trigger.** Incident Response Costs, Business Interruption, Data and System Restoration and Dependent Business Interruption each respond to a cyber event, privacy breach or dependent event that you first discover during the policy period. It does not matter whether the cyber event began earlier in the policy period or before it, as long as it did not first occur before the retroactive date and [[ref:cy.excl.prior-known-events]] does not apply. The interruption or the cost may continue after the policy period ends.
