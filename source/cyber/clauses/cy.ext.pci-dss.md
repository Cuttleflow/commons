If a payment card industry body (such as Visa, Mastercard, or their acquiring bank) imposes a fine, penalty, or assessment on you arising from a privacy breach that involves payment card data, we will pay the amount assessed — including any contractual penalties under your merchant services agreement.

Sub-limit: the amount shown in the schedule (or $250,000 in the aggregate if not stated).
