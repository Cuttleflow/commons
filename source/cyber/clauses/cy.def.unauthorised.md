---
id: cy.def.unauthorised
type: definition
heading: Unauthorised
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means, in relation to access to, or use of, a computer system, a service provider's systems, or data: access or use that is not permitted, and includes access or use by a person who holds a valid credential, authority, or right of access for some purposes but who accesses or uses the computer system, the service provider's systems, or the data beyond the scope of, or for a purpose not permitted by, that credential, authority, or right.
