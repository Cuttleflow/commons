heading: Notify Us of Circumstances
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Tell us in writing during the policy period if you become aware of any cyber event or privacy breach that might reasonably be expected to give rise to a claim. Any claim that later arises from notified circumstances will be treated as if made during this policy period, consistent with s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).

Examples of things you should tell us about:

- You discover that personal data has been accessed by an unauthorised party, even if you are not yet sure of the scope.
- A business partner or client has alleged that malware entered their systems through yours.
- You receive a regulatory inquiry about your data handling practices.
- You discover that an employee has been accessing data outside their role.
