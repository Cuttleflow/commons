Cover extends to claims first made against you during the policy period, for an act, error or omission on or after the retroactive date shown for this endorsement, alleging a negligent act, error, or omission in your provision of technology products or technology services to a third party for a fee, including failure of your products or services to perform as intended. [[ref:cy.excl.professional-services]] does not apply to this cover.

For this endorsement: technology products means hardware, software, or electronic components you design, develop, license, sell, or distribute; technology services means services such as software development, hosting, data processing, systems integration, consulting, and support.

This endorsement does not cover: (a) bodily injury or property damage; (b) your fees, cost guarantees, or estimates of probable cost; (c) any express warranty or guarantee beyond your liability at law; or (d) deliberate breach of contract.

The retention shown in the schedule applies to each claim under this endorsement. Where a single event gives rise to a claim under both this endorsement and another section of the policy, [[ref:cy.pay.excess]] applies: one claim, and only the highest excess.

Businesses with material technology errors and omissions exposure should consider the Meridian Comprehensive Technology Liability wording, which provides this cover in full form.

Schedule variables: sub-limit; retention; retroactive date for this endorsement.
