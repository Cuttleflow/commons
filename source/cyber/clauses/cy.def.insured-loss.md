heading: Insured loss
since: 1.4.0
changed: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

means a loss, cost, or expense payable to you under [[ref:cy.section.first-party]]. Two or more insured losses arising from related events (see [[ref:cy.def.related-events]]) are treated as a single **insured loss** first discovered when the earliest of them was first discovered.
