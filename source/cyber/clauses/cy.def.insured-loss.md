---
id: cy.def.insured-loss
type: definition
heading: Insured loss
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means a loss, cost, or expense payable to you under [[ref:cy.section.first-party]]. Two or more insured losses arising from the same cyber event, dependent event, cyber extortion threat, or privacy breach, or from a series of the same, similar, or related cyber events, dependent events, cyber extortion threats, or privacy breaches — including those exploiting the same vulnerability or attributable to the same threat actor or campaign — are treated as a single **insured loss** first discovered when the earliest of them was first discovered.
