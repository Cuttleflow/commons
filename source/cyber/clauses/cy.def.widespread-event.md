---
id: cy.def.widespread-event
type: definition
heading: Widespread event
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means a cyber event, dependent event, or privacy breach that arises from, or is attributable to, the same vulnerability, exploit, malicious code, or campaign that also affects one or more other parties insured under this wording (whether insured by us or by any other insurer), where the number of affected parties, or the aggregate loss across those parties, reaches the threshold shown in the schedule. See [[ref:cy.pay.widespread-events]].
