heading: Widespread event
since: 1.4.0
changed: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

means a cyber event, dependent event, or privacy breach that is, or is part of, a set of related events (see [[ref:cy.def.related-events]]) that also affect one or more other parties insured under this wording (whether insured by us or by any other insurer), where the number of affected parties, or the aggregate loss across those parties, reaches the threshold shown in the schedule. See [[ref:cy.pay.widespread-events]].
