This is the first release of the Meridian Product Recall and Contamination wording. It is new to the Commons and replaces no earlier wording.

- First-party cover. It pays your own recall costs, replacement costs, loss of gross profit, rehabilitation costs and crisis consultant costs after an insured event. Claims against you for injury or damage caused by your products stay with products liability insurance.
- Contaminated Products covers accidental contamination (including mislabelling and undeclared allergens), malicious tampering and government recalls. Defective Products, for consumer goods, applies only if the schedule shows it.
- Discovery basis: insured events first discovered during the policy period and notified during it or within 30 days after. Related insured events, including those affecting the same batch or product line, are one insured event.
- One aggregate limit of liability, with sub-limits for gross profit, rehabilitation, crisis consultant costs and each endorsement; one retention for each insured event.
- Withdrawals without a safety risk, product guarantee and performance, shelf life, changes in the law and listed product types are excluded.
- Six endorsements, each with operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger: Third-party Recall Costs, Product Extortion, Adverse Publicity, USA and Canada, Allergen Retention and Untraceable Products.
- General conditions include an express Insurance Contracts Act savings clause applying s.54 to every obligation, fixed governing law and jurisdiction, and a standalone sanctions condition.
- Licensed CC BY 4.0. Clauses are identified by their headings, not numbers.
