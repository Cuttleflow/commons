This guide explains this policy. It does not change its terms.

Products liability insurance pays other people when your products injure them or damage their property. It does not pay your own costs of getting the products back. This policy does.

For example:

- a bakery finds that a batch of muesli bars labelled nut-free was made on a line used for peanuts. It recalls the batch from supermarkets. The policy pays the costs of the recall, the cost of the destroyed bars, the profit lost while the line is shut and sales recover, and a crisis consultant, less the retention;
- a beverage maker receives a threat that bottles on supermarket shelves have been poisoned. With Endorsement E02, the policy also pays the costs of responding to the threat;
- a cosmetics brand's moisturiser fails to meet its marketed shelf life but is not unsafe. That is a withdrawal without a safety risk, and it is not covered.

Tell us as soon as you discover a problem, and follow your recall plan.
