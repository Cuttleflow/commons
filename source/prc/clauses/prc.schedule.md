(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The named insured and ABN, and the business
- The policy period, start and end dates
- The insured products
- The sections that apply
- The limit of liability in the aggregate, and the sub-limits for gross profit, rehabilitation costs, crisis consultant costs and any endorsement
- The retention for each insured event
- The indemnity period for gross profit and the rehabilitation period
- The geographical limits, where they differ from [[ref:prc.def.geographical-limits]]
- The premium, the minimum premium, stamp duty and GST
- The state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:prc.cond.governing-law]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
