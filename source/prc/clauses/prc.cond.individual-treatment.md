id: prc.cond.individual-treatment
type: condition
heading: Individual Treatment of Insureds
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Each insured is treated as having a separate policy for every provision of this policy that depends on what an insured knew, intended, did or failed to do, including [[ref:prc.cond.your-duty-of-disclosure]], [[ref:prc.excl.deliberate-acts]] and [[ref:prc.excl.known-events]]. The knowledge or conduct of a member of the control group of an entity is imputed to that entity, and to no other insured. Including more than one insured does not increase the limit of liability.
