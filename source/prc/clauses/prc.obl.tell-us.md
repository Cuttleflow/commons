---
id: prc.obl.tell-us
type: obligation
heading: Tell Us Promptly
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after you discover an insured event, or facts that make one reasonably likely, and before you begin a recall where you can do so without delay that increases the risk to people. You must also notify government authorities and customers when the law requires you to.
