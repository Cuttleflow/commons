This section applies only if the schedule shows it.

If you first discover a defect in an insured product during the policy period, and a recall is reasonably necessary to prevent bodily injury, sickness, death or property damage, we will pay the losses listed in [[ref:prc.cover.contaminated-products]] arising from that defect, in excess of the retention and up to the limit of liability.
