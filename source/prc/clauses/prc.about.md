This is a product recall and contamination policy. It pays your own costs and losses when your products have to be recalled, destroyed or withdrawn because they are, or may be, contaminated or unsafe. It is first-party insurance: it does not pay claims by other people for injury or damage your products cause, which your products liability insurance covers.

[[ref:prc.cover.contaminated-products]] applies to every policy. [[ref:prc.cover.defective-products]] applies only if the schedule shows it.

This policy covers insured events first discovered by you during the policy period and notified to us as [[ref:prc.cover.discovery-and-notification]] sets out.

This policy, together with your schedule and any endorsements shown in it, forms your contract of insurance with us. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:prc.section.definitions]].

This wording is written for businesses. It is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement. [WP REVIEW: confirm that product recall insurance is not a retail general insurance product under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) s.761G and the Corporations Regulations, so that no PDS or TMD is required.]
