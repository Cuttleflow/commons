since: 1.0.0

---

We may take over and conduct, in your name, any right of recovery you have for a loss we pay, including against a supplier whose ingredient, component or packaging caused the insured event. Any amount recovered, after the reasonable costs of the recovery, is applied first to us up to what we paid, and then to you, including for the retention. You must not give up any right of recovery after an insured event without our written consent, but you may agree in a written contract made before the insured event to limit your supplier's liability where that is usual in your trade.
