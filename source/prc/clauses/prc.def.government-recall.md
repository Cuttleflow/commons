---
id: prc.def.government-recall
type: definition
heading: Government Recall
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A compulsory recall ordered, or a recall publicly required, by a government authority under the Australian Consumer Law, the Food Standards Australia New Zealand Act 1991 (Cth), a state or territory food act, or any similar law where the insured products are sold, because an insured product is, or may be, unsafe.
