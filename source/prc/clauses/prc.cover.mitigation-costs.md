heading: Costs to Prevent a Recall
since: 1.0.0
status: draft

We will also pay reasonable costs you incur, with our consent, to test, hold or segregate insured products after you discover facts that make an insured event reasonably likely, where doing so avoids or reduces a recall that this policy would have covered. These costs count towards the limit of liability and are subject to the retention.
