---
id: prc.excl.gradual-deterioration
type: exclusion
heading: Shelf Life and Deterioration
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The ordinary deterioration, decomposition or spoilage of an insured product, or its sale after the use-by or best-before date or the end of its shelf life, unless it is caused by accidental contamination or malicious tampering.
