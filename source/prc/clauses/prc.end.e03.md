An insured event also includes the publication, by a government authority or in the media, of a report that products of the same kind as an insured product, made by someone else, are contaminated, where the report causes a fall in your sales of the insured product even though it is not contaminated. We will pay your loss of gross profit and rehabilitation costs, up to the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement.

Schedule variables: the sub-limit; the indemnity period for this endorsement (default 90 days).
