id: prc.def.recall-costs
type: definition
heading: Recall Costs
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Reasonable and necessary costs you incur in a recall, being the costs of:

- notifying customers, consumers and government authorities, including advertising and communications;
- transporting, storing, inspecting, testing and destroying the recalled insured products;
- extra staff, overtime and hired equipment; and
- your customers' costs of withdrawing the insured products, where you are legally liable to reimburse them under a written contract made before the insured event.

They do not include your normal operating costs or the salaries of your permanent staff for their normal hours.
