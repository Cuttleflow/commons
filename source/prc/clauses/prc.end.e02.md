An insured event also includes a threat, made to you and communicated to us, to commit malicious tampering unless money or something else of value is paid. We will pay, up to the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement, the amount paid with our consent to meet the demand, the crisis consultant costs of responding to it, and your reasonable costs of investigating and responding to the threat, including the reward paid for information.

Schedule variables: the sub-limit.
