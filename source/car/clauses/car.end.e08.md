The projects named in the schedule for this endorsement, which started before the policy period, are covered from the date shown, but only if an inspection or survey satisfactory to us was carried out before that date.

We do not pay for damage that happened before the date cover starts for the project, whether or not it was known.

Schedule variables: projects named; date cover starts for each; contract value completed at that date.
