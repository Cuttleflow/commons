LEG 3/06 applies in [[ref:car.excl.defects]] in place of the defects clause otherwise shown in the schedule. We pay for all damage, including damage to the defective part, except the cost of improving the original design, plan, specification, materials or workmanship.

An additional premium applies, as shown in the schedule.

Schedule variables: none.
