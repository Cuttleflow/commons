---
id: car.excl.liability-vehicles
type: exclusion
heading: Motor Vehicles
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability arising from a vehicle that is registered, or required to be registered, while being driven on a public road, or for which compulsory third party insurance or a statutory motor accident scheme provides cover. This exclusion does not apply to liability arising from the operation of a vehicle or plant as a tool of trade at the project site.
