If a claim is first made against an insured during the policy period, or during the extended reporting period in [[ref:car.ext.extended-reporting-period]], for a professional services wrongful act committed on or after the retroactive date, and is notified to us as [[ref:car.obl.claims-made-notice]] requires, we will pay:

- the compensation the insured is legally liable to pay; and
- defence costs.

The most we will pay for any one claim, and in total for all claims under this section, is the limit of liability shown for this section. Defence costs are part of that limit.

**Related claims.** All claims arising from the same professional services wrongful act, or from related or similar professional services wrongful acts that share a common cause or form one course of conduct, are one claim. It is treated as first made when the earliest of them was first made, and one excess applies.
