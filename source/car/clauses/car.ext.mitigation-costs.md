heading: Mitigation Costs
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We pay the reasonable cost of temporary protection and other measures taken to prevent or reduce damage we would cover, whether or not damage then happens. The default limit is $250,000 for any one event, unless the schedule shows another amount. This amount is in addition to the contract works sum insured.
