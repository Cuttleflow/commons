since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

1. Make the site safe and take reasonable steps to prevent further damage.
2. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
3. Tell the police about any theft or malicious damage.
4. Tell us what happened and when, and give us photographs, quotes, invoices and the relevant construction contract documents.
5. For a claim against an insured, send us the demand or court documents as soon as they are received, and do not admit liability (see [[ref:car.obl.admissions]]).
6. We will acknowledge the claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision as the General Insurance Code of Practice requires where the insurer subscribes to it.
