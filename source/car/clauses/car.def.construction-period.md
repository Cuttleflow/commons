heading: Construction Period
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

For each project, the period from the start of work at the project site, or the arrival of materials at the project site if earlier, until practical completion. For a single project it is the period shown in the schedule, as extended under [[ref:car.obl.tell-us-about-changes]]. On the annual basis it is the period that [[ref:car.cover.annual-basis]] gives each project.
