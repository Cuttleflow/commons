id: car.excl.cyber
type: exclusion
heading: Cyber Events
since: 2.1.0
changed: 2.2.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss of, damage to or corruption of electronic data, or for loss arising from use of a computer system by a person not permitted by its owner to use it. We do pay for resulting physical damage to the contract works or construction plant, and for re-creating plans, drawings, specifications and digital building models under [[ref:car.ext.plans-and-documents]].
