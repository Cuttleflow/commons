This policy insures construction work, from a single contract to an annual programme of contracts. It has four sections. The schedule shows which apply.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Material Damage | Physical loss of or damage to the contract works, materials and temporary works | Damage during the construction period |
| Public and Products Liability | Liability for injury or property damage to third parties connected with the contract works | Occurrence during the construction period, or after practical completion for completed works |
| Delay in Start-Up | The principal's loss of gross profit, revenue or rent when insured damage delays completion | Delay caused by damage insured under [[ref:car.section.material-damage|heading]] |
| Design and Construct Professional Indemnity | Claims for errors in design and other professional services connected with the contract works | Claims first made and notified during the policy period |

[[ref:car.section.material-damage|heading]] and [[ref:car.section.liability|heading]] apply whenever the schedule shows them. [[ref:car.section.delay|heading]] and [[ref:car.section.design-pi|heading]] apply only if the schedule shows them as included.

**Joint names.** This policy insures the principal, the head contractor, every subcontractor and the other parties described in the definition of [[ref:car.def.insured|heading]], each for its own rights and interests, under one policy. [[ref:car.cond.joint-names]] explains how that works.

**All risks.** [[ref:car.section.material-damage|heading]] covers physical loss or damage from any cause that is not excluded. It does not list the events that are covered. Where we rely on an exclusion, we must show that it applies.

This policy, together with the schedule, the proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:car.section.definitions]].
