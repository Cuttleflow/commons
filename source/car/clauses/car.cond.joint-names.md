heading: Joint Names and Separate Treatment
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Each insured is treated as if insured separately, but the limits and sums insured shown in the schedule are the most we will pay in total for all insureds together.

One insured may claim against another, and this policy responds to the insured against whom the claim is made, as if each had a separate policy, except that [[ref:car.section.liability|heading]] does not cover property damage to the contract works.

The conduct, knowledge or breach of one insured is not imputed to any other insured for the purpose of the duty of disclosure, [[ref:car.excl.design-pi]], [[ref:car.obl.reasonable-precautions]] or [[ref:car.cond.fraud]]. Each insured's cover is judged by its own conduct and knowledge, except that the knowledge of a director or officer of an entity who is responsible for the matter in question is imputed to that entity.
