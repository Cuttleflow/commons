**Contract works.** We pay the cost of repairing, rebuilding or replacing the damaged part of the contract works to a condition substantially the same as, but not better than, its condition immediately before the damage. The cost includes re-performing work, materials, freight and reasonable overheads. We do not deduct for depreciation.

**Construction plant.** We pay the cost of repairing the plant or, if it cannot be repaired economically, its market value immediately before the damage, unless the schedule shows replacement as new.

**Existing structures and principal's contents.** We pay the cost of repairing or reinstating them to their condition immediately before the damage. Where the construction contract requires the principal's own property insurance to respond first, we pay only the amount by which the loss exceeds what that insurance pays.

**Sum insured reinstated.** After a claim, the sum insured is reinstated automatically from the date of the damage. We may charge an additional premium for the reinstatement, calculated at the policy's rates for the remaining construction period.
