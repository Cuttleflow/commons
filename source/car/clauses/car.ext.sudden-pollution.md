heading: Sudden and Accidental Pollution
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We cover liability for personal injury or property damage caused by the discharge, dispersal, release or escape of a pollutant that results from a sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected incident that takes place in its entirety at a specific time and place during the construction period. The default sub-limit is $1,000,000 for any one occurrence and in total for all occurrences, unless the schedule shows another amount. It is part of the limit of liability. [[ref:car.excl.liability-pollution]] does not apply to this cover.
