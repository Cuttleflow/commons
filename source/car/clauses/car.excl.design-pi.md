---
id: car.excl.design-pi
type: exclusion
heading: Design Claims Not Covered
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for a claim:

- arising from facts or circumstances an insured knew of before the policy period started and that a reasonable person in the insured's position would have expected to give rise to a claim;
- for liability assumed under a contract that is greater than liability at law, including an express warranty of fitness for purpose beyond the duty to use reasonable skill and care, unless [[ref:car.end.e12]] applies;
- arising from a deliberate, dishonest or fraudulent act of an insured, established by a final judgment or by the insured's written admission; we pay defence costs until then;
- for the cost of rectifying the defective design itself; or
- for personal injury or property damage, which [[ref:car.section.liability|heading]] covers where it applies. Where a design error causes physical damage, both sections may respond, but we do not pay the same loss twice.
