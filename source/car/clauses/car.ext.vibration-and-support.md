heading: Vibration and Removal of Support
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We cover liability for property damage caused by vibration, or by the removal, weakening or interference with the support of land, buildings or structures, subject to [[ref:car.cond.vibration-and-services]]. The default sub-limit is $2,000,000 for any one occurrence, unless the schedule shows another amount. It is part of the limit of liability. The vibration excess shown in the schedule applies.
