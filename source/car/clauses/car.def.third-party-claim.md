heading: Third-Party Claim
since: 2.2.0
status: draft
---

A demand for compensation, or a civil proceeding, made against an insured for personal injury or property damage under [[ref:car.section.liability|heading]], or for a professional services wrongful act under [[ref:car.section.design-pi|heading]]. It is different from a Claim, which is a request to us for payment.
