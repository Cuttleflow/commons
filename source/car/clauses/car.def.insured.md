heading: Insured
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Each of the following, for its own rights and interests:

- the named insured;
- the principal;
- the head contractor and every contractor and subcontractor of every tier, while engaged on the contract works;
- architects, engineers, consultants, vendors and manufacturers, but only for manual work they carry out at the project site;
- the directors, officers, partners and employees of any of them, acting in that capacity; and
- any other party the construction contract requires to be insured, to the extent it requires.

For [[ref:car.section.design-pi|heading]], insured means only the named insured and its directors, officers, partners and employees, acting in that capacity, unless the schedule names another party.
