heading: Liability to Third Parties
since: 2.1.0
status: draft

We will pay the compensation an insured becomes legally liable to pay because of personal injury or property damage that:

- happens during the construction period within Australia; and
- is caused by an occurrence in connection with the contract works.

The most we will pay for any one occurrence is the limit of liability shown in the schedule (default $20,000,000). We also pay defence costs, as [[ref:car.cover.defence-costs]] sets out.
