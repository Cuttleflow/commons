For [[ref:car.section.design-pi|heading]], tell us in writing about each claim as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any case before the end of the policy period or the extended reporting period. If, during the policy period, an insured becomes aware of facts that might give rise to a claim and tells us in writing before the policy period ends, any claim later arising from those facts is treated as made during the policy period, as s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) provides.
