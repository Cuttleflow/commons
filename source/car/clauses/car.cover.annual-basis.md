If the schedule shows the annual basis, it also shows one of two footings.

**Contracts commencing.** Every construction contract that starts during the policy period, within the business described in the schedule, is a project covered by this policy. Cover for each project continues to practical completion and through its defects liability period, even after the policy period ends.

**Turnover.** Every construction contract under way during the policy period, within the business described in the schedule, is a project covered by this policy. Cover for each project ends when the policy period ends, or at practical completion if earlier, unless we agree in writing to extend it.

**Maximum project value.** A construction contract whose contract value exceeds the maximum project value shown in the schedule is covered only if you declare it to us before work starts and we accept it in writing.

Declarations and premium adjustment are set out in [[ref:car.obl.declarations]] and [[ref:car.cond.premium-adjustment]].
