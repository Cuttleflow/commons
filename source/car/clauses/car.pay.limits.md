The schedule shows the sum insured or limit of liability for each section, and any sub-limits. A sub-limit is part of, not in addition to, the limit it sits under, unless this policy says the amount is in addition. If one event or occurrence is covered under more than one section, each section pays only its own part of the loss, and we do not pay the same loss twice.
