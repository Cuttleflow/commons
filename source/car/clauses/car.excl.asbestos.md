heading: Asbestos
since: 2.1.0

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from asbestos in any form. Under [[ref:car.section.material-damage|heading]] we do pay the cost of lawfully removing asbestos that is unexpectedly found and disturbed by damage we cover, up to $100,000 for any one event, unless the schedule or [[ref:car.end.e04]] shows another amount.
