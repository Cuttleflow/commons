The named insured may cancel this policy at any time by telling us in writing. We will refund the premium for the unexpired policy period in proportion. For a single project, the refund is subject to the minimum premium shown in the schedule, because construction risk is not spread evenly over the construction period.

We may cancel this policy only on a ground allowed by the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), by giving written notice as s.59 of that Act requires. We will refund the premium for the unexpired policy period in proportion.

On the contracts commencing footing, cancellation does not end cover for projects that started before it took effect. Cancellation does not affect a claim for damage, an occurrence or a claim that happened or was made before it took effect.
