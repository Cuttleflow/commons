heading: Telling Us About Changes
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable if:

- the construction period for a single project will run past the date shown in the schedule; we will extend cover on terms we agree with you;
- the contract value increases by more than the escalation allowance;
- work on a project stops for more than 60 consecutive days; or
- the nature of the contract works changes in a way that increases the risk.

We will tell you whether the change affects your cover or premium.
