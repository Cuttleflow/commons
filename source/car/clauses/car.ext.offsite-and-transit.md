heading: Offsite Storage and Transit
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Materials for the contract works are covered while stored at a place in Australia away from the project site, and while in transit within Australia by road or rail, but not by sea or air. The default limit is $500,000 for any one location or any one conveyance, unless the schedule or [[ref:car.end.e10]] shows another amount. This limit is part of, not in addition to, the contract works sum insured.
