heading: Professional Advice and Design
since: 2.1.0
changed: 2.2.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability arising from professional advice, design or specification given for a fee. That liability, including personal injury or property damage caused by a professional services wrongful act, is covered under [[ref:car.section.design-pi|heading]] where it applies, and not under this section. This exclusion does not apply to personal injury or property damage caused by manual work at the project site.
