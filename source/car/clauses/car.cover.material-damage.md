heading: Damage to the Contract Works
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We will pay for damage to the contract works that happens during the construction period within the geographical scope, from any cause that is not excluded.

We pay as set out in [[ref:car.pay.basis-of-settlement]]. The most we will pay for any one event is the contract works sum insured, adjusted under [[ref:car.ext.escalation]], plus any separate sums insured shown in the schedule for existing structures, principal's contents and construction plant, plus the amounts the extensions to this section say are payable in addition.
