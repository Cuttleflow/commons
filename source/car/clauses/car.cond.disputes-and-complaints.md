If an insured is unhappy with our service or a decision, contact us at the details in the schedule. We will acknowledge the complaint within one business day and give our decision within 30 calendar days.

If the insured is not satisfied with our response, or we have not responded within 30 calendar days, the complaint may be taken to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), a free and independent dispute resolution service, if it is within AFCA's rules: www.afca.org.au, 1800 931 678, info@afca.org.au.
