type: obligation
heading: Telling Us About Claims
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after damage, an occurrence or a delay that may lead to a claim, and about any claim, demand or proceedings received. Tell the police promptly about any theft or malicious damage. Give us the information and documents we reasonably ask for, and let us inspect damaged property before it is repaired, unless urgent work is needed to make the site safe or to prevent further damage.
