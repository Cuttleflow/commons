The insured bears the excess shown in the schedule:

- for [[ref:car.section.material-damage|heading]], for each event;
- for [[ref:car.section.liability|heading]], for each occurrence, with defence costs in addition;
- for [[ref:car.section.delay|heading]], as the time excess; and
- for [[ref:car.section.design-pi|heading]], for each claim.

The schedule may show separate excesses for storm and water damage, earthquake, testing and commissioning, existing structures and vibration. Where more than one excess applies to the same event under one section, only the highest is deducted.
