After practical completion of each project, this section continues for its defects liability period to cover:

- damage to the contract works caused during the defects liability period by a cause that happened during the construction period; and
- damage to the contract works caused by an insured while at the project site to carry out its obligations under the construction contract to rectify defects.

We do not pay the cost of rectifying the defect itself. We pay for damage the defect causes, as [[ref:car.excl.defects]] allows.
