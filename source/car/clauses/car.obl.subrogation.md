type: obligation
heading: Recovery from Others
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

After we pay a claim, we may take over the insured's rights to recover the amount from anyone else and act in its name. The insured must help us, at our cost. We waive our rights of recovery against every insured, and against any party the insured was required by the construction contract or another written contract made before the loss to hold harmless or insure, except where the loss was caused by that party's fraudulent, dishonest or malicious act.
