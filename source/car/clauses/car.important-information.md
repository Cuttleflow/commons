**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

**Who this policy is for.** This wording is designed for businesses that carry out or commission construction work, and for distribution to wholesale clients within the meaning of s.761G of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). It is not a Product Disclosure Statement, and no Target Market Determination has been prepared for it. An insurer that offers it to retail clients must first prepare the documents the law requires for retail distribution.

**Your duty of disclosure.** Before you enter into, renew, extend, vary or reinstate this policy, s.21 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) requires you to tell us every matter you know, or that a reasonable person in your circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we waive. If you do not comply, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

Only the named insured owes this duty. Other insureds do not, and a failure by the named insured does not affect their cover except as [[ref:car.cond.joint-names]] allows.

**Claims-made cover.** [[ref:car.section.design-pi|heading]] covers only claims first made against an insured during the policy period and notified to us as [[ref:car.obl.claims-made-notice]] requires. If, during the policy period, an insured becomes aware of facts that might give rise to a claim and tells us in writing before the policy period ends, s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) means we cannot refuse a later claim arising from those facts only because it was made after the policy period ended. Tell us about possible claims as soon as you become aware of them.

**Terrorism.** This policy may be an eligible insurance contract under the Terrorism Insurance Act 2003 (Cth). Where it is, [[ref:car.excl.terrorism]] has no effect for loss or liability arising from a declared terrorist incident affecting eligible property in Australia, and our liability may be reduced by any reduction percentage declared under that Act.

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
