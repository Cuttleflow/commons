---
id: car.excl.liability-workers
type: exclusion
heading: Injury to Workers
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for personal injury to a worker of an insured, to the extent the insured is required by a workers compensation law to insure that liability, or would be but for an exemption. This exclusion does not apply to one insured's liability for personal injury to another insured's workers.
