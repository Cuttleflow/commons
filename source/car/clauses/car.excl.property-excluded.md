---
id: car.excl.property-excluded
type: exclusion
heading: Property Not Covered
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for damage to:

- land, including reclaimed land;
- existing roads and pavements that are not part of the contract works;
- dams, canals, tunnels, wharves, jetties and offshore works, unless [[ref:car.end.e07]] applies;
- watercraft and aircraft;
- vehicles registered for road use, unless they form part of the contract works;
- money, securities and documents, other than as [[ref:car.ext.plans-and-documents]] covers; or
- personal property of employees or contractors, unless the schedule shows it.
