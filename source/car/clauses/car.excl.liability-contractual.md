---
id: car.excl.liability-contractual
type: exclusion
heading: Contractual Liability
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability an insured assumes under a contract, to the extent it is greater than the liability the insured would have had without the contract. This exclusion does not apply to liability assumed under the construction contract, or under usual terms for site access or for the hire of plant and equipment.
