After practical completion of each project, we will pay the compensation an insured becomes legally liable to pay because of personal injury or property damage that happens during the policy period, or for a project on the contracts commencing footing during its defects liability period, and is caused by the completed contract works.

The most we will pay for all occurrences under this clause is the products aggregate limit shown in the schedule. This clause does not pay to repair or replace the completed work itself; see [[ref:car.excl.liability-own-work]].
