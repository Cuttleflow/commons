heading: Complying with Building Law
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

The cost of repairing damage we cover includes the extra cost of complying with the building laws and codes in force at the time of repair, for the damaged part of the contract works. It does not include the cost of complying with an order or notice served before the damage happened.
