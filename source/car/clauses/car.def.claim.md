For [[ref:car.section.design-pi|heading]]: a written demand for compensation, or civil proceedings, alleging a professional services wrongful act. For every other section: a request to us for payment under this policy. A demand or proceeding against an insured is a third-party claim, not a Claim, under every section except [[ref:car.section.design-pi|heading]].
