heading: Delay Caused by Insured Damage
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

If damage covered by [[ref:car.section.material-damage|heading]] delays the date on which a project reaches practical completion, we will pay the principal, for the period of insured delay after the time excess and up to the end of the indemnity period:

- the loss of gross profit, revenue or rent, as the schedule shows, as [[ref:car.pay.delay-loss]] calculates it; and
- the increased cost of working reasonably incurred to reduce the delay, up to the amount of loss it avoids.

The most we will pay is the sum insured for this section.
