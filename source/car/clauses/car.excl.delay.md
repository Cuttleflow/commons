---
id: car.excl.delay
type: exclusion
heading: Delay Not Covered
since: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for:

- delay not caused by damage that [[ref:car.section.material-damage|heading]] covers, or that would have happened anyway;
- fines, penalties or liquidated damages for late completion;
- loss after the end of the indemnity period; or
- delay caused by a shortage of funds, or by changes to the scope of the project.
