On the annual basis, the named insured declares the estimated contract value of the projects at the start of the policy period, and the actual contract value within 60 days after it ends. A project above the maximum project value must be declared before work starts, as [[ref:car.cover.annual-basis]] requires. We will not reduce a claim because a good-faith estimate proves too low; our remedy for under-declaration is the premium adjustment, except where the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) gives us a remedy for non-disclosure or misrepresentation.
