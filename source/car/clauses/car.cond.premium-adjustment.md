since: 2.1.0

---

Where the premium is based on declared or estimated values, we adjust it at the rates shown in the schedule once actual values are declared under [[ref:car.obl.declarations]] or [[ref:car.ext.escalation]], subject to the minimum premium. A late declaration does not end cover, but we may charge interest on premium underpaid.
