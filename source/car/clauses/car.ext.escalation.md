If the contract value increases during the construction period because of variations or cost escalation, the contract works sum insured increases automatically by up to 15% of it, or the percentage shown in the schedule. Tell us the final contract value at practical completion; we will adjust the premium under [[ref:car.cond.premium-adjustment]].
