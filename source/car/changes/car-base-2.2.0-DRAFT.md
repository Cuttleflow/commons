Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. Changes from version 2.1.0:

- Third-Party Claim: new definition for a demand or proceeding made against an insured. Claim keeps its meaning of a request to us for payment (and, for Design and Construct Professional Indemnity, a demand alleging a professional services wrongful act). The Defence Costs definition, the Defence Costs clause, Admissions, Telling Us About Claims and the Fines and Penalties exclusion now refer to a third-party claim, so that our right to defend and the duty not to admit liability attach to claims made against an insured.
- Professional Advice and Design (exclusion) and Design and Construct Professional Indemnity: personal injury or property damage caused by a professional services wrongful act is now paid under Design and Construct Professional Indemnity and not under Public and Products Liability. A new paragraph in the Design and Construct Professional Indemnity cover says so. The last exclusion in Design Claims Not Covered now applies only to injury or damage caused by manual work at the project site, which Public and Products Liability covers. The statement that both sections may respond is removed. The same loss is never paid twice.
- Cyber Events (general exclusion): does not apply to the cost of re-creating plans, drawings, specifications and digital building models under Plans, Documents and Digital Models.
- Removal of Debris: the default limit is 20% of the loss or $500,000, whichever is less. It was whichever is greater. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Professional Fees: the default limit is 10% of the loss or $250,000, whichever is less. It had no dollar cap. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Control Group: new definition. Design Claims Not Covered, for facts known before the policy period, and the Joint Names clause now turn on what a member of the insured's control group knew. Before, the Joint Names clause treated the knowledge of any director or officer responsible for the matter as the entity's knowledge.
- Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) (previously headed Insurance Contracts Act): replaced with a full statement that section 54 applies to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation.
- Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), sections 199A and 199B: new condition for the directors and officers insured under Design and Construct Professional Indemnity. Defence costs advanced in a civil penalty or criminal proceeding must be repaid if a final judgment or court finding establishes conduct that cannot be covered. Wording Partner review tag added.
- Sanctions: replaced with the library-wide wording naming the Australian regimes and, to the extent they apply to us, the UK, US and EU regimes. United Nations sanctions are no longer named separately. Wording Partner review tag added.
- Cancellation: restated. The named insured may cancel at any time for a pro-rata refund, less non-refundable government charges, subject to the single-project minimum premium. No refund applies to contract works for which we have paid a total loss. We may cancel only as the Insurance Contracts Act allows.
- Other Insurance: replaced. We do not refuse or reduce a payment because of other insurance and may seek contribution. This policy is excess only of a specified underlying policy named in the schedule or an endorsement.
