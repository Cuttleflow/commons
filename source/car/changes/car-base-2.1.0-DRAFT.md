This is the first Commons release of the Meridian Construction All Risks and Liability wording. It is converted from v2.0.0 (2026/CARL/0001, July 2026), which superseded Construction and Contract Works v1.0.0 (2026/CCW/0001). The cover and the settled positions of v2.0.0 are kept. The changes below are drafting remediation, and none reduces cover except where stated.

- Converted to single-source clauses with no clause numbers. Cross-references are by heading.
- Endorsements E01 to E12 were listed by title only in v2.0.0. Each now has full operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger.
- Terms that decided cover but were not defined are now defined: contract value, construction contract, construction plant, existing structures, principal's contents, project, project site, geographical scope, event, damage, personal injury, property damage, professional services wrongful act, retroactive date and pollutant. "Period of cover" and "period of insurance" are replaced throughout by "policy period".
- Event: storm, flood, earthquake, tsunami, bushfire and cyclone damage within 72 hours is one event, so one excess applies.
- Design and construct professional indemnity: a related-claims rule, an aggregate limit, an extended reporting period that also applies on cancellation, and a conduct exclusion that applies only on final judgment or written admission, with defence costs paid until then.
- Delay in start-up: how the loss is calculated, with savings deducted, and how the time excess works.
- Joint names: separate treatment of each insured is now applied to the duty of disclosure, the conduct exclusion, reasonable precautions and fraud.
- Added an Insurance Contracts Act savings condition (s.54), a standalone sanctions condition and a jurisdiction condition.
- Governing law is fixed to the state or territory shown in the schedule, or the project site, instead of the named insured's principal place of business, which could change during the policy.
- Cessation of work: fire, lightning and explosion are now covered while work has stopped for more than 60 days. This is a new concession.
- Licence changed from CC BY-SA 4.0 to CC BY 4.0, in line with the rest of the Commons.
- Two WP REVIEW tags: owner-builder home building cover as a retail product, and contract works as eligible property under the Terrorism Insurance Act 2003 (Cth).
