Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. It replaces version 1.3.0 (November 2026). Changes from version 1.3.0:

- Company Reimbursement (Side B): now has a claims-made trigger. It applies to a claim first made against an insured person during the policy period for a wrongful act on or after the retroactive date, as Side A does.
- Retroactive Date: new operative condition, and the Defence of Regulatory Proceedings, AFCA Complaints, Side B and Fund Assets — Third-Party Service Provider Failure clauses now state it. Related wrongful acts that began before the retroactive date are treated as committed before it. A stray reference in Item 3 of the schedule is corrected. Where no retroactive date is shown, the prior known matters and prior and pending litigation exclusions run from the start of the policy period. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Crime section: each insuring clause now pays the loss of the company or a fund, and each runs on discovery during the policy period. The definition of Discovery follows the Comprehensive Crime wording, and Responsible person has a fallback for entities without those roles. In the Crime section, you and your mean the company only. The Loss definition now has one causation test for Crime.
- New Crime clauses: Prior Discovery, Prior Insurance and Superseded Policies, Valuation and Recoveries, and Extended Discovery Period. The Single Loss extension now refers to superseded policies. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Payment to Funds: the company must apply the payment to the fund, the payment discharges us, and the clause is marked for legal review.
- Extended Reporting Period: no longer applies on cancellation for non-payment of premium or where another policy replaces this one, matching the Extended Reporting Period endorsement. Default set for underwriter confirmation. The reference to a discovery notification period in Fund Wind-Up Run-Off now points to the new Extended Discovery Period — Crime.
- Priority of Payment: now applies when a section limit or the overall aggregate is or may be insufficient, and gives discharge for good-faith payments.
- Other Insurance: the policy no longer reduces its payment because of other insurance, except where the schedule or an endorsement names an underlying policy. Fund insurance, the crime and cyber boundary and contribution are stated. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Sanctions: now a standalone general condition, removed from the war, terrorism and nuclear exclusion, which is renamed.
- Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), sections 199A and 199B: new condition, marked for legal review, with advance-and-repay defence costs.
- Fines and Penalties: new clause naming the New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland bans on insuring work health and safety penalties and excluding criminal fines. The definition of Loss and the Financial Accountability Regime extension refer to it, and the extension is marked for legal review.
- New definitions of Control group and Final adjudication. Company knowledge is the knowledge of the control group in the duty of disclosure, severability, prior known matters and Non-Avoidance provisions.
- Severability and Non-Imputation: now covers the company and funds as well as insured persons. A fund's knowledge is treated as that of its manager's control group. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Non-Avoidance — Side A: renamed from Non-Rescindable — Side A. It now covers non-disclosure as well as misrepresentation, and waives the section 28(3) reduction for every innocent insured person.
- Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): replaced with a single statement of the Act's prevailing effect and of how section 54 applies to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation.
- Cancellation: you may cancel at any time and receive a pro-rata refund. Our cancellation requires statutory notice and not less than 30 days (the 30-day period is the library's settled position).
- Side A Difference in Conditions endorsement: the trigger is restated, the other insurance is the insurance shown in the schedule, and Priority of Payment no longer says its payments are made first. The Side A limit is stated to be in addition to the overall aggregate.
- Who This Policy Is For: new statement of the retail client position, with a note on compensation arrangements for licensees.
