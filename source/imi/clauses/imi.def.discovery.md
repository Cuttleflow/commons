heading: Discovery
since: 1.3.0
changed: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

The point at which any responsible person first has reason to believe, on the information then available, that a loss covered by [[ref:imi.section.crime]] has been or is likely to have been sustained, whether or not the amount, cause, or full circumstances of the loss are then known, and regardless of when the underlying act occurred. **Discover** and **discovered** have matching meanings.
