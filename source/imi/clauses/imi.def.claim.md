heading: Claim
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Any of the following:

(a) any written demand for compensation against an insured;

(b) any writ, summons, or legal proceeding against an insured;

(c) any regulatory proceeding;

(d) any complaint to AFCA that alleges financial loss;

(e) any derivative action; and

(f) any extradition proceeding against an insured person in connection with a wrongful act.

Claims are related if they arise from the same wrongful act, or from wrongful acts that are the same, similar, or related, or that have a common originating cause, act, omission, or set of facts or circumstances. Related claims are treated as a single claim, first made when the earliest of them was first made.
