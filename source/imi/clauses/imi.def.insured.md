heading: Insured
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

(a) the company (for all sections);

(b) any insured person (for all sections); and

(c) any fund, for [[ref:imi.section.pl]] and [[ref:imi.section.crime]] only, to the extent you manage or administer the fund. If a fund has separate insurance of its own that responds to the same loss, this policy applies excess of that other insurance (see [[ref:imi.pay.other-insurance]]); the fund does not cease to be an insured merely because it holds separate insurance of its own.
