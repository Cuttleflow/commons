since: 1.3.0

---

(a) the company (for all sections);

(b) any insured person (for all sections); and

(c) any fund, for [[ref:imi.section.pl]] and [[ref:imi.section.crime]] only, to the extent you manage or administer the fund. A fund does not cease to be an insured merely because it holds separate insurance of its own; [[ref:imi.pay.other-insurance]] states how this policy applies where it does.
