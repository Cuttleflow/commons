id: imi.ext.court-attendance

heading: Court Attendance
legacy_number: '2.10'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

$500 per day for an insured person or employee attending court, a tribunal, or an AFCA hearing as a witness in connection with a covered claim, subject to a maximum of $10,000 in total for any one claim.
