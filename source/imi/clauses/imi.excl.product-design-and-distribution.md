id: imi.excl.product-design-and-distribution
type: exclusion
heading: Product Design and Distribution
legacy_number: '5.10'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Claims alleging that a financial product was inherently defective in its design, or that the product was distributed outside its target market in breach of the design and distribution obligations — except to the extent the claim alleges a wrongful act by an insured in the design, governance, or distribution process.

ASIC stop orders and product intervention orders are covered as **regulatory proceedings** under [[ref:imi.cover.defence-of-regulatory-proceedings]].
