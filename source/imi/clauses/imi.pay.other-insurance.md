If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows. This does not apply where the schedule or an endorsement states that this policy is excess of a specified underlying policy, in which case this policy pays only after the limit of that specified policy is exhausted.

**Funds:** A fund may hold separate insurance of its own. That insurance is other insurance for this clause. We will not refuse or reduce our payment because of it, and we may seek contribution from the fund's insurer as the law allows. If the schedule names a fund's separate policy as a specified underlying policy, this policy is excess of it as stated above.

**Side A:** An insured person is not required to claim under any other policy first.

**Crime and cyber:** Where a loss under [[ref:imi.section.crime]] that is direct theft of money, securities, digital assets, or property by computer-enabled fraud is also covered, or purportedly covered, under a cyber insurance policy, this policy responds as the primary policy for that loss, whatever the cyber policy says. This does not apply to a cyber policy that is written to attach in excess of this policy.

**Contribution:** If another insurer's policy and this policy each purport to be excess of the other for the same loss, we will contribute in proportion to the limits of liability each policy would otherwise provide for that loss, and will not decline the loss only because the other policy has a similar clause.
