This policy is excess over other valid insurance responding to the same loss, except [[ref:imi.section.do]] Side A, which is always primary for non-indemnifiable loss. This clause states a priority rule; it does not relieve us of liability merely because other insurance exists (see s 45 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth)).
