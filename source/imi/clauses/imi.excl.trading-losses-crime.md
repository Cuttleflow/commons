heading: Trading Losses — Crime
legacy_number: '5.8'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Direct investment or trading losses — whether authorised or unauthorised — except where the trading was conducted by an employee committing a dishonest act and qualifies under [[ref:imi.cover.employee-dishonesty]].
