If a claim is first made against an insured person during the policy period for a wrongful act in their capacity as a director or officer of the company, committed on or after the retroactive date, and the company has indemnified the insured person for loss that the company is legally permitted to indemnify, we will reimburse the company.

The Side B **excess** shown in the schedule applies.
