id: imi.excl.prior-and-pending-litigation
type: exclusion
heading: Prior and Pending Litigation
legacy_number: '5.5'
since: 1.3.0
changed: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Claims arising from proceedings pending before the retroactive date (or, if no retroactive date is shown, before the start of the policy period), or from substantially the same facts.
