[[ref:imi.pay.defence-costs]] is replaced by the following for the sections shown in the schedule for this endorsement. Defence costs are paid in addition to the limit of liability, up to the defence costs limit shown in the schedule. We advance defence costs as they are incurred. Payment of defence costs does not reduce the limit of liability available for compensation.

The defence costs limit is the most we will pay for defence costs for all claims under the section. Once it is exhausted we have no further obligation to pay defence costs. Amounts paid as compensation reduce only the limit of liability.

Schedule variables: sections; defence costs limit.
