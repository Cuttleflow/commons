heading: Prior Insurance and Superseded Policies — Crime
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

If this policy replaces a prior crime or fidelity policy, we will cover a loss under [[ref:imi.section.crime]] that is first discovered during this policy period, even if the act causing it occurred during the period of the prior policy, provided:

(a) the prior policy has expired and no claim has been made under it for the same loss;

(b) the loss would have been covered under the prior policy at the time of the act; and

(c) the loss is not excluded under this policy.
