An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables (sub-limits, excesses, periods, named sections, funds and parties) shown for it in the schedule. Where an endorsement and the rest of this policy conflict, the endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. If a variable an endorsement needs is not shown in the schedule, the endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the schedule. These endorsements are for use with version 1.4.x of this wording.

| Code | Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | US and Canada Jurisdiction Extension | Writes back the US / Canada Jurisdiction exclusion for the sections and subsidiaries named |
| E02 | Superannuation Trustee — Enhanced | Extends the superannuation cover for RSE licensees |
| E03 | Responsible Entity — Enhanced | Extends the cover for responsible entity duties, continuous disclosure and related party transactions |
| E04 | Alternative Strategies | Extends the cover for performance-fee clawback, side pockets, gates and redemption suspension |
| E05 | Custodian Failure — Enhanced | Covers claims arising from custodian insolvency or commingling of fund assets, to a sub-limit |
| E06 | ESG and Greenwashing | Extends the cover for claims about sustainability-related statements and disclosures |
| E07 | Defence Costs in Addition to the Limit | Pays defence costs in addition to the limit of liability, to a separate defence costs limit |
| E08 | Extended Reporting Period | Sets a purchased extended reporting period of 12, 24, 36 or 72 months for the liability sections |
| E09 | Run-off Following Change of Control or Cessation of AFSL | Gives run-off cover for wrongful acts before a change of control or the cessation of the AFSL |
| E10 | Prospectus and PDS Liability — Enhanced | Extends the cover for claims about disclosure documents for the offers named |
| E11 | Excess Layer | Attaches this policy excess of an underlying limit shown in the schedule |
| E12 | Employment Practices Liability | Adds cover for claims by employees about employment practices, to a separate limit |
| E13 | Cryptocurrency and Digital Asset Fund Management | Sets custody, wallet-security and valuation terms for funds with material digital-asset exposure |
| E14 | Side A Difference in Conditions | Gives insured persons a separate Side A limit that pays when other insurance does not |
