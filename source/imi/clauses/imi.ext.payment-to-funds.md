Where this Section covers a fund's direct financial loss, we will pay you, for the benefit of the affected fund, unless we agree otherwise in writing with you and the fund. [[ref:imi.def.insured]] and [[ref:imi.pay.other-insurance]] (Other Insurance) state how this Section applies where the fund has separate insurance of its own.
