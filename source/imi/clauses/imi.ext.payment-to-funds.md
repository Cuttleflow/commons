Where [[ref:imi.section.crime]] covers a fund's loss, we will pay you, for the benefit of the affected fund, unless we agree otherwise in writing with you and the fund. You must promptly apply the amount we pay to the fund's assets. Payment to you under this clause discharges our liability to the fund for that amount. [[ref:imi.def.insured]] and [[ref:imi.pay.other-insurance]] (Other Insurance) state how this policy applies where the fund has separate insurance of its own.
