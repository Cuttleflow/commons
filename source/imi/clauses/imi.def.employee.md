type: definition
heading: Employee
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Any natural person who works for the company under a contract of employment, including permanent, fixed-term, casual, and part-time employees. It includes:

(a) authorised representatives appointed under your AFSL;

(b) persons seconded to you and working under your direction;

(c) temporary and labour hire workers under your supervision; and

(d) volunteers and interns.

It does not include independent contractors, external auditors, custodians, or prime brokers.
