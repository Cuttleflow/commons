We will pay the insured's defence costs in responding to complaints lodged with the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA) during the policy period in connection with financial services activities performed on or after the retroactive date (see [[ref:imi.cond.retroactive-date]]). We will also pay any determination, award, or settlement made through the AFCA process.
