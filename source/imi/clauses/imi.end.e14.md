We will pay an insured person's non-indemnifiable loss, as described in [[ref:imi.cover.personal-liability-non-indemnifiable-loss-side-a]], up to the Side A limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement, if the loss is not paid because:

- the limit of liability of the section has been exhausted by payment; or
- any other directors' and officers' insurance shown in the schedule for this endorsement has been exhausted by payment, or does not pay the loss because the other insurer is insolvent, because the other insurer refuses or fails to pay within 60 days of a written request, or because the other insurance has been cancelled or avoided.

The Side A limit is in addition to the limit of liability of the section and the overall aggregate. It is reduced only by payments under this endorsement. No excess applies. [[ref:imi.cond.non-rescindable-side-a]] applies to it. [[ref:imi.excl.prior-known-matters]] and [[ref:imi.excl.insured-versus-insured]] apply to it as they apply to Side A. [[ref:imi.pay.priority-of-payment]] does not apply to the Side A limit, which only insured persons may claim against. Schedule variables: Side A limit; other directors' and officers' insurance.
