We will pay an insured person's non-indemnifiable loss, as described in [[ref:imi.cover.personal-liability-non-indemnifiable-loss-side-a]], up to the Side A limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement, if the loss is not paid under this policy because the limit of liability of the section has been exhausted, or is not paid under other insurance that applies to it.

The Side A limit is in addition to the limit of liability of the section. It is reduced only by payments under this endorsement. No excess applies. [[ref:imi.pay.priority-of-payment]] applies, and payments under this endorsement are made first.
