If a claim is first made against an insured during the policy period for a wrongful act in connection with financial services activities performed on or after the retroactive date, we will pay:

(a) any amount the insured becomes legally liable to pay as compensation (including the claimant's costs); and

(b) the insured's defence costs,

for any actual or alleged:

(i) negligence, breach of duty of care, or failure to exercise reasonable care and skill in providing financial services

(ii) negligent misstatement, misrepresentation, or misleading advice about a financial product or investment

(iii) breach of fiduciary duty or trustee duty (including the duty to act in the best interests of investors or members)

(iv) breach of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), including AFSL obligations, the managed investments regime (Chapter 5C), design and distribution obligations (Part 7.8A), and the financial services conduct provisions

(v) breach of the ASIC Act 2001 (Cth), including misleading or deceptive conduct (s 12DA) and unconscionable conduct (s 12CA–12CC)

(vi) breach of the Australian Consumer Law in connection with financial services

(vii) breach of the Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993 (Cth), including the best financial interests duty, the sole purpose test, and prudential standards (where you are a superannuation trustee)

(viii) breach of the Financial Accountability Regime Act 2023 (Cth) (FAR), including accountability obligations of accountable persons

(ix) failure to comply with anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing obligations under the AML/CTF Act 2006 (Cth), to the extent the failure arises from your financial services activities

(x) breach of confidence, misuse of confidential information, or breach of privacy in connection with your financial services activities

(xi) defamation arising from your research reports, investor communications, or marketing materials

(xii) infringement of intellectual property (copyright, trademark) in your publications or marketing

(xiii) any other civil liability arising from your financial services activities
