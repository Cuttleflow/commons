Claims arising from deliberately dishonest, fraudulent, or criminal conduct — but only after final adjudication (see [[ref:imi.def.final-adjudication]]). Defence costs advanced until then, repayable if there is final adjudication. Severability applies (see [[ref:imi.cond.severability-and-non-imputation]]).
