id: imi.excl.dishonesty-and-fraud
type: exclusion
heading: 'Dishonesty and Fraud — Professional Liability and Directors'' and Officers'' Liability'
legacy_number: '5.1'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Claims arising from deliberately dishonest, fraudulent, or criminal conduct — but only after final adjudication (a judgment or finding after exhaustion of all appeal rights, or a formal admission by the insured person concerned). Defence costs advanced until then, repayable if there is final adjudication. Severability applies (see [[ref:imi.cond.severability-and-non-imputation]]).
