If the limit of liability of a section, or the overall aggregate, is or may be insufficient to pay all amounts payable, we will pay in this order:

First: [[ref:imi.section.do]] Side A — personal non-indemnifiable loss and defence costs of insured persons.

Second: [[ref:imi.section.pl]] — professional liability claims and regulatory proceedings.

Third: [[ref:imi.section.do]] Side B — company reimbursement.

Fourth: [[ref:imi.section.crime]] — crime losses.

If we make a payment in good faith in this order, we have no further liability for the amount paid, even if later claims mean the limit is not enough to pay a higher-priority amount in full. This order does not apply to the separate Side A limit under [[ref:imi.end.e14]].
