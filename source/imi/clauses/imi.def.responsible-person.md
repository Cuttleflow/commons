type: definition
heading: Responsible person
since: 1.3.0
changed: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Any director, officer, partner, principal, risk manager, compliance officer, internal auditor, chief compliance officer, chief financial officer, or head of finance or operations of the company; or, where the company has no natural person holding any of those roles, its most senior officer or manager responsible for its financial affairs at the relevant time.
