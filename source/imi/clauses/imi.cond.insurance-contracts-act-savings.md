Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts, or modifies the operation of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) (the Act). Where any provision of this policy is inconsistent with the Act, the Act prevails. Without limiting the foregoing, s 54 of the Act applies to any act or omission by an insured after this policy's inception in relation to a claim or circumstance, so that we may not refuse to pay, or reduce our liability in respect of, a claim by reason of that act or omission except to the extent permitted by s 54.
