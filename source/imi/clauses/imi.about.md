[[ref:imi.section.pl]]: Professional Liability — claims by investors, clients, and regulators arising from your financial services activities.

[[ref:imi.section.do]]: Directors' and Officers' Liability — personal liability of your directors and officers for management decisions.

[[ref:imi.section.crime]]: Crime — direct financial loss from employee dishonesty, fraud, and theft.

By integrating these covers, this policy eliminates the gaps and overlaps that arise when you purchase separate PI, D&O, and crime policies — and provides a single aggregate to manage.

This is a claims-made and notified policy ([[ref:imi.section.pl]] and [[ref:imi.section.do]]) and a discovery policy ([[ref:imi.section.crime]]).

This policy, together with your schedule and any endorsements, forms your contract of insurance. Words in bold have special meanings — see [[ref:imi.section.definitions]].

**Notice under s 40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth):** if, during the policy period, you give us written notice of facts that might give rise to a claim against you, we cannot later refuse to cover a claim arising from those facts merely because it is made after this policy expires. [[ref:imi.cover.notification-of-circumstances]] and [[ref:imi.obl.notify-circumstances]] explain how to give that notice. This notice is given to you before you enter into this contract, as required by s 40(1) of the Act.
