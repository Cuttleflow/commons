Defence costs for proceedings under the Financial Accountability Regime Act 2023 (Cth) against an insured person as an accountable person.

If the proceeding results in the forfeiture or clawback of the insured person's deferred remuneration, we will pay the forfeited amount — provided no final adjudication of dishonesty. [[ref:imi.excl.fines-and-penalties]] and [[ref:imi.cond.corporations-act-199a-199b]] apply to this extension.

[WP REVIEW: whether paying forfeited or clawed-back deferred remuneration, or a penalty under the Financial Accountability Regime Act 2023 (Cth), is insurable and compatible with the prudential standards on hedging deferred remuneration.]
