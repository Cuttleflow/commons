type: definition
heading: Insured person
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Any natural person who is, was, or during the policy period becomes:

(a) a director (including shadow and de facto directors) of the company;

(b) an officer of the company (including company secretary, CEO, CFO, CIO, chief compliance officer, portfolio managers, and any person who participates in investment or management decisions affecting the whole or substantial part of the business);

(c) a member of any investment committee, compliance committee, risk committee, or equivalent body;

(d) a trustee or member of the board of a fund (where the fund has its own governing body);

(e) an equivalent person in any subsidiary; and

(f) the spouse, domestic partner, estate, or legal representative of any of the above — but only for claims arising from that person's wrongful acts.
