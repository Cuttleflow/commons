If during the policy period an insured becomes aware of any fact, matter, or circumstance that might reasonably be expected to give rise to a claim, and you notify us in writing during the policy period, any later claim arising from those circumstances will be treated as first made during this policy period. This clause implements s 40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) — see [[ref:imi.obl.notify-circumstances]] for the corresponding obligation.
