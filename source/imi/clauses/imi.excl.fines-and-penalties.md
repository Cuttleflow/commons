We do not pay any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured. In particular, we do not pay a penalty imposed under work health and safety law in a State or Territory whose law prohibits insurance of it (including New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland), and we do not pay a criminal fine.

[WP REVIEW: confirm the list of jurisdictions that prohibit insurance of WHS penalties; library sources differ on WA and Victoria.]

This applies wherever this policy pays, or includes in loss, a fine or penalty, including the fines and penalties of an insured person under [[ref:imi.def.loss]] and the amounts paid under [[ref:imi.ext.far-proceeding-costs]]. Defence costs are not affected.
