---
id: imi.def.wrongful-act
type: definition
heading: Wrongful act
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Any actual or alleged act, error, omission, misstatement, misleading statement, breach of duty (including fiduciary duty), breach of trust, breach of any statutory or regulatory obligation, or neglect, committed by an insured in connection with your financial services activities or in the management of the company.
