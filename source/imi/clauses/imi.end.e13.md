[[ref:imi.ext.digital-assets-and-cryptocurrency]] applies to the funds shown in the schedule for this endorsement on the following terms, which apply in addition to that extension and do not remove or narrow it.

The custody and wallet-security arrangements shown in the schedule apply. If you do not comply with an arrangement, we may reduce what we pay only to the extent that our interests were prejudiced by the non-compliance, as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

Digital assets are valued at the market price at the time the loss is discovered, taken from the price source shown in the schedule. A fall in the market price of a digital asset is not a loss. [[ref:imi.excl.trading-losses-crime]] continues to apply.

The sub-limit shown in the schedule is the most we will pay under this endorsement. It forms part of, and is not in addition to, the limit of liability for the section.

Schedule variables: funds covered; custody and wallet-security arrangements; price source; sub-limit; additional excess (optional).
