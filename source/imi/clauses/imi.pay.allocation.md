If a claim involves covered and uncovered matters or parties, we will agree a fair and reasonable allocation having regard to the relative legal and financial exposure of covered and uncovered matters and parties. If we cannot agree, a Senior Counsel, mutually agreed or, failing agreement, nominated by the President of the relevant State or Territory Bar Association, will determine the allocation, and that determination is binding on the allocation question only.
