heading: Why an integrated policy, not separate covers?
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

| **Scenario** | **Separate PI + D&O + Crime** | **Meridian IMI** |
|---|---|---|
| Employee commits fraud, investors sue for negligent supervision | Two policies respond. Allocation disputes between PI and D&O insurers. Two excesses. | One policy. One claims team. One excess. Clear cross-section application ([[ref:imi.pay.cross-section-application]]). |
| ASIC investigates your compliance. Is it PI or D&O? | Coverage depends on how the claim is characterised. Risk of gap. | [[ref:imi.section.pl]] covers regulatory proceedings regardless of characterisation. |
| Portfolio manager deliberately misallocates trades. Is it crime or PI? | Crime insurer says it's a PI claim (professional services). PI insurer says it's crime (dishonest act). | [[ref:imi.section.crime]] covers the theft. [[ref:imi.section.pl]] covers the investor's negligent supervision claim. Both respond. |
| AFCA complaint about advice quality. Who pays? | May fall between PI and D&O. | [[ref:imi.cover.afca-complaints]] explicitly covers AFCA complaints. |
