heading: Multiple Funds — Limit Sharing
legacy_number: '6.8'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Where the schedule (Item 7) lists more than one fund, all funds share the section limits and the overall aggregate stated in Item 4 of the schedule; a loss involving, or a claim by or on behalf of, one fund reduces the limit available to every other fund insured under this policy. Item 7 of the schedule may instead state a per-fund sub-limit, or a stated sharing rule, for one or more named funds, in which case that sub-limit or rule applies to that fund in place of unrestricted sharing.
