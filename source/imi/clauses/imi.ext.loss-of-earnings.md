heading: Loss of Earnings
legacy_number: '3.10'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

$1,000 per day for an insured person attending court, a tribunal, an AFCA hearing, or a regulatory proceeding at our request, in connection with a covered claim, subject to a maximum of $20,000 in total for any one claim.
