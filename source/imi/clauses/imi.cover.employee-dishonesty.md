You or a fund's direct financial loss caused by a dishonest act committed by an employee, first discovered during the policy period.

This covers:

(a) theft of money, securities, digital assets, cryptocurrency, or other fund assets;

(b) fraudulent transfers from your accounts or fund accounts;

(c) fraudulent alteration of records, valuations, or unit prices;

(d) deliberate misallocation of trades or investments; and

(e) embezzlement and misappropriation.
