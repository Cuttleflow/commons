heading: Third-Party Service Provider Dishonesty
legacy_number: '4.2'
since: 1.3.0

---

We will pay the loss of the company or a fund caused by a dishonest act committed by an employee of a third-party service provider while performing services for the company or the fund, first discovered during the policy period — provided the act was directed at the company or the fund.
