id: imi.cover.third-party-service-provider-dishonesty
type: cover
heading: Third-Party Service Provider Dishonesty
legacy_number: '4.2'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

You or a fund's direct financial loss caused by a dishonest act committed by an employee of a third-party service provider while performing services for you or the fund — provided the act was directed at you or the fund.
