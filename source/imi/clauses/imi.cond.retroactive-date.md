heading: Retroactive Date
since: 1.4.0

---

[[ref:imi.section.pl]] and [[ref:imi.section.do]] do not cover a claim arising from a wrongful act committed before the retroactive date shown in Item 3 of the schedule. This applies to every insuring clause and extension in those sections, including Side A, Side B, defence of regulatory proceedings, AFCA complaints and the fund assets extension, whether or not the clause repeats it.

If Item 3 shows no retroactive date, cover is not limited by a retroactive date. [[ref:imi.excl.prior-known-matters]] and [[ref:imi.excl.prior-and-pending-litigation]] then apply from the start of the policy period.

If a claim arises from a series of related wrongful acts (see the definition of Claim) and the first of them was committed before the retroactive date, all of them are treated as committed before that date.

[[ref:imi.section.crime]] is not subject to a retroactive date. [[ref:imi.excl.prior-discovery-crime]], [[ref:imi.cond.superseded-policies-crime]] and [[ref:imi.cond.extended-discovery-period-crime]] set the equivalent limits for Crime.
