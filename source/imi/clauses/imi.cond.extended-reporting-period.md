Automatic 90 days if this policy is not renewed (other than by replacement with another policy that gives equivalent cover) or is cancelled by you or by us, and the non-renewal or cancellation is not caused by non-payment of premium ([[ref:imi.section.pl]] and [[ref:imi.section.do]]). [[ref:imi.section.crime]] has its own extended discovery period under [[ref:imi.cond.extended-discovery-period-crime]]. Extended periods for [[ref:imi.section.pl]] and [[ref:imi.section.do]] beyond the automatic 90 days are available for purchase under Endorsement E08, for the durations shown in the schedule when that endorsement is operative.
