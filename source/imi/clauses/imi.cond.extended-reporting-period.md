Automatic 90 days if this policy is not renewed or is cancelled ([[ref:imi.section.pl]] and [[ref:imi.section.do]]). [[ref:imi.section.crime]] has its own 60-day post-discovery notification period under [[ref:imi.obl.notify-losses-crime]]. Extended periods for [[ref:imi.section.pl]] and [[ref:imi.section.do]] beyond the automatic 90 days are available for purchase under Endorsement E08, for the durations shown in the schedule when that endorsement is operative.
