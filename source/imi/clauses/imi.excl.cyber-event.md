id: imi.excl.cyber-event
type: exclusion
heading: Cyber Event
legacy_number: '5.12'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

First-party cyber losses (data restoration, business interruption, incident response). Third-party cyber liability is excluded except to the extent it arises from a breach of duty in your financial services activities (e.g., failure to protect client data in the course of providing financial services).
