heading: Fund assets
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

The money, securities, financial instruments, digital assets, cryptocurrency, and other assets held in or on behalf of a fund, including assets held by a custodian on behalf of the fund. See [[ref:imi.ext.digital-assets-and-cryptocurrency]] for the digital-asset position and Endorsement E13 for enhanced terms.
