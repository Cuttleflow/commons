---
id: imi.excl.prior-discovery-crime
type: exclusion
heading: Prior Discovery — Crime
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Any loss under [[ref:imi.section.crime]] that was discovered, or that a responsible person ought reasonably to have discovered, before the start of the policy period, or that was notified, or should have been notified, under any prior insurance policy.
