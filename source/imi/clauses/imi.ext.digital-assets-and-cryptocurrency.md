[[ref:imi.def.fund-assets]] includes digital assets and cryptocurrency. Loss of digital assets or cryptocurrency is covered under this Section on the same basis as other fund assets. Endorsement E13 (Cryptocurrency / Digital Asset Fund Management) provides enhanced terms — including custody, wallet-security, and valuation provisions — for funds with material digital-asset exposure; it does not remove or narrow the base-form cover in this clause.
