---
id: imi.def.financial-services-activities
type: definition
heading: Financial services activities
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Any of the following:

(a) the provision of financial product advice (general and personal) under your AFSL;

(b) dealing in financial products under your AFSL;

(c) the operation, management, or administration of a fund;

(d) acting as trustee or responsible entity of a fund;

(e) the provision of custodial or depository services;

(f) portfolio management and investment management;

(g) investor reporting, unit pricing, and fund accounting;

(h) investor communication, marketing, and distribution of financial products;

(i) compliance, risk management, and governance activities in connection with your AFSL; and

(j) any other activity authorised under your AFSL or required by law in connection with your role as investment manager, trustee, or responsible entity.
