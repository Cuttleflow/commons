heading: Prior Known Matters
legacy_number: '5.4'
since: 1.3.0
changed: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Claims arising from matters that the insured seeking cover knew, before the retroactive date (or, if no retroactive date is shown, before the start of the policy period), might lead to a claim. For the company or a fund, the knowledge is that of a member of the control group. Knowledge is assessed individually (see [[ref:imi.cond.severability-and-non-imputation]]).
