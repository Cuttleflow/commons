We will not avoid this policy, or refuse to pay, for Side A cover because of a misrepresentation or a failure to disclose, except where the insured person seeking cover personally made a fraudulent misrepresentation or fraudulently failed to disclose.

If a misrepresentation or failure to disclose by the company or by another insured person would otherwise let us reduce our liability under section 28(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), we waive that right as against each insured person who did not personally know of it or take part in it.
