Each insured person is treated as if they had a separate policy. Knowledge and conduct are not attributed between insured persons. This severability applies to every knowledge-based or conduct-based provision of this policy, including [[ref:imi.excl.dishonesty-and-fraud]], [[ref:imi.excl.personal-profit]], [[ref:imi.excl.prior-known-matters]], [[ref:imi.excl.prior-and-pending-litigation]], [[ref:imi.obl.duty-of-disclosure]], and [[ref:imi.cond.non-rescindable-side-a]].

**The company and funds:** What the company knew, or did, is what a member of the control group knew or did. The knowledge or conduct of any other insured person or employee is not attributed to the company. For a fund, knowledge and conduct are those of the members of the control group of the company that manages the fund.

[WP REVIEW: whether a fund's knowledge and conduct should be those of the control group of its manager, or the fund should be treated separately from the manager.]

**Non-imputation:** The company's knowledge is not attributed to an insured person. An insured person's knowledge is not attributed to the company unless that insured person is a member of the control group.
