For [[ref:imi.section.pl]] and [[ref:imi.section.do]]: compensatory damages and defence costs that an insured becomes legally liable to pay. For insured persons, loss also includes fines and penalties, but only as [[ref:imi.excl.fines-and-penalties]] and [[ref:imi.cond.corporations-act-199a-199b]] allow.

For [[ref:imi.section.crime]]: the direct financial loss that the company or a fund sustains directly as a result of an act covered by an insuring clause of [[ref:imi.section.crime]]. This is the only causation test applied in [[ref:imi.section.crime]]: where an insuring clause also describes loss as caused by, or resulting from, an act, that wording refers to this test and does not set an additional or different threshold. [[ref:imi.ext.payment-to-funds]] (Payment to Funds) states how we pay a fund's loss.

Loss does not include: taxes; the return of fees, commissions, or management charges to which you were not entitled (but see [[ref:imi.excl.fees-and-commissions]]); the return of improperly gained profits; amounts uninsurable at law; or indirect or consequential loss (for [[ref:imi.section.crime]]).
