heading: Extended Discovery Period — Crime
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

If this policy is not renewed, other than by replacement with another policy that gives equivalent crime cover, or is cancelled by you or by us, and the non-renewal or cancellation is not caused by non-payment of premium, the company has an automatic extended discovery period of 90 days after the end of the policy period to discover, and notify us of, a loss under [[ref:imi.section.crime]] caused by an act that occurred before the end of the policy period.

This period does not extend the policy period or reinstate the limit of liability. A loss notified in it remains subject to the limit, excess, and terms that applied at the end of the policy period. [[ref:imi.end.e08]] does not change it.
