heading: Fund Assets — Third-Party Service Provider Failure
legacy_number: '2.6'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

If a third-party service provider (such as a custodian, administrator, or prime broker) fails to perform its obligations under its contract with you or a fund, and a claim is made against you as a result, we will cover you as if you had performed the activity yourself — provided you exercised reasonable care in selecting, appointing, and monitoring the third-party service provider.

This extension does not cover the insolvency of a **third-party service provider** or the loss of **fund assets** held by a custodian — unless the loss results from the custodian's fraud or dishonesty (which may be covered under [[ref:imi.section.crime]]).
