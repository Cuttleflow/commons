heading: Fund Wind-Up Run-Off
legacy_number: '8.10'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

If a fund named in the schedule (or established during the policy period) winds up, terminates, or is transferred to another responsible entity or trustee during the policy period, cover under [[ref:imi.section.pl]], [[ref:imi.section.do]], and [[ref:imi.section.crime]] continues in respect of that fund, on this policy's terms and within its limits, for wrongful acts and dishonest acts occurring before the wind-up, termination, or transfer, until the earlier of: (a) the expiry of the policy period (including any Extended Reporting Period under [[ref:imi.cond.extended-reporting-period]] or discovery notification period under [[ref:imi.obl.notify-losses-crime]] that applies); or (b) the fund obtaining its own separate insurance covering the same period.

This clause does not extend the policy period or create a separate limit for the affected fund; [[ref:imi.pay.multiple-funds-limit-sharing]] (Multiple Funds — Limit Sharing) continues to apply.
