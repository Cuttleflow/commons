If a person acquires more than 50% of the company's voting rights, or the company's AFSL ceases, [[ref:imi.cond.change-of-control]] is extended for the run-off period shown in the schedule for this endorsement. During the run-off period we cover claims first made against an insured, and circumstances notified, but only for wrongful acts committed before the event.

The run-off period starts on the date of the event. The additional premium shown in the schedule is fully earned when the endorsement is attached. The limits of liability that applied at the date of the event apply and are not reinstated.

[[ref:imi.cond.fund-wind-up-run-off]] applies to a fund-level wind-up, termination or transfer without this endorsement.

Schedule variables: run-off period; additional premium.
