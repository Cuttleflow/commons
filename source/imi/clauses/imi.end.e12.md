If a claim is first made against an insured during the policy period by or on behalf of an employee, or a person who applied to be an employee, for an employment practices wrongful act committed on or after the retroactive date, we will pay the amount the insured becomes legally liable to pay as compensation and the insured's defence costs, up to the employment practices limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement.

An employment practices wrongful act is any actual or alleged unfair or wrongful dismissal, discrimination, harassment, bullying, or breach of an employment contract in the employment of a person.

[[ref:imi.excl.employers-liability]] does not apply to the extent of this endorsement. This endorsement does not cover: workers compensation benefits or premiums; wages, superannuation contributions, leave or other entitlements that are owed under a contract, an award or the law; the cost of complying with an order to employ or reinstate a person; fines or penalties that are not insurable at law; or any claim arising from industrial action. The employment practices limit forms part of, and is not in addition to, the overall aggregate.
