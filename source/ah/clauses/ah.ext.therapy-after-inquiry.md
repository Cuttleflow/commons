heading: Therapy and Counselling for You After an Inquiry
legacy_number: 2.2.14
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

If you attend an inquiry covered by [[ref:ah.ext.inquiry-costs]], we will reimburse the reasonable cost of psychological therapy or counselling services you obtain for yourself from a registered psychologist, psychiatrist, accredited mental health social worker or accredited counsellor in connection with the stress of that inquiry, up to the sub-limit shown in [[ref:ah.pay.sub-limits]]. No excess applies.
