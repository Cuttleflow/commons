heading: Cooperate With Us
legacy_number: '7.3'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

You must, at your own cost (except where this policy expressly pays, such as under [[ref:ah.ext.court-attendance]]): give us and our appointed lawyers, investigators and experts all information, documents and assistance we reasonably request; be truthful and frank in everything you tell us; attend interviews, conferences, mediations, hearings and trials as reasonably required; and do nothing that prejudices our position or our rights of recovery. Nothing in this obligation requires you to waive legal professional privilege otherwise than to us, or to breach any law, including privacy law.
