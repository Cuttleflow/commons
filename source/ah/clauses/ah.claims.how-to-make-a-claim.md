**Contact us.** Notify every claim, inquiry and circumstance in writing to the claims contact shown in the schedule, quoting your policy number. If a matter is urgent, for example you have been served with proceedings, received an immediate-action notice from a National Board, or discovered a data breach, telephone first, then confirm in writing.

**What to send us.** As soon as reasonably practicable, send us: every demand, claim form, originating process, complaint, notice or correspondence you have received (unanswered); your factual account of the relevant professional services, with dates; the relevant clinical records (subject to privacy law: we will guide you on lawful disclosure); the identity of any other practitioners, practices or insurers involved; and details of any other insurance or indemnity that may respond (see [[ref:ah.pay.other-insurance]] and [[ref:ah.obl.other-recoveries]]).

**What we will do.** We will: acknowledge your notification within 5 business days; tell you what further information we need; confirm or explain our position on cover as soon as we reasonably can, and meanwhile protect your position under [[ref:ah.ext.advance-claim-protection]]; appoint and pay lawyers experienced in health-practitioner matters where representation is needed; conduct the matter in consultation with you under [[ref:ah.cond.conduct-of-defence]]; and keep you informed at every material step. We will comply with the claims-handling standards of the General Insurance Code of Practice.

**What you must not do.** Do not: admit liability, settle, or offer payment without our written consent (remembering the protected steps in [[ref:ah.obl.no-admissions]], which you may always take); appoint your own lawyers at our cost without our written consent (except under [[ref:ah.ext.advance-claim-protection]] or where we have agreed separate representation under [[ref:ah.cond.conduct-of-defence]]); or destroy, alter or withhold any relevant record. Altering a clinical record after a complaint is both a policy breach and a serious disciplinary matter: if a record needs correction, make a dated supplementary note and tell us.
