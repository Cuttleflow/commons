---
id: ah.def.medical-practitioner
type: definition
heading: Medical Practitioner
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

A natural person registered in the medical profession under the National Law. Dentists, dental hygienists, dental therapists, oral health therapists, dental prosthetists, nurses, nurse practitioners, midwives, paramedics, pharmacists, and every other person registered under the National Law outside the medical profession are not medical practitioners for the purposes of this policy.
