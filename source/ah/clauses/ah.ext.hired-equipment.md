[[ref:ah.excl.property-in-your-care]] does not apply to your civil liability for property damage to plant, equipment or devices (other than motor vehicles, watercraft or aircraft) that you hire, borrow or lease for your practice, where the damage arises from the conduct of your practice. The most we will pay under this extension is the sub-limit shown in [[ref:ah.pay.sub-limits]].
