If a claim includes both matters covered by this policy and matters not covered, or is made against both persons insured under this policy and persons not insured, you and we will use our best endeavours to agree a fair and proper allocation of loss and defence costs between covered and uncovered matters and persons, taking into account the relative legal and financial exposures. If we cannot agree within 30 days, the allocation will be determined by Senior Counsel appointed under the mechanism in [[ref:ah.cond.senior-counsel]], whose determination is binding on you and us, and whose costs we will pay. Pending agreement or determination, we will advance defence costs on the allocation we reasonably consider fair.
