heading: Trauma Counselling Following Violence
legacy_number: 2.2.15
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

If, in the course of performing a Good Samaritan Act covered by [[ref:ah.ext.good-samaritan]], you are the victim of, or witness to, an act of physical violence, we will reimburse the reasonable cost of trauma counselling you obtain for yourself from a registered psychologist, psychiatrist, accredited mental health social worker or accredited counsellor, up to the sub-limit shown in [[ref:ah.pay.sub-limits]]. No excess applies.
