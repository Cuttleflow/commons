legacy_number: '8.10'

---

Cover applies to each insured person as if a separate policy had been issued to each of them. In deciding whether an exclusion applies, or whether an obligation has been breached: the conduct, state of mind and knowledge of one insured person is not imputed to any other insured person; and only the individual's own conduct, state of mind and knowledge count against them. [[ref:ah.cond.duty-of-disclosure]] states whose knowledge is relevant to disclosure. Nothing in this condition increases the limit of liability.
