Cover applies to each insured person as if a separate policy had been issued to each of them. In deciding whether an exclusion applies, or whether an obligation has been breached: the conduct, state of mind and knowledge of one insured person is not imputed to any other insured person; and only the individual's own conduct, state of mind and knowledge count against them. [[ref:ah.cond.duty-of-disclosure]] states whose knowledge is relevant to disclosure. For a practice entity named under Endorsement E03, the knowledge and conduct of the entity are only those of a member of its **control group**, and the knowledge or conduct of any other insured person is not imputed to the entity. Nothing in this condition increases the limit of liability.
