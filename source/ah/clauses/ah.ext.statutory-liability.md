We will pay:

- (a) your defence costs for a prosecution or civil penalty proceeding first brought against you and notified to us during the policy period under an Act of the Commonwealth or of a State or Territory, including WHS Law, arising from the conduct of your practice; and
- (b) to the extent permitted by law in the relevant jurisdiction, any statutory penalty imposed in that proceeding, other than a criminal fine,

provided the act, error or omission giving rise to the proceeding was not intentional, deliberate or committed with reckless disregard for the relevant law. The most we will pay under this extension is the sub-limit shown in [[ref:ah.pay.sub-limits]].

We do not pay any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured. In particular, we do not pay a penalty imposed under work health and safety law in a State or Territory whose law prohibits insurance of it (including New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland), and we do not pay a criminal fine.
