We will pay:

- (a) your defence costs for a prosecution or civil penalty proceeding first brought against you and notified to us during the policy period under an Act of the Commonwealth or of a State or Territory, including WHS Law, arising from the conduct of your practice; and
- (b) to the extent permitted by law in the relevant jurisdiction, any statutory penalty imposed in that proceeding,

provided the act, error or omission giving rise to the proceeding was not intentional, deliberate or committed with reckless disregard for the relevant law. The most we will pay under this extension is the sub-limit shown in [[ref:ah.pay.sub-limits]]. Nothing in this extension covers a WHS or other monetary penalty where the law of the relevant jurisdiction prohibits insurance or indemnity for that penalty; in every case we pay penalties only to the extent the law permits.
