legacy_number: '4.1'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

If, during the policy period, you become aware of a circumstance, you may (and, under [[ref:ah.obl.notify-us]], you must) give us written notice of it as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any event before the policy period ends. Your notice should include the identity of the potential claimant (if known), the professional services and dates involved, a description of what happened, and the reasons you consider a claim might result.
