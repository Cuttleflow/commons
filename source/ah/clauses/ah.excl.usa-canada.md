made, brought, continued or determined in the United States of America or Canada (or their territories or protectorates), or arising from professional services provided in, or products supplied in or to, the United States of America or Canada, or in respect of the enforcement of any judgment, order or award obtained there. This exclusion is absolute and applies to both sections and every extension.
