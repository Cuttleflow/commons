For the avoidance of doubt, professional services include services you provide by telehealth, videoconference, telephone, secure messaging, remote monitoring or other remote means, to patients or clients located anywhere in Australia, provided you hold the registration or accreditation required where the patient or client is located and comply with your National Board's or professional association's telehealth guidelines. Cover for telehealth to patients located outside Australia is available under Endorsement E09. [[ref:ah.excl.usa-canada]] always applies.
