Every sub-limit stated in an extension or the schedule is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability, and is the most we will pay under that extension in the aggregate for the policy period, regardless of the number of claims, inquiries or insured persons. Unless the schedule states a different amount, the sub-limits are:
