heading: Advance Claim Protection
legacy_number: 2.2.23
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

If you notify us of a claim or circumstance and we have not yet confirmed whether this policy covers it, we will appoint and pay a lawyer from our panel to protect your position, including preserving evidence, meeting court and regulatory deadlines, and advising you on immediate steps, until we confirm or decline cover. If we later decline cover, we will not seek to recover from you the panel-lawyer costs incurred before the date we notified you of our decision, unless you obtained the benefit of this extension through fraud.
