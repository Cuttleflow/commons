arising from your conduct of, or participation as an investigator in, a clinical trial, or from any procedure, therapy or treatment that is experimental, or that has not been approved (where approval is required) by the Therapeutic Goods Administration or a properly constituted human research ethics committee, unless Endorsement E02 applies. This exclusion does not apply to the routine off-label use of an approved therapy where that use is accepted practice in your profession and within your scope of practice.
