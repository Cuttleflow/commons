---
id: ah.def.defence-costs
type: definition
heading: Defence Costs
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

The reasonable legal costs, disbursements and associated expenses (including the reasonable fees of expert witnesses, investigators and adjusters) incurred with our prior written consent, which we will not unreasonably withhold or delay, in the investigation, defence, settlement or appeal of a claim, or in representation at an inquiry where this policy covers that inquiry.

Defence costs do not include your own time, wages, salary or lost income (except as [[ref:ah.ext.court-attendance]] provides), or our own internal costs. We will advance defence costs as they are incurred and before final resolution of the claim, subject to [[ref:ah.section.payment]] and to repayment under [[ref:ah.cond.repayment]] if it is finally established that you were not entitled to cover.
