legacy_number: '8.11'
since: 1.3.0

If you are entitled to an input tax credit for the premium, you must tell us the extent of that entitlement at or before the time you make a claim. Where we pay an amount under this policy, we may reduce the payment by the amount of any input tax credit or decreasing adjustment to which you are, or would be, entitled in respect of the acquisition to which the payment relates. Amounts we pay are otherwise inclusive of GST, up to the limit of liability.
