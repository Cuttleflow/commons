We are entitled to take over and conduct, in your name, the investigation, defence and settlement of any claim, and your representation at any inquiry we cover, and to appoint lawyers of our choosing for those purposes. We will: keep you informed of all material developments; consult you on strategy and on any proposed settlement; not settle any claim in a way that includes an admission of professional misconduct by you without your consent; and instruct the appointed lawyers that, in any matter bearing on your registration, reputation or clinical record, their professional duty is owed to you.

If a conflict of interest arises between you and us, or between insured persons, we will tell you promptly, and we will pay for separate representation for you by an independent lawyer agreed between us or, failing agreement, nominated under [[ref:ah.cond.senior-counsel]].
