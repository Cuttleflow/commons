We will pay your civil liability for any claim first made against you and notified to us during the policy period arising from: your teaching, training, lecturing, mentoring or examining in your professional discipline; material you author, publish or present in your professional discipline; or reports, opinions or evidence you provide as an expert in your professional discipline. This extension does not cover a guarantee of educational or commercial outcomes, or liability of an educational institution itself.
