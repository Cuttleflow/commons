You may cancel this policy at any time by written notice to us; we will refund the unearned premium on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges, unless a claim or circumstance has been notified during the policy period. We may cancel this policy only on a ground, and by the notice, permitted by ss.59 to 63 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), for example for non-payment of premium, a fraudulent claim, or breach of the duty of utmost good faith. Cancellation never affects cover for claims and circumstances already notified.
