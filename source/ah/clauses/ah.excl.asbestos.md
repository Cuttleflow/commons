directly or indirectly caused by or arising from the existence, mining, handling, processing, manufacture, sale, distribution, storage, use, removal of, or exposure to, asbestos or materials containing asbestos. This exclusion does not apply to a claim arising from your assessment, diagnosis, treatment or care of a person suffering from, or believed to be suffering from, an asbestos-related condition.
