---
id: ah.def.products
type: definition
heading: Products
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Any goods, substances, appliances, devices, aids, orthoses, prostheses, medicines or other tangible property (including their containers, labelling and instructions) sold, supplied, dispensed, fitted, repaired, serviced or installed by you or on your behalf in connection with your professional services, after they have ceased to be in your physical possession or under your control.
