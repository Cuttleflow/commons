heading: Students and Work-experience Placements
legacy_number: 2.2.17
since: 1.3.0

We will pay the civil liability of any student or person on a work-experience or vocational placement with your practice, for any claim first made and notified to us during the policy period arising from professional services performed under your direct supervision, as though they were you. This extension does not cover services a student performs outside your supervision or outside your practice.
