---
id: ah.excl.prior-circumstances
type: exclusion
heading: Prior Known Circumstances and Prior Notifications
legacy_number: '5.13'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

arising from: any circumstance of which you were aware before the policy period began (except as provided by [[ref:ah.ext.continuous-cover]]); any claim first made, or inquiry first commenced, before the policy period began; anything notified, or that ought to have been notified, under any policy of insurance in force before this policy began; or any act, error, omission or conduct occurring before the retroactive date.
