To the extent the law allows it to be insured, we will pay civil penalties imposed on you under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) or a State or Territory health records law, arising from a breach of privacy or data protection obligations first notified to us during the policy period, provided the breach was not intentional, reckless (except as covered by [[ref:ah.ext.serious-invasion-of-privacy]]), or the result of your deliberate disregard of a known obligation. The most we will pay under this extension is the sub-limit shown in [[ref:ah.pay.sub-limits]]. Where the same breach triggers both this extension and [[ref:ah.ext.data-breach-costs]], the two share one sub-limit.

We do not pay any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured, and we do not pay a criminal fine.
