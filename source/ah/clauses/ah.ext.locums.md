We will pay:

- (a) the civil liability of a locum engaged by your practice, for any claim first made and notified to us during the policy period arising from professional services provided for and on behalf of your practice during the engagement, as though the locum were you; and
- (b) your civil liability for any claim first made and notified to us during the policy period arising from professional services you provide while working as a locum for another practitioner or practice, within the scope of your professional services.

Paragraph (a) does not cover a locum for services provided otherwise than for your practice, and does not relieve the locum's own insurer of any obligation.
