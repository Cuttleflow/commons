This endorsement applies where the insured is a dental practitioner (a dentist, dental specialist, dental hygienist, dental therapist, oral health therapist or dental prosthetist) registered with the Dental Board of Australia, or a practice whose professional services described in the schedule are wholly or predominantly dental or oral health services.

**Dental practitioners are covered.** A dental practitioner registered with the Dental Board of Australia is an allied health practitioner and is not a medical practitioner for the purposes of this policy. [[ref:ah.excl.medical-practitioners]] does not apply to, and does not reduce cover for, any dental practitioner.

**Covered dental procedures.** Professional services include, without limitation, the following when performed within the insured's registration division, specialty and scope of practice under the Dental Board of Australia's scope of practice registration standard:

- (a) oral surgery, including simple and surgical tooth extractions;
- (b) the placement and restoration of dental implants;
- (c) orthodontics;
- (d) endodontics;
- (e) periodontics;
- (f) prosthodontics, including the design, fitting and adjustment of dentures and other dental prostheses (which are also products for the purposes of [[ref:ah.ext.products-liability]]); and
- (g) oral pathology, including the examination, diagnosis and referral of oral mucosal disease.

**Dental sedation and general anaesthesia.** [[ref:ah.cover.professional-indemnity]] extends to your civil liability arising from the administration of conscious sedation (including inhalational, oral and intravenous sedation) in connection with your professional services. At the time of the treatment:

- (a) the sedation must be administered in compliance with the Dental Board of Australia's registration standard and guidelines for conscious sedation (and, where applicable, an endorsement for conscious sedation on the insured's registration);
- (b) the premises must comply with any applicable State or Territory hospital, day-procedure or health-facility licensing requirements for the level of sedation administered; and
- (c) the person administering the sedation must hold current sedation-specific training and continuing professional development certification.

If (a) to (c) are not met, we may reduce the amount we pay by the amount that fairly represents the prejudice we suffer, consistent with s.54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Civil liability arising from dental treatment performed under general anaesthesia is covered only where the general anaesthesia was administered in an accredited day surgery or hospital facility by a person lawfully authorised to administer it; any claim arising from general anaesthesia administered elsewhere is excluded.

**Additional obligations.** These are added to [[ref:ah.section.obligations]] and operate on the same basis:

- (a) Radiation safety. Where you take, or cause to be taken, dental radiographs (including intraoral radiography, orthopantomography and cone-beam computed tomography), you must hold and comply with the radiation licence, registration or accreditation required by the radiation safety legislation of the relevant State or Territory, and provide evidence of it to us on request.
- (b) Infection control. You must comply with the Dental Board of Australia's guidelines on infection control and the current Australian Dental Association infection control guidelines applicable to your practice.

**Additional exclusions.** We will not cover any claim, inquiry, loss, defence costs or other amounts arising from:

- (a) Cosmetic facial injectables: the administration of botulinum toxin, dermal fillers or other cosmetic facial injectables for purely aesthetic purposes (that is, otherwise than for the treatment of temporomandibular disorders, bruxism, hyperhidrosis, dental or facial pain, or another clinical indication within the insured's scope of practice), unless the insured has declared that activity to us and Endorsement E07 applies. Where a facial injectable is administered for a clinical indication, [[ref:ah.excl.prescription-medicines]] continues to apply to the prescribing and supply of the substance.
- (b) Specialist oral and maxillofacial surgery: any surgical procedure that requires specialist registration as an oral and maxillofacial surgeon, unless the insured holds that specialist registration and it is noted in the schedule. Surgical procedures within the general dental scope of practice, including surgical extractions, remain covered.

Schedule variables: specialist registration (where held).
