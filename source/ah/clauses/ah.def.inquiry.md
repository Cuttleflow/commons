heading: Inquiry
since: 1.3.0
changed: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Any investigation, examination, audit, performance assessment, health assessment, immediate-action proceeding, panel hearing, disciplinary hearing, tribunal proceeding, inquest or other formal inquiry conducted by an inquiring body into your conduct, your professional services or your practice, at which you are legally required, or requested and we agree it is in your and our interests, to attend, respond, give evidence or produce documents. An inquiry is first made against you when you first receive written notice of it. Inquiry expressly includes performance assessments, immediate-action proceedings and tribunal hearings under Parts 8 and 8A of the National Law.
