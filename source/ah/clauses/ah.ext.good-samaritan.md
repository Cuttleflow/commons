heading: Good Samaritan Acts
legacy_number: 2.2.4
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

We will pay your civil liability for any claim first made against you and notified to us during the policy period arising from a Good Samaritan Act performed by you anywhere in the world. This extension operates in addition to any protection you have under civil liability legislation for good faith assistance at emergencies. [[ref:ah.excl.usa-canada]] continues to apply to claims made or determined in the United States of America or Canada.
