id: ah.def.inquiring-body
type: definition
heading: Inquiring Body
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Any of the following:

- (a) the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (Ahpra);
- (b) any National Board with jurisdiction over your profession;
- (c) the Health Ombudsman established under the Health Ombudsman Act 2013 (Qld);
- (d) the Health Care Complaints Commission established under the Health Care Complaints Act 1993 (NSW);
- (e) any other state or territory healthcare complaints entity, health services commissioner or equivalent statutory body;
- (f) a coroner or coronial court;
- (g) a Royal Commission, board of inquiry or parliamentary inquiry;
- (h) the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission, and the Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission, including in the exercise of its complaints, compliance, monitoring, audit, enforcement and Serious Incident Response Scheme (SIRS) functions, together with any authority investigating a reportable incident under the Serious Incident Response Scheme under the Aged Care Act or the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (Cth);
- (i) Services Australia, the Department of Health (or successor department), or the Chief Executive Medicare, in connection with Medicare, the Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme or the Child Dental Benefits Schedule;
- (j) a tribunal (including a civil and administrative tribunal of a State or Territory) exercising jurisdiction over your registration, accreditation or professional conduct;
- (k) your professional association, when exercising formal disciplinary or complaints-handling powers over your membership or accreditation; and
- (l) any other statutory regulator, licensing authority or professional body with jurisdiction over you or your professional services, including the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner and a work health and safety regulator.
