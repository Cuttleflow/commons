since: 1.3.0

  questions:

  - when: qs.ah.practice.event_services == yes

---

Professional services are extended to include first aid, event health services and sideline coverage the insured provides at the sporting, community or mass-gathering events described in the schedule, whether paid or voluntary, within the insured's scope of practice and any event-medical guidelines of the insured's National Board or professional association.

Schedule variables: events.
