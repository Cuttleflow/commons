---
id: ah.def.excess
type: definition
heading: Excess
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

The amount shown in the schedule that you must bear for each claim (or each series of claims treated as one claim under [[ref:ah.cond.aggregation]]) before we pay. Unless the schedule states otherwise, the excess applies inclusive of defence costs and applies once per claim, not once per claimant.
