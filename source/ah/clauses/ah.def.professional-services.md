heading: Professional Services
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

The allied health services described in the schedule, provided by you or on your behalf within the scope of practice of the registration category, endorsements and notations you hold under the National Law or, where paragraph (b) of the definition of allied health practitioner applies, within the scope of practice recognised by your professional association for your membership or accreditation category. Professional services include:

- (a) clinical assessment, diagnosis within your scope of practice, treatment, therapy, rehabilitation, care planning and case management;
- (b) the provision of advice, reports, referrals and certificates in your professional capacity;
- (c) first aid and emergency care provided in the course of your practice;
- (d) supervision of employees, students and volunteers in the delivery of the services described above; and
- (e) teaching, training, mentoring, presenting and publishing in your professional discipline, and acting as an expert witness in that discipline, as further described in [[ref:ah.ext.teaching-and-expert-reports]].
