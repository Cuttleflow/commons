We will pay your civil liability for any claim first made against you and notified to us during the policy period for bodily injury or property damage occurring after the retroactive date and caused by products, that is goods sold, supplied, dispensed, fitted, repaired, serviced or installed by you or on your behalf in connection with your professional services. Examples include orthoses, prostheses, mobility and daily-living aids, compression garments, mouthguards, dentures and dental appliances, spectacles and contact lenses, hearing devices, dressings, and medicines lawfully dispensed or supplied within your scope of practice.

This extension does not cover: the cost of repairing, replacing, recalling or refunding the products themselves; liability arising from goods you sell or supply otherwise than in connection with your professional services (see [[ref:ah.excl.goods-outside-services]]); or liability arising from the manufacture of goods, other than the fitting, adjustment, compounding, assembly or customisation of goods as a normal part of your professional services.
