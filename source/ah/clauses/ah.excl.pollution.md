directly or indirectly caused by or arising from the discharge, dispersal, release, seepage, migration or escape of pollutants or contaminants into or upon land, the atmosphere or any watercourse or body of water, or the cost of testing for, monitoring, removing, containing, treating, detoxifying, neutralising or cleaning up pollutants or contaminants. Under [[ref:ah.section.pl|heading]] only, this exclusion does not apply to bodily injury or property damage caused by a sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected discharge, dispersal, release or escape that takes place in its entirety at a specific time and place in the conduct of your practice.
