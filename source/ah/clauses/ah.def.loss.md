id: ah.def.loss
type: definition
heading: Loss
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

The amounts a court, tribunal or arbitrator orders you to pay, or which are payable under a settlement we have consented to in writing, as compensation or damages in respect of a claim, including claimant's costs and interest awarded against you.

Loss does not include: fines or penalties (except to the extent expressly covered by [[ref:ah.ext.statutory-liability]] or [[ref:ah.ext.data-breach-penalties]] and permitted by law); punitive, exemplary or aggravated damages; taxes; the cost of complying with an injunction or other non-monetary relief; amounts uninsurable under the law governing this policy; or the refund, reduction or set-off of your professional fees.
