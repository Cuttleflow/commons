[[ref:ah.excl.property-in-your-care]] does not apply to your civil liability for property damage to premises (including their landlord's fixtures and fittings) that you occupy as a tenant or licensee for your practice, where the damage arises from the conduct of your practice. This extension does not cover liability you assume under a lease or licence beyond the liability you would have at law, or damage that the lease requires the landlord to insure.
