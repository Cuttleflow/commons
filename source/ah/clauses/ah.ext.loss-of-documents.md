If, during the policy period, you first discover that documents belonging to you, or for which you are legally responsible, have been destroyed, damaged, lost or mislaid in the course of your practice and cannot be found after diligent search, we will pay the reasonable costs of replacing or restoring them (including re-creating electronic records from back-ups and source material), and your civil liability to third parties arising directly from the loss.

You must:

- (a) notify us as soon as practicable, and in any event within 30 days after discovery;
- (b) for electronic documents, at the time of loss have maintained back-up copies made no less frequently than daily and stored separately from the primary system; and
- (c) support the amount claimed by bills and accounts approved by a competent person we nominate with your agreement.

If you do not comply with obligation (a) or (b), we may reduce the amount we pay by the amount that fairly represents the prejudice we suffer, in accordance with s.54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).

The most we will pay under this extension is the sub-limit shown in [[ref:ah.pay.sub-limits]]. No excess applies. This extension does not cover loss caused by wear, tear, vermin, gradual deterioration, or the failure of electronic media where no back-up existed.
