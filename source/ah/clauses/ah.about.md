This policy is a contract of insurance between you, the allied health practitioner named in the schedule, and us, the insurer named in the schedule. In return for your payment of the premium, we agree to insure you on the terms set out in this document, the schedule and any endorsements we issue to you. Read the three documents together: they form one contract.

The policy has two operative sections, and both are written on a claims-made and notified basis:

- [[ref:ah.section.pi|heading]] covers your civil liability for malpractice and for certain related civil liabilities arising from your professional services, together with extensions that respond to the realities of allied health practice in Australia: regulatory inquiries, Ahpra and National Board proceedings, coronial inquests, Medicare and NDIS audits, data breaches and more.
- [[ref:ah.section.pl|heading]] covers your civil liability for bodily injury and property damage arising from the conduct of your practice, for example a client who slips in your waiting room. Products liability, your civil liability arising from goods you supply in connection with your professional services, is provided by [[ref:ah.ext.products-liability]], so one set of terms and one policy period governs everything.

Because the whole policy is claims-made, it covers only claims first made against you and notified to us during the policy period. When the policy period ends, so does your right to notify, subject to s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) and the continuous cover, run-off and extended reporting provisions of this policy. Before you enter into this contract, your insurer or its distributor must give you written notice of the effect of s.40(3), as s.40(1) of that Act requires. [[ref:ah.section.notification]] explains how notifying a circumstance protects you.

This policy is designed to satisfy the professional indemnity insurance registration standards issued by the National Boards under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law, including the requirements for retroactive cover, cover for the full scope of your practice, automatic reinstatement, and run-off cover when you cease practice. It is written for individual allied health practitioners: practitioners registered under any National Board other than the Medical Board of Australia, and practitioners in self-regulated disciplines (such as counsellors, social workers, dietitians and exercise physiologists) who hold the accreditation or membership their recognised professional association requires. It does not cover medical practitioners for their own clinical practice, because their cover is separately mandated under the Commonwealth medical indemnity framework. Privately practising midwives participating in the Commonwealth contribution scheme for midwife professional indemnity are not insured under this product. This product is written to be an eligible insurance contract for the purposes of the Allied Health High Cost Claims Indemnity Scheme established under the Medical Indemnity Act 2002 (Cth).

This wording is written for practitioners who buy it for use in a business. The insurer or distributor that issues it is responsible for deciding whether any purchaser is a retail client under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) and, if so, for issuing a Product Disclosure Statement and Target Market Determination. We have written this policy in plain English. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:ah.section.definitions]]. Where this policy refers to legislation, the reference includes that legislation as amended, replaced or re-enacted from time to time, together with its regulations and any corresponding law of a State or Territory. If anything in this document is unclear, contact us before you rely on it.
