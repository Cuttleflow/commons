arising from a warranty, guarantee, indemnity or contractual term you have given, to the extent it imposes on you a liability greater than the liability you would have at law in the absence of that term. This exclusion does not apply merely because you have agreed in a contract that the proportionate liability legislation of a State or Territory is excluded or modified, where that agreement is itself permitted by the relevant legislation; in that case we cover the liability you would have had if liability had been apportioned without regard to the proportionate liability legislation.
