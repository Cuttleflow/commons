legacy_number: '8.6'
since: 1.3.0

---

We will not require you to contest any claim, or appeal any judgment, unless Senior Counsel (agreed between you and us or, failing agreement within 21 days, nominated by the President of the Law Society or Bar Association of the State or Territory whose law governs this policy) advises that the claim or appeal should be contested, taking into account the prospects of success, the likely costs, and the interests of both you and us, including your reputational and registration interests. The costs of obtaining that advice are defence costs.
