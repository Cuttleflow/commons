We collect personal information, including health information where necessary to assess and manage claims, in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). We collect it to assess your application, issue and administer your policy, handle claims, and comply with law. We may disclose it to our reinsurers, service providers, legal and claims professionals, and regulators, some of whom may be overseas. You can ask for access to or correction of your personal information, or make a privacy complaint, using the contact details in the schedule.
