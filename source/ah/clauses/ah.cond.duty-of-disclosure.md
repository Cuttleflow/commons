Before you entered into this policy, renewed, varied, extended or reinstated it, you had a duty under s.21 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) to disclose to us every matter you knew, or a reasonable person in the circumstances could be expected to know, to be relevant to our decision whether to accept the risk and on what terms. You did not need to tell us about anything that diminishes the risk, is of common knowledge, we know or should know as an insurer, or we told you we did not need to know.

If you failed to comply with the duty, our remedies are those set out in Division 3 of Part IV of the Act: we may reduce our liability to the amount that would place us in the position we would have been in had the duty been complied with and, if the non-disclosure was fraudulent, we may avoid the policy from its beginning. For the duty of disclosure, only facts known to the person named in the schedule as the insured (or to the person completing the application) are relevant.
