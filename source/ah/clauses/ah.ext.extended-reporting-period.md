If we cancel or refuse to renew this policy for any reason other than your non-payment of premium or fraud, or you do not renew it, you may purchase an extended reporting period of 12 months, beginning on the day the policy period ends, during which you may notify us of claims first made against you in that 12-month period arising from professional services provided before the end of the policy period and after the retroactive date. The premium for the extended reporting period is 100% of the last annual premium, is payable within 30 days after the policy period ends, and is not refundable. The extended reporting period does not increase the limit of liability and is not available if you have obtained, or are required to and do obtain, replacement insurance covering the same exposure.
