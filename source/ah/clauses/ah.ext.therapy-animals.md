heading: Therapy Animals
legacy_number: 2.2.13
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

We will pay your civil liability for any claim first made against you and notified to us during the policy period for physical injury to any person caused by a therapy animal while you are providing professional services. This extension does not cover injury caused by an animal that is not a therapy animal, injury occurring outside the provision of professional services, or liability arising from the breeding, sale or boarding of animals.
