We will pay civil liability for any claim first made against you and notified to us during the policy period under the statutory cause of action for serious invasion of privacy in Schedule 2 to the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) (as inserted by the Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024 (Cth)), arising in connection with your professional services, as follows:

- (a) we will cover the person or entity named in the schedule as the insured, including for vicarious liability arising from the conduct of any other insured person;
- (b) we will cover any other insured person, but only where the invasion is alleged to have been reckless rather than intentional; and
- (c) we do not cover any person who is found by a court or tribunal, or who admits, to have intentionally committed a serious invasion of privacy, and if we have advanced defence costs to that person, they must repay them.

The most we will pay under this extension is the sub-limit shown in [[ref:ah.pay.sub-limits]]. Exemplary damages remain excluded under [[ref:ah.excl.fines-and-penalties]].
