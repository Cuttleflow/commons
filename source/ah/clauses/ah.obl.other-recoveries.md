heading: Tell Us About Other Recoveries
legacy_number: '7.6'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

You must not seek, accept or agree to indemnity, contribution or compensation for a claim or amount covered by this policy from any other insurer, indemnifier, employer or scheme without disclosing that fact to us. If you receive any such amount, you must account for it to us to the extent we have paid, or would otherwise pay, the same amount under this policy.
