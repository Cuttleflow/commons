This endorsement applies where the insured is a psychologist (including a provisionally registered psychologist) registered with the Psychology Board of Australia, or a counsellor or psychotherapist who is a full member of the Australian Counselling Association (ACA) or the Psychotherapy and Counselling Federation of Australia (PACFA) and holds a current practising certificate or equivalent credential issued by that association, or a practice whose professional services described in the schedule are wholly or predominantly psychology, counselling or psychotherapy services.

**Counsellors.** For the purposes of this endorsement, an allied health practitioner includes a person who, at the time of the relevant act, error or omission, holds either:

- (a) registration as a psychologist with the Psychology Board of Australia (including provisional registration, where the person practises under supervision arrangements that comply with the Board's requirements); or
- (b) full membership of the Australian Counselling Association or the Psychotherapy and Counselling Federation of Australia, as the person's professional association, together with the current practising certificate or equivalent credential that association requires for practising members.

**Increased sexual misconduct defence costs sub-limit.** The sexual misconduct defence costs sub-limit under [[ref:ah.excl.sexual-misconduct]] is increased from $500,000 to $750,000 in the aggregate for the policy period. All other terms of that exclusion, including the repayment obligation where the conduct is admitted or established and the protection of innocent insured persons, continue to apply.

**False and recovered memory claims.** [[ref:ah.cover.professional-indemnity]] extends to your civil liability for any claim first made against you and notified to us during the policy period alleging that a therapeutic process you conducted, including hypnotherapy, regression techniques or other memory-related therapeutic techniques, caused the claimant or a patient to form false memories, including false memories of abuse. The most we will pay for these claims is $500,000 in the aggregate for the policy period, as part of and not in addition to the limit of liability.

**Third-party psychological harm.** [[ref:ah.cover.professional-indemnity]] extends to your civil liability for any claim first made against you and notified to us during the policy period by a person who was not your patient or client, for psychological harm suffered by that person as a result of your negligent advice to your patient or client in the course of your professional services. The most we will pay for these claims is $250,000 for any one claim and $500,000 in the aggregate for the policy period, as part of and not in addition to the limit of liability. This does not extend cover to claims otherwise excluded, including under [[ref:ah.excl.related-parties]].

**Mandatory child protection reporting.** Making a report or notification you are required or permitted by law to make under child protection legislation, including the Children and Young Persons (Care and Protection) Act 1998 (NSW), the Children, Youth and Families Act 2005 (Vic), the Child Protection Act 1999 (Qld) and the corresponding legislation of every other State and Territory, or under any mandatory reporting scheme applying to your profession, is added to the protected steps in [[ref:ah.obl.no-admissions]]. It is never treated by us as an admission of liability and never of itself prejudices your cover.

**Group therapy and telepsychology.** Professional services include group therapy, group counselling and group programs you facilitate. Claims by more than one participant arising from the same act, error, omission or event are one claim under [[ref:ah.cond.aggregation]]. Consistent with [[ref:ah.ext.telehealth]], professional services include telepsychology and remote counselling delivered in compliance with the Australian Psychological Society's ethical guidelines for psychological services via telehealth or, for a counsellor, the telehealth guidance of their professional association.

**Scope limit.** A provisionally registered psychologist is an insured person only for professional services provided under supervision arrangements that comply with the Psychology Board of Australia's provisional registration requirements. This is a limit on who is covered, not an obligation, and [[ref:ah.section.obligations]] does not apply to it.

**Additional obligations.** These are added to [[ref:ah.section.obligations]] and operate on the same basis:

- (a) A counsellor must maintain the membership, practising certificate, supervision and continuing professional development requirements of the ACA or PACFA throughout the policy period.
- (b) You must keep contemporaneous clinical records of all consultations, including telehealth consultations, in accordance with your Board's or professional association's record-keeping standards.

**Additional exclusions.** We will not cover any claim, inquiry, loss, defence costs or other amounts arising from:

- (a) the prescription of a medicine by you (psychologists and counsellors hold no prescribing authority); this does not apply to advice about, or care coordination involving, a medicine lawfully prescribed by another authorised prescriber; or
- (b) the provision of change or suppression (conversion) practices prohibited under the legislation of any State or Territory.

Schedule variables: none.
