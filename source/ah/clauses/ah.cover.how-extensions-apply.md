Each extension below is part of [[ref:ah.section.pi|heading]]. Unless an extension says otherwise: each extension is subject to all the terms, conditions and exclusions of this policy; any sub-limit stated is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability and is the most we will pay under that extension in the aggregate for the policy period; and the excess applies. Where an extension covers costs rather than civil liability, no excess applies unless the schedule states one.
