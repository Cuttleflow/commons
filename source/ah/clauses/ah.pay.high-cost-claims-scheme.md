This policy is written to be an eligible insurance contract for the purposes of the Allied Health High Cost Claims Indemnity Scheme under Part 2, Division 2C of the Medical Indemnity Act 2002 (Cth), under which the Commonwealth reimburses eligible insurers 50% of the amount by which an eligible claim against an eligible privately practising allied health professional exceeds the scheme threshold. Any reimbursement we receive under that scheme is for our account, does not reduce your cover, does not increase the limit of liability, and imposes no obligation on you other than reasonable cooperation with any information requirements of the scheme.
