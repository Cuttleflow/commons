made against you by, or on behalf of: any insured person; your parent, subsidiary or related entity; or any person or entity in which you (or your spouse, domestic partner or immediate family member) hold, directly or indirectly, an interest of 15% or more, or which holds such an interest in your practice. This exclusion does not apply to a claim brought by such a person or entity for their own bodily injury, or to a claim that originates from, and is genuinely pressed on behalf of, an independent third party without solicitation, collusion or assistance by an insured person.
