If, during the policy period, you permanently retire from practice, permanently cease to provide professional services, die, or become permanently disabled such that you cannot practise, this policy is automatically extended, at no additional premium, to cover claims first made against you and notified to us during the period of 72 months after the date you ceased practice, provided the claim arises from professional services provided before that date and after the retroactive date. This run-off cover:

- (a) ends immediately if you resume practice, or if you obtain (or are required by a registration standard to obtain and do obtain) other insurance covering the same run-off exposure;
- (b) does not increase the limit of liability, which continues to apply as a single aggregate amount across the policy period and the run-off period combined;
- (c) extends to your estate, heirs, legal representatives and personal insolvency administrators as described in [[ref:ah.ext.estates-and-spouses]]; and
- (d) is provided to assist you to meet the run-off requirements of the professional indemnity insurance registration standards under the National Law. If your National Board's registration standard requires a longer run-off period, contact us: extended run-off is available under Endorsement E06.
