---
id: ah.def.professional-association
type: definition
heading: Professional Association
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

For a health discipline in which registration under the National Law is not required or not available, the professional association recognised in Australia as the certifying or self-regulating body for that discipline, including, without limitation, Dietitians Australia (for Accredited Practising Dietitians), Exercise and Sports Science Australia (for Accredited Exercise Physiologists and related accreditations), the Australian Association of Social Workers, the Psychotherapy and Counselling Federation of Australia, the Australian Counselling Association, Speech Pathology Australia, Audiology Australia, and the Australian Sonographer Accreditation Registry, or any other association named in the schedule.
