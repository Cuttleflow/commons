We will pay the reasonable legal costs and expenses you incur, with our prior written consent, in obtaining advice about, making, or responding to a mandatory notification under the mandatory notification provisions of the National Law (including a notification you are legally required to make about another registered health practitioner, and a notification made about you), where the date you first become aware of the facts that require you to make the notification, or first receive notice of a notification made about you, falls in the policy period and you notify us during the policy period. That date is treated as the date an inquiry is first made against you, for the purposes of [[ref:ah.def.inquiry]]. [WP REVIEW: add the National Law section references for mandatory notifications; the previous draft cited ss.140 to 143, which have not been verified.] Costs under this extension form part of the sub-limit for [[ref:ah.ext.inquiry-costs]]. Nothing in this policy prevents, delays or penalises your compliance with a mandatory notification obligation under the National Law: see [[ref:ah.obl.no-admissions]].
