heading: Mandatory Reporting Costs
legacy_number: 2.2.6
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

We will pay the reasonable legal costs and expenses you incur, with our prior written consent, in obtaining advice about, making, or responding to a mandatory notification under ss.140 to 143 of the National Law (including a notification you are legally required to make about another registered health practitioner, and a notification made about you), where the obligation or notification first arises and is notified to us during the policy period. Costs under this extension form part of the sub-limit for [[ref:ah.ext.inquiry-costs]]. Nothing in this policy prevents, delays or penalises your compliance with a mandatory notification obligation under the National Law: see [[ref:ah.obl.no-admissions]].
