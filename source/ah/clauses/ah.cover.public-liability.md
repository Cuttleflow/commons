We will pay your civil liability for any claim first made against you and notified to us during the policy period for:

- (a) bodily injury to any person other than an employee; or
- (b) property damage to the property of any person,

arising from the conduct of your practice, including the occupation of premises used for your practice. We will also pay your defence costs for a claim covered by this clause.

Like [[ref:ah.section.pi|heading]], this section is written on a claims-made and notified basis: it covers only claims first made against you and notified to us during the policy period, arising from bodily injury or property damage occurring after the retroactive date.
