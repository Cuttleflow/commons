---
id: ah.def.statutory-liability
type: definition
heading: Statutory Penalty
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

A civil or criminal pecuniary penalty or fine imposed under an Act of the Commonwealth or of a State or Territory in connection with your practice. A statutory penalty is never loss, compensation, damages, taxes, or an amount payable under an infringement notice for a traffic or vehicle offence.
