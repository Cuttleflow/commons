heading: What Counts as One Notification
legacy_number: '4.3'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

A notified circumstance and every claim arising from it, and all claims arising from the same or interrelated acts, errors, omissions or events (see [[ref:ah.cond.aggregation]]), are together treated as a single claim, first made when the earliest of them was notified to us.
