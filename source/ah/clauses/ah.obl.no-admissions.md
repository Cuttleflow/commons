heading: No Admissions Without Our Consent
legacy_number: '7.2'
since: 1.3.0

---

You must not admit liability for, settle, or make or promise any payment in respect of any claim or circumstance, or incur any defence costs, without our prior written consent, which we will not unreasonably withhold or delay. However, nothing in this policy prevents, and you do not breach this obligation by, any of the following, and none of the following is treated by us as an admission of liability:

- (a) expressing regret, saying sorry, or making an apology or expression of sympathy that is protected by, or consistent with, the civil liability legislation of the relevant State or Territory (for example, Part 2C of the Civil Liability Act 2002 (NSW) and its counterparts);
- (b) participating in Open Disclosure, including providing a factual explanation of what happened;
- (c) making a mandatory notification you are legally required to make under s.130 or ss.140 to 143 of the National Law, or complying with any registration, reporting or disclosure obligation under the National Law;
- (d) making a reportable incident notification you are legally required to make to the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission;
- (e) making, or contributing to, a report of a reportable incident under the Serious Incident Response Scheme, or making any other report or notification required by the Aged Care Act;
- (f) conducting an assessment of, or making a notification about, an eligible data breach as required by Part IIIC of the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) or a corresponding health records law; and
- (g) making any other disclosure, notification or report you are required by law to make.

We ask that you tell us about any of these steps as soon as practicable, so we can support you, but taking a legally required step before telling us is never, by itself, a breach of this policy.
