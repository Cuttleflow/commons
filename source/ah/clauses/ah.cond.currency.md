legacy_number: '8.13'
since: 1.3.0

All premiums, limits, sub-limits, excesses and payments under this policy are in Australian dollars. Where a loss is incurred in another currency, it is converted at the market rate of exchange published by the Reserve Bank of Australia on the date the payment obligation is finally determined.
