arising from any deliberate, dishonest, fraudulent, criminal or malicious act or omission by you, or any wilful breach of duty, statute or contract by you. This exclusion applies only where the relevant conduct is admitted by the insured person concerned or established by a final adjudication; until then we will advance defence costs subject to repayment under [[ref:ah.cond.repayment]]. This exclusion does not apply to an insured person who did not commit, participate in, or acquiesce in the conduct, and [[ref:ah.cond.severability]] applies. Nothing in this exclusion overrides paragraph (b) of [[ref:ah.ext.audits-and-employee-fraud]].
