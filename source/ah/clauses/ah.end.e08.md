Cover is extended to professional services provided temporarily outside Australia, for the destinations, purposes and maximum duration stated in the schedule (for example, humanitarian placements, sports-team travel, or conference demonstrations). [[ref:ah.excl.usa-canada]] continues to apply absolutely, and claims must be brought and determined in a jurisdiction other than the USA or Canada.

Schedule variables: destinations; purposes; maximum duration.
