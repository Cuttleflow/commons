The obligations in this section, and the obligations stated in individual extensions and endorsements, are contractual obligations, not conditions precedent to our liability. If you do not comply with an obligation, we will not refuse a claim outright on that ground alone: consistent with s.54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), we may reduce the amount we pay by the amount that fairly represents the prejudice we suffer because of your non-compliance, and we may refuse the claim only where the Act permits, for example where your act or omission caused the loss, or where the claim is fraudulent.
