arising from any act, error or omission by a person registered in the medical profession under the National Law, in that person's practice of medicine. This exclusion applies to the clinical practice of medical practitioners because their professional indemnity cover is separately mandated under the Commonwealth medical indemnity framework, including the Medical Indemnity Act 2002 (Cth) and the Medical Indemnity (Prudential Supervision and Product Standards) Act 2003 (Cth).

This exclusion does not apply to you, and does not reduce your cover, merely because you work alongside, share premises with, receive referrals from, refer patients to, act on the orders or prescriptions of, or otherwise collaborate with a medical practitioner. Your own liability for your own professional services remains covered even where a medical practitioner is involved in the same episode of care, and even where you are alleged to be vicariously or concurrently liable with a medical practitioner, in which case we cover only your own liability, not the medical practitioner's.
