If the limit of liability for [[ref:ah.section.pi|heading]] is reduced or exhausted by payment of claims in the policy period, we reinstate it once, in full, for claims that are wholly unrelated to the claims that reduced it and are first made and notified later in the same policy period. A reinstated limit never applies to a claim that reduced or exhausted the limit, or to any claim treated as one with it under [[ref:ah.cond.aggregation]], and sub-limits are not reinstated. This provision meets the automatic reinstatement requirement of the National Boards' professional indemnity insurance registration standards. Endorsement E12 is not needed for this reinstatement.
