heading: Advertising Liability
legacy_number: 3.3.4
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

We will pay your civil liability for any claim first made against you and notified to us during the policy period arising from unintentional defamation, unintentional infringement of copyright, title or slogan, or unintentional misappropriation of advertising ideas, committed in advertising your practice. This extension does not cover: statements you knew to be false or misleading; failure of goods or services to conform with advertised quality or performance; an incorrect description of the price of goods or services; or claims covered by [[ref:ah.section.pi|heading]].
