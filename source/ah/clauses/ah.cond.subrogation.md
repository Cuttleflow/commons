If we pay anything under this policy, we are subrogated to all your rights of recovery, contribution and indemnity in respect of that payment, and we may pursue those rights in your name. You must not do anything to prejudice those rights, and you must give us reasonable assistance in pursuing them at our expense. We will not exercise rights of subrogation against: an employee, student, volunteer or locum (unless their conduct was serious or wilful misconduct, or dishonest, fraudulent or criminal); or any person against whom s.65 or s.66 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) prevents recovery. Any recovery is applied first to our outlay and costs of recovery, and any balance to you.
