If you are dissatisfied with any decision or service of ours, you may use our internal complaints process at no cost, using the complaints contact details shown in the schedule. We will acknowledge your complaint within 1 business day, keep you informed, and give you our final response within 30 calendar days.

If your complaint is not resolved to your satisfaction, or we take longer than 30 days, you may refer it to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA): www.afca.org.au, info@afca.org.au, 1800 931 678, GPO Box 3, Melbourne VIC 3001. AFCA's determinations bind us but not you. Time limits apply. We subscribe to the General Insurance Code of Practice.
