All claims and inquiries arising from the same act, error, omission or event, or from a series of acts, errors, omissions or events that are causally connected or interrelated, are together treated as one claim, first made when the earliest of them was first made or notified (see [[ref:ah.cond.aggregation]]). One limit of liability and one excess apply to that one claim.
