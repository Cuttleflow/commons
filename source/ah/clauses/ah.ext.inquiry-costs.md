We will pay the reasonable legal costs and expenses you incur, with our prior written consent, to be represented at, respond to, or prepare for an inquiry, where the inquiry is first made against you during the policy period, as [[ref:ah.def.inquiry]] provides, and you notify us of it during the policy period. This includes, without limitation:

- (a) investigations, performance assessments, health assessments, immediate-action proceedings, panel hearings and tribunal proceedings by or on behalf of Ahpra or a National Board under the National Law;
- (b) investigations and proceedings by the Health Ombudsman (Queensland), the Health Care Complaints Commission (New South Wales) or any other state or territory healthcare complaints entity;
- (c) coronial investigations and inquests;
- (d) investigations and proceedings by the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission;
- (e) the Medicare, PBS and related audits and investigations described in paragraph (a) of [[ref:ah.ext.audits-and-employee-fraud]];
- (f) Royal Commissions, boards of inquiry and parliamentary inquiries; and
- (g) formal disciplinary proceedings of your professional association.

The most we will pay under this extension, together with paragraph (a) of [[ref:ah.ext.audits-and-employee-fraud]] and [[ref:ah.ext.mandatory-reporting]], which share this sub-limit, is the inquiry costs sub-limit shown in [[ref:ah.pay.sub-limits]], unless the schedule states a different amount. This extension covers costs only: it does not cover any fine, penalty, order for the repayment of fees or benefits, registration fee, or costs order made against you in favour of an inquiring body, except as expressly provided elsewhere in this policy.
