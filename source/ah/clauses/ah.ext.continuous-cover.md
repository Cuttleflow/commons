heading: Continuous Cover
legacy_number: 2.2.10
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

If you first became aware of a circumstance during a period in which you were continuously insured by us under an allied health professional indemnity policy, and you did not notify us of it under the policy in force at that time, we will cover the resulting claim under this policy, even though [[ref:ah.excl.prior-circumstances]] would otherwise apply, provided that:

- (a) you have been continuously insured by us for this class of risk from the date you first became aware of the circumstance until the date the claim is first made and notified;
- (b) the failure to notify was not fraudulent; and
- (c) cover under this extension is provided on the terms, conditions, exclusions and limits of the policy in force when you first became aware of the circumstance, or of this policy, whichever are more favourable to us, and we may reduce the amount we pay by the amount that fairly represents any prejudice we suffer from the late notification.
