id: ah.excl.prescription-medicines
type: exclusion
heading: Prescription-only Medicines Outside Authority
legacy_number: '5.4'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

arising from your prescription, supply or administration of a medicine included in schedule 4 or schedule 8 of the Poisons Standard, unless the medicine was:

- (a) prescribed by a medical practitioner, or by another person authorised by law to prescribe it; or
- (b) prescribed, supplied or administered by you under, and within, an authority you hold by law, including a scheduled medicines endorsement, a nurse practitioner or endorsed midwife authority, or the prescribing and dispensing authority of your registration (for example, as a dentist, optometrist, podiatrist or pharmacist), and in accordance with the drugs, poisons and controlled substances legislation of the relevant State or Territory.
