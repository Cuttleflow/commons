If you die, become legally incapacitated, or become bankrupt or insolvent, we will treat your estate, heirs, executors, administrators, legal personal representatives and personal insolvency administrators as you under this policy, for claims arising from your professional services. We will also pay the civil liability of your spouse or domestic partner for a claim arising solely from their status as your spouse or partner (for example, a claim against jointly owned property), provided the claim arises from your professional services and not from any act, error or omission of the spouse or partner. This extension does not extend the scope of cover beyond your own conduct.
