We will pay:

- (a) the reasonable legal costs and expenses you incur, with our prior written consent, in responding to an audit, compliance review or investigation of your billing, claiming or record-keeping first made against you, as [[ref:ah.def.inquiry]] provides, and notified to us during the policy period, by Services Australia, the Chief Executive Medicare, the Department of Health, the Professional Services Review, the National Disability Insurance Agency or the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission, in connection with Medicare benefits, the Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme, the Child Dental Benefits Schedule or NDIS funding; and
- (b) your civil liability to repay amounts, and associated defence costs, where the liability arises from the fraudulent or dishonest billing or claiming conduct of an employee committed without your knowledge, consent, connivance or reckless disregard.

This extension does not cover: repayment of amounts to which you were never entitled by your own act or omission (a trading debt, not an insured loss); any fine or penalty except as provided by [[ref:ah.ext.statutory-liability]]; or any matter in which your own fraud or dishonesty is finally established. Cover under paragraph (a) forms part of the sub-limit for [[ref:ah.ext.inquiry-costs]].
