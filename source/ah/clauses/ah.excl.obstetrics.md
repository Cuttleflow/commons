arising from the provision of obstetric care during active labour, delivery, or the 24-hour post-partum period, in any setting other than a licensed healthcare facility. Midwives may apply for Endorsement E01, which extends this policy to cover facility-based intrapartum care; intrapartum care at a homebirth or in any other non-facility setting remains excluded in all cases and cannot be endorsed under this product. This exclusion does not apply to: antenatal or postnatal care outside the periods described above; emergency care you provide as a Good Samaritan Act where no alternative care is available; or care you provide in the course of transferring a patient to a licensed healthcare facility.
