heading: Intoxicants
legacy_number: '5.7'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

arising from professional services provided by an insured person while under the influence of alcohol or of a drug (other than a drug taken as prescribed or directed by a treating practitioner and not impairing the insured person's capacity to practise safely). This exclusion applies only to the insured person who was under the influence; [[ref:ah.cond.severability]] protects the others.
