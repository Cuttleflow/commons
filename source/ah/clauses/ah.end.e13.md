This endorsement applies where the insured is a physiotherapist registered with the Physiotherapy Board of Australia, or a practice whose professional services described in the schedule are wholly or predominantly physiotherapy services.

**Covered techniques.** Professional services include the following techniques and modalities when performed by an allied health practitioner registered in the profession of physiotherapy, within the scope of practice recognised by the Physiotherapy Board of Australia:

- (a) spinal manipulation and mobilisation, including high-velocity, low-amplitude thrust techniques applied to the spine and peripheral joints;
- (b) dry needling, being the insertion of fine filiform needles into myofascial trigger points, muscles or connective tissue for the management of pain and movement dysfunction; and
- (c) exercise prescription, including the assessment, prescription, progression and supervision of therapeutic exercise programs directed at rehabilitation, injury prevention, pain management or the restoration or maintenance of physical function.

No separate endorsement is required for dry needling.

**Additional obligations.** These are added to [[ref:ah.section.obligations]] and operate on the same basis:

- (a) Continuing professional development. You must complete, and keep records evidencing, the continuing professional development required by the Physiotherapy Board of Australia's registration standard for continuing professional development as in force from time to time. Where you perform dry needling, your continuing professional development must include, or you must otherwise hold and maintain, training specific to dry needling covering safe needling technique, relevant anatomy, infection control, and the recognition and management of adverse events (including pneumothorax).
- (b) Scope of practice. Every treatment and technique you provide must be within the physiotherapy scope of practice described in the Physiotherapy Board of Australia's practice standards (or any registration standard, code or guideline that replaces them), and within any conditions, undertakings or notations recorded on your registration.

**Additional exclusions.** These are added to [[ref:ah.section.exclusions]]. We will not cover any claim, inquiry, loss, defence costs or other amounts arising from:

- (a) Podiatric surgery: the performance of podiatric surgery or any other procedure reserved to podiatrists or podiatric surgeons registered with the Podiatry Board of Australia. This exclusion is stated for certainty; such work is outside the physiotherapy scope of practice.
- (b) Cosmetic and aesthetic physiotherapy: any treatment or procedure performed wholly or predominantly for aesthetic or cosmetic purposes and not directed at physical rehabilitation, the treatment of illness or injury, or the management of pain, including facial physiotherapy, cosmetic needling, or muscle-stimulation treatments provided for purely cosmetic purposes without a clinical indication. This exclusion does not apply to scar management directed at the functional or symptomatic management of scar tissue, or to post-surgical rehabilitation (including after reconstructive or cosmetic surgery) directed at restoring movement, strength, function or lymphatic drainage, or at managing pain, swelling or scarring.

**Gym, fitness and personal training.** Professional services do not include gym instruction, group fitness classes or personal training services conducted under a separate business structure (including a separate company, partnership, trust or registered business name) that is not the practice described in the schedule. Exercise prescription and supervised therapeutic exercise delivered as part of your physiotherapy practice, including sessions delivered in a gym or fitness setting, remain covered.

Schedule variables: none.
