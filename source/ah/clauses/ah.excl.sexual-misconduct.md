arising from any actual or alleged sexual assault, sexual misconduct, molestation, grooming, or sexual relationship with a patient or client, committed or alleged to have been committed by you. However:

- (a) Defence costs. We will pay the reasonable defence costs of the accused insured person in defending a claim or inquiry alleging conduct described in this exclusion, up to the sexual misconduct defence costs sub-limit shown in [[ref:ah.pay.sub-limits]], provided the insured person denies the allegation. If the conduct is subsequently admitted by the insured person, or established by a final adjudication of a court, tribunal or inquiring body, the insured person must repay to us all amounts we paid under this paragraph; and
- (b) Innocent insureds. This exclusion does not apply to an insured person who did not commit, participate in, acquiesce in, or fail to report in breach of a legal duty, the alleged conduct, including a practice principal alleged to be vicariously liable for the conduct of another person, and [[ref:ah.cond.severability]] applies.
