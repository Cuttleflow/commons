directly or indirectly caused by or arising from ionising radiation or contamination by radioactivity from any nuclear fuel, nuclear waste, or the combustion of nuclear fuel, or from the hazardous properties of any nuclear installation, reactor, weapon or component. This exclusion does not apply to radioisotopes, radium or radium compounds used, stored or transported exclusively for diagnostic or therapeutic purposes within your professional services (including medical radiation practice and nuclear medicine imaging within your scope of practice).
