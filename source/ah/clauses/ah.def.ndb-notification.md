---
id: ah.def.ndb-notification
type: definition
heading: NDB Notification
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

The assessment of a suspected eligible data breach, and the notification of the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner and affected individuals, required under Part IIIC of the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), together with any equivalent mandatory data breach assessment or notification required under the My Health Records Act 2012 (Cth), the Health Records and Information Privacy Act 2002 (NSW), the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic), the Health Records (Privacy and Access) Act 1997 (ACT), or corresponding State or Territory law.
