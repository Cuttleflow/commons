heading: Repayment Where Cover Is Not Established
legacy_number: '8.15'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

If we advance defence costs or other amounts, and it is later admitted by the relevant insured person or established by a final adjudication that the claim or inquiry falls within [[ref:ah.excl.sexual-misconduct]] or [[ref:ah.excl.deliberate-acts]], or is otherwise not covered by this policy, that insured person must repay those amounts to us on demand. [[ref:ah.cond.severability]] protects innocent insured persons from repayment obligations arising from another person's conduct.
