heading: Effect of Notification
legacy_number: '4.2'
since: 1.3.0

---

If you give us written notice of a circumstance during the policy period, then under s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) we are not relieved of liability by reason only that the resulting claim is made against you after the policy period ends. Any claim that later arises from a notified circumstance is treated as a claim first made against you and notified to us on the date we received your notice of the circumstance, and is covered on the terms, limit of liability and excess of this policy.
