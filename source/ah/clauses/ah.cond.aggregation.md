heading: Multiple Claims Aggregation
legacy_number: '8.12'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

All claims and inquiries arising out of, based upon, or attributable to the same act, error, omission or event, or to a series of acts, errors, omissions or events that are similar or related, or interrelated by reason of a common cause, transaction, patient episode, course of conduct or originating source, are together treated as a single claim for all purposes of this policy, including the limit of liability, sub-limits and excess. That single claim is treated as first made when the earliest of them was first made against you or first notified to us, whichever is earlier, and falls in the policy period in which it is treated as first made.
