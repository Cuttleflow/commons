(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The insured, their health discipline and National Board registration or professional association membership, and any practice entity named under Endorsement E03
- The professional services covered
- The policy period, start and end dates
- The retroactive date
- The limit of liability, and whether Section A and Section B have separate limits
- Sub-limits that differ from the defaults in [[ref:ah.pay.sub-limits]]
- The excess
- The declared gross fee income used to rate the policy
- The premium, including stamp duty and GST
- The state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:ah.cond.governing-law]])
- The claims and complaints contact details of the insurer
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
