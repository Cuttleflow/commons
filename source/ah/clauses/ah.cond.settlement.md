heading: Settlement Opportunities
legacy_number: '8.7'
since: 1.3.0

---

If we recommend a settlement of a claim that is acceptable to the claimant, and you do not consent to it, our liability for that claim will not exceed: the amount for which the claim could have been settled; plus defence costs incurred up to the date your consent was requested; less the excess. You then conduct the further defence at your own risk and expense as to the difference.
