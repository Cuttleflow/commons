heading: Tell Us About Material Changes
legacy_number: '7.5'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

You must tell us in writing as soon as reasonably practicable if, during the policy period: your registration or accreditation is suspended, cancelled, surrendered or made subject to conditions or undertakings; you materially change the nature or scope of your professional services; you begin practising a discipline not described in the schedule; you merge your practice with, or sell it to, another person or entity; or you become bankrupt or enter an insolvency administration. We may then adjust the terms or premium prospectively; no adjustment affects cover for professional services already provided.
