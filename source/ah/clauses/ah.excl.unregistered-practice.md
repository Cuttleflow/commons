heading: Unregistered or Unaccredited Practice
legacy_number: '5.2'
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

arising from professional services provided at a time when you did not satisfy whichever of the following applies to your discipline:

- (a) where registration under the National Law is required for your discipline, you did not hold current registration in the relevant profession (or held registration subject to a condition, undertaking or suspension that prohibited the service in question); or
- (b) where registration under the National Law is not required or not available for your discipline, you did not hold the current membership, accreditation or certification of your professional association required for practising members.

This exclusion does not apply to an employee, student or volunteer performing tasks lawfully open to unregistered persons under your direct supervision, and does not apply where your registration or accreditation lapsed solely through administrative error and was restored with continuous effect.
