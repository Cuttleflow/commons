If, during the policy period, a person described below is legally required to attend court, a tribunal or an inquiry as a witness in connection with a claim or inquiry covered by this policy, we will pay:

- (a) $500 for each day, or part of a day, of attendance by the person named in the schedule, a principal or a partner; and
- (b) $250 for each day, or part of a day, of attendance by an employee.

The most we will pay under this extension is the sub-limit shown in [[ref:ah.pay.sub-limits]]. No excess applies. This extension does not apply to attendance by a person as a claimant or complainant.
