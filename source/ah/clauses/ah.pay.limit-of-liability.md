The most we will pay under this policy for all claims, inquiries, losses, defence costs and other amounts combined, across all sections and extensions, is the limit of liability shown in the schedule, which applies in the aggregate for the policy period. If the schedule shows separate limits for Section A and Section B, each stated limit applies in the aggregate to its section, and no amount is payable under one section's limit for a liability covered by the other.
