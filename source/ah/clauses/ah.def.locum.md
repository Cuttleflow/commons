---
id: ah.def.locum
type: definition
heading: Locum
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

A natural person who is an allied health practitioner in the same discipline as you (or a discipline within the scope of your practice as described in the schedule) engaged by you on a temporary basis to perform professional services in your place or alongside you, or the engagement of you by another practitioner or practice in that capacity.
