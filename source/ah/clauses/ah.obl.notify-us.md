legacy_number: '7.1'
since: 1.3.0

You must give us written notice, as soon as reasonably practicable and during the policy period, of: any claim first made against you; any notice of an inquiry; and any circumstance of which you become aware. [[ref:ah.section.notification]] explains the effect of notifying a circumstance. Send notices as described in [[ref:ah.claims.how-to-make-a-claim]].
