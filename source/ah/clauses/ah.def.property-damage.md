id: ah.def.property-damage
type: definition
heading: Property Damage
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Physical loss of, damage to, or destruction of tangible property, including resulting loss of use, and loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically damaged where that loss of use is caused by an occurrence connected with your practice. Electronic data is not tangible property for the purposes of [[ref:ah.section.pl|heading]].
