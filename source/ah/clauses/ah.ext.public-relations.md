since: 1.3.0

If a claim or inquiry covered by this policy, or an event reasonably likely to give rise to one, attracts or is reasonably likely to attract adverse publicity that could materially damage the reputation of you or your practice, we will pay the reasonable fees of a public relations consultant engaged with our prior written consent to mitigate that damage, provided the consultant is engaged within 30 days after the event, claim or inquiry first becomes known to you. The most we will pay under this extension is the sub-limit shown in [[ref:ah.pay.sub-limits]]. No excess applies.
