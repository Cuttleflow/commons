---
id: ah.def.retroactive-date
type: definition
heading: Retroactive Date
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

The date shown in the schedule. This policy does not cover any claim or inquiry arising from an act, error, omission or conduct that occurred, or is alleged to have occurred, before the retroactive date. If the schedule shows the retroactive date as "Unlimited", no retroactive restriction applies (other than the exclusion of matters known to you before the policy period began).
