heading: You, Your and Insured Person
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Any of the following:

- (a) the person named in the schedule as the insured;
- (b) any past, present or future partner or principal of the practice named in the schedule, but only for professional services provided for and on behalf of that practice;
- (c) any employee, but only while acting within the scope of their duties in connection with your practice;
- (d) any student or person on a work-experience or vocational placement with your practice, but only while acting under your direct supervision;
- (e) any volunteer assisting your practice, but only while acting under your direct supervision and in accordance with your documented risk-management framework; and
- (f) any locum engaged by the practice, but only for professional services provided for and on behalf of the practice during that engagement.

Each of them is an insured person. Cover for each insured person applies as if a separate policy had been issued to each of them, subject to [[ref:ah.cond.severability]] and to the single limit of liability described in [[ref:ah.section.payment]]. s.48 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) gives the persons described in paragraphs (b) to (f) the right to claim directly under this policy even though they are not parties to it.
