This is the first Commons release of the Meridian Allied Health wording. It replaces version 1.2.0 (July 2026) and the Physiotherapy, Psychology and Counselling, and Dental and Oral Health profession endorsements version 1.1.0, all published outside the Commons. Changes from version 1.2.0:

- "We", "us" and "our" now mean the insurer named in the schedule. Version 1.2.0 named Cuttleflow Systems, which is not an insurer. Claims and complaints go to the contact details shown in the schedule.
- New Automatic Reinstatement provision: the Section A limit is reinstated once, in full, for unrelated claims. The National Boards' registration standards require automatic reinstatement, which version 1.2.0 said it met but offered only as an optional endorsement. Endorsement E12 now gives a second reinstatement.
- New Insurance Contracts Act condition, stating that the Act prevails and that s.54 applies to every obligation, including those in extensions and endorsements.
- Governing law is now the state or territory shown in the schedule, with the principal place of business at the start of the policy period as the fallback. A new Jurisdiction condition sits beside it.
- Sanctions is now a standalone condition naming the Australian sanctions laws, not an exclusion.
- The Multiple Claims Aggregation condition now includes similar or related acts, and states the policy period an aggregated claim falls in.
- New Privacy condition.
- The three profession endorsements are now Endorsements E13 (Physiotherapy), E14 (Psychology and Counselling) and E15 (Dental and Oral Health) in the endorsement library, with their cover unchanged. Their notes to underwriters are not part of the contract and now sit in the platform rules.
- The nursing profession endorsement (AH-NURS) is not carried into this release.
- Each endorsement now states when the platform applies, refers, suggests or offers it.
- The licence is now CC BY 4.0, in line with the rest of the Commons.
- Clause numbers are removed. Each clause is identified by its heading.
