Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026.

- About This Policy: no longer says the policy is designed to satisfy the National Boards' standards in every respect. It now describes the retroactive cover, reinstatement and run-off it gives, says it covers the professional services described in your schedule, and says a Board's standard may need more, such as a longer run-off period. The retail-client statement is replaced by the library-wide wording. Marked for review against each Board's current standard.
- Retroactive Date: if you held professional indemnity insurance immediately before this policy, the retroactive date is no later than the start of your continuous series of cover.
- Run-off Cover on Retirement or Cessation of Practice: no longer ends because you obtain other insurance. It says the default is 72 months, that Endorsement E06 provides a longer period, and that we may seek contribution from another insurer. Marked for review.
- Extended Reporting Period: no longer unavailable because you obtain replacement insurance.
- One Claim, What Counts as One Notification and Multiple Claims Aggregation: one aggregation test and one deemed date, in Multiple Claims Aggregation. The other two clauses refer to it.
- Definition of Inquiry, definition of Claim, Inquiry Costs, Mandatory Reporting Costs, Medicare, NDIS and PBS Audits and Employee Fraud, and Notifying Possible Inquiries: one trigger for regulatory events. An inquiry, or a claim of a kind in paragraphs (c) to (f), is first made when you first receive written notice of it. Section references to the National Law for mandatory notifications are removed until verified, and marked for review.
- Cancellation: you may cancel at any time and receive a pro-rata refund in every case. We may cancel only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.
- Insurance Contracts Act: replaced with the library-wide text. Section 54 applies to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation.
- Sanctions: replaced with the library-wide text.
- Other Insurance: replaced with the library-wide text. We do not reduce or refuse a payment because of other insurance, unless the schedule or an endorsement makes this policy excess of a specified underlying policy.
- Data Breach Fines and Penalties, Statutory Liability, Fines, Penalties and Punitive Damages, and Uninsurable Penalties: criminal fines are never paid. Statutory Liability names the work health and safety penalty bans in New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland. Both extensions are marked for review.
- Control Group: new definition for a practice entity named under Endorsement E03. Severability and Non-imputation and Endorsement E03 say the entity's knowledge and conduct are those of its control group only.
- Endorsement E02: the cover is excess of the sponsor indemnity stated in the schedule.
- Endorsement E14: the supervision requirement for a provisionally registered psychologist is now a stated scope limit, not an obligation.
- Endorsement E15: the general anaesthesia limit is now an additional exclusion, stated as a limit on the cover and not an obligation.
- Default set for underwriter confirmation: Retroactive Date continuity rule and the 72-month Run-off default.
