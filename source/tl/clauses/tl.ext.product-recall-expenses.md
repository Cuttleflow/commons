id: tl.ext.product-recall-expenses
type: extension
heading: Product Recall Expenses
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Notwithstanding the product recall exclusion, we will pay reasonable recall costs where technology products have caused or are reasonably likely to cause personal injury or property damage due to: (a) the accidental omission of a component during manufacture; (b) the accidental inclusion of a harmful or contaminating substance; (c) an accidental labelling error; or (d) any other defect arising in design or manufacture. Sub-limit: as shown in the schedule.
