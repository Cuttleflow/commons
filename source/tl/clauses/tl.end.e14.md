The [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section extends to the reasonable costs you incur in the first 72 hours after a **cyber security breach** to keep your technology services available to clients, including temporary hosting, temporary staff and contractors, and expedited delivery of replacement equipment, where we have not yet had the chance to agree them.

You must give us notice of the costs as soon as reasonably practicable and keep the invoices. We pay these costs only to the extent they are also reasonable measures to reduce business interruption loss, and we do not pay for the permanent improvement of your **computer system**.

The most we will pay under this endorsement is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for it, which is part of the limit of liability. Costs paid under this endorsement reduce the amount payable for the same costs under the covers in the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section.

Schedule variables: sub-limit.
