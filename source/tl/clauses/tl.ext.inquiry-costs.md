We will pay your reasonable legal costs to respond to or attend any inquiry (investigation, examination, or inquiry by a body with legal authority) where you are first served during the policy period. Does not include routine supervision, compliance audits, or industry-wide inquiries. Sub-limit: as shown in the schedule.
