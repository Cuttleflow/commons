We may: (a) instruct you to conduct the defence if we believe the claim will not exceed the retention; (b) take over and conduct the defence or settlement of any claim at any time.

You must not negotiate, settle, admit liability, or incur defence costs without our written consent (which we will not unreasonably withhold).
