**technology services** includes the development, hosting or operation of software for the custody, transfer, exchange or issue of cryptocurrency or other digital assets, as described in the schedule for this endorsement.

The [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] section covers a claim alleging that your negligent act, error or omission in those services caused your client a financial loss. The [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section covers a cyber loss that results in the theft of digital assets from wallets or accounts you hold on behalf of a client or in your own name, up to the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement.

The value of a digital asset is its value in Australian dollars at the time of the loss, taken from the exchange named in the schedule or, if none is named, the exchange with the highest trading volume for that asset that day. We do not pay for a fall in value after the loss, or for any loss arising from trading, price movement or the failure of an exchange.

This endorsement does not cover: (a) a claim or loss arising from your private keys being held by a person you did not authorise; (b) a claim by an investor alleging a loss on an investment; or (c) a claim or loss relating to a financial product or a financial service for which a licence is required and you do not hold it.

Schedule variables: described services; sub-limit; named exchange (if any).
