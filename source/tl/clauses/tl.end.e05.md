**technology product** includes software, firmware and configuration services you supply that are embedded in, or control, the connected devices named in the schedule for this endorsement, where you did not manufacture the device.

The [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] section covers a claim alleging that a failure of that embedded software or firmware, or a failure to supply a security update you had agreed to supply, caused your client a financial loss. The [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section covers a cyber loss that results from unauthorised access to those devices through a weakness in the software or firmware you supplied.

This endorsement does not cover: (a) bodily injury or property damage, which are covered only as the [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section provides, where that section is included; (b) the cost of recalling, repairing or replacing a device; or (c) a claim arising from a device you manufactured.

The most we will pay under this endorsement is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for it, which is part of the limit of liability.

Schedule variables: named devices or product lines; sub-limit.
