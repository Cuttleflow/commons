since: 1.4.0

Notwithstanding the property-in-control exclusion, we cover property damage to: (a) premises you lease or rent, and their contents; (b) premises you temporarily occupy to carry out work; (c) the personal property of your insured persons; (d) vehicles left in a car park you operate, other than a car park you run for a fee; or (e) other property temporarily in your physical control, sub-limited as shown in the schedule.
