(a) damages; (b) defence costs; (c) court attendance costs; (d) inquiry costs; (e) regulatory penalties (where specifically covered under an extension or insuring agreement); (f) claim prevention costs. Loss does not include: (a) taxes; (b) costs under a merchant services agreement; (c) restitution, disgorgement, or unjust enrichment; (d) punitive, aggravated, exemplary, or liquidated damages; (e) costs of complying with injunctive or non-monetary relief; (f) your overheads, staff time, or internal costs; (g) your fees or commissions; (h) amounts uninsurable under the law governing this policy. Loss does not include cyber loss (as defined), which is a separate, the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section-specific concept.
