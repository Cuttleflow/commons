type: extension
heading: Joint Venture Liability
legacy_number: '4.8'
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

We will cover claims arising from your participation in a joint venture (as defined) that provides the same technology products or technology services as you — provided the joint venture was disclosed to us before inception. Cover is limited to your stated percentage interest or share of the joint venture. No cover for joint venture partners.
