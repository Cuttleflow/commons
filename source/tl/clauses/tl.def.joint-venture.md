---
id: tl.def.joint-venture
type: definition
heading: Joint venture
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means any incorporated or unincorporated arrangement, however structured, between you and one or more other parties to jointly carry on a business activity, in which you hold a stated percentage interest, share of profits or losses, or degree of control. Joint venture is relevant wherever the term is used in this policy (currently [[ref:tl.ext.joint-venture-liability]], all sections).
