---
id: tl.def.technology-provider
type: definition
heading: Technology provider
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

A third party that provides technology services to you under a written contract for hosted application services, or for processing, storing, or hosting your data. Technology provider does not include providers of utilities, telecommunications infrastructure, financial securities, stock exchanges, or clearing houses. This definition applies to the Cyber Protection section only.
