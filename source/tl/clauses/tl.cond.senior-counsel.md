heading: Senior Counsel Clause
legacy_number: '6.5'
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

We will not require you to contest any claim unless Senior Counsel advises that the claim should be contested, taking into account the likely damages, defence costs, and prospects of success. Senior Counsel's costs are part of defence costs.
