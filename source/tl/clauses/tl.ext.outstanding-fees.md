since: 1.4.0

Notwithstanding the fees exclusion, we will pay your contractually agreed fees (excluding profit and GST) that a client refuses to pay — provided: (a) there is written evidence of the client's refusal and intention to make a claim; (b) you have made all reasonable efforts to recover; (c) you will cease recovery efforts; (d) payment is reasonably likely to resolve any existing or potential claim. Sub-limit: as shown in the schedule.
