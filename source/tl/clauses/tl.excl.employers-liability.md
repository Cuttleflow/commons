---
id: tl.excl.employers-liability
type: exclusion
heading: Employers Liability
legacy_number: '5.13'
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Arising from: (a) injury to any insured person under workers' compensation or similar legislation; (b) damage to insured person property in the course of employment; (c) breach of employment practices (unless arising from a data breach or cyber security breach covered under the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section).
