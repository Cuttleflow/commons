Reimbursement for direct financial loss that you first discover during the policy period, from: (a) unauthorised electronic funds transfer; (b) unauthorised invoice manipulation; (c) impersonation-based requests for transfer of money, securities, data, or property. Sub-limit: as shown in the schedule.
