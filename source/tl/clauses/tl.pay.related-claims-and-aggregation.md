**The [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] and [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] sections (claims):** All claims arising from the same, similar, or related act, error, or omission (or a series of related acts, errors, or omissions) are deemed one claim, made at the time the earliest of them was first made. A claim arising from a circumstance notified under [[ref:tl.obl.notification]] is deemed to have been made at the time of that notification.

**The [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section (cyber loss):** All cyber loss arising from the same, similar, or related act, event, or originating cause is deemed one cyber loss, occurring when first discovered. Where related events span more than one policy period, the entire series is treated as occurring in, and is subject to the limit of liability, retention, and terms of, the policy period in which it was first discovered.

**The [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section:** All personal injury and property damage from continuous or repeated exposure to the same conditions is deemed one occurrence.
