id: tl.def.extended-reporting-period
type: definition
heading: Extended reporting period
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means a period after the policy period ends during which a claim first made against you, or a cyber loss first discovered, in the policy period or in that period may be notified to us, as provided by [[ref:tl.ext.extended-reporting-period]] and any endorsement that extends it.
