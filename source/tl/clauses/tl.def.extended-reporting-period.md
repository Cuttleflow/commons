heading: Extended reporting period
since: 1.4.0
changed: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

means a period after the policy period ends during which a claim may first be made against you and be covered, as provided by [[ref:tl.ext.extended-reporting-period]] and any endorsement that extends it. It applies to claims only. A cyber loss must be first discovered during the policy period.
