Notwithstanding the media liability exclusion, we will cover claims for unintentional: (a) defamation; (b) plagiarism; (c) copyright infringement or trademark dilution; (d) improper deep-linking, framing, or web scraping; (e) violation of privacy rights — arising from your technology products, technology services, advertising, a data breach, or a cyber security breach. Sub-limit: as shown in the schedule.
