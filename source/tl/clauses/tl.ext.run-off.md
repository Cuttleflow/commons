Applies to the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] and [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] sections only.

If you cease to exist, operate, or are acquired, this policy continues to expiry — but only for claims arising from technology products/services provided before the cessation or acquisition. **This extension is subject to, and does not limit, [[ref:tl.cond.mergers-and-consolidations]]; where the same event triggers both provisions, [[ref:tl.cond.mergers-and-consolidations]] governs the scope of continued cover for the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] and [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] sections.**
