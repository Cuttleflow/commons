Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies the operation of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) (the Act). Where this policy is inconsistent with the Act, the Act prevails.

If this policy would otherwise let us refuse to pay a claim, in whole or in part, because of something you did or did not do after this policy was entered into, section 54 of the Act applies. We may not refuse to pay the claim only because of that act or omission. Our liability is reduced by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced. Where the act or omission could reasonably be regarded as capable of causing or contributing to the loss, we may refuse to pay only the part of the claim that the act or omission caused or contributed to. We may not refuse any part that you prove the act or omission did not cause. This applies to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation in this policy, not only to the claims conditions.

(a) Utmost good faith: We will act towards you with the utmost good faith, as required by sections 13 and 14 of the Act.

(b) Non-avoidance: We will not avoid this policy for non-disclosure or misrepresentation unless the non-disclosure or misrepresentation was fraudulent. Where it was not fraudulent, we may reduce our liability under s.28(3) of the Act to the amount that would place us in the position we would have been in had the disclosure been made or the representation not been made. **For this class, we do not extend the s.28(3) reduction waiver that applies for innocent insureds under the financial-lines suite's multi-individual covers, reflecting that this class is predominantly a single-named-insured business cover rather than a cover for multiple individuals.**

(c) Innocent insured: Where this policy excludes loss caused by dishonest, fraudulent, or criminal conduct, that exclusion does not apply to an insured who did not commit, participate in, or know about the conduct.

(d) s.40(3) — notification of circumstances: Where you give us notice of facts that might give rise to a claim during the policy period, in accordance with [[ref:tl.obl.notification]], we cannot later refuse to cover a claim arising from those facts on the ground only that it was made after the policy period, as provided by s.40(3) of the Act.
