*Threading note: in the [[ref:tl.section.general-conditions|heading]] section, a reference to a claim includes, for the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section, a reference to a cyber loss (as defined), except where this wording expressly distinguishes between them. This ensures the limits, non-accumulation and retention machinery below reaches the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section's first-party payments, not only its claims.*
