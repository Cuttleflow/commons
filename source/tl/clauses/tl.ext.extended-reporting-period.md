Applies to the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] and [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] sections only.

If this policy expires without being renewed or replaced, **or is cancelled by you or by us**, we will automatically cover claims first made within the automatic extended reporting period shown in the schedule (or, if not shown, **90 days**) after the date of expiry or cancellation — provided the claim arises from acts before that date. This benefit applies to claims only. A cyber loss must be first discovered during the policy period. This benefit ceases if you obtain replacement cover. **A longer extended reporting period may be purchased by endorsement, for the period and additional premium shown in the schedule.**
