---
id: tl.def.unauthorised-access
type: definition
heading: Unauthorised access
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means access to, or use of, your computer system that is not permitted by you, and includes access or use that exceeds the scope of access or use you have permitted — whether or not the person accessing or using the system holds valid credentials to do so. This addresses an employee or other person who holds legitimate credentials but exceeds their authorised scope. Unauthorised access is relevant to the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section only. This definition applies to the Cyber Protection section only.
