If a claim (or, for the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section, a cyber loss) could be indemnified under more than one section: (a) you are entitled to indemnity under one section only per claim or cyber loss, being the section, of those under which it could be indemnified, that has the highest limit of liability; (b) the most we will pay is the limit of liability of that section; (c) limits do not accumulate across sections; (d) where more than one retention would apply, the highest applies.

*WORKED EXAMPLE — NON-ACCUMULATION. Your schedule shows a $5M limit of liability for the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] and [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] sections combined, and a $10M limit of liability for the [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section. A claim could be indemnified under both the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] section and the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section. Those two sections share one $5M limit, so the most we will pay is $5M, not $10M. A different loss could be indemnified under both the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] section and the [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section. We pay it under the [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section, which has the highest limit, so the most we will pay is $10M, not $15M. In each case the highest applicable retention applies.*
