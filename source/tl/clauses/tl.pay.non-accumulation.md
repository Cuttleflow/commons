If a claim (or, for the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section, a cyber loss) could be indemnified under more than one section: (a) you are entitled to indemnity under one section only per claim or cyber loss; (b) the maximum liability across all sections is the highest single section limit; (c) limits do not accumulate across sections; (d) where more than one retention would apply, the highest applies.

*WORKED EXAMPLE — NON-ACCUMULATION. You have the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] section (PI) with a $5M limit, the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section (Cyber) with a $5M limit, and the [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section (GL) with a $10M limit. A claim could potentially fall under both the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] section and the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section. The maximum we will pay for that claim across all sections is $10M (the highest single section limit) — not $20M. If the claim is allocated to the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] section, the most we pay is $5M.*
