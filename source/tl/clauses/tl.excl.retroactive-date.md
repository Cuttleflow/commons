(the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] and [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] sections only.) Any claim to the extent it arises from an act, event, error, or omission before the retroactive date in the schedule. This exclusion does not apply to a cyber loss, which responds to discovery during the policy period and is subject to [[ref:tl.excl.prior-or-pending]].
