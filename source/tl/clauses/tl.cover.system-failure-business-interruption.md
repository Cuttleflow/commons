We will reimburse business interruption loss from: (a) a system failure (your own systems); or (b) a dependent system failure (your technology provider's systems), in each case first discovered by you during the policy period — subject to the same indemnity period and waiting period (threshold basis) as [[ref:tl.cover.business-interruption]]. **Sub-limit for (b): as shown in the schedule.**
