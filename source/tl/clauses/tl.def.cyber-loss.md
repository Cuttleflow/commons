---
id: tl.def.cyber-loss
type: definition
heading: Cyber loss
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means any amount we pay, or agree to pay, under the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section, including business interruption loss, breach response costs, extortion payments, data restoration costs, hardware replacement costs, loss under the Reputational Harm extension, and reimbursement under [[ref:tl.ext.cyber-crime]]. Cyber loss arises only under the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section. For the avoidance of doubt, an amount payable under the Cyber Liability Extension of the [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section (3.3) for personal injury or property damage is loss, not cyber loss, because that extension responds under the [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section's occurrence trigger. Cyber loss is referred to in the exclusions (the [[ref:tl.section.exclusions|heading]] section), the notification condition ([[ref:tl.obl.notification]]), the aggregation condition ([[ref:tl.pay.related-claims-and-aggregation]]), and the limits and retention provisions ([[ref:tl.pay.limits-of-liability]], [[ref:tl.pay.retention]]) — each of which applies to the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section only in respect of cyber loss.
