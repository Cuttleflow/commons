id: tl.def.continuity-date
type: definition
heading: Continuity date
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means the date shown in the schedule from which you have been continuously and without interruption insured for the risk covered by this policy, whether by us or, where the schedule shows a predecessor insurer, by that insurer. Continuity date is distinct from Retroactive date and performs a different function: retroactive date fixes how far back covered acts, errors, omissions or events may reach; continuity date evidences unbroken prior cover for the purposes of the Continuous Cover extension ([[ref:tl.ext.continuous-cover]]) and the Prior or Pending exclusion ([[ref:tl.excl.prior-or-pending]]). The schedule contains separate fields for each. Continuity date is relevant wherever [[ref:tl.ext.continuous-cover]] applies — the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] and [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] sections and the Cyber Liability Extension of the [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section.
