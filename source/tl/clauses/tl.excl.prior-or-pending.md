id: tl.excl.prior-or-pending
type: exclusion
heading: Prior or Pending
legacy_number: '5.25'
since: 1.4.0
changed: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

Arising from: (a) matters disclosed or threatened before the policy period; (b) facts or circumstances previously notified, or which a member of the control group knew before the policy period might give rise to a claim or cyber loss. [[ref:tl.ext.severability]] applies to this exclusion. Subject to the Continuous Cover Extension ([[ref:tl.ext.continuous-cover]]).
