The [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] section covers a claim alleging that you breached the terms of an open source software licence in a technology product or technology service you supplied, including a claim for damages, for the cost of replacing the affected component, and for the defence costs of a claim for relief.

This endorsement does not cover: (a) a claim alleging that you deliberately breached a licence after you received written notice of the breach and did not remedy it within the time the notice allowed; (b) the cost of releasing your own source code; (c) loss of the value of your own intellectual property; or (d) a claim that [[ref:tl.ext.patents]] covers, which is dealt with under that extension.

The most we will pay under this endorsement is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for it, which is part of the limit of liability.
