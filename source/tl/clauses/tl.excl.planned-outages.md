heading: Planned Outages

(the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section and Cyber Liability Extension only.) Arising from planned or scheduled downtime of your systems or your technology provider's systems. However, this does not apply where you deliberately took systems offline to manage a cyber security breach.
