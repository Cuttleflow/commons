(the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] and [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] sections only.) Any act of terrorism, including any action to control, prevent, or suppress terrorism. Where property damage under the [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section arises from an incident declared a terrorist incident under the **Terrorism Insurance Act 2003 (Cth)**, that Act's provisions may override this exclusion to the extent it applies to eligible property; this exclusion does not purport to limit the operation of that Act.
