Each insured is treated separately. Where one insured person failed to disclose or made a misrepresentation, this does not prejudice any other insured person who was not aware of the failure.

The knowledge, act, omission or conduct of one insured person is not imputed to any other insured person when applying [[ref:tl.excl.conduct]], [[ref:tl.excl.prior-or-pending]], [[ref:tl.excl.known-defect]], [[ref:tl.ext.continuous-cover]] or the duty of disclosure. The knowledge of a member of the control group, and the conduct of any executive officer, is imputed to the named insured and each subsidiary.
