heading: Severability and Non-Imputation and Innocent Non-Disclosure
legacy_number: '4.13'
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Where one insured person failed to disclose or made a misrepresentation, this does not prejudice any other insured person who was not aware of the failure. The conduct of any executive officer is imputed to the entity.
