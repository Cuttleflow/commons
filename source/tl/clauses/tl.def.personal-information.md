---
id: tl.def.personal-information
type: definition
heading: Personal information
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable, whether or not true and whether or not recorded in a material form; this has the same meaning as in s.6 of the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). This definition applies to the Cyber Protection section only.
