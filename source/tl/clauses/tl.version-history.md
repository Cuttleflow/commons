This document is version-controlled. The version and date shown on the cover page and in the footer of each page govern this text. The version stated in your schedule as current at inception applies for the life of your policy period, unless your schedule or an endorsement states otherwise.

Version 1.5.0 (November 2026) is a remediation release. The Changes From note for that version lists what changed.

Version 1.4.0 was the first release of the Meridian Technology Liability wording built from clause source in the Commons. It replaces version 1.3.0 (July 2026) and brings the endorsement library (2026/TL/0002) into this document. Changes from version 1.3.0:

- Clause numbers are removed. Each clause is identified by its heading, and every cross-reference that used a section number is now a link to the clause it names. Where version 1.3.0 named a group of sections, the reference names each clause in the group.
- New definitions of Related claims and Extended reporting period. Version 1.3.0 used both terms without defining them.
- The Exclusion Applicability Matrix now names each exclusion by its heading.
- The endorsement library is now part of this wording, with 14 endorsements, each with operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger. The library in version 1.3.0 gave a title for each endorsement and no operative text.
- E01 United States and Canada Jurisdiction writes back the jurisdictional limits exclusion for the sections the schedule names.
- E02 pays defence costs for the Technology Professional Liability and Cyber Protection sections in addition to the limit, to a cap.
- E04 Excess Layer follows the underlying policy and does not drop down for uncollectible underlying insurance.
- The licence notice and the Lloyd's risk codes are now in this document as the Licence and Trademark Notice.
