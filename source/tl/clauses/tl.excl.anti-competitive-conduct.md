id: tl.excl.anti-competitive-conduct
type: exclusion
heading: Anti-Competitive Conduct
legacy_number: '5.4'
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Arising from restraint of trade, anti-competitive practices, price fixing, or tortious interference — including under any competition, consumer, or fair trading legislation. Does not apply to consumer privacy laws covered under [[ref:tl.cover.data-and-system-security-liability]].
