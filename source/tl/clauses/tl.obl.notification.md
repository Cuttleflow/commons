(a) The [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] section (claims-made): tell us in writing as soon as you become aware of a claim or inquiry. You may also notify us in writing, during the policy period, of specific facts or circumstances that may give rise to a claim, giving full particulars known to you. **A notification under this paragraph is a notification of facts under s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).**

(b) The [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section (claims-made / discovery): if any member of your **control group** (as defined) becomes aware of a claim, cyber loss, or any circumstance that may give rise to a claim or cyber loss, you must notify: (i) the incident response panel shown in the schedule; and (ii) us — as soon as reasonably practicable during the policy period. If the circumstance is an extortion threat, [[ref:tl.cover.cyber-extortion]] also applies and you must not pay a ransom without our prior written consent. **A notification of circumstances under this paragraph is also a notification of facts under s.40(3) of the Act.**

(c) The [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section (occurrence): tell us in writing as soon as you become aware of a claim, inquiry, or loss.
