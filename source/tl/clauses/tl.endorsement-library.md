An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables (sub-limits, retentions, periods, named parties) shown for it in the schedule. Where an endorsement and the rest of this policy conflict, the endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. Endorsements do not increase the limit of liability unless they say so; a sub-limit in an endorsement is part of, not in addition to, the limit of liability. If a variable an endorsement needs is not shown in the schedule, the default stated in the endorsement applies; if it has no default, the endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the schedule. These endorsements are for use with version 1.5.x of this wording.

| Code | Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | United States and Canada Jurisdiction | Writes back claims brought, or judgments enforced, in the United States and Canada |
| E02 | Defence Costs in Addition to the Limit | Pays defence costs in addition to the limit of liability for the Technology Professional Liability and Cyber Protection sections, to a cap |
| E03 | Increased Sub-limits | Increases the sub-limits for the covers and extensions named |
| E04 | Excess Layer | Writes this policy in excess of the underlying insurance named |
| E05 | IoT and Embedded Systems | Covers software and firmware you supply for the connected devices named |
| E06 | AI Governance and Model Liability | Covers claims about AI models and systems you supply, data poisoning and prompt injection |
| E07 | Open Source Software Licence Compliance | Covers claims that you breached an open source licence |
| E08 | Cryptocurrency and Digital Asset Services | Covers claims and theft in relation to digital asset services described in the schedule |
| E09 | Managed Security Services Provider | Covers claims arising from managed security services you provide |
| E10 | Government and Defence Contractor | Covers liability under the government and defence contracts named |
| E11 | Non-IT Dependent Business Interruption | Extends business interruption cover to cyber security breaches at named suppliers |
| E12 | Customer Business Interruption | Covers lost income when a cyber security breach at a named customer reduces its purchases |
| E13 | Missed Bid Income Loss | Covers net profit lost on a bid you could not submit because of a cyber security breach |
| E14 | Emergency Continuity Costs | Covers the emergency costs of keeping services available in the first 72 hours after a cyber security breach |
