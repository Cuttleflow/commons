We will pay PCI DSS fines you are liable to pay and reasonable legal costs in dealing with a PCI DSS investigation arising from a data breach. We do not pay any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured; see [[ref:tl.cover.regulatory-defence-and-fines]].
