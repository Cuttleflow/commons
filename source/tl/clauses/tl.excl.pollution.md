heading: Pollution
legacy_number: '5.24'
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Arising from the discharge, release, or escape of any pollutant, or any remediation thereof. However, in the [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section, this does not apply to sudden, unexpected, and unintended pollution during the policy period.
