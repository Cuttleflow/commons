means your chief executive officer, chief financial officer, general counsel, head of risk or insurance, chief information security officer or head of information security, and directors, or a person performing an equivalent role. If you have none of these roles, it means your most senior officer. Control group is used wherever this policy turns on what you knew or became aware of: [[ref:tl.obl.notification]], [[ref:tl.excl.prior-or-pending]], [[ref:tl.excl.known-defect]], [[ref:tl.ext.continuous-cover]] and [[ref:tl.ext.severability]]. Control group is relevant to all sections.
