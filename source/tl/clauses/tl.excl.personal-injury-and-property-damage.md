(the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] and [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] sections only.) Personal injury and/or property damage. However, this does not apply to: (a) property damage to your computer system under the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section; (b) hardware replacement under [[ref:tl.ext.hardware-replacement]]; (c) emotional distress arising from media liability under the Media and Advertising Liability Extension ([[ref:tl.ext.media-and-advertising-liability]]).
