**technology services** includes managed security services, as described in the schedule for this endorsement, namely monitoring, detection, response, vulnerability management and security consulting you provide to a client under a written contract.

The [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] section covers a claim alleging that your negligent act, error or omission in providing those services allowed a **cyber security breach** or **data breach** at your client that you were engaged to detect, prevent or contain, and your client suffered a financial loss.

This endorsement does not cover: (a) a claim alleging that you guaranteed that a breach would not occur or would be detected; (b) the cost of improving your client's security beyond what it was before the breach; or (c) a claim for a breach at a client that the contract did not require you to monitor.

The most we will pay under this endorsement is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for it, which is part of the limit of liability.

Schedule variables: described services; sub-limit.
