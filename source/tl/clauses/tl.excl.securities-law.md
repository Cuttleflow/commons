---
id: tl.excl.securities-law
type: exclusion
heading: Securities Law
legacy_number: '5.31'
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Any actual or alleged breach of a law regulating the offering, issue, purchase, or sale of securities, including Chapters 6D (fundraising) and 6CA (continuous disclosure) of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), or any comparable law of another jurisdiction.
