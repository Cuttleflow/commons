Arising from: (a) breach of any patent; (b) breach of any trade secret (the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] and [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] sections only); (c) any media liability. Subject to [[ref:tl.ext.patents]] and [[ref:tl.ext.media-and-advertising-liability]].

*IP BOUNDARY AT A GLANCE (guidance only — not operative text; the operative positions are in the clauses cross-referenced below and are unchanged by this note). Patents: excluded, written back for non-US/Canada registrations only ([[ref:tl.ext.patents]]). Trade secrets: excluded for the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] and [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] sections ([[ref:tl.excl.media-liability]], paragraph (b)); not excluded for the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section, so effectively covered there through the Data breach definition. Copyright infringement and trademark dilution: excluded as media liability, written back ([[ref:tl.ext.media-and-advertising-liability]]). Trademark infringement (other than dilution): excluded ([[ref:tl.excl.advertising-liability]], paragraph (d)), no write-back. This consolidated statement is provided per audit finding N6; no substantive IP position changes.*
