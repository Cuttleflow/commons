(Completed separately for each policy — contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- Who is insured
- The policy period (start and end dates)
- The governing law state or territory (see [[ref:tl.cond.governing-law-and-jurisdiction]])
- Which sections are operative (marked "Included" or "Not Included")
- The retroactive date and the continuity date
- The limit of liability and aggregate limit for the [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] and [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] sections combined, and any sub-limits
- The limit of liability for the [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section, for any one occurrence
- The retention for each section
- The waiting period and indemnity period for business interruption cover
- The automatic extended reporting period, if it is not the default stated in [[ref:tl.ext.extended-reporting-period]]
- The premium (including stamp duty and GST)
- Any endorsements, and the variables shown for each
