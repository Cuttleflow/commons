---
id: tl.def.related-claims
type: definition
heading: Related claims
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means two or more claims, or two or more cyber losses, that arise from the same, similar or related act, error, omission, event or originating cause, or from a series of them, or that share a common fact, circumstance or transaction. Related claims are treated as one claim as set out in [[ref:tl.pay.related-claims-and-aggregation]].
