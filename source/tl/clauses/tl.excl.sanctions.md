---
id: tl.excl.sanctions
type: exclusion
heading: Sanctions
legacy_number: '5.30'
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Any claim, loss, or cyber loss, to the extent that making a payment or providing a benefit would breach, or expose us to enforcement action under: (a) the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011 (Cth) or the Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 (Cth); or (b) as a deliberate additional scope choice, any trade or economic sanctions law of the United Nations, the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States, or Australia.
