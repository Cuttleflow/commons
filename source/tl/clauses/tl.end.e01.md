[[ref:tl.excl.jurisdictional-limits]] does not apply to the claims, cyber losses and regulatory proceedings in the scope of this endorsement. The [[ref:tl.section.professional-liability|heading]] section, the [[ref:tl.section.cyber|heading]] section and, where the schedule says so, the [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section respond to claims brought within, or judgments entered or enforced from, a court of the United States of America or Canada or their territories.

For a claim or cyber loss within the scope of this endorsement: (a) we will not pay punitive, exemplary or multiple damages, or any fine or penalty; (b) defence costs are within the limit of liability, whatever [[ref:tl.end.e02]] provides; and (c) the most we will pay is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement, if one is shown.

You must not admit liability, settle or incur defence costs for any claim within the scope of this endorsement without our prior written consent, which we will not unreasonably withhold, and we may appoint counsel of our choosing.

Schedule variables: sections to which the endorsement applies; sub-limit (if any); additional premium.
