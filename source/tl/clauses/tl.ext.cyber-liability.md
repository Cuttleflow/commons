heading: Cyber Liability
since: 1.4.0
status: draft

Notwithstanding the cyber exclusion, we cover personal injury or property damage caused by an occurrence arising from: (a) a cyber security breach; (b) your technology products transmitting malicious code. Sub-limit: as shown in the schedule.

This extension ensures physical harm from a cyber event is covered under the occurrence-based liability section, not the claims-made cyber section — the correct trigger for bodily injury claims. Amounts payable under this extension are loss (the [[ref:tl.section.liability|heading]] section), not cyber loss — see the Cyber loss definition.
