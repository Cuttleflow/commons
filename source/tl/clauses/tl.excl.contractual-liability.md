Arising from any liability assumed under a contract, agreement, guarantee, indemnity, or warranty. Subject to the Contractual Liability Extension ([[ref:tl.ext.contractual-liability]]) which writes back substantial cover. [WP REVIEW: check the warranty wording in this exclusion and in the Contractual Liability Extension against the treatment of warranties in s.24 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).]
