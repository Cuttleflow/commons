---
id: tl.def.waiting-period
type: definition
heading: Waiting period
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

The time period stated in the schedule. A business interruption must last longer than the waiting period before any business interruption loss is payable. Once the interruption exceeds the waiting period, business interruption loss is measured from the start of the interruption. This definition applies to the Cyber Protection section only.
