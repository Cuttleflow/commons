Remediation of the Meridian Industrial Special Risks wording v1.0.0 (2026/PR/0001) after the Claude review of 20 November 2026, and its first release in the Commons. Version 1.0.0 and its Endorsement Library v1.0.0 (2026/PR/0002) were held in SharePoint and were not published. Changes from version 1.0.0:

- Structure: clauses have headings only and no numbers, cross-references use headings, and the wording uses "we" and "you". The exclusions apply to both sections and the applicability matrix is removed.
- Limit of Liability: new provision. The most we pay for one Event at one Situation is the Limit of Liability, with an optional limit for one Event across Situations. Version 1.0.0 had no operative limit.
- Co-insurance: the test is now made for the Situation where the Damage happens, with a minor-loss waiver (default 10%). The percentage is a schedule item (default 85%). The worked example is corrected: version 1.0.0's example paid 70% where its own 85% rule gives 82.35%.
- Business Interruption: the measure of loss is rebuilt on the gross profit, gross revenue and gross rentals bases. Turnover, Rate of Gross Profit, Annual Turnover, Standard Turnover and Specified Working Expenses are now defined. Version 1.0.0 paid the whole reduction in turnover. Additional increased cost of working has its own limit. Each business interruption item has its own underinsurance test at the percentage shown in the schedule (default 85%, the level version 1.0.0 applied through its combined test). New provisions cover trend adjustments, turnover earned elsewhere, departments, new businesses, accumulated stock and salvage sales.
- Business Interruption trigger: the contradiction between "would be covered but for the Deductible" and "paid or admitted liability" is removed. Damage at a Situation to property you use but do not own, such as a landlord's building, now triggers the cover if it results from a cause the Material Damage section would cover.
- Terrorism: replaced. The exclusion now gives way to the Terrorism and Cyclone Insurance Act 2003 (Cth) for declared terrorist incidents. Version 1.0.0 excluded terrorism to the extent the Act required cover.
- Communicable Disease: new exclusion that names no Act. Version 1.0.0 said disease was excluded but contained no exclusion. The limited buy-back is now the COMB endorsement.
- Flood: new definition, including water from a watercourse that arrives through stormwater pipes. It no longer relies on the Insurance Contracts Regulations. Flood is covered up to the flood limit shown for each Situation.
- Cyber Events: an operative exclusion with two write-back options (fire and explosion, or all physical damage). The drafting note in the operative text is removed.
- Sanctions: moved out of the exclusions into the library-wide Sanctions condition.
- Other Insurance: replaced with the library-wide clause. Version 1.0.0's rateable contribution clause was likely void under s.45 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).
- Governing Law and Jurisdiction: the law is the one shown in the schedule, or that of the first-named insured's registered office at the start of the Period of Insurance. Jurisdiction is non-exclusive. Version 1.0.0 tied the law to where the policy was issued.
- Added for parity with Mark IV Modified forms: subsidiaries and entities acquired during the period (90 days), Interests of Other Parties, Separate Interests, Temporary Protection and Repairs, Locks and Keys, Fire Fighting and Mitigation Costs, Undamaged Foundations and Parts, Professional and Government Fees, Claims Preparation Costs for both sections, and a 72-hour rule in the definition of Event.
- Removed: the orphan definition "Excess of Loss cover"; the "Extended reporting" condition, which had no work to do in a property wording; and the exclusion of property "more specifically insured", which conflicted with s.45.
- Provenance: the Gross Profit definition, the accounting-terms sentence and the trend adjustment are redrafted in Meridian's own words. The statement that the wording was "modelled on the common market base wording" is replaced by a provenance statement in About This Policy.
- Licence: CC BY 4.0, in line with the rest of the Commons. Version 1.0.0 was labelled CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Endorsement Library: 67 endorsements replace E01 to E16. Each has operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger. They also attach to Mark IV and Mark V wordings, and ten are marked as built into this wording.
- Kept from version 1.0.0 for underwriter confirmation: earthquake is excluded unless the EQKE endorsement applies; the business interruption waiting period is a threshold, not a deduction; public utilities cover responds to any accidental failure of supply; there is no exclusion for action of the sea; and we may cancel on 30 days' written notice.
