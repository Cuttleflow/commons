heading: Locks and Keys
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If keys, access cards or combinations for a Situation, or for a safe or strongroom at a Situation, are stolen, or are lost through Damage covered by [[ref:isr.section.material-damage]], we pay the cost of replacing or recoding the locks and replacing the keys or cards, up to the sub-limit shown in the schedule.
