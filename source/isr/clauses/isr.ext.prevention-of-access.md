We cover loss under [[ref:isr.section.business-interruption]] when access to or use of a Situation is prevented or hindered by Damage, from a cause [[ref:isr.section.material-damage]] would cover, to property in the vicinity of the Situation (or within the distance shown in the schedule, if one is shown), whether or not the property is yours. The most we pay is the prevention of access sub-limit shown in the schedule, after the waiting period shown.
