Any provision of the Base Wording that limits theft cover to theft involving forcible and violent entry or exit does not apply. We cover theft of insured property from a Situation up to the theft sub-limit shown in the Schedule, which is a first-loss amount: co-insurance does not apply to theft claims. We do not pay for theft by you, your directors or partners, or any employee or contractor, whether acting alone or with others; loss found only at stocktake or that cannot be explained; theft of Money (see MONY); or theft of property left in the open unless it is designed to be kept there.

Schedule variables: theft sub-limit (first loss); theft deductible.
