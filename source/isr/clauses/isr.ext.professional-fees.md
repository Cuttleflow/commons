heading: Professional and Government Fees
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We pay the fees of architects, surveyors, engineers, quantity surveyors, project managers and lawyers necessarily incurred in reinstating Damaged property, and the fees and contributions payable to a government or local authority for approvals needed to reinstate it, up to the sub-limit shown in the schedule. Fees for preparing a claim are paid only under [[ref:isr.ext.claims-preparation]].
