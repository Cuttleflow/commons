heading: Computer Installations
since: 1.1.0

---

We cover loss under [[ref:isr.section.business-interruption]] resulting from Damage, of a kind [[ref:isr.section.material-damage]] would cover, to computer installations and data storage media in Australia that the Business uses, wherever they are, up to the sub-limit shown in the schedule. [[ref:isr.excl.cyber]] applies.
