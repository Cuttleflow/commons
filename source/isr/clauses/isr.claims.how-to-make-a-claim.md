heading: How to Make a Claim
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

1. Make the property safe and take reasonable steps to prevent further loss.
2. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
3. Tell the police about any theft or malicious damage.
4. Keep Damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, and take photographs.
5. Give us details of what happened, the Situations affected and, for business interruption, your accounts.
6. We will acknowledge your claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision as the General Insurance Code of Practice requires where the insurer subscribes to it.
