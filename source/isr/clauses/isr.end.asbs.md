We do not pay for loss, Damage, cost or expense arising from asbestos or asbestos-containing material, including its presence, removal, encapsulation or abatement, or any order to deal with it. This exclusion does not apply to:

- Damage to property, including its asbestos-containing parts, caused by a peril not otherwise excluded, such as fire or storm; or
- the extra cost of removing and disposing of asbestos-containing debris of property Damaged by such a peril, up to the asbestos debris sub-limit shown in the Schedule.

Schedule variables: asbestos debris sub-limit.
