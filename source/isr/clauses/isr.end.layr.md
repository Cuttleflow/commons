Where the Schedule shows this Policy as an excess layer:

- We pay only loss that exceeds the attachment point shown in the Schedule, which is the total of the limits of the underlying layers listed, up to the layer limit shown, for any one loss at any one Situation unless the Schedule says otherwise.
- This Policy follows the terms of the primary policy named in the Schedule, except for its limits and premium and anything this Policy states differently.
- The underlying limits are reduced or used up only by payments of loss that this Policy would also cover. We do not pay any part of the attachment point that an underlying insurer does not pay, unless the Schedule shows that drop-down applies.
