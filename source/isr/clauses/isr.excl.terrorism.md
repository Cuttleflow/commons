In this exclusion, an act of terrorism is an act, or a threat of an act, involving force, violence or damage to property, done for a political, religious, ideological or similar purpose, including to influence a government or to put the public, or part of it, in fear.

- We do not pay for Damage or loss caused by an act of terrorism, or by any action taken to control, prevent or respond to one, except as this exclusion provides.
- Where the Terrorism and Cyclone Insurance Act 2003 (Cth) applies to this policy and loss arises from a declared terrorist incident under that Act, this exclusion has effect only to the extent that Act allows. We pay that loss on the terms of this policy, subject to any reduction in our liability that the Act permits. [WP REVIEW: confirm the treatment of reductions announced under the Act, and of incidents declared after a claim has been declined.]
- If the schedule shows a terrorism sub-limit, we also cover Damage and loss from an act of terrorism that is not a declared terrorist incident, up to that sub-limit in the aggregate for the Period of Insurance.
