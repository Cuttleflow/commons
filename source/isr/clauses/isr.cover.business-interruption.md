heading: Business Interruption Cover
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We cover the loss you suffer because the Business is interrupted or interfered with during the Indemnity Period as a result of Damage that happens during the Period of Insurance and is:

- Damage to Insured Property at a Situation that is covered by [[ref:isr.section.material-damage]], or would be covered but for the Deductible; or
- Damage at a Situation to property you use in the Business but do not own and are not responsible for insuring, such as a building owned by your landlord, from a cause [[ref:isr.section.material-damage]] would cover if that property were Insured Property.

We pay as [[ref:isr.pay.bi-loss]] provides, up to the Limit of Liability and any sub-limit.
