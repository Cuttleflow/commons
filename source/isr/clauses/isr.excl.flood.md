heading: Flood
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We cover Damage by Flood at a Situation, and loss under [[ref:isr.section.business-interruption]] resulting from it, only up to the flood limit shown in the schedule for that Situation, after the flood Deductible. There is no cover for Flood at a Situation for which the schedule shows no flood limit, or at a Situation or postcode the schedule lists as excluded.
