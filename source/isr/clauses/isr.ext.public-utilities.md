heading: Public Utilities
since: 1.1.0

---

We cover loss under [[ref:isr.section.business-interruption]] caused by the accidental failure of the supply of electricity, gas, water or telecommunications to a Situation, arising at the supplier's premises or anywhere in the network that supplies the Situation, where the failure lasts longer than the waiting period shown in the schedule. The most we pay is the public utilities sub-limit shown.
