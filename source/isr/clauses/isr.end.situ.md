- **New Situations.** A location in Australia that you buy, lease or start to occupy for the business during the Period of Insurance is a Situation from that date, up to the new-Situation limit shown in the Schedule, for the notification period shown. Tell us about it within that period. We will add it to the Schedule and charge additional premium as DVAD provides.
- **Unspecified locations.** Insured property temporarily at a location in Australia that is not a Situation, other than in transit, is covered up to the unspecified-location limit shown in the Schedule.
- If you do not tell us about a new location within the notification period, cover for it continues up to the unspecified-location limit. [WP REVIEW: confirm this reduction operates consistently with section 54.]
- This endorsement does not cover a location or postcode the Schedule excludes. The flood, earthquake and other natural peril limits apply at a new location as they would to a Situation in the same value tier.

Schedule variables: new-Situation limit; notification period (default 90 days); unspecified-location limit; excluded locations or postcodes.
