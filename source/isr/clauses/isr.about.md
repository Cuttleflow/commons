This policy insures the property and business income of medium and large commercial and industrial businesses in Australia. It is an industrial special risks policy: it covers physical loss of or damage to your property from any cause it does not exclude, and the loss of income that follows when that damage interrupts your business.

It follows the two-part structure the Australian market has used for industrial special risks policies since the late 1980s, written in plain English and updated for current law. It was drafted independently and does not reproduce the text of any other wording.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Material Damage | Damage to your property at the Situations, and the costs that follow it | Damage during the Period of Insurance |
| Business Interruption | Loss of gross profit, gross revenue or gross rentals, and extra costs, when Damage interrupts the Business | Damage during the Period of Insurance, and loss during the Indemnity Period |

[[ref:isr.section.business-interruption]] applies only if the schedule shows it.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:isr.section.definitions]].
