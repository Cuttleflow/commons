The interests of the parties named in the Schedule as interested parties (for example mortgagees, financiers, lessors and owners) are noted on this Policy.

- We will pay an interested party for its interest where you ask us to or the Schedule requires it. That payment discharges us to the extent of the amount paid.
- If the Schedule shows that non-vitiation applies to an interested party, we will not refuse or reduce that party's claim for its interest because of an act, omission, misrepresentation or non-disclosure by you that happened without that party's knowledge. [WP REVIEW: confirm our recovery rights against the Insured after paying an innocent interested party.]
- Noting an interest does not make the interested party liable for premium and does not increase the Limit of Liability.
- If we cancel this Policy, we will tell each interested party at the same time as we tell you.

Schedule variables: interested parties and their interest; non-vitiation (yes or no, per party); payment instructions.
