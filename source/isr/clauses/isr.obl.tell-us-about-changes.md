type: obligation
heading: Tell Us About Changes
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Tell us during the Period of Insurance, as soon as reasonably practicable, about any change in the Business, in the occupation of a Situation, or in the construction or fire protection of a building, that materially increases the risk of Damage. We will tell you whether the change affects your cover or premium.
