---
id: isr.def.you-your
type: definition
heading: You, Your
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

The insured named in the schedule and each of its subsidiaries, as defined in the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), at the start of the Period of Insurance. It also includes an entity that becomes such a subsidiary during the Period of Insurance, for 90 days after it does so, or longer if we agree in writing, but not for Damage that happened before it became a subsidiary. Each is insured for its own interest. The entity named first in the schedule acts for all of them in giving and receiving notices, paying premium and receiving claim payments.
