Despite COMX, we cover Business Interruption loss from the closure or restriction of the whole or part of a Situation, ordered by a competent authority because a person at the Situation has a human communicable disease. We also pay the reasonable cost of cleaning and disinfecting the Situation that the order requires. We do not cover an order that applies generally to a region, a state or territory, or Australia, rather than specifically to the Situation; or loss from fear or threat of disease without such an order. Cover is limited to the aggregate sub-limit shown in the Schedule for the Period of Insurance, whatever the number of orders, Situations or diseases, and to the shorter of the indemnity period shown and the actual closure.

Schedule variables: aggregate sub-limit; indemnity period; waiting period.
