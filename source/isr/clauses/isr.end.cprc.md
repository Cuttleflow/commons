We pay the reasonable fees of your accountants, auditors, engineers, valuers and other professionals for producing the information we ask for to assess a claim under this Policy, up to the sub-limit shown in the Schedule. We do not pay fees for disputing a claim with us, the fees of anyone paid by a share of the claim, or the cost of your own employees unless the Schedule says so.

Schedule variables: sub-limit; whether your own employees' time is included.
