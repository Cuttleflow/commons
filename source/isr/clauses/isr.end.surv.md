- We, or a surveyor we appoint, may inspect any Situation at a reasonable time after giving you reasonable notice.
- Where the Schedule lists a risk improvement requirement for a Situation, complete it by its due date and tell us when it is done.
- If a requirement is not completed by its due date, we may by written notice agree a new due date with you; or apply the special deductible shown for that requirement to Damage at that Situation of the kind the requirement was meant to prevent, from the date of our notice until the requirement is completed; or cancel this Policy as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

Schedule variables: requirements per Situation, each with due date and special deductible.
