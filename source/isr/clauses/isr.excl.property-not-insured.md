id: isr.excl.property-not-insured

heading: Property Not Insured
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

[[ref:isr.section.material-damage]] does not cover:

- Money, except up to the Money sub-limit shown in the schedule;
- vehicles registered for road use, and their accessories, while away from a Situation;
- property in transit, except movement within a Situation and property covered by [[ref:isr.ext.temporary-removal]];
- land, water, roads and dams, and the cost of the land component of any reinstatement;
- livestock, growing crops and standing timber;
- aircraft and watercraft, except small watercraft the schedule shows;
- data and software as such, except as [[ref:isr.ext.data-restoration]] provides. Physical Damage to the media holding them is covered.
