since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us in writing. We refund the premium for the unexpired Period of Insurance on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. If we have paid a total loss of all the Insured Property at a Situation, no refund applies to the premium for that Situation.

We may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you at least 30 days' written notice, or any longer notice that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired Period of Insurance on a pro-rata basis.

Cancellation does not affect a claim for Damage that happened before cancellation.
