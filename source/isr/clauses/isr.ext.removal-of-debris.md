heading: Removal of Debris
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We pay the reasonable costs you incur, following Damage covered by [[ref:isr.section.material-damage]], to demolish, dismantle, shore up or prop up the Damaged property; to remove, store and dispose of its debris; and to clean up Pollutants at the Situation released by that Damage. We do not pay to clean up Pollutants beyond the boundary of the Situation, or costs an authority requires where there is no Damage covered by this policy. The most we pay is the removal of debris sub-limit shown in the schedule.
