In this exclusion, a communicable disease is any disease that can be transmitted from one person, animal or thing to another by any means, including by a virus, bacterium, parasite or other organism.

We do not pay for loss, cost or expense arising from a communicable disease, or the fear or threat of one, including any order, direction or restriction by an authority relating to it, or the cost of cleaning, disinfecting or decontaminating property because of one. This exclusion does not apply to Damage by fire, explosion or another physical cause that is not a communicable disease, or to cover provided by [[ref:isr.end.incd]] or [[ref:isr.end.comb]], to the extent and limits they state.
