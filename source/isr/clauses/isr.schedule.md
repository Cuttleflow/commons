(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The insurer or insurers, each insurer's share, and the leading insurer if there is one
- The insured, any other entities insured, and any interested parties
- The Business and the Period of Insurance
- Each Situation, with its address and its Declared Values by category of property
- The Limit of Liability, whether it is combined for both sections, and any limit for any one Event across Situations
- Each sub-limit, with its basis: per Event, in the aggregate, in addition, or most we pay
- The co-insurance percentages for each section, and the minor-loss percentage
- The flood limit for each Situation, and any Situation or postcode where Flood is excluded
- The Deductibles, including any for particular perils, and the waiting period
- For business interruption: the basis, the Declared Value of each item, the Indemnity Period for each Situation and item, and the specified working expenses
- The cyber option that applies
- The premium, with the terrorism premium, any fire services or emergency services levy, stamp duty and GST, by state or territory
- Any commission or fee paid to your broker
- The state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:isr.cond.governing-law]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
