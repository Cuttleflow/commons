heading: Tell Us About a Claim
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

When Damage happens that may lead to a claim:

- tell us as soon as reasonably practicable;
- tell the police as soon as reasonably practicable about any theft or malicious damage; and
- give us the information and documents we reasonably ask for, including, for [[ref:isr.section.business-interruption]], your accounts and records for the relevant periods.

A certificate from your auditors is accepted as evidence of the figures it covers.
