heading: Material Damage Cover
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We cover Damage to Insured Property at a Situation that happens during the Period of Insurance, from any cause this policy does not exclude.

We pay as [[ref:isr.pay.basis-of-settlement]] provides, up to the Limit of Liability and any sub-limit, after the Deductible.
