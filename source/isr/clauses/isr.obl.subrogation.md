heading: Our Rights of Recovery
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

After we pay a claim, we may take over your rights to recover the amount from anyone else, and act in your name. Help us do so, at our cost.

You may, in contracts made in the ordinary course of the Business and before the Damage, agree to limit your right to recover from others. Doing so does not affect your cover. We will not exercise rights of recovery against an entity insured under this policy, unless the loss was caused by its fraudulent, dishonest, malicious or criminal act.
