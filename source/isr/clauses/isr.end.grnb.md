When we pay to reinstate Damaged property and you choose to replace it with property that has a better environmental or energy-efficiency performance (for example solar generation, more efficient plant, water recycling or lower-carbon materials), we also pay the additional cost. The most we pay is the lesser of the percentage of the claim and the amount shown in the Schedule. Costs of complying with laws or codes are paid under XCOR, not this endorsement.

Schedule variables: percentage of claim; maximum amount.
