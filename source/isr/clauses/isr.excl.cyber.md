---
id: isr.excl.cyber
type: exclusion
heading: Cyber Events
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

In this exclusion, a computer system is any computer, device, network, software, cloud service, industrial or process control system, and the data stored in or processed by any of them, whether owned by you or anyone else. A cyber event is an unauthorised, malicious or criminal act involving a computer system, or an error, omission, failure or malfunction of a computer system.

We do not pay for loss of, damage to, or loss of use or function of data or software, however caused, except as [[ref:isr.ext.data-restoration]] provides. We do not pay for Damage or loss caused by a cyber event, except as set out in the option shown in the schedule. If the schedule shows no option, Option A applies.

- **Option A, fire and explosion.** Damage by fire or explosion that results from a cyber event is covered, and so is loss under [[ref:isr.section.business-interruption]] resulting from that Damage.
- **Option B, physical damage.** Physical Damage to property other than data or software that results from a cyber event is covered, and so is loss under [[ref:isr.section.business-interruption]] resulting from that Damage.
