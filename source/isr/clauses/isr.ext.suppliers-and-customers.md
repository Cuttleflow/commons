We cover loss under [[ref:isr.section.business-interruption]] resulting from Damage, of a kind [[ref:isr.section.material-damage]] would cover, at the premises in Australia of your suppliers and customers. The most we pay is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for suppliers and customers named in the schedule, and the lower sub-limit shown for those not named. Suppliers and customers not named means those you deal with directly, not their own suppliers or customers.
