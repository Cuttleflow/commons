The schedule shows which basis applies. Each figure is adjusted as [[ref:isr.pay.bi-adjustments]] provides.

**Gross profit basis.** We pay the Rate of Gross Profit applied to the amount by which Turnover during the Indemnity Period falls short of Standard Turnover because of the Damage. We also pay the additional expenditure you necessarily and reasonably incur to avoid or reduce that shortfall during the Indemnity Period, up to the Rate of Gross Profit applied to the shortfall it avoids. From the total we deduct any savings during the Indemnity Period in charges and expenses payable out of Gross Profit that stop or reduce because of the Damage.

**Gross revenue basis.** We pay the amount by which Turnover during the Indemnity Period falls short of Standard Turnover because of the Damage, and additional expenditure to avoid or reduce that shortfall up to the amount of the shortfall it avoids, less savings in charges and expenses that stop or reduce because of the Damage.

**Gross rentals basis.** We pay the amount by which Gross Rentals during the Indemnity Period fall short of the Gross Rentals for the corresponding period in the 12 months before the Damage, and additional expenditure to avoid or reduce that shortfall up to the amount it avoids, less savings in charges and expenses that stop or reduce because of the Damage.

**Additional increased cost of working.** We also pay reasonable additional expenditure you incur during the Indemnity Period to keep the Business running or to reduce the interruption, beyond the amounts above, up to the separate amount shown in the schedule for this item.

**Underinsurance.** The annual amount at risk is the Rate of Gross Profit applied to the Annual Turnover, the Annual Turnover, or the Gross Rentals for the 12 months before the Damage, according to the basis. Where the longest Indemnity Period is more than 12 months, the annual amount at risk is increased in proportion. If the Declared Value of the item is less than the business interruption co-insurance percentage shown in the schedule (85% if none is shown) of the annual amount at risk, we pay only the proportion of the loss under that item that the Declared Value bears to that percentage of the annual amount at risk.
