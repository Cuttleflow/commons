---
id: isr.excl.earth-movement
type: exclusion
heading: Earth Movement
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for Damage caused by earthquake, volcanic eruption, subterranean fire, subsidence, settlement, landslip, erosion, or shrinkage or expansion of the ground, unless the earth movement itself results from a cause this policy covers. [[ref:isr.end.eqke]], where the schedule shows it, adds cover for earthquake, volcanic eruption and subterranean fire.
