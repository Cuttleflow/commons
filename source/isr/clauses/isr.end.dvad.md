---
id: isr.end.dvad
type: endorsement
heading: DVAD Declared Values Adjustment (Mid-Term)
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
trigger:
  questions:
  - qs.isr.policy.situation_count
  rules:
  - when: qs.isr.policy.situation_count >= 1
    action: apply
  reason: Applies to every policy so that Declared Values can be changed during the year at the agreed rate.
---

- You may change the Declared Values for any Situation during the Period of Insurance by telling us in writing, for acquisitions, disposals, new or demolished buildings, or revaluation.
- An increase applies from the date we receive your notice, or a later date you ask for. A reduction applies from the date we receive your notice or a later date, never an earlier one.
- We adjust the premium pro rata for the rest of the Period of Insurance at the agreed rate shown in the Schedule. For a new Situation, or a Situation whose occupation or construction changes, we may quote a different rate; until we do, the agreed rate applies.
- Co-insurance is tested against the Declared Values in force at the time of the Damage.

Schedule variables: agreed rate; minimum adjustment premium.
