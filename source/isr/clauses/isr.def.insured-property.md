---
id: isr.def.insured-property
type: definition
heading: Insured Property
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

All real and personal property of every kind that you own, that you are responsible for, or that you agreed in writing before the Damage to insure, other than the property listed in [[ref:isr.excl.property-not-insured]]. It includes property you acquire during the Period of Insurance, as [[ref:isr.ext.capital-additions]] provides.
