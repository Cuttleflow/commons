heading: Interests of Other Parties
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

The interest of a mortgagee, financier, lessor or owner in Insured Property is noted, whether or not the schedule names it. Noting an interest does not make that party liable for premium and does not increase any limit. [[ref:isr.pay.who-we-pay]] says who we pay. Where the schedule shows that non-vitiation applies to a named party, [[ref:isr.end.intp]] applies.
