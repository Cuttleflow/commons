type: endorsement
heading: 'CAPA Capital Additions: Increased Limit'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
trigger:
  questions:
  - qs.isr.policy.situation_count
  rules:
  - when: qs.isr.policy.situation_count >= 1
    action: apply
  reason: Applies to every policy at the agency's standard capital additions limit.
---

Property that you buy, build or install at a Situation during the Period of Insurance is insured up to the capital additions limit shown in the Schedule for any one Situation until it is included in the Declared Values. Declare it as DVAD provides, or at the end of the Period of Insurance at the latest. We charge premium at the agreed rate from the date of the addition. Building work in progress is covered only as CNWK provides.

Schedule variables: capital additions limit per Situation.
