heading: Breakdown
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for Damage to a machine or electrical installation caused by its own mechanical or electrical breakdown or derangement. Damage to other property that results from the breakdown is covered. [[ref:isr.end.mbrk]], [[ref:isr.end.elec]] or [[ref:isr.end.fusn]], where the schedule shows them, add breakdown cover.
