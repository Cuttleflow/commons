heading: Capital Additions
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Property that you buy, build or install at a Situation during the Period of Insurance is insured up to the capital additions sub-limit shown in the schedule for any one Situation, until it is included in the Declared Values. Declare it to us by the end of the Period of Insurance. We charge premium at the rate shown in the schedule from the date of the addition.
