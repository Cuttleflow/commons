id: isr.cond.fraud
type: condition
heading: Fraudulent Claims
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If a claim is made fraudulently, we may refuse to pay it, as s.56 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows. Fraud by one insured entity does not affect the cover of another insured entity that did not take part in it or know of it.
