---
id: isr.ext.data-restoration
type: extension
heading: Data Restoration
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Where Damage covered by [[ref:isr.section.material-damage]] destroys or damages data storage media, we pay the reasonable cost of restoring data and software from back-ups or source documents, up to the data restoration sub-limit shown in the schedule. We do not pay for the value of the data itself, or for anything [[ref:isr.excl.cyber]] excludes.
