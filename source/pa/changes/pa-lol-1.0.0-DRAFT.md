This is the first release of the Meridian Aircrew Loss of Licence wording. It is built on the Meridian Personal Accident and Sickness base wording version 1.1.0-DRAFT. It differs from the base in these ways:

- The benefits are triggered by a CASA decision about the insured person's Class 1 medical certificate because of an injury or sickness. Permanent Loss of Licence pays the capital sum once; Temporary Loss of Licence pays a monthly benefit after the waiting period for up to 12 or 24 months.
- New definitions: CASA, Class 1 Medical Certificate, Licence, Monthly Benefit, Permanent Loss of Licence and Temporary Loss of Licence. Benefit Period, Capital Sum, Insured Person and Waiting Period are redefined. The weekly benefit, income, occupation class, scope of cover and disablement definitions are removed.
- The capital benefits table, weekly benefits, additional benefits, offsets, mental illness limit, accumulation and Medicare clauses are removed.
- New exclusion: Loss of Licence for Other Reasons. Alcohol and Drugs includes a failed or refused CASA test. Initial Period is 90 days for sickness. The flying exclusion is removed.
- Telling Us About a Claim and E05 Named Hazardous Pursuit are restated without the weekly benefit offsets and the flying exclusion.
- Renewal states that the policy is annual and reviewable, with a WP REVIEW tag on s.9A of the Life Insurance Act 1995 (Cth).
- Endorsements E01 to E04, E09 to E12 and E14 are removed. New endorsements: E15 Trauma Benefit, E16 Foreign Licence Trigger and E17 Cabin Crew Fitness to Fly.
