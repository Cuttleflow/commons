Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026.

- Non-Medicare Medical Expenses: the benefit is removed. It paid 85% of the cost of general treatment such as physiotherapy, chiropractic, dental treatment and ambulance services, and then excluded general treatment within the meaning of the Private Health Insurance Act 2007 (Cth), so it either did nothing or looked like health insurance. Nothing replaces it. What the Law Does Not Allow Us to Pay carries a Wording Partner question on the removal.
- Important Information: adds a statement of who the policy is for (retail clients) and a note for issuers about the Product Disclosure Statement and Target Market Determination.
- Important Information, terms that differ from standard cover: the summary now matches the operative text. The first 30 days of sickness cover exclusion no longer says the schedule may vary it, because the clause has no such variation. The pregnancy item now says that pregnancy, childbirth and miscarriage are excluded for weekly sickness benefits, and that a complication is covered only from the 26th week. The summary also now lists the deduction of weekly benefits from capital benefits, the accumulation limit, the elective treatment, alcohol and drugs, criminal and malicious acts, flying, professional sport, intentional self-injury, war and nuclear exclusions, and the 90-day limit on cover outside Australia.
- Living and Travelling Overseas: moved from the obligations section to the payment section, because it limits cover.
- One Capital Benefit for Each Injury: one rule now applies. We pay only the one event with the highest percentage for an injury, and we no longer add the percentages of Events 8 to 18. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Capital Benefits, Permanent and Permanent Total Disablement: an event that must be permanent now only needs to start within 12 months of the accident, and is assessed once 12 months have passed. This removes the conflict between the 12-month window and the 12-month test. Event 18 now uses the Fifth Edition of the American Medical Association Guides unless the schedule shows another edition. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Other Payments for the Same Disablement: the weekly benefit is now reduced only by statutory compensation schemes and by employer pay, and no longer by other personal accident, sickness or income protection insurance. The Wording Partner question on this reduction is removed.
- Other Insurance: new clause using the library's standard text. We do not refuse or reduce payment because of other insurance, and may seek contribution as the law allows. If the schedule names a corporate travel policy, this policy pays weekly and additional benefits only for what that policy does not pay, and both pay their capital benefits in full. The schedule can now name that policy.
- Alcohol and Drugs: the exclusion now applies only to the extent that the alcohol or drug caused or contributed to the injury, and we must show that it did. It no longer applies merely 'while' the person is affected, and the person no longer has to prove otherwise.
- Criminal and Malicious Acts: the exclusion now applies only to the extent that the criminal offence or malicious act caused or contributed to the injury, and acting maliciously is now defined.
- Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): replaced with the library's standard text. Section 54 now applies expressly to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation. The premium-proportion reduction for an untold change, and its Wording Partner question, are removed.
- Sanctions: replaced with the library's standard text, naming the Australian, United Nations, United Kingdom, United States and European Union regimes. A Wording Partner question is added.
- Cancellation: replaced with the library's standard text. The insured may cancel at any time for a pro-rata refund less non-refundable government charges.
