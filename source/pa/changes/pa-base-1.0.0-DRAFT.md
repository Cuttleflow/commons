This is the first release of the Meridian Personal Accident and Sickness wording, for one named insured person. It is derived from the Meridian Group Personal Accident and Sickness wording v1.0.0 (July 2026), which was published outside the Commons. Changes from the group wording:

- The insured person is the policyholder. Categories of covered persons, the policyholder's declarations and premium adjustment, automatic cover for new members and the s.48 statement are removed.
- New Important Information section: the duty to take reasonable care (s.20B) for consumer contracts and the duty of disclosure (s.21) for other contracts, a summary of the terms that differ from standard cover under s.35, a 21-day cooling-off period at inception and each renewal, and the Financial Claims Scheme notice.
- The Table of Events is aligned to the Australian individual market: 18 events, with loss of sight in one eye and loss of one limb at 100%, loss of the lens of one eye, burns at 40% of body surface, and a finger and toe scale. Permanent partial disablement is assessed by loss of whole-body function under the AMA Guides, up to 75%. Permanent and incurable insanity is removed as a capital event.
- Capital benefit events must occur within 12 months of the accident (previously 24 months).
- The weekly benefit is the lower of the scheduled weekly benefit and a percentage of income (default 85%). Income is defined separately for employees and for self-employed persons and working directors. An agreed value basis is available by Endorsement E01.
- Temporary partial disablement pays the weekly benefit less what you earn, in place of the previous 60% cap.
- One waiting period and one benefit period apply to both injury and sickness, as shown in the schedule.
- New definition of Pre-existing Condition: treatment, medication or advice in the 12 months before cover started, or symptoms a reasonable person would have been aware of in the 3 months before, with a carve-back for conditions disclosed and accepted. The exclusion now applies to injury to the extent a pre-existing condition contributed, and is expressly subject to s.47. It still stops applying after 24 months of continuous cover.
- New exclusions: first 30 days of sickness cover, hazardous pursuits (with a write-back by Endorsement E05), military service and elective treatment. Pregnancy now excludes weekly sickness benefits only, with complications covered.
- Weekly sickness benefits for a mental illness are limited to 26 weeks for any one sickness, unless the mental illness results from an injury.
- Weekly benefits stop at age 65 (age 70 by Endorsement E09). Capital benefits halve from age 70 and stop at 75.
- New Accumulation Limit for capital benefits across all policies on the same life (default $2,000,000).
- Additional benefits removed: bed care (a benefit measured by time in hospital would be health insurance business), domestic help, and unexpired membership. New: transport to work, escalation applies after 12 months, broken bones is now part of the base wording.
- New obligations: medical care and examination, telling us about changes of occupation and pursuits, and living and travelling overseas (90 days on any one trip, extendable by Endorsement E10).
- General conditions: an express Insurance Contracts Act savings clause applying s.54 to every obligation, renewal under s.58, fraud under s.56, a standalone sanctions condition naming the regimes, a fixed governing law from the schedule, and jurisdiction.
- The endorsement library has 14 endorsements, each with operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger.
- The licence is CC BY 4.0, in line with the rest of the Commons.
- Clause numbers are removed. Each clause is identified by its heading.
