type: definition
heading: Permanent Loss of Licence
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A decision by CASA, because of an injury or sickness, to cancel your Class 1 medical certificate or to refuse to issue or renew it, that you cannot reverse by any review or appeal available to you, where a medical practitioner we accept certifies that you will not meet the medical standard for a Class 1 medical certificate again. A decision to impose a condition on your certificate that stops you exercising the privileges of your licence in commercial operations is treated as a cancellation.
