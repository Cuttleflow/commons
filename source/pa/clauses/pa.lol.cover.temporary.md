heading: Temporary Loss of Licence
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If the schedule shows the monthly benefit, and you suffer an injury, or a sickness first shows itself, during the policy period that results in temporary loss of licence, we will pay you the monthly benefit after the waiting period, for as long as the temporary loss of licence continues, up to the benefit period.

The monthly benefit stops on the earliest of: the day CASA reinstates your Class 1 medical certificate or you again meet the medical standard; the end of the benefit period; and the day we pay the capital sum for permanent loss of licence.
