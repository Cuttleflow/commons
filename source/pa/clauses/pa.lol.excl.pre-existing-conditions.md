heading: Pre-existing Conditions
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay any benefit for a loss of licence caused by a pre-existing condition, unless we accepted it in writing or Endorsement E08 applies to it.

This exclusion stops applying to a pre-existing condition once you have been insured with us continuously under this policy, and the policies it renews, for 24 months, unless Endorsement E07 excludes the condition.

This exclusion is subject to s.47 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): we will not rely on it for a condition that you were not aware of, and that a reasonable person in your circumstances could not have been expected to be aware of, when you were first insured.
