heading: One Capital Benefit for Each Injury
since: 1.0.0

For any one injury, we pay only one of Events 1 to 18 in the [[ref:pa.cover.capital-benefits|heading]] table, the one with the highest percentage. We do not add the percentages of two or more events together.

Once we have paid 100% of the capital sum for one or more injuries, your cover under [[ref:pa.cover.capital-benefits]] ends. The rest of this policy continues, and we will refund the part of the premium that relates to capital benefits for the unexpired policy period.
