heading: Recurring Disablement
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If you return to work and, within 6 months, you are again disabled from the same or a related injury or sickness, we treat it as a continuation of the earlier disablement. No new waiting period applies, and the benefit period already used counts towards the benefit period.

If you have worked full time in your usual occupation for 6 consecutive months, a further disablement is a new claim.
