type: obligation
heading: Telling Us About a Claim
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after CASA, or a designated aviation medical examiner, tells you that your Class 1 medical certificate may be suspended, cancelled or not renewed, or that you should not fly.

Give us the information and documents we reasonably need to assess the claim, including the CASA decision and correspondence, your medical certificate history and your medical reports, at your own cost. For the monthly benefit, give us evidence each month that the temporary loss of licence is continuing when we reasonably ask for it.
