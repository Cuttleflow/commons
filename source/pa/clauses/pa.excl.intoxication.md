We do not pay any benefit for an injury to the extent that it was caused or contributed to by:
- you driving or being in charge of a motor vehicle with a blood alcohol concentration above the legal limit where you are driving;
- at any other time, your blood alcohol concentration being above 0.10 grams per 100 millilitres; or
- you being under the influence of a drug that a medical practitioner did not prescribe for you, or that you did not take as prescribed.

It is for us to show that the alcohol or drug caused or contributed to the injury.

We do not pay any benefit for a sickness that results from your addiction to alcohol or a drug.
