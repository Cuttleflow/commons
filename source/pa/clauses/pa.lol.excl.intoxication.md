---
id: pa.lol.excl.intoxication
type: exclusion
heading: Alcohol and Drugs
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay any benefit for a loss of licence caused by:

- the misuse of alcohol or a drug, or your dependence on alcohol or a drug; or
- a positive result in a CASA drug and alcohol test, or your refusal to take one.

We do pay for a loss of licence caused by a drug a medical practitioner prescribed for you and that you took as prescribed. It is for us to show that the exclusion applies.
