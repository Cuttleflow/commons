This policy is for the policy period shown in the schedule, which is no longer than 12 months. Each renewal is a new contract. At each renewal we may review, and change, the premium, the capital sum, the monthly benefit and the terms of this policy, for you alone or for all policyholders. Before this policy ends, we will tell you whether we offer to renew it and on what terms, as s.58 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) requires. We are not obliged to offer renewal.
