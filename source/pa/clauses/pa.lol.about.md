This policy pays benefits to a pilot whose Class 1 medical certificate is cancelled, suspended or not renewed because of an injury or sickness, so that they cannot exercise the privileges of their licence. It pays a lump sum for permanent loss of licence, and, if the schedule shows it, a monthly benefit while a temporary loss of licence lasts.

It is a benefit policy, not an indemnity policy. The lump sum and the monthly benefit are fixed amounts shown in the schedule. They do not depend on the income you lose.

It is an annual policy. Each renewal is a new contract, and we may review the premium, the benefits and the terms at each renewal (see [[ref:pa.cond.renewal]]). It is not life insurance, workers compensation or private health insurance, and it does not replace any of them. It does not pay medical expenses.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:pa.section.definitions]].

This wording is the Meridian Personal Accident and Sickness base wording with the changes needed for loss of licence cover. It is a complete wording in its own right.
