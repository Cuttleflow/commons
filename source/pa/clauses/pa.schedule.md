(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The insured person's name and date of birth, and their occupation and occupation class
- The policy period, with its start and end dates
- The scope of cover: 24 hours a day, occupational only, or non-occupational only
- The capital sum
- Whether weekly benefits apply, and if so the weekly benefit, the percentage of income it is limited to (default 85%), the waiting period and the benefit period
- Whether weekly sickness benefits apply
- The limits of the additional benefits, where they differ from the defaults in [[ref:pa.section.additional-benefits]]
- The accumulation limit, if different from the default in [[ref:pa.pay.accumulation]]
- Any pre-existing condition we have accepted, and any condition excluded by endorsement
- Any hazardous pursuit we have agreed to cover
- Any corporate travel policy that this policy is excess of, for weekly and additional benefits (see [[ref:pa.pay.other-insurance]])
- The premium, including stamp duty and GST
- The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:pa.cond.governing-law]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
