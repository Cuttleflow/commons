type: definition
heading: Temporary Loss of Licence
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A period during which, because of an injury or sickness, CASA has suspended your Class 1 medical certificate, or you do not meet the medical standard for it and a designated aviation medical examiner has told you not to exercise the privileges of your licence, so that you cannot fly in commercial operations.
