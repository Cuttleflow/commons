**You** may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired period of insurance on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. If you cancel within the cooling-off period, the refund is as [[ref:pa.important-information]] states.

**We** may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you written notice as that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis.

Cancellation does not affect a claim for an injury that happened, or a sickness that first showed itself, before cancellation.
