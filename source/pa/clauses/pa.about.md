This policy pays fixed benefits if you suffer an injury, or (if the schedule shows sickness cover) a sickness, during the policy period. It pays a lump sum for death and for the permanent disablements listed in the [[ref:pa.cover.capital-benefits|heading]] clause, and a weekly benefit while you are temporarily disabled and cannot work.

It is a benefit policy, not an indemnity policy. The capital benefits are fixed amounts that do not depend on your financial loss. The weekly benefits replace part of your income and are measured against it, as [[ref:pa.pay.weekly-benefit-amount]] sets out.

It is not workers compensation, life insurance or private health insurance, and it does not replace any of them. It cannot pay any medical expense for which a Medicare benefit is payable (see [[ref:pa.pay.medicare]]).

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:pa.section.definitions]].
