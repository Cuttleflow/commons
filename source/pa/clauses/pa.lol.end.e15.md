If, during the policy period, you are diagnosed by a specialist medical practitioner with one of the conditions listed in the schedule for this endorsement (by default: heart attack, stroke, cancer, coronary artery bypass surgery and kidney failure), as defined there, and you survive for 14 days after the diagnosis, we will pay the trauma benefit shown in the schedule (default 25% of the capital sum, up to $100,000), whether or not it causes a loss of licence. We pay the trauma benefit once. It is paid in advance of, and deducted from, any capital sum we later pay for the same condition.

[WP REVIEW: confirm a trauma benefit on diagnosis, in an annual and reviewable policy, does not make the policy a life policy or health insurance business.]

Schedule variables: listed conditions and their definitions; trauma benefit amount.
