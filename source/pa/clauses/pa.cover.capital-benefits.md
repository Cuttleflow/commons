If you suffer an injury during the policy period and within the scope of cover, and within 12 months of the accident the injury results in one of the events in this table, we will pay the percentage of the capital sum shown against that event. An event that must be permanent only needs to have started within those 12 months. We assess whether it is permanent once 12 months have passed since the accident, and may do so after the 12 months have ended.

| Event | | Percentage of the capital sum |
|---|---|---|
| Event 1 | Death | 100% |
| Event 2 | Permanent total disablement | 100% |
| Event 3 | Permanent paraplegia or quadriplegia | 100% |
| Event 4 | Permanent total loss of sight in both eyes | 100% |
| Event 5 | Permanent total loss of sight in one eye | 100% |
| Event 6 | Permanent total loss of use of two or more limbs | 100% |
| Event 7 | Permanent total loss of use of one limb | 100% |
| Event 8 | Permanent total loss of hearing in both ears | 80% |
| Event 9 | Permanent total loss of hearing in one ear | 20% |
| Event 10 | Permanent total loss of speech | 80% |
| Event 11 | Permanent total loss of the lens of one eye | 50% |
| Event 12 | Third-degree burns to 40% or more of the body surface | 50% |
| Event 13 | Permanent total loss of use of four fingers and the thumb of one hand | 75% |
| Event 14 | Permanent total loss of use of four fingers of one hand | 40% |
| Event 15 | Permanent total loss of use of a thumb: both joints / one joint | 30% / 15% |
| Event 16 | Permanent total loss of use of a finger: three joints / two joints / one joint | 15% / 10% / 5% |
| Event 17 | Permanent total loss of use of all toes of one foot / the big toe, both joints / the big toe, one joint / any other toe, each | 15% / 5% / 3% / 1% |
| Event 18 | Permanent partial disablement not listed above | The percentage of permanent loss of whole-body function, assessed under the Fifth Edition of the American Medical Association Guides to the Evaluation of Permanent Impairment (or the edition shown in the schedule), up to 75% |

In this table, loss of use of a limb includes loss of the hand or foot, and loss of a limb, hand, finger or toe includes its physical separation.
