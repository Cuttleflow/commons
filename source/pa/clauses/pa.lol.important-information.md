**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

**Who this policy is for.** This wording is written for commercial pilots, and for cabin crew if Endorsement E17 applies. A pilot who buys it for themselves is a retail client under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). The issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement. Where an airline or a pilots' association buys cover for its members, the issuer must decide whether each member is a retail client.

**Annual and reviewable.** This policy is for 12 months. It is general insurance, and not life insurance, because it is annual and we may review the premium, the benefits and the terms at each renewal. We are not obliged to renew it.

**Your duty when you apply.** What you must tell us depends on the kind of contract this is.

- If you take out this policy wholly or mainly for personal, domestic or household purposes, it is a consumer insurance contract. You have a duty under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated. Answer every question in the proposal honestly and completely, including the questions about your medical certificate and your health.
- Otherwise, you have the duty of disclosure in s.21 of that Act: you must tell us every matter you know, or that a reasonable person in your circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms, other than a matter that reduces the risk, is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we waive.

If you do not comply, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

**Terms that differ from standard cover.** The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) sets standard cover for sickness and accident contracts. This policy differs from standard cover. In particular:
- it pays only when CASA acts on your Class 1 medical certificate because of an injury or sickness, not for loss of licence for any other reason (see [[ref:pa.lol.excl.licence-actions]]);
- it does not cover a pre-existing condition in the first 24 months (see [[ref:pa.excl.pre-existing-conditions]]);
- it does not cover a condition that first shows itself in the first 90 days (see [[ref:pa.excl.initial-sickness-period]]);
- it does not cover loss of licence caused by alcohol or drug misuse, or a failed CASA drug or alcohol test (see [[ref:pa.excl.intoxication]]);
- it does not cover normal pregnancy (see [[ref:pa.excl.pregnancy]]), intentional self-injury, war or nuclear risks;
- the monthly benefit starts only after the waiting period and stops at the end of the benefit period;
- no benefit is payable for a loss of licence that starts after your 65th birthday (see [[ref:pa.pay.age-limits]]).

**Cooling-off period.** You may cancel this policy within 21 days of the date it starts, and within 21 days of each renewal, by telling us in writing. If you have not made a claim, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover. After that, you may still cancel under [[ref:pa.cond.cancellation]].

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
