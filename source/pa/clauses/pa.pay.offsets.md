heading: Other Payments for the Same Disablement
since: 1.0.0

status: draft
---

We reduce the weekly benefit by any weekly payment you receive, or are entitled to receive, for the same period of disablement from:

- a workers compensation or transport accident scheme, or any other statutory compensation scheme; and
- sick leave or other pay from your employer or your business that continues while you are disabled,

so that the total of all those payments and our weekly benefit is no more than your income, or no more than the weekly benefit if Endorsement E01 applies.

We do not reduce the weekly benefit because of any other personal accident, sickness, income protection or disability income insurance. [[ref:pa.pay.other-insurance]] explains how this policy fits with other insurance.

We do not reduce capital benefits, or the benefits in [[ref:pa.section.additional-benefits]], for any other payment.
