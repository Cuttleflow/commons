The musical instruments shown in the schedule for this endorsement are covered while used to earn an income, including at performances, rehearsals, recordings and lessons, and while in transit to and from them. [[ref:pcl.excl.business-use]] does not apply to them.

We do not pay for theft of an instrument from a performance venue while it is left unattended there and is not locked in a room or case to which only you, or the venue, hold the key.

Schedule variables: instruments covered; any special excess for them.
