since: 1.0.0

---

For any one event, after deducting the excess, the most we pay is:

- for a specified item, its agreed value, together with any amount payable under [[ref:pcl.ext.loss-in-value]] within the limit that extension sets;
- for all items in a category, the category sum insured; and
- for all categories together, the total sum insured.

The amounts payable under [[ref:pcl.ext.new-acquisitions]] and [[ref:pcl.ext.protecting-your-collection]] are in addition to these limits, up to the amounts those extensions set.
