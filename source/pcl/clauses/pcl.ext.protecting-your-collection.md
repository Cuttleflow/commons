---
id: pcl.ext.protecting-your-collection
type: extension
heading: Costs to Protect Your Collection
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We pay the reasonable costs you incur to protect your collection from imminent damage covered by this policy, or to protect it from further damage after such damage. This includes the cost of packing, moving and storing items temporarily.

The most we pay for any one event is the amount shown in the schedule for this extension (default $10,000), in addition to the total sum insured.
