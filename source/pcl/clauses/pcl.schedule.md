(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- Your name, and the name of anyone else insured; for a trust or self-managed superannuation fund, the trustee and the name of the trust or fund
- The premises, which is the address where your collection is normally kept
- The policy period, with its start and end dates
- The total sum insured, and the sum insured for each category
- Each specified item, with its description, any serial number and its agreed value
- The unspecified item limit for each category
- The excess
- The security at the premises on which the premium is based
- The name of any vault where items are kept
- The limits and periods of the extensions, where they differ from the defaults stated in them
- Any interested party, such as a financier
- The premium, including stamp duty, any emergency services levy, and GST
- Any commission or fee paid to your broker
- The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:pcl.cond.governing-law]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
