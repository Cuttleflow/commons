---
id: pcl.excl.unoccupied
type: exclusion
heading: Unoccupied Premises
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for theft or malicious damage at the premises while the premises are unoccupied for more than the number of consecutive days shown in the schedule (default 60), unless [[ref:pcl.end.e07]] applies. This exclusion does not apply to items in a vault.
