heading: Recovered Property
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If property is recovered after we have paid you for it, tell us. You may choose to buy it back from us for the amount we paid for it, less the reasonable cost of repairing any damage to it. If you do not, it becomes our property.

If property is recovered before we pay you, we pay the reasonable cost of repairing any damage to it, and of returning it to you, instead of its value.
