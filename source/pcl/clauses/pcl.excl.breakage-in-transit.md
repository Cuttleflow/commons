---
id: pcl.excl.breakage-in-transit
type: exclusion
heading: Breakage in Transit
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for breakage of glass, ceramics, sculpture or any other fragile item while it is being moved, unless it was packed and moved by a professional fine art packer or carrier, or carried by hand by you or a member of your household.
