heading: How to Make a Claim
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

1. If an item is stolen, or deliberately damaged, tell the police as soon as you can and get the report number.
2. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
3. Tell us what happened, when and where, and which items are affected.
4. Keep any damaged item, and do not have it repaired or restored, until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.
5. Give us the valuation, receipt and photograph for each item you claim for.
6. We will acknowledge your claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision as the General Insurance Code of Practice requires where the insurer subscribes to it.
