---
id: pcl.excl.deliberate-acts
type: exclusion
heading: Deliberate Acts and Household Theft
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for damage caused deliberately by you, by a member of your household, or by a person acting with your authority, or for theft by a member of your household or by a person living at the premises. [[ref:pcl.cond.joint-insureds]] applies.
