---
id: pcl.excl.work-on-items
type: exclusion
heading: Work on Items
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for damage caused by cleaning, repairing, restoring, altering, framing or reframing an item, or by any other work on it. We do pay for damage from a fire or theft that happens while the work is being done.
