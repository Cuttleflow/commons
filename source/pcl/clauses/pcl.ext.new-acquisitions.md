---
id: pcl.ext.new-acquisitions
type: extension
heading: Newly Acquired Items
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If you acquire an item during the policy period that belongs in a category shown in the schedule, it is covered automatically from the time you become responsible for it, for up to the number of days shown in the schedule for this extension (default 60).

The most we pay under this extension for all newly acquired items in a category is the percentage of the category sum insured shown in the schedule (default 25%), and for any one item the amount shown in the schedule (default $25,000). This is in addition to the category sum insured.

To keep the item covered after that time, tell us about it before the time ends and pay any additional premium we ask for. We will then add it to the schedule.
