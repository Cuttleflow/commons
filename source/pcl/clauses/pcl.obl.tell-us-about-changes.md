type: obligation
heading: Telling Us About Changes
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable if:

- the premises change;
- security shown in the schedule is removed, or is out of action for more than 7 days;
- you sell or give away a specified item, so that we can remove it from the schedule and refund the premium for it;
- an item is to be lent to a gallery, museum or exhibition; or
- an item kept in a vault is to be kept elsewhere.
