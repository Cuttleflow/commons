If any part of your collection suffers damage during the policy period, in a place where [[ref:pcl.cover.where-covered]] says it is covered, we will pay for the damage as set out in [[ref:pcl.pay.how-we-settle]].

This cover applies to damage from any cause, unless an exclusion in [[ref:pcl.section.exclusions]] applies. The most we pay is set out in [[ref:pcl.pay.most-we-pay]].
