This policy insures the jewellery, watches, art, antiques, collectables, wine, musical instruments and other valuables that you list in the schedule, for physical loss or damage from any cause that the policy does not exclude.

It is designed for collections that are worth more than a home contents policy will cover. You can hold it alongside your home contents policy, or on its own.

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Your Collection | Covers damage to your collection, where the table in [[ref:pcl.cover.where-covered]] says |
| How We Pay Claims | Sets out what we pay: the agreed value of a specified item, or the cost to repair or replace an unspecified item |
| What We Do Not Cover | The exclusions |
| What You Must Do | Your obligations before and after a loss |
| General Conditions | How the policy works, including cancellation and complaints |

The schedule lists your specified items, each with its agreed value, and the sum insured for each category of your collection. Unspecified items in a category are covered up to the unspecified item limit shown in the schedule.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:pcl.section.definitions]].
