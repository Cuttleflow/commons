Each category is covered in the places shown in this table.

| Category | Where it is covered |
|---|---|
| Jewellery and watches | Anywhere in the world |
| Jewellery and watches in a vault | In the vault, and anywhere in the world while out of the vault as [[ref:pcl.end.e02]] allows |
| Other valuables | Anywhere in the world |
| Fine art; antiques and decorative arts; collectables; wine; musical instruments | At the premises; at any other address in Australia for up to the number of consecutive days shown in the schedule (default 60); and in transit between them within Australia |

Where [[ref:pcl.end.e01]] applies, every category is covered anywhere in the world.
