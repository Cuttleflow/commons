---
id: pcl.excl.temperature
type: exclusion
heading: Temperature and Humidity
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for damage caused by changes in temperature or humidity, unless the change was itself caused by a fire, or by sudden damage to the premises. Wine spoiled by a failure of cellar temperature control is covered only where [[ref:pcl.end.e04]] applies.
