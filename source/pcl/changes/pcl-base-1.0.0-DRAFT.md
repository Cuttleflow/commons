This is the first release of the Meridian Private Collections wording (2026/PCL/0001). It was drafted for the Commons from first principles, for a Cuttleflow Go template (PCL-AU-GO). No other insurer's or market body's wording was used.

- All-risks cover for personal collections in eight categories, with specified items at agreed values and unspecified items up to a per-item limit for each category.
- Damage includes theft and disappearance that cannot be explained. This is a deliberate position, for the underwriter to confirm.
- Jewellery, watches and other valuables are covered anywhere in the world. Other categories are covered in Australia, and anywhere in the world under E01.
- No reduction for underinsurance. Pairs and sets: the reduction in value, or the whole value if the insured gives us the undamaged items.
- Loss in value after repair of fine art and antiques, within the agreed value.
- Endorsements E01 to E11, each with full operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger. E08 carries a restriction for collectables owned by a self-managed superannuation fund and kept at a related party's home.
- Three WP REVIEW tags: whether standard cover and a Key Facts Sheet apply to a standalone valuables policy; the insurer's cancellation notice; and the SMSF endorsement against the superannuation regulations. The library-wide sanctions tag is also carried.
