(Completed separately for each policy — contains your specific details)

The schedule shows:

- Who is insured (the named insured and any subsidiary)
- The policy period (start and end dates)
- Which sections are operative (marked "Included")
- The retroactive date (if any) for each section (see the Retroactive date definition)
- The prior and pending date, if different from the start of the policy period
- The limit of liability for each section, and the overall aggregate
- Any sub-limits
- The excess for each section
- The governing law (the Australian state or territory)
- The premium (including stamp duty and GST)
- Any endorsements
