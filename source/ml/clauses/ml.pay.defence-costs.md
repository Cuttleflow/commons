Defence costs are included within the limit of liability for each section. We will advance defence costs as they are incurred. If it is later established that a claim is not covered, you must repay any defence costs we have advanced for the uncovered portion.

An option to remove defence costs from the limit of liability, so that defence costs are payable in addition to the limit, is available by endorsement — see [[ref:ml.end.e02]].
