Defence costs are included within the limit of liability for each section. We will advance defence costs as they are incurred.

The person or company we paid must repay us any defence costs we advanced if:

(a) it is later established that a claim, or part of a claim, is not covered, for the uncovered portion;

(b) the company later indemnifies an insured person for the same defence costs, or another insurer pays them, for the amount so indemnified or paid; or

(c) a final adjudication establishes conduct to which an exclusion in this policy applies, as that exclusion states.

An option to remove defence costs from the limit of liability, so that defence costs are payable in addition to the limit, is available by endorsement — see [[ref:ml.end.e02]].
