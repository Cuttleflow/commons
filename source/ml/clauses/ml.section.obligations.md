This section states your obligations when a claim is made, and when you discover a Crime Loss under the Crime section. Except where the Crime section states a different timeframe or process (see [[ref:ml.cover.employee-dishonesty]] and [[ref:ml.cond.extended-discovery-period]]), the obligations below — in particular Cooperate With Us, Mitigate Your Loss, and Preserve Our Recovery Rights — apply equally to a claim and to a Crime Loss.
