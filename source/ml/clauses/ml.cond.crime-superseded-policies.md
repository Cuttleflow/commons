id: ml.cond.crime-superseded-policies
type: condition
heading: Prior Insurance and Superseded Policies — Crime
since: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

If this policy replaces a prior crime or fidelity policy, we will cover a Crime Loss that is first discovered during this policy period, even if the dishonest act occurred during the period of the prior policy, provided:

(a) the prior policy has expired and no claim has been made under it for the same Crime Loss;

(b) the Crime Loss would have been covered under the prior policy at the time of the dishonest act; and

(c) the Crime Loss is not excluded under this policy.
