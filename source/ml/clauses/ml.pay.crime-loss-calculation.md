since: 1.4.0

status: draft
---

We will calculate the Crime Loss by deducting from the amount the company has lost:

(a) any money or property recovered from the employee or any third party;

(b) any amounts the employee owes the company (whether or not related to the dishonest act); and

(c) the value of any property of the employee that the company holds.

If we pay a Crime Loss and the company later recovers any amount from the employee or any third party, the recovery is first applied to the company's uninsured loss (including the excess and any amount above the limit of liability), and the remainder is applied to reimburse us.
