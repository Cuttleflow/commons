means:

(a) any written demand for compensation made against an insured;

(b) any writ, summons, cross-claim, or other legal proceeding issued against an insured;

(c) any formal investigation, examination, or inquiry by a regulator into the conduct of an insured, where the investigation could result in personal liability or a penalty; or

(d) any extradition proceeding, or the execution of a warrant for arrest, against an insured person in connection with a wrongful act.

Claims are related if they arise from the same wrongful act, or from wrongful acts that are the same, similar, or related, or that have a common originating cause, act, omission, or set of facts or circumstances. Related claims are treated as a single claim, first made when the earliest of them was first made.
