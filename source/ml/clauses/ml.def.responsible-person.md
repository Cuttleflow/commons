---
id: ml.def.responsible-person
type: definition
heading: Responsible person
since: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

means any director, officer, partner, principal, risk manager, compliance officer, internal auditor, chief financial officer, or head of finance of the company; or, where the company has no natural person holding any of those roles, its most senior officer or manager responsible for its financial affairs at the relevant time.
