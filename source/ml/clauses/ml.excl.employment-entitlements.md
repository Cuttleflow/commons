---
id: ml.excl.employment-entitlements
type: exclusion
heading: Employment Entitlements
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

We do not cover any claim for wages, salary, overtime, leave entitlements, superannuation contributions, or any other amount that you were legally obliged to pay in the ordinary course of the employment relationship — as distinct from compensation for a wrongful act.

We cover damages for wrongful dismissal, but not underpaid wages that triggered the complaint.
