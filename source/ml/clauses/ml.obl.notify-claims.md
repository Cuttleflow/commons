---
id: ml.obl.notify-claims
type: obligation
heading: Notify Us of Claims
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Tell us in writing as soon as reasonably practicable after you become aware that a claim has been made. Provide:

(a) who is making the claim and what they are alleging;

(b) which insured is the subject of the claim;

(c) when you first became aware of the claim;

(d) details of the wrongful act involved; and

(e) your estimate of the potential value (if you can).
