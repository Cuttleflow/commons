heading: Direct Payment
since: 1.4.0
changed: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

If a claim is first made against an insured person during the policy period for a wrongful act in their capacity as a director or officer of the company, committed on or after any retroactive date shown in the schedule for this section (see [[ref:ml.cond.retroactive-date]]), and the company is legally permitted to indemnify the insured person but has not done so — for example, because the company is insolvent or has refused to indemnify — we will pay the insured person directly.
