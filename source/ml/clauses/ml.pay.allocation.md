If a claim involves both covered and uncovered matters, or both covered and uncovered parties, we will work with you to agree a fair allocation between the covered and uncovered portions. If we cannot agree, the allocation will be determined by a senior counsel agreed by both parties, or failing agreement, appointed by the President of the relevant state or territory Law Society. Until the allocation is agreed or determined, we will advance defence costs for the portion we agree is covered.
