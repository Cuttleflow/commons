---
id: ml.def.discovery
type: definition
heading: Discovery
since: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

means the point at which any responsible person first has reason to believe, on the information then available, that a Crime Loss covered under the Crime section has been or is likely to have been sustained, whether or not the amount, cause, or full circumstances of the loss are then known, and regardless of when the dishonest act occurred. **Discover** and **discovered** have matching meanings.
