If the limit of liability of a section, or the overall aggregate, is or may be insufficient to pay all amounts payable, we will pay in this order:

1. the personal liability and defence costs of insured persons under the Directors' and Officers' Liability section, where the company has not indemnified them;
2. any other amount owed to, or on behalf of, an insured person; and
3. amounts owed to, or on behalf of, the company, including reimbursement of the company under [[ref:ml.cover.company-reimbursement]], and any Crime Loss.

This protects individual directors and officers when resources are limited. If we make a payment in good faith in this order, we have no further liability for the amount paid, even if later claims mean the limit is not enough to pay a higher-priority amount in full.

A Side A Difference in Conditions extension, which gives insured persons a separate limit that pays when this policy or other insurance does not, is available by endorsement — see [[ref:ml.end.e06]].
