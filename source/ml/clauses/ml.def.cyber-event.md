---
id: ml.def.cyber-event
type: definition
heading: Cyber event
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means:

(a) any access to, or use of, a computer system by a person who has no authority to access it, or who exceeds the authority they have;
(b) any malicious code, ransomware, denial of service attack or other attack on a computer system;
(c) any failure, outage or error of a computer system; or
(d) any actual or suspected unauthorised acquisition, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of data held electronically.

A computer system is any computer, hardware, software, network, cloud service or electronic storage that is used, owned, leased or relied on by the company.
