---
id: ml.def.insured-person
type: definition
heading: Insured person
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means any natural person who is, was, or during the policy period becomes:

(a) a director (including a shadow director or de facto director) of the company;

(b) an officer of the company (including the company secretary, chief financial officer, chief executive officer, or any person who participates in making decisions that affect the whole or a substantial part of the company's business);

(c) a member of a committee of management (for associations and not-for-profits); or

(d) an equivalent position in any subsidiary.
