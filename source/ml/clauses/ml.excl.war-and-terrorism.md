---
id: ml.excl.war-and-terrorism
type: exclusion
heading: War and Terrorism
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

We do not cover any claim, loss, or liability arising from:

(a) war (whether or not declared), invasion, hostilities, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, civil unrest, or the use or usurpation of military power, or any similar belligerent act; or

(b) terrorism, as defined by any relevant Australian or foreign law.
