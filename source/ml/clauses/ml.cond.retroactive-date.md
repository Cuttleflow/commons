---
id: ml.cond.retroactive-date
type: condition
heading: Retroactive Date
since: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

If the schedule shows a retroactive date for a liability section, that section does not cover a claim arising from a wrongful act committed before that date. This applies to every insuring clause and extension in that section, whether or not the clause repeats it.

If the schedule shows no retroactive date for a section, cover under that section is not limited by a retroactive date. [[ref:ml.excl.prior-and-pending]] and [[ref:ml.excl.prior-known-matters]] still apply.

If a claim arises from a series of related wrongful acts (see the definition of Claim) and the first of them was committed before the retroactive date, all of them are treated as committed before that date.

The retroactive date does not apply to the Crime section. [[ref:ml.excl.discovered-before]], [[ref:ml.cond.crime-superseded-policies]] and [[ref:ml.cond.extended-discovery-period]] set the equivalent limits for Crime.
