since: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

We will not avoid this policy, or refuse to pay, in respect of an insured person's cover under the Directors' and Officers' Liability section (including the Side A limit under [[ref:ml.end.e06]]) because of a misrepresentation or a failure to disclose, except where that insured person personally made a fraudulent misrepresentation or fraudulently failed to disclose.

If a misrepresentation or failure to disclose by the company or by another insured person would otherwise let us reduce our liability under section 28(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), we waive that right as against an insured person who did not personally know of it or take part in it.
