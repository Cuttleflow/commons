---
id: ml.excl.professional-services
type: exclusion
heading: Professional Services
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

We do not cover any claim for errors or omissions in professional advice or services provided by the insured person to a third-party client. This is the domain of professional indemnity insurance.

However: this exclusion does not apply to the insured person's oversight, supervision, or governance of the company's professional services business. For example, if a director is sued for failing to implement adequate quality controls across the firm, that is a governance claim covered by this section.
