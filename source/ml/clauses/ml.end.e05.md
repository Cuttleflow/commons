The ASIC investigation costs sub-limit in [[ref:ml.ext.asic-investigation-costs]] is replaced by the increased sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement. The increased sub-limit is part of the Directors' and Officers' Liability section limit of liability.

The increased sub-limit also applies to an insured person's defence costs in responding to a formal investigation, examination or inquiry by any other regulator into their conduct as a director or officer of the company, even if no claim has yet been made.

Schedule variables: increased sub-limit.
