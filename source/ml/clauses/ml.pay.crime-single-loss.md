**Single loss** means all Crime Losses arising from the same act, or from a series of the same, similar, or related acts, by the same person or group of persons acting together (whether acting alone or in collusion, and whether or not every person involved is identified). A single loss is treated as one Crime Loss for the limit of liability and the excess.

Where a single loss would otherwise span two or more consecutive policy periods issued by us to the company, it is treated as discovered in, and is subject to the limit, excess and terms of, the policy period in which it was first discovered — even if the acts making up the single loss continue, or further amounts are discovered, in a later policy period. This is subject to [[ref:ml.cond.crime-superseded-policies]] where the loss began under a separate, expired prior policy.

Where a single loss falls within more than one insuring clause or endorsement in the Crime section, we will pay it once, under the one with the highest applicable limit, and only one excess applies.
