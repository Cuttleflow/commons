This endorsement adds a Side A limit of liability, shown in the schedule for this endorsement, for loss of an insured person that the company does not indemnify. It is in addition to, and does not reduce, the Directors' and Officers' Liability section limit of liability or the overall aggregate.

The Side A limit pays only after the Directors' and Officers' Liability section limit of liability, and any other directors' and officers' insurance shown in the schedule for this endorsement, have been exhausted by payment, or when that section or that other insurance does not pay the loss because:

- the company is unable to indemnify the insured person because of its financial impairment, or is not permitted by law to do so;
- the other insurer refuses or fails to pay within 60 days of a written request, unless the refusal is because of a matter that this policy also excludes; or
- the other insurance has been cancelled or avoided.

Under the Side A limit, the exclusions in the Directors' and Officers' Liability section apply only as stated in the schedule for this endorsement, and [[ref:ml.excl.insured-versus-insured]] and [[ref:ml.excl.prior-known-matters]] do not apply. No excess applies. Where we pay under the Side A limit because another insurer refused or failed to pay, we are subrogated to the insured person's rights against that insurer.

Schedule variables: Side A limit of liability; other directors' and officers' insurance; exclusions that apply.
