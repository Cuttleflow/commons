This endorsement adds a Side A limit of liability, shown in the schedule for this endorsement, for loss of an insured person that the company does not indemnify. It is in addition to, and does not reduce, the Directors' and Officers' Liability section limit of liability or the overall aggregate.

The Side A limit pays an insured person's loss, as described in [[ref:ml.cover.non-indemnifiable-loss]] and [[ref:ml.cover.direct-payment]], where the company does not indemnify the insured person because it is not permitted by law to do so, is insolvent (it cannot pay its debts as they fall due, or an administrator, liquidator, receiver or similar officer has been appointed), or refuses to do so. It pays only when:

- the Directors' and Officers' Liability section limit of liability has been exhausted by payment; or
- any other directors' and officers' insurance shown in the schedule for this endorsement has been exhausted by payment, or does not pay the loss because the other insurer is insolvent, because the other insurer refuses or fails to pay within 60 days of a written request (unless the refusal is because of a matter that this policy also excludes), or because the other insurance has been cancelled or avoided.

The exclusions in the Directors' and Officers' Liability section apply to the Side A limit, including [[ref:ml.excl.insured-versus-insured]] and [[ref:ml.excl.prior-known-matters]], and each conduct exclusion applies only after a final adjudication, as it does in that section. No excess applies. [[ref:ml.cond.non-avoidance-insured-persons]] applies to the Side A limit. Where we pay under the Side A limit because another insurer refused or failed to pay, we are subrogated to the insured person's rights against that insurer.

Schedule variables: Side A limit of liability; other directors' and officers' insurance.
