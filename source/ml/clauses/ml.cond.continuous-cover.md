since: 1.4.0

If a claim is made during this policy period and arises from a wrongful act that should have been notified under a previous management liability policy, and the insured seeking cover did not know about it at the time (for the company, no member of the control group knew about it), and the company had continuous management liability cover between the wrongful act and the start of this policy period — we will cover the claim subject to the terms and limits of this policy, as this clause qualifies them.

For that claim, the most we will pay is the lower of the limit of liability of this policy and the limit of liability of the previous policy. This policy applies to the claim on the terms of whichever of the two policies is less favourable to you.

This clause does not extend cover to a wrongful act that occurred before the retroactive date (if any) shown in the schedule for the relevant section (see [[ref:ml.cond.retroactive-date]]). This clause applies to claims under the liability sections. For the Crime section, see [[ref:ml.excl.discovered-before]] and [[ref:ml.cond.extended-discovery-period]].
