An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables (sub-limits, excesses, periods, named sections and parties) shown for it in the schedule. Where an endorsement and the rest of this policy conflict, the endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. If a variable an endorsement needs is not shown in the schedule, the endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the schedule. These endorsements are for use with version 1.5.x of this wording.

| Code | Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Extended Reporting and Discovery Period | Sets a purchased Extended Reporting Period and Extended Discovery Period of 12, 24 or 36 months |
| E02 | Defence Costs in Addition to the Limit | Pays defence costs in addition to the limit of liability, to a separate defence costs limit |
| E03 | USA and Canada Jurisdiction | Writes back the US / Canada Jurisdiction exclusion for the sections and subsidiaries named |
| E04 | Increased WHS Defence Costs Sub-limit | Increases the WHS defence costs sub-limit |
| E05 | Increased ASIC Investigation Costs Sub-limit | Increases the ASIC investigation costs sub-limit |
| E06 | Side A Difference in Conditions | Gives insured persons a separate Side A limit that drops down when other cover does not pay |
| E07 | Social Engineering Fraud | Adds social engineering fraud to the Crime section, to a sub-limit |
| E08 | Third-Party Crime | Adds theft, forgery and computer fraud by people other than employees to the Crime section |
| E09 | Not-for-Profit and Association | Extends the policy to committee members, trustees and members of a not-for-profit or association |
| E10 | Excess Layer | Writes this policy in excess of the underlying insurance named |
| E11 | Aggregate Limit Reinstatement | Reinstates the overall aggregate once in the policy period |
