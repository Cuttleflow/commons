heading: Loss
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

The amount an insured becomes legally liable to pay as compensation to a third party, including the claimant's legal costs awarded against the insured, and including defence costs. It does not include taxes or amounts that are not insurable at law. **This definition does not apply to the Crime section — see the Crime Loss definition in that section.**
