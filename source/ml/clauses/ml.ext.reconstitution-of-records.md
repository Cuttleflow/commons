If an employee destroys or alters your financial records to conceal a dishonest act, we will pay the reasonable costs of reconstructing those records.

This section can be extended to cover social engineering fraud and third-party (non-employee) dishonesty by endorsement — see [[ref:ml.end.e07]] and [[ref:ml.end.e08]].
