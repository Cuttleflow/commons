**Severability:** Each insured person is treated as if they had their own separate policy. The conduct and the knowledge of one insured person are not attributed to any other insured person when we decide cover under this policy.

This applies to every provision of this policy that turns on conduct or knowledge, including [[ref:ml.excl.deliberate-acts]], [[ref:ml.excl.personal-profit]], [[ref:ml.excl.criminal-conduct]], [[ref:ml.excl.deliberate-unlawful-employment-practices]], [[ref:ml.excl.prior-known-matters]], [[ref:ml.excl.prior-and-pending]], [[ref:ml.cond.continuous-cover]], [[ref:ml.cond.duty-of-disclosure]] and [[ref:ml.cond.non-avoidance-insured-persons]].

**The company:** What the company knew, or did, is what a member of the control group knew or did. The knowledge or conduct of any other insured person or employee is not attributed to the company.

**Non-imputation:** The company's knowledge is not attributed to an insured person. An insured person's knowledge is not attributed to the company unless that insured person is a member of the control group.
