---
id: ml.def.retroactive-date
type: definition
heading: Retroactive date
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means the date shown in the schedule (if any) for a section of this policy, before which a wrongful act is not covered under that section. If no retroactive date is shown for a section, cover under that section is not limited by a retroactive date, subject to [[ref:ml.excl.prior-and-pending]] and any section-specific prior-knowledge exclusion. **The retroactive date does not apply to the Crime section **— see [[ref:ml.excl.discovered-before]] and [[ref:ml.cond.extended-discovery-period]] for the equivalent restrictions.
