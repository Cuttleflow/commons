id: ml.def.retroactive-date
type: definition
heading: Retroactive date
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

The date shown in the schedule (if any) for a section of this policy, before which a wrongful act is not covered under that section. If no retroactive date is shown for a section, cover under that section is not limited by a retroactive date, subject to the Prior and Pending Matters exclusion and any section-specific prior-knowledge exclusion. **The retroactive date does not apply to the Crime section **— see the Discovered Before This Policy exclusion and the Extended Discovery Period clause in that section for the equivalent restrictions.
