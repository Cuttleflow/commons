id: ml.def.crime-loss
type: definition
heading: Crime Loss
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means the direct financial loss the company sustains as a direct result of a dishonest act covered under [[ref:ml.cover.employee-dishonesty]], calculated in accordance with [[ref:ml.pay.crime-loss-calculation]]. It does not include indirect or consequential loss (see [[ref:ml.excl.indirect-loss]]). **This definition applies in place of [[ref:ml.def.loss]] for the purposes of this section.** References elsewhere in this policy to "claim or Crime Loss" (for example, in [[ref:ml.def.excess]], [[ref:ml.pay.section-limits]] and [[ref:ml.pay.excess]]) include a Crime Loss under this section.
