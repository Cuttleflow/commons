id: ml.def.crime-loss
type: definition
heading: Crime Loss
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

The direct financial loss the company sustains as a direct result of a dishonest act covered under the Employee Dishonesty clause, calculated in accordance with the How We Calculate the Crime Loss clause. It does not include indirect or consequential loss (see the Indirect and Consequential Loss exclusion). **This definition applies in place of the general Loss definition in the Definitions section for the purposes of this section.** References elsewhere in this policy to "claim or Crime Loss" (for example, in the Excess definition and the Section Limits and Excess clauses) include a Crime Loss under this section.
