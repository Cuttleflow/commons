---
id: ml.cover.personal-liability
type: cover
heading: Personal Liability of Directors and Officers
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

If a claim is first made against an insured person during the policy period for a wrongful act in their capacity as a director or officer of the company, we will pay:

(a) any loss the insured person becomes personally liable to pay; and

(b) defence costs.
