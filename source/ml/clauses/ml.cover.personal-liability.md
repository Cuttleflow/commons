heading: Personal Liability of Directors and Officers
since: 1.4.0
changed: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

If a claim is first made against an insured person during the policy period for a wrongful act in their capacity as a director or officer of the company, committed on or after any retroactive date shown in the schedule for this section (see [[ref:ml.cond.retroactive-date]]), we will pay:

(a) any loss the insured person becomes personally liable to pay; and

(b) defence costs.
