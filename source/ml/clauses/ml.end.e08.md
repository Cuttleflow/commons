The Crime section is extended to cover the company's direct financial loss, discovered during the policy period, caused by any of the following committed by a person other than an employee or an insured person, acting alone or with others:

- theft of money, securities or tangible property from the company's premises or while in transit in the custody of an employee;
- forgery or fraudulent alteration of a cheque, draft, promissory note or other written instruction to pay money; and
- fraudulent entry or alteration of electronic data in the company's computer system that causes money, securities or property to be transferred, paid or delivered.

The most we will pay under this endorsement during the policy period is the third-party crime sub-limit shown in the schedule, which is part of the Crime section limit of liability. [[ref:ml.pay.crime-loss-calculation]], [[ref:ml.excl.indirect-loss]], [[ref:ml.excl.discovered-before]] and [[ref:ml.excl.trading-losses]] apply to this endorsement. Loss caused by an instruction from a person impersonating another is covered only under [[ref:ml.end.e07]].
