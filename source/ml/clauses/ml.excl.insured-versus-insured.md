---
id: ml.excl.insured-versus-insured
type: exclusion
heading: Insured Versus Insured
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

We do not cover any claim brought by one insured against another insured — except for:

(a) a claim brought by a liquidator, administrator, receiver, or similar officer appointed to the company;

(b) a claim brought by an employee under the Employment Practices Liability section;

(c) a claim brought by a whistleblower; and

(d) a cross-claim or contribution claim arising from a claim first brought by an independent third party.
