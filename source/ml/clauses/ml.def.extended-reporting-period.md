---
id: ml.def.extended-reporting-period
type: definition
heading: Extended Reporting Period
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means the period described in [[ref:ml.cond.extended-reporting-period]] in [[ref:ml.section.conditions]], during which you may notify us of a claim arising from a wrongful act that occurred before the end of the policy period, for the purposes of the liability sections. It does not extend the policy period itself, and does not apply to the Crime section — see [[ref:ml.def.extended-discovery-period]].
