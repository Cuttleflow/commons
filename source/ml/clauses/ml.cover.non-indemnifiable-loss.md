If a claim is first made against an insured person during the policy period for a wrongful act in their capacity as a director or officer of the company, committed on or after any retroactive date shown in the schedule for this section (see [[ref:ml.cond.retroactive-date]]), and the company is not legally permitted to indemnify the insured person (for example, where section 199A of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) prohibits it), we will pay the insured person directly.

This cover is for the insured person. The company's actions, and the company's financial position, do not reduce it. [[ref:ml.cond.non-avoidance-insured-persons]] applies to it.

See also [[ref:ml.cond.corporations-act-199a-199b]], which applies wherever a claim under this policy involves a civil penalty order, compensation order, or related proceeding under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth).
