The excess does not apply to:

(a) defence costs incurred by an insured person under the Directors' and Officers' Liability section where the company has not indemnified them;

(b) defence costs for WHS prosecutions under [[ref:ml.cover.whs-defence-costs]]; and

(c) the following extensions: Retired Directors and Officers; Spouse, Estate, and Legal Representative; and Employee Counselling Costs.
