---
id: ml.excl.discovered-before
type: exclusion
heading: Discovered Before This Policy
since: 1.4.0
changed: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

We do not cover any Crime Loss arising from a dishonest act that was discovered, or that a responsible person ought reasonably to have discovered, before the start of this policy period, or that was notified, or should have been notified, under any prior insurance policy.
