If a prosecution, proceeding, or enforcement action is brought against any insured during the policy period under any Australian federal, state, or territory statute in connection with the operations of the company, we will pay:

(a) insurable fines and penalties; and

(b) defence costs.

This is subject to the Uninsurable Penalties exclusion and the Corporations Act 2001 — Sections 199A and 199B clause, which restrict cover for specific proceedings, including those listed in the table below.

This covers proceedings under legislation including (but not limited to):

| Legislation | Examples of covered proceedings | Insurability position |
|---|---|---|
| Work Health and Safety Act 2011 (and state equivalents) | Prosecution for failing to ensure health and safety of workers | **Fines and penalties are not insurable in most Australian jurisdictions** — statutory bans apply in NSW, Victoria and Queensland, with the position converging nationally. Defence costs only — see Uninsurable Penalties. |
| Fair Work Act 2009 | Proceedings for breach of general protections, adverse action, sham contracting | Insurable fines and defence costs — see Uninsurable Penalties. |
| Privacy Act 1988 | Civil penalty proceedings by the OAIC | Insurable fines and defence costs — see Uninsurable Penalties. |
| Competition and Consumer Act 2010 | Civil proceedings for misleading or deceptive conduct, unconscionable conduct | Insurable fines and defence costs — see Uninsurable Penalties. |
| Corporations Act 2001 | Civil penalty proceedings for breach of directors' duties, insolvent trading | **Section 199B prohibits insuring a pecuniary penalty order or compensation order, and certain defence costs where liability is established.** Defence costs only, subject to the Corporations Act 2001 — Sections 199A and 199B clause — see Uninsurable Penalties. |
| Environment Protection Acts (state/territory) | Prosecution for breach of environmental licence conditions | Insurable fines and defence costs — see Uninsurable Penalties. |
| Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 | Civil penalty proceedings for compliance failures | Insurable fines and defence costs — see Uninsurable Penalties. |
| Tax legislation | Penalty proceedings (not the underlying tax liability) | Insurable fines and defence costs — see Uninsurable Penalties and Tax and Revenue. |
