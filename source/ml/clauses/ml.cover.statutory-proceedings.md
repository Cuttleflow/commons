If a prosecution, proceeding, or enforcement action is brought against any insured during the policy period under any Australian federal, state, or territory statute in connection with the operations of the company, in respect of conduct occurring on or after any retroactive date shown in the schedule for this section (see [[ref:ml.cond.retroactive-date]]), we will pay:

(a) fines and penalties, but only those that the law allows to be insured and that this clause does not exclude; and

(b) defence costs.

We do not pay any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured. In particular, we do not pay a penalty imposed under work health and safety law in a State or Territory whose law prohibits insurance of it (including New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland), and we do not pay a criminal fine.

This is subject to [[ref:ml.excl.uninsurable-penalties]] and [[ref:ml.cond.corporations-act-199a-199b]], which restrict cover for specific proceedings, including those listed in the table below.

The table shows what we pay for each kind of proceeding. We pay defence costs in every case, subject to the exclusions in this policy.

| Legislation | Examples of covered proceedings | What we pay |
|---|---|---|
| Work Health and Safety Act 2011 (and state and territory equivalents) | Prosecution for failing to ensure health and safety of workers | **Defence costs only.** We do not pay a penalty that the law of the relevant State or Territory prohibits insuring, including in New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland. See [[ref:ml.cover.whs-defence-costs]] and [[ref:ml.excl.uninsurable-penalties]]. |
| Fair Work Act 2009 | Proceedings for breach of general protections, adverse action, sham contracting | Defence costs. A civil penalty only to the extent the law allows it to be insured. We do not pay a criminal fine. |
| Privacy Act 1988 | Civil penalty proceedings by the OAIC | Defence costs. A civil penalty only to the extent the law allows it to be insured. |
| Competition and Consumer Act 2010 | Civil proceedings for misleading or deceptive conduct, unconscionable conduct | Defence costs. A civil penalty only to the extent the law allows it to be insured. We do not pay a criminal fine. |
| Corporations Act 2001 | Civil penalty proceedings for breach of directors' duties, insolvent trading | Defence costs, advanced and repayable as stated in [[ref:ml.cond.corporations-act-199a-199b]]. We cover a liability for a pecuniary penalty order or a compensation order only as that clause allows. |
| Environment Protection Acts (state/territory) | Prosecution for breach of environmental licence conditions | **Defence costs only.** We do not pay a criminal fine. |
| Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 | Civil penalty proceedings for compliance failures | Defence costs. A civil penalty only to the extent the law allows it to be insured. |
| Tax legislation | Penalty proceedings (not the underlying tax liability) | Defence costs. A penalty only to the extent the law allows it to be insured. We do not pay the underlying tax. See [[ref:ml.excl.tax-and-revenue]]. |
