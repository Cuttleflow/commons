---
id: ml.excl.related-party-claims
type: exclusion
heading: Related Party Claims
since: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

We do not cover any claim brought by any person or entity that holds:

(a) 15% or more of the issued shares or voting rights of the company or any parent entity (the same threshold used in [[ref:ml.excl.directors-dishonesty]]); or

(b) an equivalent executive or managerial interest — meaning a role, such as chief executive officer or an equivalent position, carrying authority to control or materially influence the company's management —

except where the claim is brought by a liquidator, administrator, or receiver of that entity.
