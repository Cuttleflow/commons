Where the Crime section pays for the loss of money, securities, or property:

(a) securities are valued at their market value on the date of Discovery or, at our election, we may instead pay the reasonable cost of a replacement in kind, or arrange a bond of indemnity;

(b) foreign-currency money is converted to Australian dollars at the exchange rate published by the Reserve Bank of Australia (or, if none is published for that currency, a comparable published rate we reasonably nominate) on the date of Discovery;

(c) where a security or currency has no readily ascertainable market value on the date of Discovery, we will determine its value using the most recent published value before that date, adjusted as reasonably necessary to reflect known intervening events; and

(d) tangible property is valued at the lowest of the cost to repair it, the cost to replace it with property of a similar kind and quality, and its actual cash value, in each case on the date of Discovery.
