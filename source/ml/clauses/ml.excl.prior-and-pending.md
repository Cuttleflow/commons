heading: Prior and Pending Matters
since: 1.4.0
changed: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

We do not cover any claim arising from any proceeding, investigation, or dispute that was pending, or any fact or circumstance that was known to the insured seeking cover (for the company, known to a member of the control group), on or before the date shown as the "prior and pending date" in the schedule (or, if not stated, the retroactive date for the relevant section, or if no retroactive date applies, the start date of this policy period).

This exclusion applies to each insured individually and does not affect cover for an insured who did not have that knowledge — the severability principle in [[ref:ml.cond.severability]] applies to this exclusion.

This is a general backstop exclusion. Where a specific section of this policy (for example, [[ref:ml.excl.prior-known-matters]]) states its own prior-knowledge test for that section, the section-specific test applies in place of this clause for claims under that section.
