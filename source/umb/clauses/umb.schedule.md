(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The named insured and ABN, and the business
- The policy period, start and end dates
- The underlying insurance: each policy by class, insurer, policy number, wording and version, period, limit for any one occurrence, any aggregate limit, and any sub-limit this policy sits excess of
- The limit of liability for any one occurrence and the aggregate limits
- The self-insured retention for any one occurrence under Coverage B
- The geographical limits, where they differ from [[ref:umb.def.geographical-limits]]
- The premium, the minimum premium, stamp duty and GST
- The state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:umb.cond.governing-law]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
