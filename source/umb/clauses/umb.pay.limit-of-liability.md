The most we will pay for loss from any one occurrence, under both coverages together, is the limit of liability.

The most we will pay in the policy period, in total, is the limit of liability:

- for all loss arising from your products; and, separately,
- for all loss under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-b]]; and, separately,
- for all other loss under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]] to the extent the underlying insurance that applies to it has an aggregate limit.

Each aggregate is reduced by every payment of loss counted in it. Defence costs paid in addition under [[ref:umb.cover.defence-costs]] do not reduce the limit of liability or any aggregate.
