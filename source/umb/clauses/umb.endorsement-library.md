An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables shown for it in the schedule. Where an endorsement and the rest of this policy conflict, the endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. If a variable an endorsement needs is not shown in the schedule, the default stated in the endorsement applies; if it has no default, the endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the schedule. These endorsements are for use with this wording at version 1.0.x.

| Code | Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | USA and Canada | Covers claims brought in the USA and Canada, and exported products, to a sub-limit |
| E02 | Principals and Others | Covers a principal or other party you must insure under a written contract, and liability you assume under that contract |
| E03 | Excess Only Basis | Removes Coverage B and the aggregate drop-down, so this policy is excess of the underlying insurance only |
| E04 | Heavy Vehicles | Applies Coverage A above heavy motor vehicle liability insurance shown as underlying insurance |
| E05 | Employers' Liability Underlying | Applies Coverage A above employers' liability or workers compensation common law insurance shown as underlying insurance |
| E06 | Increased Self-insured Retention | Applies a higher self-insured retention to Coverage B |
| E07 | Excess of Underlying Sub-limits | Applies Coverage A above the sub-limits of the underlying insurance shown in the schedule |
