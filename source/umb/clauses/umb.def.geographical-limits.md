id: umb.def.geographical-limits
type: definition
heading: Geographical Limits
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Anywhere in the world except the USA and Canada. Under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]], this policy also applies within any wider geographical limits of the underlying insurance that applies to the loss, but never in the USA and Canada unless [[ref:umb.end.e01]] applies. The schedule or an endorsement may change these limits.
