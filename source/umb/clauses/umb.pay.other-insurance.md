This policy is written as a true excess layer above the underlying insurance. It is excess of, and does not contribute with, the underlying insurance, and pays only after the retained limit is reached.

If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy that is not underlying insurance, this policy is excess of that other insurance, unless the other insurance is written specifically to be excess of this policy. Where the other insurance is written on the same basis as this policy, we contribute with the other insurer in the proportion that our limit of liability bears to the total limits.
