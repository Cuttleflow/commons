---
id: umb.excl.registered-vehicles
type: exclusion
heading: Registered Vehicles
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Personal injury or property damage arising from the ownership, use or operation by you or on your behalf of any vehicle that is registered, or is required by law to be registered, or for which compulsory third party insurance or a statutory motor accident scheme provides cover or would if the vehicle were registered.
