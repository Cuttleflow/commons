---
id: umb.excl.workers-compensation-and-employers-liability
type: exclusion
heading: Workers Compensation and Employers' Liability
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

- Any obligation under any workers compensation, accident compensation or similar law, whether or not you have insurance for it; or
- personal injury to an employee arising out of and in the course of their employment by you.

The second point does not apply under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]] to liability covered by employers' liability or workers compensation common law insurance shown as underlying insurance, where [[ref:umb.end.e05]] applies.
