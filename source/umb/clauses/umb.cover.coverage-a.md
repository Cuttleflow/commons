heading: Coverage A — Excess of the Underlying Insurance
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We will pay loss in excess of the retained limit for liability that is covered by the underlying insurance, up to the limit of liability.

Under this coverage, this policy follows the terms of the underlying insurance that applies to the loss as they are at the start of the policy period, including its insuring clauses, definitions, exclusions, conditions and claims trigger, subject to the terms of this policy. Where this policy and the underlying insurance differ, the narrower terms apply, except for the limits, the premium, the policy period and any term of this policy that is wider in [[ref:umb.cover.defence-costs]], [[ref:umb.cond.individual-treatment]] or [[ref:umb.cond.your-duty-of-disclosure]].

Where the underlying insurance has a sub-limit for a particular liability, this coverage does not apply above that sub-limit unless [[ref:umb.end.e07]] applies.
