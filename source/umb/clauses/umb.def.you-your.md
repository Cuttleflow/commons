---
id: umb.def.you-your
type: definition
heading: You, Your
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

- the named insured and its subsidiaries;
- any past, present or future director, officer, partner or principal of an entity in the first point, while acting in that capacity;
- any employee, while acting for the business;
- any person or entity that is an insured under the underlying insurance, but only under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]], only to the extent the underlying insurance covers them, and never for a higher limit than the underlying insurance gives them; and
- if the named insured dies, its legal personal representative for liability arising from the business.
