heading: Tell Us About Occurrences and Claims
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable when you become aware of:

- an occurrence or claim for which the loss, including reserves for defence costs, is likely to exceed half of the underlying limit for any one occurrence;
- an occurrence that results in death, brain or spinal injury, amputation, serious burns or loss of sight; or
- an occurrence or claim that may involve [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-b]].

A notice given to an underlying insurer under its terms is notice to us as long as we also receive a copy within a reasonable time. Notice of circumstances that may give rise to a claim has the effect provided in s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).
