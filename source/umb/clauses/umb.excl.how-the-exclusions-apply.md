since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]], the exclusions of the underlying insurance that applies to the loss also apply, and the exclusions of this policy that apply to both coverages apply even where the underlying insurance would cover the liability. Where the underlying insurance has a write-back, carve-back or sub-limit for a matter excluded by this policy, it does not extend this policy unless this policy or the schedule says so.

The knowledge or conduct of one insured is not imputed to another insured for the purpose of any exclusion, as [[ref:umb.cond.individual-treatment]] sets out.
