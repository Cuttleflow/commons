For each occurrence under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-b]], you bear the self-insured retention, which applies to compensation and defence costs together. We pay only the part of the loss that exceeds it. The self-insured retention does not apply under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]].

You may not insure the self-insured retention unless the schedule says so. If you cannot pay the self-insured retention because you are insolvent, we will not pay any part of it, but we will pay the loss above it as if you had paid it.
