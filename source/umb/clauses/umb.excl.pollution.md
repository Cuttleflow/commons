---
id: umb.excl.pollution
type: exclusion
heading: Pollution
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Personal injury or property damage, and any cost of testing, monitoring, cleaning up, removing, containing or neutralising, arising from the discharge, dispersal, release, seepage or escape of any pollutant.

This exclusion does not apply if the discharge, dispersal, release, seepage or escape is caused by a sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected event that takes place in its entirety at a specific time and place during the policy period, outside the USA and Canada. It also does not apply under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]] to gradual pollution that the underlying insurance covers, where the schedule shows that this policy sits excess of that cover.
