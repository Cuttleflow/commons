---
id: umb.excl.aircraft-and-aviation-products
type: exclusion
heading: Aircraft and Aviation Products
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

- The ownership, maintenance, operation or use by you or on your behalf of any aircraft, or of any airfield, runway or hangar; or
- any of your products that you knew, or ought reasonably to have known, would be used in or installed in an aircraft or used for the guidance, navigation or control of an aircraft.

The first point does not apply to unmanned aircraft of less than 25 kilograms operated within the law, where the underlying insurance covers the liability and this policy is under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]].
