heading: Occurrence
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

An event, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general conditions, that results in personal injury or property damage that the insured claiming cover did not expect or intend. For advertising injury, the occurrence is the publication. How occurrences are counted is set out in [[ref:umb.pay.one-occurrence]].
