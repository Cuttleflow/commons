heading: Individual Treatment of Insureds
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Each insured is treated as having a separate policy for every provision of this policy that depends on what an insured knew, expected, intended, did or failed to do. This includes [[ref:umb.cond.your-duty-of-disclosure]], the definition of [[ref:umb.def.occurrence|heading]], and [[ref:umb.excl.known-injury]].

The knowledge, conduct or failure of one insured is not imputed to any other insured. The one exception is that the knowledge or conduct of a member of the control group of an entity is imputed to that entity. Where the underlying insurance gives an insured greater protection than this condition, this policy gives the same protection to the same insured under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]].

Including more than one insured does not increase the limit of liability.
