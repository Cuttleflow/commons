---

A loss is paid under one coverage only. [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]] applies to liability that any underlying insurance covers, or would cover but for the reduction or exhaustion of its limits. [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-b]] applies only to liability that no underlying insurance covers.

Where one occurrence gives rise to liability under both coverages, each part of the loss is paid under the coverage that applies to it, the limit of liability applies once to the whole occurrence, and you bear the self-insured retention only once for the occurrence.
