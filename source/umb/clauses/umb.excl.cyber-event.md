---
id: umb.excl.cyber-event
type: exclusion
heading: Cyber Event
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Any access to or use of a computer system, network or data without the permission of its owner, any disruption, corruption or loss of a computer system, network or data, any malicious program, or the release of personal information held in a computer system, and any liability arising from any of them.
