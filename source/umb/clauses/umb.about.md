This is an umbrella liability policy. It has two coverages.

[[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]] pays loss above the underlying insurance shown in the schedule, for liability that the underlying insurance covers. It follows the terms of the underlying insurance, except where this policy says otherwise.

[[ref:umb.cover.coverage-b]] pays loss above a self-insured retention, for liability that no underlying insurance covers, on the terms of this policy alone.

This policy is written on an occurrence basis. It drops down when an aggregate limit of the underlying insurance is reduced or exhausted by payment of loss, as [[ref:umb.cover.retained-limit-and-exhaustion]] sets out. It does not drop down for any other reason, including the insolvency of an underlying insurer.

This policy, together with your schedule and any endorsements shown in it, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read it together with the underlying insurance, and tell us if anything in the schedule is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:umb.section.definitions]].

This wording is written for businesses. Some buyers, such as small businesses, may be retail clients under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). Where a buyer is a retail client, the issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.
