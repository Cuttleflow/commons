---
id: umb.excl.professional-services
type: exclusion
heading: Professional Services
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The giving of, or failure to give, professional advice or services for a fee, including design, specification, engineering, architectural, surveying, medical, legal, financial or information technology advice or services.

Under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]], this exclusion does not apply to liability that the underlying insurance covers under a carve-back or extension for incidental advice or for first aid.
