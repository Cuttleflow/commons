The business described in the schedule. It includes: owning, occupying and maintaining premises; activities incidental to the business, such as trade fairs, exhibitions, conferences, sponsorships and charitable activities; providing first aid, fire-fighting and emergency services; and social, sporting and welfare activities you organise for employees and their families.
