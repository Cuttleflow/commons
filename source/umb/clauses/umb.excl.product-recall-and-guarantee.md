type: exclusion
heading: Your Products and Work, Recall and Guarantee
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

- Property damage to your products, if the damage is caused by a defect in them or by their nature;
- the cost of repairing, replacing, reconditioning, modifying or redoing any of your products or work;
- the cost of withdrawing, recalling, inspecting, repairing or replacing any of your products, or of any property of which they form part, because of a known or suspected defect or contamination; and
- any guarantee or representation by you that your products or work will be fit for purpose or perform as represented, or any claim that they did not.

This exclusion does not apply to property damage to other property caused by your products or work.
