We pay defence costs for a claim for loss covered by this policy in addition to the limit of liability, except as this clause says.

Under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]], we do not pay defence costs that an underlying insurer pays or must pay. Once the underlying limits that apply to the claim have been exhausted, we pay defence costs as they are incurred. We may take part, at our own cost, in the defence of any claim that is likely to involve this policy.

Under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-b]], we have the right to defend the claim. Defence costs count towards the self-insured retention, and we pay them once the self-insured retention has been paid.

For a claim brought in the USA and Canada, or to enforce a judgment made there, defence costs are part of, and reduce, the limit of liability.
