type: obligation
heading: Tell Us About Changes
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable if:

- any underlying insurance is cancelled, not renewed, changed, or reduced other than by payment of loss;
- an underlying insurer becomes insolvent or enters administration or liquidation;
- the number of heavy vehicles you operate increases by more than five; or
- you begin to export products to, or carry out work in, the USA and Canada.
