This policy responds to a loss only once the retained limit has been reached for that loss.

Under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]], the retained limit is reached when the full underlying limit of each policy of underlying insurance that applies to the loss has been paid by any one or more of the underlying insurers, you, or any other insurer or person paying on your behalf. A payment counts only if it is a payment of loss that the underlying insurance would cover, disregarding any deductible or excess under it. It does not matter whether the payment was made by way of judgment, award or settlement. A settlement for less than an underlying limit does not prevent the retained limit being reached, as long as the difference is paid by you or on your behalf.

Where an aggregate underlying limit is reduced by payment of loss for personal injury or property damage that happens during the policy period, the retained limit is reduced by the amount of that payment. Where the aggregate underlying limit is exhausted by such payments, this policy responds in place of that underlying insurance for liability it would have covered, subject to the deductible or excess of that underlying insurance, which you bear. In both cases this policy covers nothing wider than the underlying insurance that was reduced or exhausted, and a payment of loss that happened before the start of the policy period does not reduce the retained limit.

Under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-b]], the retained limit is reached when you, or someone on your behalf, have paid the self-insured retention for that occurrence.
