---
id: umb.excl.pfas
type: exclusion
heading: PFAS
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Personal injury, property damage or any cost arising from per- and poly-fluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), including perfluorooctane sulfonate (PFOS), perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA) and perfluorohexane sulfonate (PFHxS), or any product or material containing them, including firefighting foam.
