heading: Fines and Penalties
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Any fine, penalty, punitive, exemplary or aggravated damages, or civil pecuniary penalty.

Under [[ref:umb.cover.coverage-a]], this exclusion does not apply to aggravated or exemplary damages that the underlying insurance covers, where the law allows them to be insured. We do not pay any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured, whatever law governs this policy, including a penalty imposed under work health and safety law in a State or Territory whose law prohibits insurance of it, or a criminal fine.
