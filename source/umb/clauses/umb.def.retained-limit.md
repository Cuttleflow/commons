---
id: umb.def.retained-limit
type: definition
heading: Retained Limit
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For each occurrence:

- for liability that is covered by the underlying insurance, the total of the limits of the underlying insurance that apply to it, as shown in the schedule, plus the limits of any other insurance available to you that covers it; and
- for liability that is not covered by the underlying insurance, the self-insured retention.

[[ref:umb.cover.retained-limit-and-exhaustion]] says when the retained limit is reached and how it changes when an aggregate limit of the underlying insurance is reduced or exhausted.
