---
id: umb.pay.one-occurrence
type: payment
heading: One Occurrence
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

All personal injury, property damage and advertising injury arising from the same, similar or related causes, or from continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general conditions, is one occurrence, however many people are injured, things damaged or claims made.

An occurrence that results in personal injury or property damage over more than one policy period is treated as happening when the personal injury or property damage first happens, and the limit of liability, retained limit and terms of the policy period in which it first happens apply to all of it.
