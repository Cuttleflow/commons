heading: Coverage B — Umbrella Cover
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We will pay loss in excess of the self-insured retention that you become legally liable to pay because of personal injury, property damage or advertising injury that:

- happens during the policy period within the geographical limits;
- is caused by an occurrence in connection with the business; and
- is not covered by any underlying insurance, and would not be covered by it if its limits had not been reduced or exhausted.

This coverage applies on the terms of this policy alone. It does not apply to liability that the underlying insurance covers, even if the underlying insurance does not pay it for a reason set out in [[ref:umb.cover.no-drop-down-for-other-reasons]].
