This is the first release of the Meridian Umbrella Liability wording. It is new to the Commons and replaces no earlier wording.

- Two coverages. Coverage A pays above the underlying insurance shown in the schedule and follows its terms. Coverage B pays above a self-insured retention for liability that no underlying insurance covers, on the terms of this policy alone.
- Occurrence basis, matching the Meridian Public and Products Liability wording, which is the usual primary policy below it.
- Drop-down only for an underlying aggregate limit reduced or exhausted by payment of loss for injury or damage that happens during the policy period. There is no drop-down for the insolvency of an underlying insurer, for cancellation of the underlying insurance, or for any other reason. Exhaustion is by payment, including payment by you.
- Defence costs are paid in addition to the limit of liability, except for claims brought in the USA and Canada. This follows the Australian market position for umbrella wordings and is a deliberate choice.
- Exclusions are in two sections: those that apply to both coverages, and those that apply to Coverage B only. Asbestos, PFAS, pollution (with a sudden-and-accidental carve-back), product recall and guarantee, professional services, USA and Canada, and workers compensation apply to both.
- Seven endorsements, each with operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger: USA and Canada, Principals and Others, Excess Only Basis, Heavy Vehicles, Employers' Liability Underlying, Increased Self-insured Retention, and Excess of Underlying Sub-limits.
- General conditions include an express Insurance Contracts Act savings clause applying s.54 to every obligation, individual treatment of insureds, fixed governing law and jurisdiction, and a standalone sanctions condition.
- Licensed CC BY 4.0. Clauses are identified by their headings, not numbers.
