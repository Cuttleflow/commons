**Limit.** The most we pay under [[ref:trd.section.payment-fraud|heading]] for all losses and costs first discovered during the policy period is $25,000, or the limit shown in the schedule.

**One loss.** All losses that arise from the same fraudulent instruction, or from fraudulent instructions sent by the same person or group of people, are one loss, and one excess applies. One loss is discovered when the first part of it is discovered, and is covered by the policy in force at that time only.

**Discovery and notice.** A loss is discovered when you, a partner or a director first becomes aware of facts that would cause a reasonable person to believe that a loss covered by this section has happened. Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable, and no later than 60 days after the policy period ends.

**Checking changed payment details.** We ask you to confirm any new or changed bank details by telephoning the person concerned on a number you held before the instruction arrived, before you pay. This is not a condition of cover. If the loss arises from a payment you made without that check, we pay 50% of the loss, unless you show that the check would not have prevented it.
