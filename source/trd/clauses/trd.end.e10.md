[[ref:trd.section.liability|heading]] does not cover the failure of a pest, termite or weed treatment to prevent or remove pests, termites or weeds, or the cost of treating them again.

You must apply chemicals as their label and the law of the state or territory require, through a person who holds the licence the law requires. Property damage caused by chemical spray drifting beyond the area being treated is covered only where [[ref:trd.excl.liability-pollution]] does not apply to it.
