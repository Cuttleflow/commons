Under [[ref:trd.section.mobile-plant|heading]], we do not pay for:

- mechanical or electrical breakdown, wear and tear, rust, corrosion or gradual deterioration, unless it results from other damage covered by this section;
- damage to tyres, tracks, buckets and other wearing parts, unless the item is damaged in the same event in other ways;
- damage while the plant is being operated by a person who does not hold a licence or competency the law requires to operate it;
- damage while the plant is underground, or in or on water, other than while being driven across a creek or ford;
- damage while the plant is used for demolition, or for work outside the business;
- damage while the plant is being driven on a public road as a vehicle, which motor insurance covers;
- plant you hire to others, with or without an operator; or
- liability under a hire agreement that makes you responsible for damage whatever its cause, to a standard beyond that of a reasonable person caring for the plant, unless you told us about the agreement before the damage.
