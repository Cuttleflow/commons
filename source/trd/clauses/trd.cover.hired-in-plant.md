If hired-in plant suffers damage during the policy period while in your care, custody or control, we will pay:

- the amount you are legally liable to pay its owner for the damage, including liability you have taken on under the hire agreement;
- the hire charges you must keep paying under the hire agreement while the plant is repaired or replaced, for up to 30 days; and
- the owner's reasonable recovery, salvage and towing costs that you are liable to pay.

The most we pay for any one item of hired-in plant for one event, including the hire charges and costs, is $50,000, or the hired-in plant limit shown in the schedule.
