---
id: trd.excl.liability-contracts
type: exclusion
heading: Liability Under Contracts
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability you have taken on under a contract or agreement, unless you would have had it without the contract or agreement, or [[ref:trd.ext.principals]] covers it, or it arises under a lease of premises, an agreement with a public authority or utility for access to a site, or a hire agreement for hired-in plant.
