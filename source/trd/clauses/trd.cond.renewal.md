Each renewal is a new contract. Before this policy ends, we will tell you whether we offer to renew it and on what terms, as s.58 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) requires. We are not obliged to offer renewal. At each renewal, give us the business's current trade, turnover, number of workers and the tools, plant and vehicles to be insured.
