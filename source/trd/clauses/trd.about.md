This policy insures a trade business: a sole trader or a small trade contractor working on other people's homes, buildings, sites and gardens. It covers the business's liability to others, its tools, its mobile plant, the works it carries out under small contracts, a tax audit, payment fraud and the time a work vehicle is off the road.

It has seven sections. [[ref:trd.section.liability|heading]] always applies. The other sections apply only if the schedule shows them.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| [[ref:trd.section.liability|heading]] | Your legal liability for injury and property damage caused by your work or your products | Occurrence during the policy period |
| [[ref:trd.section.tools|heading]] | Loss of or damage to your tools, including theft from a locked vehicle | Loss or damage during the policy period |
| [[ref:trd.section.mobile-plant|heading]] | Loss of or damage to mobile plant you own, and to mobile plant you hire in | Loss or damage during the policy period |
| [[ref:trd.section.contract-works|heading]] | Damage to the works you carry out under a construction contract up to the maximum contract value | Damage before practical completion of a contract that starts during the policy period |
| [[ref:trd.section.tax-audit|heading]] | Professional fees for a tax audit of the business | Audits first notified during the policy period |
| [[ref:trd.section.payment-fraud|heading]] | Money lost to a fraudulent payment instruction, and the cost of restoring your email and devices | Loss first discovered during the policy period |
| [[ref:trd.section.vehicle-downtime|heading]] | A daily amount while a work vehicle insured under a linked motor policy is off the road | Damage or theft during the policy period |

Motor insurance for your vehicles, and personal accident and illness insurance for you, are not part of this policy. They may be arranged as separate policies, which the schedule may show as linked to this policy. This policy does not provide workers compensation insurance or statutory home warranty insurance.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:trd.section.definitions]].
