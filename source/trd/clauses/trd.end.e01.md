This endorsement applies to welding, cutting, grinding, brazing, soldering, the use of an open flame or a heat gun, and any other process producing sparks or heat (hot work) that you carry out away from your own premises.

When you carry out hot work, you must:

- follow a hot work permit system, or the principal's own permit system where the site has one, that identifies and removes or protects combustible materials within 11 metres of the work;
- keep a suitable fire extinguisher at the place of the work; and
- keep a person on fire watch during the work and for at least 30 minutes after it ends.

If you do not, and the failure caused or contributed to the occurrence, the special excess shown for this endorsement applies to that occurrence, in addition to [[ref:trd.cond.insurance-contracts-act]]. We do not refuse a claim only because the conditions were not met.

Schedule variables: special excess (default: $5,000).
