heading: Tell Us About Changes
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us during the policy period, as soon as reasonably practicable, if:

- the business takes on a new trade or activity, or starts work at greater heights or depths, or hot work, waterproofing, roof work, gas fitting or tree work it did not do before;
- the annual turnover is expected to be more than 25% above the figure in the schedule, or the number of people working in the business reaches 20;
- you start residential building work for which the law requires statutory insurance; or
- your licence to carry on the trade is suspended, cancelled or made subject to conditions.

We will tell you whether the change affects your cover or premium.
