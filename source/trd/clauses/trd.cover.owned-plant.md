id: trd.cover.owned-plant
type: cover
heading: Plant You Own
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We will pay for damage during the policy period to the mobile plant shown in the schedule, which you own or are buying under a finance agreement, anywhere in Australia, from any cause that is not excluded, including while it is:

- at a work site, at your premises or in storage;
- being loaded, unloaded or towed, or carried on a vehicle or trailer; and
- being operated, maintained or left unattended.

The most we pay for an item is the sum insured shown for it in the schedule, and the most we pay for all owned mobile plant for one event is the owned plant sum insured shown in the schedule.
