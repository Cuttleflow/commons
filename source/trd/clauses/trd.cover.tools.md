heading: Loss of or Damage to Tools
since: 1.0.0

---

We will pay for damage to your tools that happens during the policy period anywhere in Australia, from any cause that is not excluded, including while the tools are:

- in or on a vehicle, including in a locked toolbox or compartment fixed to it;
- at a work site, at your premises or at your home; and
- being carried, used or stored.

**Limits.** The most we pay for all damage from one event is the tools sum insured shown in the schedule. The most we pay for any one item that is not specified in the schedule is $5,000, or the per item limit shown in the schedule. A set of tools, such as a socket set, or a tool with its batteries and charger, is one item. Specified items are insured for the value shown for them in the schedule.
