Under [[ref:trd.section.tax-audit|heading]], we do not pay for:

- tax, interest, penalties or fines;
- fees for preparing a return, an activity statement or other work you would have needed in any case;
- an audit that results from fraud, a false statement or a deliberate failure to lodge by you or someone acting for you; or
- an audit of which you were notified before the policy period, or which you knew was likely when this policy was entered into.
