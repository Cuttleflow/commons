heading: Vehicle Downtime Exclusions
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Under [[ref:trd.section.vehicle-downtime|heading]], we do not pay for:

- a vehicle that is not a work vehicle when the damage or theft happens;
- mechanical or electrical breakdown, wear and tear, or servicing;
- any day of delay caused by you, such as not authorising repairs, not collecting the repaired vehicle, or not giving the motor insurer what it reasonably asks for; or
- any day after the linked motor policy ends, unless the damage or theft happened while it was in force.
