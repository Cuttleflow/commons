---
id: trd.excl.cyber
type: exclusion
heading: Cyber Events
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss of, damage to or corruption of electronic data, or for loss arising from a cyber event, other than personal injury or physical property damage that results from it. This exclusion does not apply to [[ref:trd.section.payment-fraud|heading]].
