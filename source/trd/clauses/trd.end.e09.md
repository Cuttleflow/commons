[[ref:trd.section.liability|heading]] does not cover liability arising from a security, fire, gas or other alarm or detection system that you installed, serviced or monitored failing to operate, or failing to operate as intended, or from a monitoring or response service.

This endorsement does not apply to personal injury, or to property damage, caused directly by your installation or servicing work, such as drilling into a pipe or cable.
