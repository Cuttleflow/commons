type: obligation
heading: Recovery from Others
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

After we pay a claim, we may take over your rights to recover the amount from anyone else, and act in your name. Help us do so, at our cost.

Where a construction contract or hire agreement you made before the loss releases a principal or a plant owner from liability, or requires us to waive our rights against them, that does not affect your cover, and we will not exercise any right of recovery the contract or agreement has released or waived.
