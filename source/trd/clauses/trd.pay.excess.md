You pay the excess shown in the schedule for each claim, for the section that applies. An endorsement may set a special excess for the matter it describes; that excess replaces the section's excess for that matter.

**One event, one excess.** If one event gives rise to claims under more than one section of this policy, we deduct only the highest excess that applies.

**Linked policies.** If one event also gives rise to a claim under a linked motor policy or another policy issued by us and shown in the schedule as linked to this policy, and the excess under that policy is deducted, we do not deduct an excess under this policy for that event, unless ours is higher, in which case we deduct only the difference.

No excess applies to [[ref:trd.section.vehicle-downtime|heading]]; the waiting period applies instead.
