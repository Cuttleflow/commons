[[ref:trd.excl.liability-own-property]] does not apply to property damage to property of others in your care, custody or control, including the building or room in which you are working. The most we pay under this extension for all property damage arising from one occurrence is $250,000, or the amount shown in the schedule, within the liability limit.

This extension does not pay for damage to the part of any property on which you are working that results from that work, which [[ref:trd.excl.liability-own-work]] deals with, or for damage to hired-in plant, which [[ref:trd.section.mobile-plant|heading]] covers if the schedule shows it.
