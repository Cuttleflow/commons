Before you excavate, trench, bore, drive pegs or stakes, or use powered equipment to break ground, you must:

- obtain the location of underground services from Before You Dig Australia, and from the owner or occupier of the site;
- where that information shows a service within 1 metre of the work, find its exact position by hand digging, vacuum excavation or a qualified service locator before using machinery there; and
- keep a record of the information you obtained, for each site.

If you do not, the special excess shown for this endorsement applies to property damage to underground services, and to the loss of use of them, arising from that work. We do not refuse the claim only because the steps were not taken.

Schedule variables: special excess (default: $5,000).
