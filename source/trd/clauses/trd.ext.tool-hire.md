---
id: trd.ext.tool-hire
type: extension
heading: Hire of Replacement Tools
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

After damage to your tools covered by this section, we pay the reasonable cost of hiring replacement tools so that you can keep working, from the day of the damage until the tools are repaired or replaced or we pay the claim, up to $1,000 for each event, or the amount shown in the schedule. This amount is paid in addition to the tools sum insured, and no excess applies to it.
