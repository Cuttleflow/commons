id: trd.pay.one-loss-one-section
type: payment
heading: One Loss, One Section
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A loss is paid under one section only, even if more than one section could apply. Where it could be paid under more than one, we pay it under the section that gives you the higher amount, except that:

- tools are paid under [[ref:trd.section.tools|heading]] whether they are damaged at a site or in a vehicle;
- damage to contract works is paid under [[ref:trd.section.contract-works|heading]] where the schedule shows it, and not as property damage; and
- damage to hired-in plant is paid under [[ref:trd.section.mobile-plant|heading]] where the schedule shows it, and not as property damage.
