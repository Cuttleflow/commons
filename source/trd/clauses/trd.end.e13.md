The interested party shown for this endorsement, such as a financier of mobile plant, has an interest in the property shown with its name. We pay a claim for that property to you and the interested party as their interests appear, or as you and they direct in writing. An act or omission of yours does not reduce what we pay the interested party for its interest, unless the interested party knew of it and did not tell us.

Schedule variables: interested party; the property in which it has an interest.
