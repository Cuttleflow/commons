---
id: trd.ext.principals
type: extension
heading: Principals
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Where a construction contract requires you to insure a principal, the principal is insured under this section for its liability for personal injury or property damage arising from your work under that contract, to the extent the contract requires and on no wider terms than this policy provides. This extension does not insure the principal for its own negligence beyond what the contract requires you to insure, and does not increase the limit.

Where a principal is named in the schedule under [[ref:trd.end.e12]], that endorsement also applies.
