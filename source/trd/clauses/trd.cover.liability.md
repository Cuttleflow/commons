We will pay the amount you become legally liable to pay as compensation for personal injury or property damage that happens during the policy period in Australia and is caused by an occurrence in connection with the business, including by your products.

**Who is insured under this section.** You, each of your employees while working in the business, and each principal to the extent [[ref:trd.ext.principals]] provides, are insured as if each were named in the schedule.

**Limit.** The most we pay for all compensation arising from one occurrence is the liability limit shown in the schedule. For personal injury and property damage caused by your products, the liability limit is also the most we pay for all occurrences in the policy period.

**Defence costs.** In addition to the limit, we pay the reasonable legal costs we incur or approve in defending or settling a claim covered by this section, and interest on a judgment up to the date we pay or offer to pay it.

**Settlement.** We may take over and conduct the defence or settlement of any claim in your name. We may at any time pay you the limit, less any amount already paid, and then we have no further obligation for that occurrence except for defence costs already incurred.

**More than one insured.** This section applies to each insured as if a separate policy had been issued to each, but the limit is not increased.
