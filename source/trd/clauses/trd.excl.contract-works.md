heading: Contract Works Exclusions
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Under [[ref:trd.section.contract-works|heading]], we do not pay for:

- the cost of repairing, replacing or redoing any part of the contract works that is defective in design, materials or workmanship. We do pay for damage to other parts of the contract works that the defect causes;
- wear and tear, rust, corrosion, gradual deterioration, or mechanical or electrical breakdown;
- damage after practical completion, other than damage you cause while returning to fix defects;
- loss found only on a stocktake, or that cannot be explained;
- penalties, liquidated damages, delay costs or loss of profit;
- damage to existing buildings and structures, which [[ref:trd.section.liability|heading]] may cover as property damage; or
- damage to contract works caused by a cessation of work for more than 30 consecutive days, unless you protect and secure the site during the cessation.
