---
id: trd.ext.employees-tools
type: extension
heading: Employees' Tools
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Tools owned by an employee and used in the business are covered as if they were your tools, up to $2,500 for each employee for each event, within the tools sum insured. Where the employee has other insurance for the tools, this extension pays only the amount that insurance does not pay.
