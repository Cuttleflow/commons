Each principal named for this endorsement is insured under [[ref:trd.section.liability|heading]], for the construction contract shown with its name, as [[ref:trd.ext.principals]] provides. In addition, where that construction contract requires it:

- the liability limit applies to each insured separately for claims made by one insured against another, but is not increased;
- we waive our rights of recovery against the principal for a loss covered by this policy; and
- the principal is named on the certificate of currency we issue.

Where the principal's contact details are shown for this endorsement, we will tell the principal if this policy is cancelled before the end of the policy period.

Schedule variables: each principal and its construction contract; the principal's contact details.
