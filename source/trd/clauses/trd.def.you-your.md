id: trd.def.you-your
type: definition
heading: You, Your
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The person, partnership, company or trust named in the schedule as the insured. Where more than one is named, each of them. Where the insured is a trustee, the insured in that capacity. Where the insured is a partnership, each partner while acting in the business.
