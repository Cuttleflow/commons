You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. If you cancel within the cooling-off period, [[ref:trd.important-information]] says what we refund. A principal or a financier may require you to keep this insurance, so arrange replacement cover before you cancel.

We may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you written notice as that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis.

Cancellation does not affect a claim for damage, an occurrence or a loss that happened before cancellation, or a contract works claim for a construction contract that started before cancellation.
