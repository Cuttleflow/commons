This endorsement applies to waterproofing membranes you install, apply or repair, in wet areas, on balconies, on roofs or below ground.

[[ref:trd.section.liability|heading]] covers property damage caused by water escaping through such a membrane only where:

- the membrane was installed to the Australian Standard that applies to it, such as AS 3740 for wet areas inside residential buildings or AS 4654 for external membranes above ground; and
- the work was done by a person who holds any licence or registration for waterproofing that the law of the state or territory requires.

The special excess shown for this endorsement applies to each occurrence of property damage caused by water escaping through a membrane you installed, applied or repaired. The cost of redoing the membrane is not covered, as [[ref:trd.excl.liability-own-work]] states.

Schedule variables: special excess (default: $2,500).
