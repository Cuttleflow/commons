---
id: trd.excl.deliberate-acts
type: exclusion
heading: Deliberate Acts
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss, damage or liability caused deliberately by you, or by someone acting with your knowledge or consent. This exclusion does not apply to an employee's act that you did not know about or consent to, other than as [[ref:trd.excl.payment-fraud|heading]] provide.
