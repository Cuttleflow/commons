---
id: trd.def.contract-works
type: definition
heading: Contract Works
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For each construction contract:

- the permanent and temporary works you carry out, including materials, fixtures and fittings to be built into them; and
- materials and supplies for incorporation into the works while at the site, in transit to it within Australia, or stored away from it within Australia, including materials the principal supplies to you.

Contract works do not include your tools, your mobile plant, your vehicles, the existing buildings and structures into or onto which the works are built, or property of the principal other than materials supplied for the works.
