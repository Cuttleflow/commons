The most we pay under each section is the limit shown in the schedule for it:

| Section | Limit | Defence costs |
|---|---|---|
| [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]] | Any one occurrence, and for all occurrences in the policy period if an aggregate is shown | In addition to the limit |
| [[ref:fsl.cover.errors-and-omissions|heading]] | Any one claim, and for all claims first made in the policy period | Part of the limit |
| [[ref:fsl.cover.fines-and-penalties|heading]] | For all claims first made in the policy period | Part of the limit |
| [[ref:fsl.cover.customs-duty-liability|heading]] | For all claims first made in the policy period | Part of the limit |
| [[ref:fsl.cover.public-liability|heading]] | Any one occurrence, and for all occurrences in the policy period if an aggregate is shown | In addition to the limit |

Each section's limit is separate. A payment under one section does not reduce the limit of another. A sub-limit is part of, and reduces, the limit of the section it applies to. The limits apply however many insureds, claims or claimants there are.

Where defence costs are in addition to the limit, and the compensation for an occurrence exceeds the limit, we pay only the proportion of defence costs that the limit bears to the total compensation paid to settle or satisfy the claim. For a claim brought in a court in the USA or Canada, or to enforce a judgment of such a court, defence costs are part of the limit for every section.

Worked example. The Cargo Liability limit shown in the schedule is $1,000,000. A claim settles for $1,250,000 and defence costs are $100,000. We pay $1,000,000 of the compensation and four-fifths of the defence costs ($80,000), because the limit is four-fifths of the total compensation.
