We intend this policy to be a contract to which the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) applies. Some of the liabilities it covers arise in the course of carriage by sea. A court could decide that the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) applies to the part of this policy that covers those liabilities. If so, the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) would not apply to that part by force of law.

If that happens, we agree that:

- your duty of disclosure, and our remedies if it is not met, are those set out in [[ref:fsl.cond.your-duty-of-disclosure]], and not those in the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth);
- [[ref:fsl.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to that part as a term of this policy; and
- we will not rely on any right under the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth), including a term that Act implies, that is greater than the right we would have if the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) applied.
