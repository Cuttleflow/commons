The following extensions are included automatically. Each is subject to the terms of this policy and to the limit of the section it extends. A sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, that limit. The default sub-limits are in [[ref:fsl.pay.sub-limits]]; a different amount shown in the schedule replaces the default.
