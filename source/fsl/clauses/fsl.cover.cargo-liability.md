We will pay the compensation you become legally liable to pay for physical loss of or damage to goods, and for delay in delivering goods, that:

- happens during the policy period, wherever the goods are; and
- is caused by an occurrence in the course of the freight services.

This includes your legal liability:

- as agent, bailee, carrier, contracting carrier under a bill of lading, air waybill or consignment note you issue, warehouse operator or customs broker;
- for the acts and omissions of your subcontractors;
- for loss of goods by theft, including theft by your employee or a subcontractor; and
- for loss of goods delivered to the wrong person, or released to a person who obtained them by deception.

Liability for delay is covered only to the extent your standard trading conditions, or a law that overrides them, make you liable for it.

[[ref:fsl.pay.trading-conditions]] sets the most we pay. We also pay defence costs and supplementary payments, as set out in [[ref:fsl.cover.defence-costs]] and [[ref:fsl.cover.supplementary-payments]].
