Tell us within 30 days if, during the policy period:

- you start to provide a kind of freight services not shown in the schedule, such as issuing your own bills of lading, operating your own vehicles or operating a warehouse;
- you open an office, warehouse or depot outside Australia;
- a warehouse or depot you operate becomes licensed under the Customs Act 1901 (Cth);
- a customs broker licence held by you or your nominee is suspended or cancelled, or a new condition is imposed on it; or
- you change your standard trading conditions.

We may then change the premium or terms for the rest of the policy period, as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.
