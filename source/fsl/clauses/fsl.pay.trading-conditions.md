This provision sets the most we pay under [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]] and [[ref:fsl.cover.errors-and-omissions|heading]] for a claim by a customer, or by anyone who claims through a customer or through the contract under which you provided the freight services.

**The measure.** We pay the compensation you are legally liable to pay, but not more than you would have been liable to pay if your standard trading conditions had formed part of the contract, as they take effect subject to any law that the parties cannot exclude by contract. This includes their limits of liability, exclusions and time limits.

**Where your conditions did not form part of the contract.** If your standard trading conditions did not form part of the contract, for example because you did not refer to them or give them to the customer before the contract was made, we still pay the claim, but only up to the amount in the measure above. We do not refuse the claim because the conditions did not apply.

**Where a law overrides your conditions.** If a law that the parties cannot exclude by contract makes you liable for more than your standard trading conditions allow, the measure includes that greater liability. Examples are:

- the amended Hague Rules as they have effect under the Carriage of Goods by Sea Act 1991 (Cth), or another sea carriage regime that applies by law;
- the Montreal Convention or another convention given effect by the Civil Aviation (Carriers' Liability) Act 1959 (Cth);
- a convention or law that governs international road or rail carriage in another country;
- the unfair contract terms provisions of the Australian Consumer Law or the Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001 (Cth), where a court or tribunal decides that a term of your standard trading conditions is unfair and void; and
- the consumer guarantees in the Australian Consumer Law.

**Contracts on other terms.** If you agreed in writing to terms that make you liable for more than your standard trading conditions, such as a customer's own contract, a higher limit per kilogram or an indemnity, we cover the extra liability only if the contract is named in the schedule under Endorsement E01, and only as far as that endorsement allows.

**Declared value.** If a customer declared a value for the goods, or paid you for a higher limit of liability, under your standard trading conditions, the measure includes that higher liability, up to the Cargo Liability limit, if the declared value or higher limit is recorded in your documents for the consignment.
