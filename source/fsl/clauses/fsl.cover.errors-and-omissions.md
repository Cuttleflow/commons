If a claim is first made against you during the policy period, and you tell us about it during the policy period or within the time allowed by [[ref:fsl.ext.extended-reporting-period]], we will pay the compensation you become legally liable to pay for financial loss caused by an error committed on or after the retroactive date.

This section covers financial loss that is not physical loss of or damage to goods and is not caused by delay in delivering goods. Examples are loss caused by:

- sending goods to the wrong destination, so that they must be re-forwarded;
- an error in a document, including a bill of lading, air waybill, certificate of origin, permit, or a document presented under a letter of credit;
- failing to follow a customer's written instructions;
- an error in a customs entry, including a wrong tariff classification, value or origin, or failing to claim a concession, refund or drawback the customer was entitled to; and
- failing to arrange insurance of goods when a customer instructed you in writing to arrange it, or arranging it on terms other than those instructed.

For a failure to arrange insurance, we pay no more than the insurance you were instructed to arrange would have paid.

[[ref:fsl.pay.trading-conditions]] sets the most we pay. We also pay defence costs, as set out in [[ref:fsl.cover.defence-costs]].
