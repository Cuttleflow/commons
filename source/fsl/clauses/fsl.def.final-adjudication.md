---
id: fsl.def.final-adjudication
type: definition
heading: Final Adjudication
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A judgment, decision or ruling of a court, tribunal or arbitrator of competent jurisdiction, after all appeals or reviews have been decided or the time to appeal or seek review has passed without one being filed, that finally determines the relevant conduct. A written admission of the conduct by the insured concerned has the same effect. A consent order, settlement or compromise that does not include a determination or admission of the conduct is not a final adjudication.
