**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

[WP REVIEW: the General Insurance Code of Practice does not apply to insurance to which the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) applies. Confirm the Code position for this policy in light of [[ref:fsl.cond.statutory-framework]].]

**Who is insured.** The insureds are listed in [[ref:fsl.def.you-your]].

**Your duty when you apply.** [[ref:fsl.cond.your-duty-of-disclosure]] sets out what you must tell us before this policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated. [[ref:fsl.cond.statutory-framework]] explains which Act governs this policy.

**What to tell us during the policy period.** [[ref:fsl.obl.tell-us-about-changes]] lists the changes you must tell us about, such as starting to issue your own bills of lading or opening a warehouse.

**Who this policy is for.** This wording is written for businesses that provide freight services to other businesses. It is intended for distribution to wholesale clients. Freight services liability insurance is not one of the classes of general insurance listed in section 761G(5) of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), so a buyer is not a retail client for it only because the buyer is a small business. This wording is not a Product Disclosure Statement.

[WP REVIEW: confirm that freight services liability insurance is not a retail product under s.761G(5) of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) and the Corporations Regulations, including where the insured is a small business or a sole trader.]

**Commission and fees.** The schedule or the quote shows any commission or fee paid to your broker in connection with this policy.

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
