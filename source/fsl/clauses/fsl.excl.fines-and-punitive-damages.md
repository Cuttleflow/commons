For [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]], [[ref:fsl.cover.errors-and-omissions|heading]] and [[ref:fsl.cover.public-liability|heading]]: fines, penalties, civil penalties, infringement notices, punitive, exemplary or aggravated damages, and the multiple part of a multiplied damages award. Fines and penalties imposed on you are covered only under [[ref:fsl.cover.fines-and-penalties|heading]].

This exclusion does not apply to compensation you are legally liable to pay a customer for a fine or penalty imposed on the customer because of your error, where the law allows that compensation to be insured.
