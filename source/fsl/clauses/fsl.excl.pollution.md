---
id: fsl.excl.pollution
type: exclusion
heading: Pollution
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

- For [[ref:fsl.cover.public-liability|heading]]: personal injury or property damage arising from the discharge, dispersal, release, seepage or escape of any pollutant; and
- for every section: any cost of testing, monitoring, cleaning up, removing, containing or neutralising a pollutant.

This exclusion does not apply:

- if the discharge, dispersal, release, seepage or escape is caused by a sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected event that takes place in its entirety at a specific time and place during the policy period;
- to costs covered by [[ref:fsl.ext.pollution-clean-up]]; or
- to physical loss of or damage to goods, including contamination of goods by a pollutant, which [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]] covers.
