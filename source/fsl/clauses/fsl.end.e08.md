[[ref:fsl.excl.usa-and-canada]] does not apply to the freight services you provide from the offices, warehouses and depots in the United States of America or Canada named in the schedule for this endorsement.

If the schedule shows it for this endorsement, the exclusion also does not apply to claims under [[ref:fsl.cover.public-liability|heading]] brought in a court in the United States of America or Canada that arise from those freight services. Defence costs for those claims are part of the limit, as [[ref:fsl.pay.limits]] provides.

Schedule variables: the locations; whether Public Liability claims brought in United States or Canadian courts are covered; any special excess.
