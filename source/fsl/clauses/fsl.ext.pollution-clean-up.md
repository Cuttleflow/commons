---
id: fsl.ext.pollution-clean-up
type: extension
heading: Cargo Pollution Clean-up
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If goods escape, spill or leak from their packaging or container during the policy period, because of an occurrence that [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]] covers, we pay the costs you are legally liable to pay, or that an authority requires you to pay, to clean up, remove, contain or neutralise the goods and any pollutant released from them. The most we pay for any one occurrence, and for all occurrences in the policy period, is the sub-limit for this extension.

This extension applies despite [[ref:fsl.excl.pollution]].
