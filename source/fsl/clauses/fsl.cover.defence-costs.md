For a claim this policy may cover, we have the right to take over and conduct the defence or settlement of the claim in your name. For a claim under [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]] or [[ref:fsl.cover.public-liability|heading]], we also have the duty to defend it, even if it is groundless, false or fraudulent. We will choose the lawyers and manage the defence, and we will consult you about strategy.

We pay defence costs as they are incurred and do not wait until the claim is resolved. [[ref:fsl.pay.limits]] says whether defence costs are in addition to, or part of, the limit.

We will not settle a claim under [[ref:fsl.cover.errors-and-omissions|heading]], [[ref:fsl.cover.fines-and-penalties|heading]] or [[ref:fsl.cover.customs-duty-liability|heading]] without your consent, which you will not unreasonably withhold. If you do not consent to a settlement that we recommend and the claimant or authority would accept, the most we pay for that claim is the amount of that settlement and the defence costs incurred up to the date you refused.
