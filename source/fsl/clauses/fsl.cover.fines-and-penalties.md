If an authority first gives you notice of a fine or penalty during the policy period, and you tell us about it during the policy period or within the time allowed by [[ref:fsl.ext.extended-reporting-period]], we will pay:

- the fine or penalty imposed on you, including an infringement notice or administrative penalty, because of an error committed on or after the retroactive date; and
- defence costs.

We do not pay:

- a fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured, including a penalty under work health and safety law in a state or territory whose law prohibits insurance of it (including New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland), and any criminal fine;
- a fine or penalty imposed for conduct that you, or a member of your control group, knew was a breach of the law when it took place, as established by final adjudication;
- a fine or penalty for paying duty, tax or other charges late, where you held the money to pay them on time;
- a fine or penalty for a road traffic, parking or speeding offence committed in driving a vehicle;
- duty, tax or other charges, which are covered only under [[ref:fsl.cover.customs-duty-liability|heading]] if it is operative; or
- the cost of complying with any order, condition or direction.
