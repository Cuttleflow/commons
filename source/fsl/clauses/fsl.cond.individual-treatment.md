heading: Individual Treatment of Insureds
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Each insured is treated as having a separate policy for every provision that depends on what an insured knew, expected, intended, did or failed to do. This includes:

- [[ref:fsl.cond.your-duty-of-disclosure]];
- the definition of [[ref:fsl.def.occurrence|heading]], the test of what the insured claiming cover did not expect or intend; and
- [[ref:fsl.excl.dishonest-acts]] and [[ref:fsl.excl.known-claims-and-circumstances]].

The knowledge, conduct or failure of one insured is not imputed to any other insured. The one exception is that the knowledge or conduct of a member of the control group of an entity is imputed to that entity.
