---
id: fsl.excl.contractual-liability
type: exclusion
heading: Contractual Liability
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Liability that you assume under a contract or agreement, to the extent it is greater than the liability you would have had under your standard trading conditions, as [[ref:fsl.pay.trading-conditions]] measures it. This includes liability under a promise of delivery by a set time, an agreed or liquidated damages clause, a service credit, an indemnity or a hold-harmless agreement.

This exclusion does not apply to:

- liability you would have had without the contract or agreement; or
- liability under a contract named in the schedule under Endorsement E01, to the extent that endorsement covers it.
