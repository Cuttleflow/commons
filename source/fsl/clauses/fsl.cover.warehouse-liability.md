heading: Warehouse and Bailee Liability
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

This section applies only if the schedule shows it as operative.

We will pay the compensation you become legally liable to pay, as bailee or warehouse operator, for physical loss of or damage to goods in warehouse storage at a warehouse or depot named in the schedule, that:

- happens during the policy period; and
- is caused by an occurrence in the course of the freight services.

[[ref:fsl.pay.trading-conditions]] sets the most we pay. The limit for this section applies to each warehouse or depot separately, as [[ref:fsl.pay.limits]] says. We also pay defence costs and supplementary payments, as set out in [[ref:fsl.cover.defence-costs]] and [[ref:fsl.cover.supplementary-payments]].

Where this section is operative, goods in warehouse storage are covered under this section and not under [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]]. Where it is not operative, [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]] continues to cover them.
