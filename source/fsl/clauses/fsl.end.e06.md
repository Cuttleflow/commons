We pay the duty, goods and services tax and other charges that you become legally liable to pay to the Commonwealth because goods under customs control were lost, stolen or destroyed during the policy period while in your custody, including in a warehouse or depot you operate under a licence granted under the Customs Act 1901 (Cth). We pay only the amount you cannot recover from the owner of the goods, or from anyone else, after taking reasonable steps to recover it.

The most we pay for any one occurrence, and for all occurrences in the policy period, is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement. It is part of the [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]] limit. This endorsement adds this cover to Cargo Liability, despite [[ref:fsl.pay.which-section-responds]]. It does not cover any fine or penalty.

[WP REVIEW: confirm the liability of a licence holder, or a person with custody of goods under customs control, for duty on goods that are lost or stolen, including under s.35A of the Customs Act 1901 (Cth), and that it may be insured.]

Schedule variables: sub-limit any one occurrence and in the aggregate; the licensed premises.
