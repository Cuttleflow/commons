---
id: fsl.excl.property-you-own-or-hire
type: exclusion
heading: Property You Own, Hire or Lease
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Physical loss of or damage to property that you own, hire, lease or borrow, or that is in your care for your own use, including vehicles, vessels, aircraft, trailers, containers, pallets, handling equipment and premises and their contents. This exclusion does not apply to goods. Containers and other equipment that you take into your possession from carriers or lessors are covered only under Endorsement E05.
