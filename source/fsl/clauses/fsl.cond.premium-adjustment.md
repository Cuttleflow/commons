since: 1.0.0

---

If the schedule shows that the premium is adjustable, the premium is a deposit based on the gross receipts you estimated for the policy period. Within 60 days after the policy period ends, tell us your actual gross receipts for it. We will then calculate the premium at the rate shown in the schedule. If that premium is higher than the deposit, you pay us the difference. If it is lower, we refund the difference, but the premium will not be less than the minimum premium shown in the schedule.
