**Occurrences.** All physical loss of or damage to goods, delay, personal injury and property damage attributable to one source or original cause is one occurrence, whenever it happens and however many consignments, customers or people are affected. If the first loss, damage, delay or injury from an occurrence happens during the policy period, the whole occurrence is treated as happening in that policy period, and only the limit, excess and terms of that policy period apply to it.

**Claims.** All claims arising from the same error, or from errors that have the same source or original cause, are one claim. It is treated as first made when the earliest of them was first made, and only the limit, excess and terms of the policy period in which it was first made apply to it.
