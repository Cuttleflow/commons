heading: Paying Out the Limit
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

At any time, we may pay you the amount of the limit that remains for an occurrence or claim, less any amount already paid, together with the defence costs and supplementary payments incurred to that date. When we do, we have no further liability for that occurrence or claim, including for defence costs incurred after that date. We will consult you before doing this and will give you reasonable time to take over the defence.
