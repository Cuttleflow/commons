[[ref:fsl.excl.property-you-own-or-hire]] does not apply to containers, trailers, flat racks, pallets and other equipment that a carrier or lessor provides and that you take into your possession under an equipment interchange agreement or a similar arrangement. We cover your legal liability for physical loss of or damage to that equipment as if it were goods, up to the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement for any one occurrence. The sub-limit is part of the [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]] limit.

We do not cover wear and tear, gradual deterioration, mechanical breakdown, or detention, demurrage, per diem or hire charges.

Schedule variables: sub-limit any one occurrence; any special excess.
