heading: Claim
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

- A written demand for compensation made against you; or
- a writ, summons, cross-claim, arbitration or other proceeding in which compensation is sought from you.

For [[ref:fsl.cover.fines-and-penalties|heading]] and [[ref:fsl.cover.customs-duty-liability|heading]], a claim is a written notice from an authority that imposes, or states an intention to impose, a fine or penalty on you, or that demands duty, tax or charges from you. It is first made when you first receive it.
