heading: How to Make a Claim
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

1. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
2. Give us written details of the loss, claim or notice, and copies of the documents for the consignment: booking, consignment note or bill of lading, invoices, delivery receipts and the trading conditions that applied (see [[ref:fsl.obl.tell-us-about-claims]]).
3. Give written notice to any carrier, warehouse or other subcontractor who may be responsible, within the time their contract allows (see [[ref:fsl.obl.protect-recovery-rights]]).
4. Keep the damaged goods, packaging and records available for inspection. We may appoint a surveyor.
5. Do not admit liability or agree to any settlement without our written consent (see [[ref:fsl.obl.do-not-admit-liability]]).
6. We will acknowledge your notification, assign a claims manager and keep you informed.
