---
id: fsl.def.you-your
type: definition
heading: You, Your
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

- The named insured and its subsidiaries;
- any past, present or future director, officer, partner or principal of an entity in the first point, while acting in that capacity;
- any employee, while acting for the named insured or a subsidiary in the freight services; and
- if the named insured dies, its legal personal representative, for liability arising from the freight services.
