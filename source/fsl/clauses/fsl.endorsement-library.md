An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables (sub-limits, excesses, named contracts, locations and procedures) shown for it in the schedule. Where an endorsement and the rest of this policy conflict, the endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. If a variable an endorsement needs is not shown in the schedule, the default stated in the endorsement applies; if it has no default, the endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the schedule.

| Code | Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Scheduled Customer Contracts | Covers liability under the customer contracts named, beyond your standard trading conditions |
| E02 | Special Cargo Conditions | Sets handling conditions for dangerous, temperature-controlled and high-value goods |
| E03 | Warehouse Security Conditions | Sets alarm and fire protection conditions for goods in your warehouses and depots |
| E04 | Customs Broker Licence Investigation Costs | Covers the costs of responding to an investigation of a customs broker licence, to a sub-limit |
| E05 | Containers and Equipment | Covers liability for containers and equipment provided by carriers and lessors, to a sub-limit |
| E06 | Duty on Goods in Your Custody | Covers duty you become liable for when goods under customs control are lost from your custody, to a sub-limit |
| E07 | Release Fraud Sub-limit | Applies a sub-limit to loss of goods released to a person who obtained them by deception |
| E08 | USA and Canada Operations | Writes back the USA and Canada exclusion for the locations named |
