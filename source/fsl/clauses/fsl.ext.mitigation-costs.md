id: fsl.ext.mitigation-costs
type: extension
heading: Costs of Preventing or Reducing Loss
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If, during the policy period, something happens in the course of the freight services that has caused, or is about to cause, a loss for which you would be legally liable under [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]] or [[ref:fsl.cover.errors-and-omissions|heading]], we pay the reasonable costs you incur to prevent or reduce that loss. Examples are re-packing or re-conditioning goods, moving them to another conveyance or warehouse, re-forwarding goods sent to the wrong destination, and survey fees.

You must ask for our consent before incurring the costs, unless it is not practicable to do so in the time available. We do not pay more than the amount we would have paid for the loss the costs were incurred to prevent or reduce. The most we pay is the sub-limit for this extension.

This extension applies despite [[ref:fsl.excl.your-fees-and-charges]].
