We pay the reasonable costs you incur, with our consent, to respond to an investigation, inquiry, show-cause notice or disciplinary proceeding by the Australian Border Force, the Comptroller-General of Customs or the National Customs Brokers Licensing Advisory Committee about a customs broker licence held by you or by your nominee. The investigation must first be notified to you during the policy period and must arise from customs broking you performed on or after the retroactive date.

The most we pay for all investigations in the policy period is $50,000 or the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement. These costs are part of, and reduce, the [[ref:fsl.cover.errors-and-omissions|heading]] limit.

We do not pay a fine, a penalty or the cost of complying with any condition, order or direction.

Schedule variables: sub-limit (default $50,000, as shown in the schedule).
