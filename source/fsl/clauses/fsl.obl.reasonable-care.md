---
id: fsl.obl.reasonable-care
type: obligation
heading: Reasonable Care
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Take reasonable care in choosing subcontractors and in handling, storing, securing and documenting goods, and comply with the laws that apply to the freight services, including laws about customs, biosecurity, dangerous goods and road transport. We will rely on this obligation only if you acted recklessly, meaning you recognised a danger and deliberately chose not to take, or did not care whether you took, measures to avert it.
