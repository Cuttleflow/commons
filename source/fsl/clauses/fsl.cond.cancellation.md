**You** may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges.

**We** may cancel this policy only on a ground the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you at least 30 days' written notice. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis.

If the premium is adjustable, we adjust it on your actual gross receipts up to the date of cancellation, subject to the minimum premium. Cancellation does not affect a claim, occurrence or circumstance you told us about before the cancellation took effect.
