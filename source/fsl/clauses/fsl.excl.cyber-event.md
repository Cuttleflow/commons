---
id: fsl.excl.cyber-event
type: exclusion
heading: Cyber Event
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Any loss, liability or cost arising from access to or use of a computer system or electronic data by a person without authority, from malicious code, or from the corruption, deletion or unavailability of a computer system or electronic data.

This exclusion does not apply to:

- physical loss of or damage to goods, including loss of goods released to a person who used a computer system or electronic communication to deceive you or a subcontractor; or
- personal injury, or property damage to tangible property other than electronic data, caused directly by such an event.
