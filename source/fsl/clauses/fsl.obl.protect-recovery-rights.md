type: obligation
heading: Protect Rights Against Carriers and Others
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

When goods are lost, damaged or delayed, or a claim is made against you, take reasonable steps to protect the right to recover from any subcontractor, carrier, warehouse operator or other person who may be responsible. This includes:

- giving them written notice of the loss, damage or delay, or of the claim, within the time their contract or the law requires (for sea carriage, notice can be due within days of delivery, and the time to start proceedings can be one year or less);
- asking for a joint survey where one is available; and
- not agreeing to release or limit anyone's liability, except under your standard trading conditions, a written contract made before the loss, or [[ref:fsl.cond.subrogation-waiver]].

If we pay a claim, we may take over your rights of recovery and act in your name, and you must help us.
