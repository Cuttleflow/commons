id: fsl.cond.subrogation-waiver
type: condition
heading: Waiver of Subrogation
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We waive our rights of recovery against any person or entity who is an insured under this policy, except to the extent that another insurance policy covers that person or entity for the liability. We also waive our rights of recovery against a customer where, before the occurrence or error, you agreed in writing to waive your rights against that customer.

We do not waive our rights of recovery against a subcontractor, unless the schedule says so.
