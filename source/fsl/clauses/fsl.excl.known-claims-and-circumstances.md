For [[ref:fsl.cover.errors-and-omissions|heading]], [[ref:fsl.cover.fines-and-penalties|heading]] and [[ref:fsl.cover.customs-duty-liability|heading]]:

- a claim first made before the policy period, or a proceeding started before it;
- facts that you told an insurer about under an earlier policy; and
- facts that a member of your control group knew about before the policy period began, and that a reasonable person in that position would have expected might give rise to a claim.

For [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]] and [[ref:fsl.cover.public-liability|heading]]: loss, damage, delay or injury that began before the policy period and that a member of your control group knew about before it began.

This exclusion does not apply if you told us about the matter before the policy period began and we agreed in writing to cover it, or to a claim covered by [[ref:fsl.ext.continuous-cover]].
