heading: Theft From Unattended Vehicles
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]]: theft of goods from a vehicle, trailer or container while no one is in or on it, or keeping it under observation.

This exclusion does not apply if, at the time of the theft:

- all doors and other openings were locked and the keys removed; or
- the vehicle, trailer or container was inside a locked building, or in a yard enclosed by a locked fence or gate.
