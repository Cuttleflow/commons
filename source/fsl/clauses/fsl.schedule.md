(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The named insured, ABN, and any subsidiaries named
- The freight services insured, the share of gross receipts from each, and any country other than Australia from which you provide them
- The standard trading conditions, by name and version
- The policy period, start and end dates
- Which sections are operative
- The limit for each operative section, and any aggregate limit for Cargo Liability or Public Liability
- Sub-limits that differ from the defaults in [[ref:fsl.pay.sub-limits]]
- The excess for each section, and any special excess
- The retroactive date
- Any goods added to, or removed from, the excluded goods
- The declared gross receipts, the rate, the deposit and minimum premium, and whether the premium is adjustable, including stamp duty and GST
- The premium for a 12-month extended reporting period, if offered
- The state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:fsl.cond.governing-law]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables (sub-limits, excesses, named contracts, locations and procedures)
- Interested parties to be noted
