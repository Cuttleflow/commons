---
id: fsl.def.occurrence
type: definition
heading: Occurrence
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

An event, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general conditions, that results in physical loss of or damage to goods, delay in delivering goods, personal injury or property damage that the insured claiming cover did not expect or intend. How occurrences are counted is set out in [[ref:fsl.pay.counting]].
