heading: Licence Defence and Investigation Costs
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We pay the reasonable costs you incur, with our consent, to respond to an investigation, inquiry, audit, show-cause notice or disciplinary proceeding by an authority about your freight services, including by the Australian Border Force, the Comptroller-General of Customs, the National Customs Brokers Licensing Advisory Committee, the biosecurity regulator or the National Heavy Vehicle Regulator, and about a customs broker licence held by you or your nominee. The investigation must first be notified to you during the policy period and must arise from freight services performed on or after the retroactive date.

The most we pay for all investigations in the policy period is the sub-limit for this extension. These costs are part of, and reduce, the [[ref:fsl.cover.fines-and-penalties|heading]] limit. We do not pay a fine, a penalty or the cost of complying with any condition, order or direction.
