---
id: fsl.excl.usa-and-canada
type: exclusion
heading: USA and Canada
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

- Liability arising from freight services you provide from an office, warehouse or depot in the United States of America or Canada, or with vehicles you operate there; and
- for [[ref:fsl.cover.public-liability|heading]], any claim brought in a court in the United States of America or Canada, or to enforce a judgment or award of such a court.

The United States of America and Canada include their territories and possessions. Cover may be written back by Endorsement E08.
