id: fsl.excl.employers-liability
type: exclusion
heading: Employers' Liability
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For [[ref:fsl.cover.public-liability|heading]]: personal injury to an employee arising out of or in the course of their employment by you, and any liability for personal injury that you are required by a workers compensation law to insure, or would be required to insure but for an exemption you hold.
