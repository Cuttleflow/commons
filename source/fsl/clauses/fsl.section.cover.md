since: 1.0.0

Cargo Liability, Errors and Omissions, and Fines and Penalties always apply. Customs Duty Liability and Public Liability apply only if the schedule shows them as operative. [[ref:fsl.pay.which-section-responds]] says which section covers each kind of loss.
