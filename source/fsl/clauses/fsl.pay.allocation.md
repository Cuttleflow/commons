Where a claim involves amounts that this policy covers and amounts it does not, or is made against you and against someone this policy does not insure, we and you will use reasonable efforts to agree a fair and reasonable allocation of compensation and defence costs between them, having regard to their relative legal and financial exposures.

If we and you cannot agree within a reasonable time, a Senior Counsel will decide the allocation. The Senior Counsel is agreed between us and you or, failing agreement within 21 days, nominated by the President of the Bar Association or Bar Council of the state or territory whose law governs this policy. The decision binds us and you. Until an allocation is agreed or decided, we pay defence costs on a fair and reasonable interim allocation.
