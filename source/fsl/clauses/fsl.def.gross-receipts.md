---
id: fsl.def.gross-receipts
type: definition
heading: Gross Receipts
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

All amounts you charge for the freight services, including freight, fees, commission and the amounts you pay to subcontractors and carriers. Gross receipts do not include goods and services tax, or duty, taxes and other charges that you pay for customers and recover from them without a margin.
