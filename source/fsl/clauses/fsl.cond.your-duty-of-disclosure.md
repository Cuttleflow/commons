This policy is not a consumer insurance contract. Before you enter into, renew, extend, vary or reinstate it, you have the duty of disclosure in section 21 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). You must tell us every matter that you know, or that a reasonable person in the circumstances could be expected to know, is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. What the named insured knows includes what a member of its control group knows.

You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we tell you we do not need to know.

If you do not comply, we may reduce our liability for a claim, or cancel this policy, only as the Act allows. We may avoid this policy only if the failure was fraudulent. As between us and each insured, this duty and its consequences are assessed as [[ref:fsl.cond.individual-treatment]] sets out.
