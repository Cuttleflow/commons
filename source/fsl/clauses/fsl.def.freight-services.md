---
id: fsl.def.freight-services
type: definition
heading: Freight Services
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The services described in the schedule that you provide in the course of your business, from your premises in Australia or in any other country shown in the schedule, as:

- a freight forwarder, arranging the carriage of goods by sea, air, road, rail or post, as agent or as principal;
- a non-vessel operating common carrier or contracting carrier, issuing your own bills of lading, air waybills or consignment notes;
- a carrier, carrying goods in vehicles you operate;
- a warehouse or depot operator, receiving, storing and dispatching goods;
- a third-party logistics provider, including packing, consolidation, pick and pack, labelling and distribution; or
- a customs broker or agent, lodging customs entries and arranging clearances, permits and inspections.

They include services that are incidental to these, such as preparing documents, arranging inspections, collecting amounts on delivery and arranging insurance of goods as agent for a customer, and services that subcontractors perform for you.
