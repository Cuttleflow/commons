heading: Unexplained Shortages
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]]: a shortage of goods that is found only when you take stock or reconcile inventory records, and that you cannot explain.

This exclusion does not apply if your records show which consignment the goods are missing from and that the goods were received into your custody or a subcontractor's custody, or if there is evidence of theft.
