type: obligation
heading: Tell Us About Claims and Losses
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us in writing as soon as reasonably practicable after you become aware of:

- a claim against you;
- an occurrence that has caused, or may cause, physical loss of or damage to goods, delay, personal injury or property damage for which a claim may be made against you; or
- a notice from an authority that imposes, or states an intention to impose, a fine or penalty on you, or that demands duty, tax or charges from you.

Send us copies of every demand, notice and document you receive. For [[ref:fsl.cover.errors-and-omissions|heading]], [[ref:fsl.cover.fines-and-penalties|heading]] and [[ref:fsl.cover.customs-duty-liability|heading]], you must tell us about the claim during the policy period or within the time allowed by [[ref:fsl.ext.extended-reporting-period]]. [[ref:fsl.cover.notification-of-circumstances]] protects you for facts you tell us about during the policy period.
