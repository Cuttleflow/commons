This extension applies to [[ref:fsl.cover.errors-and-omissions|heading]], [[ref:fsl.cover.fines-and-penalties|heading]] and [[ref:fsl.cover.customs-duty-liability|heading]].

If a claim is first made against you during the policy period, and:

- it arises from facts that you first became aware of while insured under an earlier policy that we issued to the named insured;
- you did not tell us about those facts under that earlier policy, through oversight and not fraud; and
- you have been insured by us without a break in cover since then,

we will cover the claim under this policy, despite [[ref:fsl.excl.known-claims-and-circumstances]]. The most we pay is the lower of the limit under this policy and the limit that applied under the earlier policy, and the terms of the earlier policy apply where they are narrower than this policy.
