This section applies only if the schedule shows it as operative.

We will pay the compensation you become legally liable to pay because of personal injury, or property damage to property other than goods, that:

- happens during the policy period; and
- is caused by an occurrence in connection with the freight services.

We also pay defence costs and supplementary payments, as set out in [[ref:fsl.cover.defence-costs]] and [[ref:fsl.cover.supplementary-payments]].
