---
id: fsl.excl.seizure-and-confiscation
type: exclusion
heading: Seizure and Confiscation
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Loss, damage, delay or financial loss arising from the seizure, detention, confiscation, forfeiture or destruction of goods by order of an authority.

This exclusion does not apply where an error caused the seizure, detention, confiscation, forfeiture or destruction.
