id: fsl.excl.dishonest-acts
type: exclusion
heading: Dishonest and Deliberate Acts
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Any dishonest, fraudulent, criminal or malicious act or omission, or any deliberate breach of the law, committed by you, once it is established by final adjudication. Until then, we pay defence costs as this policy provides. If the conduct is established by final adjudication, the insured concerned must repay the defence costs we paid for it.

This exclusion does not apply to your legal liability for theft, dishonesty or other wrongful conduct of an employee or a subcontractor, if no member of your control group took part in it or knew of it. It applies only to the insured who committed, took part in or knew of the conduct, as [[ref:fsl.cond.individual-treatment]] sets out.
