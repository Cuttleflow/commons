id: fsl.obl.use-your-trading-conditions
type: obligation
heading: Use Your Standard Trading Conditions
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Take reasonable steps to make your standard trading conditions part of every contract under which you provide the freight services. Reasonable steps include:

- referring to them, and saying where they can be read, in your quotations, booking confirmations, consignment notes, house bills and invoices;
- giving a copy to each new customer before you first provide the freight services to it and, where you open a credit account, having the customer sign an account agreement that includes them; and
- keeping a record of the version in force when each contract was made.

If you do not, [[ref:fsl.pay.trading-conditions]] sets the most we pay for the claim. We do not otherwise reduce or refuse a claim because of this obligation.
