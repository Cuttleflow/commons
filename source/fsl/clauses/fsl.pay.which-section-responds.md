Each kind of loss is covered under one section only:

- physical loss of or damage to goods, including loss by theft, delivery to the wrong person or release to a person who obtained the goods by deception, and liability for delay in delivering goods, is covered only under [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]];
- personal injury, and property damage to property other than goods, is covered only under [[ref:fsl.cover.public-liability|heading]], if it is operative;
- other financial loss caused by an error is covered only under [[ref:fsl.cover.errors-and-omissions|heading]];
- a fine or penalty imposed on you is covered only under [[ref:fsl.cover.fines-and-penalties|heading]]; and
- duty, taxes and charges you become liable to pay in your own right are covered only under [[ref:fsl.cover.customs-duty-liability|heading]], if it is operative.

If one occurrence or one error causes losses of more than one kind, each section pays its part up to its own limit, and only one excess applies, as [[ref:fsl.pay.excess]] says. If a section is not operative, the loss it would have covered is not covered under another section.
