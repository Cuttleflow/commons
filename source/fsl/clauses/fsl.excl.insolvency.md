---
id: fsl.excl.insolvency
type: exclusion
heading: Insolvency and Financial Default
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Liability or loss arising from:

- your insolvency, administration, receivership, liquidation or bankruptcy;
- the insolvency, administration, receivership, liquidation or bankruptcy of a subcontractor, or its failure to pay any amount or to perform any part of the freight services because of its financial difficulty; or
- your failure to pay or account for money, including freight, duty, tax and other money you receive from or hold for customers, carriers or authorities, unless the failure was caused by an error and not by a lack of funds or a decision not to pay.

This exclusion does not apply to physical loss of or damage to goods that [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]] covers.
