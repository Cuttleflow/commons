id: fsl.excl.your-fees-and-charges
type: exclusion
heading: Your Fees and Charges
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

- The return, reduction or loss of your fees, commission, freight or other charges, or of amounts you would have earned;
- the cost of providing, correcting or performing again the freight services; and
- amounts a customer or anyone else fails to pay you.

This exclusion does not apply to costs covered by [[ref:fsl.ext.mitigation-costs]].
