---
id: fsl.excl.motor-vehicles
type: exclusion
heading: Motor Vehicles
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For [[ref:fsl.cover.public-liability|heading]]: personal injury or property damage arising from the ownership, use or operation by you or on your behalf of any vehicle that is registered, or is required by law to be registered, or that is being driven on a public road, or for which compulsory third party insurance or a statutory motor accident scheme provides cover or would if the vehicle were registered.

This exclusion does not apply to:

- property damage caused by a vehicle on your premises or in a yard you use, other than damage to the vehicle itself; or
- personal injury or property damage caused by loading goods onto, or unloading goods from, a vehicle, to the extent that no motor vehicle insurance, compulsory third party insurance or statutory motor accident scheme covers it.

Physical loss of or damage to goods carried in a vehicle is covered under [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]], and this exclusion does not apply to it.
