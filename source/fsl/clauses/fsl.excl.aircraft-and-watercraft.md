---
id: fsl.excl.aircraft-and-watercraft
type: exclusion
heading: Aircraft and Watercraft
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For [[ref:fsl.cover.public-liability|heading]]: personal injury or property damage arising from the ownership, operation, maintenance or use, by you or on your behalf, of any aircraft or vessel. This exclusion does not apply to liability arising from goods you arranged to be carried by an aircraft or vessel that someone else operates.
