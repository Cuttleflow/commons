heading: Removal and Disposal of Damaged Goods
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If goods suffer physical loss or damage for which you are legally liable and which [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]] covers, we pay the reasonable costs you incur, or that an authority or the owner of the goods requires you to pay, to remove, destroy or dispose of the damaged goods. The most we pay is the sub-limit for this extension.
