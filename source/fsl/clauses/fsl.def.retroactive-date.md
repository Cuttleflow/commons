id: fsl.def.retroactive-date
type: definition
heading: Retroactive Date
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The date shown in the schedule as the retroactive date. If no date is shown, it is the date this policy first started or, if this policy renews or replaces an earlier policy that we issued to the named insured without a break in cover, the date the earliest of those policies started.
