heading: Other Insurance
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If a liability covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy that insures you, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows.

Insurance of the goods themselves, such as a marine cargo policy held by the owner of the goods or arranged by you for a customer, is not other insurance for this purpose. If a cargo insurer pays the owner and then claims against you, this policy responds to that claim as it would to a claim by the owner.

This provision does not apply where the schedule or an endorsement states that this policy is excess of a specified underlying policy.
