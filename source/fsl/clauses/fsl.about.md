This policy is for businesses that arrange, carry, store or clear other people's goods: freight forwarders, non-vessel operating common carriers, customs brokers, carriers, warehouse operators and third-party logistics providers. The market also calls this cover freight forwarders' liability, logistics operators' liability, carriers' liability and customs brokers' liability.

It covers your legal liability arising from your freight services. It does not insure the goods themselves. The owner of the goods insures them under its own marine cargo or transit policy.

It has five sections. Cargo Liability, Errors and Omissions, and Fines and Penalties always apply. Customs Duty Liability and Public Liability apply only if the schedule shows them as operative.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| [[ref:fsl.cover.cargo-liability|heading]] | Your legal liability for physical loss of or damage to goods in the course of your freight services, and for delay in delivering them | Loss, damage or delay that happens during the policy period |
| [[ref:fsl.cover.errors-and-omissions|heading]] | Your legal liability for other financial loss caused by an error in your freight services | Claims first made against you, and told to us, during the policy period |
| [[ref:fsl.cover.fines-and-penalties|heading]] | Fines and penalties an authority imposes on you because of an error in your freight services, where the law allows them to be insured | Notices first given to you, and told to us, during the policy period |
| [[ref:fsl.cover.customs-duty-liability|heading]] | Duty, taxes and charges you become liable to pay in your own right because of an error in a customs entry | Demands first made on you, and told to us, during the policy period |
| [[ref:fsl.cover.public-liability|heading]] | Your legal liability for personal injury, and for damage to property other than goods, caused by an occurrence in connection with your freight services | Injury or damage that happens during the policy period |

**Your standard trading conditions set the measure of cover.** This policy pays the liability you would have under the standard trading conditions named in the schedule, as they take effect subject to any law that the parties cannot exclude by contract. [[ref:fsl.pay.trading-conditions]] explains how this works, including when your conditions did not form part of a contract.

**Three sections are claims-made.** Errors and Omissions, Fines and Penalties and Customs Duty Liability cover claims first made against you during the policy period that you tell us about during the policy period, or within the time [[ref:fsl.ext.extended-reporting-period]] allows. They do not cover a claim made after that time, unless you told us during the policy period about facts that might give rise to it. You have that right under section 40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), and [[ref:fsl.cover.notification-of-circumstances]] sets it out. These sections also do not cover errors committed before the retroactive date shown in the schedule.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Where an endorsement differs from either, the endorsement applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:fsl.section.definitions]].
