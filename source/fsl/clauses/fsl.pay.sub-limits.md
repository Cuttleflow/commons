These are the default sub-limits. A different amount shown in the schedule replaces the default.

| Extension or cover | Default sub-limit | Part of the limit of |
|---|---|---|
| [[ref:fsl.ext.mitigation-costs|heading]] | $100,000 any one occurrence or claim, or the schedule amount | Cargo Liability or Errors and Omissions, whichever the loss would have fallen under |
| [[ref:fsl.ext.removal-and-disposal|heading]] | $100,000 any one occurrence, or the schedule amount | Cargo Liability |
| [[ref:fsl.ext.pollution-clean-up|heading]] | $250,000 any one occurrence and in the aggregate, or the schedule amount | Cargo Liability |
