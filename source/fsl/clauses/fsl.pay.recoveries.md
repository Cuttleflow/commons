After we have paid a claim, any amount recovered from a subcontractor, carrier, the owner of the goods or anyone else is applied, after deducting the reasonable costs of recovery, in this order:

1. to you, for any amount you paid above the limit;
2. to us, up to the amount we paid; and
3. to you, for the excess.

If you recover an amount that we have paid, you must pay it to us in this order.
