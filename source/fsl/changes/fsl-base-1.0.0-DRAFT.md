This is the first release of the Meridian Freight Services Liability wording. It is a new wording, drafted for the Commons from first principles and public sources. It does not reproduce any insurer's or underwriting agency's wording.

- A liability policy for freight forwarders, non-vessel operating common carriers, customs brokers, carriers, warehouse operators and third-party logistics providers. It does not insure the goods themselves.
- Five sections. Cargo Liability, Errors and Omissions, and Fines and Penalties always apply. Customs Duty Liability and Public Liability are optional.
- Cargo Liability and Public Liability respond on an occurrence basis. Errors and Omissions, Fines and Penalties and Customs Duty Liability respond to claims first made and notified during the policy period, with one retroactive date, notification of circumstances under s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), continuous cover and a 90-day automatic extended reporting period.
- The insured's standard trading conditions set the measure of cover. If they did not form part of a contract, the claim is still paid, up to what they would have allowed. Where a law the parties cannot exclude by contract overrides them, cover follows that liability up to the limit.
- Cargo Liability includes theft, misdelivery and release of goods to a person who obtained them by deception. Endorsement E07 lets an insurer sub-limit release fraud.
- A Which Section Responds provision sends each kind of loss to one section, with one excess where one occurrence or error engages several.
- Defence costs are in addition to the limit for Cargo Liability and Public Liability, reduced pro rata when compensation exceeds the limit, and part of the limit for the three claims-made sections and for claims brought in the USA and Canada.
- A statutory framework condition gives the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) protections by agreement to any part of the policy that the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) governs.
- The endorsement library has 8 endorsements, each with operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger.
