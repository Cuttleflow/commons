Where a law, or an accreditation scheme under which you provide a service, requires the person who provides that service to hold a licence, registration, accreditation or endorsement, the service is a professional service under this policy only if it is provided by a person who holds it, or under the direct control and supervision of such a person to the extent that the law or scheme permits.

This condition and [[ref:fs.ext.representatives]] work together as follows. If a service is one that your licence, or a representative's authorisation, permits, [[ref:fs.ext.representatives]] applies to it even where the representative acted outside the authority you gave them. If no one acting under your licence was permitted to provide the service, this condition applies and the service is not a professional service.
