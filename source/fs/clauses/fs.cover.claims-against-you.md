If a claim is first made against you during the policy period, and the claim arises from your business activity performed on or after the retroactive date within the geographical limits, we will pay:

(a) any loss you become legally liable to pay; and

(b) your defence costs,

for any actual or alleged:

- (i) negligence, breach of duty, or failure to exercise reasonable care and skill
- (ii) negligent misstatement, misrepresentation, or misleading advice
- (iii) breach of confidence or misuse of **confidential information**
- (iv) infringement of copyright, trademark, or moral rights, or passing off
- (v) unintentional defamation
- (vi) breach of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) or any state or territory fair trading legislation, in connection with your professional services
- (vii) fraud or dishonesty of your employees and **representatives** (where you are liable to a third party for their conduct)
- (viii) breach of an obligation under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) or the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (Cth) in providing your professional services, including a best interests obligation, to the extent that it gives rise to a liability to pay compensation
- (ix) any other civil liability arising from your professional services

unless excluded under [[ref:fs.section.exclusions]].
