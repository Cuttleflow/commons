---
id: fs.excl.prior-known-circumstances
type: exclusion
heading: Prior Known Circumstances
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Arising from any fact, matter, or circumstance: (a) that you knew about, or ought reasonably to have known about, before the start of this policy period (or before we first agreed to insure you, if earlier); and (b) that you knew, or ought reasonably to have known, might lead to a claim against you.

Lodging a report with ASIC about a reportable situation is not, by itself, knowledge that a fact, matter or circumstance might lead to a claim.
