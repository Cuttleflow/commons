We will pay your defence costs, and any fine or penalty that the law allows to be insured, arising from a prosecution or formal regulatory proceeding first brought against you during the policy period and notified to us during the policy period, under any Australian statute, in connection with your business activity performed on or after the retroactive date.

A prosecution or proceeding is first brought against you when you are first notified of it in writing. If it follows a formal investigation or inquiry covered by [[ref:fs.ext.inquiry-costs]], or a claim, it is first brought when that investigation, inquiry or claim was first made against you.

We do not pay any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured. In particular, we do not pay a penalty imposed under work health and safety law in a State or Territory whose law prohibits insurance of it (including New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland), and we do not pay a criminal fine. We also do not pay any amount arising from deliberate or reckless conduct.

[[ref:fs.cond.corporations-act-199b]] applies to this extension for an insured person. Where we advance defence costs in a civil penalty or criminal proceeding against an insured person, we advance them as they are incurred, and the insured person must repay them if a Final adjudication, or a court's finding, establishes conduct that this policy or the law does not allow to be covered.
