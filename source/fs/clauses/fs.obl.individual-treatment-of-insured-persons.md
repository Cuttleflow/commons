Each insured person's cover, and the company's cover, is assessed individually. A failure by one insured person to comply with [[ref:fs.obl.tell-us-about-claims]], [[ref:fs.obl.tell-us-about-circumstances]], [[ref:fs.obl.do-not-admit-liability]], [[ref:fs.obl.cooperate-with-us]], [[ref:fs.obl.mitigate-your-loss]] or [[ref:fs.obl.preserve-our-recovery-rights]], or any knowledge, act, error, omission, or non-disclosure of one insured person, does not affect the cover of another insured person, or of the company, who did not know of, participate in, or contribute to that failure, knowledge, act, error, omission, or non-disclosure. The exception is that the knowledge, acts, omissions and failures of a member of the **control group** count as the company's. This section does not affect [[ref:fs.excl.deliberate-and-dishonest-acts]] or its Final adjudication and non-imputation mechanics, which continue to apply according to their terms.
