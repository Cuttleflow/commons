The following endorsements apply to this policy only when the schedule shows that they apply.

An endorsement does not apply to the extent that it would exclude cover that ASIC Regulatory Guide 126 requires the policy to provide. Those covers are:

- AFCA determinations, and AFCA conciliated or negotiated amounts we consented to (see [[ref:fs.ext.afca-determinations]]);
- your liability for the conduct of your representatives (see [[ref:fs.ext.representatives]]);
- your liability for the fraud or dishonesty of your officers, employees and representatives (see [[ref:fs.cover.claims-against-you]]);
- defence costs payable in addition to the limit of liability (see [[ref:fs.pay.defence-costs]]);
- one automatic reinstatement of the limit (see [[ref:fs.pay.automatic-reinstatement]]); and
- retroactive cover to the start of your continuous series of cover (see [[ref:fs.def.retroactive-date]]).
