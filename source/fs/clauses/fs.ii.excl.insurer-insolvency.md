Arising from the insolvency of an insurer, reinsurer, underwriting agency or premium funder with which you placed or arranged business.

This exclusion does not apply to a claim that you were negligent, or breached your duty, in choosing or monitoring the insurer, reinsurer, underwriting agency or premium funder, even if it later became insolvent.
