id: fs.ext.newly-acquired-subsidiaries
type: extension
heading: Newly Acquired Subsidiaries
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If during the policy period you acquire or create a new subsidiary, it is automatically covered for 60 days — provided its annual revenue does not exceed the amount shown in the schedule for this purpose (or $5,000,000 if no amount is shown) and its business activity is the same as or similar to yours. You must tell us within 60 days; we may adjust the premium or terms.
