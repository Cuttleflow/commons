This policy, together with your schedule and any endorsements, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read all documents together and let us know if anything is incorrect.

This policy is written for Australian financial services licensees, Australian credit licensees and their representatives. It is designed to meet the professional indemnity insurance requirements that ASIC sets for those licensees, and [[ref:fs.cond.compulsory-insurance-requirements]] applies where it would otherwise fall short of them.

Words shown in bold have special meanings defined in [[ref:fs.section.definitions]].

This wording implements the notification-of-circumstances protection required by s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) — see [[ref:fs.cover.notification-of-circumstances]]. Before you enter into this contract, your insurer or its distributor must give you written notice of the effect of s.40(3) as required by s.40(1) of that Act.
