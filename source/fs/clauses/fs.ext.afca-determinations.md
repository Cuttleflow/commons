---
id: fs.ext.afca-determinations
type: extension
heading: AFCA Determinations
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
note: ASIC RG 126 and RG 210 require cover for awards made by the licensee's external dispute resolution scheme. A sub-limit is permitted; its amount is checked by a platform restriction.
---

We will pay, as loss, any amount that you are required to pay under a determination of **AFCA**, including compensation, interest and costs it awards, even if a court would not have ordered you to pay that amount.

Responding to a complaint in the way your internal dispute resolution obligations require is not an admission of liability for [[ref:fs.obl.do-not-admit-liability]]. You must not offer or agree to pay compensation to resolve a complaint without our consent, which we will not unreasonably withhold or delay.

If the schedule shows a sub-limit for AFCA determinations, it is the most we will pay for amounts under this extension in the policy period. It is part of the limit of liability.
