since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0

---

(a) any written demand for compensation made against you;

(b) any writ, summons, cross-claim, or other legal proceeding issued against you;

(c) any formal investigation or inquiry by a regulator that could lead to a finding of personal civil liability against you;

(d) any written assertion of a right against you under a statute that could lead to your civil liability;

(e) any formal complaint or proceeding before a professional disciplinary body; or

(f) any complaint made against you to **AFCA** seeking compensation.

A claim of a kind described in paragraph (c), (d), (e) or (f) is first made when you are first notified of it in writing.

Two or more claims arising from the same, similar, or related act(s), error(s), or omission(s) are treated as a single claim first made when the earliest was first made.
