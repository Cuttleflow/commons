heading: Defence Costs
since: 1.0.0

note: ASIC RG 126 expects defence costs to be payable in addition to the minimum limit, or the limit to be higher to allow for them.
---

Defence costs are payable in addition to the limit of liability, up to a further amount equal to the limit of liability in total for all claims during the policy period, unless the schedule states that defence costs are included within the limit. That further amount is not reinstated under [[ref:fs.pay.automatic-reinstatement]]. If defence costs are included within the limit, they reduce the amount available to pay compensation. We will advance defence costs as they are incurred, without waiting for the final outcome of a claim. If a claim is later found not to be covered, you must repay any defence costs advanced for the uncovered portion, subject to [[ref:fs.pay.allocation]] where the claim was only partly covered.

Where a claim involves conduct excluded under [[ref:fs.excl.deliberate-and-dishonest-acts]], repayment of defence costs advanced in connection with that conduct is required only after a Final adjudication establishes that the exclusion applies, consistent with [[ref:fs.cond.insurance-contracts-act]].
