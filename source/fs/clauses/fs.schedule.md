(Completed separately for each policy — contains your specific details)

The schedule shows:

- Named insured and any subsidiary

- Your Australian financial services licence or Australian credit licence, or the licensee you represent, with its number

- Professional services covered

- Any representative who is insured under a separate policy that gives you a right of indemnity (every other representative is an insured person: see [[ref:fs.ext.representatives]])

- Policy period — start and end dates

- Retroactive date

- Limit of liability — the most we will pay for all claims combined (see [[ref:fs.pay.limit-of-liability]])

- Sub-limits — lower limits for specific cover types, including any sub-limit for AFCA determinations

- Whether defence costs are included within the limit of liability. If the schedule says nothing, they are payable in addition, as [[ref:fs.pay.defence-costs]] sets out

- Excess — the amount you pay first on each claim

- Premium — including stamp duty and GST

- Court attendance daily rate and aggregate amount, if different from the policy defaults of $500 a day and $25,000 (see [[ref:fs.ext.court-attendance]])

- Newly acquired subsidiary revenue threshold, if different from the policy default of $5,000,000 (see [[ref:fs.ext.newly-acquired-subsidiaries]])

- The State or Territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:fs.cond.governing-law]])

- Endorsements that change the standard terms, and any endorsement schedule (for example, the period and premium of a run-off extension that we have offered and you have accepted under [[ref:fs.ext.run-off-cover]])
