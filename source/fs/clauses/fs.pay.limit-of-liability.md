The most we will pay for all claims during the policy period is the limit of liability shown in your schedule. This is an aggregate limit — it applies to all claims combined, regardless of how many are made or how many insured persons are involved. Defence costs are paid as set out in [[ref:fs.pay.defence-costs]].
