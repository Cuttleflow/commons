The most we will pay under this policy for all claims during the policy period is the limit of liability shown in your schedule. This is an aggregate limit: it applies to all claims combined, regardless of how many are made or how many insured persons are involved. The most we will pay for any one claim is also the limit of liability, whether or not the limit has been reinstated.

Defence costs are paid as [[ref:fs.pay.defence-costs]] sets out. The limit may be reinstated once, as [[ref:fs.pay.automatic-reinstatement]] sets out.

Taken together, and unless the schedule states otherwise:

- the most we will pay for loss for all claims in the policy period, including after reinstatement, is twice the limit of liability;
- defence costs are payable in addition to that amount, up to one further limit of liability in total for the policy period, which is not reinstated; and
- so the most we will pay in total for all claims in the policy period is three times the limit of liability.

If the schedule states that defence costs are included within the limit of liability, defence costs reduce the limit, and the most we will pay in total for all claims in the policy period, including after reinstatement, is twice the limit of liability.
