heading: Deliberate and Dishonest Acts
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Arising from any act, error, or omission you deliberately, dishonestly, fraudulently, criminally, or maliciously commit, condone, or ignore — but only to the extent that a **Final adjudication** establishes that you personally committed, condoned, or participated in that conduct. Until Final adjudication, we will advance defence costs under [[ref:fs.pay.defence-costs]]. If Final adjudication then establishes that this exclusion applies, you must repay the defence costs advanced in connection with that conduct, in accordance with [[ref:fs.pay.defence-costs]].

However: we will still cover any insured person who did not personally commit, condone, or participate in the conduct — this protection applies separately to each insured person, and a Final adjudication establishing the conduct against one insured person does not, of itself, establish that conduct against another insured person. This exclusion does not apply to [[ref:fs.ext.first-party-dishonesty]] or to the cover for dishonesty of your employees under [[ref:fs.cover.claims-against-you]].
