A financial product that, when the advice was given or the service provided:

(a) was on the approved product list maintained by the licensee shown in the schedule, under its approval process; or

(b) had been researched and approved in writing by the licensee for the particular client, under that process.

The licensee's approval process is its documented process for researching, approving, reviewing and removing financial products before they are recommended to clients.

If the schedule states that the licensee does not maintain an approved product list, every financial product that you are authorised to advise on is an approved product.

Advice to acquire a financial product that is not an approved product is not a professional service. This is so even if the claim alleges that the licensee did not follow its approval process. Paragraphs (c) and (d) of the definition of professional services, which deal with switching and holding advice, are not affected.
