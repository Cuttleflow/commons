---
id: fs.excl.cyber-event
type: exclusion
heading: Cyber Event
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Arising from: (a) **unauthorised access** to or interference with your computer system or data; (b) ransomware, malware, or denial-of-service attacks; (c) failure of your computer system's security; or (d) loss, theft, or unauthorised disclosure of data held electronically — unless the liability arises directly from a breach of your professional duty of care performing business activity for a client.

This exposure is designed to be covered under a cyber insurance policy.
