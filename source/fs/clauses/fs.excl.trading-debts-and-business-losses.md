type: exclusion
heading: Trading Debts and Business Losses
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For: (a) fees, charges, or debts owed to you; (b) your lost profit, mark-up, or overheads; (c) any trading loss or loss of any client, contract, or business opportunity; or (d) your liability for GST or any other tax.
