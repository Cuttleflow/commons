---
id: fs.excl.non-compensatory-payments
type: exclusion
heading: Non-Compensatory Payments
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For levies under the Compensation Scheme of Last Resort, levies or fees payable to ASIC or **AFCA**, fines, penalties, punitive damages, exemplary damages, aggravated damages, or multiple damages — except where cover for fines or penalties is specifically provided under [[ref:fs.ext.statutory-liability]].
