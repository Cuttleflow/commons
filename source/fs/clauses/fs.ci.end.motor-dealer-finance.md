Credit assistance that you provide as a motor vehicle dealer, or as a credit representative of a lender or broker in connection with the sale of vehicles, is a professional service under this policy.

We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from the sale, condition or repair of a vehicle, or from add-on insurance or warranty products sold with it, unless you are authorised to provide those products under a licence shown in the schedule.
