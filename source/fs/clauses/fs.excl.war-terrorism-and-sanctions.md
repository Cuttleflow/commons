heading: War and Terrorism
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Arising from: (a) war (whether or not declared), invasion, hostilities, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, civil unrest, or the use or usurpation of military power, or any similar belligerent act; or (b) terrorism as defined by relevant Australian or foreign law.

Sanctions are dealt with by [[ref:fs.cond.sanctions]], not by this exclusion.
