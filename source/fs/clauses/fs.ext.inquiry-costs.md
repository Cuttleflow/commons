heading: Inquiry Costs
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We will pay your defence costs in responding to a formal investigation or inquiry by a regulator (including ASIC, OAIC, ACCC, or a state or territory equivalent) into your conduct in connection with your business activity performed on or after the retroactive date, provided the investigation or inquiry is first made against you during the policy period and you notify us of it during the policy period. It is first made against you when you are first notified of it in writing, as [[ref:fs.def.claim]] provides for a claim of the kind described in paragraph (c).

This extension applies to a formal investigation or inquiry that is not yet a claim within paragraph (c) of [[ref:fs.def.claim]]. If the investigation or inquiry becomes such a claim, it is the same event, not a second one: it is treated as a single claim first made on the date the investigation or inquiry was first made against you, cover for it continues under [[ref:fs.cover.claims-against-you]] instead of this extension, and the limit and excess applicable to a claim apply from that point.
