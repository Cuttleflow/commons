id: fs.def.retroactive-date
type: definition
heading: Retroactive date
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
note: ASIC RG 126 (Table 4) expects a policy that follows an immediately previous policy to give retroactive cover to the earlier of these dates.
---

The date shown in the schedule. We only cover claims arising from acts, errors, or omissions on or after this date.

If you held professional indemnity insurance for your professional services immediately before this policy began, the retroactive date is no later than the earlier of the retroactive date in that insurance and the start date of the first policy in your continuous series of such insurance.
