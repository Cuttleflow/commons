The following services, to the extent that you are authorised to provide them under the Australian financial services licence shown in the schedule, or as an authorised representative of the licensee shown in the schedule:

(a) personal and general advice on financial products, including superannuation, self-managed superannuation funds, managed investments, securities, life risk insurance and retirement income products;

(b) financial planning, including strategy advice on investment, superannuation, retirement, estate planning, debt and cash flow;

(c) arranging or dealing in financial products for your clients;

(d) tax (financial) advice provided by a qualified tax relevant provider; and

(e) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

Advice to switch from one financial product to another, or to continue holding a financial product, is a professional service whether or not either product is on your approved product list.
