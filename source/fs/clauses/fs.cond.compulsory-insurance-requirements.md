This condition applies where a law, or a requirement made under a law by a regulator or licensing body, requires you to hold professional indemnity insurance with minimum terms in order to provide your professional services. Each such requirement is a compulsory insurance requirement.

For a claim to which a compulsory insurance requirement applies, this policy is to be read as providing at least the cover, limit of liability, excess terms, reinstatement and run-off that the requirement prescribes. Where this policy would otherwise provide less, we will pay what the requirement obliges an insurer to pay.

If we pay an amount only because of this condition, and we would not otherwise have been liable to pay it under this policy, you must repay that amount to us, to the extent that the law and the requirement allow us to recover it.

[WP REVIEW: confirm that recovering from you an amount paid only because of this condition is consistent with s 912B of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) and the compensation arrangements it requires, with s 54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) and with unfair contract terms law, and that it does not make the minimum cover illusory.]
