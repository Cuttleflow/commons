heading: Limit of liability
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

The most we will pay during the policy period, as shown in the schedule, for loss and the other amounts payable under extensions, unless a specific sub-limit applies. Defence costs are paid as [[ref:fs.pay.defence-costs]] sets out, and [[ref:fs.pay.limit-of-liability]] states how the limit, defence costs and reinstatement fit together.
