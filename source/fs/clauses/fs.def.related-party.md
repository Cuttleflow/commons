---
id: fs.def.related-party
type: definition
heading: Related party
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

(a) any entity that controls you, is controlled by you, or is under common control with you;

(b) any person or entity holding a financial, executive, or managerial interest of 15% or more in you; and

(c) any entity in which you hold such an interest.
