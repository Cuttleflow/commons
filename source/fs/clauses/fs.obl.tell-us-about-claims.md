---
id: fs.obl.tell-us-about-claims
type: obligation
heading: Tell Us About Claims
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You must tell us in writing as soon as reasonably practicable after you become aware that a claim has been made against you. Tell us: (a) who is making the claim and what they allege; (b) when you first became aware; (c) details of the act, error, or omission involved; and (d) your estimate of the potential value if you can.
