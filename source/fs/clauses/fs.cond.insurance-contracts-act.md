since: 1.0.0

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts, or modifies the operation of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) (the **ICA**). Where this policy is inconsistent with the ICA, the ICA prevails.

Without limiting the above, s.54 of the ICA applies to any act or omission by you connected with a claim after it arises, including under [[ref:fs.obl.tell-us-about-claims]], [[ref:fs.obl.do-not-admit-liability]], [[ref:fs.obl.cooperate-with-us]], [[ref:fs.obl.mitigate-your-loss]] and [[ref:fs.obl.preserve-our-recovery-rights]]. We may not refuse to pay a claim, or reduce our liability under it, by reason only of such an act or omission, except to the extent (if any) that we are prejudiced by it, or as the ICA otherwise permits in the case of fraud.
