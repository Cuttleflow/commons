Credit assistance and other credit activities, to the extent that you are authorised to engage in them under the Australian credit licence shown in the schedule, or as a credit representative of the licensee shown in the schedule, including for residential, investment, commercial and asset finance; and any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.
