---
id: fs.def.professional-services
type: definition
heading: Professional services
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
note: Replaces the PI base approach, in which the Financial Services exclusion removes ASIC-regulated activity and a carve-back restores scheduled licensed services. Here the licence defines the cover,
  so the Financial Services exclusion is not part of this wording.
---

The financial services and credit activities that you are authorised to provide under:

(a) the Australian financial services licence or Australian credit licence shown in the schedule; or

(b) your authorisation as an authorised representative or credit representative of the licensee shown in the schedule,

and any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.
