heading: Tell Us About Circumstances
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us during the policy period about any fact, matter, or circumstance you first become aware of that might reasonably be expected to give rise to a claim. This protects you — see [[ref:fs.cover.notification-of-circumstances]].

Examples: a client has expressed serious dissatisfaction and may seek compensation; you have discovered a significant error in advice you gave; a regulator has contacted you about a formal investigation; you have received a complaint through a professional body.
