heading: First-Party Dishonesty
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If during the policy period you discover that an employee has caused you a direct financial loss through dishonest or fraudulent conduct — intending to cause you loss and to obtain a personal financial benefit beyond their normal compensation — we will pay your direct financial loss. We will deduct any amounts the employee owes you or any property of the employee that you hold.
