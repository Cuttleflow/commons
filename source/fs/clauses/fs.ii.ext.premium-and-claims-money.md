The cover for fraud or dishonesty of your employees and representatives under [[ref:fs.cover.claims-against-you]] includes their misappropriation of premium, claims money or other money that you hold for a client or an insurer, where you are liable for it to a third party who did not take part in the conduct.

We also pay your own loss of that money under [[ref:fs.ext.first-party-dishonesty]].
