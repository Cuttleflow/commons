This policy is governed by the law of the State or Territory of Australia shown in the schedule for this purpose. If none is shown, it is governed by the law of the State or Territory in which the named insured had its principal place of business at the start of the policy period. The governing law is fixed at the start of the policy period and does not change if the named insured later moves.
