First release as an integrated wording, built on the Financial Services PI base 1.0.0-DRAFT and written to meet ASIC RG 126 (November 2024, updated December 2025). It replaces MERIDIAN-PI-FINP-v1.0.0 (June 2026), which was withdrawn because it was derived from a third-party wording. This wording was drafted from first principles.

- Professional services cover personal and general advice, financial planning, arranging and dealing, life risk advice and tax (financial) advice, as your licence authorises. Switching and holding advice is covered whether or not a product is on your approved product list.
- New exclusions: Product Issuer Failure (losses caused solely by an issuer's failure, with advice claims carved back) and Market Participants.
- Optional endorsements: Approved Product List Condition, Managed Discretionary Accounts Exclusion, Geared Investment Advice Sub-limit, Related Products Exclusion and Named Products Exclusion. No endorsement may remove cover that RG 126 requires.
