First release as an integrated wording, built on the Financial Services PI base 1.0.0-DRAFT. It replaces the endorsement MERIDIAN-PI-INBR-v1.0.0 (June 2026), which was written against the PI base and was removed by its Financial Services exclusion.

- Underwriting agencies and claims handlers are included.
- Employee and representative theft of premium and claims money is covered. The June fraud extension excluded it.
- Deleted: the exclusion of client disclosures not confirmed in writing.
- Insurer insolvency is excluded by default, with a buy-back endorsement.
- Optional endorsements: Insurer Insolvency Buy-back (adds cover), Exceeding Authority Sub-limit, Life Risk Insurance Advice Exclusion, Premium Funding (adds cover) and Strata Insurance Remuneration Exclusion.
