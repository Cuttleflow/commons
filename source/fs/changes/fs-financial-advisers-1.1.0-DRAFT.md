Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. This wording is now built on version 1.1.0 of the Financial Services base wording, so it includes every change listed in the base wording's changes.

- Approved product (definition): now defines the approval process. It says that advice to acquire a product that is not an approved product is not a professional service, even if the claim alleges the licensee did not follow its approval process. Switching and holding advice are unaffected. Marked for review.
- Professional services (definition): the words "provided for a fee or other remuneration" are marked for review.
- Product Issuer Failure: the carve-back for negligent advice applies where the advice is a professional service.
- Related Products Exclusion: uses the defined term Related party. The limb about receiving a benefit other than advice fees is replaced by an ownership or profit-sharing interest, so ordinary commission and rebates no longer fall within it.
- Financial Advisers Endorsements (introduction): lists the covers that RG 126 requires and that no endorsement removes.
- Default set for underwriter confirmation: Related Products benefit limb replaced by an ownership or profit-sharing interest.
