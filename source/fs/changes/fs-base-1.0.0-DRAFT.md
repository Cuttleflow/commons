First release. A base wording for Australian financial services and credit licensees, derived from the Meridian PI base wording 1.4.0-DRAFT, with these differences:

- Professional services are the services your licence, or your authorisation as a representative, allows you to provide. The PI base Financial Services exclusion is not part of this wording.
- New definitions: AFCA and Representative.
- The insuring clause covers fraud or dishonesty of representatives as well as employees, and breach of Corporations Act or National Consumer Credit Protection Act obligations that gives rise to compensation.
- New extensions: Representatives and AFCA Determinations.
- Defence costs are paid in addition to the limit of liability, up to an amount equal to the limit, unless the schedule says otherwise.
- New exclusion: Investment Performance. Compensation Scheme of Last Resort levies are not insured.
- Lodging a report with ASIC is not, by itself, knowledge of a circumstance under the Prior Known Circumstances exclusion.
- The Professional Standards Schemes condition is not included.
