Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. This wording is now built on version 1.1.0 of the Financial Services base wording, so it includes every change listed in the base wording's changes.

- Insurer Insolvency: the exclusion no longer applies to a claim that you were negligent in choosing or monitoring the insurer, reinsurer, underwriting agency or premium funder. Marked for review.
- Premium and Claims Money: the promise of your own loss now says that it applies where an employee caused the loss, as First-Party Dishonesty provides. It does not apply to a representative who is not an employee.
- Life Risk Insurance Advice Exclusion: marked for review as a deliberate trade-off.
- Strata Insurance Remuneration Exclusion: uses the defined term Related party.
- Default set for underwriter confirmation: negligent-selection carve-back to the Insurer Insolvency exclusion.
