heading: Joint Insureds
since: 1.1.0
changed: 1.2.0
status: draft
---

Where more than one person is named in the schedule as the insured, this policy applies to each of them separately.

A fraud, deliberate act, illegal activity or failure to comply with this policy by one of them, or by a member of your household who is not named as an insured, does not affect the cover of any named insured who did not know of it, did not take part in it and did not consent to it.
