id: hom.obl.tell-us-about-claims
type: obligation
heading: Telling Us About a Claim
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after damage or an occurrence that may lead to a claim, and tell the police promptly about any theft or malicious damage.

Give us the information and documents we reasonably ask for to assess the claim, including proof of ownership or value for contents, and allow us to inspect the damaged property before it is repaired, unless emergency repairs are needed under [[ref:hom.ext.emergency-repairs]].
