heading: Illegal Use
since: 1.1.0
changed: 1.2.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for any loss, damage or liability arising from illegal activity at the home by you or by a member of your household, that you knew of, or had reason to suspect, and did not take reasonable steps to stop. Illegal activity by someone else, such as a burglar, does not affect your cover. [[ref:hom.cond.joint-insureds]] applies.
