The building is listed on a heritage register. We pay the extra cost of repairing heritage features using the methods and materials the heritage authority requires, up to the heritage limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement, in addition to the building sum insured.

Schedule variables: heritage limit (default 10% of the building sum insured).
