We cover sudden and accidental damage to the building and the contents at the home, as if it were an insured event listed in [[ref:hom.cover.building]]. Every other term of the Home Building and Contents sections applies, and the jewellery and watch limit in [[ref:hom.pay.contents-limits]] is $5,000 an item.

We do not pay under this endorsement for damage to a television, computer or other screen caused by it being dropped while being carried, for chipping, scratching or denting that does not affect how the item works, or for loss of property that is misplaced.

Schedule variables: none.
