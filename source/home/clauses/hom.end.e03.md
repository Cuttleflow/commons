We cover the contents a member of your household carries or wears away from the home, anywhere in Australia, for loss or damage by any sudden and accidental cause, up to the portable contents limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement for all unspecified items together, and $1,000 for any one item. Items specified under [[ref:hom.end.e06]] are covered anywhere in the world.

We do not pay for property left unattended in a public place, or in an unlocked vehicle, for mobile phones damaged by being dropped unless the screen is the only damage, or for sporting equipment while in use.

Schedule variables: portable contents limit (default $3,000).
