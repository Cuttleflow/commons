If the building suffers damage during the policy period caused by an insured event, we will pay to repair or rebuild it, as set out in [[ref:hom.pay.building-settlement]].

The insured events are:

- **fire**, including bushfire, and smoke and soot from a fire;
- **lightning**, and power surge caused by lightning;
- **explosion**;
- **storm**, and rainwater or water run-off that enters the building because of a storm;
- **flood**;
- **earthquake and tsunami**;
- **escape of liquid** from a fixed pipe, drain, tank, appliance, toilet, bath, shower base or waterbed, or from an aquarium, or of oil from a fixed heating system;
- **theft or attempted theft** by a person who is not a member of your household and is not at the home with your permission;
- **malicious damage** by a person who is not a member of your household;
- **impact** by a vehicle, aircraft, watercraft, falling tree or branch, power pole, aerial, satellite dish, or space debris;
- **riot or civil commotion**;
- **glass breakage** of fixed glass in the building, including glass in a cooktop or oven;
- **accidental discharge** of a fire sprinkler or extinguisher.

The most we will pay for any one event is the building sum insured, plus the amounts the extensions to this section say are payable in addition.
