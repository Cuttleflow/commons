---
id: hom.excl.unoccupied
type: exclusion
heading: Unoccupied Home
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If the home has been unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days, we pay for damage that happens after that only if it is caused by fire, lightning, explosion, earthquake or impact, unless [[ref:hom.end.e11]] applies. Cover is restored when a member of your household returns to live in the home.
