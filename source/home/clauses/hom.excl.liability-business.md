id: hom.excl.liability-business

heading: Business and Professional Activities
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability arising from a business, trade or profession of a member of your household, or from letting the home or any part of it, unless [[ref:hom.end.e04]] or [[ref:hom.end.e05]] applies.
