**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

**Who this policy is for.** This wording is designed for retail clients. An issuer using it must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available, as the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) requires. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.

**Your duty when you apply.** This policy is a consumer insurance contract under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). You have a duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated. Answer every question in the proposal honestly and completely. If you do not take reasonable care, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

**Terms that differ from standard cover.** The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) and the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017 set standard cover for home building and home contents contracts. This policy gives less cover than standard cover in each of the ways listed here. They are listed so that you are clearly informed of each of them before this policy is entered into.

- flood is covered, but we do not pay for damage by storm or flood in the first 72 hours after this policy first starts, except as [[ref:hom.excl.first-72-hours]] allows;
- we do not pay for damage by erosion, subsidence or landslip except as [[ref:hom.excl.earth-movement]] allows, or for damage by actions of the sea (see [[ref:hom.excl.action-of-the-sea]]);
- we do not pay to fix a defect, or for damage from gradual causes (see [[ref:hom.excl.defects-and-gradual-causes]]);
- cover is limited while the home is unoccupied for more than 60 days (see [[ref:hom.excl.unoccupied]]);
- we do not pay for theft or deliberate damage by a member of your household or a person at the home with your permission (other than a tradesperson), land, loss of market value, replacing undamaged items to make a matching set, a retaining wall not built to the building laws, water entering through an opening made for building works or left open, an order or action of a government or public authority, damage to property while it is being worked on, cleaned or repaired, mechanical or electrical breakdown, or damage by an animal kept at the home (see [[ref:hom.excl.property-other]]);
- we do not pay for loss, damage or liability caused deliberately by you, by a member of your household or by a person acting with your authority, or arising from illegal activity by you or a member of your household that you knew of or had reason to suspect (see [[ref:hom.excl.deliberate-acts]] and [[ref:hom.excl.illegal-use]]);
- we do not pay for loss of, damage to or corruption of electronic data, or for loss from unauthorised use of a computer system (see [[ref:hom.excl.cyber]]);
- we do not pay for loss, damage, liability or cost arising from terrorism, war or nuclear risks (see [[ref:hom.excl.terrorism]], [[ref:hom.excl.war]] and [[ref:hom.excl.nuclear]]);
- we pay no more than the limits in [[ref:hom.pay.contents-limits]] for jewellery and watches, money, documents, contents in the open air, and any one artwork, antique or collection, unless the schedule specifies an item;
- we pay for an item that is one of a pair or set only for the item damaged, and if we cannot find matching materials we replace materials only in the room or area where the damage happened (see [[ref:hom.pay.contents-settlement]] and [[ref:hom.pay.building-settlement]]);
- the extensions to each section are limited as each states, including emergency repairs, spoiled food, guests' and domestic workers' property, keys and locks, landscaping and fire services charges;
- temporary accommodation is limited as [[ref:hom.ext.temporary-accommodation]] sets out;
- contents away from the home are covered only as [[ref:hom.ext.contents-away]] and [[ref:hom.end.e03]] provide.

**Key Facts Sheet.** A Key Facts Sheet for the Home Building section and one for the Contents section are given with this policy, as the law requires.

**Cooling-off period.** You may cancel this policy within 21 days of the date it starts, and within 21 days of each renewal, by telling us in writing. If you have not made a claim, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover. After that, you may still cancel under [[ref:hom.cond.cancellation]].

**Commission and fees.** The schedule or the quote shows any commission or fee paid to your broker in connection with this policy.

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
