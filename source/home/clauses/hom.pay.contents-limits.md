Unless the schedule specifies the item under [[ref:hom.end.e06]], the most we pay for any one event is:

| Contents | Limit |
|---|---|
| Any one item of jewellery or any one watch | $3,000 ($5,000 where [[ref:hom.end.e01]] applies) |
| All jewellery and watches together | 20% of the contents sum insured |
| Money, gift cards and negotiable instruments | $500 |
| Documents, including the cost of replacing identity documents | $2,000 |
| Contents in the open air at the home | $3,000 |
| Any one artwork, antique or collection | $10,000 |

The schedule may show different limits.
