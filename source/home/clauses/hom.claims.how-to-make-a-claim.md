heading: How to Make a Claim
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

1. Make the home safe and arrange emergency repairs if needed (see [[ref:hom.ext.emergency-repairs]]).
2. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
3. Tell the police about any theft or malicious damage, and get the report number.
4. Tell us what happened and when, and give us photographs, receipts, quotes and invoices.
5. If the home is unfit to live in, tell us straight away so that we can arrange temporary accommodation.
6. We will acknowledge your claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision as the General Insurance Code of Practice requires where the insurer subscribes to it.
