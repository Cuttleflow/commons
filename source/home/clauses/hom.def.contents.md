Household goods and personal effects that you or a member of your household own, or for which you are legally responsible, kept at the home, including furniture, furnishings, carpets and floor coverings that are not fixed, curtains and blinds, clothing, electrical appliances and electronics, jewellery and watches, tools and garden equipment, and sporting equipment.

It does not include money other than as [[ref:hom.pay.contents-limits]] provides, vehicles, trailers, caravans, watercraft and aircraft (other than ride-on mowers, mobility scooters, toys and e-bikes not needing registration), animals, plants in the ground, property used mainly for a business other than a home office, or property of a tenant or boarder.
