You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. If you cancel within the cooling-off period in [[ref:hom.important-information]], we refund the premium as that section states. Otherwise we refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. If we have paid a total loss of the building, or of an item of contents, no refund applies to that building or item.

We may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you written notice as that Act requires, which is at least 30 days' written notice or any longer notice the Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis. [WP REVIEW: confirm the notice an insurer must give to cancel a home building and contents contract, including whether s 60 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) applies as well as s 59.]

Cancellation does not affect a claim for damage, or an occurrence that happened before cancellation.
