---
id: hom.excl.liability-vehicles
type: exclusion
heading: Vehicles, Aircraft and Watercraft
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability arising from the use of a vehicle that is registered or required to be registered, or of an aircraft or a watercraft longer than 8 metres. This exclusion does not apply to a ride-on mower, mobility scooter, or e-bike that does not need registration.
