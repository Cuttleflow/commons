This policy insures the home you live in, the contents you own, and your legal liability as a householder.

You can insure the building, the contents, or both. The schedule shows which. Personal liability applies to every policy.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Home Building | Damage to the building from the insured events | Damage during the policy period |
| Contents | Damage to your contents at the home from the insured events | Damage during the policy period |
| Personal Liability | Your legal liability for injury or property damage, at home and elsewhere in Australia | Occurrence during the policy period |

Temporary accommodation and the other extensions are part of the section they appear in. Accidental damage, portable contents and specified valuables are added by endorsement.

If you own a lot in a strata scheme, the body corporate insures the building. Insure your contents, and [[ref:hom.end.e07]] covers the fixtures and improvements in your lot that the body corporate does not insure.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:hom.section.definitions]].
