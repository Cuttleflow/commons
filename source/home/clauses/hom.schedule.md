(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- Your name, and the name of anyone else insured
- The address of the home, and whether it is a house, a townhouse or a lot in a strata scheme
- The policy period, with its start and end dates
- The sections that apply, with the building sum insured and the contents sum insured
- The basic excess, and any flood or earthquake excess
- The limits of the extensions, where they differ from the defaults stated in them
- Any specified valuables and portable contents, with their values
- Any interested party, such as a financier
- The premium, including stamp duty, any emergency services levy, and GST
- Any commission or fee paid to your broker
- The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:hom.cond.governing-law]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
