You pay the basic excess shown in the schedule for each claim under the Home Building and Contents sections. If one event gives rise to claims under both sections, only one basic excess is deducted.

The schedule may show a separate excess for damage by flood (see [[ref:hom.end.e02]]) or by earthquake. That excess replaces the basic excess for that damage. The motor burnout excess applies as [[ref:hom.ext.motor-burnout]] sets out. No excess applies to [[ref:hom.section.liability]].
