If your contents suffer damage at the home during the policy period caused by an insured event listed in [[ref:hom.cover.building]], we will repair or replace them, as set out in [[ref:hom.pay.contents-settlement]].

The most we will pay for any one event is the contents sum insured, subject to [[ref:hom.pay.contents-limits]].
