heading: "Guests' and Domestic Workers' Property"
since: 1.1.0
changed: 1.2.0

We cover property at the home owned by a visitor or a domestic worker, for damage by an insured event, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $2,000) for any one event. [[ref:hom.pay.other-insurance]] applies.
