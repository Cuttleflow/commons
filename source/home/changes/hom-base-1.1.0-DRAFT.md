This release moves the Meridian Home and Contents wording v1.0.0 (July 2026, SharePoint 02 Products) to the Commons, one file per clause, and remediates it, as Max agreed on 1 November 2026 (Linear PLT-642). The v1.0.0 Word file carries a sensitivity label that blocks text extraction, so this release was drafted from the archived open-source v1.0.0 text it was derived from (SharePoint 09 and 10 Archive) and from the Meridian Home and Contents Tenant Configuration Matrix v1.0. Its document reference (2026/HC/0001) is to be reconciled with the v1.0.0 cover page.

- **Statutory posture.** Important Information sets out the consumer duty of reasonable care, the terms that differ from standard cover with references to each clause, a Key Facts Sheet note for each section and a 21-day cooling-off period. A general Insurance Contracts Act condition applies s.54. A standalone sanctions condition, fixed governing law and a jurisdiction clause are added. The v1.0.0 conversational format (emoji tables, rhetorical examples) is replaced by operative clauses.
- **Flood covered as standard** under the statutory definition. The v1.0.0 flood endorsement becomes E02, a separate flood excess only. A 72-hour exclusion for storm and flood applies only when a policy first starts, with exceptions for continuous cover and a newly bought home.
- **Sum insured with a 20% safety net and no under-insurance penalty.** The v1.0.0 proportional reduction for under-insurance is removed. The Tenant Configuration Matrix's "total replacement basis" is not adopted; total replacement can be a later endorsement.
- **Temporary accommodation** is 10% of the building sum insured for up to 12 months, with up to a further 5% where we or our builder cause delay, and 10% of the contents sum insured for contents-only policies (net of rent saved). It applies to a strata lot when the body corporate's building is damaged.
- **Contents limits** follow the matrix: jewellery and watches $3,000 an item ($5,000 with accidental damage) and 20% of the contents sum insured in all, money $500, documents $2,000, contents in the open air $3,000, artwork and collections $10,000 an item. Spoiled food is $2,000 (matrix), guests' and domestic workers' property $2,000, motor burnout $15,000 a motor with a $250 excess.
- **Personal liability** of $20m covers the household at home and in Australia, with defence costs in addition.
- **Removed from v1.0.0:** the legal expenses section, credit card fraud and identity theft, and modifications for disability. They are not part of standard cover and were not in the matrix.
- **Endorsement library re-cut to 13** with operative text, schedule variables and platform triggers: accidental damage, flood excess, portable contents, home business, short-stay letting, specified valuables, strata lot owner, agreed value, heritage, building works, unoccupied home, interested party and Lloyd's underwriters. Withdrawn from the v1.0.0 list: earthquake sub-limit (now an excess in the payment clause), pool liability limit, caravan, musical instruments, student increase, green rebuild, electric vehicle charger (now part of the building), pet vet cover and wedding presents.
- **One WP REVIEW item** for the Wording Partner: whether the summary of departures from standard cover meets s.35 and matches the Key Facts Sheets.
