Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026.

- Important Information, Terms that differ from standard cover: the list is rebuilt to cover every term that gives less cover than standard cover. It now includes the Other Property Exclusions (each one), Deliberate Acts, Illegal Use, Electronic Data, Terrorism, War, Nuclear, the artwork limit, the pair or set rule, matching materials, and the limits on the extensions. A tag asks the Wording Partner to confirm the list and which of the terms the Regulations permit.
- Important Information: a new statement says this wording is for retail clients, that the issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available, and that the wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.
- Damage: now means physical loss of, or physical damage to, property. The words "sudden and accidental" are removed, so that theft, malicious damage and riot are within the definition. Accidental Damage (E01) continues to state its own sudden and accidental test.
- You, Your, and Joint Insureds: each named person is an insured. A fraud, deliberate act, illegal activity or failure to comply by one named insured, or by a member of the household who is not named, does not affect the cover of a named insured who did not know of it, take part in it or consent to it.
- Deliberate Acts, Illegal Use and Other Property Exclusions: these now refer to Joint Insureds. Illegal Use now applies only to illegal activity that you knew of, or had reason to suspect, and did not take reasonable steps to stop.
- Cancellation: you may cancel at any time and we refund the unexpired premium pro rata, with no refund for a building or item after we pay a total loss of it. Our notice to cancel is at least 30 days, or any longer notice the Insurance Contracts Act requires. A tag asks the Wording Partner to confirm the notice provisions.
- Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): replaced with a general condition that states the Act prevails and applies section 54 to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation, not only to the claims conditions.
- Sanctions: restated as a standalone condition naming the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011 (Cth), the Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 (Cth), and the sanctions laws of the United Kingdom, the United States of America and the European Union, to the extent they apply to us.
- Other Insurance: restated so that we do not refuse or reduce a payment because of other insurance, and may seek contribution. Guests' and Domestic Workers' Property no longer depends on the visitor's own insurance not covering the loss; Other Insurance applies.
