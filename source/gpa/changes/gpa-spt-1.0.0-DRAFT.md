This is the first release of the Meridian Sports Injury wording. It is built on the Meridian Group Personal Accident and Sickness base wording version 1.1.0-DRAFT. It differs from the base in these ways:

- The policyholder is a sporting organisation. Covered Person is a registered participant, coach, umpire, referee, judge, official or volunteer. Sporting Activity is a new definition, and Scope of Cover is taking part in a sporting activity and travelling directly to and from it.
- A Medical and Hospital Benefits section is added, with a non-Medicare medical expenses benefit (85% to $2,500 an injury after a $50 excess, by default), and new definitions Exempt Group, Health Fund, Non-Medicare Medical Expenses and Registered Health Practitioner. What the Law Does Not Allow Us to Pay is restated for exempt groups, with WP REVIEW tags.
- Endorsements E01, E02, E03, E06, E07, E10, E12, E14 and E15 are removed. New endorsements: E17 School Sport Carnivals, E18 Concussion Assessment Benefit and E19 Personal Top-Up Cover.
