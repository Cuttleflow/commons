Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026.

- Non-Medicare Medical Expenses: the benefit is removed. It paid 85% of the cost of general treatment such as physiotherapy, chiropractic, dental treatment and ambulance services, and then excluded general treatment within the meaning of the Private Health Insurance Act 2007 (Cth), so it either did nothing or looked like health insurance. Nothing replaces it. What the Law Does Not Allow Us to Pay carries a Wording Partner question on the removal. Endorsement E15 Students no longer applies the benefit, and Endorsement E03 Sports Teams no longer has a non-Medicare medical limit.
- Important Information: adds a statement of who the policy is for, written for businesses and professionals and recognising that a buyer may be a retail client, and a note for issuers about the Product Disclosure Statement and Target Market Determination.
- Important Information, terms that differ from standard cover: the summary now matches the operative text. The first 30 days of sickness cover exclusion no longer says the schedule may vary it; it says the exclusion does not apply on renewal, to a sickness that results from an injury, or to a person who transfers from the policyholder's previous group policy. The pregnancy item now says that pregnancy, childbirth and miscarriage are excluded for weekly sickness benefits, and that a complication is covered only from the 26th week. The summary also now lists the deduction of weekly benefits from capital benefits, the accumulation limit, the elective treatment, alcohol and drugs, criminal and malicious acts, flying, professional sport, intentional self-injury, war and nuclear exclusions, and the 90-day limit on cover outside Australia.
- Living and Travelling Overseas: moved from the obligations section to the payment section, because it limits cover.
- Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): replaced with the library's standard text. Section 54 now applies expressly to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation. The premium-proportion reduction for an untold change, which cut a covered person's own claim because of the policyholder's omission, is removed together with its Wording Partner question. The Declarations and Premium Adjustment condition already recovers the premium.
- One Capital Benefit for Each Injury: one rule now applies. We pay only the one event with the highest percentage for an injury, and we no longer add the percentages of Events 8 to 18. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Capital Benefits, Permanent and Permanent Total Disablement: an event that must be permanent now only needs to start within 12 months of the accident, and is assessed once 12 months have passed. This removes the conflict between the 12-month window and the 12-month test. Event 18 now uses the Fifth Edition of the American Medical Association Guides unless the schedule shows another edition. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Other Payments for the Same Disablement: the weekly benefit is now reduced only by statutory compensation schemes and by employer pay, and no longer by other personal accident, sickness or income protection insurance. The Wording Partner question on this reduction is removed.
- Other Insurance: new clause using the library's standard text. We do not refuse or reduce payment because of other insurance, and may seek contribution as the law allows. Where Endorsement E08 applies, this policy pays weekly and additional benefits only for what the named corporate travel policy does not pay, and both pay their capital benefits in full.
- E08 Corporate Travel Interface: rewritten to match Other Insurance. The named corporate travel policy is a specified underlying policy. The sentence that said nothing reduces a benefit below what this policy would pay alone is removed, because it contradicted the rule before it.
- E01 Voluntary Workers: a voluntary worker aged 80 or over is covered only if the schedule shows age limits for the category that allow it. Age Limits otherwise ends cover at age 75, which conflicted with the reduced capital sum for those aged 80 or over. There is a new schedule variable for the age limits.
- Alcohol and Drugs: the exclusion now applies only to the extent that the alcohol or drug caused or contributed to the injury, and we must show that it did. It no longer applies merely 'while' the person is affected, and the person no longer has to prove otherwise.
- Criminal and Malicious Acts: the exclusion now applies only to the extent that the criminal offence or malicious act caused or contributed to the injury, and acting maliciously is now defined.
- About This Policy: the statement that this wording uses the same definitions and conditions as the individual wording is replaced with a statement that this wording is complete in its own right. A duplicated word in the reference to the definition of You, Your is corrected.
- Sanctions: replaced with the library's standard text, naming the Australian, United Nations, United Kingdom, United States and European Union regimes. A Wording Partner question is added.
- Cancellation: replaced with the library's standard text. The policyholder may cancel at any time for a pro-rata refund less non-refundable government charges, after the premium adjustment and subject to any minimum premium.
