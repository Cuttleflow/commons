This is the first release of the Meridian Accident Medical and Hospital Benefits wording. It is built on the Meridian Group Personal Accident and Sickness base wording version 1.1.0-DRAFT. It differs from the base in these ways:

- A Medical and Hospital Benefits section is added: a non-Medicare medical expenses benefit and a hospital cash benefit, paid only to members of an exempt group under the Private Health Insurance (Health Insurance Business) Rules 2018 (Cth). New definitions: Exempt Group, Health Fund, Hospital, Non-Medicare Medical Expenses and Registered Health Practitioner.
- What the Law Does Not Allow Us to Pay is restated for exempt groups. About This Policy and Important Information say the policy is not private health insurance. WP REVIEW tags ask the Wording Partner to confirm each exempt group and the hospital cash benefit under rule 13.
- The capital and weekly benefits of the base remain available for a category if the schedule shows them.
- Endorsements E01, E02, E03, E06, E07, E10, E12, E14 and E15 are removed. New endorsements: E17 Ambulance at Full Cost and E18 Dental Injury Enhancement.
