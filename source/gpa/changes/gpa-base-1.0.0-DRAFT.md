This is the first Commons release of the Meridian Group Personal Accident and Sickness wording. It replaces Meridian Group Personal Accident and Sickness Policy Wording v1.0.0 (July 2026), which was published outside the Commons. It is built from the Commons individual wording, Meridian Personal Accident and Sickness 2026/PA/0001, so that the two share their definitions, Table of Events, additional benefits, exclusions and conditions. Clauses that differ between the two are listed below. Changes from the July 2026 group wording:

- Everything the individual wording changed from the July 2026 group wording applies here too: the 18-event Table of Events, the 12-month window for capital events, the income-based weekly benefit (default 85%), one waiting period and one benefit period, the s.47-aligned pre-existing condition definition, the new exclusions, the 26-week mental illness limit, the age limits, the accumulation limit, the Insurance Contracts Act savings clause, and the CC BY 4.0 licence.
- "You" means a covered person. The policyholder is a defined party and the only party to the contract. A new Covered Persons' Rights condition states the s.48 right to claim directly.
- New definitions: Category, Covered Person and Policyholder. Capital Sum may be a multiple of annual income. Scope of Cover adds occupational and non-occupational options for each category. Insured Person is removed.
- Important Information addresses the policyholder's duty (s.20B or s.21), says how a covered person's claim can be affected under s.48, and requires the policyholder to give covered persons a copy of the wording.
- Pre-existing conditions are measured from when a person first becomes a covered person, without individual acceptance. The 24-month continuous cover waiver counts the policyholder's previous group policy with any insurer. The first-30-days sickness exclusion does not apply to people transferring from that policy.
- New group conditions: automatic cover for new covered persons up to a free cover limit (default $1,000,000 capital sum and $3,000 a week), declarations and premium adjustment, and notices through the policyholder.
- New Aggregate Limits clause: any one occurrence (default $10,000,000), non-scheduled air travel (default $2,000,000) and policy period (default $20,000,000), with proportional reduction.
- New Policyholder's Records obligation. Telling us about changes covers occupation class mix (more than 10%), headcount (more than 25%) and concentrations of covered persons.
- Who We Pay allows the weekly benefit to be paid to the policyholder where it continued to pay the covered person, with the covered person's consent.
- The endorsement library has 16 group endorsements: voluntary workers (with domestic help for those without income), journey cover, sports teams, overseas secondment, named hazardous pursuits, fly-in fly-out, extended benefit period, corporate travel interface, weekly benefits to age 70, passive war zones, workers compensation top-up, key persons, Lloyd's, enterprise agreement terms, students, and a specific condition exclusion. The individual endorsements for agreed value, business expenses and a single insured's circumstances are not carried over.
