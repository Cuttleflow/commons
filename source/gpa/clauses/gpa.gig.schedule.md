(Completed separately for each policy.)

The schedule shows:

- The policyholder, its ABN and address, the platform, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:gpa.cond.governing-law]])
- The policy period, with its start and end dates
- The kinds of platform work covered (see [[ref:gpa.gig.def.platform-work]])
- The benefit level, and for it: the capital sum; the percentage of earnings and the maximum weekly benefit; the waiting period; the benefit period; and whether weekly sickness benefits apply
- The aggregate limits (see [[ref:gpa.pay.aggregate-limits]]) and the accumulation limit, if different from the defaults
- The limits of the additional benefits, where they differ from the defaults in [[ref:gpa.section.additional-benefits]]
- The premium basis (a rate for each engaged hour, by kind of vehicle, or for each completed job), the deposit premium, the minimum premium, and the declaration period (see [[ref:gpa.cond.declarations-and-adjustment]])
- The premium, including stamp duty and GST
- The insurer and its licence
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
