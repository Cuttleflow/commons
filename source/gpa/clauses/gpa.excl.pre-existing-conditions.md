heading: Pre-existing Conditions
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay weekly sickness benefits for disablement caused by a pre-existing condition. We do not pay any benefit for an injury to the extent that a pre-existing condition caused or contributed to the disablement or event.

This exclusion stops applying to a pre-existing condition once you have been continuously covered for 24 months. Continuous cover includes time immediately before you became a covered person during which you were covered for the same kind of benefit under the policyholder's previous group personal accident policy, with any insurer.

This exclusion is subject to s.47 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): we will not rely on it for a condition that you were not aware of, and that a reasonable person in your circumstances could not have been expected to be aware of, when you became a covered person.
