type: definition
heading: Covered Person
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A natural person who, at the time of the accident (or when the sickness first shows itself), holds an active account on the platform and performs platform work through it, other than as an employee of the policyholder, and who is at least 18 years old and inside the age limits in [[ref:gpa.pay.age-limits]]. A person stops being a covered person when their account is closed or suspended, or when the policy ends or is cancelled, whichever is first.
