type: definition
heading: Hospital
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A facility that is licensed or approved as a hospital under the law of the state or territory, or the country, where it is. It does not include a nursing home, an aged care facility, a hospice, a facility for the treatment of alcohol or drug dependence, or a rehabilitation facility while you are not receiving acute care there.
