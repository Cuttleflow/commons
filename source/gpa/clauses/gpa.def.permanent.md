type: definition
heading: Permanent
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Lasting, or in the opinion of a medical practitioner we accept expected to last, at least 12 months from the date of the injury, and beyond hope of improvement at the end of that time. We assess whether a loss is permanent once 12 months have passed since the injury, even if that is after the 12-month period in [[ref:gpa.cover.capital-benefits]].
