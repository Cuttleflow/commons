**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

**Who this policy is for.** This wording is written for organisations that buy cover for an exempt group. Some buyers, such as small clubs and small businesses, may be retail clients under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). Where a buyer is a retail client, the issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.

**Not health insurance.** The medical and hospital benefits are paid only to members of an exempt group. This policy is not private health insurance, does not meet any requirement to hold private health insurance or overseas visitors or overseas student health cover, and does not count for any Medicare levy surcharge or lifetime health cover purpose.

**The policyholder's duty when it applies.** If this policy is a consumer insurance contract, the policyholder has a duty under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated. Otherwise, the policyholder has the duty of disclosure in s.21 of that Act. If the policyholder does not comply, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

**Terms that differ from standard cover.** This policy differs from the standard cover the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) sets for sickness and accident contracts. In particular:
- it pays medical and hospital benefits for injury only, not sickness, unless the schedule shows otherwise;
- it pays only a percentage of non-Medicare medical expenses, after an excess and up to a limit (see [[ref:gpa.xmed.cover.medical-expenses]]);
- the hospital cash benefit starts after 48 hours in hospital and is paid for up to 365 days (see [[ref:gpa.xmed.cover.hospital-cash]]);
- it does not cover a pre-existing condition (see [[ref:gpa.excl.pre-existing-conditions]]), or an injury caused by alcohol or drug use, a criminal act, intentional self-injury, war, nuclear risks or hazardous pursuits;
- all benefits are subject to the aggregate limits (see [[ref:gpa.pay.aggregate-limits]]).

**Cooling-off period.** The policyholder may cancel this policy within 21 days of the date it starts, and within 21 days of each renewal, by telling us in writing. If no claim has been made, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover.

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, covered persons may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
