---
id: gpa.excl.criminal-acts
type: exclusion
heading: Criminal and Malicious Acts
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay any benefit for an injury to the extent that it was caused or contributed to by you committing, or attempting to commit, a criminal offence punishable by imprisonment, or by you acting maliciously.

Acting maliciously means acting with the intention of harming another person or damaging another person's property.
