This policy pays fixed benefits to people who perform platform work through the policyholder's platform, if they suffer an injury while they are engaged on that work during the policy period, or (if the schedule shows sickness cover) a sickness. It pays a lump sum for death and for the permanent disablements listed in the [[ref:gpa.cover.capital-benefits|heading]] clause, and a weekly benefit while a covered person is temporarily disabled and cannot work.

It is bought by the operator of a digital platform (the policyholder) for the people who accept work through that platform. The policyholder pays the premium. The policyholder may buy it to meet an obligation in a minimum standards order of the Fair Work Commission; whether this cover meets that obligation is for the policyholder to decide, and we do not say that it does. The policyholder is the only party to the contract. Each covered person can claim the benefits under this policy directly, as [[ref:gpa.cond.covered-persons-rights]] explains.

It is a benefit policy, not an indemnity policy. The capital benefits are fixed amounts that do not depend on financial loss. The weekly benefits replace part of a covered person's earnings from platform work and are measured against them, as [[ref:gpa.pay.weekly-benefit-amount]] sets out.

It is not workers compensation, compulsory third party motor insurance, life insurance or private health insurance, and it does not replace any of them. It does not cover damage to vehicles or goods. It cannot pay any medical expense for which a Medicare benefit is payable (see [[ref:gpa.pay.medicare]]).

This policy, together with the schedule, the policyholder's proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms the contract of insurance between the policyholder and us. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:gpa.section.definitions]]. In this wording "you" means a covered person, as [[ref:gpa.def.you-your]] says.

This wording is the Meridian Group Personal Accident and Sickness base wording with the changes needed for people who work through digital platforms. It is a complete wording in its own right.
