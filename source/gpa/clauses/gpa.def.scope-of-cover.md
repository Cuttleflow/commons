since: 1.0.0

---

The times when this policy covers you, shown in the schedule for your category:

- **24 hours a day:** at all times, anywhere in the world, subject to [[ref:gpa.obl.residence-and-travel]];
- **occupational:** while you are working for the policyholder, or engaged in an activity the policyholder has authorised, and while travelling directly between your home and that work or activity;
- **non-occupational:** at all times except while you are working, and except while travelling directly between your home and your place of work.

If the schedule shows no scope of cover for your category, 24 hours a day applies.
