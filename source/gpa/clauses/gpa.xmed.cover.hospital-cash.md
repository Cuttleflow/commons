This clause applies only if the schedule shows the hospital cash benefit for your category, and only while you are a member of an exempt group.

If an injury you suffer during the policy period and within the scope of cover results in you being admitted to a hospital as an inpatient within 30 days after the accident, and you stay in hospital continuously for more than 48 hours, we will pay the daily amount shown in the schedule for each full day you stay in hospital after the first 48 hours. We pay for no more than 365 days for any one injury. Unless the schedule shows otherwise, we pay no hospital cash benefit for sickness.

[WP REVIEW: a daily benefit calculated by time in hospital is health insurance business under rule 13 of the Private Health Insurance (Health Insurance Business) Rules 2018 (Cth) unless the person is in an exempt group. Confirm the exempt group gate in this clause and in the definition of Exempt Group is sufficient.]
