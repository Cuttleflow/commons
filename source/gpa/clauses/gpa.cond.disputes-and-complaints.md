If the policyholder or a covered person is unhappy with our service or a decision, contact us at the details in the schedule. We will acknowledge your complaint within one business day and give you our decision within 30 calendar days.

If they are not satisfied with our response, or we have not responded within 30 calendar days, they may take the complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), a free and independent dispute resolution service: www.afca.org.au, 1800 931 678, info@afca.org.au.
