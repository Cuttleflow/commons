**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

**Who this policy is for.** This wording is written for sporting organisations, which buy it for their participants, officials and volunteers. A small club may be a retail client under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), and a participant who buys extra cover is a retail client. Where that is so, the issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.

**The policyholder's duty when it applies.** What the policyholder must tell us depends on the kind of contract this is.

- If this policy is a consumer insurance contract (taken out wholly or mainly for the personal, domestic or household purposes of the people it covers), the policyholder has a duty under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated.
- Otherwise, the policyholder has the duty of disclosure in s.21 of that Act: it must tell us every matter it knows, or that a reasonable person in its circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure and on what terms, other than a matter that reduces the risk, is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we waive.

If the policyholder does not comply, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows. Covered persons do not have to tell us anything when they register.

**Terms that differ from standard cover.** This policy differs from the standard cover the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) sets for sickness and accident contracts. In particular:
- it covers a covered person only while taking part in a sporting activity, and while travelling directly to and from it (see [[ref:gpa.def.scope-of-cover]]);
- it does not cover a pre-existing condition (see [[ref:gpa.excl.pre-existing-conditions]]);
- it does not cover professional sport (see [[ref:gpa.excl.professional-sport]]), or an injury caused by alcohol or drug use, a criminal act, intentional self-injury, war or nuclear risks;
- weekly benefits start only after the waiting period, stop at the end of the benefit period, and are limited to a percentage of income;
- the medical expenses benefit pays a percentage of non-Medicare medical expenses only, after an excess and up to a limit (see [[ref:gpa.xmed.cover.medical-expenses]]);
- all benefits are subject to the aggregate limits (see [[ref:gpa.pay.aggregate-limits]]).

**Copies for covered persons.** The policyholder must make a summary of the cover available to each covered person when they register, and a copy of this wording on request.

**Cooling-off period.** The policyholder may cancel this policy within 21 days of the date it starts, and within 21 days of each renewal, by telling us in writing. If no claim has been made, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover.

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, covered persons may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
