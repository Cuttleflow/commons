This clause applies only if the schedule shows the non-Medicare medical expenses benefit for your category, and only while you are a member of an exempt group.

If you suffer an injury during the policy period and within the scope of cover, we will pay the percentage shown in the schedule of the non-Medicare medical expenses you incur for that injury within 52 weeks after the accident, after the excess, up to the limit for each injury. Unless the schedule shows otherwise:

- the percentage is 85%;
- the excess is $50 for each injury, and no excess applies to an expense of which a health fund has paid part; and
- the limit for each injury is $2,500.

We pay the expenses to you, or with your agreement to the provider. You must give us the accounts and receipts, and tell us what any health fund or scheme paid. [[ref:gpa.xmed.pay.medicare]] limits what we may pay.
