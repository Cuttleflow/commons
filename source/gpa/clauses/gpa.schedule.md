(Completed separately for each policy.)

The schedule shows:

- The policyholder, its ABN and address, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:gpa.cond.governing-law]])
- The policy period, with its start and end dates
- Each category of covered persons, and for each category:
  - who is in it, and the number of covered persons (or the declaration basis);
  - the occupation class;
  - the scope of cover: 24 hours a day, occupational, or non-occupational;
  - the capital sum, as a fixed amount or a multiple of annual income, and any maximum;
  - whether weekly benefits apply, and if so the percentage of income, the maximum weekly benefit, the waiting period and the benefit period;
  - whether weekly sickness benefits apply;
  - any age limits that differ from [[ref:gpa.pay.age-limits]]
- The aggregate limits (see [[ref:gpa.pay.aggregate-limits]]) and the accumulation limit, if different from the defaults
- The limits of the additional benefits, where they differ from the defaults in [[ref:gpa.section.additional-benefits]]
- The free cover limit for new covered persons (see [[ref:gpa.cond.automatic-cover]])
- The premium basis, the deposit premium, the minimum premium, and the declaration date (see [[ref:gpa.cond.declarations-and-adjustment]])
- The premium, including stamp duty and GST
- The insurer and its licence
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
