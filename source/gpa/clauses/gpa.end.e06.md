For the category named in the schedule for this endorsement, the scope of cover includes travel between your home and a remote work site on a roster arranged by the policyholder, including air travel on a charter flight the policyholder arranges, and time at accommodation the policyholder provides at or near the site. The non-scheduled air travel limit in [[ref:gpa.pay.aggregate-limits]] applies to charter flights.

Schedule variables: category; work sites; non-scheduled air travel limit for this category.
