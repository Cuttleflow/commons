---
id: gpa.obl.tell-us-about-changes
type: obligation
heading: Tell Us About Changes
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The policyholder must tell us during the policy period, as soon as reasonably practicable, if:

- the work done by the people in a category changes so that a higher occupation class would apply, or the proportion of people in each occupation class changes by more than 10%;
- the number of covered persons in a category changes by more than 25%; or
- covered persons start to travel together, or work in one place, in numbers greater than those shown in the proposal.

We will tell the policyholder whether the change affects the cover or the premium.
