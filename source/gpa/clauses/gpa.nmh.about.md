This policy pays part of the non-Medicare medical expenses of an injury, and a daily hospital cash benefit if the schedule shows one, to the people it covers. It is written only for exempt groups: groups of people for whom a general insurer may pay these benefits without carrying on health insurance business (see [[ref:gpa.xmed.def.exempt-group]] and [[ref:gpa.xmed.pay.medicare]]). It also pays capital and weekly benefits for a category if the schedule shows them.

It is bought by an organisation (the policyholder), such as a sporting body, a volunteer organisation, a school or university, an employer with staff working overseas, or an education agent for visiting students, for the categories of people shown in the schedule. The policyholder is the only party to the contract. Each covered person can claim the benefits under this policy directly, as [[ref:gpa.cond.covered-persons-rights]] explains.

It is not workers compensation, life insurance or private health insurance, and it does not replace any of them. It never pays a medical expense for which a Medicare benefit is payable, or any gap between a Medicare benefit and the fee charged.

This policy, together with the schedule, the policyholder's proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms the contract of insurance between the policyholder and us. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:gpa.section.definitions]]. In this wording "you" means a covered person, as [[ref:gpa.def.you-your]] says.

This wording is the Meridian Group Personal Accident and Sickness base wording with medical and hospital benefits added. It is a complete wording in its own right.
