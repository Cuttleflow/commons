since: 1.0.0

---

Where the schedule shows the premium is adjustable, the premium shown is a deposit premium based on the estimated number of covered persons (or wages, volunteers, players or students, as the schedule shows) in each category. Within 30 days after the policy period ends, the policyholder must declare the actual figures. We will then adjust the premium at the rates used for the deposit premium, subject to the minimum premium shown in the schedule. If the policyholder does not declare, we may adjust the premium on a reasonable estimate and will tell the policyholder how we made it.
