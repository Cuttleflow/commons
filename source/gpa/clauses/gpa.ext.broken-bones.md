If an injury causes you to fracture a bone, and a medical practitioner confirms the fracture by X-ray or other imaging, we will pay the percentage below of the broken bones limit. Unless the schedule shows otherwise, the broken bones limit is $7,500.

| Bone | Percentage |
|---|---|
| Skull, neck or spine (excluding the coccyx) | 100% |
| Pelvis or thigh bone | 50% |
| Arm, leg, collarbone, shoulder blade, jaw or kneecap | 25% |
| Ribs, hand, wrist, foot, ankle, coccyx or breastbone | 10% |
| Finger, toe or nose | 5% |

We pay for the highest-percentage bone only for any one injury. We do not pay this benefit if we pay a capital benefit for the same injury.
