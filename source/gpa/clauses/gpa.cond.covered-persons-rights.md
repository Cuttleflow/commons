Covered persons are not parties to this contract and do not pay the premium. Under s.48 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), a covered person has a right to recover from us the benefits this policy provides for them, in accordance with this policy, as if they were the insured. A covered person has the same obligations under this policy in making a claim, and we have the same defences, as that section provides.

The policyholder does not hold this policy on trust for covered persons, does not act for us, and does not give covered persons advice about it. The policyholder receives no payment from us for arranging cover for covered persons, unless the schedule says otherwise.
