This policy pays fixed benefits, and non-Medicare medical expenses, to the people a sporting organisation registers or appoints, if they suffer an injury while taking part in a sporting activity during the policy period. It pays a lump sum for death and for the permanent disablements listed in the [[ref:gpa.cover.capital-benefits|heading]] clause, a weekly benefit for loss of income while a covered person is temporarily disabled, and part of the non-Medicare medical expenses of the injury.

It is bought by a sporting organisation, such as a national or state body, an association or a club (the policyholder), for its registered participants, officials and volunteers. The policyholder is the only party to the contract. Each covered person can claim the benefits under this policy directly, as [[ref:gpa.cond.covered-persons-rights]] explains.

The capital and weekly benefits are fixed amounts and do not depend on financial loss. The medical expenses benefit pays a share of actual expenses. It is paid only because people taking part in sport are an exempt group for health insurance purposes (see [[ref:gpa.xmed.pay.medicare]]).

It is not workers compensation, life insurance or private health insurance, and it does not replace any of them. It cannot pay any medical expense for which a Medicare benefit is payable, or any gap between a Medicare benefit and the fee charged.

This policy, together with the schedule, the policyholder's proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms the contract of insurance between the policyholder and us. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:gpa.section.definitions]]. In this wording "you" means a covered person, as [[ref:gpa.def.you-your]] says.

This wording is the Meridian Group Personal Accident and Sickness base wording with the changes needed for sport. It is a complete wording in its own right.
