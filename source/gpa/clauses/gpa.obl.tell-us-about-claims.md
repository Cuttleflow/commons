---
id: gpa.obl.tell-us-about-claims
type: obligation
heading: Tell Us About Claims
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You, or the policyholder for you, must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after an accident, injury or sickness that may lead to a claim. For weekly benefits, give us a medical certificate covering the start of the disablement, and further certificates when we reasonably ask for them.

Give us the information and documents we reasonably need to assess the claim, including evidence of your income and of other payments under [[ref:gpa.pay.offsets]], at your own cost. The policyholder must give us any record it holds that we reasonably need, such as your income, your category and the dates you were covered.
