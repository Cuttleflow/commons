heading: Scope of Cover
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The time when this policy covers you: while you are taking part in a sporting activity as a participant, coach, umpire, referee, judge, official or volunteer, or helping to run it, and while travelling directly to and from it. This replaces any other scope of cover shown in the schedule.
