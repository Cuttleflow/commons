since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

1. Tell the policyholder, or contact the policyholder's broker or us directly at the details shown in the schedule. You can claim directly, without the policyholder (see [[ref:gpa.cond.covered-persons-rights]]).
2. Tell us what happened and when, and the name of the medical practitioner treating you (see [[ref:gpa.obl.tell-us-about-claims]]).
3. For weekly benefits, send us a medical certificate and evidence of your income for the last 12 months. The policyholder will confirm your category and the dates you were covered.
4. Tell us about any workers compensation, transport accident, sick leave or other insurance payments you receive or have claimed.
5. We will acknowledge your claim, send you a claim form within 7 days and keep you informed.
