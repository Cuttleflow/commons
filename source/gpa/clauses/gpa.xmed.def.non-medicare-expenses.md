type: definition
heading: Non-Medicare Medical Expenses
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Reasonable and necessary expenses you incur for the treatment of an injury, given or prescribed by a medical practitioner or a registered health practitioner, including physiotherapy, chiropractic, osteopathy, dental treatment, ambulance transport by a licensed ambulance service, and medical aids and appliances prescribed for the injury. They do not include:

- any amount for a professional service for which a Medicare benefit is payable, or any part of the difference between that Medicare benefit and the fee charged;
- any amount a health fund, a workers compensation or transport accident scheme, or another statutory scheme has paid or must pay; or
- treatment given outside Australia, unless the schedule shows that your category is covered outside Australia.
