heading: How Much We Pay Each Week
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Each week we pay the lower of:

- the weekly benefit for your category; and
- the percentage of your income shown in the schedule for your category. Unless the schedule shows otherwise, it is 85%.

We pay weekly benefits fortnightly in arrears, for each day of disablement at one-seventh of the weekly amount. We pay no weekly benefit for the waiting period.
