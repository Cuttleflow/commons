**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

**Who this policy is for.** This wording is written for the operators of digital platforms, which buy it for the people who work through them. A platform operator is usually a wholesale client under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). Where the policyholder is a retail client, or where covered persons may buy extra cover themselves, the issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.

**The policyholder's duty.** Before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated, the policyholder has the duty of disclosure in s.21 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): it must tell us every matter it knows, or that a reasonable person in its circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure and on what terms. It need not tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we waive. If it does not, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, as that Act allows. Because covered persons claim under s.48 of that Act, a claim can be affected by what the policyholder told us. Covered persons do not have to tell us anything.

**Terms that differ from standard cover.** This policy differs from the standard cover the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) sets for sickness and accident contracts. In particular:
- it covers a covered person only during engaged time on platform work (see [[ref:gpa.def.scope-of-cover]] and [[ref:gpa.gig.def.engaged-time]]);
- it does not cover a pre-existing condition (see [[ref:gpa.excl.pre-existing-conditions]]);
- weekly benefits start only after the waiting period, stop at the end of the benefit period, and are limited to a percentage of earnings from platform work (see [[ref:gpa.pay.weekly-benefit-amount]] and [[ref:gpa.def.income]]);
- weekly benefits are reduced by workers compensation, transport accident and other statutory payments for the same disablement (see [[ref:gpa.pay.offsets]]);
- it does not cover an injury caused by alcohol or drug use, a criminal act, intentional self-injury, war or nuclear risks, or hazardous pursuits (see [[ref:gpa.section.exclusions]]);
- all benefits are subject to the aggregate limits (see [[ref:gpa.pay.aggregate-limits]]).

**Copies for covered persons.** The policyholder must make a summary of the cover available to each covered person, and a copy of this wording on request.

**Cooling-off period.** The policyholder may cancel this policy within 21 days of the date it starts, and within 21 days of each renewal, by telling us in writing. If no claim has been made, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover.

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, covered persons may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
