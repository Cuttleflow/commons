---
id: gpa.def.pre-existing-condition
type: definition
heading: Pre-existing Condition
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A condition, illness or injury:

- for which you received treatment, medication or advice from a medical practitioner in the 12 months before the date you first became a covered person; or
- whose symptoms you were aware of, or a reasonable person in your circumstances would have been aware of, in the 3 months before that date.

A condition that we have accepted in writing for you, and that is shown in the schedule, is not a pre-existing condition.
