heading: Covered Person
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A natural person who, at the time of the accident, is registered with or appointed by the policyholder (or by a club or association affiliated with it and named in the schedule) as a participant, coach, umpire, referee, judge, official or volunteer, is within a category, and is inside the age limits for that category. A person stops being a covered person when their registration or appointment ends, or when the policy ends or is cancelled, whichever is first.
