**The policyholder** may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired period of insurance on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges, after the premium adjustment in [[ref:gpa.cond.declarations-and-adjustment]] and subject to any minimum premium shown in the schedule. If the policyholder cancels within the cooling-off period, the refund is as [[ref:gpa.important-information]] states.

**We** may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving the policyholder written notice as that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis.

A covered person cannot cancel or vary this policy. When this policy is cancelled, cover ends for every covered person. Cancellation does not affect a claim for an injury that happened, or a sickness that first showed itself, before cancellation.
