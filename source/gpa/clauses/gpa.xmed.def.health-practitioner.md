type: definition
heading: Registered Health Practitioner
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A person registered under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law, as in force in the state or territory where the treatment is given, to practise a health profession, who is practising within that registration. It does not include you, a member of your family, or a person who lives with you.
