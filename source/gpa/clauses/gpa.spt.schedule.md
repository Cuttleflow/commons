(Completed separately for each policy.)

The schedule shows:

- The policyholder, its ABN and address, the sports it administers, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:gpa.cond.governing-law]])
- The policy period, with its start and end dates
- Each category of covered persons (for example senior players, junior players, masters players, coaches and officials, volunteers), and for each category:
  - the sport's risk category and the number of registered persons (or the declaration basis);
  - the capital sum, and any lower capital sum for juniors;
  - whether loss of income (weekly) benefits apply, and if so the percentage of income, the maximum weekly benefit, the waiting period and the benefit period;
  - whether the non-Medicare medical expenses benefit applies, and its percentage, excess and limit for each injury;
  - that the category is an exempt group (see [[ref:gpa.xmed.def.exempt-group]])
- The aggregate limits (see [[ref:gpa.pay.aggregate-limits]])
- The premium basis (a rate for each registered person by risk category), the deposit premium, the minimum premium, and the declaration date (see [[ref:gpa.cond.declarations-and-adjustment]])
- The premium, including stamp duty and GST
- The insurer and its licence
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
