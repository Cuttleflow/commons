[[ref:gpa.excl.hazardous-pursuits]] and [[ref:gpa.excl.flying]] do not apply to the pursuits named in the schedule for this endorsement, for the categories named, while covered persons take part in them as amateurs, under the rules of the governing body for the pursuit where there is one, and holding any licence or qualification it requires.

Schedule variables: named pursuits and categories; conditions for each pursuit; any capital sum limit for injury during the pursuit.
