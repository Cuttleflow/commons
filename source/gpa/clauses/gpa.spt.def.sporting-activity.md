id: gpa.spt.def.sporting-activity
type: definition
heading: Sporting Activity
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A game, match, competition, training session, practice, trial or other activity in a sport shown in the schedule that is organised, sanctioned or supervised by the policyholder or by a club or association affiliated with it, and the official functions and administration of the policyholder.
