heading: Who We Pay
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We pay every benefit to you, except that the Event 1 benefit and the benefits that follow it are paid to your legal personal representative. If you are unable to manage your affairs, we may pay a person legally entitled to act for you.

If the policyholder has continued to pay you while you are disabled, we may pay the weekly benefit for that period to the policyholder instead, but only with your written consent.

Payment as this clause allows discharges our liability for the amount paid.
