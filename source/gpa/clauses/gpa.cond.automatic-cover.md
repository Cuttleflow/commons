---
id: gpa.cond.automatic-cover
type: condition
heading: Automatic Cover for New Covered Persons
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A person who joins a category during the policy period is covered automatically from the date they join, on the terms for that category, if their capital sum and weekly benefit are within the free cover limit shown in the schedule. A person whose benefits would exceed the free cover limit is covered up to that limit until we accept them in writing for the full amount.

Unless the schedule shows otherwise, the free cover limit is a capital sum of $1,000,000 and a weekly benefit of $3,000.
