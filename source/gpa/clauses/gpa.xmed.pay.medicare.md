The Health Insurance Act 1973 (Cth) does not allow us to pay any medical expense for a professional service given in Australia for which a Medicare benefit is payable, or any gap between the Medicare benefit and the fee charged. The Private Health Insurance Act 2007 (Cth) allows only a registered private health insurer to carry on health insurance business.

So we pay the benefits in [[ref:gpa.xmed.section.medical]] only to a covered person who is a member of an exempt group when the treatment is given or the hospital stay happens, and only for expenses that are non-Medicare medical expenses. We will not pay any benefit, or part of a benefit, to the extent that paying it would breach those Acts or would be health insurance business under them.
