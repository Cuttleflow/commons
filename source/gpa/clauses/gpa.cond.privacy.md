We collect, use and disclose personal information about covered persons, including health information, to administer this policy and manage claims, in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and our privacy policy. The policyholder may give us personal information about covered persons, and must tell them that it does so and where to find our privacy policy. Our privacy policy explains how a covered person can access and correct their information and how to complain about a privacy matter.
