For the categories named in the schedule for this endorsement, the benefits shown in the schedule are those an enterprise agreement or award requires the policyholder to provide. Where a term of this policy gives less than the agreement requires for those benefits, the agreement's term applies to the extent shown in the schedule. This endorsement does not make us a party to the agreement.

Schedule variables: categories; agreement name and clause; the terms that apply.
