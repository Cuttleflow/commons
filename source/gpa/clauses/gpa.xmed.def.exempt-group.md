A group of people shown in the schedule for your category that the Private Health Insurance (Health Insurance Business) Rules 2018 (Cth), as in force when the treatment is given, treat as outside health insurance business for cover of the kind in [[ref:gpa.xmed.section.medical]]. The schedule may show any of these groups:

- people taking part in a sporting activity as a participant, coach, umpire, referee, judge or official, or helping to run it, and the elected or appointed officials of a sporting organisation, including while travelling directly to and from the activity;
- volunteers working without pay for the policyholder;
- students taking part in an activity organised and supervised by their school, college or university;
- members of a youth organisation taking part in its activities;
- Australian residents temporarily employed outside Australia, and their dependants, while they are outside Australia because of that employment; and
- people temporarily in Australia who are not eligible for Medicare.
