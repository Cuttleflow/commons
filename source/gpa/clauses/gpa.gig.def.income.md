---
id: gpa.gig.def.income
type: definition
heading: Income
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Your average weekly earnings from platform work through the platform, before tax, over the 12 weeks before the disablement started, or over the weeks you have performed platform work if that is shorter, as shown in the platform's records. Earnings include amounts the platform paid or credited to you for platform work and tips paid to you through the platform. They do not include reimbursed expenses. If Endorsement E19 applies, your earnings from the other platforms named in the schedule are included.
