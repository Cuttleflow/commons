This policy pays fixed benefits to the people it covers if they suffer an injury, or (if the schedule shows sickness cover for their category) a sickness, during the policy period. It pays a lump sum for death and for the permanent disablements listed in the [[ref:gpa.cover.capital-benefits|heading]] clause, and a weekly benefit while a covered person is temporarily disabled and cannot work.

It is bought by an employer, club, association or other organisation (the policyholder) for the categories of people shown in the schedule. The policyholder is the only party to the contract. Each covered person can claim the benefits under this policy directly, as [[ref:gpa.cond.covered-persons-rights]] explains.

It is a benefit policy, not an indemnity policy. The capital benefits are fixed amounts that do not depend on financial loss. The weekly benefits replace part of a covered person's income and are measured against it, as [[ref:gpa.pay.weekly-benefit-amount]] sets out.

It is not workers compensation, life insurance or private health insurance, and it does not replace any of them. It cannot pay any medical expense for which a Medicare benefit is payable (see [[ref:gpa.pay.medicare]]).

This policy, together with the schedule, the policyholder's proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms the contract of insurance between the policyholder and us. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:gpa.section.definitions]]. In this wording "you" means a covered person, as the [[ref:gpa.def.you-your]] definition says.

This wording uses the same definitions, Table of Events, exclusions and conditions as the Meridian Personal Accident and Sickness wording for one person (2026/PA/0001), with group clauses added where a group policy differs.
