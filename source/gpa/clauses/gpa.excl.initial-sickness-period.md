---
id: gpa.excl.initial-sickness-period
type: exclusion
heading: First 30 Days of Sickness Cover
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay weekly sickness benefits for a sickness that first shows itself in the first 30 days after you first become covered for weekly sickness benefits under this policy. This exclusion does not apply on renewal, to a sickness that results from an injury, or if you were covered for weekly sickness benefits under the policyholder's previous group personal accident policy immediately before you became a covered person.
