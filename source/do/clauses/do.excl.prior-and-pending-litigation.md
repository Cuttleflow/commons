type: exclusion
heading: Prior and Pending Litigation
since: 1.3.0
changed: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Claims arising from proceedings pending on or before the Retroactive date, or from substantially the same facts. See [[ref:do.def.retroactive-date]] for the date that applies where the schedule states “Full Prior Acts”.
