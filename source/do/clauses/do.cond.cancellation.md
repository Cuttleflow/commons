The company may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired period of insurance on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges.

We may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving the company written notice as that Act requires, and in any event at least 30 days' written notice. Where we are entitled to cancel, we will also give each insured person written notice individually. We refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis.

Cover already provided for notified claims and notified circumstances is unaffected by cancellation.
