(a) Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts, or modifies the operation of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) (“the Act”). Where any provision of this policy is inconsistent with the Act, the Act prevails.

(b) Under section 54 of the Act, our rights in respect of an act or omission by an insured after the relevant contract of insurance was entered into — including under [[ref:do.obl.notify-claims]], [[ref:do.obl.do-not-admit-liability]] and [[ref:do.obl.cooperate]] — are limited as set out in section 54 of the Act. Except to the extent the claim is fraudulent, we may not refuse to pay a claim by reason only of such an act or omission, but may reduce our liability to the extent we can show we were prejudiced by it.

(c) Under section 40(3) of the Act, see [[ref:do.obl.notify-circumstances]], which implements section 40(3) of the Act — late notification of a claim arising from a properly notified circumstance is not, of itself, a ground to refuse the claim.

(d) This clause does not limit any other right the insured has under the Act.
