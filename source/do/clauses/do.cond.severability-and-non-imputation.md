Each insured person is treated as if they have their own separate policy. Conduct and knowledge are not attributed between insured persons. This applies to every exclusion, condition and obligation in this policy that turns on what an insured person knew, did or failed to do, including [[ref:do.excl.dishonesty-and-fraud]], [[ref:do.excl.personal-profit-and-illegal-remuneration]], [[ref:do.excl.insured-versus-insured]], [[ref:do.excl.prior-known-matters]] and [[ref:do.cond.duty-of-disclosure]].

Side B pays the company for an insured person's loss. Whether an exclusion applies to that loss is decided by the conduct and knowledge of that insured person only, and not by the conduct or knowledge of the company or of any other insured person.

For Side C, and for the company's duty of disclosure, what the company knew means what was known to a member of the control group (see [[ref:do.def.control-group]]). The knowledge or conduct of a member of the control group is not attributed to any other insured person.
