heading: Severability and Non-Imputation
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Each insured person is treated as if they have their own separate policy. Conduct and knowledge are not attributed between insured persons.

When assessing the company's duty of disclosure, we consider only knowledge held by the persons responsible for the company's insurance arrangements.
