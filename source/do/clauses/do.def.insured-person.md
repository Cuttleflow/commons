id: do.def.insured-person
type: definition
heading: Insured person
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

means any natural person who is, was, or during the policy period becomes: (a) a director of the company (including executive, non-executive, independent, alternate, shadow, and de facto directors); (b) the company secretary; (c) an officer of the company within the meaning of section 9 of the Corporations Act 2001 — including the CEO, CFO, COO, CRO, CIO, CLO/general counsel, and any person who participates in making decisions that affect the whole or a substantial part of the company's business; (d) a member of any board committee (audit, risk, remuneration, nomination, ESG, or other); (e) any Employee in a managerial or supervisory role, but only for claims alleging a wrongful act in that capacity; (f) an equivalent person in any Subsidiary; and (g) the lawful spouse, domestic partner, estate, heir, or legal representative of any person described in (a)–(f), but only for claims arising from that person's wrongful acts.
