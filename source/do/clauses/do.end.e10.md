It converts this policy to a run-off (tail) policy following a change of control, merger, or cessation of listing.

**Trigger Event**

This endorsement takes effect upon the occurrence of: change of control / merger / scheme of arrangement / delisting / voluntary administration / other (as specified in the schedule).

**Run-Off Reporting Period**

This policy will cover claims first made against any insured during the period shown in the schedule (12, 24, 36, or 72 months) following the trigger event, for wrongful acts that occurred before the trigger event.

**No New Wrongful Acts**

This policy does not cover wrongful acts committed after the trigger event.

**Non-Cancellable**

Once this endorsement is activated and the premium is paid, neither we nor the company may cancel the run-off cover.

**Premium**

The run-off premium is as specified in the schedule — typically 100–300% of the expiring annual premium depending on duration.

Schedule variables: Trigger event; run-off period (12, 24, 36 or 72 months); run-off premium.
