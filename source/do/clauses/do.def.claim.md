heading: Claim
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

means any of the following first made against an insured during the policy period: (a) any written demand for compensation; (b) any writ, summons, statement of claim, cross-claim, counterclaim, or originating process in any civil, criminal, or administrative proceeding; (c) any Securities claim; (d) any Derivative action; (e) any Investigation; (f) any extradition proceeding or warrant for arrest in connection with a wrongful act; and (g) any formal demand by a liquidator, administrator, or receiver for payment of money or return of property. Two or more claims arising from the same, similar, or related wrongful acts, or from the same or related facts, matters, or circumstances, are treated as a single claim first made when the earliest was first made.
