id: do.excl.major-transaction-ipo
type: exclusion
heading: Major Transaction — IPO
since: 1.3.0
changed: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Claims arising from the company's initial public offering — unless a separate IPO policy is in place or the IPO occurred before the Retroactive date. Subsequent capital raisings and continuous disclosure obligations post-listing are covered. See [[ref:do.def.retroactive-date]] for the date that applies where the schedule states “Full Prior Acts”.
