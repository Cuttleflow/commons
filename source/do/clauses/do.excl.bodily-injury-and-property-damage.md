id: do.excl.bodily-injury-and-property-damage
type: exclusion
heading: Bodily Injury and Property Damage
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Death, bodily injury, or physical damage to tangible property — except: (a) mental injury or emotional distress in a Securities claim or Employment claim; (b) defence costs in WHS proceedings; and (c) defence costs in coronial inquests.
