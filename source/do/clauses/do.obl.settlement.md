heading: Settlement
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

We will not settle any claim without the consent of the relevant insured person if the settlement involves an admission of liability or wrongdoing by that insured person, or imposes non-monetary obligations on them. For monetary-only settlements, we may settle with the insured's consent, not unreasonably withheld.
