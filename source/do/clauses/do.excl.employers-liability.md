This policy does not cover an Employment claim, except: (a) to the extent cover is preserved for an insured person under paragraph (e) of [[ref:do.excl.insured-versus-insured]] or paragraph (a) of [[ref:do.excl.bodily-injury-and-property-damage]]; or (b) to the extent [[ref:do.end.e13]] is shown as operative in the schedule, in which case the Relationship to the Employer's Liability Exclusion paragraph of [[ref:do.end.e13]] governs the relationship between this exclusion and that endorsement.

This is a management liability / EPL exposure. Broader cover — including cover for the company itself, and cover for third-party discrimination claims — is available only via [[ref:do.end.e13]].
