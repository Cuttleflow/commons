If this policy is not renewed, or is cancelled, other than by us for non-payment of premium, there is an automatic discovery period of 90 days after the end of the policy period. During it, each insured may notify claims first made in that period for wrongful acts committed before the end of the policy period.

A longer discovery period of 12, 24, 36, 72 or 84 months, or for life, is available under [[ref:do.end.e06]] for the additional premium shown in the schedule. It must be requested within 30 days after the policy ends or is cancelled.
