(a) Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

(b) A reference to a statute includes regulations and instruments made under it, and amendments, re-enactments, or replacements of it.

(c) “Including” and similar expressions are not words of limitation.

(d) A reference to a person includes a natural person, a body corporate, and an unincorporated body.

(e) Words in the singular include the plural, and vice versa.
