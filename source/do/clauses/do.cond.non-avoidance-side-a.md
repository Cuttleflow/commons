heading: Non-Avoidance (Side A)
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

We will not exercise any right to avoid this policy in relation to Side A cover, for any reason (including non-disclosure or misrepresentation), except where the specific insured person seeking cover personally committed fraudulent non-disclosure or fraudulent misrepresentation, within the meaning of sections 26 and 28 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).

We also waive, in relation to Side A cover, any right we may otherwise have under section 28(3) of that Act to reduce our liability on account of an innocent, non-fraudulent non-disclosure or misrepresentation — whether that of the company, the relevant insured person, or another insured person.

An innocent director cannot lose Side A cover, and cannot have it reduced, because of someone else's non-disclosure or misrepresentation, or their own innocent non-disclosure or misrepresentation.
