heading: Retroactive date
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

means the date shown in Item 7 of the schedule. This policy covers wrongful acts occurring on or after the retroactive date only, unless the schedule states “Full Prior Acts,” in which case no retroactive-date restriction applies. The retroactive date (not the Continuity date) governs [[ref:do.excl.prior-known-matters]], [[ref:do.excl.prior-and-pending-litigation]], and [[ref:do.excl.major-transaction-ipo]].
