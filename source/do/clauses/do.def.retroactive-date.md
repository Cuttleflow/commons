means the date shown in Item 7 of the schedule. This policy covers wrongful acts occurring on or after the retroactive date only, unless Item 7 states “Full Prior Acts”, in which case no retroactive-date restriction applies to which wrongful acts are covered. If Item 7 is blank, the retroactive date is the continuity date or, if Item 6 is also blank, the start of the policy period.

The retroactive date (not the Continuity date) governs [[ref:do.excl.prior-known-matters]], [[ref:do.excl.prior-and-pending-litigation]], and [[ref:do.excl.major-transaction-ipo]]. Where Item 7 states “Full Prior Acts”, those three exclusions apply by reference to the continuity date or, if Item 6 is blank, the start of the policy period.

Where two or more wrongful acts are treated as a single claim under [[ref:do.def.claim]], the retroactive date is applied to that claim by reference to the earliest of those wrongful acts.
