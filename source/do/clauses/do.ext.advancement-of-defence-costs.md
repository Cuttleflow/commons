heading: Advancement of Defence Costs
since: 1.3.0
changed: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

We will advance defence costs as they are incurred, without waiting for the final outcome of a claim. We will never require an insured person to self-fund their defence. We advance defence costs in the same way in civil-penalty and criminal proceedings.

An insured person must repay advanced defence costs to the extent that:

(a) it is later established that the claim, or part of it, is not covered, by Final adjudication or by an allocation under [[ref:do.pay.allocation]];

(b) a Final adjudication, or a court's finding, establishes conduct that [[ref:do.excl.dishonesty-and-fraud]] or [[ref:do.excl.personal-profit-and-illegal-remuneration]] excludes, or that the law does not allow to be covered (see [[ref:do.cond.corporations-act-199a-199b]]); or

(c) the company later indemnifies the insured person for the same defence costs, to the extent of the indemnity received, so that there is no double recovery.

Where the claim involves conduct excluded under [[ref:do.excl.dishonesty-and-fraud]] or [[ref:do.excl.personal-profit-and-illegal-remuneration]], repayment is required only after Final adjudication (as defined in [[ref:do.def.final-adjudication]]), consistent with [[ref:do.cond.insurance-contracts-act-1984-cth]].
