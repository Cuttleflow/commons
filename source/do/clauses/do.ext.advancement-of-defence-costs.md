heading: Advancement of Defence Costs
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

We will advance defence costs as they are incurred, without waiting for the final outcome of a claim. We will never require an insured person to self-fund their defence.

If it is later determined that a claim is not covered, the insured must repay advanced defence costs for the uncovered portion — but only after a final determination. Where the claim involves conduct excluded under [[ref:do.excl.dishonesty-and-fraud]] or [[ref:do.excl.personal-profit-and-illegal-remuneration]], repayment is required only after Final adjudication (as defined in [[ref:do.def.final-adjudication]]), consistent with paragraph (b) of [[ref:do.cond.insurance-contracts-act-1984-cth]].
