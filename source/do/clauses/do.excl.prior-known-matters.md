id: do.excl.prior-known-matters
type: exclusion
heading: Prior Known Matters
since: 1.3.0
changed: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

Claims arising from facts, matters, or circumstances that the specific insured person knew about before the Retroactive date and knew, or ought reasonably to have known, might lead to a claim. For a claim under Side C, the knowledge that counts is the knowledge of a member of the control group (see [[ref:do.def.control-group]]). Knowledge assessed individually. Not imputed between insured persons. See [[ref:do.def.retroactive-date]] for the date that applies where the schedule states “Full Prior Acts”.
