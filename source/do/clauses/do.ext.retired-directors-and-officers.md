since: 1.3.0

If an insured person ceases to hold office during or before the policy period (for any reason other than disqualification by final court order), this policy continues to cover claims first made against them for 84 months after the end of the policy period, at no additional premium.

Reconciliation with [[ref:do.cond.discovery-period]] and [[ref:do.end.e06]]: if this policy is not renewed, or is cancelled, before that person's 84-month period under this clause has ended, the longer of (a) the remainder of that person's 84-month period under this clause, and (b) any discovery period applicable to the policy generally under [[ref:do.cond.discovery-period]] or a purchased extension under [[ref:do.end.e06]], applies to that person. The shorter period does not curtail the longer one.
