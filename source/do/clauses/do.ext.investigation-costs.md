since: 1.3.0

We will pay defence costs incurred by an insured in responding to an investigation, even before a claim has been made. This includes an investigation that is a Royal Commission, Parliamentary Committee inquiry, or equivalent public inquiry within limb (g) of [[ref:do.def.investigation]] — for every insured, not only where [[ref:do.end.e03]] is operative.

For the company (Side C), investigation costs are covered only where the investigation relates to matters that could give rise to a securities claim.
