If a claim involves covered and uncovered matters or parties, we will agree a fair allocation. The allocation is made by reference to the relative legal and financial exposure of the covered and the uncovered matters and parties. If we cannot agree, a senior counsel agreed by both parties (or appointed by the President of the relevant Law Society) will determine allocation.

We advance defence costs for the covered portion pending allocation.
