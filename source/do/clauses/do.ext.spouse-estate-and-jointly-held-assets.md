heading: Spouse, Estate, and Jointly Held Assets
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

We will cover the lawful spouse, domestic partner, estate, heir, or legal representative of an insured person for claims arising from the insured person's wrongful acts.

We will also cover the personal assets of a spouse or domestic partner that are jointly held with the insured person — including jointly owned real property and joint bank accounts — to the extent those assets are at risk in connection with a claim against the insured person.
