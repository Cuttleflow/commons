heading: Do Not Admit Liability
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

No insured should admit liability or settle without our written consent. Consent not unreasonably withheld. (See [[ref:do.cond.insurance-contracts-act-1984-cth]] for the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) savings clause that applies to this obligation, including section 54.)
