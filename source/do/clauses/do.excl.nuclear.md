---
id: do.excl.nuclear
type: exclusion
heading: Nuclear
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Loss directly caused by radioactivity, or by radioactive, nuclear, or ionising-radiation material of any kind, including nuclear fuel, nuclear waste, and nuclear weapons material. (See [[ref:do.cond.sanctions]] for the separate, standalone sanctions payment-limitation clause, which applies to all cover under this policy and is not part of this exclusion.)
