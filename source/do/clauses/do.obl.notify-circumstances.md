id: do.obl.notify-circumstances
type: obligation
heading: Notify Circumstances
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Tell us during the policy period of any circumstance that might reasonably give rise to a claim. Later claims arising from notified circumstances are treated as made during this policy period. This clause implements section 40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) — see also [[ref:do.cond.insurance-contracts-act-1984-cth]].

Examples: Media alleging non-disclosure. Investigation or ASX inquiry about disclosure practices. Significant share price drop following announcements. Shareholders engaging litigation funders. Personal notice from a regulator. Internal investigation into misconduct.
