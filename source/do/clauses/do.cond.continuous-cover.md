---
id: do.cond.continuous-cover
type: condition
heading: Continuous Cover
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

If a claim arises from a wrongful act that should have been notified under a previous D&O policy and was not known at the time, and continuous cover has been maintained since the Continuity date shown in Item 6 of the schedule, we will cover the claim under this policy's terms and limits — provided that our liability for that claim does not exceed the lower of this policy's limit of liability and the limit of liability that was available under the previous D&O policy, and is subject to any term of either policy that is less favourable to the insured.
