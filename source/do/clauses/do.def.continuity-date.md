heading: Continuity date
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

means the date shown in Item 6 of the schedule, being the date from which continuous D&O coverage has been maintained for the purposes of [[ref:do.cond.continuous-cover]]. The continuity date does not limit which wrongful acts are covered by this policy — that is the function of the Retroactive date, defined below.
