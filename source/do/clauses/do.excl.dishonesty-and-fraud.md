id: do.excl.dishonesty-and-fraud
type: exclusion
heading: Dishonesty and Fraud
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

Any claim arising from deliberately dishonest, fraudulent, or criminal conduct — but only after a Final adjudication (including all appeals) establishes that the specific insured personally committed the conduct. Until Final adjudication, we advance defence costs, on the terms in [[ref:do.ext.advancement-of-defence-costs]]. The presumption of innocence applies.

Severability: Applied individually. One person's dishonesty does not affect another's cover.
