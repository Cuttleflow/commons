type: definition
heading: Non-indemnifiable loss
since: 1.3.0
changed: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

means Loss that the company: (a) is legally prohibited from indemnifying (including under section 199A of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth); see [[ref:do.cond.corporations-act-199a-199b]]); (b) is financially unable to indemnify (due to insolvency, liquidation, administration, receivership, or scheme of arrangement); or (c) refuses or fails to indemnify (despite being permitted and able to do so).
