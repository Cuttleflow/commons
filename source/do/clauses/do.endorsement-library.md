An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables (sub-limits, excesses, periods, named entities and parties) shown for it in the schedule. Where an endorsement and the rest of this policy conflict, the endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. If a variable an endorsement needs is not shown in the schedule, the endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the schedule. These endorsements are for use with version 1.4.x of this wording.

| Code | Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | United States / Canada Jurisdiction Extension | Writes back the US / Canada Jurisdiction exclusion, with ERISA excluded and a separate US securities claim retention |
| E02 | Small Capitalisation and Resources Companies | Adds JORC, capital-raising, voluntary suspension, insolvent trading and native title cover for small-capitalisation and resources companies |
| E03 | Financial Institutions | Adds FAR, prudential, superannuation trustee, DDO and AML/CTF cover for APRA-regulated financial institutions |
| E04 | Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) | Adds greenwashing, climate disclosure and activist shareholder claims |
| E05 | Prospectus and Capital Raising — Enhanced | Covers a specified capital raising for 72 months, including roadshows and due diligence defence |
| E06 | Extended Discovery Period | Extends the discovery period to the period shown in the schedule |
| E07 | Defence Costs in Addition to Limit | Pays defence costs in addition to the limit of liability |
| E08 | Side A Standalone / Excess DIC | Converts Side A into a standalone excess and difference-in-conditions layer with drop-down triggers |
| E09 | Excess Layer | Writes this policy in excess of the primary policy named |
| E10 | Run-Off / Tail Policy | Converts the policy to a run-off policy after a change of control, merger or delisting |
| E12 | Outside Directorship Liability (ODL) | Covers insured persons serving on an outside entity's board at the company's request |
| E13 | Employment Practices Liability (EPL) | Adds employment practices liability for the company and insured persons |

Endorsements E11 and E14 to E20 are not yet available in this version. A schedule must not show any of them.
