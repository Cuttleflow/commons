heading: Loss
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

means Compensatory damages and Defence costs. For Side A and Side B, loss also includes insurable penalties and fines imposed on an insured person in connection with a wrongful act. Loss does not include: (i) fines, penalties, or sanctions not insurable at law; (ii) taxes, levies, or statutory charges; (iii) disgorgement of remuneration or equity-based compensation to which the insured person was not legally entitled; (iv) the cost of complying with an injunction or specific performance order; or (v) matters uninsurable at law in the relevant jurisdiction.
