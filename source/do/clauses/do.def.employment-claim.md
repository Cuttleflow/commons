id: do.def.employment-claim
type: definition
heading: Employment claim
since: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

means a claim by an Employee, former Employee, or applicant for employment with the company, alleging: (a) wrongful, unfair, or constructive dismissal; (b) discrimination, harassment (including sexual harassment), vilification, or victimisation, including an alleged breach of the positive duty under Part IIB of the Sex Discrimination Act 1984 (Cth); (c) breach of an applicable employment-related statute (including the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth), state and territory anti-discrimination legislation, and equivalent foreign legislation); (d) negligent provision of, or failure to provide, an employment reference; (e) breach of an employment contract; or (f) retaliation against an Employee for a protected disclosure, exercise of a workplace right, or a complaint of a kind described in (b). [[ref:do.excl.employers-liability]] states the extent to which this policy responds to an Employment claim, and [[ref:do.end.e13]] provides fuller cover where operative.
