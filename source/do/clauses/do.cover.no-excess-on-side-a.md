heading: No Excess on Side A
since: 1.3.0
changed: 1.4.0
status: draft
---

No excess or deductible applies to Side A, except the DIC retention shown in the schedule for [[ref:do.end.e08]], which applies only to loss paid under that endorsement because the primary D&O policy does not respond. We will never require an insured person to pay out of pocket before we respond to Side A loss that is not within that retention.
