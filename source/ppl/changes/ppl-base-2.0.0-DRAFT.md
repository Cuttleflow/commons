This is the first Commons release of the Meridian Public and Products Liability wording. It replaces version 1.0.0 (July 2026), which was published outside the Commons. Coverage changes from version 1.0.0:

- Labour hire workers are no longer employees. The Employers' Liability exclusion now removes only liability that workers compensation law requires you to insure, so a host business is covered for injury to a labour hire worker. A new Injury to Labour Hire Workers extension says so expressly.
- The Deliberate Acts exclusion now applies only to injury or damage you expected or intended, not to any injury from an intended act. It applies to each insured individually.
- New Supplementary Payments clause: post-judgment interest, appeal bonds, first aid, temporary protection of property, attendance at court, inquest representation and claim preparation costs, all in addition to the limit.
- Defence costs stay in addition to the limit, but are reduced pro rata when compensation exceeds the limit, and are within the limit for claims brought in the USA and Canada.
- The Products definition now includes packaging, labelling, instructions, warnings, design, formula and specification, and products for which you are treated by law as the manufacturer, including as an importer under the Australian Consumer Law.
- The default geographical limits are now worldwide except the USA and Canada. Outside Australia and New Zealand, public liability covers non-manual business visits only; physical work overseas needs Endorsement E04.
- The Occurrence definition now aggregates by one source or original cause.
- The Pollution exclusion now uses the sudden, identifiable event test in place of the 72-hour and 14-day time limits, and does not apply its carve-back in the USA and Canada.
- The Contractual Liability exclusion has new carve-backs for terms void or unenforceable under the Australian Consumer Law, and for warranties and consumer guarantees implied by law.
- New exclusions: Abuse, Silica, PFAS, Mould, Communicable Disease, Aircraft Products, Licensed Venues, Vibration and Removal of Support, Shoring and Underpinning, Work Outside Australia and New Zealand, USA and Canada, and Known Injury or Damage. Each has a write-back endorsement where one is offered.
- Incidental supply of liquor is covered in the base wording. Licensed venues need Endorsement E07.
- The watercraft length in the Aircraft and Watercraft exclusion remains 8 metres by default and can be changed in the schedule.
- New general conditions: Individual Treatment of Insureds, Waiver of Subrogation, Premium Adjustment, a standalone Sanctions condition, Jurisdiction, and an express Insurance Contracts Act savings clause applying s.54 to every obligation. A new Reasonable Precautions obligation applies only to reckless conduct. Governing law is fixed by the schedule rather than following the principal place of business.
- New Excess provision allowing a worker-to-worker excess and special excesses, with only the highest excess applying to one occurrence.
- The endorsement library now has 23 endorsements, each with operative text and schedule variables: E16 Product Recall Expenses, E17 Product Errors and Omissions, E18 New Zealand Exemplary Damages, E19 Abuse, E20 Silica, E21 Worker-to-worker Excess, E22 PFAS and E23 Communicable Disease are new. E06 no longer refers to an endorsement in another wording. E12 now names principals; the definition of you continues to include principals required by written contract.
- The licence is now CC BY 4.0, in line with the rest of the Commons.
- Clause numbers are removed. Each clause is identified by its heading.
