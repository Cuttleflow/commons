---
id: ppl.excl.high-hazard-products
type: exclusion
heading: High-Hazard Products
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Personal injury or property damage arising from your products that are:

- therapeutic goods, medicines, medical devices or other goods regulated by the Therapeutic Goods Administration, unless written back by Endorsement E06;
- tobacco, nicotine or vaping products;
- firearms, ammunition, explosives or fireworks; or
- radioactive materials.
