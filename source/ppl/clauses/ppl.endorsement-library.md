An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables (sub-limits, excesses, named parties, countries, dates) shown for it in the schedule. Where an endorsement and the rest of this policy conflict, the endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. If a variable an endorsement needs is not shown in the schedule, the default stated in the endorsement applies; if it has no default, the endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the schedule.

| Code | Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Increased Tenants' Liability | Increases the Tenants' Liability sub-limit |
| E02 | Increased Property in Your Care | Increases the Property in Your Care, Custody or Control sub-limit, with an optional special excess |
| E03 | Defence Costs Within the Limit | Makes defence costs part of the limit of liability for all claims |
| E04 | Overseas Work | Covers physical work in the countries named |
| E05 | Gradual Pollution | Writes back gradual pollution, on a discovery and reported basis, to a sub-limit |
| E06 | Therapeutic Goods and Medical Devices | Writes back the therapeutic goods part of the High-Hazard Products exclusion for the products named |
| E07 | Licensed Venues | Writes back the Licensed Venues exclusion, with responsible service conditions |
| E08 | Incidental Professional Services | Covers personal injury and property damage from incidental advice, to a sub-limit |
| E09 | Drones | Covers the drones named, operated under CASA rules |
| E10 | USA and Canada | Writes back the USA and Canada exclusion for the exposures named, including business visits |
| E11 | Excess Layer | Writes this policy in excess of the underlying insurance named |
| E12 | Named Principals | Names principals as insureds and gives the cover their contracts require |
| E13 | Vibration and Removal of Support | Writes back the Vibration and Removal of Support exclusion, to a sub-limit |
| E14 | Shoring and Underpinning | Writes back the Shoring and Underpinning exclusion, with engineering conditions |
| E15 | Hot Works Conditions | Sets conditions and a special excess for hot works away from your premises |
| E16 | Product Recall Expenses | Covers product recall expenses, to an aggregate sub-limit |
| E17 | Product Errors and Omissions | Covers product failure to perform, on a claims-made basis |
| E18 | New Zealand Exemplary Damages | Covers exemplary damages awarded in New Zealand, to an aggregate sub-limit |
| E19 | Abuse Write-back | Writes back the Abuse exclusion, on a claims-made basis, with risk management conditions |
| E20 | Silica Write-back | Writes back the Silica exclusion, with dust control conditions |
| E21 | Worker-to-worker Excess | Applies a separate excess to worker-to-worker claims |
| E22 | PFAS Write-back | Writes back the PFAS exclusion for sudden events, to a sub-limit |
| E23 | Communicable Disease Write-back | Writes back the Communicable Disease exclusion, to an aggregate sub-limit |
