---
id: ppl.excl.licensed-venues
type: exclusion
heading: Licensed Venues
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Liability arising from the sale or supply of liquor by you, or by anyone on your behalf, under a liquor licence for which you are the licensee or approved manager, including liability for the actions of intoxicated persons. Cover may be written back by Endorsement E07. This exclusion does not apply to [[ref:ppl.ext.incidental-liquor]].
