id: ppl.cond.subrogation-waiver
type: condition
heading: Waiver of Subrogation
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We waive our rights of recovery against any person or entity who is an insured under this policy, except to the extent that another insurance policy covers that person or entity for the liability. We also waive our rights of recovery against any other person or entity where, before the occurrence, you agreed in writing to waive your rights against them.
