---
id: ppl.excl.aircraft-and-watercraft
type: exclusion
heading: Aircraft and Watercraft
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Personal injury or property damage arising from the ownership, use or operation by you or on your behalf of any aircraft (including any remotely piloted aircraft or drone), hovercraft, or any watercraft longer than 8 metres (or the length shown in the schedule).

This exclusion does not apply to aircraft you do not own or operate on which your employees travel as passengers, or to watercraft at or below that length. Drones are covered only by Endorsement E09.
