---
id: ppl.excl.communicable-disease
type: exclusion
heading: Communicable Disease
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Personal injury, property damage or any cost arising from the actual or alleged transmission of, or failure to prevent the transmission of, any communicable disease. This exclusion does not apply to food-borne illness caused by your products that are food or beverages. Other cover may be written back by Endorsement E23.
