---
id: ppl.excl.silica
type: exclusion
heading: Silica
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Personal injury arising from inhaling, ingesting or being exposed to respirable crystalline silica, silica dust, or any material containing silica, including engineered stone. Cover may be written back by Endorsement E20. Since 1 July 2024, the manufacture, supply, processing and installation of engineered stone benchtops, panels and slabs has been prohibited in Australia under work health and safety laws.
