---
id: ppl.excl.professional-services
type: exclusion
heading: Professional Services
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Any act, error or omission in professional advice, design, specification, inspection, certification or other professional service that you give for a fee, or that would usually be given for a fee.

This exclusion does not apply to personal injury or property damage that is a direct physical result of that act, error or omission, or to advice given free of charge in connection with the sale of your products or the installation of them.
