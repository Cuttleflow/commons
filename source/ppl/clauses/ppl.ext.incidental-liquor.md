heading: Incidental Supply of Liquor
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We cover your liability arising from the supply of liquor in the course of the business where it is incidental to the business, such as at functions, events, client entertainment or as a gift. Liability arising from the sale or supply of liquor under a licence that you hold as licensee of a venue is covered only by Endorsement E07 (see [[ref:ppl.excl.licensed-venues]]).
