---
id: ppl.excl.your-products-and-work
type: exclusion
heading: Your Products and Work Themselves
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

- Property damage to your products, if the damage is caused by a defect in them or by their nature;
- the cost of repairing, replacing, reconditioning, modifying or redoing any of your products or work;
- the cost of withdrawing, recalling, inspecting, repairing or replacing any of your products, or of any property of which they form part, because of a known or suspected defect (product recall is covered only by Endorsement E16); and
- loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically damaged, arising from a delay in or failure to perform a contract by you, or from your products failing to meet the level of performance, quality or fitness you warranted or represented.

This exclusion does not apply to property damage to other property caused by your products or work.
