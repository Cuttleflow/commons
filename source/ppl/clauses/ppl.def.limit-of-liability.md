heading: Limit of Liability
since: 2.0.0

---

The amount shown in the schedule as the most we will pay for compensation for any one occurrence under [[ref:ppl.cover.public-liability]], and in total for all occurrences in the policy period under [[ref:ppl.cover.products-liability]] (the products aggregate).
