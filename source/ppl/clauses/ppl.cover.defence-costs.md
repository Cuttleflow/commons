We have the right, and the duty, to defend any claim against you that this policy may cover, even if the claim is groundless, false or fraudulent. We will choose the lawyers and manage the defence, and we will consult you about strategy.

We pay defence costs in addition to the limit of liability, except as set out in [[ref:ppl.pay.defence-costs-and-the-limit]]. We pay defence costs as they are incurred and do not wait until the claim is resolved.
