---
id: ppl.def.you-your
type: definition
heading: You, Your
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

- the named insured and its subsidiaries;
- any past, present or future director, officer, partner or principal of an entity in the first point, while acting in that capacity;
- any employee, labour hire worker or voluntary worker, while acting for the business;
- any social, sports or welfare club or canteen run for employees, and its members while acting in that capacity;
- any person or entity, including a principal, that you are required by a written contract entered into before the occurrence to include as an insured, but only for liability arising from the business and only to the extent the contract requires; and
- if the named insured dies, its legal personal representative for liability arising from the business.
