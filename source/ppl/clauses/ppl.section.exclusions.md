heading: What Is Not Covered
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not cover any claim, compensation, defence costs or other amount described in the exclusions below. Where an exclusion states that it does not apply in a particular case, that exception (a carve-back) applies only to the extent the exclusion says. Where an extension or endorsement states that it applies despite an exclusion, the extension or endorsement prevails to the extent it says so. [[ref:ppl.excl.how-the-exclusions-apply]] shows which exclusions have carve-backs.
