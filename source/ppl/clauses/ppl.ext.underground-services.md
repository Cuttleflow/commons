heading: Damage to Underground Services
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We cover your liability for property damage to underground pipes, cables, mains and other services, if before starting work you took reasonable steps to find them, including making an enquiry through Before You Dig Australia or the relevant authority and following the information provided. The default sub-limit is $500,000 for any one occurrence. We do not pay the cost of repairing the service itself unless you are legally liable for it.
