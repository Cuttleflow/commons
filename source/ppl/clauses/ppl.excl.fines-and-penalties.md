type: exclusion
heading: Fines, Penalties and Punitive Damages
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Fines, penalties, penalty notices, civil penalties, punitive, exemplary or aggravated damages, liquidated damages, and the multiple part of any multiplied damages award. New Zealand exemplary damages are covered only by Endorsement E18. This exclusion does not apply to defence costs under [[ref:ppl.ext.statutory-defence-costs]].
