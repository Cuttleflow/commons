---
id: ppl.excl.usa-canada
type: exclusion
heading: USA and Canada
since: 2.0.0
changed: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

- Any claim brought in a court in the USA and Canada, or to enforce a judgment or award of such a court;
- any liability arising from your products exported to the USA and Canada, by you or with your knowledge; and
- any liability arising from business visits to, or work in, the USA and Canada.

Cover may be written back by Endorsement E10.
