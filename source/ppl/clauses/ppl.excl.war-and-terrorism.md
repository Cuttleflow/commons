since: 2.0.0

note: Contains a WP REVIEW tag for the Wording Partner.
---

- War, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities or warlike operations (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, military or usurped power, or confiscation by any government or public authority; and
- any act of terrorism, meaning an act, or the threat of an act, involving the use of force or violence by any person or group for political, religious, ideological or similar purposes, including to influence a government or put the public in fear, and any action to control, prevent or suppress it.

The terrorism part of this exclusion does not apply to personal injury from an act of terrorism that occurs in Australia, to the extent the Terrorism and Cyclone Insurance Act 2003 (Cth) makes it ineffective.
