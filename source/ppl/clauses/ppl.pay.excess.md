---

You bear the excess shown in the schedule for each occurrence. It applies to compensation, and not to defence costs unless the schedule says so.

If a worker-to-worker excess is shown in the schedule, it applies to each worker-to-worker claim instead of the standard excess. If a special excess is shown in the schedule or an endorsement for a particular type of occurrence, it applies to that type of occurrence instead of the standard excess. Where more than one excess could apply to one occurrence, only the highest applies.
