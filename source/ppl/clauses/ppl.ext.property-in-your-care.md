type: extension
heading: Property in Your Care, Custody or Control
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We cover your liability for property damage to property of others that is in your physical or legal care, custody or control for the business. The default sub-limit is $250,000 for any one occurrence. We do not cover property you own, hire or lease, property in transit, or the part of any property you are working on that is damaged because of that work. This extension applies despite [[ref:ppl.excl.property-in-your-control]].
