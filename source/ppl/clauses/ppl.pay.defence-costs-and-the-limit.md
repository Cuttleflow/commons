heading: Defence Costs and the Limit

We pay defence costs in addition to the limit of liability, with two exceptions:

- if the compensation you are liable to pay for an occurrence exceeds the applicable limit of liability, we pay only the proportion of defence costs that the limit of liability bears to the total compensation paid to settle or satisfy the claim; and
- for any claim brought in a court in the USA and Canada, or to enforce a judgment or award of such a court, defence costs are part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability.

Endorsement E03 changes this so that defence costs are within the limit of liability for all claims, if it is shown in the schedule.

Worked example. Limit of liability $10,000,000. A claim settles for $15,000,000 and defence costs are $900,000. We pay $10,000,000 of the compensation and two-thirds of the defence costs ($600,000), because the limit is two-thirds of the total compensation.
