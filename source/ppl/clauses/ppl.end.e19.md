This endorsement applies despite the words "happens during the policy period" in the covers. [[ref:ppl.excl.abuse]] does not apply to a claim for abuse that is first made against you during the policy period and that you tell us about as set out in [[ref:ppl.cond.claims-made-endorsements]], if the abuse first happened after the retroactive date shown in the schedule for this endorsement.

You must hold the child-safe accreditation, or follow the National Principles for Child Safe Organisations, as you told us in your proposal, and require working with children checks or equivalent screening for every person working with children or vulnerable people. If you do not, we may reduce our liability for a claim to the extent that the failure caused or contributed to the loss, as s.54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

We do not cover the person who committed the abuse, or any other insured who knew of it and failed to report it as the law required. One insured's knowledge is not imputed to another, except for the control group of an entity (see [[ref:ppl.cond.individual-treatment]]). The most we will pay for all claims in the policy period, including defence costs, is the aggregate sub-limit shown for this endorsement, and the special excess shown applies to each claim.
