[[ref:ppl.excl.abuse]] does not apply to a claim for abuse that is first made against you during the policy period and that you tell us about during the policy period, if:

- the abuse first happened after the retroactive date shown in the schedule for this endorsement; and
- at the time of the abuse you held the child-safe accreditation, or followed the National Principles for Child Safe Organisations, as you told us in your proposal, and you required working with children checks or equivalent screening for every person working with children or vulnerable people.

We do not cover the person who committed the abuse, or any person who knew of it and failed to report it as the law required. The most we will pay for all claims in the policy period, including defence costs, is the aggregate sub-limit shown for this endorsement, and the special excess shown applies to each claim.
