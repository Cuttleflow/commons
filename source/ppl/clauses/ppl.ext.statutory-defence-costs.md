We pay defence costs you incur, with our consent, to defend a prosecution brought under any Australian law for a breach of that law in connection with an occurrence that this policy covers or would cover but for the excess. The default sub-limit is $500,000 for all prosecutions in the policy period.

We do not pay any fine, penalty or penalty notice, or the cost of complying with any order. Work health and safety laws in New South Wales, Victoria, Queensland and other jurisdictions prohibit insurance against work health and safety penalties, and this extension does not cover them.
