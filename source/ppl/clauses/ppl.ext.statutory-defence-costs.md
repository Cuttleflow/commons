We pay defence costs you incur, with our consent, to defend a prosecution brought under an Australian law against an insured for a breach of that law in connection with an occurrence that this policy covers or would cover but for the excess. The default sub-limit is $500,000 for all prosecutions in the policy period. This extension applies despite [[ref:ppl.excl.directors-and-officers]] and [[ref:ppl.excl.fines-and-penalties]], but only for defence costs.

We do not pay any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured. In particular, we do not pay a penalty imposed under work health and safety law in a State or Territory whose law prohibits insurance of it (including New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland), and we do not pay a criminal fine. We do not pay the cost of complying with any order.

We advance defence costs as they are incurred. If a court finally decides, after any appeal or after the time for appeal has passed, that an insured person is guilty of an offence, or is liable in a civil penalty proceeding, and the law or [[ref:ppl.cond.corporations-act-199]] does not allow us to insure the legal costs of that proceeding, that person must repay the costs we advanced for it.
