heading: Retroactive Date
since: 2.0.0
changed: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

The date shown in the schedule for a claims-made endorsement (E17 or E19), or for a discovery-based endorsement (E05), as the earliest date from which that endorsement can respond. How the date applies is set out in the endorsement and in [[ref:ppl.cond.claims-made-endorsements]].
