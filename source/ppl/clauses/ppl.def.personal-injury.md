heading: Personal Injury
since: 2.0.0
changed: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

- bodily injury, sickness, disease, disability or death;
- mental injury, nervous shock, mental anguish or emotional distress, whether or not it results from bodily injury;
- wrongful entry into, wrongful eviction from, or other invasion of the right of private occupancy; and
- assault and battery not committed by you or at your direction, unless committed to protect people or property.

False arrest, wrongful detention, false imprisonment, malicious prosecution and advertising injury are not personal injury. They are covered only by [[ref:ppl.ext.wrongful-arrest]] and [[ref:ppl.ext.advertising-injury]], to the limits stated there.
