---
id: ppl.def.personal-injury
type: definition
heading: Personal Injury
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

- bodily injury, sickness, disease, disability or death;
- mental injury, nervous shock, mental anguish or emotional distress, whether or not it results from bodily injury;
- false arrest, wrongful detention, false imprisonment or malicious prosecution;
- wrongful entry into, wrongful eviction from, or other invasion of the right of private occupancy;
- assault and battery not committed by you or at your direction, unless committed to protect people or property; and
- advertising injury.
