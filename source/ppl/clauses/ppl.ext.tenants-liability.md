heading: Tenants' Liability
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We cover your liability for property damage to premises, or their fixtures and fittings, that you lease, rent or occupy for the business. The default sub-limit is $500,000 for any one occurrence. We do not cover wear and tear, gradual deterioration, or damage that existed before you took occupation. This extension applies despite [[ref:ppl.excl.property-in-your-control]].
