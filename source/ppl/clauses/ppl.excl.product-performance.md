---
id: ppl.excl.product-performance
type: exclusion
heading: Product Performance
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Failure of your products to perform their intended function or to meet the level of performance, quality, fitness or durability warranted or represented by you, unless the failure causes personal injury or property damage to other property. Product errors and omissions are covered only by Endorsement E17.
