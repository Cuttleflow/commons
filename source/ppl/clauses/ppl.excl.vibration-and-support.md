---
id: ppl.excl.vibration-and-support
type: exclusion
heading: Vibration and Removal of Support
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Property damage to land, buildings or structures caused by vibration, or by the removal, weakening or interference with the support of land, buildings or structures, in connection with excavation, piling, demolition or similar work by you or on your behalf. Cover may be written back by Endorsement E13.
