This is a broadform liability policy. It covers your legal liability to pay compensation when someone other than an employee is injured, or their property is damaged, because of your business or your products. It has two covers: [[ref:ppl.cover.public-liability]] and [[ref:ppl.cover.products-liability]].

Both covers respond on an occurrence basis. What matters is when the injury or damage happens, not when the claim is made against you. A claim made years after the policy period ends is still covered if the injury or damage happened during the policy period. Endorsements E17 and E19 are different: they respond to claims first made during the policy period, as set out in [[ref:ppl.cond.claims-made-endorsements]]. Endorsement E05 responds to pollution first discovered during the policy period.

This policy, together with your schedule and any endorsements shown in it, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:ppl.section.definitions]]. Who the policy is written for is explained in [[ref:ppl.who-this-policy-is-for]].
