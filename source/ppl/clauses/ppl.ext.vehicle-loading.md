id: ppl.ext.vehicle-loading
type: extension
heading: Loading and Unloading Vehicles
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We cover your liability arising from the loading or unloading of any vehicle in connection with the business, and from the delivery or collection of goods to or from it. This extension applies despite [[ref:ppl.excl.motor-vehicles]]. It does not cover liability arising from the vehicle being driven.
