heading: Loading and Unloading Vehicles
since: 2.0.0
changed: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

We cover your liability arising from the loading or unloading of any vehicle in connection with the business, and from the delivery or collection of goods to or from it. This extension applies despite [[ref:ppl.excl.motor-vehicles]]. It does not cover liability arising from the vehicle being driven. It does not apply to any liability that a compulsory third party scheme or statutory motor accident scheme covers, or would cover if the vehicle were registered. Liability arising from the use of the vehicle, other than its loading or unloading, is for your motor vehicle insurance. Where a motor vehicle insurance policy also covers a liability that this extension covers, [[ref:ppl.pay.other-insurance]] applies.
