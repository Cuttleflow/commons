id: ppl.ext.contractors
type: extension
heading: Liability for Contractors
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We cover your liability for personal injury or property damage caused by a contractor or subcontractor working for you in connection with the business. We do not cover the contractor or subcontractor themselves unless they are an insured under the definition of [[ref:ppl.def.you-your|heading]].
