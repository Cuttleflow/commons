Property damage to property you own, or that is in your care, custody or control. This exclusion does not apply to the extent [[ref:ppl.ext.tenants-liability]], [[ref:ppl.ext.property-in-your-care]] or [[ref:ppl.ext.employees-property]] covers the damage.
