---
id: ppl.excl.motor-vehicles
type: exclusion
heading: Motor Vehicles
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Personal injury or property damage arising from the ownership, use or operation by you or on your behalf of any vehicle that is registered, or is required by law to be registered, or that is being driven on a public road, or for which compulsory third party insurance or a statutory motor accident scheme provides cover or would if the vehicle were registered.

This exclusion does not apply to:

- property damage caused by a vehicle on your premises or at a work site, or in a car park, other than property damage to the vehicle itself;
- liability covered by [[ref:ppl.ext.vehicle-loading]] or [[ref:ppl.ext.tool-of-trade]]; or
- personal injury where no compulsory third party scheme or statutory motor accident scheme provides cover, and the vehicle is not required to be registered.
