id: ppl.ext.cross-liabilities
type: extension
heading: Cross Liabilities
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Where you consist of more than one party, each is treated as if it had a separate policy, so that one of you can claim against another. This does not increase the limit of liability. This extension does not cover personal injury to an employee of the party against whom the claim is made, which [[ref:ppl.excl.employers-liability]] deals with.
