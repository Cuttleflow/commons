---
id: ppl.def.communicable-disease
type: definition
heading: Communicable Disease
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Any disease, illness or condition that can be transmitted from one person, animal or thing to a person by any means, including by a virus, bacterium, parasite, fungus or other organism, or any variation of one. It includes COVID-19 and any listed human disease under the Biosecurity Act 2015 (Cth).
