[[ref:ppl.excl.aircraft-and-watercraft]] does not apply to the remotely piloted aircraft shown in the schedule for this endorsement.

You must make sure that the aircraft is registered with the Civil Aviation Safety Authority (CASA) where required, and is flown by a person holding the accreditation or licence CASA requires, in accordance with the Civil Aviation Safety Regulations 1998 (Cth) Part 101. If you do not, we may reduce our liability for an occurrence involving the aircraft to the extent that the failure caused or contributed to the loss, as s.54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

These CASA conditions are part of this endorsement and are not variables. We do not cover property damage to the aircraft itself or anything it carries. The sub-limit shown for this endorsement applies.

Schedule variables: aircraft and CASA registration numbers; sub-limit (any one occurrence).
