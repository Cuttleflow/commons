---
id: ppl.excl.known-injury
type: exclusion
heading: Known Injury or Damage
since: 2.0.0
changed: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Personal injury or property damage that began before the policy period and that a member of your control group knew about, or ought reasonably to have known about, before the policy period began, including any continuation, change or resumption of it during the policy period. This exclusion does not apply if you told us about it before the policy period began and we agreed in writing to cover it.
