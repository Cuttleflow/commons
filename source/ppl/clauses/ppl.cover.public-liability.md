heading: Public Liability
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We will pay the compensation you become legally liable to pay because of personal injury or property damage that:

- happens during the policy period within the geographical limits;
- is caused by an occurrence in connection with the business; and
- is not caused by your products.

We will also pay defence costs and supplementary payments, as set out in [[ref:ppl.cover.defence-costs]] and [[ref:ppl.cover.supplementary-payments]].
