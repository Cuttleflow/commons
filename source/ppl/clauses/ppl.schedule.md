(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The named insured, ABN, and any subsidiaries named
- The business, and its ANZSIC class or classes
- The policy period, start and end dates
- The limit of liability for public liability (any one occurrence) and the products aggregate limit for products liability
- Sub-limits that differ from the defaults stated in [[ref:ppl.section.extensions]]
- The excess for each cover, any worker-to-worker excess, and any special excess (for example for hot works or property in your care)
- The geographical limits, if different from the default in [[ref:ppl.def.geographical-limits]]
- The maximum watercraft length, if different from the default in [[ref:ppl.excl.aircraft-and-watercraft]]
- The rating basis and the declared figures (turnover, wages and products turnover) used for [[ref:ppl.cond.premium-adjustment]]
- The premium, the deposit and minimum premium, and whether the premium is adjustable, including stamp duty and GST
- The state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:ppl.cond.governing-law]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables (sub-limits, excesses, named parties, countries, retroactive dates)
- Interested parties and principals to be noted
