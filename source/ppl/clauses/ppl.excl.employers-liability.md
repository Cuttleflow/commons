heading: Employers' Liability
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Personal injury to an employee arising out of or in the course of their employment by you.

This exclusion also applies to any liability for personal injury to any other person, to the extent that you are required by any workers compensation law to insure that liability, or would be required to but for any exemption you hold.

This exclusion does not apply to liability for personal injury to a labour hire worker, a voluntary worker, or the employee of a contractor or subcontractor, except to the extent you are required by a workers compensation law to insure it. A host business is not usually required to insure a labour hire worker under workers compensation law, because the labour hire business is the worker's employer.
