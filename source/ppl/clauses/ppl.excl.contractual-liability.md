type: exclusion
heading: Contractual Liability
since: 2.0.0
changed: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Liability you assume under any contract or agreement, to the extent it is greater than the liability you would have had without the contract or agreement.

This exclusion does not apply to:

- liability you would have had at law without the contract;
- a term of a contract that relieves a party from liability, or that requires you to indemnify a party, where that term is void or unenforceable under the Australian Consumer Law (including as an unfair contract term) and you remain liable at law;
- warranties or guarantees as to the fitness or quality of your products that are implied or imposed by law, including consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law, but only for resulting personal injury or property damage to other property;
- a lease, licence or tenancy agreement for premises you occupy, other than any obligation to repair the premises;
- an agreement with a railway, road, port or public authority for access, siding, crossing or the like; and
- a contract with a principal that requires you to indemnify it for liability arising from the business, to the extent the principal is an insured under the definition of [[ref:ppl.def.you-your|heading]].
