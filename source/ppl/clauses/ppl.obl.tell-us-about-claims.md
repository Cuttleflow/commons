heading: Tell Us About Occurrences and Claims
since: 2.0.0

Tell us in writing as soon as reasonably practicable about any occurrence that could give rise to a claim, and immediately about any claim, demand, writ, summons or proceeding against you. Send us copies of any documents you receive.
