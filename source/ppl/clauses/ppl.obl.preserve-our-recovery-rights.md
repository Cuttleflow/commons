---
id: ppl.obl.preserve-our-recovery-rights
type: obligation
heading: Preserve Our Recovery Rights
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Do not give up any right to recover from another person, except under [[ref:ppl.cond.subrogation-waiver]] or under a written contract made before the occurrence. If we pay a claim, we may take over your rights of recovery and act in your name, and you must help us.
