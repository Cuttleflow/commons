---
id: ppl.excl.pfas
type: exclusion
heading: PFAS
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Personal injury, property damage or any cost arising from per- and poly-fluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), including perfluorooctane sulfonate (PFOS), perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA) and perfluorohexane sulfonate (PFHxS), or any product or material containing them, including firefighting foam. Cover may be written back by Endorsement E22.
