Endorsements E17 and E19 respond to claims first made against you during the policy period, and not to the time when injury, damage or abuse happened. For those endorsements this condition applies despite the words "happens during the policy period" in the covers. Endorsement E05 responds on a discovery basis, as it states.

**Reporting a claim.** Tell us in writing about a claim as soon as reasonably practicable after you become aware of it. We will accept notice given during the policy period, or within 14 days after it ends if the claim was first made during the policy period.

**Notice of circumstances.** If, during the policy period, you give us written notice of facts that might give rise to a claim, as soon as reasonably practicable after you become aware of them, any claim later made against you that arises from those facts is treated as first made during the policy period. This implements section 40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). The notice must say what the facts are and why you think a claim might be made.

**Related claims.** Claims arising from the same source or original cause, or from the same person's conduct, are one claim. It is treated as first made when the earliest of them was first made.

**Retroactive date.** Each claims-made endorsement has its own retroactive date shown in the schedule (see [[ref:ppl.def.retroactive-date]]). If none is shown, the endorsement does not apply.

**Known matters.** A claims-made endorsement does not cover a claim arising from a fact or circumstance that a member of your control group knew of before the policy period began, unless you told us about it before then and we agreed in writing to cover it.

There is no extended reporting period for these endorsements unless the schedule shows one.
