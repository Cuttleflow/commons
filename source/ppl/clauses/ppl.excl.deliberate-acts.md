heading: Deliberate Acts
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Personal injury or property damage that you expected or intended. This exclusion does not apply to personal injury or property damage that results from reasonable force used to protect people or property. It applies only to the person who expected or intended the injury or damage, as set out in [[ref:ppl.cond.individual-treatment]].
