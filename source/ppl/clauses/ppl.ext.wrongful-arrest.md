We cover your liability for compensation because of false arrest, wrongful detention, false imprisonment or malicious prosecution, including the detention of a person suspected of theft, committed during the policy period within the geographical limits in connection with the business. This is an extension of [[ref:ppl.cover.public-liability]] and its other terms apply. The default sub-limit is $250,000 for any one occurrence.
