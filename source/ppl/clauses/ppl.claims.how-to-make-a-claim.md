heading: How to Make a Claim
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

1. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
2. Give us written details of the occurrence or claim, and copies of any documents you have received (see [[ref:ppl.obl.tell-us-about-claims]]).
3. Make the scene safe and look after anyone injured. First aid is not an admission of liability.
4. Keep any physical evidence, photographs and records.
5. Do not admit liability or agree to any settlement without our written consent (see [[ref:ppl.obl.do-not-admit-liability]]).
6. We will acknowledge your notification, assign a claims manager and keep you informed.
