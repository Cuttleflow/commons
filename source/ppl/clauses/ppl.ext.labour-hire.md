id: ppl.ext.labour-hire
type: extension
heading: Injury to Labour Hire Workers
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We cover your liability for personal injury to a labour hire worker, as if that person were a member of the public, except to the extent [[ref:ppl.excl.employers-liability]] applies. A worker-to-worker excess applies to these claims if one is shown in the schedule (see [[ref:ppl.pay.excess]]).
