For a claim this policy covers, we will also pay, in addition to the limit of liability:

- interest on a judgment against you that accrues after judgment and before we pay or offer to pay our share of it;
- the cost of appeal bonds and bonds to release attachments, up to the limit of liability (we do not have to provide the bonds);
- expenses you incur for first aid or emergency medical treatment given to others at the time of an occurrence;
- reasonable costs of temporary measures to protect property damaged by an occurrence, to prevent further damage;
- your reasonable expenses, with our consent, including loss of earnings of your directors and employees of up to $500 a day for each person (or the amount shown in the schedule), for attending court or hearings at our request;
- the costs of legal representation, with our consent, at any coronial inquest or official inquiry into a death or personal injury that may result in a claim under this policy; and
- your reasonable costs of preparing information we ask for in support of a claim, up to $25,000 for any one occurrence (or the amount shown in the schedule).
