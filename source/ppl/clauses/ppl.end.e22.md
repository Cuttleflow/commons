[[ref:ppl.excl.pfas]] does not apply to personal injury or property damage caused by a sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected release of PFAS that takes place in its entirety at a specific time and place during the policy period in Australia, if you tell us about it in writing within 30 days. We do not cover liability arising from your products that contain PFAS, from firefighting foam you supply, or from any release from premises you own, lease or occupy that began before the policy period. The aggregate sub-limit and special excess shown for this endorsement apply.
