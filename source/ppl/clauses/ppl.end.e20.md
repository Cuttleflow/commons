[[ref:ppl.excl.silica]] does not apply to personal injury to a person other than an employee, labour hire worker or person working on the same site, if at the time of exposure you complied with the workplace exposure standard for respirable crystalline silica and the work health and safety laws that apply to silica dust control, including on-tool water suppression or extraction and air monitoring where required. It never applies to engineered stone work. The aggregate sub-limit and special excess shown for this endorsement apply.
