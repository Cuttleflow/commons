[[ref:ppl.excl.silica]] does not apply to personal injury to a person other than an employee, labour hire worker or person working on the same site. It never applies to engineered stone work.

You must comply with the workplace exposure standard for respirable crystalline silica and the work health and safety laws that apply to silica dust control, including on-tool water suppression or extraction and air monitoring where required. If you do not, we may reduce our liability for the claim to the extent that the failure caused or contributed to the loss, as s.54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.

The aggregate sub-limit and special excess shown for this endorsement apply.
