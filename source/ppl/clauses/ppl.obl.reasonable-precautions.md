---
id: ppl.obl.reasonable-precautions
type: obligation
heading: Reasonable Precautions
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Take reasonable precautions to prevent personal injury and property damage, and comply with laws and regulations that apply to the safety of people and property. Once you know of a dangerous condition or defect, take reasonable steps to correct it. We will rely on this obligation only if you acted recklessly, meaning you recognised a danger and deliberately chose not to take, or did not care whether you took, measures to avert it.
