Before you enter into, renew, extend, vary or reinstate this policy, you have a duty under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) to tell us anything that is known to you, or that you could reasonably be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. For an entity, what is known to a member of its control group is known to the entity (see [[ref:ppl.def.control-group]]). If we ask you specific questions, you must answer them honestly and with reasonable care.

You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we have told you we do not need to know.

If you do not comply with this duty, we may reduce our liability for a claim to the extent the Act permits, or, if the non-disclosure was fraudulent, avoid this policy. As between us and each insured, the duty and its consequences are assessed individually, as set out in [[ref:ppl.cond.individual-treatment]].
