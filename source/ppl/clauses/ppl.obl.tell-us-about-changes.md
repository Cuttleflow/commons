heading: Tell Us About Changes
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us within 30 days if, during the policy period, the business changes materially in a way that increases the risk, including a new activity, a new class of products, products being exported to the USA and Canada, or the business starting to hold a liquor licence. We may then change the premium or terms for the rest of the policy period, as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.
