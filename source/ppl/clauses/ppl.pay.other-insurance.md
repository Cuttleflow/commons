If another insurance policy covers you for the same liability, tell us as soon as you know of a claim. You may claim under this policy or the other policy. If we pay, we may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows, including under s.76 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Nothing in this provision limits s.45 of that Act. This provision does not apply where this policy is written as an excess layer under Endorsement E11, which sets out how that layer relates to the underlying insurance.
