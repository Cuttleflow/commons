We cover your liability for compensation because of advertising injury committed during the policy period within the geographical limits in the course of advertising your goods, services or business. This is an extension of [[ref:ppl.cover.public-liability]] and its other terms apply. The most we will pay for all advertising injury in the policy period combined is the public liability limit of liability.
