---
id: ppl.def.pollutant
type: definition
heading: Pollutant
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Any solid, liquid, gaseous, biological, radiological or thermal irritant or contaminant, including smoke, vapour, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, waste, noise, vibration and electromagnetic fields. Waste includes material to be recycled, reconditioned or reclaimed.
