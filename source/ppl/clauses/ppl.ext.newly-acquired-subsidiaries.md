heading: Newly Acquired Subsidiaries
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

An entity that becomes a subsidiary during the policy period is covered automatically for 60 days from the date it becomes a subsidiary, or to the end of the policy period if sooner, if its annual turnover is $10,000,000 or less (or the amount shown in the schedule) and its business is similar to the business. To continue cover after 60 days, you must give us details within those 60 days and pay any additional premium we reasonably require. We do not cover occurrences that happened before the entity became a subsidiary.
