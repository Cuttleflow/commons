[[ref:ppl.excl.overseas-work]] does not apply to work performed in the countries shown in the schedule for this endorsement, during the periods (if any) shown. The geographical limits for public liability include those countries. This endorsement does not extend cover to the USA and Canada unless Endorsement E10 also applies. Any claim brought in the courts of a country shown is subject to any sub-limit shown for this endorsement.

Schedule variables: countries; periods (optional); sub-limit (optional).
