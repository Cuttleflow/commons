Any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from access to or use of any computer system or electronic data by a person without authority, or from the corruption, deletion or unavailability of any computer system or electronic data, or from any breach of privacy or data protection law.

This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury, or to property damage to tangible property other than electronic data, caused directly by such an event. It also does not apply to mental injury that results from that bodily injury.

Any other mental injury, nervous shock, mental anguish or emotional distress, and any claim for invasion of privacy, including under any statutory or common-law action for serious invasion of privacy, that arises from such an event or breach is excluded.
