---
id: ppl.excl.cyber-event
type: exclusion
heading: Cyber Event
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from access to or use of any computer system or electronic data by a person without authority, or from the corruption, deletion or unavailability of any computer system or electronic data, or from any breach of privacy or data protection law.

This exclusion does not apply to personal injury, or to property damage to tangible property other than electronic data, caused directly by such an event.
