---
id: ppl.def.products
type: definition
heading: Products
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Anything (including its packaging, container, labelling, instructions, warnings, design, formula or specification) that you or anyone acting for you or under your licence has manufactured, grown, extracted, produced, processed, assembled, constructed, erected, installed, repaired, serviced, treated, sold, supplied, distributed, imported or exported, after it has left your physical possession or control. It includes anything for which you are treated by law as the manufacturer, including as an importer under the Australian Consumer Law. It does not include land or buildings.
