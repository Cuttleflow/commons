All personal injury and property damage attributable to one source or original cause is one occurrence, whenever it happens and however many people or properties are affected. This is the only rule for counting occurrences.

If the first personal injury or property damage from an occurrence happens during the policy period, the whole occurrence is treated as having happened in that policy period. This applies even if some of the injury or damage happens after the policy period ends, and despite the words "happens during the policy period" in the covers. Only the limit of liability and the excess of that policy period apply to it. No later policy period of a policy issued by us responds to it.

If the first personal injury or property damage happened before the policy period, [[ref:ppl.excl.known-injury]] applies to it, and this policy covers only the injury or damage that happens during the policy period. This paragraph does not apply if an earlier policy issued by us covers the occurrence under the rule above.

An occurrence falls under [[ref:ppl.cover.products-liability]] if it is caused by your products, and otherwise under [[ref:ppl.cover.public-liability]]. It cannot fall under both.
