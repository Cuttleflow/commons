heading: One Occurrence, One Limit
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

All personal injury and property damage attributable to one source or original cause is one occurrence, under the definition of [[ref:ppl.def.occurrence|heading]]. It is treated as having happened when the first personal injury or property damage happened. Only one limit of liability and one excess apply to it, even if it continues into later policy periods, and if it could fall under both covers we will treat it as falling under the one that gives you more cover.
