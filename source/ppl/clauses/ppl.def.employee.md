---
id: ppl.def.employee
type: definition
heading: Employee
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

A person employed by you under a contract of service or apprenticeship, whether full-time, part-time, fixed-term, casual or seasonal. It also includes a person who is a worker of yours, or is deemed to be your worker, under any workers compensation law. It does not include a labour hire worker, a voluntary worker, a work experience student, an independent contractor or the employee of an independent contractor.
