heading: Employee
since: 2.0.0

---

A person employed by you under a contract of service or apprenticeship, whether full-time, part-time, fixed-term, casual or seasonal. It also includes a person who is your worker under any workers compensation law, other than a labour hire worker. It does not include a labour hire worker, a voluntary worker, a work experience student, an independent contractor or the employee of an independent contractor. A labour hire worker who a workers compensation law requires you to insure is dealt with in [[ref:ppl.excl.employers-liability]].
