heading: Employees' and Visitors' Property
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We cover your liability for property damage to the personal property of employees, labour hire workers and visitors while on your premises. The default sub-limit is $100,000 for any one occurrence. This extension applies despite [[ref:ppl.excl.property-in-your-control]].
