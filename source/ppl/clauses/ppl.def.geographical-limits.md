heading: Geographical Limits
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

For public liability, anywhere in the world except the USA and Canada, but outside Australia and New Zealand only for business visits by your directors, officers and employees who do no manual work while there. For products liability, anywhere in the world except the USA and Canada. The schedule or an endorsement may change these limits.
