---
id: ppl.ext.tool-of-trade
type: extension
heading: Vehicles as Tools of Trade
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We cover your liability arising from the use of any vehicle as a tool of trade or plant, such as a crane, hoist, forklift, elevating work platform or excavator, while it is being used for its mechanical function at a work site and not while it is travelling. This extension applies despite [[ref:ppl.excl.motor-vehicles]]. It does not apply to any liability that a compulsory third party scheme or statutory motor accident scheme covers, or would cover if the vehicle were registered.
