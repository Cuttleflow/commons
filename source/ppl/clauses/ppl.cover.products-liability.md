heading: Products Liability
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

We will pay the compensation you become legally liable to pay because of personal injury or property damage that:

- happens during the policy period within the geographical limits; and
- is caused by an occurrence in connection with your products.

The most we will pay for compensation under this cover for all occurrences in the policy period is the products aggregate limit shown in the schedule. We will also pay defence costs and supplementary payments, as set out in [[ref:ppl.cover.defence-costs]] and [[ref:ppl.cover.supplementary-payments]].
