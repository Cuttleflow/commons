since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

If the schedule shows that the premium is adjustable, the premium is a deposit based on the turnover, wages or products turnover you estimated for the policy period. Within 60 days after the policy period ends, give us the actual figures. We will then calculate the adjusted premium at the rates shown in the schedule. If the adjusted premium is higher, you pay us the difference; if it is lower, we refund the difference, but the premium will not be less than the minimum premium shown in the schedule.
