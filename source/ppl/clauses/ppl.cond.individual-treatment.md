heading: Individual Treatment of Insureds
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Each insured is treated as having a separate policy for the purposes of [[ref:ppl.cond.your-duty-of-disclosure]], [[ref:ppl.excl.deliberate-acts]], [[ref:ppl.excl.known-injury]] and [[ref:ppl.obl.reasonable-precautions]]. The knowledge, conduct or failure of one insured is not imputed to any other insured, except that the knowledge or conduct of a director or officer of an entity who is responsible for the matter in question is imputed to that entity.
