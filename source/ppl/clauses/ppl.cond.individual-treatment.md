since: 2.0.0
changed: 2.1.0

Each insured is treated as having a separate policy for every provision of this policy that depends on what an insured knew, expected, intended, did or failed to do. This includes:

- [[ref:ppl.cond.your-duty-of-disclosure]];
- the definition of [[ref:ppl.def.occurrence|heading]], the test of what you did not expect or intend;
- [[ref:ppl.excl.deliberate-acts]], [[ref:ppl.excl.known-injury]] and [[ref:ppl.obl.reasonable-precautions]]; and
- Endorsements E16, E17, E18 and E19, and [[ref:ppl.cond.claims-made-endorsements]].

The knowledge, conduct or failure of one insured is not imputed to any other insured. The one exception is that the knowledge or conduct of a member of the control group of an entity is imputed to that entity. See [[ref:ppl.def.control-group]].
