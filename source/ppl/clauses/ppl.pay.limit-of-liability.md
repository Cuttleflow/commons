The most we will pay for compensation for any one occurrence under [[ref:ppl.cover.public-liability]] is the public liability limit of liability shown in the schedule.

The most we will pay for compensation for all occurrences in the policy period under [[ref:ppl.cover.products-liability]] is the products aggregate limit shown in the schedule. Each occurrence under that cover is also subject to the products aggregate limit as a limit for any one occurrence.

The limit of liability applies however many people or entities are insured, claims are made or claimants there are.
