heading: Paying Out the Limit
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

At any time, we may pay you the amount of the limit of liability that remains for an occurrence (less any amount already paid), together with defence costs and supplementary payments incurred to that date. When we do, we will have no further liability for that occurrence, including for defence costs incurred after that date. We will consult you before doing this and will give you reasonable time to take over the defence.
