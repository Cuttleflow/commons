id: pi.excl.directors-and-officers-liability
type: exclusion
heading: Directors' and Officers' Liability
legacy_number: '3.3'
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Arising from an insured person's personal liability as a director or officer of any entity other than the company, or from the management of the company (as distinct from the performance of professional services for clients).

This exposure is designed to be covered under a directors' and officers' liability policy.
