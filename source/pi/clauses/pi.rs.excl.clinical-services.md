---
id: pi.rs.excl.clinical-services
type: exclusion
heading: Clinical Services
profession: risk-safety-claims
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Arising from the diagnosis, treatment or clinical assessment of any person by a medical practitioner, nurse, psychologist or other registered health practitioner, including fitness-for-work medical assessments.

This exposure is designed to be covered under an allied health or medical indemnity policy.
