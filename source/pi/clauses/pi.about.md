This policy, together with your schedule and any endorsements, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read all documents together and let us know if anything is incorrect.

Words shown in bold have special meanings defined in [[ref:pi.section.definitions]].

This wording implements the notification-of-circumstances protection required by s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) — see [[ref:pi.cover.notification-of-circumstances]]. Before you enter into this contract, your insurer or its distributor must give you written notice of the effect of s.40(3) as required by s.40(1) of that Act.
