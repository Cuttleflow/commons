The following endorsement is available and may be attached to this policy as shown in the schedule.

| Code | Endorsement |
|---|---|
| E01 | Run-off extension — quotation-basis extension of run-off cover, for a period requested by you of up to 84 months after the end of the policy period following permanent cessation of practice, offered at our discretion (see [[ref:pi.ext.run-off-cover]]); premium and terms as shown in the schedule or endorsement schedule if offered and accepted |

Endorsements E02 (defence costs in addition to the limit of liability), E04 (contractual liability buy-back), E05 (pollution liability buy-back), E06 (United States and Canada jurisdiction extension), E07 (increased sub-limits), E08 (additional reinstatements) and E10 (excess layer) are not yet available in this version.

Wordings for particular professions are published separately. Each sets out its own endorsements.
