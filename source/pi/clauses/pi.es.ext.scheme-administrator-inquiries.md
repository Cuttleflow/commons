We also pay defence costs under [[ref:pi.ext.inquiry-costs]] for an audit, investigation or inquiry into your work by a body that accredits you, or by the administrator of a rating or certificate scheme under which you work, that is prompted by a complaint, an allegation or a suspected breach. For this purpose that audit, investigation or inquiry is treated as a formal investigation or inquiry by a regulator.

This extension does not cover the cost of a routine audit, spot check or compliance review that the body or administrator carries out as part of its regular monitoring, and not because of a complaint, allegation or suspected breach.
