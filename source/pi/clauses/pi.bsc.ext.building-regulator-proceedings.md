In [[ref:pi.def.claim]] and [[ref:pi.ext.inquiry-costs]], a professional disciplinary body and a regulator include any authority of a state or territory that registers, licenses, audits or disciplines building surveyors or certifiers.

This extension does not cover any fine or penalty, or the cost of complying with an order to carry out or pay for rectification work, except to the extent that the order compensates a third party for loss caused by your negligence.
