In [[ref:pi.def.claim]] and [[ref:pi.ext.inquiry-costs]], a professional disciplinary body and a regulator include any authority of a state or territory that registers, licenses, audits or disciplines building surveyors or certifiers. A formal complaint or proceeding before such an authority is a claim within paragraph (e) of [[ref:pi.def.claim]], for which we pay defence costs under [[ref:pi.cover.claims-against-you]]. An investigation or inquiry by it that has not yet become a formal complaint or proceeding is dealt with under [[ref:pi.ext.inquiry-costs]].

Despite [[ref:pi.ext.statutory-liability]], we do not pay any fine or penalty imposed on you by such an authority, or the cost of complying with an order to carry out or pay for rectification work, except to the extent that the order compensates a third party for loss caused by your negligence.
