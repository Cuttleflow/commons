Arising from the provision of a financial service or a credit activity, including financial product advice, dealing in or arranging dealing in a financial product, funds management, and credit activities, as those terms are used in the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) or the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (Cth).

This exclusion does not apply to a service that is shown in the schedule as a professional service and that you are licensed or authorised to provide, including as an authorised representative or credit representative, under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) or the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (Cth).

For the avoidance of doubt, this exclusion does not apply to audit, review or insolvency work, or to a report, valuation or assessment prepared for a disclosure document or a public report, where that work is shown as a professional service and does not involve financial product advice, dealing in a financial product or a credit activity.
