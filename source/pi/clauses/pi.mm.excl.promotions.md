---
id: pi.mm.excl.promotions
type: exclusion
heading: Prizes and Over-Redemption
profession: marketing-media
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For the cost of prizes, discounts, rebates or redemptions provided under a trade promotion, competition or offer, including any cost above the amount expected, and any amount payable under prize indemnity insurance.

This exclusion does not apply to a claim that you negligently designed the promotion's terms or failed to obtain a permit that the law required.
