For the cost of prizes, discounts, rebates or redemptions provided under a trade promotion, competition or offer, including any cost above the amount expected, and any amount payable under prize indemnity insurance.

This exclusion does not apply to a claim that you negligently designed the promotion's terms, or negligently failed to obtain a permit that the law required, to the extent that the claim seeks compensation for loss that your negligence caused. We do not pay a fine or penalty (see [[ref:pi.excl.non-compensatory-payments]]), or the cost of prizes or other awards that you were required to provide or refund because a promotion was unlawful for want of a permit.
