We cover you for a claim by a student, or a person paying for a student, that you failed to provide the education, training or assessment that you promised or that the law required, including a claim arising from the cancellation of a qualification or statement of attainment that you issued.

The cover is for compensation for loss that your failure caused, such as the cost of replacement training or assessment. It does not extend to the refund of tuition fees, which is dealt with by [[ref:pi.et.excl.fee-refunds]], or to liability greater than the liability you would have at law, which is dealt with by [[ref:pi.excl.contractual-liability]].
