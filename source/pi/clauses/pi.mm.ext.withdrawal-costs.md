If a claim is made about material that you created or published on or after the retroactive date, or you tell us about a circumstance relating to that material under [[ref:pi.cover.notification-of-circumstances]], we will pay the reasonable costs, incurred with our consent, of withdrawing, correcting or taking down that material to reduce the loss.

The most we will pay under this extension in the policy period is the sub-limit shown in the schedule, or $50,000 if no sub-limit is shown, which is part of the limit of liability.
