heading: Retroactive date
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

The date shown in the schedule. We only cover claims arising from acts, errors, or omissions on or after this date.

If no date is shown in the schedule, the retroactive date is the date this policy first started or, if this policy renews or replaces an earlier policy that we issued to the named insured without a break in cover, the date the earliest of those policies started.

A date that an extension of this policy expressly sets for a particular claim applies to that claim instead of the date shown in the schedule.
