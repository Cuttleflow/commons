Despite [[ref:pi.pay.limit-of-liability]], if payments under this policy reduce or exhaust the limit of liability, it is reinstated once during the policy period, in full and without additional premium.

The reinstated limit of liability:

(a) is available only for a claim that does not arise from the same, similar or related acts, errors or omissions as a claim already paid under this policy; and

(b) does not increase the most we will pay for any one claim, which remains the limit of liability.

The most we will pay for all claims during the policy period is twice the limit of liability.
