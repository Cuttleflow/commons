heading: Design Consultants and Subcontractors
profession: design-construct
since: 1.0.0

note: Replaces the base Subcontractors and Outsourcers extension. States that a design and construct contractor is covered for design it procures from others, which is the main source of its design liability (see the Pafburn note in the change file).
---

We will cover you for any claim arising from professional services performed on your behalf by a **design consultant**, or by any other subcontractor or outsourcer, as if you had performed them yourself, provided the work falls within your business activity. This cover does not depend on whether the design consultant holds its own professional indemnity insurance.

This extension covers your liability only. It does not cover the liability of the design consultant, subcontractor or outsourcer unless the schedule names that person as an insured.

If we pay a claim under this extension, we may recover what we have paid from the design consultant, subcontractor or outsourcer, as [[ref:pi.obl.preserve-our-recovery-rights]] provides.
