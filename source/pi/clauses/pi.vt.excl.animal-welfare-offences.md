heading: Animal Welfare and Biosecurity Penalties
profession: veterinary
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For a fine or penalty under an animal welfare, prevention of cruelty to animals or biosecurity law, including a fine or penalty that [[ref:pi.ext.statutory-liability]] would otherwise pay.

This exclusion does not apply to defence costs in a proceeding under such a law.
