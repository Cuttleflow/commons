id: pi.re.ext.franchisors
type: extension
heading: Franchisor's Indemnity
profession: real-estate-strata
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
note: The franchisor indemnity was in the notes box of the June endorsement and had no operative effect.
---

If you operate under a franchise agreement that requires you to insure the franchisor, and the franchisor is named in the schedule, we will cover the franchisor for a claim against it arising from your professional services, as if the franchisor were an insured.

This extension covers the franchisor only for its liability for your acts, errors or omissions, and not for its own conduct.
