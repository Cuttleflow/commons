Tell us the extent of any input tax credit you are entitled to claim for the premium. If you do not, we will assume you are entitled to a full input tax credit.

When we pay a claim, we will reduce our payment by any input tax credit you are entitled to for that payment, as required by the A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth).
