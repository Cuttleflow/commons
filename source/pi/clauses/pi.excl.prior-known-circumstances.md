heading: Prior Known Circumstances
legacy_number: '3.9'
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.5.0
status: draft
---

Arising from any fact, matter, or circumstance that a member of the **control group**, or the insured person seeking cover, knew about or ought reasonably to have known about before the start of this policy period (or before we first agreed to insure you, if earlier), and that the person knew, or ought reasonably to have known, might lead to a claim against you.

This exclusion applies to the company on the knowledge of a member of the control group, and to an insured person on that insured person's own knowledge. The knowledge of one insured person is not imputed to another insured person: see [[ref:pi.obl.individual-treatment-of-insured-persons]].
