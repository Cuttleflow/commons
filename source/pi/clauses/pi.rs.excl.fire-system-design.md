heading: Fire Safety System Design and Certification
profession: risk-safety-claims
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
note: This is engineering and certifier work, covered by the Architects and Engineers and Building Surveyors and Certifiers wordings.
---

Arising from the design, specification, installation, commissioning or certification of a fire safety system, or the preparation of a fire safety performance solution under the National Construction Code.

The definition of [[ref:pi.rs.def.professional-services|heading]] already leaves this work out. This exclusion applies even where the schedule describes a fire safety service in general terms. It does not apply to work that the schedule names specifically as a professional service.
