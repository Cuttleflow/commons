If you are a member of a scheme approved under professional standards legislation in a state or territory of Australia, and the scheme limits your occupational liability, this policy is intended to meet the insurance standard that the scheme sets for its members.

For a claim to which the scheme applies, if a term of this policy is inconsistent with that insurance standard, the standard applies to the extent of the inconsistency.

This condition does not increase the limit of liability. You are responsible for choosing a limit of liability that is at least the amount the scheme requires.
