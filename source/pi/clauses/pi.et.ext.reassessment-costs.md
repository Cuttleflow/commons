If a regulator finds that your assessment of students did not meet the standards that apply, and the finding is made against you in a claim, or you tell us about it as a circumstance under [[ref:pi.cover.notification-of-circumstances]], during the policy period, and the assessments were made on or after the retroactive date, we will pay the reasonable costs, incurred with our consent, of re-assessing the affected students, providing additional training to them, and re-issuing their qualifications or statements of attainment.

The most we will pay under this extension in the policy period is the sub-limit shown in the schedule, or $50,000 if no sub-limit is shown, which is part of the limit of liability.
