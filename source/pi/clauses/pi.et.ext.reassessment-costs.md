profession: education-training
since: 1.0.0

---

If a regulator finds that your assessment of students did not meet the standards that apply, we will pay the reasonable costs, incurred with our consent, of re-assessing the affected students, providing additional training to them, and re-issuing their qualifications or statements of attainment.

The most we will pay under this extension in the policy period is the sub-limit shown in the schedule, which is part of the limit of liability.
