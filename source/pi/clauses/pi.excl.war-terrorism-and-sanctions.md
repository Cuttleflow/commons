---

Arising from: (a) war (whether or not declared), invasion, hostilities, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, civil unrest, or the use or usurpation of military power, or any similar belligerent act; (b) terrorism as defined by relevant Australian or foreign law; or (c) trade or economic sanctions imposed by Australia, the UN, EU, UK, or USA. We will not make any payment that would expose us to enforcement action.
