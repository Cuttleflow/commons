The following services provided to a third party for a fee:

(a) marketing, advertising, branding and public relations;

(b) graphic, digital and web design;

(c) writing, editing and producing content in any medium, including audio, video, photography and social media content;

(d) publishing and broadcasting;

(e) market and social research;

(f) media planning and buying;

(g) designing and administering trade promotions and competitions;

(h) influencer and talent management; and

(i) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.
