note: The base insuring clause covers only unintentional defamation. Publishers and agencies publish deliberately, so cover must respond to intentional publication without malice. The statutory privacy
  tort commenced on 10 June 2025.
---

The cover under [[ref:pi.cover.claims-against-you]] includes a claim arising from material that you create, publish or broadcast in providing your professional services, for:

(a) defamation or injurious falsehood, whether or not the publication was intentional, unless a **Final adjudication** establishes that you published the material with malice;

(b) invasion of privacy, including a claim under the statutory tort for serious invasions of privacy in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth);

(c) infringement of copyright, trade marks, registered designs or moral rights, passing off, or misuse of a name, image or likeness; and

(d) breach of confidence.

This extension does not affect [[ref:pi.excl.deliberate-and-dishonest-acts]], and does not cover patent infringement.
