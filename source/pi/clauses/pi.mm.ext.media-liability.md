The cover under [[ref:pi.cover.claims-against-you]] includes a claim arising from material that you create, publish or broadcast in providing your professional services, for:

(a) defamation, whether or not the publication was intentional, unless a **Final adjudication** establishes that you published the material with malice;

(b) invasion of privacy, including a claim under the statutory tort for serious invasions of privacy in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), on the terms set out below;

(c) infringement of copyright, trade marks, registered designs or moral rights, passing off, or misuse of a name, image or likeness; and

(d) breach of confidence.

For a claim for invasion of privacy, we advance defence costs under [[ref:pi.pay.defence-costs]] until a **Final adjudication** establishes that you intended the invasion or were reckless as to it. If it does, this extension does not cover that claim, and the defence costs advanced for it must be repaid as that clause provides.

This extension does not cover injurious falsehood or patent infringement, and does not otherwise affect [[ref:pi.excl.deliberate-and-dishonest-acts]].
