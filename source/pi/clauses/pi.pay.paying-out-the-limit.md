heading: Paying Out the Limit
legacy_number: '4.6'
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

At any time during a claim, we may pay you the limit of liability (or whatever remains after earlier payments) and our obligation to defend or pay further amounts will end. We will give you reasonable notice and the opportunity to take over the defence at your own cost before doing so.
