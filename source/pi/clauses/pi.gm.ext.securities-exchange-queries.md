We also pay defence costs under [[ref:pi.ext.inquiry-costs]] for a written query from a securities exchange, or a formal review by ASIC, that is directed to you, requires you to respond about a public report that you prepared or contributed to, and could lead to a claim against you. For this purpose that query or review is treated as a formal investigation or inquiry by a regulator, and it must be first notified to us during the policy period.

This extension does not cover a routine query or review that is not directed to you, or that does not require you to respond about your own work.
