heading: Dishonesty of Employees and Agents

since: 1.0.0

status: draft
note: For the Property and Stock Agents Regulation 2022 (NSW), which requires a licensee's PI to cover vicarious liability for the fraud or dishonesty of employees and agents where the claimant is not at
  fault.
---

Despite [[ref:pi.excl.deliberate-and-dishonest-acts]], the cover for dishonesty of your employees under [[ref:pi.cover.claims-against-you]] also applies to the dishonest or fraudulent conduct of your agents, including contractors, sub-agents and assistant agents acting on your behalf, where you are legally liable for that conduct to a claimant who did not take part in it. That exclusion otherwise continues to apply, including to an insured person who committed, condoned or participated in the conduct.

This extension does not apply to conduct described in [[ref:pi.re.excl.trust-money-taken-by-principals]].
