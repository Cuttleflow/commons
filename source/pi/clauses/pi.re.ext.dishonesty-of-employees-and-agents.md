heading: Dishonesty of Employees and Agents
profession: real-estate-strata
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
note: For the Property and Stock Agents Regulation 2022 (NSW), which requires a licensee's PI to cover vicarious liability for the fraud or dishonesty of employees and agents where the claimant is not at
  fault.
---

The cover for dishonesty of your employees under [[ref:pi.cover.claims-against-you]] also applies to the dishonest or fraudulent conduct of your agents, including contractors, sub-agents and assistant agents acting on your behalf, where you are legally liable for that conduct to a claimant who did not take part in it.

This extension does not apply to conduct described in [[ref:pi.re.excl.trust-money-taken-by-principals]].
