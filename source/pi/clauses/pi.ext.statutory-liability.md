We will pay insurable fines, penalties, and defence costs arising from a prosecution or formal regulatory proceeding brought against you during the policy period under any Australian statute, in connection with your business activity performed on or after the retroactive date. The most we will pay under this extension in the policy period is the sub-limit for statutory liability in [[ref:pi.pay.sub-limits]].

We do not pay any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured. In particular, we do not pay a penalty imposed under work health and safety law in a State or Territory whose law prohibits insurance of it (including New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland), and we do not pay a criminal fine.

We do not pay any amount arising from conduct that a **Final adjudication** establishes was deliberate or reckless. Until then, we advance defence costs under [[ref:pi.pay.defence-costs]]. If a Final adjudication, or a court's finding, then establishes conduct that this policy or the law does not allow to be covered, the insured person concerned must repay the defence costs advanced for it.

Where an insured person who is a director or officer of the company is the subject of the prosecution or proceeding, [[ref:pi.cond.corporations-act-ss-199a-199b]] also applies.
