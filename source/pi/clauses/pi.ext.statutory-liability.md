note: 1.4.0 states expressly that penalties the law prohibits from being insured (including work health and safety penalties in NSW, WA and Queensland) are not paid.
---

We will pay insurable fines, penalties, and defence costs arising from a prosecution or formal regulatory proceeding brought against you during the policy period under any Australian statute, in connection with your business activity.

We do not pay:

(a) any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured or indemnified, including a penalty under a work health and safety law that prohibits insurance or indemnity for it; or

(b) any amount arising from deliberate or reckless conduct.
