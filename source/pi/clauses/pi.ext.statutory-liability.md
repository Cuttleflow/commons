legacy_number: 2.4.3
since: 1.0.0

---

We will pay insurable fines, penalties, and defence costs arising from a prosecution or formal regulatory proceeding brought against you during the policy period under any Australian statute, in connection with your business activity. We do not pay fines or penalties that are not insurable at law, or any amount arising from deliberate or reckless conduct.
