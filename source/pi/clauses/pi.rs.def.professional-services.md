The following services provided to a third party for a fee:

(a) work health and safety consulting, including psychosocial hazard assessment;

(b) risk management consulting;

(c) fire safety consulting, other than fire safety engineering and the design or certification of fire safety systems;

(d) loss assessing and loss adjusting;

(e) claims management services, other than deciding or settling insurance claims, unless that service is shown in the schedule;

(f) workplace investigations;

(g) investigation and surveillance services, provided by a person who holds the licence the law requires;

(h) non-clinical injury management and return-to-work coordination;

(i) training in any of these services; and

(j) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

[[ref:pi.excl.financial-services]] does not apply to the services in (d) and (e), other than deciding or settling insurance claims, unless the work involves financial product advice, dealing in a financial product or a credit activity.
