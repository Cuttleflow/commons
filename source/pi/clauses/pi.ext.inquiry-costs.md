We will pay your defence costs in responding to a formal investigation or inquiry by a regulator (including ASIC, OAIC, ACCC, or a state or territory equivalent) into your conduct in connection with your business activity performed on or after the retroactive date, provided the investigation is first notified to us during the policy period.

This extension applies to a formal investigation or inquiry that has not yet become a claim within paragraph (c) of [[ref:pi.def.claim]]. If the investigation or inquiry becomes such a claim, cover for it continues under [[ref:pi.cover.claims-against-you]] instead of this extension, and the trigger, limit, and excess applicable to a claim apply from that point.

A formal complaint or proceeding before a professional disciplinary body is a claim within paragraph (e) of [[ref:pi.def.claim]]. Cover for it is under [[ref:pi.cover.claims-against-you]], not this extension.
