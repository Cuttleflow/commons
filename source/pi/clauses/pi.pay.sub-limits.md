Where a sub-limit is shown — in this wording or the schedule — it is the most we will pay for that type of cover. Sub-limits are included within — not in addition to — the limit of liability, unless the schedule states otherwise.

Unless the schedule shows a different amount, the following default sub-limits apply for the policy period. If the limit of liability is lower than a default sub-limit, the limit of liability applies.

| Cover | Default sub-limit |
|---|---|
| [[ref:pi.cover.claims-against-you|heading]]: defence costs of a disciplinary complaint or proceeding | $100,000 |
| [[ref:pi.ext.statutory-liability|heading]] | $250,000 |
| [[ref:pi.ext.first-party-dishonesty|heading]] | $250,000 |
| [[ref:pi.ext.public-relations-costs|heading]] | $50,000 |
| [[ref:pi.ext.court-attendance|heading]] | $25,000 |
