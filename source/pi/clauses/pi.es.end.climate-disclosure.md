For all claims arising from advice on climate-related financial disclosure, including sustainability reports under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), the most we will pay in total, including defence costs, is the sub-limit shown in the schedule for this endorsement. The sub-limit is part of, and not in addition to, the limit of liability.
