We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from advice you give about building defects in a strata or community scheme, or about their rectification, other than passing on advice from a qualified building professional.

This endorsement does not apply to a claim that you failed to notify the owners corporation of a defect, or to arrange an inspection, when your agreement required it.
