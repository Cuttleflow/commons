The cover under [[ref:pi.cover.claims-against-you]] includes your liability for any actual or alleged unintentional infringement of a registered design, or plagiarism, in the course of providing your professional services.

This extension does not cover infringement of a patent: see [[ref:pi.arch.excl.patent-infringement]].
