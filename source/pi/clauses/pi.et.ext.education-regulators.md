We also pay defence costs under [[ref:pi.ext.inquiry-costs]] for an audit, investigation or inquiry into your conduct by an education regulator, including ASQA, TEQSA, the Victorian Registration and Qualifications Authority, the Training Accreditation Council of Western Australia, and a state or territory school registration authority.

This extension does not cover the cost of a routine registration or re-registration audit.
