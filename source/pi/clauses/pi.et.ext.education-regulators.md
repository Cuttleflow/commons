We also pay defence costs under [[ref:pi.ext.inquiry-costs]] for an audit, investigation or inquiry into your conduct by an education regulator, including ASQA, TEQSA, the Victorian Registration and Qualifications Authority, the Training Accreditation Council of Western Australia, and a state or territory school registration authority, that is prompted by a complaint, an allegation or a suspected breach. For this purpose that audit, investigation or inquiry is treated as a formal investigation or inquiry by a regulator.

This extension does not cover the cost of a routine registration or re-registration audit, or any other audit that the regulator carries out as part of its regular monitoring and not because of a complaint, allegation or suspected breach.
