id: pi.excl.related-party-claims
type: exclusion
heading: Related Party Claims
legacy_number: '3.15'
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For any claim brought by: (a) any entity that controls you, is controlled by you, or is under common control with you; (b) any person or entity holding a financial, executive, or managerial interest of 15% or more in you; or (c) any entity in which you hold such an interest. However: this exclusion does not apply to a claim based on your liability to an independent third party arising directly from the performance of your business activity for that third party.
