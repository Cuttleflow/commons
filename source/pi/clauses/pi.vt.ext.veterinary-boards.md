We also pay defence costs under [[ref:pi.ext.inquiry-costs]] for an investigation or inquiry by a veterinary practitioners board or veterinary surgeons board of a state or territory into your professional conduct, before it becomes a formal complaint or disciplinary proceeding.

A formal complaint or disciplinary proceeding before such a board is a claim within paragraph (e) of [[ref:pi.def.claim]]. Cover for it continues under [[ref:pi.cover.claims-against-you]] instead of this extension.
