If the company permanently ceases to carry on its business activity during the policy period (for any reason other than merger or acquisition), this policy continues, without additional premium, until the end of the policy period to cover claims first made against you and notified to us before the end of the policy period. All terms, conditions, exclusions, and the limit of liability continue to apply. This clause confirms that the company ceasing to practise does not end cover before the end of the policy period. The cover under this paragraph ends at the end of the policy period and is not extended beyond that date, except as set out below. An insured person other than the company ceasing to practise does not start run-off cover under this clause.

You may ask us for a quotation to extend cover under this policy for claims first made after the end of the policy period, for a period of up to 84 months after the end of the policy period (or such other maximum period as is shown in the schedule). We will respond to a request made under this paragraph within 30 days of receiving it. We are not obliged to offer terms for an extension, and may decline to offer an extension or may offer one on conditions we consider appropriate. If we agree to offer an extension, it is implemented under Endorsement E01, for the period, on payment of the premium, and subject to the terms shown in the schedule or endorsement schedule for that endorsement.

Nothing in this extension affects your rights, or our obligations, under [[ref:pi.cover.notification-of-circumstances]] or s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). A fact, matter, or circumstance you notify to us in accordance with [[ref:pi.cover.notification-of-circumstances]] before the end of the policy period remains covered as a claim first made during the policy period, whether or not you request, or we offer or agree to, an extension of run-off cover under this clause.
