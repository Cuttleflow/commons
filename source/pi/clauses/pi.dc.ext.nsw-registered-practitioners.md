note: Follows the Architects and Engineers extension of the same name, and adds registered building practitioners, whose insurance requirement under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 (NSW) is deferred to 1 July 2027.
---

This extension applies to you, or to any insured person, who is registered under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 (NSW) as a design practitioner, principal design practitioner, professional engineer or building practitioner.

For a claim arising from work that the Act requires that person to be registered to carry out, the retroactive date is the date that person was first registered under the Act, if that date is earlier than the retroactive date shown in the schedule. That date applies to the claim in place of the date shown in the schedule, as [[ref:pi.def.retroactive-date]] provides.

This extension does not affect [[ref:pi.excl.prior-known-circumstances]].
