profession: recruitment-labour-hire
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
note: These are your own debts as an employer, not compensation for a professional error. A client's claim that you miscalculated its payroll is added by the Payroll Administration endorsement.
---

For any amount that you owe as an employer, including wages, allowances, leave entitlements, superannuation guarantee contributions and charges, payroll tax and workers compensation premiums, or any amount ordered to be paid to a worker to meet a same job, same pay or equal remuneration order.
