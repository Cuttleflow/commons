For any loss of value of, or failure to achieve a return on, an investment or financial product.

This exclusion does not apply to a claim that the loss was caused by your negligent accounting or tax advice, or by financial product advice that is a professional service under [[ref:pi.acct.end.licensed-financial-advice]].
