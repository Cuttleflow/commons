The following services provided to a third party for a fee, by or under the direct control and supervision of a person holding a tertiary qualification in engineering, architecture, surveying or quantity surveying:

(a) architecture;

(b) town planning;

(c) landscape design;

(d) interior design;

(e) drafting;

(f) quantity surveying;

(g) land surveying;

(h) civil engineering;

(i) structural engineering;

(j) mechanical engineering;

(k) electrical engineering;

(l) project management; and

(m) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.
