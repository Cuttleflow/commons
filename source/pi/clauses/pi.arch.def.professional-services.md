The following services provided to a third party for a fee:

(a) architecture;

(b) town planning;

(c) landscape design;

(d) interior design;

(e) drafting;

(f) quantity surveying, including the preparation of tax depreciation schedules;

(g) cadastral, land and engineering surveying;

(h) civil engineering;

(i) structural engineering;

(j) mechanical engineering;

(k) electrical engineering;

(l) project management; and

(m) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

A service in (a) to (l) is a professional service only if it is provided by, or under the direct control and supervision of, a person who holds a tertiary or vocational qualification in the discipline concerned. For drafting in (e), the qualification may be in drafting, architecture, engineering or surveying. For project management in (l), the qualification may be in project management, architecture, engineering, surveying or quantity surveying. For a service in (m), the schedule states any qualification required.

Tax depreciation schedules in (f) are also subject to [[ref:pi.cond.licences-registrations-and-accreditations]].
