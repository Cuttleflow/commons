note: The June EDUC Molestation extension ($250,000 sub-limit) is removed. Since AA v Trustees of the Roman Catholic Church for the Diocese of Maitland-Newcastle [2026] HCA 2, abuse claims are direct institutional
  liability claims, and several states impose a reverse-onus duty on organisations. They need a dedicated liability policy.
---

Arising from the sexual, physical or psychological abuse, molestation or harassment of any person, or from any failure to prevent, detect, report or respond to it, whether the claim is for negligence, breach of a non-delegable duty or any other cause of action.

This exposure is designed to be covered under an abuse liability policy.
