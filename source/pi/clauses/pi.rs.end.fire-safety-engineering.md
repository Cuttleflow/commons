We do not cover any claim, loss or liability arising from fire safety engineering advice, including fire modelling, egress analysis and the assessment of fire safety performance.

This endorsement applies even where the schedule describes a fire safety service in general terms. It does not apply to advice that the schedule names specifically as a professional service.
