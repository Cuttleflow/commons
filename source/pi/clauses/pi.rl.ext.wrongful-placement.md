heading: Wrongful Placement
profession: recruitment-labour-hire
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We cover you for a claim by a client or host business that a candidate or worker you placed did not hold the qualifications, licences, experience, right to work or character that you represented, or that your checks should have revealed, where the claim arises from your negligence in selecting, checking or placing that person.

We will not apply [[ref:pi.excl.employers-liability]] to a claim under this extension.
