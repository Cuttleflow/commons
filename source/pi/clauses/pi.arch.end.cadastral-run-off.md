If you permanently cease to practise during the policy period, and this endorsement is shown in the schedule, [[ref:pi.ext.run-off-cover]] continues for claims arising from cadastral or boundary surveys for the period shown in the schedule for this endorsement, of up to 10 years after the end of the policy period, on payment of the premium shown in the schedule.

All other terms of this policy, including the limit of liability, continue to apply to those claims.
