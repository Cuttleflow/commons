heading: Formal Valuations
profession: real-estate-strata
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
note: Narrows the June valuation exclusion, which applied to appraisals unless the agent had given a written disclaimer.
---

Arising from a formal valuation of real property, unless the valuation was prepared by a person registered or certified as a valuer where the law requires it, and is shown in the schedule as a professional service.

This exclusion does not apply to an appraisal or market assessment of the likely selling price or rent of a property.
