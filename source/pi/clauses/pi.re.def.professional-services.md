The following services provided to a third party for a fee or commission:

(a) the sale, purchase, leasing and management of real property, including residential, commercial, industrial and rural property;

(b) the management of strata and community schemes, including acting as strata managing agent;

(c) stock and station agency, including the sale of livestock and rural property;

(d) auctioneering;

(e) arranging general insurance as an authorised representative, where shown in the schedule; and

(f) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

Appraisals and market assessments of the likely selling price or rent of a property are part of these services.
