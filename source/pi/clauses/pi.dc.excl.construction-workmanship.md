id: pi.dc.excl.construction-workmanship
type: exclusion
heading: Construction Workmanship
profession: design-construct
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
note: The line between design and workmanship. Defective workmanship and materials belong to contract works and liability cover, not to this policy.
---

Arising from construction work performed by you or on your behalf, including the quality of the workmanship, the materials or goods used or supplied, and any failure to build in accordance with the design.

This exclusion does not apply to the extent that the claim arises from an error in the professional services. Where a claim arises partly from an error in the professional services and partly from construction work, we cover the part of the loss and defence costs that is fairly attributable to the error in the professional services, determined as [[ref:pi.pay.allocation]] provides.
