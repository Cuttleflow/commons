The following services provided for a fee, by or under the supervision of a veterinarian registered under the law of the state or territory in which the service is provided:

(a) the examination, diagnosis, treatment and surgery of animals, including general, specialist, equine, livestock, wildlife and exotic animal practice;

(b) prescribing, dispensing, administering and compounding veterinary medicines;

(c) veterinary advice and consultations given remotely (telehealth);

(d) pre-purchase and soundness examinations;

(e) health certificates and other veterinary certification, other than certification for the export of live animals;

(f) euthanasia; and

(g) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

For a service given remotely under (c), the state or territory in which the service is provided is the one in which the animal is located.

Artificial breeding and reproductive services are professional services only if the Reproductive Services endorsement applies.
