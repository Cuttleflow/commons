Clauses in this policy are identified by their headings, not by numbers. A reference in this policy, the schedule, or an endorsement to a clause by its heading (for example, "the Financial Services exclusion") is a reference to the clause with that heading in the version of this policy that applies to you.

Headings do not otherwise limit or extend the meaning of the clause they introduce.
