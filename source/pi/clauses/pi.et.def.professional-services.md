The following services provided for a fee, a grant or other funding:

(a) education and training by a school, higher education provider or registered training organisation, including teaching, assessment, the issue of qualifications and statements of attainment, and student support;

(b) tutoring and coaching;

(c) course and curriculum design;

(d) recruiting and advising students as an education agent; and

(e) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.

A service in (a) is a professional service only while you hold the registration that the law requires to provide it.
