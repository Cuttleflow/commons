Tax depreciation schedules are professional services under this policy only if they are prepared by, or under the direct control and supervision of, the person or entity whose Tax Practitioners Board registration number is shown in the schedule, where that registration is required by law.

We do not cover any claim for a tax shortfall penalty or general interest charge imposed on your client, except to the extent that your client is legally entitled to recover it from you as compensation for your negligence.
