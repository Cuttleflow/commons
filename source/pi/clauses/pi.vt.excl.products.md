heading: Products

since: 1.0.0

note: Replaces the base Products exclusion, which would otherwise remove cover for dispensing and compounding medicines, a core veterinary service.
---

Arising from the design, manufacture, supply, distribution, sale, or recall of any tangible product.

This exclusion does not apply to a claim that you were negligent in prescribing, dispensing, administering or compounding a medicine, or in selecting or supplying a product, in the course of treating or advising on an animal, even if the product was made by someone else and was defective. It continues to apply to a claim that is made only because a product made by someone else was defective, where you are not alleged to have been negligent.

This exposure is designed to be covered under a products liability policy.
