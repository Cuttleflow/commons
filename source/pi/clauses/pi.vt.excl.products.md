---
id: pi.vt.excl.products
type: exclusion
heading: Products
profession: veterinary
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
note: Replaces the base Products exclusion, which would otherwise remove cover for dispensing and compounding medicines, a core veterinary service.
---

Arising from the design, manufacture, supply, distribution, sale, or recall of any tangible product.

This exclusion does not apply to a claim that you were negligent in prescribing, dispensing, administering or compounding a medicine, or in selecting or supplying a product, in the course of treating or advising on an animal. It continues to apply to a claim that a product made by someone else was defective.

This exposure is designed to be covered under a products liability policy.
