1. Contact your broker or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
2. Provide written details of the claim or circumstance — see [[ref:pi.obl.tell-us-about-claims]] and [[ref:pi.obl.tell-us-about-circumstances]] for what to include.
3. We will acknowledge your notification and assign a claims manager.
4. We will work with you to investigate and manage the claim and keep you informed of progress.
5. Do not admit liability or agree to any settlement without our written consent — see [[ref:pi.obl.do-not-admit-liability]].
