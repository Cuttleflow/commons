profession: veterinary

note: The base exception for injury or damage caused by a breach of professional duty does not reach losses such as an escape or a kennel injury. The June wording had no write-back for animals in care.
---

Despite [[ref:pi.excl.bodily-injury-and-property-damage]], we cover your liability for the death of, injury to, or loss of an animal while it is in your care, custody or control for your professional services, including while it is hospitalised, boarded or transported by you.

The most we will pay under this extension for all claims in the policy period is the sub-limit shown in the schedule, which is part of the limit of liability. This extension does not cover an animal that you own.
