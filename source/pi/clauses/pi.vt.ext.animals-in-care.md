Despite [[ref:pi.excl.bodily-injury-and-property-damage]], we cover your liability, arising from your negligence or your failure to exercise reasonable care and skill, for the death of, injury to, or loss of an animal while it is in your care, custody or control for your professional services, including while it is hospitalised, boarded or transported by you. We do not cover liability that arises only because you have custody of the animal, without negligence.

The most we will pay under this extension for all claims in the policy period is the sub-limit shown in the schedule, or $100,000 if no sub-limit is shown, which is part of the limit of liability. This extension does not cover an animal that you own.
