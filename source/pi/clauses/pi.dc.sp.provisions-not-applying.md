[[ref:pi.ext.continuous-cover]], [[ref:pi.ext.run-off-cover]] and [[ref:pi.ext.newly-acquired-subsidiaries]] do not apply, and the run-off extension endorsement in [[ref:pi.endorsement-library]] is not available. [[ref:pi.ext.joint-ventures]] applies only to a joint venture that is a party to the construction contract for the project.
