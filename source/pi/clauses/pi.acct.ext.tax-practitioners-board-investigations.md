We also pay defence costs under [[ref:pi.ext.inquiry-costs]] for an investigation by the Tax Practitioners Board into your conduct as a registered tax agent or BAS agent, and for a review or audit by the Australian Taxation Office of your conduct as a registered agent.

This extension does not cover the cost of responding to an audit or review of a client's tax affairs, except to the extent that the client is entitled to recover that cost from you because of your negligence.
