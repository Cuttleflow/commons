If you acquire a rent roll or strata management rights, we will cover you for a claim arising from the management of a property in that rent roll or scheme before you acquired it, to the extent that you are legally liable for it as the successor to the vendor, if you told us about the acquisition in your proposal or within 30 days after it.

For a claim under this extension, the retroactive date does not apply to the management of the property before you acquired the rent roll or rights. [[ref:pi.excl.prior-known-circumstances]] and the rest of this policy continue to apply. If another insurance policy also covers the claim, [[ref:pi.pay.other-insurance]] applies; this extension does not make this policy excess of that other insurance.
