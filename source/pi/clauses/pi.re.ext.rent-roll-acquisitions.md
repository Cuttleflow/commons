profession: real-estate-strata
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If you acquire a rent roll or strata management rights, we will cover you for a claim arising from the management of a property in that rent roll or scheme before you acquired it, to the extent that you are legally liable for it as the successor to the vendor, if:

(a) you told us about the acquisition in your proposal or within 30 days after it; and

(b) no other insurance responds to the claim.
