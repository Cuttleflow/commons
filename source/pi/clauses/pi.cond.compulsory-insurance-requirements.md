This condition applies where a law, or a requirement made under a law by a regulator or licensing body, requires you to hold professional indemnity insurance with minimum terms in order to provide your professional services. Each such requirement is a compulsory insurance requirement. The schedule lists any compulsory insurance requirement that we know applies to you. A requirement that is not listed in the schedule still applies if the law makes it apply to you.

For a claim to which a compulsory insurance requirement applies, a term of this policy applies only to the extent that the requirement permits it. This includes the retroactive date, an exclusion, a sub-limit, the excess, reinstatement and run-off. Where a term is not permitted, this policy is read as providing the cover, excess terms, reinstatement and run-off that the requirement prescribes, and we will pay what the requirement obliges an insurer to pay. If the requirement prescribes a minimum limit that is higher than the limit of liability shown in the schedule, this condition does not increase the limit of liability: you are responsible for choosing a limit at least equal to the minimum.

We pay an amount under this condition to the person the requirement says is entitled to it. That is you, unless the requirement gives the claimant a right to be paid directly, in which case it is the claimant. An amount we pay under this condition reduces the limit of liability in the same way as any other payment, and [[ref:pi.pay.automatic-reinstatement]] applies to it.

If we pay an amount only because of this condition, and we would not otherwise have been liable to pay it under this policy, you must repay that amount to us, to the extent that the law and the requirement allow us to recover it.
