Each insured person's cover is assessed individually. A failure by one insured person to comply with [[ref:pi.obl.tell-us-about-claims]], [[ref:pi.obl.tell-us-about-circumstances]], [[ref:pi.obl.do-not-admit-liability]], [[ref:pi.obl.cooperate-with-us]], [[ref:pi.obl.mitigate-your-loss]] or [[ref:pi.obl.preserve-our-recovery-rights]], or any knowledge, act, error, omission, or non-disclosure of one insured person, does not affect the cover of another insured person who did not know of, participate in, or contribute to that failure, knowledge, act, error, omission, or non-disclosure. This section does not affect [[ref:pi.excl.deliberate-and-dishonest-acts]] or its Final adjudication and non-imputation mechanics, which continue to apply according to their terms.
