id: pi.def.insured-person
type: definition
heading: Insured person
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

(a) any past, present, or future partner, principal, director, officer, or member of the company;

(b) any employee of the company; and

(c) any person who was a partner, principal, director, officer, member, or employee of a predecessor practice that the company has acquired, merged with, or succeeded.
