Where a law, or an accreditation scheme under which you provide a service, requires the person who provides that service to hold a licence, registration, accreditation or endorsement, the service is a professional service under this policy only if it is provided by a person who holds it, or under the direct control and supervision of such a person to the extent that the law or scheme permits.

This condition looks at whether the licence, registration, accreditation or endorsement was held when the service was provided. If it lapses or is cancelled later, cover for a service already provided is not affected.
