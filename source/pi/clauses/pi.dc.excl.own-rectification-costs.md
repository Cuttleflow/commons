---
id: pi.dc.excl.own-rectification-costs
type: exclusion
heading: Your Own Rectification Costs
profession: design-construct
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For any cost that you incur yourself, as distinct from a liability you have to a third party, to re-perform or correct the professional services, or to remove, replace, repair or complete construction work, including under an obligation in a construction contract to rectify defects, except as [[ref:pi.dc.ext.design-rectification]] provides.

This exclusion does not prevent cover for a claim by a third party, including the principal under a construction contract, for the cost of rectifying construction work that was built in accordance with a design containing an error in the professional services.
