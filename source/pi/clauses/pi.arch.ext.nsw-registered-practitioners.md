This extension applies to you, or to any insured person, who is registered under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 (NSW) as a design practitioner, principal design practitioner or professional engineer.

For a claim arising from work that the Act requires you to be registered to carry out, the retroactive date is the date you were first registered under that Act, if that date is earlier than the retroactive date shown in the schedule. That date applies to the claim in place of the date shown in the schedule, as the definition of [[ref:pi.def.retroactive-date]] provides.

This extension does not affect [[ref:pi.excl.prior-known-circumstances]].
