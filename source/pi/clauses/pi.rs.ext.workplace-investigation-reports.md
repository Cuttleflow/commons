type: extension
heading: Workplace Investigation Reports
profession: risk-safety-claims
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
note: Investigation reports are published deliberately to the client, so the base cover for unintentional defamation may not respond. This extension makes the cover express.
---

The cover for defamation under [[ref:pi.cover.claims-against-you]] includes a claim by a person who was the subject of, or a witness in, a workplace investigation you conducted, arising from statements in a report you gave to your client, unless a **Final adjudication** establishes that you published the statements with malice.
