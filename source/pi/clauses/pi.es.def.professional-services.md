The following services provided to a third party for a fee:

(a) building energy and water ratings and assessments, including under NatHERS, BASIX, NABERS, Green Star and the Commercial Building Disclosure Program;

(b) energy and water audits;

(c) advice on energy efficiency and renewable energy options, other than the design of solar or battery systems;

(d) embodied carbon assessments and advice on climate-related disclosure, other than assurance engagements; and

(e) any other service shown in the schedule as a professional service.
