---
id: pi.rl.excl.on-hired-work
type: exclusion
heading: Work Performed by On-hired Workers
profession: recruitment-labour-hire
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Arising from the work performed by a worker you supply to a host business, while the worker is working under the direction or supervision of the host.

This exclusion does not apply to a claim arising from your negligence in selecting, checking, inducting or placing the worker, and is subject to the On-hired Workers endorsement if it is shown in the schedule.
