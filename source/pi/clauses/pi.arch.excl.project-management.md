Arising from your project management services, to the extent the claim arises from:

(a) a failure to arrange or maintain insurance, or performance or other bonds;

(b) a failure to obtain finance;

(c) financial, insurance or tax advice;

(d) the insolvency of any party to the project;

(e) construction costs exceeding any estimate, unless the estimate was prepared by a qualified quantity surveyor; or

(f) bodily injury to an employee of any party arising out of their employment, unless the injury arises directly from an error in a design covered by this policy.
