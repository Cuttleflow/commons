For money held in trust that is misappropriated by a principal, director or licensee in charge of your business, or with their knowledge — but only to the extent that a **Final adjudication** establishes the misappropriation. Until then, we will advance defence costs under [[ref:pi.pay.defence-costs]]. If a Final adjudication then establishes the misappropriation, the defence costs advanced for it must be repaid, as that clause provides.

This exclusion does not apply to an insured person who did not commit, condone or know of the misappropriation. That protection applies separately to each insured person.
