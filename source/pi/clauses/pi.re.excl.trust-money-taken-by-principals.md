heading: Trust Money Taken by Principals
profession: real-estate-strata
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
note: Shortfalls caused by principals are a crime and fidelity fund risk, not a professional indemnity risk. The base Compulsory Insurance Requirements condition applies if a state law requires this cover.
---

For money held in trust that is misappropriated by a principal, director or licensee in charge of your business, or with their knowledge.

This exclusion does not apply to an insured person who did not commit, condone or know of the misappropriation.
