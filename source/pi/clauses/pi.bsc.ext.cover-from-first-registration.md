This extension applies to a building surveyor or certifier registered under the Building and Development Certifiers Act 2018 (NSW), and to any other building surveyor or certifier registered under a law of a state or territory that requires insurance to cover liability for work carried out since first registration.

For a claim arising from work carried out since first registration, the retroactive date is the date you were first registered under that law, if that date is earlier than the retroactive date shown in the schedule. That date applies to the claim in place of the date shown in the schedule, as the definition of [[ref:pi.def.retroactive-date]] provides.

This extension does not affect [[ref:pi.excl.prior-known-circumstances]].
