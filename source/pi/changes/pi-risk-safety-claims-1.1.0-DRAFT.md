Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. Built on base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.

- Base changes flow through: Financial Services exclusion, Sanctions, Statutory Liability, Other Insurance, Cancellation, the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition and the other changes listed for base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.
- Professional services: loss assessing, loss adjusting and claims management (other than deciding or settling claims) are stated to be outside the Financial Services exclusion. Flagged for review of whether loss assessing and loss adjusting need an Australian financial services licence.
- Workplace Investigation Reports extension: now states that it applies despite the limit to unintentional defamation, and covers defamation in a report you give your client unless a Final adjudication establishes malice.
- Claims Handling Authorisation endorsement: flagged for review of ASIC RG 126.
- Fire Safety System Design and Certification exclusion and Fire Safety Engineering Exclusion endorsement: each now states that the definition of professional services operates first, and that each applies where the schedule describes a fire safety service in general terms but not where the schedule names the work specifically.
