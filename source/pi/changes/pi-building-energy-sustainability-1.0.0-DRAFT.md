First release as an integrated profession wording, built on base PI 1.4.0-DRAFT. It replaces the endorsement MERIDIAN-PI-ENER-v1.0.0 (June 2026).

- The restriction to domestic, school and not-for-profit clients is removed.
- The conflict and financial-interest exclusions are removed; a proposal question asks about financial interests in suppliers.
- The investment-returns wording is replaced by a narrow exclusion of guarantees of savings, ratings or returns.
- Physical installation is excluded through the Construction Work exclusion, with a carve-back for design and inspection.
- New extension: Scheme Administrator Inquiries.
- Optional endorsements: Energy Certificate Schemes Exclusion, Climate Disclosure Sub-limit, Combustible Cladding Exclusion and Solar and Battery System Design (adds cover).
