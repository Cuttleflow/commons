First release as an integrated profession wording, built on base PI 1.4.0-DRAFT. It replaces the endorsements MERIDIAN-PI-EDUC-v1.0.0 and MERIDIAN-PI-RTOO-v1.0.0 (June 2026).

- Removed: the Molestation extension and its $250,000 sub-limit. Abuse is now excluded absolutely and is designed to be covered by an abuse liability policy.
- Removed: the RTO Standards Compliance extension, which referred to standards issued by ANTA (abolished in 2005).
- New extensions: Failure to Educate (merging the June scenarios), Re-assessment and Re-issue Costs, Education Regulators, and Teachers, Contractors and Volunteers.
- New exclusions: Abuse, Fee Refunds and Research.
- Auspicing, previously only in a notes box, is now the Third-Party Delivery Exclusion endorsement.
- Optional endorsements: Third-Party Delivery Exclusion, International Students Sub-limit, VET Student Loans Exclusion, High-Risk Practical Training Exclusion and Increased Re-assessment Sub-limit (adds cover).
