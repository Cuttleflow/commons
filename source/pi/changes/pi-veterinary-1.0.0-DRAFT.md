First release as an integrated profession wording, built on base PI 1.4.0-DRAFT. It replaces the endorsement MERIDIAN-PI-VETS-v1.0.0 (June 2026), rebuilt from the April 2026 source text because the June file could not be opened.

- Professional services cover examination, treatment and surgery, medicines, telehealth, pre-purchase examinations, certification (other than live export) and euthanasia, by or under the supervision of a registered veterinarian.
- Products: the base exclusion is replaced so that negligent prescribing, dispensing, administering and compounding of medicines is covered. Defective products made by others remain excluded.
- New extensions: Animals in Care (care, custody and control write-back), Veterinary Boards and Emergency Treatment.
- New exclusion: Animal Welfare and Biosecurity Penalties (defence costs still covered).
- High-Value Animals Excess carried over ($5,000 where an animal is worth more than $25,000 or a herd or flock more than $100,000), with the valuation date stated.
- Artificial insemination and gene therapy are now proposal questions. Optional endorsements: Racing Animals Excess, Pre-purchase Examinations Sub-limit, Reproductive Services (adds cover) and Live Export Certification Exclusion.
