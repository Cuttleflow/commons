Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. Built on base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.

- Base changes flow through: Financial Services exclusion, Sanctions, Statutory Liability, Other Insurance, Cancellation, the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition and the other changes listed for base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.
- Professional services: advice on climate-related disclosure, including advice on a sustainability report under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), is stated to be outside the Financial Services exclusion unless it involves financial product advice, dealing in a financial product or a credit activity. Flagged for review of whether any accreditation scheme sets minimum insurance terms.
- Scheme Administrator Inquiries extension: now covers an audit, investigation or inquiry that is prompted by a complaint, an allegation or a suspected breach, and treats it as a formal inquiry. It does not cover a routine audit, spot check or compliance review.
