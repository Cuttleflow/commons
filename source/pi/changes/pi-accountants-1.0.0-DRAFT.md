First release as an integrated profession wording, built on base PI 1.4.0-DRAFT. It replaces the endorsement MERIDIAN-PI-ACCT-v1.0.0 (June 2026).

- Professional services now list tax (financial) advice, payroll, restructuring and trustee work. Registrations are required through the base Licences, Registrations and Accreditations condition.
- Deleted: the Audit Failure exclusion (fraud not discovered). It removed cover where auditors are most often liable.
- Deleted: the Financial Services Advice exclusion. The base Financial Services exclusion, with its carve-back for scheduled licensed services, applies instead.
- Deleted: the Predecessor Firm extension. The base Predecessor Practices extension is wider and applies instead.
- Investment Performance exclusion: the carve-back now applies to any claim that the loss was caused by negligent accounting or tax advice, not only to claims arising "solely" from it.
- New extension: Tax Practitioners Board and ATO Investigations. New exclusion: Tax Exploitation Schemes.
- Optional endorsements: Registered Liquidators (adds fidelity and run-off cover), Listed Entity Audits Exclusion, SMSF Audits Sub-limit, Licensed Financial Advice (adds cover), Valuations Sub-limit and Crypto-assets Exclusion.
