Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. Built on base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.

- Base changes flow through: Financial Services exclusion, Sanctions, Statutory Liability, Other Insurance, Cancellation, the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition and the other changes listed for base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.
- Failure to Educate extension: now states its scope. It covers compensation for loss caused by the failure, such as replacement training. It does not cover the refund of tuition fees or liability greater than at law.
- Re-assessment and Re-issue Costs extension: now responds to a regulator's finding made in a claim, or notified as a circumstance during the policy period, for assessments made on or after the retroactive date. It has a default sub-limit of $50,000. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Abuse exclusion: flagged for review of whether it is compatible with any insurance condition of school or registered training organisation registration.
- Education Regulators extension: now covers an audit, investigation or inquiry prompted by a complaint, an allegation or a suspected breach, treats it as a formal inquiry, and does not cover routine audits.
