Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. Built on base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.

- Base changes flow through: Financial Services exclusion, Sanctions, Statutory Liability, Other Insurance, Cancellation, the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition and the other changes listed for base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.
- Cover from First Registration extension: now names the Building and Development Certifiers Act 2018 (NSW) and states that the date of first registration applies in place of the schedule date. Flagged for review.
- External Cladding Exclusion endorsement: now sets out, in a table, when it applies in New South Wales, Victoria, Queensland and elsewhere. The permitted forms for New South Wales and Victoria, and the Queensland end date, are flagged for review.
- Building Regulator Proceedings extension: now says that a formal complaint or proceeding before a building regulator is a claim for which we pay defence costs, and that an earlier investigation or inquiry falls under Inquiry Costs. It states that it applies despite the Statutory Liability extension, so no fine or penalty imposed by the regulator is paid.
- Apartment Buildings Sub-limit and Retirement Run-off endorsements: each is flagged for review of whether a New South Wales requirement means it cannot be an optional priced endorsement.
