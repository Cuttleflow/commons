First release as an integrated profession wording, built on base PI 1.4.0-DRAFT. It replaces the endorsement MERIDIAN-PI-RISK-v1.0.0 and the consulting services in MERIDIAN-PI-OHS-v1.0.0 (June 2026).

- The June OHS asbestos, nuclear and terrorism "replacement" clauses are not carried over. They removed the war and sanctions exclusions without providing an exclusion of their own. The base exclusions apply.
- Clinical occupational health services move to the Allied Health wording and are excluded here.
- Work health and safety penalties that the law does not allow to be insured are not covered (base Statutory Liability extension).
- New extension: Workplace Investigation Reports. New exclusions: Clinical Services; Fire Safety System Design and Certification.
- Optional endorsements: Claims Handling Authorisation (adds cover), Surveillance Exclusion, Psychosocial Hazards Sub-limit, Fire Safety Engineering Exclusion and Expert Witness (adds cover).
