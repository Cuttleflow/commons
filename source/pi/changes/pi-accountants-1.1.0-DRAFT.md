Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. Built on base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.

- Base changes flow through: Financial Services exclusion, Sanctions, Statutory Liability, Other Insurance, Cancellation, the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition and the other changes listed for base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.
- Professional services: audit, review and insolvency work in (b) and (e) is now stated to be outside the Financial Services exclusion unless it involves financial product advice, dealing in a financial product or a credit activity.
- Tax Exploitation Schemes exclusion: now turns on a Final adjudication only, with defence costs advanced and repaid if the promotion is established. A civil penalty order no longer triggers it.
- Investment Performance exclusion: the carve-back now also covers loss caused by financial product advice that is a professional service under the Licensed Financial Advice endorsement.
- Licensed Financial Advice endorsement: confirms that the Investment Performance exclusion does not apply to a claim that the loss was caused by negligent advice given under the licence. Flagged for review against ASIC RG 126.
- Registered Liquidators endorsement: says how the appointment money cover works alongside the Deliberate and Dishonest Acts exclusion and the First-Party Dishonesty extension. The cover does not reach a principal or director who is dishonest. The fidelity sub-limit and the run-off of up to seven years are flagged for review against ASIC RG 258.
