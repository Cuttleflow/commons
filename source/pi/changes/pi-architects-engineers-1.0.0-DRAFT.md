First release as an integrated profession wording. It replaces the endorsement MERIDIAN-PI-ARCH-v1.0.0 (June 2026), which was written against base PI v1.0.0. The same terms are also published as an endorsement to base PI v1.3.0-DRAFT; both are generated from one source.

- Professional services: disciplines carried over from the v1.0.0 endorsement, with limb (m) so that an unlisted discipline is covered only when shown in the schedule. A service that the law requires a registered, licensed or endorsed person to provide is a professional service only if provided by, or under the permitted supervision of, such a person. Other services must be provided by, or under the supervision of, a tertiary-qualified person.
- Registered Designs and Plagiarism extension: replaces the v1.0.0 "Modified Extension — Intellectual Property", which would have removed cover for moral rights and passing off. The base Claims Against You clause already covers copyright, trademark, moral rights, passing off and breach of confidence.
- NSW Registered Practitioners extension (new): for work regulated by the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 (NSW), the retroactive date is the date of first registration if earlier.
- Subcontractors: the v1.0.0 conditions are not carried over; the base Subcontractors and Outsourcers extension applies, and the matters are asked on the proposal.
- Construction Work exclusion: carves design, contract administration, site inspection, site observation and certification back in. The v1.0.0 text excluded "supervision" of construction.
- Excluded Engineering Activities: bridges and tunnels moved to an optional endorsement; environmental engineering removed, so the base Pollution exclusion applies.
- Liquidated Damages and Contractual Penalties exclusion kept; the mining-equipment warranty exclusion moved to an optional endorsement.
- Automatic Reinstatement (new): the limit of liability is reinstated once in the policy period.
- Architects and Engineers Endorsements (new): optional Bridges and Tunnels, Combustible Cladding and Mining Equipment Warranties exclusions, which apply only when shown in the schedule and not where a law requiring the insurance prohibits the exclusion.
- Products and contractual-liability limbs of the v1.0.0 endorsement omitted because the base wording already contains them.
- Cross-references cite clause headings; there are no clause numbers.
