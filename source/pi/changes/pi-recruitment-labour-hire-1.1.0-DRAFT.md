Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. Built on base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.

- Base changes flow through: Financial Services exclusion, Sanctions, Statutory Liability, Other Insurance, Cancellation, the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition and the other changes listed for base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.
- Labour Hire Licence Condition endorsement: the licence is now tested when the service was provided. A later lapse or cancellation does not remove cover for services already provided. Flagged for review.
- Wrongful Placement extension: states that the Employer's Liability exclusion applies to you as the employer of your own workers, and does not apply to a claim by a candidate, or by a client of your human resources consulting services, who was not employed by you. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- On-hired Workers endorsement: now has a sub-limit, which is the schedule amount or 25% of the limit of liability if none is shown. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Wages, Superannuation and Employer Payments exclusion: now limited to amounts you owe as the employer of your own workers, including workers you supply to hosts, which matches the Payroll Administration endorsement.
