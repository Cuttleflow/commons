Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. Built on base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.

- Base changes flow through: Financial Services exclusion, Sanctions, Statutory Liability, Other Insurance, Cancellation, the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) condition and the other changes listed for base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT.
- Animals in Care extension: now covers liability arising from your negligence or failure to exercise reasonable care and skill, and does not cover liability that arises only because you have custody of the animal. It has a default sub-limit of $100,000. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Veterinary Boards extension: now covers an investigation or inquiry before it becomes a formal complaint or disciplinary proceeding. A formal complaint or proceeding is a claim covered under Claims Against You.
- Products exclusion: the carve-back now says that negligent selection or supply of a product is covered even if the product was made by someone else and was defective. The exclusion continues to apply to a claim made only because such a product was defective.
- Professional services: for telehealth, the state or territory in which the service is provided is the one in which the animal is located. Flagged for review of cross-border registration.
