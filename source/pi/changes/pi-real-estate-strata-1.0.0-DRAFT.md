First release as an integrated profession wording, built on base PI 1.4.0-DRAFT. It replaces the endorsement MERIDIAN-PI-REAL-v1.0.0 (June 2026).

- Professional services include strata management, stock and station agency, auctioneering and, where scheduled, insurance distribution as an authorised representative. Appraisals are covered.
- Deleted: the conflict-of-interest and financial-interest exclusions. They removed misleading-conduct and breach-of-duty cover that NSW licence conditions require.
- Valuation exclusion narrowed to formal valuations, with no disclaimer condition for appraisals.
- New extensions: Dishonesty of Employees and Agents, Franchisor's Indemnity (now operative), Rent Roll Acquisitions and Tribunal Proceedings.
- New exclusion: Trust Money Taken by Principals.
- Optional endorsements: Payment Redirection (adds cover), Commercial Property over $10 Million Exclusion, Strata Defects Advice Exclusion, Business Broking (adds cover), Off-the-Plan Sales Sub-limit and Livestock Sales Injury and Damage Exclusion.
