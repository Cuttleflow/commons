First release as an integrated profession wording, built on base PI 1.5.0-DRAFT, for builders and trade contractors who take design responsibility under their construction contracts (Linear PLT-1405, 27 November 2026). It covers an annual basis and a single-project basis in one wording.

- Professional services: design of construction work (including temporary works), design documentation, design management and the co-ordination of design consultants, value engineering, and contract administration, when provided in connection with a construction contract. Construction work itself is not a professional service.
- New definitions: Construction contract, Construction work and Design consultant.
- Design Consultants and Subcontractors replaces the base Subcontractors and Outsourcers extension. Design procured from consultants is covered as the insured's own, whether or not the consultant is insured. This responds to the non-delegable duty in s 37 of the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 (NSW), which Pafburn Pty Ltd v The Owners - Strata Plan No 84674 [2024] HCA 49 held is not apportionable for developers and head contractors.
- New extensions: Design Rectification Costs (first-party cost of correcting a design error before a claim, sub-limited by default to one quarter of the limit of liability) and NSW Registered Practitioners (adds registered building practitioners to the Architects and Engineers form).
- New exclusions: Construction Workmanship (with a carve-back and allocation for the design part of a mixed claim), Your Own Rectification Costs, Delay and Cost Overruns, Liquidated Damages and Contractual Penalties, and Excluded Projects.
- New section: Single Project Cover, which applies only when the schedule shows the single-project basis: one project, a construction period and a reporting period, one aggregate limit with no automatic reinstatement, and a premium fully earned from the start date.
- Optional endorsements: Combustible Cladding Exclusion (applies unless removed on referral) and Apartment Buildings Sub-limit (refers for Class 2 buildings and buildings over three storeys).
