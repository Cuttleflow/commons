Changes that let one base wording carry profession wordings with statutory insurance requirements. Recorded from the profession review approved on 31 October 2026.

- Financial Services exclusion: does not apply to a service shown in the schedule that you are licensed or authorised to provide under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) or the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (Cth). This replaces the separate carve-backs that profession endorsements would otherwise need.
- Statutory Liability extension: states expressly that we do not pay a fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured, including work health and safety penalties.
- Automatic Reinstatement (new): the limit of liability is reinstated once in the policy period. Endorsement E08 now provides additional reinstatements.
- Licences, Registrations and Accreditations condition (new): a service that the law or an accreditation scheme requires a licensed, registered or accredited person to provide is covered only when such a person provides or supervises it.
- Compulsory Insurance Requirements condition (new): where a law or regulator sets minimum insurance terms for your profession, the policy is read as providing at least those terms, with a right for us to recover amounts paid only because of this condition.
- Professional Standards Schemes condition (new): the policy is intended to meet the insurance standard of any professional standards scheme of which you are a member.
