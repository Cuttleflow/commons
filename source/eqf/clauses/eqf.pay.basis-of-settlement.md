heading: Basis of Settlement
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We may repair the item, replace it with a new item of the same kind and capacity, or pay you the cost of doing so. Where the item was less than 5 years old at the time of the loss, we pay on that basis without deduction for age. Where it was 5 years old or more, we pay its market value at the time of the loss, unless the schedule shows that the item is insured for its agreed value.

We pay no more than the sum insured for a specified item, or the amount shown in the schedule for any one unspecified item. We do not apply average or reduce a payment because the sum insured is less than the value of the property.
