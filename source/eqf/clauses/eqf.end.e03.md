heading: E03 Overnight Vehicle Storage
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
trigger:
  questions:
  - qs.eqf.security.overnight_storage
  rules:
  - when: qs.eqf.security.overnight_storage == VEHICLE
    action: apply
  reason: Applies because your equipment is kept in a vehicle overnight.
---

Insured property kept overnight in a vehicle is covered for theft only where the vehicle is locked, has an engaged alarm or immobiliser, and is parked at your premises, at your home or in a locked garage or compound, or the property is in a locked tool box or rack fixed to the vehicle. [[ref:eqf.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to these conditions.

Schedule variables: none.
