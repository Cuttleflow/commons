---
id: eqf.obl.tell-us-about-loss
type: obligation
heading: Tell Us About Loss
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable about any loss or damage that may give rise to a claim. Theft and malicious damage must be reported to the police within 48 hours of your discovering it. You must give us the information and help we reasonably ask for, including proof of ownership and value, and keep damaged items available for inspection until we have had a reasonable chance to inspect them.
