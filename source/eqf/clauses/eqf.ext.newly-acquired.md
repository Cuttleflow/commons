---
id: eqf.ext.newly-acquired
type: extension
heading: Newly Acquired Equipment
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Portable equipment you acquire during the policy period is insured property for 30 days after you acquire it, up to the amount shown in the schedule, or if none is shown, 20% of the total sum insured or $10,000, whichever is less. You must tell us about it within those 30 days for it to remain insured after them.
