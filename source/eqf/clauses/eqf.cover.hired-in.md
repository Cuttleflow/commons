id: eqf.cover.hired-in
type: cover
heading: Hired-In Equipment Cover
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We pay for accidental loss of or damage to hired-in equipment that happens during the policy period anywhere in Australia, up to the sum insured for this section shown in the schedule for any one item and in total for any one event. We pay the lesser of the cost of repair, the cost of replacement and the amount the hire agreement makes you liable for, together with the hire charges you are obliged to keep paying while the item cannot be used, up to 4 weeks.

The exclusions in [[ref:eqf.excl.portable-equipment]] apply to this section as if hired-in equipment were insured property.
