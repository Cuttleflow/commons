---
id: eqf.cover.hire-costs
type: cover
heading: Replacement Hire Costs Cover
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

After accidental loss of or damage to insured property that [[ref:eqf.cover.portable-equipment|heading]] covers, we pay the reasonable cost of hiring a similar item while the lost or damaged item is repaired or replaced, from the time of the loss or damage until the repaired or replacement item is available, up to the limit for this section shown in the schedule for any one event, or if none is shown, $2,500, and for no more than 4 weeks.
