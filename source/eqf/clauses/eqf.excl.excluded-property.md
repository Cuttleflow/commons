id: eqf.excl.excluded-property
type: exclusion
heading: Excluded Property
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss of or damage to:

- motor vehicles, trailers, caravans, watercraft and aircraft, including drones and unmanned aircraft, and their parts and accessories while fitted;
- self-propelled plant, or plant registrable for use on a public road;
- stock in trade, materials and goods held for sale or for installation in a customer's property;
- money, cards, jewellery and watches;
- mobile phones, unless the schedule shows them as specified items; and
- property used wholly or predominantly for personal, domestic or household purposes.
