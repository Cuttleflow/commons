heading: Portable Equipment Cover
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We pay for accidental loss of or damage to insured property that happens during the policy period anywhere in Australia, including while it is in a vehicle, on a site, at your premises or at your home, or in transit between them.

We pay for a specified item up to its sum insured, and for unspecified tools up to the blanket sum insured, but not more than the amount shown in the schedule for any one unspecified item.
