id: eqf.cond.theft-from-vehicles
type: condition
heading: Theft From an Unattended Vehicle
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For theft of insured property from an unattended vehicle, we pay only if the vehicle was locked and its windows closed, and entry was gained by force or violence, or the property was in a locked tool box or rack fixed to the vehicle and was removed by force or violence. The special excess in [[ref:eqf.pay.excess]] applies to each such claim.

[[ref:eqf.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to this condition: if the security was not in place but did not cause or contribute to the theft, we pay the claim.
