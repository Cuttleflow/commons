since: 1.0.0
status: draft
trigger:
  questions:
  - qs.eqf.equipment.employees_tools
  rules:
  - when: qs.eqf.equipment.employees_tools == true
    action: apply
  reason: Applies because you want your employees' tools insured.
---

Tools owned by your employees and used in your business are insured property while they are at your premises, on a site or in your vehicle, up to the amount shown in the schedule for each employee, and within the blanket sum insured for unspecified tools.

Schedule variables: amount for each employee (default $2,000).
