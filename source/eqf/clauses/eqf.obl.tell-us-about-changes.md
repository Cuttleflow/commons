type: obligation
heading: Tell Us About Changes
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You must tell us within 30 days if, during the policy period, the property starts to be used wholly or predominantly for personal, domestic or household purposes, or you start to leave insured property in the open overnight. We may change the premium or terms for the rest of the policy period, as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows.
