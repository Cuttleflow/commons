For each claim you bear the excess shown in the schedule, for each event. For theft from an unattended vehicle, the special excess shown in the schedule applies in addition, or if none is shown, $500. Where an endorsement shows a special excess, it applies instead of the excess for the kind of loss it names. Where more than one excess applies to the same event, only the highest is deducted, except the theft from vehicles special excess, which applies in addition.
