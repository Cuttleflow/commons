since: 1.0.0

---

[[ref:eqf.cover.portable-equipment|heading]] extends to insured property that you take outside Australia for business use for no more than 90 consecutive days on any one trip, including while in transit to and from Australia as accompanied baggage or by courier. We do not pay for loss or damage in a country to which the Australian Government's Smartraveller service advised against all travel at the time.
