Under [[ref:eqf.section.portable-equipment|heading]] we do not pay for:

- mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdown or failure, unless it results from an external accidental event;
- wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion, or damage from cleaning, repairing or adjusting;
- scratching, chipping or denting that does not stop the item working;
- loss of or damage to consumable parts, bits, blades and batteries, unless the item they are part of is lost or damaged at the same time;
- loss of data or software, or the cost of recreating them;
- unexplained disappearance, or a shortage found only on taking stock; or
- theft of insured property left unattended in the open overnight, unless endorsement E01 applies.
