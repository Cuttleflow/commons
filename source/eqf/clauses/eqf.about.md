This policy insures the portable tools and equipment you use in your business, wherever they are in Australia: at your premises, in your vehicle, on a site or in transit between them. It is for tools and equipment that are carried by hand or in a vehicle. It does not insure self-propelled or registrable plant, such as excavators, loaders, forklifts or trailers; a plant and equipment policy insures those.

The schedule shows which sections apply.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Portable Equipment | Accidental loss of or damage to your specified items, and to unspecified tools on a blanket sum insured | Loss or damage that happens during the policy period |
| Hired-In Equipment | Portable equipment you hire or borrow from others | As above |
| Replacement Hire Costs | The cost of hiring a replacement while an insured item is repaired or replaced | After loss or damage the Portable Equipment section covers |
| Worldwide Use | Extends the Portable Equipment section to temporary use outside Australia | As above |

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. [[ref:eqf.cond.order-of-precedence]] says which part applies where they differ.
