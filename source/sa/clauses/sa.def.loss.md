The amount an insured person becomes personally liable to pay, including: (a) compensatory damages (including interest); (b) the claimant's legal costs awarded against the insured person; (c) defence costs; and (d) settlements agreed with our prior written consent.

We do not pay any fine or penalty that the law does not allow to be insured. In particular, we do not pay a penalty imposed under work health and safety law in a State or Territory whose law prohibits insurance of it (including New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland), and we do not pay a criminal fine. [WP REVIEW: confirm the list of jurisdictions that prohibit insurance of WHS penalties; library sources differ on WA and Victoria.]

Loss does not include: (i) any amount the company has actually paid to, or on behalf of, the insured person by way of indemnity or advancement; (ii) taxes, or the return of remuneration to which the insured person was not entitled; (iii) fines, penalties, pecuniary penalty orders, or compensation orders, to the extent the law does not permit us to pay them; or (iv) matters uninsurable at law. Who may pay the premium is addressed in [[ref:sa.cond.corporations-act-199a-199b]].]
