This policy protects individual directors and officers against loss that their company cannot or will not indemnify. It sits above the company's directors' and officers' liability or management liability insurance (the underlying D&O insurance) and pays only after that insurance has been exhausted by payment.

It is called “Side A” cover because it protects only the individuals, not the company. Its limit is reserved for directors and officers. The company cannot claim under it, and claims against the company that use up the underlying D&O insurance do not reduce it.

For cover of loss the company does not indemnify, this policy follows the terms of the underlying D&O insurance, except where this policy says otherwise. It does not drop down to fill gaps in the underlying D&O insurance unless the schedule shows that [[ref:sa.end.e04]] applies.

This policy, together with your schedule and any endorsements, forms your contract of insurance with us. Words shown in bold have special meanings, set out under [[ref:sa.section.definitions]].

Before this policy was entered into, you were given written notice, as required by section 40(1) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), of the effect of section 40(3) (see [[ref:sa.obl.notify-circumstances]]).
