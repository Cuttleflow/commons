id: sa.obl.notify-circumstances
type: obligation
heading: Notify Us of Circumstances
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us during the policy period if you become aware of any fact, matter, or circumstance that might reasonably be expected to give rise to a claim. Notified circumstances will be treated as a claim first made during this policy period, in accordance with section 40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).
