If the limit of liability is or may be insufficient to cover all insured persons' losses, we pay defence costs as they are incurred, and we pay other loss in the order in which claims are finally agreed or determined.

If we pay loss in good faith in that order, we are discharged to the extent of each payment, even if a later claim then cannot be paid in full because the limit of liability has been used up.
