When we make a payment under this policy, we take over the insured person's rights of recovery for that loss, including any right to be indemnified, or to have defence costs advanced, by the company.

We will not exercise any right of recovery against an insured person, except to recover amounts that must be repaid under [[ref:sa.excl.dishonesty-and-fraud]] or [[ref:sa.excl.personal-profit]] after final adjudication. The insured person must give us reasonable help to exercise these rights, at our cost.

Any amount recovered, after deducting the reasonable costs of recovery, is applied first to repay the insured person for loss they have paid that this policy did not cover because the limit of liability was exhausted, and then to repay us.
