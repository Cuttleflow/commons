This policy treats each insured person as if they have their own separate policy. The conduct, knowledge, or statements of one insured person are not attributed to any other insured person. This applies to every exclusion, condition and obligation in this policy that turns on what an insured person knew, did or failed to do.

The knowledge and conduct of the company, including of a member of the control group, are not attributed to any insured person other than that member.
