heading: No Drop-Down
since: 2.0.0
changed: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

Unless [[ref:sa.end.e04]] applies, this policy does not pay any loss that the underlying D&O insurance does not cover, and does not pay below the attachment point for any reason, including the insolvency of the underlying insurer or its refusal or failure to pay. An amount the underlying insurer does not pay does not reduce the attachment point.
