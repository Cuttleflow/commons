Once this policy has attached, we will advance defence costs as they are incurred, without waiting for the outcome of the claim. We advance defence costs in the same way in civil-penalty and criminal proceedings.

The insured person must repay advanced defence costs to the extent that:

(a) it is later established, by Final adjudication or by an allocation under [[ref:sa.pay.allocation]], that they were not covered;

(b) a Final adjudication, or a court's finding, establishes conduct that [[ref:sa.excl.dishonesty-and-fraud]] or [[ref:sa.excl.personal-profit]] excludes, or that the law does not allow to be covered (see [[ref:sa.cond.corporations-act-199a-199b]]); or

(c) the company later indemnifies the insured person for the same loss (including from funds recovered in an insolvency, liquidation, administration, receivership, or deed of company arrangement), to the extent of the indemnity received, so that there is no double recovery.
