heading: Side A Excess Cover
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

If a claim is first made against an insured person during the policy period for a wrongful act committed on or after the retroactive date, we will pay the insured person's non-indemnifiable loss in excess of the attachment point, up to the limit of liability of this policy.
