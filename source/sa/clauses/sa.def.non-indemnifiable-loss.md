heading: Non-indemnifiable loss
since: 2.0.0
changed: 2.1.0
status: draft
---

A loss that the company: (a) is not legally permitted to indemnify (for example, where indemnification is prohibited by section 199A of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth); see [[ref:sa.cond.corporations-act-199a-199b]]); (b) is financially unable to indemnify (because of insolvency, liquidation, administration, receivership, or financial incapacity); or (c) refuses or fails to indemnify (despite being legally permitted and financially able to do so).

See also [[ref:sa.cover.presumptive-indemnification]], which governs how this definition is applied where the company's ability or willingness to indemnify is uncertain or contested.
