This policy attaches only when the underlying D&O insurance has been exhausted by payment of loss. Payments count towards exhaustion whether they were made: (a) under any insuring clause of the underlying D&O insurance, including cover for the company's own liability and reimbursement of the company; (b) by the underlying insurer; or (c) by or on behalf of an insured person or the company, in place of the underlying insurer, in settlement of loss covered by the underlying D&O insurance. [WP REVIEW: confirm whether payments under (c), for example on a settlement with the underlying insurer below its limit, should count towards exhaustion, and whether we should require written notice of them]

The attachment point is fixed at the start of the policy period. It is not reduced because the underlying D&O insurance has been partly exhausted by payment: this policy attaches only when the whole of the attachment point has been exhausted.
