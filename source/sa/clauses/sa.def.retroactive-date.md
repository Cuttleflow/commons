---
id: sa.def.retroactive-date
type: definition
heading: Retroactive date
since: 2.0.0
status: draft
---

means the date shown in the schedule. This policy does not cover any claim arising from a wrongful act committed, in whole or in part, before the retroactive date.

Where two or more wrongful acts are treated as a single claim under the definition of [[ref:sa.def.claim]], the retroactive date is applied to that claim by reference to the date of the earliest of those wrongful acts.
