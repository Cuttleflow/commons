Except where this policy states otherwise, this policy follows the terms, conditions, definitions and exclusions of the followed policy, as they stand at the start of the policy period, that apply to cover for an insured person's loss which the company does not indemnify. Where the underlying D&O insurance has more than one policy, this policy follows only the followed policy (see [[ref:sa.def.followed-policy]]), and not the terms of any other policy in the underlying D&O insurance. Where this policy and the followed policy are inconsistent, this policy applies.

This policy does not follow: (a) any cover for the company's own liability, or for reimbursement of the company; (b) any provision about limits, sub-limits, retentions, excesses, premium, or cancellation; or (c) any change to the followed policy made after the start of the policy period, unless we agree to it in writing.
