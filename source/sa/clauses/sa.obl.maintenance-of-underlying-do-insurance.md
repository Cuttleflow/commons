The company must keep the underlying D&O insurance in force, on the terms and limits shown in the schedule, for the policy period, and tell us promptly if it is cancelled, not renewed, or its limits or terms are reduced.

If the underlying D&O insurance is not maintained, we will pay only what we would have paid had it been maintained, and only to the extent permitted by section 54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) (see [[ref:sa.cond.insurance-contracts-act-1984-cth]]).
