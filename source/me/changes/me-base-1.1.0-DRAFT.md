This is the first Commons release of the Meridian Medical Establishments wording. It replaces version 1.0.0 (July 2026) and the GP Medical Centre, Day Surgery, Private Hospital and Aged Care endorsements version 1.0.0, all published outside the Commons. Changes from version 1.0.0:

- Allied health clinics are a facility category, covered by Endorsement E13 with general practices and medical centres.
- Default sub-limits are now stated in the Sub-limits provision. Version 1.0.0 left every sub-limit to the schedule, so an extension had no limit when the schedule was silent.
- Run-off cover now says what happens if the additional premium for months 13 to 84 is not paid: run-off ends at 12 months.
- The Aged Care endorsement's 14-day notification after a SIRS report is now an obligation subject to s.54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), not a condition precedent.
- The Insurance Contracts Act condition now states that s.54 applies to every obligation, including those in extensions and endorsements.
- Governing law is now the state or territory shown in the schedule, with the principal place of business at the start of the policy period as the fallback. A new Jurisdiction condition sits beside it.
- Sanctions is now a standalone condition naming the Australian sanctions laws, not an exclusion.
- The Multiple Claims Aggregation condition now includes similar or related incidents.
- The Statutory Liability extension now names Victoria and Queensland, as well as New South Wales and Western Australia, as jurisdictions where WHS penalties are uninsurable.
- The four establishment endorsements are now Endorsements E13 (GP Medical Centre and Primary Care), E14 (Day Surgery and Procedure Centre), E15 (Private Hospital) and E16 (Aged Care and Residential Care), with their cover unchanged. Their notes to underwriters are not part of the contract and now sit in the platform rules.
- Each endorsement now states when the platform applies, refers, suggests or offers it.
- The licence is now CC BY 4.0, in line with the rest of the Commons.
- Clause numbers are removed. Each clause is identified by its heading.
