Change of 8 November 2026.

- About This Policy: the retail-client paragraph now states the law. Medical indemnity insurance for medical practitioners is a retail product under s.761G(5)(b)(viii) of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), because regulation 7.1.17A of the Corporations Regulations 2001 (Cth) prescribes a medical indemnity insurance product, which regulation 1.0.02(1) defines by reference to medical practitioners under the Medical Indemnity (Prudential Supervision and Product Standards) Act 2003 (Cth). This policy never covers the personal civil liability of a medical practitioner, and liability insurance for a healthcare establishment is not a retail kind of product, so a buyer of this policy is not a retail client for it.
