For the avoidance of doubt, the cover under [[ref:me.cover.inquiry-costs]] includes your defence costs in responding to: a Serious Incident Response Scheme investigation; a request, notice or requirement of the Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission (including audits, assessment contacts and compliance monitoring where legal representation is reasonably necessary); and enforcement or civil-penalty proceedings brought by the Commission. Nothing in this policy prevents, penalises or discourages you from making a report or notification required by the Serious Incident Response Scheme: see [[ref:me.obl.do-not-admit-liability]].
