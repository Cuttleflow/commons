Any of the following, but only while acting for or on behalf of the company and within the scope of their duties for the company:

- (a) any past, present or future director, officer, board or committee member, or trustee of the company;
- (b) any employee;
- (c) any allied health practitioner engaged by the company, whether as an employee or contractor;
- (d) any volunteer;
- (e) any student;
- (f) any locum tenens (subject to [[ref:me.ext.locum-tenens]]); and
- (g) the estate, heirs, legal representatives, or lawful spouse or domestic partner of any of the above (subject to [[ref:me.ext.estates-and-spouses]]).

A medical practitioner, including a visiting medical officer, is never an insured person under this policy in respect of their own civil liability. An allied health practitioner is an insured person only for the capacity described above, and this policy does not replace the insurance that a National Board may require of them for their own practice: see [[ref:me.about]].
