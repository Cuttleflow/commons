assumed by you under any contract, agreement, warranty or guarantee, to the extent the liability is greater than the liability you would have had at law in the absence of the contract, including liability arising because a contract excludes or modifies the operation of proportionate liability legislation. However, this exclusion does not reduce cover for the share of any loss for which you would have been liable at law in the absence of the contract, including your proportionate share as it would have been determined under applicable proportionate liability legislation.
