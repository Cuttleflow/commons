arising from healthcare services performed by any person while under the influence of alcohol or of a drug not prescribed for them (or prescribed but taken contrary to medical direction), but only where the company, through a member of the **control group**, knew of or connived at the person performing healthcare services in that state. The knowledge or conduct of the impaired person is not imputed to the company or to any other innocent insured person, and cover for the innocent company and innocent insured persons is preserved.
