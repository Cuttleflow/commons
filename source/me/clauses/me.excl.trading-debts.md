for: (a) any trading debt or personal debt; (b) the refund, return, remission or offset of fees, charges, subsidies or payments (including Medicare, PBS, NDIS, or aged care subsidies or accommodation payments); (c) liquidated damages or amounts payable only because a contract fixes them; (d) your lost profit, mark-up or overheads; or (e) your liability for any tax.
