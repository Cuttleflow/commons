type: obligation
heading: Tell Us About Material Changes
legacy_number: '5.9'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable about any material change to your healthcare operations during the policy period, for example: commencing a new category of healthcare services (including obstetrics services or cosmetic procedures); opening, acquiring or closing a facility; a merger or acquisition; loss or suspension of an accreditation, approval or licence material to your operations; or the appointment of an administrator or liquidator. We may adjust the premium or terms for the changed risk. Under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), we cannot refuse a claim solely because you failed to tell us about a change, except to the extent we are prejudiced.
