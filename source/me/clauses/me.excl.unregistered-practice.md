id: me.excl.unregistered-practice
type: exclusion
heading: Unregistered or Unlicensed Practice
legacy_number: '3.6'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

arising from healthcare services performed by any person who, at the time of the relevant act, error or omission: was not registered, endorsed, licensed or accredited as required by law for that service; was practising while their registration was suspended, cancelled or lapsed; or was practising in breach of a condition, undertaking or restriction on their registration that is material to the claim, where the company knew, or ought reasonably to have known through its credentialing processes, of the deficiency. This exclusion does not apply to a student acting under supervision in accordance with [[ref:me.ext.students]], or to an insured person whose registration deficiency was concealed from the company despite reasonable credentialing checks.
