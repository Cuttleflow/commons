brought by or on behalf of: (a) the company or any insured person; (b) any entity that controls the company, is controlled by the company, or is under common control with the company; or (c) any person or entity that holds a financial, executive or managerial interest of 20% or more in the company together with the right to appoint or occupy a seat on its board, or any entity in which the company holds such an interest.

However, this exclusion does not apply to: a claim for bodily injury brought by a natural person (including a relative of an insured person) in their capacity as a patient, resident or recipient of healthcare services; or a claim brought by an independent third party for which a related party seeks contribution or indemnity from you.
