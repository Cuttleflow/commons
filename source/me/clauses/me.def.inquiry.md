---
id: me.def.inquiry
type: definition
heading: Inquiry
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Any formal inquiry, investigation, examination, hearing, disciplinary proceeding, performance assessment, panel or tribunal proceeding, audit, compliance monitoring activity, or coronial inquest, conducted by an inquiring body, into your conduct or your healthcare operations, at which your attendance is required or at which you are legally entitled and reasonably required to be represented, including a proceeding, investigation, audit or compliance monitoring activity of the Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission, and any investigation arising from a report under the Serious Incident Response Scheme.
