arising from the provision of, or failure to provide, obstetrics services, unless the schedule or Endorsement E01 states that obstetrics cover is included. This exclusion does not apply to emergency treatment covered by [[ref:me.ext.good-samaritan]] where no obstetric care could reasonably have been referred or transferred in time.
