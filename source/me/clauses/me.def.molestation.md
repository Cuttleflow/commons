heading: Molestation
since: 1.1.0
changed: 1.2.0
status: draft
---

Any actual, alleged or threatened sexual abuse, sexual assault, sexual misconduct, sexual harassment or grooming of any person.

Molestation does not include physical or psychological abuse, neglect or ill-treatment of a patient or resident that is not sexual in nature. The company's liability for that conduct is covered as part of its liability under [[ref:me.cover.healthcare-operations]], subject to the other terms of this policy.
