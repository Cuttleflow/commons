heading: War and Terrorism
legacy_number: '3.20'
since: 1.1.0

arising from: (a) war (whether declared or not), invasion, civil war, rebellion, revolution, or military or usurped power; or (b) any act of terrorism, or any action taken in controlling, preventing or suppressing terrorism. However, paragraph (b) does not apply to your liability arising from the healthcare services you provide to victims of an act of terrorism.
