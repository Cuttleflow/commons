---

Neither you nor we are required to contest any claim, or appeal any judgment, unless senior counsel advises that the proceeding or appeal has reasonable prospects of success, taking into account the interests of both you and us, including damage to your professional reputation and the prospect of an award exceeding any available settlement. The costs of obtaining senior counsel's advice form part of defence costs.
