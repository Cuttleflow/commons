We will pay:

- (a) your defence costs in responding to an audit, investigation or review by Medicare, Services Australia, the Chief Executive Medicare, the Professional Services Review, the Department of Health and Aged Care, or the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission, concerning claiming, billing or payments in connection with your healthcare services; and
- (b) your defence costs where such an audit or investigation involves alleged fraud or dishonesty by an employee, but only for the benefit of the company and any insured person who did not commit, condone, participate in, or wilfully ignore the fraudulent or dishonest conduct.

We do not pay: repayments of benefits, rebates or payments incorrectly claimed or received; fines or penalties (except as provided by [[ref:me.ext.statutory-liability]]); or the defence costs of any person who committed or condoned the fraud or dishonesty (and any defence costs advanced to such a person must be repaid). The sub-limit for this extension applies.
