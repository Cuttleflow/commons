---
id: me.def.healthcare-operations
type: definition
heading: Healthcare Operations
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

The overall conduct and management of the company's business as a healthcare establishment of the categories shown in the schedule, including: the provision of healthcare services; the ownership, occupation, management and operation of each facility; the engagement, credentialing, rostering and supervision of clinical and non-clinical staff; the operation of clinical governance, infection control, triage, referral, recall and patient-management systems; the storage, management and dispensing of medication; the maintenance of medical records; the provision of food, accommodation and personal care incidental to healthcare services; and first aid and emergency response at a facility.
