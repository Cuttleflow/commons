The most we will pay under this policy for all loss and defence costs combined, for all claims and inquiries during the policy period, is the limit of liability shown in the schedule. This is an aggregate limit: it applies to all claims and inquiries combined, regardless of how many are made, how many insured persons are involved, or how many facilities are affected.
