---
id: me.def.final-adjudication
type: definition
heading: Final Adjudication
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

A judgment, decision or ruling of a court, tribunal or arbitrator of competent jurisdiction, after the exhaustion of all appeals or the expiry of the time to appeal without an appeal being filed, that finally determines the relevant conduct. A consent order, settlement, compromise or admission of fact that does not include a determination of the relevant conduct is not a final adjudication.
