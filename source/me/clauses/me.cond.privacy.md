We collect personal information (including, where necessary for claims, health information) to assess applications, administer the policy and handle claims, in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. We may disclose it to reinsurers, claims professionals, lawyers and regulators as reasonably required, some of whom may be overseas. Our privacy policy, including how to access or correct information and how to complain, is available on request.
