heading: Estates, Heirs and Spouses

If an insured person dies, becomes legally incapacitated or becomes insolvent, we will cover their estate, heirs or legal representatives for a claim arising from the insured person's conduct that would have been covered under this policy. We will also cover the lawful spouse or domestic partner of an insured person for a claim made against them solely because of their spousal relationship or their joint ownership of property, but not for any act, error or omission of the spouse or partner themselves.
