We will pay your defence costs in defending a prosecution, civil-penalty proceeding or formal enforcement proceeding brought against the company or an insured person during the policy period under any Australian statute in connection with your healthcare operations, including proceedings under work health and safety legislation, privacy legislation, the Aged Care Act (including civil-penalty proceedings brought by the Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission), and food safety, radiation safety, and drugs and poisons legislation.

We will also pay fines and civil pecuniary penalties imposed on the company or an insured person in such a proceeding, but only to the extent permitted by law. In particular:

- (a) the law in many jurisdictions, including New South Wales, Victoria, Queensland and Western Australia, prohibits insurance for monetary penalties under work health and safety legislation; we will never pay a WHS monetary penalty where the law prohibits it, and in those jurisdictions this extension provides defence costs only;
- (b) penalties are not covered where the conduct was deliberate, dishonest, fraudulent or reckless, or where payment would be against public policy or otherwise uninsurable at law; and
- (c) certain penalties, including penalties at the quantum available under the Aged Care Act and privacy legislation, may be uninsurable at law in whole or in part; we pay only what the law permits.

The sub-limit for this extension applies. This extension does not cover medical practitioners.
