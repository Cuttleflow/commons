heading: Cooperate With Us
legacy_number: '5.4'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Give us all information, documents and assistance we reasonably request to investigate, defend or settle a claim or respond to an inquiry. Attend meetings, conferences, examinations and proceedings we reasonably require. Nothing in this obligation requires you to waive legal professional privilege otherwise than to us, or to breach any law, including privacy legislation.
