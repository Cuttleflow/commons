arising from: the death, bodily injury, illness or disease of any employee or other worker arising out of or in the course of their employment or engagement by you; any liability under workers compensation, accident compensation or industrial legislation; or any employment-related claim, including wrongful dismissal, discrimination, harassment, bullying, underpayment or breach of employment law. These exposures are designed to be covered under workers compensation and employment practices liability insurance.
