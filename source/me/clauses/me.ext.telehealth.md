---
id: me.ext.telehealth
type: extension
heading: Telehealth and Remote Care
legacy_number: 2.5.22
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

For the avoidance of doubt, healthcare services include services provided by telehealth and remote patient monitoring, wherever the patient is located within the geographical limits, provided the service is lawful both where it is provided from and where the patient is located. This extension does not cover claims made in, or governed by the law of, the United States of America or Canada, or services provided to patients located there.
