legacy_number: '6.9'
since: 1.1.0

Tell us the extent of any input tax credit you are entitled to claim for the premium. If you do not, we will assume you are entitled to a full input tax credit. When we pay a claim, we will reduce the payment by any input tax credit you are entitled to claim for the repair, replacement or supply concerned, in accordance with A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth).
