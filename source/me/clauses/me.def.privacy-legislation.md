id: me.def.privacy-legislation
type: definition
heading: Privacy Legislation
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

The Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), including the Australian Privacy Principles, Part IIIC (Notification of Eligible Data Breaches) and Schedule 2 (the statutory tort for serious invasions of privacy); the My Health Records Act 2012 (Cth); the Health Records and Information Privacy Act 2002 (NSW); the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic); the Health Records (Privacy and Access) Act 1997 (ACT); and any other Commonwealth, state or territory legislation governing the privacy, confidentiality or handling of personal or health information, each as amended or replaced from time to time.
