Do not admit liability for, make an offer or promise to pay, or settle any claim, or waive any right of recovery, without our prior written consent. We will not unreasonably withhold or delay consent.

This obligation never prevents, and is not breached by, any of the following:

- (a) an apology or expression of regret, sorrow or sympathy made in accordance with applicable civil liability legislation (including s.69 of the Civil Liability Act 2002 (NSW), Part IIC of the Wrongs Act 1958 (Vic), ss.71 and 72 of the Civil Liability Act 2003 (Qld), and their equivalents), which by law does not constitute an admission of liability;
- (b) open disclosure communications;
- (c) a mandatory notification to Ahpra or a National Board under the mandatory notification provisions of the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law, or any other notification a registered practitioner or the company is legally required to make;
- (d) a report of a reportable incident under the Serious Incident Response Scheme, or any other report or notification required by the Aged Care Act or the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (Cth);
- (e) a data breach assessment, statement or notification required by Part IIIC of the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth); and
- (f) any other disclosure, report or notification you are required by law to make.

Making a legally required disclosure, or communicating with patients and families in accordance with open disclosure, is not an admission of liability for the purposes of this policy and will never of itself prejudice your cover. Where practicable, tell us before or promptly after making such a disclosure so we can help you manage it.
