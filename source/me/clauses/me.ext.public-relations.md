legacy_number: 2.5.10
since: 1.1.0

Following a claim or inquiry covered by this policy, or an incident notified under [[ref:me.cover.notification-of-circumstances]] that has attracted or is reasonably likely to attract adverse publicity, we will pay the reasonable costs of engaging a public relations or crisis management consultant, with our prior written consent, to protect or restore the reputation of the company. The sub-limit for this extension applies.
