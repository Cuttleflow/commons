This policy insures healthcare establishments (general practices and medical centres, allied health clinics, day surgeries and procedure centres, private hospitals, and aged care and community health facilities) for the civil liability of the establishment arising from the provision of healthcare services.

It covers:

- the company's own civil liability for bodily injury or property damage arising from its healthcare operations;
- the company's vicarious liability for the acts, errors and omissions of its employees, allied health practitioners, volunteers, students and locum tenens;
- the company's vicarious liability for the acts, errors and omissions of medical practitioners, including visiting medical officers, practising at or for the company;
- premises liability while healthcare services are being provided;
- administrative and system errors: care-plan failures, medication management and dispensing errors, inadequate supervision, and triage and referral failures; and
- the costs of responding to inquiries, investigations, and disciplinary and regulatory proceedings.

**Medical practitioners.** This policy never covers the personal civil liability of a medical practitioner. We will not pay the civil liability of a medical practitioner for any act, error or omission in the provision of healthcare services in their capacity as a medical practitioner. Every medical practitioner practising at or for the company must hold their own medical indemnity insurance that complies with the Medical Indemnity (Prudential Supervision and Product Standards) Act 2003 (Cth). What this policy does cover is the company's own liability, including its vicarious liability and its non-delegable duty of care, arising from a medical practitioner's conduct. See [[ref:me.cover.healthcare-operations]] and [[ref:me.cover.vicarious-liability]].

**Allied health practitioners.** This policy covers an allied health practitioner only while acting for or on behalf of the company. It is not written to be, and does not claim to be, the professional indemnity insurance that the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law and a National Board's registration standard require of a registered practitioner for their own practice. It has no unlimited retroactive cover, and its run-off cover belongs to the company, not to the practitioner. Each practitioner should check with their National Board whether this policy satisfies the standard that applies to them. **Claims made and notified.** This policy covers only claims first made against you during the policy period and notified to us during the policy period. It does not cover claims first made after the policy period ends, even if the treatment or incident occurred during the policy period, subject to your statutory rights under s.40(3) of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), which [[ref:me.cover.notification-of-circumstances]] explains. Before you enter into this contract, your insurer or its distributor must give you written notice of the effect of s.40(3), as s.40(1) of that Act requires.

This policy, together with your schedule and any endorsements, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read all documents together and tell us if anything is incorrect. We enter into this contract, and each of us must act towards the other, with the utmost good faith, as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) requires. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:me.section.definitions]].

**Retail clients.** Under s.761G(5) of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), a general insurance product provided to an individual, or for use in connection with a small business, is provided to a retail client only if it is one of the kinds of product listed in s.761G(5)(b). Liability insurance for a healthcare establishment is not among them. Medical indemnity insurance for medical practitioners is one of them: regulation 7.1.17A of the Corporations Regulations 2001 (Cth) prescribes a medical indemnity insurance product for s.761G(5)(b)(viii). Regulation 1.0.02(1) of those Regulations defines a medical indemnity insurance product as an arrangement to which the Medical Indemnity (Prudential Supervision and Product Standards) Act 2003 (Cth) applies, under which medical indemnity cover is provided to a medical practitioner as defined in s.4 of that Act (an individual registered or licensed as a medical practitioner under a State or Territory law), or to a registered health professional prescribed by regulations made under that Act. So medical indemnity insurance for a medical practitioner is a retail product when it is provided to an individual or for use in connection with a small business. This policy never covers the personal civil liability of a medical practitioner (see [[ref:me.def.insured-person]] and [[ref:me.cover.vicarious-liability]]). It insures the company's own liability, and an allied health practitioner only while acting for the company, so a buyer of this policy is not a retail client for it, and this wording is not a Product Disclosure Statement.
