---
id: me.def.medical-practitioner
type: definition
heading: Medical Practitioner
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

A natural person registered (including with limited, provisional or specialist registration) in the medical profession under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law with the Medical Board of Australia, or a person who is required to be so registered to practise medicine lawfully. A medical practitioner is not an allied health practitioner, and a dental practitioner is not a medical practitioner for the purposes of this policy.
