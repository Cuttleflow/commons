heading: Prior Known Circumstances and Retroactive Date
legacy_number: '3.11'
since: 1.1.0
changed: 1.2.0
status: draft
---

arising from: (a) any fact, matter, circumstance or incident that you knew about, or ought reasonably to have known about, before the start of the policy period (or before we first agreed to insure you on continuous terms, if earlier) and that you knew or ought reasonably to have known might give rise to a claim or inquiry; (b) any claim, inquiry or circumstance notified, or that ought to have been notified, under any earlier policy (subject to [[ref:me.ext.continuous-cover]]); or (c) any act, error, omission or incident occurring before the retroactive date. Knowledge under paragraph (a) is assessed as [[ref:me.cond.severability]] provides: for the company, by what a member of the **control group** knew or ought reasonably to have known; for an insured person, by what that person knew or ought reasonably to have known.
