Defence costs are included within the limit of liability and reduce the amount available to pay compensation, unless the schedule states that defence costs are payable in addition to the limit of liability, in which case the schedule will show the separate defence costs limit that applies.

We will advance defence costs as they are incurred, before final resolution. If it is finally established that a claim or inquiry, or part of it, is not covered, you must repay the defence costs advanced for the uncovered portion.
