Where the company operates an aged care or community health facility, cover under [[ref:me.cover.healthcare-operations]] extends to claims for statutory compensation under the Aged Care Act, including an application, claim or proceeding for compensation for loss or damage suffered by an individual as a result of a contravention of the statutory duty under the Aged Care Act, and a compensation order sought in, or in connection with, a civil-penalty proceeding. Civil penalties themselves are addressed, and limited, by [[ref:me.ext.statutory-liability]]. A claim alleging molestation remains subject to [[ref:me.excl.molestation]] and [[ref:me.ext.molestation-defence-costs]].
