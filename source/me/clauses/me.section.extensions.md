The following extensions are included automatically. Each is subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions of this policy and to the limit of liability. Where an extension refers to a sub-limit, the sub-limit in [[ref:me.pay.sub-limits]] applies unless the schedule states a different amount. Sub-limits are part of, not in addition to, the limit of liability.
