If you have other insurance that may cover the same loss or defence costs, tell us promptly. Consistent with s.45 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), nothing in this policy excludes or limits your cover by reason only that other insurance exists, and you may claim under this policy or the other policy. If we pay, we may seek contribution from the other insurer in proportion to each policy's liability, and you must give us reasonable assistance to do so.
