type: condition
heading: Multiple Claims Aggregation
legacy_number: '6.12'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

All claims and inquiries arising from, based upon, or attributable to the same act, error, omission or incident, or a series of acts, errors, omissions or incidents that are similar or related, causally connected or interrelated, or that arise from the same clinical, administrative or systemic cause, are treated together as a single claim first made when the earliest of them was first made, and a single limit of liability and a single excess apply.
