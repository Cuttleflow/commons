Where a sub-limit applies, it is the most we will pay for that type of cover for all claims and inquiries combined during the policy period. Sub-limits are included within, not in addition to, the limit of liability, unless the schedule states otherwise. Unless the schedule states a different amount, the sub-limits are:

| Cover | Sub-limit (aggregate) |
|---|---|
| [[ref:me.cover.inquiry-costs|heading]] | $1,000,000 |
| [[ref:me.ext.privacy|heading]] | $250,000 |
| [[ref:me.ext.audits-and-employee-fraud|heading]] | $250,000 |
| [[ref:me.ext.statutory-liability|heading]] | $250,000 |
| [[ref:me.ext.molestation-defence-costs|heading]] | $1,000,000 |
| [[ref:me.ext.loss-of-documents|heading]] | $250,000 |
| [[ref:me.ext.public-relations|heading]] | $100,000 |
| [[ref:me.ext.advance-claim-protection|heading]] | $100,000 |
