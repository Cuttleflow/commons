---
id: me.def.open-disclosure
type: definition
heading: Open Disclosure
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Communication with a patient, resident or client, or their family, carer or support person, about an incident that caused or could have caused harm, conducted in accordance with the Australian Open Disclosure Framework published by the Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care, as updated or replaced from time to time.
