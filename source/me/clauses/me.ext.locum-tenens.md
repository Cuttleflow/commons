heading: Locum Tenens

since: 1.1.0

---

We will cover the company's vicarious liability arising from healthcare services performed by a locum tenens. Where the locum tenens is not a medical practitioner, we will also cover their personal liability as an insured person. Where the locum tenens is a medical practitioner, this extension covers the company's liability only: we will not pay the civil liability of a medical practitioner for any act, error or omission in the provision of healthcare services in their capacity as a medical practitioner.
