1. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the claims contact details shown in the schedule, as soon as reasonably practicable, and always before the policy period ends.
2. Provide written details of the claim, inquiry or circumstance: see [[ref:me.obl.tell-us-about-claims]] and [[ref:me.obl.tell-us-about-circumstances]] for what to include. For urgent matters (coronial notices, SIRS deadlines, regulatory demands), tell us by telephone first; [[ref:me.ext.advance-claim-protection]] lets us appoint a panel lawyer immediately.
3. We will acknowledge your notification, assign a claims manager experienced in healthcare liability, and confirm the next steps.
4. We will work with you to investigate, defend and manage the matter, and keep you informed of progress. Continue your open disclosure and legally required notifications: they do not prejudice your cover (see [[ref:me.obl.do-not-admit-liability]]).
5. Do not admit liability or agree to any settlement without our written consent, subject to the exceptions in [[ref:me.obl.do-not-admit-liability]]. We will not unreasonably withhold or delay consent.
