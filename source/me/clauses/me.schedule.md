(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- The named insured, ABN or ACN, and any subsidiary
- The facilities covered, by category: general practice or medical centre; allied health clinic; day surgery or procedure centre; private hospital; aged care or community health facility
- The healthcare services covered, being the establishment's operations as described
- The policy period, start and end dates
- The retroactive date
- The limit of liability, the aggregate for all claims and defence costs combined, and whether defence costs are inclusive (the default) or in addition
- Sub-limits that differ from the defaults in [[ref:me.pay.sub-limits]]
- The excess, the amount you pay first on each claim
- The geographical limits, if different from the default in [[ref:me.def.geographical-limits]]
- The declared gross revenue used to rate the policy
- The premium, including stamp duty and GST
- The state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:me.cond.governing-law]])
- The claims and complaints contact details of the insurer
- The endorsements that change the standard terms, by code, with their variables
