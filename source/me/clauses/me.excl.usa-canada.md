heading: United States of America and Canada
legacy_number: '3.24'
since: 1.1.0

---

made, brought or maintained in the United States of America or Canada or their territories or protectorates; arising from healthcare services provided in, or to a patient located in, the United States of America or Canada; or based on a judgment, order or settlement of a court of the United States of America or Canada, including proceedings to enforce such a judgment elsewhere. This exclusion is absolute and cannot be varied except by express endorsement.
