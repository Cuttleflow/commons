---
id: me.excl.cosmetic-procedures
type: exclusion
heading: Cosmetic and Aesthetic Procedures
legacy_number: '3.5'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

arising from any procedure, treatment or service performed wholly or predominantly to alter or enhance appearance rather than to treat illness, injury or disease, including cosmetic injectables (such as botulinum toxin and dermal fillers), liposuction, breast or other augmentation, cosmetic surgery, laser and light-based aesthetic treatments, and hair transplantation. This exclusion does not apply to reconstructive procedures performed to address the consequences of illness, injury, congenital condition or medical treatment.
