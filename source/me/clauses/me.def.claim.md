heading: Claim
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Any of the following:

- (a) any written demand for compensation made against you;
- (b) any writ, summons, statement of claim, cross-claim, counter-claim, third-party notice, arbitration, or other civil legal proceeding issued against you seeking compensation;
- (c) any formal complaint against you to, or any proceeding against you before, an inquiring body that could result in a finding of civil liability against you or an order that you pay compensation;
- (d) any notification, complaint, referral, assessment, panel hearing, performance assessment, or tribunal proceeding concerning you under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law;
- (e) any application, claim or proceeding seeking compensation from you under a statute, including an application for statutory compensation, or for a compensation order, under the Aged Care Act; and
- (f) any proceeding brought against you by the Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission that includes a claim for compensation on behalf of, or for the benefit of, an individual.

[[ref:me.cond.aggregation]] states when two or more claims are treated as one.
