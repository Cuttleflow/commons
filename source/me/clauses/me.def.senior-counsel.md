heading: Senior Counsel
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

A barrister holding the rank of Senior Counsel or King's Counsel, practising in the state or territory whose law governs this policy, agreed between you and us or, if we cannot agree within 21 days, nominated at the request of either of us by the President of the Law Society (or, if the President declines, the President of the Bar Association) of that state or territory.
