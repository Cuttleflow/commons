If a claim is first made against you during the policy period and notified to us during the policy period, and the claim arises from your healthcare operations conducted on or after the retroactive date within the geographical limits, we will pay the loss you become legally liable to pay, and your defence costs, for any actual or alleged:

- (i) death, bodily injury, mental injury, illness or disease of any person arising from the provision of, or failure to provide, healthcare services;
- (ii) physical loss of or damage to tangible property (including resulting loss of use) arising from your healthcare operations;
- (iii) breach of a duty of care, including a non-delegable duty of care, owed by the company in connection with healthcare services;
- (iv) administrative or system error in the conduct of healthcare operations, including failures of care planning, clinical handover, triage, referral, recall, test-result follow-up, patient identification, credentialing, rostering or supervision, and errors in the storage, management, prescribing systems or dispensing of medication;
- (v) liability of the company as occupier of a facility for bodily injury or property damage occurring while healthcare services are being provided or in connection with the conduct of healthcare operations;
- (vi) breach of privacy or confidentiality in connection with healthcare services (see also [[ref:me.ext.privacy]] and [[ref:me.ext.serious-invasion-of-privacy]]);
- (vii) unintentional defamation committed in the course of providing healthcare services; and
- (viii) any other civil liability arising from your healthcare operations,

unless excluded under [[ref:me.section.exclusions]].
