made against a director, officer or committee member of the company for a wrongful act in their capacity as a director or officer, that is in the management or governance of the company as distinct from the conduct of healthcare operations, or arising from the company's or an insured person's capacity as trustee of a superannuation fund.

However, this exclusion does not apply to defence costs covered under [[ref:me.cover.inquiry-costs]] or [[ref:me.ext.statutory-liability]] for an inquiry or statutory proceeding arising from your healthcare operations, including a proceeding against a person who is a responsible person of the company within the meaning of the Aged Care Act, to the extent those costs relate to the healthcare operations rather than to the person's management of the company's corporate affairs. Broader directors' and officers' cover is designed to be provided under a management liability policy.
