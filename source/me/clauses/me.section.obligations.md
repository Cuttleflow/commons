since: 1.1.0

Each obligation in this section is a term of the contract, not a condition precedent to our liability. If you do not comply with an obligation, we may reduce our liability for the relevant claim or inquiry by the amount that fairly represents the prejudice we suffer as a result, and we may refuse the claim only where the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), including s.54, permits.
