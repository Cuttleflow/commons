---
id: me.def.locum-tenens
type: definition
heading: Locum Tenens
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

A natural person engaged by the company on a temporary basis to perform the duties of an absent employee or allied health practitioner, or a medical practitioner engaged on a temporary basis; in the case of a medical practitioner, only for the purposes of the company's vicarious liability under [[ref:me.ext.locum-tenens]] and never for the practitioner's own liability.
