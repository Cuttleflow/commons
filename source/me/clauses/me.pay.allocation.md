If a claim or inquiry involves both covered and uncovered matters, or covered and uncovered parties (for example, a claim brought against both the company and a medical practitioner; the company's liability for the practitioner's conduct is not allocated away, see [[ref:me.cover.vicarious-liability]]), you and we will use our best endeavours to agree a fair and reasonable allocation of loss and defence costs between the covered and uncovered portions, taking into account the relative legal and financial exposures. If we cannot agree within 30 days, the allocation will be determined by senior counsel, whose determination is binding on both of us and whose costs form part of defence costs. Pending agreement or determination, we will advance the defence costs we consider fair and reasonable.
