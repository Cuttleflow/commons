If during the policy period the company permanently ceases its healthcare operations, or is merged into or acquired by another entity, this policy automatically continues to cover claims and inquiries first made against you and notified to us during the 84 months following the cessation, merger or acquisition, but only for healthcare operations conducted before the cessation, merger or acquisition and on or after the retroactive date. All terms, conditions, exclusions and the remaining limit of liability continue to apply. No additional premium is payable for the first 12 months. For the balance of the run-off period, the additional premium is the amount shown in the schedule or, if none is shown, 200% of the annual premium for the last policy period. We will notify you of the amount before the first 12 months end. If you do not pay it within 30 days of our notice, run-off cover ends at the end of the first 12 months.
