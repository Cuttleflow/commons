We have the right, and at your request the duty, to take over and conduct in your name the investigation, defence and settlement of any claim, and to appoint lawyers to represent you at an inquiry. We will:

- (a) consult you and keep you reasonably informed;
- (b) take account of the professional reputation and clinical accreditation interests of the company and insured persons in conducting the defence; and
- (c) if an actual conflict of interest arises between you and us, or between insured persons we are defending, notify you and arrange separate legal representation for the conflicted parties at our expense (as part of defence costs), with lawyers reasonably acceptable to both of us. Common facts alone, or a mere reservation of rights, do not of themselves create a conflict.
