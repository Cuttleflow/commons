---
id: me.def.subsidiary
type: definition
heading: Subsidiary
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Any entity in which the named insured, directly or indirectly, holds more than 50% of the voting rights, controls the composition of the board, or holds more than half the issued share capital, at the time the relevant act, error or omission occurs; and any entity covered by [[ref:me.ext.new-subsidiaries-and-facilities]].
