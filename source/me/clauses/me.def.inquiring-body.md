id: me.def.inquiring-body
type: definition
heading: Inquiring Body
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Any of the following, acting under statutory or other lawful authority:

- (a) a coroner or coronial court;
- (b) the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (Ahpra), any National Board established under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law, and any committee, panel or delegate of either;
- (c) the Health Care Complaints Commission (NSW), the Office of the Health Ombudsman (Qld), and any equivalent health complaints entity of any state or territory;
- (d) the Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission, including in the exercise of its complaints, compliance, monitoring, audit, enforcement and Serious Incident Response Scheme (SIRS) functions;
- (e) any authority investigating a reportable incident under the Serious Incident Response Scheme, under the Aged Care Act or the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (Cth);
- (f) Medicare, Services Australia, the Chief Executive Medicare, the Professional Services Review, and the Department of Health and Aged Care, in relation to Medicare, PBS, or related benefit and payment schemes;
- (g) the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission;
- (h) the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner and any state or territory privacy or health records regulator;
- (i) a Royal Commission, board of inquiry or parliamentary committee;
- (j) a work health and safety regulator; and
- (k) any other statutory regulator, ombudsman, tribunal or disciplinary body with jurisdiction over you or your healthcare operations.
