---
id: me.excl.prescription-medicines
type: exclusion
heading: Prescription-only Medicines
legacy_number: '3.7'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

arising from the supply, dispensing or administration of any medication that may lawfully be supplied only on the prescription of an authorised prescriber, where no such prescription (or lawful standing order, or lawful emergency authority) existed.
