heading: Obstetrics Services
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Any of the following:

- (a) the management or care of a patient from the onset of labour, including the onset of contractions, rupture of membranes, or any other physical symptom of labour;
- (b) the management or care of a patient during the 48-hour period immediately preceding the actual childbirth;
- (c) the delivery of a baby, by any means, including assisted delivery and caesarean section; and
- (d) the management or care of the mother or baby during the 24-hour period immediately following childbirth, to the extent the care relates to the childbirth.

Obstetrics services do not include: antenatal care provided before the periods described in paragraphs (a) and (b); family planning, fertility or contraceptive advice; routine postnatal care provided after the period in paragraph (d); or emergency treatment covered by [[ref:me.ext.good-samaritan]].
