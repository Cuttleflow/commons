The Civil Liability Act 2002 (NSW), Wrongs Act 1958 (Vic), Civil Liability Act 2003 (Qld), Civil Liability Act 1936 (SA), Civil Liability Act 2002 (WA), Civil Liability Act 2002 (Tas), Civil Law (Wrongs) Act 2002 (ACT), Personal Injuries (Liabilities and Damages) Act 2003 (NT), and any equivalent or successor legislation.
