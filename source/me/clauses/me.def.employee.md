---
id: me.def.employee
type: definition
heading: Employee
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Any natural person who works for the company under a contract of employment or apprenticeship (permanent, fixed-term or casual), including nurses, allied health practitioners, care workers, technicians, administrative and reception staff, orderlies and maintenance staff employed by the company. Employee does not include a medical practitioner engaged other than under a contract of employment, or an independent contractor (but see the definitions of insured person, locum tenens and visiting medical officer).
