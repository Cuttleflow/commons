heading: Paying Out the Limit
legacy_number: '4.7'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

At any time we may pay you the limit of liability (or what remains of it after earlier payments), less any applicable excess, and our obligations to defend or make further payments will end. Before doing so, we will give you reasonable written notice and the opportunity to take over the conduct of any defence at your own cost.
