heading: Preserve Our Recovery Rights
legacy_number: '5.11'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Do not do anything after a claim is made, or after you become aware of a circumstance, that might prejudice our right to recover from any third party, including a medical practitioner or their insurer. If we pay, we may exercise your rights of recovery in your name; you must give us reasonable assistance. We will not exercise recovery rights against an insured person unless [[ref:me.excl.intoxicants]], the fraud carve-outs in [[ref:me.ext.audits-and-employee-fraud]], or paragraph (d) of [[ref:me.ext.molestation-defence-costs]] applies to that person. Any recovery (less our costs of recovery) will be applied first to reduce your uninsured loss, then ours.
