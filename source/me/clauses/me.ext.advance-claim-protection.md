heading: Advance Claim Protection
legacy_number: 2.5.12
since: 1.1.0

---

If you notify us of a claim or inquiry and urgent legal representation is required before we have confirmed coverage, we will appoint and pay a lawyer from our panel to protect your position immediately, including responding to procedural deadlines, coronial notices, regulatory information demands and SIRS timeframes, pending our coverage decision. If we subsequently and properly decline cover, you must repay the amounts we advanced for the matters declined; if we confirm cover, the amounts form part of your defence costs. Payments under this extension are capped at its sub-limit.
