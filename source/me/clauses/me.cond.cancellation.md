You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us in writing. We will refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges, unless a claim or circumstance has been notified, in which case we may retain a reasonable portion reflecting the notified exposure.

We may cancel this policy only on the grounds permitted by s.60 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), for example fraudulent claims, breach of the duty of utmost good faith, non-disclosure, or non-payment of premium, and only by giving you written notice in accordance with s.59. If we cancel, we will refund the premium for the unexpired portion on a pro-rata basis.
