---
id: me.def.documents
type: definition
heading: Documents
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Deeds, wills, agreements, plans, records, medical records, care plans, medication charts, books, letters, certificates, forms and documents of any nature, whether written, printed or reproduced by any method, including electronic records, but not bearer bonds, coupons, bank notes, currency or negotiable instruments.
