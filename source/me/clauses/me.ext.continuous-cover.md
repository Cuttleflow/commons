heading: Continuous Cover

If a claim is first made against you during the policy period and:

- (a) it arises from a fact, matter or circumstance you first became aware of during an earlier policy period, and should have notified under an earlier medical malpractice or civil liability policy issued by us;
- (b) you did not notify it earlier through error or oversight and not through fraud;
- (c) you have held continuous equivalent cover with us from the date you first became aware of the fact, matter or circumstance until this policy period; and
- (d) the claim would have been covered under the earlier policy had it been notified,

then we will cover the claim under this policy, on the terms, conditions, exclusions and limits of the earlier policy or this policy, whichever are narrower and lower, and reduced by any amount that fairly represents the prejudice we have suffered from the late notification.
