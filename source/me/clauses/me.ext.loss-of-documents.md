heading: Loss of Documents
legacy_number: 2.5.9
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If during the policy period you discover that any documents (including patient records) necessary for your healthcare operations have been physically lost, damaged or destroyed while in your possession or in the possession of a person to whom you entrusted them, we will pay the reasonable costs of replacing or restoring them, incurred with our prior written consent, and your civil liability arising from the loss. We do not pay for loss caused by wear, tear, vermin or gradual deterioration, or for electronic data loss arising from a cyber event of the kind excluded by [[ref:me.excl.cyber-events]] except as covered by [[ref:me.ext.privacy]]. The sub-limit for this extension applies.
