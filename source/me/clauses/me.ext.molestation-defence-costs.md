Despite [[ref:me.excl.molestation]], we will pay the defence costs of the company, and of any insured person who is not the alleged perpetrator, in defending a claim alleging molestation committed by an employee in connection with your healthcare operations, provided that:

- (a) no member of the **control group** knew of, condoned or wilfully ignored the alleged conduct or any propensity of the alleged perpetrator;
- (b) cover under this extension is limited to defence costs only: we will not pay any loss, compensation, settlement or damages in respect of molestation;
- (c) the most we will pay under this extension for all claims combined during the policy period is the molestation defence costs sub-limit; and
- (d) if it is established by judgment, adverse admission or other final adjudication that the molestation occurred and was committed, condoned or wilfully ignored by the person for whose benefit defence costs were paid, that person must repay to us all defence costs we advanced on their behalf.

This extension never covers the alleged perpetrator where the allegation is established, and never covers a medical practitioner.
