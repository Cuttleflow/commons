heading: Serious Invasion of Privacy
legacy_number: 2.5.3

We will cover the company's civil liability for loss and defence costs arising from a claim under Schedule 2 of the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) (the statutory cause of action for serious invasions of privacy, inserted by the Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024 (Cth) and in force from 10 June 2025), including the company's vicarious liability for the conduct of an insured person.

We will also cover an insured person (other than a medical practitioner) for such a claim, but only where the invasion of privacy is alleged to have been reckless rather than intentional, and only where the insured person was acting in the course of their duties for the company. We do not cover any person who is found by a court, or who admits, to have intentionally committed a serious invasion of privacy, and if defence costs have been advanced to that person, they must be repaid.
