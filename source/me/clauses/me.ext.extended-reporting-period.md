If we cancel or refuse to renew this policy (other than for non-payment of premium or fraud), or you do not renew it, you may purchase an extended reporting period of 365 days from the end of the policy period, for the additional premium shown in the schedule (or, if none is shown, a premium we set acting reasonably, not exceeding 100% of the expiring annual premium). During the extended reporting period, claims and inquiries first made and notified are covered, but only for healthcare operations conducted before the end of the original policy period. You must elect and pay within 30 days after the policy period ends. The extended reporting period does not increase or reinstate the limit of liability and does not apply if run-off cover under [[ref:me.ext.run-off]] or any replacement insurance responds.
