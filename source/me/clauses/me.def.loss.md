id: me.def.loss
type: definition
heading: Loss
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

The amount you become legally liable to pay as compensation to a third party in respect of a claim, including damages, statutory compensation, compensation ordered under the Aged Care Act, interest, and the claimant's legal costs awarded against you or payable under a settlement we have approved.

Loss does not include: fines or civil or criminal penalties (except to the extent [[ref:me.ext.statutory-liability]] applies); punitive, exemplary, aggravated or multiple damages (except to the extent aggravated damages are insurable at law and awarded as compensation); taxes; the cost of complying with any injunction or order for non-monetary relief; the refund, return or offset of fees or charges; or any amount you are not legally liable to pay or that is uninsurable at law.
