The proposal and all information supplied to us are treated as a separate application by each insured person. No fact known to, statement by, or conduct of one insured person will be imputed to any other insured person, or to the company, except that the knowledge and conduct of a member of the **control group** is the company's knowledge and conduct. Cover for each innocent insured person continues unaffected by another's non-disclosure, misrepresentation or breach.
