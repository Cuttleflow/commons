---
id: me.def.defence-costs
type: definition
heading: Defence Costs
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

The reasonable legal costs, expert fees and disbursements incurred with our prior written consent in investigating, defending, appealing or settling a claim, or in responding to an inquiry. We will not unreasonably withhold or delay consent. We will advance defence costs as they are incurred, before final resolution of the claim or inquiry. Defence costs do not include your internal costs (salaries, wages or overheads), except where [[ref:me.ext.court-attendance]] applies.
