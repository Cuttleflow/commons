An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables shown for it in the schedule. No endorsement is available, and none will be issued, that provides direct cover for the civil liability of a medical practitioner in their capacity as a medical practitioner.

| Code | Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Obstetrics Buy-back | Deletes the Obstetrics exclusion in whole or part for the services described |
| E02 | Defence Costs in Addition | Makes defence costs payable in addition to the limit of liability |
| E03 | Cosmetic Procedures Buy-back | Deletes the Cosmetic and Aesthetic Procedures exclusion for the procedures listed |
| E04 | Clinical Trials | Covers the company's liability for the trials or studies listed |
| E05 | Additional Extended Reporting Period | Extends the extended reporting period beyond 365 days |
| E06 | Increased Molestation Defence Sub-limit | Increases the molestation defence costs sub-limit |
| E07 | Increased Sub-limits | Increases one or more sub-limits |
| E08 | Aggregate Limit Reinstatement | Reinstates the limit of liability once |
| E09 | Additional Facilities | Adds named facilities outside the automatic cover |
| E10 | Contractual Liability Buy-back | Varies the Contractual Liability exclusion for listed contracts |
| E11 | Excess Layer | Applies this wording in excess of underlying insurance |
| E12 | Prior and Pending Litigation Date | Adds a prior and pending litigation exclusion date |
| E13 | GP Medical Centre and Primary Care | Establishment terms for general practices and primary care |
| E14 | Day Surgery and Procedure Centre | Establishment terms for day surgeries and procedure centres |
| E15 | Private Hospital | Establishment terms for private hospitals |
| E16 | Aged Care and Residential Care | Establishment terms for aged care and community care |
