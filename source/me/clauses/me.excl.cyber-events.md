arising from unauthorised access to or interference with your computer systems, ransomware, malware, denial-of-service attacks, or failure of your computer system security, unless, and only to the extent that, the liability arises from a breach of privacy or confidentiality covered by [[ref:me.ext.privacy]] or [[ref:me.ext.serious-invasion-of-privacy]], or from bodily injury caused by a failure of clinical care. Broader cover is designed to be provided under a cyber insurance policy.
