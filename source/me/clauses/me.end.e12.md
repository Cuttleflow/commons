We do not cover any claim or inquiry arising from any litigation, proceeding or inquiry commenced or pending before the prior and pending litigation date shown in the schedule, or from the facts alleged in it. This endorsement is used for run-off or takeover placements.

Schedule variables: prior and pending litigation date.
