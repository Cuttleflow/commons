Tell us in writing during the policy period about any fact, matter or circumstance you first become aware of that might reasonably be expected to give rise to a claim or inquiry. This protects you: see [[ref:me.cover.notification-of-circumstances]]. Examples: a patient or resident has suffered an unexpected adverse outcome; a serious incident has been reported under SIRS; a family has expressed an intention to seek compensation; Ahpra has notified you of a complaint touching your systems or supervision; a coroner has requested records; a regulator has signalled a formal investigation.
