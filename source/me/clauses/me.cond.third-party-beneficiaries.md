heading: Insured Persons as Third-party Beneficiaries
legacy_number: '6.5'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Each insured person who is not a contracting party (including employees, allied health practitioners, volunteers, students, and locum tenens other than medical practitioners) has the benefit of this policy as a third-party beneficiary under s.48 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) and may claim directly under it, subject to its terms. Only the named insured may agree to vary or cancel the policy or an endorsement.
