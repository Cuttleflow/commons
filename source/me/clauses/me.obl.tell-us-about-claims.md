heading: Tell Us About Claims and Inquiries
legacy_number: '5.1'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Tell us in writing as soon as reasonably practicable after you become aware that a claim has been made against you or that you are required to respond to an inquiry. Include: who is making the claim or conducting the inquiry, and what is alleged; when you first became aware; details of the act, error, omission or incident involved; and your estimate of the potential value, if you can. This policy only covers claims and inquiries first made and notified during the policy period, so notify promptly and before the policy period ends.
