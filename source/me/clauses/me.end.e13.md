This endorsement applies where the company operates one or more facilities shown in the schedule in the category general practice or medical centre, including general practices, GP medical centres, GP super clinics, urgent care clinics and other primary care establishments, and allied health clinics. [[ref:me.excl.medical-practitioners]] continues to apply: this policy never covers the personal civil liability of a medical practitioner.

**GP credentialing.** The company must establish and maintain a documented credentialing system for every medical practitioner (employed, contracted or visiting) who provides healthcare services at or for the company, which must include: verification of the practitioner's current registration under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law, including any conditions, undertakings or notations; verification that the practitioner holds current individual medical indemnity insurance that complies with the Medical Indemnity (Prudential Supervision and Product Standards) Act 2003 (Cth) and applicable minimum cover requirements; and an annual re-verification cycle, with records retained for at least seven years. This is an obligation under [[ref:me.section.obligations]].

**Telehealth compliance.** Where the company provides or facilitates telehealth services, those services must be provided in compliance with the Medicare Benefits Schedule telehealth requirements current at the time of the service where the service is billed to Medicare, and the applicable Ahpra or National Board telehealth guidelines for the profession of the person providing the service. This is an obligation under [[ref:me.section.obligations]] and supplements, and does not narrow, [[ref:me.ext.telehealth]].

**Pathology collection.** Cover under [[ref:me.cover.healthcare-operations]] and [[ref:me.cover.vicarious-liability]] extends to the company's vicarious liability, and its liability for breach of a non-delegable duty of care, arising from the acts, errors and omissions of pathology collection staff operating at a facility under a contractor or licence arrangement with a pathology provider, including specimen collection, labelling and handling errors occurring at the facility. This covers the company's liability only. It does not cover the liability of the pathology provider itself; the personal liability of the collection staff (who are not insured persons unless they are employees); or any liability of the requesting or reporting medical practitioner.

**Cosmetic injection clinics.** [[ref:me.excl.cosmetic-procedures]] continues to apply. A cosmetic injection or aesthetic clinic operated as a discrete business unit (identified by separate branding, separate billing, or separate premises or a clearly demarcated section of a facility) is not covered under this endorsement; it must be declared to us separately and cover, if any, arranged under Endorsement E03 or a separate policy. Cosmetic injectables provided incidentally within the general practice, as a minor proportion of the company's revenue, must be declared to us and are covered only where, and on the terms on which, the schedule or Endorsement E03 states they are covered.

**After-hours and locum arrangements.** Consistent with [[ref:me.ext.locum-tenens]], cover extends to the company's vicarious liability arising from healthcare services provided at or for the company by an after-hours, deputising or locum medical practitioner, provided that at the time of the service the practitioner held current registration and current individual medical indemnity insurance, verified through the company's credentialing system. The practitioner's own liability is never covered.

**System and administrative errors.** For the avoidance of doubt, administrative and system error under [[ref:me.cover.healthcare-operations]] includes failures of the company's recall and reminder systems, test-result follow-up systems, triage protocols and referral tracking in the primary care setting.

Schedule variables: facilities in this category.
