heading: Newly Acquired Subsidiaries and Facilities
legacy_number: 2.5.8

---

If during the policy period the company acquires or creates a new subsidiary, or acquires or opens a new healthcare facility, the new entity or facility is automatically covered for 60 days from the date of acquisition or opening, provided that:

- (a) its healthcare activities are the same as or similar to the healthcare services shown in the schedule;
- (b) its annual revenue does not exceed 20% of the company's annual revenue;
- (c) it does not provide obstetrics services, cosmetic procedures, or services in the United States of America or Canada; and
- (d) cover applies only to acts, errors, omissions and incidents occurring after the acquisition or opening.

You must give us written details within 60 days; we may then adjust the premium or terms. If you do not, cover for the new entity or facility ends at the expiry of the 60 days.
