heading: Settlement Opportunities
legacy_number: '5.7'
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If we recommend that a claim be settled for an amount within the limit of liability that the claimant is willing to accept, and senior counsel (instructed under [[ref:me.obl.senior-counsel]] at the request of either of us) advises that the settlement is reasonable, but you do not consent to the settlement, then our total liability for that claim will not exceed the amount for which it could have been settled, plus the defence costs incurred up to the date your consent was requested, less the excess. You may then continue the defence at your own cost and risk.
