This exclusion does not apply to, and nothing in it limits, the company's own civil liability (including vicarious liability and liability for breach of a non-delegable duty of care) arising from the conduct of a medical practitioner, which is covered under [[ref:me.cover.healthcare-operations]] and [[ref:me.cover.vicarious-liability]]. Medical practitioners must hold their own medical indemnity insurance. This policy is not, and must not be represented as, medical indemnity cover for any doctor.
