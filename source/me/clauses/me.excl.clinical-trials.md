arising from any clinical trial, medical research study, or investigational use of any drug, device or procedure; or from any procedure or treatment that is experimental in nature and not approved or recognised by the relevant professional colleges, the Therapeutic Goods Administration, or the Medical Services Advisory Committee. Cover for clinical trials is available under Endorsement E04.
