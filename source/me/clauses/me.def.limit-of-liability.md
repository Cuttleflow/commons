---
id: me.def.limit-of-liability
type: definition
heading: Limit of Liability
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

The maximum aggregate amount shown in the schedule that we will pay under this policy for all loss and defence costs combined for all claims and inquiries during the policy period, unless a specific sub-limit applies. Defence costs are included within, and erode, the limit of liability unless the schedule states that they are payable in addition.
