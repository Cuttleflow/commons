Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026.

- Damage: now means physical loss of, or physical damage to, property. The words "sudden and accidental" are removed, so that the Tenant Damage section, which covers malicious and intentional damage and theft by a tenant, no longer contradicts the definition. Accidental Damage by a Tenant and E01 Accidental Damage continue to state their own accidental test.
- Important Information, Your duty when you apply: the policy is sold to individuals as a consumer insurance contract, and the duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation applies. If the insured is a company, trust or other entity, the duty of disclosure applies instead. The wording on reducing a claim, cancelling and avoiding for fraud now follows the Insurance Contracts Act remedies. A tag asks the Wording Partner to confirm which duty applies.
- Important Information, Terms that differ from standard cover: the list now includes the Other Property Exclusions, Deliberate Acts, Illegal Use, Electronic Data, Terrorism, War, Nuclear, the age deduction on contents more than ten years old, the pair or set rule and matching materials. The short-stay item is restated. A tag asks the Wording Partner to confirm whether these sections are prescribed contracts and which terms the Regulations permit.
- Important Information and Licence and Trademark Notice: a new statement says this wording is for retail clients, that the issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available, and that the wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement. The licence notice now says the same thing, so the two no longer differ.
- Short-Stay Letting (definition and exclusion): the exclusion now applies only to loss, damage or liability that short-stay letting caused or contributed to. A weekend booking of a spare room no longer removes cover for a fire a guest did not start. The definition no longer overlaps the definition of Residential Tenancy Agreement.
- Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): replaced with a general condition that states the Act prevails and applies section 54 to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation, including the loss of rent, tenant default, inspection and tenant damage conditions, not only to the claims conditions.
- Cancellation: you may cancel at any time and we refund the unexpired premium pro rata, with no refund for a building or item after we pay a total loss of it. The cooling-off wording is corrected. Our notice to cancel is at least 30 days, or any longer notice the Insurance Contracts Act requires. A tag asks the Wording Partner to confirm the notice provisions.
- Sanctions: restated as a standalone condition naming the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011 (Cth), the Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 (Cth), and the sanctions laws of the United Kingdom, the United States of America and the European Union, to the extent they apply to us.
- Other Insurance: restated so that we do not refuse or reduce a payment because of other insurance, and may seek contribution.
- Loss of Rent: the section now says that the rent cover limit applies only to Tenant Default and Death of a Sole Tenant. Loss of Rent After Damage and Prevention of Access are limited by their own periods and by the weekly rent limit.
