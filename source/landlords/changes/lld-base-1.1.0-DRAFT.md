This release moves the Meridian Landlords wording v1.0.0 (2026/PR/0003, July 2026) to the Commons, one file per clause, and remediates it. It is a minor release: the structure is kept, the cover is raised to the market standard, and the defects a legal review would raise are fixed.

- **Loss of rent raised.** Tenant default and a tenant leaving without notice are covered for up to 20 weeks (previously 15 and 6 weeks). Where the tribunal orders possession and the tenant does not leave, rent is paid until they leave, for up to 52 weeks in total. Death of a sole tenant is covered for up to 15 weeks (previously 8). A rent cover limit (default $30,000) and a weekly rent limit (default $1,500) are added, with a tenant default excess (default one week's rent).
- **Tenant damage widened.** Accidental damage by a tenant is now covered, alongside malicious and intentional damage, each to a default $70,000 for one tenancy. Drug contamination is covered to a default $30,000 in the policy period, with up to 12 weeks' rent while it is cleaned. Theft by a tenant moves from the property sections to the tenant damage section so that one excess and one limit apply to it.
- **Flood covered as standard** under the statutory definition. The v1.0.0 flood buy-back endorsement (E02) is withdrawn. A 72-hour exclusion for storm and flood applies only when a policy first starts, with exceptions for continuous cover and a newly bought property.
- **Vetting made objective.** The v1.0.0 exclusion for "rent owed by a Tenant the Insured did not properly vet" is replaced by a condition listing the checks (a signed agreement, identity and one of a reference, a tenancy database or proof of income, and a lodged bond), with the Insurance Contracts Act s.54 applying expressly.
- **Statutory posture explicit.** Important Information now sets out the duty that applies, the terms that differ from standard cover, a Key Facts Sheet note and a 21-day cooling-off period. A general Insurance Contracts Act condition applies s.54, and a joint owners condition protects an innocent co-owner. A standalone sanctions condition, fixed governing law and a jurisdiction clause are added.
- **Unoccupancy** now restricts cover after 60 days (previously 90), matching the market and the Home and Contents matrix; Endorsement E09 extends it.
- **Benefits kept.** Legal costs for tenant disputes ($10,000), tax audit costs ($5,000), body corporate excess ($5,000), tenant's emergency accommodation ($3,000), reletting costs ($2,500) and loan repayments while the property is untenantable are kept, with machinery added to each. Removing abandoned goods ($2,500) is added.
- **Endorsement library re-cut to 13**, each with operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger. Kept: accidental damage (E01), pet damage (E03), short-stay letting (E04), agreed value (E07), building works (E08), unoccupied property (E09), heritage (E10). New: higher weekly rent (E02), rooming house or student accommodation (E05), specialist disability or supported accommodation (E06), interested party (E11), Lloyd's underwriters (E12) and self-managed property (E13). Withdrawn: flood buy-back, increased rent default period (now standard), multiple properties discount (a pricing matter, not a policy term), commercial tenancy and government or corporate tenancy (outside a residential landlord wording).
- **Three WP REVIEW items** for the Wording Partner: which duty applies to an individual landlord (s.11AB); whether the Building and Landlord's Contents sections are prescribed contracts, so that standard cover, s.35 notice and a Key Facts Sheet apply; and terrorism cover eligibility.
