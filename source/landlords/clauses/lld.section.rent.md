This section applies to every policy. The schedule shows the weekly rent limit, the rent cover limit and the tenant default excess.

The rent cover limit (default $30,000) is the most we pay under this section for all claims in the policy period. It applies to [[ref:lld.cover.tenant-default]], [[ref:lld.cover.death-of-tenant]], [[ref:lld.cover.rent-after-damage]] and [[ref:lld.cover.prevention-of-access]] together. Each cover is also limited by its own period and by the weekly rent limit.
