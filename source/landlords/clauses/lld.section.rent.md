This section applies to every policy. The schedule shows the weekly rent limit, the rent cover limit and the tenant default excess.

The rent cover limit applies only to [[ref:lld.cover.tenant-default]] and [[ref:lld.cover.death-of-tenant]]. [[ref:lld.cover.rent-after-damage]] and [[ref:lld.cover.prevention-of-access]] are limited by their own periods and by the weekly rent limit, and the rent cover limit does not apply to them.
