since: 1.1.0

The residential dwelling at the address shown in the schedule, and on the same land:

- garages, carports, sheds and other outbuildings;
- fixed fixtures and fittings, including kitchen and bathroom fittings, built-in furniture, fixed floor coverings, fixed heating and cooling, and fixed light fittings;
- fences, gates, retaining walls, paths, driveways and fixed outdoor surfaces;
- fixed swimming pools, spas, decks and pergolas;
- services, including water, gas, electrical and telephone connections, that you own.

It does not include the land, plants and lawns (except as [[ref:lld.ext.landscaping]] provides), carpets and other contents, or anything the tenant owns. Where the property is a lot in a strata scheme, it does not include any part that the body corporate must insure.
