You pay the basic excess shown in the schedule for each claim under the Building and Landlord's Contents sections. If one event gives rise to claims under both sections, only one basic excess is deducted.

The tenant damage excess shown in the schedule applies to claims under [[ref:lld.section.tenant-damage]], once for each tenancy. The tenant default excess applies as [[ref:lld.pay.rent-settlement]] sets out. No excess applies to [[ref:lld.section.liability]] or [[ref:lld.section.additional-benefits]].

The schedule may show a separate excess for damage by earthquake. That excess replaces the basic excess for that damage.
