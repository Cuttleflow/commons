---
id: lld.excl.illegal-use
type: exclusion
heading: Illegal Use You Knew Of
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for any loss, damage or liability arising from illegal activity at the property that you or your property manager knew of, or had reason to suspect, and did not take reasonable steps to stop. Illegal activity by a tenant that you did not know of does not affect your cover.
