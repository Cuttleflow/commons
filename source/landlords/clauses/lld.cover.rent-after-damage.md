id: lld.cover.rent-after-damage
type: cover
heading: Loss of Rent After Damage
since: 1.1.0
changed: 1.3.0
status: draft
---

If damage covered by the Building or Landlord's Contents section makes the property unfit to live in while a residential tenancy agreement is in force, or while you have signed a residential tenancy agreement for a tenancy due to start, we pay the rent you lose from the date of the damage until the property is fit to live in again, for up to 52 weeks, subject to the rent cover limit.

This cover also applies where the body corporate's building is damaged by an event that this policy would have covered, if the lot is unfit to live in because of it.
