---
id: lld.excl.short-stay-letting
type: exclusion
heading: Short-Stay Letting
since: 1.1.0
changed: 1.2.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss, damage or liability to the extent that it is caused or contributed to by short-stay letting of the property, or any part of it, including by a guest, unless [[ref:lld.end.e04]] applies. This does not exclude loss, damage or liability that the short-stay letting did not cause or contribute to, for example a fire that a guest did not start.
