since: 1.1.0

If damage covered by the Building section makes the property unfit to live in and you must, under the residential tenancy agreement or the tenancy legislation, pay for the tenant's temporary accommodation, we pay that cost, up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $3,000) for any one event.
