This policy insures a residential property that you own and let to a tenant under a residential tenancy agreement. It covers the things that most often go wrong for a landlord: damage to the building and to the contents you provide, rent you lose, damage by a tenant, and your legal liability as owner.

It has six sections. The schedule shows which of the first two apply. The other four apply to every policy.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Building | Damage to the building from the insured events | Damage during the policy period |
| Landlord's Contents | Damage to the contents you provide for the tenant | Damage during the policy period |
| Loss of Rent | Rent lost after damage, on tenant default, when a tenant leaves without notice or will not leave, and on a tenant's death | Loss that begins during the policy period |
| Tenant Damage | Malicious, intentional and accidental damage by a tenant, and drug contamination | Damage during the policy period |
| Liability | Your legal liability as owner of the property | Occurrence during the policy period |
| Additional Benefits | Legal costs, tax audit costs and other set benefits | As each benefit states |

If you own a lot in a strata scheme, the body corporate insures the building. You can insure the contents you provide, and the other sections then apply to your lot.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies.
