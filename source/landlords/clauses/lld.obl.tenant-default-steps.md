since: 1.1.0

When a tenant is behind in rent, you or your property manager must take the steps the tenancy legislation provides to recover the rent or end the tenancy, including giving the notices it requires and applying to the tribunal within a reasonable time. Tell us before you apply to the tribunal for an order about a claim under this policy.
