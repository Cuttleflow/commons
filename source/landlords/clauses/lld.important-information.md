**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so.

**If a property manager arranged this policy.** A property manager may arrange this policy as the insurer's distributor. They do not give advice about whether it suits you. Read this wording, the Product Disclosure Statement and the Target Market Determination before you buy it.

**Who this policy is for.** This wording is designed for retail clients. An issuer using it must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available, as the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) requires. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.

**Your duty when you apply.** This policy is sold to individuals as a consumer insurance contract under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). You have a duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated. Answer every question in the proposal honestly and completely. If you do not take reasonable care, we may be entitled to reduce our liability for a claim, to cancel the policy, or, where the misrepresentation is fraudulent, to avoid the policy from its beginning, as that Act allows.

If the insured is a company, trust or other entity that is not an individual, this policy is not a consumer insurance contract. You then have the duty of disclosure in s.21 of that Act. Before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated, you must tell us every matter you know, or that a reasonable person in your circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we waive. If you do not comply, we may be entitled to reduce our liability for a claim or to cancel the policy, as that Act allows.

**Terms that differ from standard cover.** The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) sets standard cover for home building and home contents contracts. This policy gives less cover than standard cover in each of the ways listed here. They are listed so that you are clearly informed of each of them before this policy is entered into.

- flood is covered, but we do not pay for damage by storm or flood in the first 72 hours after this policy first starts, except as [[ref:lld.excl.first-72-hours]] allows;
- we do not pay for theft or malicious damage by the tenant or the tenant's guests under the Building or Landlord's Contents sections; [[ref:lld.section.tenant-damage]] covers them, with its own limit and excess;
- we do not pay for damage by erosion, subsidence or landslip except as [[ref:lld.excl.earth-movement]] allows, or for damage by actions of the sea (see [[ref:lld.excl.action-of-the-sea]]);
- we do not pay to fix a defect, or for damage from gradual causes (see [[ref:lld.excl.defects-and-gradual-causes]]);
- cover is limited while the property is unoccupied for more than 60 days (see [[ref:lld.excl.unoccupied]]);
- we do not pay for loss, damage or liability caused or contributed to by short-stay letting, unless [[ref:lld.end.e04]] applies (see [[ref:lld.excl.short-stay-letting]]);
- we do not pay for land, loss of market value, a retaining wall not built to the building laws, water entering through an opening made for building works or left open, an order or action of a government or public authority, or damage to property while it is being worked on (see [[ref:lld.excl.property-other]]);
- we do not pay for loss, damage or liability caused deliberately by you or a person acting with your authority, or arising from illegal activity at the property that you or your property manager knew of or had reason to suspect (see [[ref:lld.excl.deliberate-acts]] and [[ref:lld.excl.illegal-use]]);
- we do not pay for loss of, damage to or corruption of electronic data, or for loss from unauthorised use of a computer system (see [[ref:lld.excl.cyber]]);
- we do not pay for loss, damage, liability or cost arising from terrorism, war or nuclear risks (see [[ref:lld.excl.terrorism]], [[ref:lld.excl.war]] and [[ref:lld.excl.nuclear]]);
- for contents more than ten years old we may deduct a reasonable amount for age and condition, we pay for an item that is one of a pair or set only for the item damaged, and if we cannot find matching materials we replace materials only in the room or area where the damage happened (see [[ref:lld.pay.contents-settlement]] and [[ref:lld.pay.building-settlement]]);
- there is no cover for contents other than those you provide for the tenant's use.

**Key Facts Sheet.** Where the law requires it, a Key Facts Sheet for the Building section and one for the Landlord's Contents section are given with this policy.

**Cooling-off period.** You may cancel this policy within 21 days of the date it starts, and within 21 days of each renewal, by telling us in writing. If you have not made a claim, we will refund the full premium paid for that period, less any non-refundable government charges we cannot recover. After that, you may still cancel under [[ref:lld.cond.cancellation]].

**Commission and fees.** The schedule or the quote shows any commission or fee paid to your property manager or broker in connection with this policy.

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
