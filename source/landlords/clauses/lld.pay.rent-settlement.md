heading: How We Settle Rent Claims
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

We work out the rent you lose at the weekly rent payable under the residential tenancy agreement at the time of the loss, up to the weekly rent limit. From that we deduct:

- any part of the bond that is paid to you for rent;
- any rent the tenant or anyone else pays you for the same period;
- for [[ref:lld.cover.tenant-default]], the tenant default excess shown in the schedule (default one week's rent).

We pay each month, or at the end of the claim if you prefer. If the tribunal orders the tenant to pay rent, or the bond authority releases bond for rent, after we have paid you, you must repay us that amount, up to what we paid.
