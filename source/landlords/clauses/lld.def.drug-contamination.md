---
id: lld.def.drug-contamination
type: definition
heading: Drug Contamination
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Contamination of the building or the contents by chemical residue from the manufacture, cultivation or storage of an illegal drug at the property, at a level that a government authority, or an environmental or forensic scientist we approve, says must be cleaned or removed before the property can be lived in.
