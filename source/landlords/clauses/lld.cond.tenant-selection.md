since: 1.1.0

---

For [[ref:lld.cover.tenant-default]], [[ref:lld.cover.death-of-tenant]] and [[ref:lld.section.tenant-damage]], the tenancy must meet these conditions:

- there is a residential tenancy agreement for it, signed by you or your property manager and the tenant;
- before a tenancy that started after this policy first started, you or your property manager checked each adult tenant's identity, and checked at least one of a previous landlord's or property manager's reference, a residential tenancy database, or proof of income;
- a bond of the amount the tenancy legislation allows, up to four weeks' rent, was paid and lodged with the bond authority.

If a condition is not met, [[ref:lld.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies. We will not refuse a claim only because of it, unless it could reasonably be regarded as capable of causing or contributing to the loss.
