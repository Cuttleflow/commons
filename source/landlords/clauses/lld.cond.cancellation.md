You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us in writing. We will refund the premium for the unexpired policy period in proportion, unless you cancel within the cooling-off period in [[ref:lld.important-information]].

We may cancel this policy only on a ground allowed by the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), by giving you written notice as s.59 of that Act requires. We will refund the premium for the unexpired policy period in proportion.

Cancellation does not affect a claim for damage, a loss of rent or an occurrence that happened before cancellation.
