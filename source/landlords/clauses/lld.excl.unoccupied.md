---
id: lld.excl.unoccupied
type: exclusion
heading: Unoccupied Property
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If the property has been unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days, we pay for damage that happens after that only if it is caused by fire, lightning, explosion, earthquake or impact, unless [[ref:lld.end.e09]] applies. Cover is restored when a tenant moves in.
