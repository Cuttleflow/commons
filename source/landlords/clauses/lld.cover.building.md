If the building suffers damage during the policy period caused by an insured event, we will pay to repair or rebuild it, as set out in [[ref:lld.pay.building-settlement]].

The insured events are:

- **fire**, including bushfire, and smoke and soot from a fire;
- **lightning**, and power surge caused by lightning;
- **explosion**;
- **storm**, and rainwater or water run-off that enters the building because of a storm;
- **flood**;
- **earthquake and tsunami**;
- **escape of liquid** from a fixed pipe, drain, tank, appliance, toilet, bath, shower base or waterbed, or from an aquarium;
- **theft or attempted theft**, and **malicious damage**, by a person who is not a tenant or a tenant's guest;
- **impact** by a vehicle, aircraft, watercraft, falling tree or branch, power pole, aerial, satellite dish, or space debris;
- **riot or civil commotion**, and damage by a person taking part in it;
- **glass breakage** of fixed glass in the building, including glass in a cooktop or oven.

The most we will pay for any one event is the building sum insured, plus the amounts the extensions to this section say are payable in addition.
