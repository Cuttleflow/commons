heading: "Damage to the Landlord's Contents"
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If the contents suffer damage at the property during the policy period caused by an insured event listed in [[ref:lld.cover.building]], we will repair or replace them, as set out in [[ref:lld.pay.contents-settlement]].

The most we will pay for any one event is the contents sum insured.
