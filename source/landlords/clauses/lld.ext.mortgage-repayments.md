id: lld.ext.mortgage-repayments
type: extension
heading: Loan Repayments While the Property Is Untenantable
since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If damage covered by this section makes the property unfit to live in while no residential tenancy agreement is in force, so that [[ref:lld.cover.rent-after-damage]] does not apply, we pay the repayments due on any loan secured over the property for the period the property remains unfit to live in, up to three months and up to the limit shown in the schedule (default $10,000). This amount is in addition to the building sum insured.
