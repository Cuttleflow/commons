We cover damage to the building and the contents caused accidentally by anyone other than a tenant or a tenant's guest, as if it were an insured event listed in [[ref:lld.cover.building]]. Every other term of the Building and Landlord's Contents sections applies.

We do not pay under this endorsement for damage to glass in a television, computer or other screen, for mechanical or electrical breakdown, or for damage to property while it is being repaired or worked on.

Schedule variables: none.
