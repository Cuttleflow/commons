**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule.

**Who this policy is for.** This wording is designed for businesses that own or operate a domestic commercial vessel in Australia: charter and tour boats, water taxis, small ferries, workboats, barges, fishing vessels and similar vessels. It is not for a vessel owned by individuals and used only for private recreation, which a pleasure craft policy insures.

**Which law applies.** A contract of marine insurance is governed by the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth). The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) does not apply to it of its own force (s.9(1)(d) of that Act). This policy gives you, by agreement, the protections of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) that [[ref:chl.cond.statutory-framework]] lists. If that Act applies to this policy by law, for example because the vessel operates only on inland waters, it applies in full and nothing in this policy reduces it.

**Your duty of disclosure.** Before you enter into, renew, extend, vary or reinstate this policy, you must tell us every matter you know, or that a reasonable person in your circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we waive. The Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) sets a stricter duty and lets an insurer avoid the contract for any breach of it. By agreement, we do not rely on that Act's remedies. [[ref:chl.cond.disclosure]] sets out what we may do if you do not tell us something.

**Wholesale insurance.** Insurance to which the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) applies is not a prescribed kind of general insurance for retail clients under the Corporations Regulations 2001 (Cth). This wording is not a Product Disclosure Statement, and no Target Market Determination has been prepared for it. **General Insurance Code of Practice.** The Code of Practice does not apply to marine insurance. Where the insurer applies it to this policy voluntarily, the schedule says so.

**Commission and fees.** The schedule or the quote shows any commission or fee paid to your broker in connection with this policy.

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
