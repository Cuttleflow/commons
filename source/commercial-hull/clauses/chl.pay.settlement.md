heading: Settling a Claim for the Vessel
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If the vessel is a total loss, we pay the agreed value, without any excess, together with any other amount the Institute clauses provide for. The Institute clauses and the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) decide when the vessel is a constructive total loss, using the agreed value as the repaired value.

For partial loss or damage, we pay the reasonable cost of repairs, as the Institute clauses provide, less the excess.

If the schedule names a financier, we pay a claim for a total loss to the financier to the extent of its interest, and the rest to you.
