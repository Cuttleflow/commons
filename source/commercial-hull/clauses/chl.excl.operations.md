---
id: chl.excl.operations
type: exclusion
heading: Operations Not Insured
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss, damage, liability or cost arising while the vessel is:

- performing salvage or towage under a contract made before the service was needed, except as endorsement E13 allows;
- supporting offshore oil or gas exploration or production;
- carrying oil, chemicals or gas in bulk as cargo, or dangerous goods as cargo for reward;
- used for a thrill ride, for parasailing, or for towing people on inflatables or skis for reward;
- bareboat or demise chartered to another person, other than a hire under endorsement E15;
- under construction, before its delivery to you;
- racing or in a speed trial; or
- used outside Australia's territorial sea and exclusive economic zone, or on a voyage to or from a place outside Australia.
