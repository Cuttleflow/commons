id: chl.def.service-category
type: definition
heading: Service Category
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The vessel use category and the operational area shown together on the vessel's certificate of operation, such as 1D or 2C. The use categories are 1 (passenger vessel), 2 (non-passenger vessel), 3 (fishing vessel) and 4 (hire and drive vessel).
