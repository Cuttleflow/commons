For each claim you bear the excess that applies:

- the basic excess, for each accident or occurrence under [[ref:chl.section.hull|heading]], except a total loss;
- the machinery excess, for a claim to which the Institute Machinery Damage Additional Deductible Clause applies, as that clause provides;
- the protection and indemnity excess, for each occurrence under [[ref:chl.section.pi|heading]], including costs;
- the passenger excess, instead of the protection and indemnity excess, for a claim for the death of, or injury or illness to, a passenger, where endorsement E03 applies; and
- a special excess shown for an endorsement, where that endorsement says it applies.

Where one accident or occurrence gives rise to claims under both sections, you bear only the higher excess.
