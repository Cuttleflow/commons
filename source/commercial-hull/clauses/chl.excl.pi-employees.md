---
id: chl.excl.pi-employees
type: exclusion
heading: Employees
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability for the death of, or injury or illness to, your employee arising out of their employment, where it is covered, or required by law to be covered, under a workers' compensation law or scheme, including the Seafarers Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1992 (Cth). This exclusion does not apply to a liability that no such law requires you to insure.
