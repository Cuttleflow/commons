heading: Acts and Omissions After the Policy Starts
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If this policy would otherwise let us refuse to pay a claim, in whole or in part, because of something you or another insured did or did not do after this policy was entered into, we may not refuse to pay the claim only because of that act or omission. Our liability is reduced by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced.

Where the act or omission could reasonably be regarded as capable of causing or contributing to the loss, we may refuse to pay only the part of the claim that the act or omission caused or contributed to. We may not refuse any part that you prove the act or omission did not cause.

This applies to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation in this policy, in an endorsement and in the Institute clauses, as s.54 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) would if that Act applied.
