---
id: chl.excl.pi-water-activities
type: exclusion
heading: Swimming, Snorkelling and Diving
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability for the death of, or injury or illness to, a person in the water while swimming, snorkelling or diving from the vessel as an activity you organise, offer or allow, unless endorsement E04 or E05 applies to that activity. This exclusion does not apply to a person who falls or is thrown overboard, or whom the crew is rescuing.
