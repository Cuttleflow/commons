The most we pay for all claims arising from one occurrence, including costs, is the protection and indemnity limit.

Within that limit, the most we pay for one occurrence is:

- for pollution under paragraph (e) of [[ref:chl.cover.pi]]: $1,000,000, or the amount shown in the schedule, and never less than endorsement E12 requires where it applies; and
- for diversion costs under paragraph (f) of [[ref:chl.cover.pi]]: $25,000.

Where you are entitled to limit your liability under the Limitation of Liability for Maritime Claims Act 1989 (Cth) or any other law, we pay no more than your liability as limited, and the reasonable cost of establishing the limitation, within the protection and indemnity limit. We may ask you to claim limitation where you are entitled to, and you must not give up a right to limit without our written consent.
