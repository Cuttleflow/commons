  - Attach

For a vessel more than 24 metres in length:

- before cover starts, or by the date shown in the schedule, the vessel must have a condition survey by a marine surveyor we accept, and you must carry out its recommendations by the dates it sets; and
- the vessel must carry at least the crew its certificate of operation requires whenever it is under way.

[[ref:chl.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to these conditions.

Schedule variables: survey date; recommendations and their dates.
