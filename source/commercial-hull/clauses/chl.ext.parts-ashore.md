since: 1.0.0

We pay for loss of or damage to equipment and parts of the vessel while temporarily removed from it and stored ashore in Australia, or while carried in Australia to or from the vessel, caused by fire, flood, storm, theft following forcible entry, or a collision or overturning of the vehicle carrying them. The most we pay for one occurrence is 10% of the agreed value or $25,000, whichever is less. The basic excess applies.
