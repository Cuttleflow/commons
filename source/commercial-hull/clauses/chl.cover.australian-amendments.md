Each Institute clause applies to this policy as amended below.

1. **Collision liability.** Where the Institute clauses that apply include a collision liability clause, it applies as if it insured four-fourths of your liability instead of three-fourths. The added fourth is paid on the same terms as the rest, and within the same limit.
2. **Law and practice.** Each provision of the Institute clauses that makes the insurance subject to English law and practice, or to the jurisdiction of the English courts, is replaced by [[ref:chl.cond.law-and-practice]] and [[ref:chl.cond.jurisdiction]].
3. **Deductible.** The deductible in the Institute Time Clauses Hulls is the basic excess shown in the schedule, and the deductible in the Institute Machinery Damage Additional Deductible Clause is the machinery excess. No excess applies to a claim for a total loss.
4. **Classification.** The vessel is not required to be classed. Every provision of the Institute clauses about a classification society, the vessel's class or a class recommendation does not apply to a vessel that the schedule does not show as classed. [[ref:chl.obl.certificates]] applies in its place.
5. **Notice of claim and tenders.** The provision of the Institute Time Clauses Hulls that reduces a claim by a percentage if you do not give notice of an accident or let us take tenders for repairs does not apply. [[ref:chl.obl.tell-us-about-loss]] applies in its place.
6. **Change of ownership, flag, management or charter.** Where the Institute clauses end this insurance automatically on a change of ownership, flag or management of the vessel, or on a bareboat charter, [[ref:chl.cond.cancellation]] decides any return of premium.
7. **Negligence of repairers and charterers.** To the extent the Institute Time Clauses Hulls do not already insure it, loss of or damage to the vessel caused by the negligence of a repairer or charterer of the vessel who is not insured under this policy is a peril insured, on the same terms as the perils that are subject to the due diligence proviso of those clauses.
8. **Returns of premium.** A return of premium for lay-up applies only where endorsement E09 applies. Any other return is decided by [[ref:chl.cond.cancellation]].
9. **Warranties.** Every warranty in the Institute clauses is a condition of this policy, as [[ref:chl.cond.statutory-framework]] sets out.
