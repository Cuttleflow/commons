since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

1. Make the vessel, its passengers and crew safe, and take reasonable steps to prevent further loss (see [[ref:chl.obl.reduce-loss]]).
2. Contact your broker, or contact us directly at the details shown in the schedule.
3. Report the marine incident to the Australian Maritime Safety Authority as the national law requires, and report any theft or malicious damage to the police.
4. Tell us what happened and when, and give us photographs, the vessel's logbook entries, the passenger count and any survey or incident report.
5. Do not start repairs, other than emergency repairs, until we have had a chance to inspect the damage (see [[ref:chl.obl.tell-us-about-loss]]).
6. For a claim against you, send us the demand as soon as it is received, and do not admit liability (see [[ref:chl.obl.admissions]]).
7. We will acknowledge the claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision.
