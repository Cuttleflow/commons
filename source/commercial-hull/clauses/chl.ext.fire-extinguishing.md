heading: Fire Extinguishing Costs
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We pay the reasonable cost of recharging or replacing fire extinguishers and fixed fire-fighting systems on the vessel that were used to fight, or to prevent, a fire on board or near the vessel. The most we pay for one occurrence is $5,000.
