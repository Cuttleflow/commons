This policy is a contract of marine insurance governed by the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth). The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) does not apply to it of its own force.

By agreement, we give you these protections as if that Act applied:

- the duty of utmost good faith, binding us as well as you ([[ref:chl.cond.utmost-good-faith]]);
- the duty of disclosure in s.21, and remedies limited as s.28 limits them ([[ref:chl.cond.disclosure]]);
- the treatment of acts and omissions after the policy is entered into in s.54 ([[ref:chl.cond.insurance-contracts-act]]);
- interest on claims as s.57 allows ([[ref:chl.pay.interest]]);
- refusal of a fraudulent claim only as s.56 allows ([[ref:chl.cond.fraud]]); and
- cancellation only for the reasons s.60 allows ([[ref:chl.cond.cancellation]]).

The obligations in this policy, including those in an endorsement, and every warranty in the Institute clauses, are conditions of cover. They are not warranties. We will not rely on a breach of any warranty, express or implied by the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth), to be discharged from liability. A breach of an implied warranty is treated as a breach of a condition, and [[ref:chl.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to it.

If the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) applies to this policy by law, it applies in full, and where this policy is inconsistent with it, that Act prevails.
