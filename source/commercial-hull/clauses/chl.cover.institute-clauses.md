The Institute clauses that apply depend on the conditions shown in the schedule for this section.

| Conditions in the schedule | Institute clauses |
|---|---|
| Full conditions | Institute Time Clauses Hulls CL280 1/10/83; Institute Additional Perils Clauses Hulls 1/10/83, with the Institute Machinery Damage Additional Deductible Clause dated 1/10/83 |
| Total loss only | Institute Time Clauses Hulls Total Loss Only (including Salvage, Salvage Charges and Sue and Labour) CL289 1/10/83 |

The following also apply under either conditions:

- Institute War and Strikes Clauses Hulls–Time CL281 1/10/83, as [[ref:chl.cover.war-and-strikes]] sets out;
- Institute Radioactive Contamination, Chemical, Biological, Bio-chemical and Electromagnetic Weapons Exclusion Clause CL370 10/11/03; and
- Institute Cyber Attack Exclusion Clause CL380 10/11/03.

CL370 and CL380 apply to every section of this policy, not only to this section.

An endorsement may add another Institute clause: endorsement E09 for lay-up and endorsement E11 for disbursements and increased value.

[[ref:chl.cond.institute-clause-text]] explains where to find the text of these clauses.
