We pay for loss of or damage to the clothing and personal effects of the crew while on board the vessel, caused by an accident that this section covers for the vessel, or by theft following forcible entry. The most we pay is $1,000 for each person and $5,000 for one occurrence. We do not pay for cash, cards, jewellery, or property of passengers.
