---
id: chl.excl.pi-property
type: exclusion
heading: Property You Own or Carry
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability for loss of or damage to property owned by, leased to or hired by you, or in the care, custody or control of you or your crew, including goods carried on the vessel. Your liability as a carrier of goods is not covered by this policy.

This exclusion does not apply to the clothing, baggage and personal effects of passengers, up to $1,000 for each passenger.
