---
id: chl.obl.tell-us-about-changes
type: obligation
heading: Tell Us About Changes
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You must tell us within 30 days if, during the policy period, the vessel's service category or operational area changes, the vessel is modified in a way that requires a new survey or stability assessment, you start a new kind of operation with the vessel, the vessel is laid up for more than 30 days, or its home port moves to another state or territory. We may change the premium or terms for the rest of the policy period, in the way [[ref:chl.cond.statutory-framework]] allows.
