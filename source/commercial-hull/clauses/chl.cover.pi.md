We pay the amounts you become legally liable to pay, as owner or operator of the vessel, arising from an occurrence during the policy period in connection with the vessel or its operation, for:

(a) death of, or bodily injury or illness to, any person, including a passenger;

(b) loss of or damage to property, including another vessel and its cargo, a wharf, jetty, pontoon, mooring, buoy, navigation aid, aquaculture structure or fishing gear, and the loss of use of that property;

(c) collision with another vessel, to the extent your liability is more than [[ref:chl.section.hull|heading]] pays for it, or is a liability that the collision liability clause of the Institute clauses does not insure;

(d) the cost of raising, removing, destroying, lighting or marking the wreck of the vessel, where a law or a public authority requires it or the wreck is a danger to navigation, less the value of anything recovered from it;

(e) pollution: liability, and the reasonable cost of preventing or cleaning up pollution, caused by a pollutant discharged or escaping from the vessel, or threatening to, where the discharge or escape is sudden and accidental, including costs a public authority recovers from you under a law; and

(f) the reasonable cost of diverting the vessel to land a sick or injured person, a stowaway or a person rescued at sea, including port charges, fuel and wages for the extra time.

We also pay the reasonable legal costs and expenses incurred with our written consent to investigate, defend or settle a claim under this section, and costs awarded against you. These costs are part of, not in addition to, the protection and indemnity limit.

This section does not pay a liability that [[ref:chl.section.hull|heading]] pays.
