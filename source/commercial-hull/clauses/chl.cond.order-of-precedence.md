since: 1.0.0
status: draft

Where parts of your contract of insurance differ, they apply in this order: an endorsement shown in the schedule, then the schedule, then this wording, then the Institute clauses.

Two exceptions apply. CL370 and CL380 prevail over every other part of this policy, as they state. Nothing in an Institute clause, the schedule or an endorsement reduces a protection that [[ref:chl.cond.statutory-framework]] gives you.
