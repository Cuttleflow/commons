The vessel may tow barges, pontoons and workboats that you own or operate, in the course of your business and within its operational area. [[ref:chl.excl.operations]] and [[ref:chl.excl.pi-towage]] do not apply to that towage. [[ref:chl.excl.pi-property]] still applies to the towed vessel and anything on it.

Each tow must use towing gear suitable for it and inspected at least once every 12 months, and must display the lights and shapes the collision regulations require. [[ref:chl.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to these conditions.

Towage of vessels or objects owned by others under a contract remains excluded.
