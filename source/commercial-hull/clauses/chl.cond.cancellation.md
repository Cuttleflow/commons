You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges.

We may cancel this policy only for a reason for which s.60 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) would allow an insurer to cancel if that Act applied, by giving you at least 30 days' written notice. We refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis. The cover under CL281 may be cancelled on the shorter notice that CL281 allows, with a pro-rata refund of its premium.

If this insurance ends automatically under the Institute clauses, we refund the premium for the unexpired policy period on a pro-rata basis.

No refund is due if we have paid, or agreed to pay, a claim for a total loss of the vessel.

Cancellation does not affect a claim for an accident or occurrence that happened before it took effect.
