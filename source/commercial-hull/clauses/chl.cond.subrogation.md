---
id: chl.cond.subrogation
type: condition
heading: Our Rights of Recovery
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

After we pay a claim, we may take over your rights to recover from anyone else, in your name, and you must cooperate with us. You must not give up or limit a right of recovery without our written consent, except under a contract with a port, harbour, wharf or marina operator on its standard terms.

We will not seek recovery from your employee or crew, unless the loss arose from their serious or wilful misconduct, or from a subsidiary or related entity of yours that charters the vessel from you.
