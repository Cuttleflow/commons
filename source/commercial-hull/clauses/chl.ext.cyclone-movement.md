id: chl.ext.cyclone-movement
type: extension
heading: Cyclone Movement Costs
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If the Bureau of Meteorology issues a warning for a named cyclone that includes the place where the vessel is kept, we pay half of the reasonable cost of moving the vessel to a safer place or hauling it out of the water, and of returning it, whether or not the vessel is then damaged. The most we pay in the policy period is $5,000.
