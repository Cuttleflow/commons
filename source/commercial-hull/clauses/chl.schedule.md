(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- Your name, ABN and address, and any financier with an interest in the vessel
- The policy period, with its start and end dates
- The vessel: its name, unique vessel identifier, length, hull material, year built, and any tender insured with it
- The vessel's service category and operational area, as shown on its certificate of operation, and its home port
- The agreed value
- The sections that apply, and whether the Hull and Machinery section is on full conditions or total loss only (see [[ref:chl.cover.institute-clauses]])
- The limit for the Protection and Indemnity section, and its sub-limits where they differ from [[ref:chl.cover.pi-limits]]
- The excesses: the basic excess, the machinery excess, the protection and indemnity excess, and any passenger excess or special excess shown for an endorsement
- The amounts for the additional benefits where they differ from those stated in [[ref:chl.section.additional-benefits]]
- The premium, including any stamp duty and GST
- Any commission or fee paid to your broker
- The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:chl.cond.law-and-practice]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
