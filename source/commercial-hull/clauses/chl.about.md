This policy insures a commercial vessel: a vessel used in a business and operated under the Marine Safety (Domestic Commercial Vessel) National Law. It is built on the Institute hull clauses, which are the standard hull terms of the London and Australian markets. This wording does not repeat those clauses. It names the clauses that apply, amends them for Australian law and practice and for small domestic vessels, and adds what they do not say: the vessel and its operational area, cover for your liabilities as owner or operator, additional benefits, the excesses, and the protections of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) that we give you by agreement.

It has two sections. The schedule shows which apply.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Hull and Machinery | Loss of or damage to the vessel, and the collision liability, general average, salvage and sue and labour costs the Institute clauses provide for, with war and strikes cover | Accidents and occurrences during the policy period |
| Protection and Indemnity | Your legal liability as owner or operator of the vessel for death, injury, property damage, wreck removal and pollution, including liability to passengers | Occurrences during the policy period |

The schedule shows the vessel, its service category and operational area, its agreed value, whether the Hull and Machinery section is on full conditions or total loss only, the limit for the Protection and Indemnity section, and the excesses.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal, the Institute clauses named in it and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. [[ref:chl.cond.order-of-precedence]] says which part applies where they differ.
