The Institute War and Strikes Clauses Hulls–Time CL281 1/10/83 apply to the vessel. They insure the war, strikes, terrorism and malicious acts perils that the Institute Time Clauses Hulls exclude, subject to their own exclusions.

The provisions of CL281 that allow notice of cancellation, and that end its cover automatically on the events it names, apply to the cover CL281 gives and to nothing else. The rest of this policy continues.

No premium for the Australian Reinsurance Pool Corporation's terrorism cover is charged, because the Terrorism Insurance Act 2003 (Cth) does not apply to a contract of marine insurance.
