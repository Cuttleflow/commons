We may take over and conduct the defence or settlement of any claim under this section in your name. You must give us the information and help we reasonably ask for.

We may at any time pay you the protection and indemnity limit for an occurrence, less any amount already paid for it, or any lower amount for which the claim can be settled. We then have no further obligation for that occurrence, except for costs we agreed to before that payment.
