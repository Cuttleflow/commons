[[ref:chl.excl.pi-water-activities]] does not apply to passengers swimming or snorkelling from the vessel, with or without a mask, snorkel, fins or wetsuit, but without breathing apparatus, where:

- the crew briefs them on the activity and its risks before they enter the water;
- a crew member keeps watch on everyone in the water, from the vessel or the water, at all times;
- the crew counts the people in the water before they enter it and again when they return;
- flotation aids are offered to everyone and given to anyone who asks or appears to need one; and
- the vessel's engines are stopped, or its propellers are disengaged, while anyone is in the water near it.

The special excess shown in the schedule applies to each claim for a person in the water. [[ref:chl.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to these conditions.

Schedule variables: special excess (default $5,000).
