---
id: chl.excl.illegal-use
type: exclusion
heading: Illegal Use
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss, damage or liability arising while the vessel is used for an illegal purpose, including fishing that the law does not allow, or for the seizure, forfeiture or detention of the vessel, its gear or its catch by a public authority because of a breach of the law.
