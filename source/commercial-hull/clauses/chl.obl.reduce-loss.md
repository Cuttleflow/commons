heading: Reduce the Loss
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

After an accident or occurrence, you and your crew must take reasonable steps to prevent or reduce loss, damage or liability, including salvaging and protecting the vessel. We pay the reasonable costs of doing so as the Institute clauses provide for the vessel, and as part of the protection and indemnity limit for a liability under [[ref:chl.section.pi|heading]].
