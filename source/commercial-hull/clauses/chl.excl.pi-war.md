---
id: chl.excl.pi-war
type: exclusion
heading: War
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay under this section for liability arising from war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities or warlike operations (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, or military or usurped power, or from a mine, torpedo, bomb or other weapon of war. This section covers liability arising from strikes, riots, civil commotion, malicious acts and terrorism.
