heading: Non-Disclosure and Misrepresentation
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If you did not comply with the duty of disclosure in [[ref:chl.important-information]], or made a misrepresentation to us, before this policy was entered into, and the failure or misrepresentation was not fraudulent, we may reduce our liability for a claim to the amount that would place us in the position we would have been in if you had complied or had not made it. We may not avoid this policy.

If the failure or misrepresentation was fraudulent, we may avoid this policy from its beginning.

The provisions of the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) that let an insurer avoid a contract for non-disclosure or misrepresentation do not apply, except for fraud.
