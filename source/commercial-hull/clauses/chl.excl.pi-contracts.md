---
id: chl.excl.pi-contracts
type: exclusion
heading: Liability Under Contract
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for liability you have only because you agreed to it under a contract, and that you would not have without the contract. This exclusion does not apply to a contract with a port, harbour, wharf or marina operator, on its standard terms, for the use of its facilities.
