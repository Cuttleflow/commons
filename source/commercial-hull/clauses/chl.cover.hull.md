We insure the vessel during the policy period on the Institute clauses that [[ref:chl.cover.institute-clauses]] lists for the conditions shown in the schedule, as amended by [[ref:chl.cover.australian-amendments]].

We pay for loss of or damage to the vessel caused by a peril those clauses insure, and the collision liability, general average and salvage contributions, and sue and labour costs those clauses provide for, subject to their exclusions, this wording and the schedule.

The most we pay for loss of or damage to the vessel from one accident or occurrence is the agreed value. The Institute clauses say which other amounts are paid in addition to the agreed value. A total loss is settled as [[ref:chl.pay.settlement]] sets out.
