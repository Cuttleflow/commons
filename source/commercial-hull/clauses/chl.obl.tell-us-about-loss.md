heading: Tell Us About Loss
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable about any accident, loss, damage, occurrence or demand that may give rise to a claim under this policy. Theft and malicious damage must be reported to the police. You must report marine incidents to the Australian Maritime Safety Authority as the national law requires.

Before repairs start, except emergency repairs, you must give us a reasonable chance to inspect the damage, and to obtain tenders for the repairs if we ask. We pay the reasonable cost of a survey we ask for. You must keep damaged parts available for inspection until we agree they may be disposed of.
