heading: Operational Area
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You must operate the vessel only within its operational area, except to save life, to escape danger, or to reach the nearest safe haven or place of repair. If the vessel goes outside its operational area for any other reason, tell us as soon as you know. We may charge an additional premium for the time it was outside.
