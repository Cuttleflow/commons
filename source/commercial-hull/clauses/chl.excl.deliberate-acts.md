---
id: chl.excl.deliberate-acts
type: exclusion
heading: Deliberate Acts
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss, damage or liability caused deliberately by you, or by a person acting with your consent or at your direction. This exclusion does not apply to a deliberate act of the crew that you did not know of or consent to, where a peril insured by the Institute clauses covers it.
