id: ccl.cover.nominated-clients

heading: Nominated Clients Cover
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For goods carried for a nominated client, or goods of a nominated commodity, we pay for accidental loss of or damage to the goods in an insured transit during the policy period, from any cause that this policy does not exclude, as if [[ref:ccl.section.comprehensive|heading]] applied to them. Endorsement E07 records the nominated clients and commodities and any special excess for them.
