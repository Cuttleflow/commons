---
id: ccl.pay.one-section
type: payment
heading: One Section for Each Loss
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We pay any one loss under one section only. Where more than one section could respond to the same loss of the same goods, we pay under the section that gives you the most cover, and only one excess applies. A payment for the goods under any section reduces any amount we pay under [[ref:ccl.section.legal-liability|heading]] for the same goods.
