heading: Reduce the Loss
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You must take reasonable steps to prevent further loss or damage after an event, including salvaging, protecting and resecuring the goods. We pay the reasonable cost of doing so under [[ref:ccl.ext.onforwarding]] and [[ref:ccl.ext.resecuring]].
