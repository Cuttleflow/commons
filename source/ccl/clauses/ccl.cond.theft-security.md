id: ccl.cond.theft-security
type: condition
heading: Theft From an Unattended Vehicle
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

For theft of goods from a conveying vehicle left unattended, we pay under [[ref:ccl.cover.standard-perils|heading]] only if:

- the vehicle's doors and load compartment were locked and its keys removed, or entry was gained by force or violence; and
- for a detached trailer or semi-trailer left outside a locked compound, a kingpin lock or wheel clamp was fitted.

[[ref:ccl.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to this condition: if the security was not in place but did not cause or contribute to the theft, we pay the claim.
