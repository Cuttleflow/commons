This policy is for road transport operators who carry goods for other people. It insures loss of or damage to the goods while they are in your care during a transit within Australia. The core cover, the Basic Load section, pays for loss or damage caused by a listed event whether or not you are legally liable to the owner of the goods. That lets you settle with your customer without first arguing about who was at fault.

It is built in tiers, with options. The schedule shows which sections apply.

| Section | What it covers | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Basic Load | Loss of or damage to goods in transit caused by fire, collision, overturning and the other events listed in it | Loss or damage that happens during the policy period |
| Standard Perils | Theft and non-delivery, loading and unloading accidents, rainwater and shedding of load | As above |
| Comprehensive | Accidental loss of or damage to all goods, from any cause not excluded | As above |
| Nominated Clients | Accidental loss or damage cover for the clients or commodities named in the schedule | As above |
| Refrigeration Breakdown | Deterioration of refrigerated goods caused by a refrigeration unit failing | As above |
| Legal Liability | Your legal liability as a carrier for loss of, damage to or delay of goods, measured by your approved terms of carriage, and defence costs | Loss, damage or delay that happens during the policy period |

The three tiers are Essentials (Basic Load alone), Standard (Basic Load and Standard Perils) and Comprehensive (all three). [[ref:ccl.section.additional-benefits|heading]] applies to every policy.

This policy, together with your schedule, your proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms your contract of insurance with us. Please read them together and tell us if anything is incorrect. [[ref:ccl.cond.order-of-precedence]] says which part applies where they differ.
