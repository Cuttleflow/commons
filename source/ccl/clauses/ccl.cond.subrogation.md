since: 1.0.0

After we pay a claim, we may take over your rights to recover from anyone else, in your name, and you must cooperate with us. [[ref:ccl.cover.subcontractors]] sets out when we do not seek recovery from a subcontractor. We will not seek recovery from your employee, unless the loss arose from the employee's serious or wilful misconduct.
