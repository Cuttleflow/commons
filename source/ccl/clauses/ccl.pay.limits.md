The most we pay for loss of or damage to goods on one conveying vehicle from one event, under all sections other than [[ref:ccl.section.legal-liability|heading]], is the limit any one vehicle. [[ref:ccl.ext.accumulation]] sets the most we pay for one event at a depot. Under [[ref:ccl.section.legal-liability|heading]], the most we pay is the limit for that section shown in the schedule, plus defence costs as [[ref:ccl.cover.defence-costs]] allows. The additional benefits are paid in addition to these limits.
