---
id: ccl.excl.dishonesty
type: exclusion
heading: Dishonesty
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss or damage caused by a dishonest or fraudulent act of you or your employee, or of a subcontractor or its employee, acting alone or with others. Theft by a person who gains possession of the goods by deception, such as by impersonating a consignee or another carrier, is covered under [[ref:ccl.cover.standard-perils|heading]] where that section applies.
