id: ccl.cover.temporary-storage
type: cover
heading: Goods Held at a Depot
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Goods in temporary storage are covered on the same terms as goods on a conveying vehicle, while they are at a depot. Theft of goods held at a depot is covered under [[ref:ccl.section.standard-perils|heading]] only if the depot is locked and secured, or attended, when the theft happens, or entry is gained by force or violence. Storage beyond 30 days is covered only if endorsement E09 applies.
