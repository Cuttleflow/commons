---
id: ccl.excl.cyber
type: exclusion
heading: Cyber
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for any loss, damage, liability or cost arising from any access to or use of a computer system, software, network or electronic data without the authority of its owner, malicious or harmful code, or a denial of service attack. This exclusion does not apply to physical loss of or damage to goods that a fire, collision or other event listed in [[ref:ccl.cover.basic-load|heading]] causes, or to theft of goods under [[ref:ccl.cover.standard-perils|heading]].
