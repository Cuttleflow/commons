heading: Admissions
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

You must not admit liability for, offer to settle, or settle any claim by an owner or consignor under [[ref:ccl.section.legal-liability|heading]] without our written consent, which we do not unreasonably withhold. Paying an owner for goods under another section, with our agreement, is not an admission of liability.
