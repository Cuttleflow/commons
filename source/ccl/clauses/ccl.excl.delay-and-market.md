---
id: ccl.excl.delay-and-market
type: exclusion
heading: Delay and Loss of Market
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay for loss of market, loss of profit or other consequential loss, or for loss or damage caused by delay, even if an event this policy covers causes the delay. This exclusion does not apply to [[ref:ccl.ext.additional-expenses]], [[ref:ccl.ext.onforwarding]], or to delay liability under [[ref:ccl.section.legal-liability|heading]].
