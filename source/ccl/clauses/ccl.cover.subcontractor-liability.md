heading: Liability as a Subcontractor
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

When you carry goods as a subcontractor for another carrier, [[ref:ccl.cover.legal-liability|heading]] applies to your liability to that carrier, provided the principal carrier's terms limit your liability at least as well as your approved terms would, and extend their protection to you.
