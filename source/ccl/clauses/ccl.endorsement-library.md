An endorsement applies only if its code is shown in the schedule. Each endorsement changes this policy only as it states, and is read together with the variables shown for it in the schedule. Where an endorsement and the rest of this policy conflict, the endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. If a variable an endorsement needs is not shown in the schedule, the default stated in the endorsement applies; if it has no default, the endorsement does not apply until we show the variable in the schedule.

| Code | Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Livestock | Covers death or injury of livestock from an insured event, with agistment and mustering costs |
| E02 | Refrigerated Goods | Sets the conditions for carrying refrigerated goods |
| E03 | Household Removals | Covers household furniture and personal effects in a removal, on named terms |
| E04 | Dangerous Goods | Records the dangerous goods carried in packages and the conditions for carrying them |
| E05 | Sea Leg to Tasmania and Islands | Confirms cover for Bass Strait and island crossings and sets the sea terms |
| E06 | Increased Accumulation | Increases the accumulation limit at a named depot |
| E07 | Nominated Clients | Records the nominated clients and commodities and any special excess |
| E08 | Terms of Carriage Not Approved | Limits legal liability cover where your terms of carriage are not approved |
| E09 | Extended Storage | Extends cover to goods held at a depot beyond 30 days |
