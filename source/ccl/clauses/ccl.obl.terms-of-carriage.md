heading: Use Your Terms of Carriage
since: 1.0.0

---

Where [[ref:ccl.section.legal-liability|heading]] applies, you must use your approved terms in each contract of carriage, by including or referring to them on the consignment note or in the written agreement with your customer. [[ref:ccl.pay.measure-of-liability]] says what we pay where you do not.
