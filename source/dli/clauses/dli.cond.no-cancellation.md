since: 1.0.0

Once we have issued the certificate of currency, this policy cannot be cancelled by us, the developer, the owners corporation or anyone else, and no premium is refundable except as [[ref:dli.cond.commencement]] provides. We will not exercise any right the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) or any other law gives us to cancel this policy.

Before we issue the certificate of currency, the developer may withdraw from this policy by telling us in writing. We refund the premium paid, less the technical inspector's fees incurred and the non-refundable government charges.
