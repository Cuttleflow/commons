since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

After we accept a claim, we agree a scope of rectification with you. Then, as you and we agree, we either:

1. arrange and pay for the rectification ourselves, using contractors we appoint, in which case we are responsible for the quality of that work; or
2. pay you the rectification costs of the agreed scope, as the work is done or as invoices are presented.

We pay a cash amount instead of rectification only if you ask for it and we agree.

If we arrange the rectification and the rectification work is itself defective, we treat the defect in that work as a relevant defect notified on the date it is discovered, for the longer of six years from the completion of the rectification and the rest of the period of insurance.
