---
id: dli.cond.recovery-from-others
type: condition
heading: Our Right to Recover From Others
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

After we pay for a relevant defect, we may take over the owners corporation's rights to recover the amount from anyone responsible for it, including the builder, the developer, a designer, a certifier, a subcontractor, a supplier or a manufacturer, and act in the owners corporation's name.

We will not seek to recover anything from the owners corporation, a lot owner, an occupier of a lot or the strata managing agent, except from a person who caused damage deliberately or carried out work [[ref:dli.excl.alterations]] describes.

From any amount we recover, we first take what we paid and our reasonable costs of recovery. We pay any balance to the owners corporation.
