heading: The Owners Corporation as the Insured
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

This policy is taken out by the developer in favour of the owners corporation. The owners corporation becomes the insured when the strata plan for the building is registered. Until then, the developer holds this policy for the owners corporation that will be constituted, and may not deal with it in a way that reduces the owners corporation's rights.

A change in the lot owners does not affect this policy. The owners corporation acts for the lot owners from time to time.
