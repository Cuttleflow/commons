---
id: dli.ext.building-law
type: extension
heading: Compliance With Current Building Law
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Where the rectification of a relevant defect must comply with a law or standard in force at the time of rectification that differs from the one that applied to the building work, we pay the additional cost of complying with it, for the part of the building element being rectified.
