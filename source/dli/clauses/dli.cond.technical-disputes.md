If the owners corporation and we disagree about whether a defect is a relevant defect, or about the scope or cost of rectification, the owners corporation may refer the disagreement to an independent expert. The expert is a building consultant or engineer agreed by the owners corporation and us or, if we do not agree within 10 business days, nominated by the president of the Resolution Institute.

The expert decides as an expert and not as an arbitrator. The expert's decision binds us. It does not bind the owners corporation, which keeps its rights under [[ref:dli.cond.disputes-and-complaints]] and at law. We pay the expert's fees.
