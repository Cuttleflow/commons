---
id: dli.cond.no-double-recovery
type: condition
heading: Amounts You Recover From Others
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If the owners corporation recovers money from anyone else for a relevant defect that we have paid for, it must repay us the part of that money that covers what we paid, up to the amount we paid. We do not reduce a claim because the owners corporation could have recovered from someone else.
