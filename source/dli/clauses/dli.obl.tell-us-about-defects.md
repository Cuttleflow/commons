---
id: dli.obl.tell-us-about-defects
type: obligation
heading: Telling Us About Defects
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us in writing as soon as reasonably practicable after the owners corporation, its strata committee or its strata managing agent becomes aware of a defect that may be a relevant defect. A lot owner may tell us on the owners corporation's behalf.

Give us the information and documents you have that we reasonably ask for, including the building manual, maintenance records and any reports about the defect. Let us inspect the defect before it is rectified, unless urgent work is needed to make the building safe or prevent further damage.

If you tell us late, we may reduce what we pay only to the extent that the delay prejudiced us, as [[ref:dli.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] sets out.
