heading: Protection of the Owners Corporation
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We will not refuse, reduce or delay a claim by the owners corporation because of:

- a failure by the developer to comply with its duty of disclosure, or a misrepresentation or fraud by the developer;
- a breach of this policy by the developer, or a breach of the construction contract by the developer or the builder;
- a failure to pay premium after the certificate of currency has been issued; or
- the insolvency, deregistration or death of the developer or the builder.

Where we pay a claim that we could have refused or reduced but for this condition, we may recover the amount from the developer to the extent the law allows. We will not recover it from the owners corporation or a lot owner.
