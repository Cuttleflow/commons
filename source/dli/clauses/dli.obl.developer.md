heading: The Developer's Obligations Before Cover Starts
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Before this policy takes effect, the developer must:

- appoint the technical inspector, or allow us to appoint it, and give it the access, information and documents the technical inspection plan requires;
- tell us promptly about any change to the design, the builder, the building products for the building enclosure and the waterproofing, the contract value or the construction programme;
- arrange for the builder to rectify the defects the technical inspector reports during the building work, and give us the technical inspector's confirmation that they have been rectified;
- give the owners corporation the building manual, the maintenance schedule and the final technical inspection report; and
- declare the final contract value and pay the premium as [[ref:dli.cond.premium]] sets out.

These obligations are the developer's. A failure by the developer to meet them does not reduce the owners corporation's cover once this policy has taken effect: [[ref:dli.cond.protection-of-the-owners-corporation]] applies.
