---
id: dli.excl.lot-property
type: exclusion
heading: Defects in Lot Property
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not pay to rectify a defect in a part of a lot that is not common property. This exclusion does not apply to damage to a lot that a relevant defect causes, to the costs [[ref:dli.ext.access-and-reinstatement]] covers, or where an endorsement extends the cover to lot property.
