---
id: dli.cond.other-insurance
type: condition
heading: Other Insurance
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy, including the building insurance the owners corporation holds under the Strata Schemes Management Act 2015 (NSW), we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows.
