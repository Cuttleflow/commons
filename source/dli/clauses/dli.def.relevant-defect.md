heading: Relevant Defect
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Has the meaning given in s.211AA of the Strata Schemes Management Act 2015 (NSW) as in force when this policy takes effect. At the date of this wording it means a defect in a building element that is:

- a failure to comply with the Building Code of Australia, a relevant Australian Standard or the approved plans and specifications, that causes or is likely to cause damage to the building, or a risk of death or serious injury;
- defective design, workmanship or materials that causes or is likely to cause the building or a part of it to be uninhabitable or unusable for its intended purpose, its destruction or the threat of its collapse;
- the use of a building product in contravention of the Building Products (Safety) Act 2017 (NSW); or
- a defect in a lift, escalator or other vertical transportation product that causes or is likely to cause a risk of death or serious injury,

and any other defect the regulations under that Act prescribe.
