This policy is decennial liability insurance for a new residential apartment building in New South Wales. The developer of the building takes it out. It insures the owners corporation of the strata scheme for the building. It pays to rectify a relevant defect in a building element of the common property for ten years from the date of the occupation certificate.

| What it covers | Who it protects | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|
| The cost of rectifying a relevant defect in a building element of the common property, and damage the defect causes | The owners corporation, for the benefit of the lot owners from time to time | Ten years from the date of the occupation certificate |

**First resort.** The owners corporation does not have to sue the builder or the developer, or show that either is insolvent, before it claims. It needs to show that there is a relevant defect, not who caused it. [[ref:dli.cover.first-resort]] explains this.

**Strict liability.** We pay whether or not anyone was at fault.

**No cancellation.** Once the certificate of currency for the occupation certificate application has been issued, nobody can cancel this policy. [[ref:dli.cond.no-cancellation]] explains this.

**The law that requires this policy.** This policy is intended to be decennial liability insurance under the Strata Schemes Management Act 2015 (NSW). [[ref:dli.cond.statutory-basis]] explains how the policy is read so that it meets that Act.

This policy, together with the schedule, the developer's proposal and any endorsements shown in the schedule, forms the contract of insurance. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule applies, except where [[ref:dli.cond.statutory-basis]] requires otherwise. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:dli.section.definitions]].
