Unless the schedule shows shorter periods, we will:

- acknowledge a claim within 10 business days after we receive it;
- inspect the defect, or arrange an inspection, within 20 business days after we receive the claim, or within 2 business days where the defect creates a risk to safety;
- tell you in writing whether we accept the claim within 60 days after we have the information we reasonably need, with our reasons if we do not accept it; and
- agree the scope of rectification, or make you an offer, within 90 days after we accept the claim.

If we cannot meet a time limit because the investigation is complex, we will tell you before it ends why, and when we will decide. We may do this once for each time limit. Interest on any amount we pay late is payable as s.57 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) requires.
