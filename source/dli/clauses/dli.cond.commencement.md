since: 1.0.0

We issue this policy before the developer applies for the occupation certificate. It takes effect on the date the occupation certificate is issued, and the period of insurance starts on that date. Before that date this policy gives no cover for building work under construction; the builder's construction insurance covers that.

We issue the certificate of currency only when:

- the developer has paid the premium in full; and
- the technical inspector has issued the final technical inspection report.

If the occupation certificate is not issued within three years after we issue the certificate of currency, this policy does not take effect unless we agree in writing to extend that time. We then refund the premium, less the technical inspector's fees and the non-refundable government charges.

Once this policy has taken effect, it continues for the whole period of insurance.
