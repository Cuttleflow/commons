heading: Defects Discovered Near the End of the Period
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If you first discover a relevant defect during the period of insurance but tell us about it after the period of insurance ends, we treat it as notified during the period of insurance, as long as you tell us within the late notification period shown in the schedule.
