---
id: dli.ext.make-safe
type: extension
heading: Making the Building Safe
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We pay the reasonable cost of urgent temporary work to make the building safe, or to prevent further damage, after a possible relevant defect is discovered. You do not need our agreement before urgent work starts, but you must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable.

If we later decide that the defect is not a relevant defect, we still pay the reasonable cost of urgent work done before we told you our decision, up to the make-safe limit shown in the schedule.
