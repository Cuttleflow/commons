heading: Rectification Costs
since: 1.0.0

---

The rectification costs of a relevant defect are the reasonable costs of repairing, replacing or redesigning the building element so that it is free of the relevant defect and complies with the Building Code of Australia and the approved plans, using materials and methods of a quality at least equal to those the approved plans required. They include the costs [[ref:dli.ext.make-safe]], [[ref:dli.ext.investigation-costs]], [[ref:dli.ext.access-and-reinstatement]], [[ref:dli.ext.professional-fees]] and [[ref:dli.ext.building-law]] describe, and GST as [[ref:dli.pay.gst]] sets out.
