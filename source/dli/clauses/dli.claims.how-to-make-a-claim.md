since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

1. If the defect creates a risk to safety, make the building safe and tell us straight away. [[ref:dli.ext.make-safe]] covers the reasonable cost.
2. Contact us at the details shown in the schedule, or ask the strata managing agent or your broker to contact us.
3. Tell us what the defect is, where it is, when it was found and what damage it has caused, and send photographs and any reports you have.
4. Give us, and the people we appoint, access to inspect the defect.
5. We will acknowledge the claim, inspect and decide within the time limits in [[ref:dli.pay.claims-timeframes]], and keep you informed.
6. If you disagree with our decision, you may use [[ref:dli.cond.technical-disputes]] or [[ref:dli.cond.disputes-and-complaints]].
