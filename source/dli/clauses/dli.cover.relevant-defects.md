id: dli.cover.relevant-defects
type: cover
heading: Relevant Defects in the Common Property
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

We will pay the rectification costs of a relevant defect in a building element of the common property that is first discovered during the period of insurance, and that you tell us about as [[ref:dli.obl.tell-us-about-defects]] requires.

We pay whether or not the relevant defect has caused damage yet. We also pay the cost of repairing physical damage to the building, including the fixtures and finishes in a lot, that the relevant defect causes.

We pay whether or not anyone was at fault, and whether or not the builder, the developer or anyone else is liable, insolvent, deregistered or can be found.

We pay as [[ref:dli.section.payment]] sets out.
