This is the first release of the Meridian Decennial Liability Insurance (New South Wales) wording. It is drafted for the change of 1 December 2026, when new Class 2 apartment buildings with more than two residential lots no longer need home building compensation cover in New South Wales, and developers of strata-titled buildings must lodge a strata building bond or take out decennial liability insurance.

- Drafted to s.211AA of the Strata Schemes Management Act 2015 (NSW) and cll.55A to 55C of the Strata Schemes Management Regulation 2016 (NSW): taken out by the developer in favour of the owners corporation, ten years from the occupation certificate, strict liability, first resort and not cancellable. Relevant defect and building element take their statutory meanings, so a change to the statute changes the cover.
- A read-down condition makes any term that would stop the policy meeting s.211AA give way to the statute.
- The owners corporation is protected from the developer's conduct: non-disclosure, misrepresentation, fraud, breach and non-payment of premium after the certificate of currency do not reduce its claim. The insurer's remedies are against the developer.
- Deliberate positions for underwriter confirmation: make-safe costs are paid even where the defect turns out not to be a relevant defect, up to a limit; one excess applies to related defects and none to make-safe costs; rectification work the insurer arranges is guaranteed for six years or the rest of the period; the sum insured is the final contract value even if the developer declared less; and an expert's decision on a technical dispute binds the insurer but not the owners corporation.
- Claims time limits: acknowledge in 10 business days, inspect in 20 (2 for safety risks), decide in 60 days, scope or offer in 90 days.
- No exclusion for combustible cladding or non-compliant building products, because the use of a building product in contravention of the Building Products (Safety) Act 2017 (NSW) is a relevant defect. The platform refers these risks at underwriting.
- Endorsements E01 to E05 with platform triggers: alternative accommodation, waterproofing in lots, outstanding defects at completion, mixed-use buildings, and modular and prefabricated construction.
- Nine WP REVIEW tags: the statutory text, which buildings may use decennial liability insurance, retail or wholesale distribution, whether each exclusion is consistent with strict liability, the read-down clause, the owners corporation's protection from the insurer's statutory remedies, cancellation under the Insurance Contracts Act, the outstanding defects endorsement, and the library-wide sanctions regimes.
- Not yet accepted by the Secretary as a form of decennial liability insurance.
