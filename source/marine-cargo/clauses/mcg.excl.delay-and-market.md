heading: Delay and Loss of Market
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.2.0
status: draft
---

This policy does not insure loss of market, loss of profit, or any loss caused by delay, even if the delay is caused by a risk insured, except:

- general average and salvage charges;
- the costs paid under [[ref:mcg.ext.transit-interruption-costs]]; and
- the extra expense paid under [[ref:mcg.end.e11]], where it applies.
