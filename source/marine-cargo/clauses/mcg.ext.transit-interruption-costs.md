heading: Transit Interruption Costs
since: 1.2.0
status: draft

If an insured transit ends or is interrupted at a place other than its destination because of loss of or damage to the goods that this policy insures, or because of an accident to the conveyance that this policy would insure if it had caused loss of or damage to the goods, we pay the reasonable costs you incur:

- to unload, store, sort, repack or recondition the goods that are not lost, so that they can continue;
- to reload the goods and forward them to their destination, or to return them to you; and
- of any other extra expense needed to complete the transit by the most suitable means available.

This extension applies to every kind of transit shown in the schedule: import transits, export transits and inland transits, whatever cover level applies.

The most we pay for any one transit is the amount shown in the schedule for this extension. If none is shown, it is 10% of the limit any one conveyance or $50,000, whichever is less. We pay it in addition to the limit any one conveyance. No excess applies to these costs, other than the excess that applies to the loss or damage itself.

We do not pay the same cost twice. Where the Institute Clauses that apply to the transit pay forwarding charges, we pay under them first, and this extension pays only the costs they do not pay. This extension does not pay general average or salvage charges, which the Institute Clauses cover, or the extra expense paid under [[ref:mcg.end.e11]] where it applies. The exclusions that apply to the transit apply to this extension.
