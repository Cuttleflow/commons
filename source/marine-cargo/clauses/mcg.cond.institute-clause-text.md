The Institute Clauses are published by the Lloyd's Market Association and the International Underwriting Association of London, which own their copyright. They form part of this policy by reference to their number and date. They are not part of this wording's text and are not licensed under [[ref:mcg.licence-notice|heading]].

We give you a copy of each Institute clause that applies at or before the start of the policy period, and again at any time on request. This includes the Institute Cargo Clauses (A) and (B) whose exclusions [[ref:mcg.cover.inland-transit]] applies to inland transits as if they were written there.
