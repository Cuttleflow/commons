A transit, or the part of a transit, that is carried by sea. This includes carriage by sea between Australian ports (coastal carriage) and any land carriage that is incidental to the sea carriage and is part of the same transit. A Sea Transit may be an import transit, an export transit or an inland transit.

The law treats a Sea Transit as marine insurance. [[ref:mcg.cond.statutory-framework]] explains the effect.
