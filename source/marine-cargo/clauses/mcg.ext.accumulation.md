If, through circumstances beyond your control, goods insured by this policy accumulate at any one place in the course of transit to a value above the limit any one conveyance, we cover them up to the accumulation limit shown in the schedule or, if none is shown, up to twice the limit any one conveyance. Examples are goods held at a port or depot after a delay, a missed connection or a carrier's failure.

You must tell us as soon as you know of the accumulation. We charge an additional premium on the value above the limit any one conveyance, at the accumulation rate shown in the schedule. If the schedule shows no accumulation rate, the rate is the annual rate for the transit, applied to that value for the days the goods were accumulated.
