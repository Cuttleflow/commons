If the schedule shows the War and Strikes section, the following apply:

| Transit | Institute Clauses |
|---|---|
| Import and export transits by sea, or by sea and land | Institute War Clauses (Cargo) CL385 1/1/09 and Institute Strikes Clauses (Cargo) CL386 1/1/09 |
| Import and export transits by air, or by air and land | Institute War Clauses (Air Cargo) (excluding sendings by Post) CL388 1/1/09 and Institute Strikes Clauses (Air Cargo) CL389 1/1/09 |
| Sendings by post, for import and export transits | Institute War Clauses (Sendings by Post) CL390 1/1/09 |
| Inland transits | Institute Strikes Clauses (Cargo) CL386 1/1/09, as if the inland transit were a transit those clauses insure |

War cover does not apply to inland transits, and war cover for goods on land outside Australia applies only as the Institute War Clauses allow.

For import and export transits, you or we may cancel the cover under this section by giving the other 7 days' written notice, or 48 hours' written notice for strikes cover on transits to or from the United States of America. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the notice period and does not affect a transit that started before then. For inland transits, the cover under this section ends only as [[ref:mcg.cond.cancellation]] allows.

Each of these clauses applies as amended by [[ref:mcg.cond.law-and-practice]].
