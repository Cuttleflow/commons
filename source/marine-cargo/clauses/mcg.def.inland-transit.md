heading: Inland Transit
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

A transit of goods that begins and ends in Australia, by road, by rail, by sea between Australian ports, by air within Australia, or by post or courier within Australia. The part of an inland transit that is carried by sea is also a [[ref:mcg.def.sea-transit|heading]], and the rest is a [[ref:mcg.def.non-sea-transit|heading]].
