When we pay a claim, we take over your rights to recover from a carrier, bailee or any other person who is responsible for the loss or damage, as far as the law allows and up to the amount we paid. You must give us the help we reasonably ask for to bring a recovery, at our cost.

Anything recovered from another person, after the reasonable costs of recovery, is applied first to make you whole for the part of your loss that we did not pay, including your excess, and then to reimburse us. A recovery under a certificate of insurance is dealt with in the same way for the person named in the certificate.

We do not take over a right against a person who is insured under this policy, unless that person caused the loss deliberately.
