since: 1.0.0
status: draft
trigger:
  questions:
  - qs.mcg.goods.machinery
  rules:
  - when: qs.mcg.goods.machinery == yes
    action: apply
  reason: Applies because the goods include machinery or equipment made up of parts.
---

The Institute Replacement Clause CL372 1/12/08 applies to machinery, equipment and other goods made up of parts. Where a part is lost or damaged, we pay the cost of replacing or repairing the part, including forwarding and refitting, but not more than the insured value of the whole item.

Schedule variables: none.
