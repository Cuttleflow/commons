heading: How to Make a Claim
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

1. Take reasonable steps to protect the goods from further loss or damage (see [[ref:mcg.obl.reduce-loss]]).
2. Contact your broker, or contact us or the surveyor or agent shown in the schedule or on the certificate, as soon as you can.
3. Make a written claim on the carrier, and keep the delivery receipt, the contract of carriage and the packing (see [[ref:mcg.obl.rights-against-carriers]]).
4. Tell the police about any theft.
5. Give us the invoice, the packing list, the bill of lading, air waybill or consignment note, any survey report, and photographs of the damage.
6. We will acknowledge your claim, keep you informed, and tell you our decision.
