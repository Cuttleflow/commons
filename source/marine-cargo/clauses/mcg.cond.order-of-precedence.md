heading: How the Parts of This Policy Fit Together
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Where the parts of this policy deal with the same matter differently, they apply in this order: the schedule, then an endorsement, then this wording, then the Institute Clauses.

An Institute clause applies only to the transits and cover level to which this wording, the schedule or an endorsement applies it. Where this wording amends an Institute clause, the amendment is made by this wording; the published text of the clause is not changed.
