heading: Preserving Rights Against Carriers
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

You must take reasonable steps to preserve the rights of recovery against a carrier, bailee or other person who may be responsible for loss or damage, including:

- making a written claim on them within the time their contract of carriage or the law allows;
- not giving a clean receipt for goods that are damaged or short; and
- not agreeing to release them from liability or to limit their liability beyond the terms of their usual contract of carriage, without our agreement.

This obligation applies to all transits, and is in addition to the Duty of Assured Clause in the Institute Clauses for import and export transits. It is an obligation, not a warranty. If you do not meet it, we may reduce our payment only by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our rights of recovery were prejudiced, as [[ref:mcg.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] sets out.
