heading: Fraudulent Claims
since: 1.0.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

If a claim is made fraudulently, we may refuse to pay it. For a Non-Sea Transit, s.56 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) applies. For a Sea Transit, the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) and the general law apply.
