heading: Fraudulent Claims
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If a claim is made fraudulently, we may refuse to pay it. For inland transits, s.56 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) applies. For import and export transits, the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) applies.
