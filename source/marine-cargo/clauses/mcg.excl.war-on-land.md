---
id: mcg.excl.war-on-land
type: exclusion
heading: War on Land
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

This policy does not insure loss or damage during an inland transit caused by war, civil war, revolution, rebellion, insurrection or civil strife arising from them, or any hostile act by or against a belligerent power, or by capture, seizure, arrest, restraint or detainment arising from them.
