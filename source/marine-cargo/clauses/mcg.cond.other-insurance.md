If a loss covered by this policy is also covered by another insurance policy, we will not refuse or reduce our payment because of that other insurance. We may seek contribution from the other insurer as the law allows. This does not apply where the schedule or an endorsement states that this policy is excess of a specified underlying policy, in which case this policy pays only after the limit of that specified policy is exhausted.

This applies to goods that are also insured by a buyer, a seller or any other person, including goods insured under a certificate of insurance issued under this policy and goods that a buyer also insures on its own policy. Where goods are insured both under this policy and under a policy that a buyer or seller holds for the same transit, each insurer pays its rateable share of the loss, in proportion to the amount each has insured, and neither insurer may refuse to pay because the other policy exists.
