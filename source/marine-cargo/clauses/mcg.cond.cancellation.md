**You** may cancel this policy at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired period of insurance on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges, as [[ref:mcg.pay.premium-adjustment]] adjusts it.

**We** may cancel this policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you written notice as that Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired period on a pro-rata basis. This applies to a [[ref:mcg.def.non-sea-transit|heading]].

For a [[ref:mcg.def.sea-transit|heading]], the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) does not apply of its own force. We may cancel this policy as it applies to Sea Transits by giving you 30 days' written notice. The War and Strikes section may be cancelled on shorter notice as [[ref:mcg.cover.war-and-strikes]] sets out.

Cancellation does not affect a transit that started before it took effect. That transit stays insured until it ends under [[ref:mcg.cover.duration]].

When this policy is cancelled, we adjust the premium under [[ref:mcg.pay.premium-adjustment]] on the annual sendings for transits that started before cancellation. We keep at least the proportion of the minimum premium for the time the policy was in force.
