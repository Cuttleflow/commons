You may cancel this policy at any time by telling us in writing.

We may cancel this policy:

- for inland transits, only on a ground allowed by the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), by giving you written notice as s.59 of that Act requires; and
- for import and export transits, by giving you 30 days' written notice. The War and Strikes section may be cancelled on shorter notice as [[ref:mcg.cover.war-and-strikes]] sets out.

Cancellation does not affect a transit that started before it took effect. That transit stays insured until it ends under [[ref:mcg.cover.duration]].

When this policy is cancelled, we adjust the premium under [[ref:mcg.pay.premium-adjustment]] on the annual sendings for transits that started before cancellation, and we keep at least the proportion of the minimum premium for the time the policy was in force.
