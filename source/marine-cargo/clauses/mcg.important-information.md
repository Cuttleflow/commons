**Who issues this policy.** The insurer, its Australian Financial Services Licence and its contact details are shown in the schedule. Where the insurer subscribes to the General Insurance Code of Practice, the schedule says so and which parts of this policy the Code applies to.

**Who is insured.** You are the business named in the schedule. A person who holds a certificate of insurance issued under this policy is insured for the transit the certificate describes, as [[ref:mcg.ext.certificates]] sets out.

**Your duty when you apply.** The law sets a different duty depending on how the goods are carried. This policy treats each transit according to its legal character: a Sea Transit (carriage by sea, including coastal carriage between Australian ports) is marine insurance, and a Non-Sea Transit (carriage by air, post, courier, road or rail) is not. [[ref:mcg.cond.statutory-framework]] explains the effect.

- *Sea Transits* are governed by the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth). Before the policy is entered into, you must disclose to us every circumstance you know that is material to the risk: every circumstance that would influence a prudent insurer in fixing the premium or deciding whether to take the risk. You do not need to disclose a circumstance that reduces the risk, that we know or are presumed to know, or that we waive. If you do not disclose a material circumstance, or you misrepresent one, that Act may allow us to avoid the contract as it applies to Sea Transits.
- *Non-Sea Transits* are governed by the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Before the policy is entered into, renewed, extended, varied or reinstated, you have the duty of disclosure in s.21 of that Act: you must tell us every matter you know, or that a reasonable person in your circumstances could be expected to know, that is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is common knowledge, that we know or should know as an insurer, or that we waive. If you do not comply, we may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy, only as that Act allows.

We will not use a failure to disclose, or a misrepresentation, that relates to Sea Transits to avoid this policy as it applies to Non-Sea Transits, and we will not use one that relates to Non-Sea Transits to avoid this policy as it applies to Sea Transits.

**What to tell us during the policy period.** [[ref:mcg.obl.tell-us-about-changes]] lists the changes you must tell us about, such as carrying goods of a new kind or by a new route.

**Who This Policy Is For.** This wording is written for businesses and professionals. Some buyers, such as small businesses, may be retail clients under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). Where a buyer is a retail client, the issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement.

**Commission and fees.** The schedule or the quote shows any commission or fee paid to your broker in connection with this policy.

**Financial Claims Scheme.** If the schedule shows the insurer is an APRA-authorised general insurer, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme if the insurer becomes insolvent. Information is available at www.fcs.gov.au.
