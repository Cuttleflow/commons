We insure the goods against loss or damage during transit, for the kinds of transit shown in the schedule:

- for import transits and export transits, on the terms of the Institute Clauses that [[ref:mcg.cover.institute-clauses]] applies to the transit and to the cover level shown in the schedule; and
- for inland transits, on the terms of [[ref:mcg.cover.inland-transit]] at the cover level shown in the schedule.

Every transit of a kind shown in the schedule that starts during the policy period is insured automatically. You do not need to declare each transit to us. You tell us your annual sendings as [[ref:mcg.pay.premium-adjustment]] requires.

For any one conveyance, or any one place in the course of transit, we pay up to the limit any one conveyance, less the excess. [[ref:mcg.ext.accumulation]] applies where goods accumulate at a place through circumstances beyond your control. General average and salvage charges are paid in addition to the limit any one conveyance where the Institute Clauses provide for them, up to the proportion that the insured value of the goods bears to their contributory value.
