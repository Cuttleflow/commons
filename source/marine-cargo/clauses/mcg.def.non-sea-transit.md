since: 1.1.0
changed: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

Any transit, or part of a transit, that is not a Sea Transit. This includes carriage by air, by post or courier, by road and by rail, and carriage by air and land. A Non-Sea Transit may be an import transit, an export transit or an inland transit.

The Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) applies to a Non-Sea Transit. [[ref:mcg.cond.statutory-framework]] explains the effect.
