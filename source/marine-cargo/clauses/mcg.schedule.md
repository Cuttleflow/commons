(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The schedule shows:

- Your name, ABN and address
- The policy period, with its start and end dates
- The sections that apply
- The kinds of transit insured: import transits, export transits and inland transits, and for each the conveyances used and your estimate of the annual sendings
- The cover level for the Transit section: (A), (B) or (C)
- The goods insured, described by kind
- The limit any one conveyance, and the accumulation limit if it differs from [[ref:mcg.ext.accumulation]]
- The basis of valuation, where it differs from [[ref:mcg.pay.basis-of-valuation]]
- The excess, and any special excess shown for an endorsement
- Whether the premium is adjustable, the adjustment rates and the minimum premium (see [[ref:mcg.pay.premium-adjustment]])
- The premium, including any stamp duty and GST
- Any commission or fee paid to your broker
- The insurer and its licence, and the state or territory whose law governs this policy (see [[ref:mcg.cond.law-and-practice]])
- The endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
