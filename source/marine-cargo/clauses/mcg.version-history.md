This document is version-controlled. The version and date shown on the cover page and in the footer of each page govern this text. The version stated in your schedule as current at the start of the policy period applies for the whole policy period, unless your schedule or an endorsement states otherwise.

The Institute clauses that apply are identified by their number and date in [[ref:mcg.cover.institute-clauses]] and [[ref:mcg.cover.war-and-strikes]]. A later edition of an Institute clause applies only if a new version of this wording, or an endorsement, names it.
