For import transits and export transits, the following Institute Clauses apply.

| Transit | Cover level in the schedule | Institute Clauses |
|---|---|---|
| By sea, or by sea and land | (A) | Institute Cargo Clauses (A) CL382 1/1/09 |
| By sea, or by sea and land | (B) | Institute Cargo Clauses (B) CL383 1/1/09 |
| By sea, or by sea and land | (C) | Institute Cargo Clauses (C) CL384 1/1/09 |
| By air, or by air and land | Any | Institute Cargo Clauses (Air) (excluding sendings by Post) CL387 1/1/09 |
| By post or courier | Any | The clauses for the conveyance that carries the goods: Institute Cargo Clauses (Air) CL387 1/1/09 for goods carried by air, and the clauses for the cover level shown in the schedule for goods carried by sea |

The following also apply to every import transit and export transit:

- Institute Classification Clause CL354 1/1/01, for goods carried by sea;
- Institute Cargo ISM Endorsement JC98/019 1/5/98, for goods carried by sea;
- Institute Radioactive Contamination, Chemical, Biological, Bio-chemical and Electromagnetic Weapons Exclusion Clause CL370 10/11/03;
- Institute Cyber Attack Exclusion Clause CL380 10/11/03;
- Termination of Transit Clause (Terrorism) JC2009/056 1/1/09;
- Sanction Limitation and Exclusion Clause JC2010/014 11/08/10; and
- Communicable Disease Exclusion (Cargo) JC2020-011 17/04/20.

The Institute Cargo Clauses (A) are an all risks cover, subject to their exclusions. The Institute Cargo Clauses (B) and (C) cover named risks only. The Institute Cargo Clauses (Air) are an all risks cover, whatever cover level the schedule shows.

Each of these clauses applies as amended by [[ref:mcg.cond.law-and-practice]]. [[ref:mcg.cond.institute-clause-text]] explains where to find their text.
