heading: Errors and Omissions
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

An unintentional error or omission by you in describing the goods, the conveyance or the route of a transit does not prejudice your cover, provided you tell us as soon as you discover it and pay any additional premium we reasonably charge. This extension does not insure excluded goods, or a transit of a kind not shown in the schedule.
