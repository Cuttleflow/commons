The premium includes GST where it applies, under A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth). Insurance of the international transport of goods may be GST-free; the schedule shows the GST charged. Claims are paid as [[ref:mcg.pay.gst]] sets out. Tell us your entitlement to input tax credits when you make a claim.
