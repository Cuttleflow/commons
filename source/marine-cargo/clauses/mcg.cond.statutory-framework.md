Import and export transits are marine insurance, governed by the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth). Inland transits, and any other part of this policy to which the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) does not apply, are governed by the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).

To the extent the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) applies, nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies any right you have under it. Where you fail to do something this policy requires, or do something it does not allow, after the policy is entered into, s.54 of that Act applies. We will not refuse a claim only because of that act or omission. We may reduce our payment by the amount that fairly represents the extent to which our interests were prejudiced. We may refuse the claim only where the act or omission could reasonably be regarded as capable of causing or contributing to the loss, and then only as that section allows.
