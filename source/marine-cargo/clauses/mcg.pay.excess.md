We deduct the excess shown in the schedule from each claim for loss or damage from one event, or to goods on one conveyance. If an endorsement shows a special excess for the loss or damage, that excess replaces the excess shown in the schedule.

The excess does not apply to general average or salvage charges, or to a total loss of all the goods on a conveyance.
