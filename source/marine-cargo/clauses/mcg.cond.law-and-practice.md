Each Institute clause that applies to this policy is amended so that its Law and Practice clause reads: "This insurance is subject to Australian law and practice."

This policy is governed by the law of the state or territory of Australia shown in the schedule for this purpose. If none is shown, it is governed by the law of New South Wales.
