Goods are valued as follows, both for the annual sendings and for claims, unless the schedule states another basis.

| Transit | Value |
|---|---|
| Import transits | The invoice value plus freight and the cost of insurance, plus 10% |
| Export transits | The invoice value plus freight and the cost of insurance, plus 10%, or the value a certificate of insurance shows where the sale contract requires a certificate |
| Inland transits of goods sold or bought | The invoice value plus freight, plus 10% |
| Inland transits of goods you own that are not sold or bought, such as transfers between your premises | The cost of the goods to you plus freight |

The value is the agreed value of the goods for the Institute Clauses. Where an invoice is in a foreign currency, it is converted to Australian dollars at the rate on the date of the invoice. Import duty and taxes are not part of the value unless [[ref:mcg.end.e07]] applies.

For a partial loss, we pay the agreed value of the goods lost, or the proportion of the agreed value that the reduction in sound value bears to the sound value, or the reasonable cost of repair or reconditioning, whichever is less.
