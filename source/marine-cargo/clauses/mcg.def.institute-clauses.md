The clauses published by the Lloyd's Market Association and the International Underwriting Association of London, through their Joint Cargo Committee, that are named by number and date in [[ref:mcg.cover.institute-clauses]], [[ref:mcg.cover.war-and-strikes]] or an endorsement.
