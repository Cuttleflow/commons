This is the first release of the Meridian Marine Cargo wording. It is a new wording, drafted for the Commons. It does not draft cargo cover: it is a framework around the Institute Cargo Clauses, which it names by number and date.

- An annual open policy for goods in transit. Every transit of a kind shown in the schedule that starts during the policy period is insured automatically, and the premium is adjusted on the actual annual sendings.
- Two sections: Transit, and War and Strikes.
- Import and export transits are insured on the Institute Cargo Clauses (A), (B) or (C) 1/1/09 for sea and the Institute Cargo Clauses (Air) 1/1/09 for air, with the standard companion clauses: Classification, Cargo ISM, the radioactive and cyber exclusions, Termination of Transit (Terrorism), Sanction Limitation and Communicable Disease.
- Inland transits within Australia are insured on a short Cuttleflow clause that mirrors the Institute Cargo Clauses: Cover (A) all risks, and named-risk Covers (B) and (C), with the Institute exclusions applied as if written. War on land is excluded.
- War and Strikes uses the Institute War and Strikes Clauses for sea, air and post, and the Institute Strikes Clauses for inland transits.
- The Law and Practice clause of every Institute clause is amended to Australian law and practice. Governing law is fixed to the state or territory in the schedule, or New South Wales.
- The basis of valuation is CIF plus 10% for imports and exports, invoice plus freight plus 10% for inland sales, and cost plus freight for your own goods.
- Extensions: accumulation up to twice the limit any one conveyance, errors and omissions, certificates of insurance for CIF and CIP sales, and packing and labels.
- A statutory framework condition states that the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth) governs import and export transits and the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) governs inland transits, with an s.54 savings clause where that Act applies.
- The endorsement library has 10 endorsements, each with operative text, schedule variables and a platform trigger.
- The Institute Clauses are published by the Lloyd's Market Association and the International Underwriting Association of London. They are incorporated by reference and are not licensed under this wording's CC BY 4.0 licence.
