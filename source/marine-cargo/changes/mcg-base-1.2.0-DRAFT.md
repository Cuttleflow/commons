Transport and logistics extensions (Linear PLT-1202, 21 November 2026).

- Transit Interruption Costs: a new extension for every kind of transit. Where an insured loss, or an accident to the conveyance, ends or interrupts a transit before its destination, we pay the reasonable costs of unloading, storing, sorting, repacking and reconditioning the goods, forwarding them or returning them, and other extra expense to complete the transit. The default limit is 10% of the limit any one conveyance or $50,000, whichever is less, in addition to the limit. Before this version, only the Inland Transit Cover paid reloading and forwarding costs, within the 10% it shares with fire-fighting and debris removal.
- Inland Transit Cover: reloading and forwarding costs move to Transit Interruption Costs. Fire-fighting and debris removal keep their 10% of the limit any one conveyance. For a limit any one conveyance above $500,000, forwarding costs now have the $50,000 cap of the new extension, separate from fire-fighting and debris removal.
- Delay and Loss of Market: the exclusion no longer applies to Transit Interruption Costs or to the extra expense paid under E11.
- E11 Delay Extra Expense: a new optional endorsement. It pays extra expense to reduce the effect of a delay caused by insured loss or damage, such as faster freight or replacement goods from another supplier, within 90 days, up to $25,000 any one loss and $100,000 in the period, with a $1,000 excess. It is priced as a loading.
- E12 Stock in Storage: a new optional endorsement for stock at named third-party warehouses ($250,000 a location) and at your premises ($100,000 a location, in excess of a named property policy), $500,000 in total, natural catastrophe at 50% of a location limit. It is priced as a loading by the value of stock in storage. More than $1,000,000 at one location, or more than 3 locations, refers.
- Schedule and Endorsement Library: the new extension limit and endorsements are added.
- Questions: five new questions for E11 and E12.
