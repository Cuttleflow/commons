Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026.

- Marine Insurance Act and Insurance Contracts Act: the policy now sorts transits by the legal character of the carriage, not by direction. Carriage by sea, including coastal carriage between Australian ports, is marine insurance under the Marine Insurance Act 1909 (Cth). Carriage by air, post, courier, road and rail is governed by the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Each Act applies only to its own part of the policy.
- Sea Transit and Non-Sea Transit: two new definitions. The definitions of Import Transit, Export Transit and Inland Transit now point to them.
- Important Information: the duty-of-disclosure text now follows the sea and non-sea split. It promises that a disclosure failure on one part will not be used to avoid the other part. The paragraph on retail or wholesale buyers is replaced by the library's standard statement of who the policy is for.
- Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): a new clause with the library's standard section 54 savings text. It also applies the same treatment by agreement to Sea Transits for the obligations and conditions written in this wording. The old section 54 paragraph in the statutory framework condition is removed.
- Obligations and conditions: Telling Us About Changes, Preserving Rights Against Carriers and endorsements E01, E03, E04 and E09 now say that they are not warranties and what follows if you do not meet them.
- Other Insurance: a new clause. Other insurance does not reduce our payment, we may seek contribution, and where a buyer or seller also insures the transit each insurer pays its rateable share.
- Recoveries: a new clause. It sets out our right to take over your recovery rights and the order in which recoveries are applied.
- Cancellation: replaced with the library's standard text. We may cancel cover for Non-Sea Transits only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows. For Sea Transits, 30 days' written notice.
- Sanctions: replaced with the library's standard text.
- Fraudulent Claims: now refers to Sea Transits and Non-Sea Transits.
- Accumulation: the accumulation limit shown in the schedule applies first, and otherwise twice the limit any one conveyance. An additional premium is now always charged on the value above the limit, at the schedule rate or, if none is shown, at the annual rate for the days of accumulation. Default set for underwriter confirmation.
- Text of the Institute Clauses: we must give you a copy of each Institute clause at or before the start of the policy period, including the clauses whose exclusions apply to inland transits.
- Licence and Trademark Notice: the assumption about which Act applies now follows the sea and non-sea split.
