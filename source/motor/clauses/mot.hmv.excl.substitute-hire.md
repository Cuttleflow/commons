since: 1.1.0
status: draft
---

In addition to [[ref:mot.section.general-exclusions]], we do not pay under [[ref:mot.hmv.section.substitute-hire|heading]] for:

- any day for which we pay a hire vehicle benefit in [[ref:mot.hmv.ext.additional-benefits]] or a downtime benefit in [[ref:mot.hmv.cover.downtime]] for the same vehicle. Where more than one could apply, you choose which one we pay; or
- hire of a vehicle that you or a related business owns.
