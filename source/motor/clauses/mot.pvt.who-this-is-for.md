This Policy is for individuals insuring a car used mainly for private purposes. It is a consumer insurance contract, and the duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation in [[ref:mot.pvt.cond.duty-to-take-reasonable-care]] applies instead of the duty of disclosure.

This Policy is a retail product. Before it is issued, the issuer must give you a Product Disclosure Statement and must have made a target market determination for it, and the General Insurance Code of Practice applies. This wording is not a Product Disclosure Statement.

This Policy is a prescribed contract for motor vehicle insurance under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) and the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017 (Cth). Where it gives less cover than standard cover, the issuer must clearly inform you in writing of each difference before the contract is entered into; otherwise the standard cover applies.
