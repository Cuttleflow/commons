This Policy is for individuals insuring a car used mainly for private purposes. It is a consumer insurance contract, and the duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation in [[ref:mot.pvt.cond.duty-to-take-reasonable-care]] applies instead of the duty of disclosure.

This wording is designed for retail clients. An issuer using it must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available, as the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) requires. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement. The General Insurance Code of Practice applies.

This Policy is a prescribed contract for motor vehicle insurance under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) and the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017 (Cth). Where it gives less cover than standard cover, the issuer must clearly inform you in writing of each difference before the contract is entered into; otherwise the standard cover applies. An issuer must check any change to a Default Schedule Value against the list of differences it gives you.
