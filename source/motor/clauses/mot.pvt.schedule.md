(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The Schedule shows:

- The named insured and the regular driver
- The Period of Insurance
- Your car: registration, make, model and year, garaging address, Permitted use, cover level, value basis and Sum insured
- The listed drivers, if any
- The Excesses, and your no claim bonus rating
- The third party property damage limit and supplementary bodily injury sub-limit
- Sub-limits and other values that differ from the defaults in [[ref:mot.pvt.appendix.default-schedule-values]]
- The premium, with stamp duty, levies and GST
- The State or Territory whose law governs this Policy (see [[ref:mot.cond.governing-law-and-jurisdiction]])
- The Endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
- Interested parties, such as a financier
