[[ref:mot.cover.third-party-property-damage]] extends to liability arising from a trailer, including a Non-owned trailer, while it is attached to, or has accidentally become detached from, a Powered vehicle. Loss of or damage to a Non-owned trailer itself is not covered by [[ref:mot.section.liability|heading]]. [[ref:mot.hmv.section.non-owned-trailers|heading]] covers it where the Schedule shows that section applies.
