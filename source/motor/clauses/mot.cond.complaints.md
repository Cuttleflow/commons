If you have a complaint about this Policy or about a claim, contact us first. We will acknowledge your complaint within one business day and give you a written response within 30 calendar days. If you are not satisfied with our response, or we do not respond in time, you may take the complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), www.afca.org.au, 1800 931 678, subject to its terms of reference. AFCA's service is free to you.
