Before you enter into, renew, extend, vary or reinstate this Policy, you have a duty under s 21 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) to tell us every matter that you know, or that a reasonable person in the circumstances could be expected to know, is relevant to our decision whether to insure you and on what terms. You do not need to tell us anything that reduces the risk, that is of common knowledge, that we know or should know in the ordinary course of our business, or that we have told you we do not need to know.

If you do not comply with this duty, we may be entitled to reduce our liability for a claim or to cancel the Policy, as the Act allows. If the non-disclosure is fraudulent, we may also be entitled to avoid the Policy from its beginning.
