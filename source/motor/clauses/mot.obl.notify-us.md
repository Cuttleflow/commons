---
id: mot.obl.notify-us
type: obligation
heading: Tell Us About an Incident
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us as soon as reasonably practicable after any accident, theft or other event that may give rise to a claim. Give us when and where it happened; the registration and description of each vehicle involved; the names and contact details of the drivers, passengers and witnesses; what happened and what was damaged; the other party's insurance details if known; and a copy of any police report. Report theft and malicious damage to the police as soon as reasonably practicable.
