id: mot.hmv.def.permitted-use
type: definition
heading: Permitted Use
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The use shown in the Schedule for each Insured vehicle. Unless the Schedule says otherwise, Permitted use is business use, including carrying goods for hire or reward, and private use. It does not include carrying passengers for hire, fare or reward, or hiring the vehicle out to someone else without a driver you provide.
