In addition to [[ref:mot.section.general-exclusions]], we do not pay under [[ref:mot.section.liability|heading]] for liability:

- for damage to property owned by, leased to, or in the custody or control of an Insured person, including a Non-owned trailer and any Load, other than a Substitute vehicle and employees' and visitors' vehicles in a car park you operate as part of your business;
- for death or bodily injury, except as provided by [[ref:mot.ext.supplementary-bodily-injury]];
- that is covered, or is required by law to be covered, by workers' compensation insurance, or that arises under an industrial award or agreement;
- arising from the carriage of Dangerous goods in breach of the law that applies to their carriage;
- arising from the use of a vehicle as a Tool of trade (road risk while the vehicle is driven as transport remains covered);
- arising from pollution or contamination, unless it results from a sudden, identifiable and unintended event that happens during the Period of Insurance, such as a collision or overturning that spills fuel, oil or Load (liability arising from Dangerous goods carried in compliance with the law remains subject to the Dangerous goods sub-limit);
- assumed under a contract, except to the extent you would have been liable without it; or
- for fines or penalties, or aggravated, punitive or exemplary damages.
