Where an Insured vehicle can be economically repaired, we may pay you the reasonable cost of repair, or arrange the repair. [[ref:mot.pay.choice-of-repairer]] applies to who repairs the vehicle. We do not use second-hand or non-genuine parts without your consent, except where the vehicle is more than five years old and genuine parts are not reasonably available. We will not pay for an improvement in the vehicle's condition beyond its condition before the loss, except where the only reasonable repair is with new parts.
