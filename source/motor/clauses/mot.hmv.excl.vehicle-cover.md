since: 1.0.0

---

In addition to [[ref:mot.section.general-exclusions]], we do not pay under [[ref:mot.section.vehicle-cover|heading]] for:

- mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdown, failure, wear and tear, rust or corrosion, unless caused by an insured event;
- damage to tyres by road punctures, cuts or bursts, unless other insured damage to the vehicle happens at the same time;
- loss of value or depreciation after a repair;
- loss of use, loss of income or any other consequential loss, except the benefits in [[ref:mot.hmv.ext.additional-benefits]] and the cover in [[ref:mot.hmv.section.downtime|heading]];
- loss of or damage to a Load, which this Policy does not insure;
- loss of or damage to a Non-owned trailer, except under [[ref:mot.hmv.section.non-owned-trailers|heading]]; or
- loss of or damage to a vehicle taken by a person who obtained it by deception or a dishonoured payment.
