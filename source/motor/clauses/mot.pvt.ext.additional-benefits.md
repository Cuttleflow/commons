These benefits are paid in addition to the Sum insured, for the cover levels shown, up to the limits shown in the Schedule or, if none is shown, in [[ref:mot.pvt.appendix.default-schedule-values]]. No Excess applies to them unless the Schedule says otherwise.

| Benefit | Cover levels | What we pay |
|---|---|---|
| Hire car after theft | Comprehensive; Third party, fire and theft | The reasonable cost of hiring a similar car, per day, until your car is recovered or the claim is settled |
| Hire car after accident | Comprehensive | The reasonable cost of hiring a similar car, per day, until the repair is complete, where the driver was Not at fault and the other party is identified |
| Towing | Comprehensive; Third party, fire and theft | The reasonable cost of moving your car to the nearest repairer or a safe place |
| Emergency repairs | Comprehensive | The reasonable cost of repairs needed to make your car safe to drive home or to a repairer |
| Windscreen and window glass | Comprehensive | Replacement or repair of broken glass, with no Excess |
| Personal effects | Comprehensive | Personal effects in your car lost or damaged in the same Occurrence, excluding money and jewellery |
| Locks and keys | Comprehensive; Third party, fire and theft | Replacing or recoding locks and keys after the keys are stolen |
| Child seats and restraints | Comprehensive | Replacing child seats and restraints damaged in the Occurrence |
| Emergency travel and accommodation | Comprehensive | Reasonable travel and accommodation costs where your car cannot be driven after loss or damage more than the distance shown in the Schedule from home |
| Trailer while attached | Comprehensive | Loss of or damage to a trailer you own while it is attached to your car |
