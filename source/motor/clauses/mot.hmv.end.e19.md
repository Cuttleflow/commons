This Endorsement applies to each Combination shown in the Schedule as a multi-trailer combination: a B-double, B-triple, road train or other Combination of a Powered vehicle and more than one trailer. For each of them:

- each trailer in the Combination must be an Insured vehicle or a Non-owned trailer;
- the multi-trailer excess shown in the Schedule applies, in addition to the basic excess, to a claim arising from an Occurrence involving the Combination; and
- you must hold the access approvals, permits or notices that the Heavy Vehicle National Law, or the law of the State or Territory, requires for that Combination on the route travelled. Under [[ref:mot.cond.insurance-contracts-act]], we may reduce a claim because of a failure to hold them only to the extent that the failure caused or contributed to the loss.

Schedule variables: multi-trailer combinations; multi-trailer excess.
