id: mot.def.not-at-fault
type: definition
heading: Not at Fault
since: 3.1.0
status: draft
---

A driver is Not at fault for an Occurrence where, on the evidence reasonably available to us, the driver did not cause or contribute to it. We decide this reasonably and in good faith, and we give you our reasons in writing. If you disagree, you may ask us to review the decision, and you may complain under [[ref:mot.cond.complaints]].
