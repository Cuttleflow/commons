---
id: mot.pvt.excl.hire-fare-or-reward
type: exclusion
heading: Hire, Fare or Reward
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Loss, damage or liability while the vehicle is used for hire, fare or reward, including rideshare and food delivery for a platform, unless the Schedule shows it as Permitted use or an Endorsement covers it. Sharing the cost of a trip with passengers, where you make no profit, is not use for hire, fare or reward.
