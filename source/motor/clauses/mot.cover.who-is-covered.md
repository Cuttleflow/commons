since: 3.0.0

Each of the following is an Insured person under this section: you; any person driving, using or in charge of an Insured vehicle with your permission; any passenger in, or getting into or out of, an Insured vehicle; and your employer or principal, where an Insured vehicle is being used in their business with your consent. Each Insured person is covered as if a separate policy had been issued to them, but our total liability for each Occurrence does not exceed the limits shown in the Schedule.
