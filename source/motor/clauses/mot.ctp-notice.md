This Policy is not compulsory third party (CTP) insurance. CTP is a separate statutory insurance, arranged through each State or Territory scheme, that covers liability for death or bodily injury caused by a motor vehicle. You must keep CTP, or registration that includes it, in force for every registrable Insured vehicle. [[ref:mot.ext.supplementary-bodily-injury]] gives limited cover only where no CTP or statutory scheme responds.
