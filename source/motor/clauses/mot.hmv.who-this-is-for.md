This Policy is designed for owner-drivers and small transport operators: businesses that own, lease or use heavy rigid trucks and prime movers, and the trailers they tow, to carry their own goods or goods for others. It is not designed for buses, mobile plant or fleets insured on a fleet basis, which are insured under other wordings.

Cover for a vehicle with a Carrying capacity of more than 2 tonnes is not a motor vehicle insurance product under the Corporations Regulations 2001 (Cth), so it is not provided to a retail client on that account. Cover for a vehicle with a Carrying capacity of 2 tonnes or less, such as a light commercial vehicle insured with the trucks, may be provided to a retail client where the buyer is an individual or a small business. Where any part of this Policy is provided to a retail client, the issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement, make a Target Market Determination available and meet the General Insurance Code of Practice. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement. This Policy is intended to be entered into for business purposes, so the duty of disclosure in [[ref:mot.cond.duty-of-disclosure]] applies. If it is a consumer insurance contract, that clause says which duty applies instead. [WP REVIEW: confirm whether an owner-driver who is a sole trader or partnership is a party to a consumer insurance contract under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), and so which duty applies.]

Where the insured or one of the insureds is a natural person, cover for a vehicle with a Carrying capacity of 2 tonnes or less may be a prescribed contract for motor vehicle insurance under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) and the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017 (Cth). The issuer must then clearly inform the insured in writing, before the contract is entered into, of each way in which this Policy differs from standard cover for that vehicle, and must check any change to a Default Schedule Value against that list.
