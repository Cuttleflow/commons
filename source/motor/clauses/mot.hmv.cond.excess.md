The Excesses shown in the Schedule apply to each claim, for each Insured vehicle, as follows:

| Excess | When it applies |
|---|---|
| Basic excess | Every claim, unless this condition says no excess applies or another excess applies instead |
| Age excess | In addition, where the driver at the time of the loss was under the age shown in the Schedule |
| Inexperienced driver excess | In addition, where the driver had held a licence for that class of vehicle for less than the period shown in the Schedule |
| Combination driver excess | Instead of the age excess and the inexperienced driver excess, where the vehicle was a prime mover or part of a Combination and the driver was under the age, or had held a licence for that class of vehicle for less than the period, shown in the Schedule for this excess |
| Unlisted driver excess | In addition, where the Schedule shows named drivers and the driver was not one of them |
| Outside radius excess | In addition, where the Occurrence happened outside the Radius of operation |
| Tipping excess | In addition, for loss of or damage to an Insured vehicle that happened while its tipping body, hoist, ram or stabiliser legs were raised or extended |
| Non-owned trailer excess | For each Non-owned trailer, instead of the basic excess, on a claim under [[ref:mot.hmv.section.non-owned-trailers|heading]] |
| Voluntary excess | In addition, where you chose a higher excess for a lower premium |

No excess applies to a claim under [[ref:mot.hmv.section.downtime|heading]], to a claim for windscreen or window glass only on a vehicle with a Gross vehicle mass of 4.5 tonnes or less, or where the driver of the Insured vehicle was Not at fault and you give us the name and address of the person responsible. Where more than one Insured vehicle is damaged in the same Occurrence, including a Powered vehicle and a trailer in the same Combination, you pay one basic excess (the highest that applies), plus any additional excess for the driver.
