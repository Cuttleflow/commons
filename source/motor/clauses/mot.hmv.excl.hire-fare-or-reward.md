---
id: mot.hmv.excl.hire-fare-or-reward
type: exclusion
heading: Hire, Fare or Reward
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Loss, damage or liability while the vehicle is used to carry passengers for hire, fare or reward, including rideshare, or while it is hired out to someone else without a driver you provide, unless the Schedule shows that use as Permitted use or an Endorsement covers it. Carrying goods for hire or reward, including as a subcontractor to another carrier, is not excluded by this clause.
