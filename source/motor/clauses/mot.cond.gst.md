heading: GST
since: 3.0.0

Where you are entitled to an input tax credit for the GST on a payment we make, we reduce the payment by that entitlement. You must tell us the extent of your entitlement when you make a claim. Limits and Sums insured include GST unless the Schedule says otherwise.
