heading: Tell Us About Changes
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us during the Period of Insurance when you change your car; when the address where it is normally kept overnight changes; when it is modified; when someone not shown in the Schedule starts to drive it regularly; or when you start to use it for business, rideshare or delivery. We may change the premium or terms from the date of the change.
