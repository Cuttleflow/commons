Tell us during the Period of Insurance when you change your car; when the address where it is normally kept overnight changes; when it is modified; when someone not shown in the Schedule starts to drive it regularly; or when you start to use it for business, rideshare or delivery.

We may change the premium or terms because of a change you tell us about, but only by giving you written notice of the new premium or terms. They apply only from the date stated in the notice, which is not earlier than the notice period shown in [[ref:mot.pvt.appendix.default-schedule-values]] after we send it. If you do not accept them, you may cancel the Policy as [[ref:mot.cond.cancellation]] allows, we will refund the premium for the unexpired Period of Insurance on a pro-rata basis from the date of cancellation, and the new premium or terms do not apply to you.
