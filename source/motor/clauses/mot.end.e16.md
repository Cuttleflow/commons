For Insured vehicles used as tow trucks or recovery vehicles, we pay for loss of or damage to a vehicle being towed, carried or recovered by them, for which you are legally liable, up to the amount shown in the Schedule for each Occurrence. For that property, the exclusion in [[ref:mot.excl.liability]] for damage to property in the custody or control of an Insured person, and the exclusion for use of a vehicle as a Tool of trade (where the use is towing, winching, loading or carrying the property), do not apply. Every other exclusion in that clause continues to apply. The driver must hold any tow truck licence or authority the law requires.

Schedule variables: goods in care limit; excess.
