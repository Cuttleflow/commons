id: mot.ext.supplementary-bodily-injury
type: extension
heading: Supplementary Bodily Injury
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

We will pay the compensation an Insured person becomes legally liable to pay for the death of, or bodily injury to, another person caused by an Occurrence during the Period of Insurance arising from the use of an Insured vehicle, but only where the liability is not covered, in whole or in part, by any compulsory third party insurance or statutory accident compensation scheme, and the vehicle was registered, and any required compulsory third party insurance was in force, at the time (or the vehicle was not required to be registered). The most we pay is the supplementary bodily injury sub-limit shown in the Schedule for each Occurrence, which is part of, not in addition to, the third party property damage limit.

We do not pay under this extension any amount a scheme has paid or would have paid had the registration, insurance and claim requirements of the scheme been met; death of or injury to the driver of the Insured vehicle; or fines, penalties or aggravated, punitive or exemplary damages.
