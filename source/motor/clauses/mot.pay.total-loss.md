Where an Insured vehicle is a Total loss, we pay on the value basis shown in the Schedule, less the Excess and any unpaid premium for that vehicle. Cover for that vehicle then ends, and no premium is refunded for it. Where the Schedule names a financier, [[ref:mot.cond.subrogation-and-interested-parties]] applies to the payment.
