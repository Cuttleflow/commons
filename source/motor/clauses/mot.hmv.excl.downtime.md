heading: What Is Not Covered for Downtime
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

In addition to [[ref:mot.section.general-exclusions]], we do not pay under [[ref:mot.hmv.section.downtime|heading]] for:

- any day for which we pay a hire vehicle benefit in [[ref:mot.hmv.ext.additional-benefits]] for the same vehicle; or
- any day on which the vehicle is missing after theft or attempted theft.
