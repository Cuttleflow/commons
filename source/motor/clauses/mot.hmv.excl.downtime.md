since: 1.0.0

In addition to [[ref:mot.section.general-exclusions]], we do not pay under [[ref:mot.hmv.section.downtime|heading]] for:

- any day for which we pay a hire vehicle benefit in [[ref:mot.hmv.ext.additional-benefits]] or substitute vehicle hire in [[ref:mot.hmv.cover.substitute-hire]] for the same vehicle; or
- any day on which the vehicle is missing after theft or attempted theft.
