---
id: mot.cover.third-party-property-damage
type: cover
heading: Third Party Property Damage
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

If an Insured person becomes legally liable to pay compensation for damage to other people's property caused by an Occurrence during the Period of Insurance arising from the use of an Insured vehicle or a Substitute vehicle, we will pay that compensation, up to the third party property damage limit shown in the Schedule for each Occurrence. We also pay the reasonable legal costs of defending the claim that we incur or approve in writing; these costs are paid in addition to the limit.
