heading: Declarations and Premium Adjustment
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

You must declare vehicles added to and removed from the fleet at the frequency shown in the Schedule (monthly, quarterly or at the end of the Period of Insurance). Where the Schedule shows a pro-rata basis, we adjust the premium for each vehicle added or removed from the date of the change. Where the Schedule shows an annual declaration basis, we adjust the premium at the end of the Period of Insurance on the difference between the vehicles declared at inception and at expiry, at the percentage of the annual rate shown in the Schedule. We will not refuse or reduce a claim only because a vehicle had not yet been declared, where it was covered under [[ref:mot.fleet.automatic-cover]].
