[[ref:mot.cover.third-party-property-damage]] extends to liability arising from the use of a Substitute vehicle, as though it were an Insured vehicle. It does not extend to loss of or damage to the Substitute vehicle itself. If the Substitute vehicle is also insured under another policy, [[ref:mot.cond.other-insurance]] applies. [WP REVIEW: confirm that relying on contribution, rather than a limit for vehicles insured elsewhere, is consistent with s 45 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).]
