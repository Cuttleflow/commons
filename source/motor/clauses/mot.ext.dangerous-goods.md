---
id: mot.ext.dangerous-goods
type: extension
heading: Dangerous Goods Liability
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

Where an Insured vehicle carries Dangerous goods in compliance with the law that applies to their carriage, [[ref:mot.cover.third-party-property-damage]] applies to liability arising from the Dangerous goods, up to the Dangerous goods sub-limit shown in the Schedule for each Occurrence. The sub-limit is part of, not in addition to, the third party property damage limit. Carriage in breach of that law is excluded by [[ref:mot.excl.liability]].
