---
id: mot.hmv.def.insured-vehicle
type: definition
heading: Insured Vehicle
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Each motor vehicle and trailer shown in the Schedule, including its standard equipment, its body, any refrigeration unit, tipping body, crane or loading equipment permanently fitted to it, its accessories, modifications and signwriting, and any vehicle covered under [[ref:mot.hmv.ext.newly-acquired-vehicles]].
