heading: Interstate Registration and Garaging
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

Vehicles registered or garaged in different States and Territories are covered under this one Policy. Stamp duty and levies are charged according to the State or Territory where each vehicle is normally garaged. You must tell us when a vehicle's normal garaging changes to another State or Territory.
