These benefits are paid in addition to the Sum insured, for the cover levels shown, up to the limits shown in the Schedule or, if none is shown, in [[ref:mot.appendix.default-schedule-values]]. No Excess applies to them unless the Schedule says otherwise.

| Benefit | Cover levels | What we pay |
|---|---|---|
| Hire vehicle after theft or Total loss | Comprehensive; Third party, fire and theft | The reasonable cost of hiring a similar vehicle, per day, until the claim is settled |
| Hire vehicle after accident | Comprehensive | The reasonable cost of hiring a similar vehicle, per day, until the repair is complete, where the driver was Not at fault and the other party is identified |
| Towing and recovery | Comprehensive; Third party, fire and theft | The reasonable cost of moving the vehicle to the nearest repairer or a safe place |
| Emergency repairs | Comprehensive | The reasonable cost of repairs needed to make the vehicle safe to drive home or to a repairer |
| Windscreen and window glass | Comprehensive | Replacement or repair of broken glass, with no Excess |
| Personal effects | Comprehensive | Personal effects in the vehicle lost or damaged in the same Occurrence, excluding money, jewellery and goods carried for business |
| Tools of trade | Comprehensive | Tools of trade owned by you or your employees lost or damaged in the same Occurrence |
| Locks and keys | Comprehensive; Third party, fire and theft | Replacing or recoding locks and keys after the keys are stolen |
| Child seats and restraints | Comprehensive | Replacing child seats and restraints damaged in the Occurrence |
| Trailer while attached | Comprehensive | Loss of or damage to a trailer you own while it is attached to the Insured vehicle |
| Emergency travel and accommodation | Comprehensive | Reasonable travel and accommodation costs where the vehicle cannot be driven after loss or damage more than the distance shown in the Schedule from its garaging address |
| Recovery after theft | Comprehensive; Third party, fire and theft | Reasonable costs of recovering and returning the vehicle after it is found |
| Employees' effects | Comprehensive | An employee's personal effects in the vehicle, where the driver is your employee |

Signwriting, livery, modifications and accessories are part of the Insured vehicle and its Sum insured. You must tell us before inception or renewal where modifications and accessories on a vehicle are worth more than the amount shown in [[ref:mot.appendix.default-schedule-values]].
