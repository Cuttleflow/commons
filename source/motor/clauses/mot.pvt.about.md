This Policy insures your private car. The car is insured at the cover level shown in the Schedule:

| Cover level | What it includes |
|---|---|
| Comprehensive | Accidental loss or damage to your car from any cause not excluded, your legal liability for damage to other people's property, and supplementary bodily injury cover |
| Third party, fire and theft | Loss or damage to your car caused by fire or theft, your legal liability for damage to other people's property, and supplementary bodily injury cover |
| Third party only | Your legal liability for damage to other people's property, and supplementary bodily injury cover. There is no cover for loss of or damage to your car |

The Policy is made up of this wording, the Schedule and any Endorsement. Where the Schedule and this wording differ, the Schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:mot.section.definitions]].
