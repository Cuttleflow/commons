heading: New Vehicle Replacement
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If a Powered vehicle shown as Comprehensive is a Total loss within the period shown in the Schedule after its first registration as a new vehicle, we will replace it with a new vehicle of the same make, model and specification, including its body, fitted equipment and on-road costs, where one is reasonably available in Australia. The most we pay is the percentage of the Sum insured shown in the Schedule. If a replacement is not reasonably available, we pay on the value basis shown in the Schedule. This provision does not apply to a trailer.
