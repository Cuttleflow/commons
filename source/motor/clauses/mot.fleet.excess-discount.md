heading: Fleet Excess Discount
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

Where the fleet has 10 or more Insured vehicles and there are no more than the number of at-fault claims shown in [[ref:mot.appendix.default-schedule-values]] across the fleet in a Period of Insurance, we will reduce the basic excess at the next renewal by the percentage shown there.
