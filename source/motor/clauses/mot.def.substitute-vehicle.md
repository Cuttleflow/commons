heading: Substitute Vehicle
since: 3.0.0
changed: 3.1.0
status: draft
---

A vehicle you use temporarily, for no longer than 30 days, while an Insured vehicle is being repaired or serviced or cannot be used because of breakdown or loss, where the substitute vehicle is not owned by you. [[ref:mot.ext.substitute-vehicles|heading]] sets out the cover.
