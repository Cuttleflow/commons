(Completed separately for each policy. It contains your specific details.)

The Schedule shows:

- The named insured, ABN and contact details
- The Period of Insurance
- The vehicle schedule: for each Insured vehicle, its registration, make, model and year, Vehicle class, Gross vehicle mass (and Gross combination mass for a prime mover), Carrying capacity, body type, Permitted use, garaging postcode, cover level, value basis and Sum insured, and any Excess or Endorsement that applies to that vehicle only
- The Radius of operation
- The driver basis: any authorised driver, or the named drivers
- The third party property damage limit and the sub-limits for supplementary bodily injury and Dangerous goods
- Whether [[ref:mot.hmv.section.downtime|heading]] applies and, if it does, the weekly downtime benefit for each Powered vehicle it applies to, the waiting period and the maximum period
- Whether [[ref:mot.hmv.section.non-owned-trailers|heading]] applies and, if it does, the Non-owned trailer limit
- The Excesses
- Sub-limits and other values that differ from the defaults in [[ref:mot.hmv.appendix.default-schedule-values]]
- The premium, with stamp duty, levies and GST
- The State or Territory whose law governs this Policy (see [[ref:mot.cond.governing-law-and-jurisdiction]])
- The Endorsements that apply, by code, with their variables
- Interested parties, such as financiers and lessors
