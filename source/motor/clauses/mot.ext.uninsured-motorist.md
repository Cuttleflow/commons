id: mot.ext.uninsured-motorist
type: extension
heading: Uninsured Motorist Damage
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

If an Insured vehicle shown as Third party, fire and theft or Third party only is damaged in a collision with another vehicle whose driver is uninsured, and we agree that the driver of the Insured vehicle was not at fault, we will pay for the damage up to the amount shown in the Schedule, provided you give us the name and address of the other driver and the registration of their vehicle.
