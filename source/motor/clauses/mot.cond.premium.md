The premium is shown in the Schedule. Where you pay by instalments and an instalment is unpaid for one month or more, we may refuse a claim that arises after the instalment fell due, and we may cancel the Policy by written notice in accordance with the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). Before we refuse a claim or cancel for an unpaid instalment, we will give you written notice that the instalment is overdue and what you must pay to keep the Policy in force.
