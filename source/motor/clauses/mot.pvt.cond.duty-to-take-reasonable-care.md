This is a consumer insurance contract. Before you enter into, renew, extend, vary or reinstate this Policy, you have a duty under s 20B of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to us. Answer each of our questions honestly and accurately, and as fully as you can. If you do not meet this duty, we may be entitled to reduce our liability for a claim, to cancel the Policy, or, where the misrepresentation is fraudulent, to avoid the Policy from its beginning, as the Act allows.
