These benefits are paid in addition to the Sum insured, for the cover levels shown, up to the limits shown in the Schedule or, if none is shown, in [[ref:mot.hmv.appendix.default-schedule-values]]. No Excess applies to them, except as this table or [[ref:mot.hmv.cond.excess]] says.

| Benefit | Cover levels | What we pay |
|---|---|---|
| Hire vehicle after theft or Total loss | Comprehensive; Third party, fire and theft | The reasonable cost of hiring a similar vehicle, per day, until the claim is settled |
| Hire vehicle after accident | Comprehensive | The reasonable cost of hiring a similar vehicle, per day, until the repair is complete, where the driver was Not at fault and the other party is identified |
| Towing, salvage and recovery | Comprehensive; Third party, fire and theft | The reasonable cost of recovering the vehicle, including lifting, winching and righting it, and of moving it to the nearest repairer able to repair it or to a safe place |
| Removal of debris | Comprehensive; Third party, fire and theft | The reasonable cost of removing the vehicle's wreckage and its Load from the scene of an Occurrence, and of cleaning the road, where a public authority requires it. Dangerous goods are dealt with under [[ref:mot.ext.dangerous-goods]], not under this benefit |
| Load transfer and journey completion | Comprehensive | Where the vehicle cannot be driven after loss or damage covered by this section more than the distance shown in the Schedule from its garaging address, the reasonable extra cost of moving its Load to another vehicle and delivering it to its destination or returning it. This benefit does not pay for loss of or damage to the Load |
| Emergency repairs | Comprehensive | The reasonable cost of repairs needed to make the vehicle safe to drive to its destination or to a repairer |
| Windscreen and window glass | Comprehensive | Replacement or repair of broken glass. For a vehicle with a Gross vehicle mass of more than 4.5 tonnes, the basic excess applies |
| Personal effects | Comprehensive | Personal effects in the vehicle lost or damaged in the same Occurrence, excluding money, jewellery and the Load |
| Tools of trade | Comprehensive | Tools of trade owned by you or your employees lost or damaged in the same Occurrence |
| Load restraint equipment | Comprehensive; Third party, fire and theft | Chains, straps, tarpaulins, gates, ramps and similar equipment used to secure a Load, lost or damaged in the same Occurrence as the vehicle |
| Locks and keys | Comprehensive; Third party, fire and theft | Replacing or recoding locks and keys after the keys are stolen |
| Small trailers | Comprehensive | Loss of or damage to a trailer you own with a Gross vehicle mass of 4.5 tonnes or less that is not shown in the Schedule, while it is attached to the Insured vehicle |
| Driver travel and accommodation | Comprehensive | Where the vehicle cannot be driven after loss or damage covered by this section more than the distance shown in the Schedule from its garaging address, the driver's reasonable accommodation costs and the cost of travel home or on to the destination |
| Recovery after theft | Comprehensive; Third party, fire and theft | Reasonable costs of recovering and returning the vehicle after it is found |
| Employees' effects | Comprehensive | An employee's personal effects in the vehicle, where the driver is your employee |

We do not pay the hire vehicle benefits for any day for which we pay [[ref:mot.hmv.cover.downtime]] for the same vehicle. Where both could apply, you choose which one we pay.

Signwriting, livery, modifications and accessories are part of the Insured vehicle and its Sum insured. You must tell us before inception or renewal where modifications and accessories on a vehicle are worth more than the amount shown in [[ref:mot.hmv.appendix.default-schedule-values]].
