---
id: mot.excl.hire-fare-or-reward
type: exclusion
heading: Hire, Fare or Reward
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

Loss, damage or liability while the vehicle is used for hire, fare or reward, including rideshare and food delivery for a platform, unless the Schedule shows it as Permitted use or an Endorsement covers it. Carrying your own goods, staff or customers in the course of your business is not use for hire, fare or reward.
