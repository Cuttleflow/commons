If a Powered vehicle to which this section applies cannot be used for its Permitted use because of loss or damage that we have accepted under [[ref:mot.section.vehicle-cover|heading]], we pay the reasonable cost of hiring a substitute vehicle of a similar kind, so that you can keep carrying on your business. We pay whoever was at fault.

We pay hire costs from the day after the Occurrence until the earliest of:

- the day the vehicle is repaired and available for use;
- the day we pay or offer to pay for its Total loss; and
- the end of the maximum hire period shown in the Schedule.

The most we pay for each Powered vehicle for any one Occurrence is the substitute hire limit shown in the Schedule. If none is shown, the limit and the maximum hire period are those in [[ref:mot.hmv.appendix.default-schedule-values]]. No Excess applies to this section.

We pay the hire cost of the substitute vehicle only. We do not pay its fuel, tolls, registration charges, driver costs or any damage to it, or any loss of income, profit or contract.

If repairs are delayed because you did not authorise them, or did not make the vehicle available to the repairer, within a reasonable time after we accepted the claim, we do not pay hire costs for the days of that delay.
