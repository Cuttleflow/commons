heading: Newly Acquired Vehicles
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A Powered vehicle or trailer that you buy or lease during the Period of Insurance is an Insured vehicle from the time you take possession of it, up to the value limit shown in [[ref:mot.hmv.appendix.default-schedule-values]], provided that you tell us about it within the notice period shown there and pay any additional premium we ask for. Its cover level and terms are those of the Insured vehicle it replaces or, if it is an additional vehicle, those of the Insured vehicle of the same Vehicle class with the highest cover level. If there is no Insured vehicle of the same Vehicle class, it is insured at Third party only until we accept it in writing.

A vehicle you tell us about after the notice period is an Insured vehicle only from the date we accept it in writing. This extension does not apply to a vehicle whose Vehicle class is not one listed in [[ref:mot.hmv.def.vehicle-class]], such as a bus or a piece of mobile plant.
