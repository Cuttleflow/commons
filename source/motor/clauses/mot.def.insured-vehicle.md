Each motor vehicle shown in the Schedule, including its standard equipment, accessories, modifications and signwriting, and any vehicle covered under [[ref:mot.fleet.automatic-cover]], [[ref:mot.fleet.courtesy-vehicles]] or [[ref:mot.fleet.undeclared-vehicles]].
