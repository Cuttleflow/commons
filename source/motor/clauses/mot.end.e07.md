You agree to keep the telematics system named in the Schedule installed and operating in the Insured vehicles shown, and to give us, on request and not more often than quarterly, the summary reports named in the Schedule. We use the reports only to review the premium and terms at renewal and to assess claims. A failure of the system that you did not cause does not affect cover.

Schedule variables: telematics system; vehicles covered; reports.
