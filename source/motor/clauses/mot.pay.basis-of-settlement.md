We settle a claim for loss of or damage to an Insured vehicle on the value basis shown for it in the Schedule:

| Value basis | How we settle a Total loss |
|---|---|
| Market value (the default) | We pay the Market value of the vehicle immediately before the loss, up to its Sum insured, less the Excess |
| Agreed value | We pay the Agreed value shown in the Schedule, less the Excess |

Where the vehicle can be economically repaired, we settle as set out in [[ref:mot.pay.repairs]]. Where it is a Total loss, [[ref:mot.pay.total-loss]] applies.
