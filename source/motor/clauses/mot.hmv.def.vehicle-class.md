heading: Vehicle Class
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

The class shown in the Schedule for an Insured vehicle: a light commercial vehicle (a Powered vehicle with a Gross vehicle mass of 4.5 tonnes or less), a light rigid truck (a rigid truck with a Gross vehicle mass of more than 4.5 tonnes and not more than 8 tonnes), a heavy rigid truck (a rigid truck with a Gross vehicle mass of more than 8 tonnes), a prime mover (a Powered vehicle built to tow a semi-trailer), or a trailer (a trailer or semi-trailer, including a dolly or converter).
