This Policy insures trucks, prime movers and trailers used in a business, and any light commercial vehicles insured with them. Each Insured vehicle is insured at one of three cover levels, shown for that vehicle in the Schedule:

| Cover level | What it includes |
|---|---|
| Comprehensive | Accidental loss or damage to the Insured vehicle from any cause not excluded, your legal liability for damage to other people's property, and supplementary bodily injury cover |
| Third party, fire and theft | Loss or damage to the Insured vehicle caused by fire or theft, your legal liability for damage to other people's property, and supplementary bodily injury cover |
| Third party only | Your legal liability for damage to other people's property, and supplementary bodily injury cover. There is no cover for loss of or damage to the Insured vehicle, except as [[ref:mot.ext.uninsured-motorist|heading]] provides |

Two optional sections apply only where the Schedule shows that they do. [[ref:mot.hmv.section.downtime]] pays a weekly benefit while a Powered vehicle is off the road after loss or damage we have accepted. [[ref:mot.hmv.section.non-owned-trailers]] covers loss of or damage to a Non-owned trailer while it is in your control.

This Policy does not insure goods carried. Insurance for goods in transit and for a carrier's liability for goods must be arranged separately.

The Policy is made up of this wording, the Schedule and any Endorsement. Where the Schedule and this wording differ, the Schedule applies. Words with special meanings are listed in [[ref:mot.section.definitions]].
