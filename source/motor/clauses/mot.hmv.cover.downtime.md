heading: Downtime Benefit
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If a Powered vehicle to which this section applies cannot be used for its Permitted use because of loss or damage that we have accepted under [[ref:mot.section.vehicle-cover|heading]], we will pay you the weekly downtime benefit shown for that vehicle in the Schedule. We pay one seventh of the weekly benefit for each day the vehicle cannot be used:

- starting when the waiting period shown in the Schedule has passed after the Occurrence; and
- ending when the vehicle is repaired and available for use, when we pay or offer to pay for its Total loss, or when the maximum period shown in the Schedule ends, whichever is earliest.

We do not pay for the waiting period. No Excess applies to this section; the waiting period takes its place. The most we pay for each Powered vehicle for any one Occurrence is the weekly downtime benefit multiplied by the maximum period.

We pay the weekly downtime benefit as an agreed amount, without proof of the income lost. We do not pay any other loss of income, profit or contract.

If repairs are delayed because you did not authorise them, or did not make the vehicle available to the repairer, within a reasonable time after we accepted the claim, we reduce the payment by the days of that delay.
