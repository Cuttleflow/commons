id: mot.fleet.employees-vehicles
type: extension
heading: Employees' and Directors' Vehicles
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

While an employee or director uses their own private vehicle in your business, [[ref:mot.cover.third-party-property-damage]] extends to your liability arising from that use, where their own motor insurance does not respond or is not enough. This extension does not cover loss of or damage to the employee's or director's own vehicle.
