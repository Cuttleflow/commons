While an employee or director uses their own private vehicle in your business, [[ref:mot.cover.third-party-property-damage]] extends to your liability arising from that use. If their own motor insurance also covers that liability, [[ref:mot.cond.other-insurance]] applies. This extension does not cover loss of or damage to the employee's or director's own vehicle. [WP REVIEW: confirm that relying on contribution, rather than a limit for vehicles insured elsewhere, is consistent with s 45 of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).]
