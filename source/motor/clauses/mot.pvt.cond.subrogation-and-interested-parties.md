heading: Subrogation and Interested Parties
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

After we pay a claim, we may take recovery action in your name against any person responsible, and you must give us reasonable help with it. We will not take recovery action against an Insured person, or against a member of your family or household who was driving with your permission, unless the loss was caused by their deliberate or criminal act.

Where the Schedule names a financier or lessor as an interested party, we will pay a claim for the loss of or damage to that Insured vehicle to them, or to you and them jointly, to the extent of their interest.
