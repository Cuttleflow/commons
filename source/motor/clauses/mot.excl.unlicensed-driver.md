---
id: mot.excl.unlicensed-driver
type: exclusion
heading: Unlicensed or Disqualified Driver
since: 3.0.0
changed: 3.1.0
status: draft
---

Loss, damage or liability while the Insured vehicle is driven by a person who does not hold a current licence appropriate for the vehicle, or whose licence is suspended, cancelled or disqualified. This exclusion does not apply to an Insured person who did not know, and could not reasonably have known, that the driver was unlicensed or disqualified, or where the vehicle was driven without your consent.
