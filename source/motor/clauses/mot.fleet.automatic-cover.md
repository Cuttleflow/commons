id: mot.fleet.automatic-cover
type: extension
heading: Automatic Cover for New and Replacement Vehicles
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

A vehicle you buy or lease during the Period of Insurance is covered automatically from the time you take possession of it, on the same cover level and terms as the highest-covered Insured vehicle of the same type, up to the value limit shown in [[ref:mot.appendix.default-schedule-values]], provided that you declare it by the earlier of the next Declaration date and the notice period shown in that appendix, its type is consistent with the vehicles you have declared, and you pay any additional premium. A vehicle declared after the notice period is covered from the date we accept it in writing.
