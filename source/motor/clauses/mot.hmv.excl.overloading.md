---
id: mot.hmv.excl.overloading
type: exclusion
heading: Overloading
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

Loss, damage or liability caused or contributed to by an Insured vehicle or a Combination carrying a Load, or towing, beyond its Gross vehicle mass, its Gross combination mass, its manufacturer's rated capacity, or the mass or dimension limits that apply to it under the law, including any permit or notice under the Heavy Vehicle National Law. This exclusion does not apply where the excess mass or dimension did not cause or contribute to the loss, damage or liability, or where you and the driver took reasonable steps to keep the vehicle within those limits.
