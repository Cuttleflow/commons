heading: Courtesy and Loan Vehicles
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

Vehicles you own and lend to customers as courtesy or loan vehicles are covered on the same terms as your other Insured vehicles of the same type, provided they are shown in the Schedule or declared within the notice period in [[ref:mot.fleet.automatic-cover]].
