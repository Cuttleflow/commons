---
id: mot.excl.alcohol-and-drugs
type: exclusion
heading: Alcohol and Drugs
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

Loss, damage or liability while the Insured vehicle is driven by a person whose ability to drive is impaired by alcohol or a drug, who has a blood or breath alcohol level above the legal limit for that driver, or who refuses a lawful test. This exclusion does not apply where the vehicle was driven without your consent.
