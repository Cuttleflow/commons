id: mot.excl.alcohol-and-drugs
type: exclusion
heading: Alcohol and Drugs
since: 3.0.0
changed: 3.1.0
status: draft
---

Loss, damage or liability while the Insured vehicle is driven by a person whose ability to drive is impaired by alcohol or a drug, who has a blood or breath alcohol level above the legal limit for that driver, or who refuses a lawful test. This exclusion does not apply to an Insured person who did not know, and could not reasonably have known, of the driver's state, or where the vehicle was driven without your consent.
