Your no claim bonus rating is shown in the Schedule. It improves by one step at each renewal after a Period of Insurance with no at-fault claim, up to the highest rating, and falls by the number of steps shown in [[ref:mot.pvt.appendix.default-schedule-values]] for each at-fault claim. A claim does not count as at fault where the driver was Not at fault and you give us the name and address of the person responsible, or where it is for windscreen or window glass only, or for theft, storm, hail or flood. Your rating affects only the premium; it does not change your cover.
