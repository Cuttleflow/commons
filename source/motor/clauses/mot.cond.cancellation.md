You may cancel this Policy, or remove an Insured vehicle from it, at any time by telling us. We refund the premium for the unexpired Period of Insurance on a pro-rata basis, less any non-refundable government charges. If we have paid a Total loss of an Insured vehicle, no refund applies to that vehicle.

We may cancel this Policy only as the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) allows, by giving you written notice as that Act requires, which is at least 30 days' written notice or any longer notice the Act requires. We refund the premium for the unexpired Period of Insurance on a pro-rata basis.

Cancellation does not affect a claim for loss, damage or liability that occurred before the cancellation took effect.
