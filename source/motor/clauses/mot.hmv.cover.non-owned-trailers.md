---
id: mot.hmv.cover.non-owned-trailers
type: cover
heading: Non-owned Trailers in Your Control
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

If a Non-owned trailer suffers Accidental loss or damage during the Period of Insurance while it is attached to a Powered vehicle insured as Comprehensive, or while it is detached and in the custody of an Insured person in the course of a journey with that vehicle, we will pay the lesser of:

- the reasonable cost of repairing it; and
- its Market value immediately before the loss or damage,

up to the Non-owned trailer limit shown in the Schedule for each trailer for each Occurrence, less the Non-owned trailer excess in [[ref:mot.hmv.cond.excess]]. We pay whether or not an Insured person is legally liable to the trailer's owner for the loss or damage.

We pay you or, if you ask us to, the trailer's owner. A payment to either is a full settlement of that loss or damage under this section.
