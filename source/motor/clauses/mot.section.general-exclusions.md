These exclusions apply to every section of this Policy. A section may have its own exclusions as well. Where an exclusion refers to a driver, it applies only to loss, damage or liability that arises while that person is in charge of the Insured vehicle. [[ref:mot.cond.insurance-contracts-act]] applies to each of them.
