status: draft
---

In addition to [[ref:mot.section.general-exclusions]], we do not pay under [[ref:mot.section.vehicle-cover|heading]] for:

- mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdown, failure, wear and tear, rust or corrosion, unless caused by an insured event;
- damage to tyres by road punctures, cuts or bursts, unless other insured damage to your car happens at the same time;
- loss of value or depreciation after a repair;
- loss of use or any other consequential loss, except the benefits in [[ref:mot.pvt.ext.additional-benefits]]; or
- loss of your car to a buyer who has not paid for it, or who paid with a dishonoured payment, when you were selling it.
