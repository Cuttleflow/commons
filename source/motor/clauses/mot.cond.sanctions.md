heading: Sanctions
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

We do not provide cover, pay any claim or provide any benefit to the extent that doing so would expose us to a sanction, prohibition or restriction under the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011 (Cth), the Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 (Cth), United Nations resolutions, or the trade or economic sanctions laws or regulations of the European Union, the United Kingdom or the United States of America.
