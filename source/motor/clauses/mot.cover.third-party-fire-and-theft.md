since: 3.0.0
changed: 3.1.0

If an Insured vehicle shown as Third party, fire and theft suffers loss or damage during the Period of Insurance caused by fire, lightning or explosion, or by theft or attempted theft, we will pay for the loss or damage as set out in [[ref:mot.pay.basis-of-settlement]]. This cover does not include collision, malicious damage, storm, flood or any other accidental damage to the Insured vehicle, except as [[ref:mot.ext.uninsured-motorist|heading]] provides.
