These are the defaults that apply where the Schedule does not show another amount. Each figure is a Schedule value: an issuer may vary it in the Schedule without amending this wording. Where this Policy is a prescribed contract for motor vehicle insurance for a light vehicle, the issuer must first check the change against the list of departures from standard cover that it gives the insured.
