heading: What Is Not Covered for Non-owned Trailers
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

In addition to [[ref:mot.section.general-exclusions]], we do not pay under [[ref:mot.hmv.section.non-owned-trailers|heading]] for:

- mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdown, failure, wear and tear, rust or corrosion, unless caused by an insured event;
- damage to tyres by road punctures, cuts or bursts, unless other insured damage to the trailer happens at the same time;
- loss of or damage to the Load or anything else carried on the trailer;
- loss of use of the trailer, hire charges paid to its owner or any other consequential loss; or
- a trailer being towed, carried or recovered as part of a towing or recovery business.
