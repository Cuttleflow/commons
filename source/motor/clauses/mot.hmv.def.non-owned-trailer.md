type: definition
heading: Non-owned Trailer
since: 1.0.0
status: draft
---

A trailer that is not an Insured vehicle and is not owned by an Insured person, or leased or hired to an Insured person for a continuous period longer than the period shown in [[ref:mot.hmv.appendix.default-schedule-values]].
