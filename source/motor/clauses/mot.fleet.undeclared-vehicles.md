Where you use a vehicle in your business that is not shown in the Schedule and is not covered by [[ref:mot.fleet.automatic-cover]], [[ref:mot.section.liability|heading]] applies to it for up to the grace period shown in [[ref:mot.appendix.default-schedule-values]], provided you tell us about it and pay any additional premium.
