This Policy is designed for businesses that own, lease or use motor vehicles for their business. It is not a consumer insurance contract: it is not entered into wholly or predominantly for personal, domestic or household purposes, and the duty of disclosure in [[ref:mot.cond.duty-of-disclosure]] applies.

This wording is designed for distribution to wholesale clients within the meaning of s 761G of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). A business that is a small business under s 761G may be a retail client for motor vehicle insurance. Where this Policy is issued to a retail client, the issuer must first give a Product Disclosure Statement, make a target market determination and meet the General Insurance Code of Practice. This wording is not a Product Disclosure Statement.

Where the insured or one of the insureds is a natural person, the Policy may be a prescribed contract for motor vehicle insurance under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) and the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017 (Cth). The issuer must then clearly inform the insured in writing, before the contract is entered into, of each way in which this Policy differs from standard cover.
