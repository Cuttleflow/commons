This Policy is designed for businesses that own, lease or use motor vehicles for their business.

This wording is written for businesses and professionals. Some buyers, such as small businesses, may be retail clients under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth). Where a buyer is a retail client, the issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement and make a Target Market Determination available. This wording is not itself a Product Disclosure Statement. Where this Policy is issued to a retail client, the issuer must also meet the General Insurance Code of Practice.

This Policy is intended to be entered into for business purposes, so the duty of disclosure in [[ref:mot.cond.duty-of-disclosure]] applies. If it is a consumer insurance contract, that clause says which duty applies instead. [WP REVIEW: confirm whether a sole trader or partnership insuring a vehicle for business use is a consumer insurance contract under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), and so which duty applies.]

Where the insured or one of the insureds is a natural person, the Policy may be a prescribed contract for motor vehicle insurance under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) and the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017 (Cth). The issuer must then clearly inform the insured in writing, before the contract is entered into, of each way in which this Policy differs from standard cover, and must check any change to a Default Schedule Value against that list.
