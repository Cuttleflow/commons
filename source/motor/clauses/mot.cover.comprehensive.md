If an Insured vehicle shown as Comprehensive suffers Accidental loss or damage during the Period of Insurance, we will pay for the loss or damage as set out in [[ref:mot.pay.basis-of-settlement]]. This includes Accidental loss or damage caused by collision or overturning; fire, lightning or explosion; theft or attempted theft; storm, flood, hail or cyclone; malicious damage or vandalism; falling objects; impact with an animal; immersion in water; and landslide, subsidence or earthquake.
