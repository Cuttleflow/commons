Tell us during the Period of Insurance about vehicles added to or removed from the Schedule; a change in the use of a vehicle, for example starting to carry Dangerous goods or starting rideshare, courier or delivery work for a platform; a change in where a vehicle is normally garaged; modifications to a vehicle worth more than the amount shown in [[ref:mot.appendix.default-schedule-values]]; and a change to the drivers, where the Schedule shows named drivers.

We may change the premium or terms because of a change you tell us about, but only by giving you written notice of the new premium or terms. They apply only from the date stated in the notice, which is not earlier than the notice period shown in [[ref:mot.appendix.default-schedule-values]] after we send it. If you do not accept them, you may cancel this Policy as [[ref:mot.cond.cancellation]] allows, and the new premium or terms do not apply to you.
