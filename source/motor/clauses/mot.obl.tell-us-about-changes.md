heading: Tell Us About Changes
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

Tell us during the Period of Insurance about vehicles added to or removed from the Schedule; a change in the use of a vehicle, for example starting to carry Dangerous goods or starting rideshare, courier or delivery work for a platform; a change in where a vehicle is normally garaged; modifications to a vehicle worth more than the amount shown in [[ref:mot.appendix.default-schedule-values]]; and a change to the drivers, where the Schedule shows named drivers. We may change the premium or terms from the date of the change.
