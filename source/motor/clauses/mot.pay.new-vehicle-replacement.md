heading: New Vehicle Replacement
since: 3.0.0
status: draft
---

If an Insured vehicle shown as Comprehensive is a Total loss within the period shown in the Schedule after its first registration as a new vehicle, and its gross vehicle mass does not exceed the limit shown in the Schedule, we will replace it with a new vehicle of the same make, model and specification, including on-road costs, where one is reasonably available in Australia. The most we pay is the percentage of the Sum insured shown in the Schedule. If a replacement is not reasonably available, we pay on the value basis shown in the Schedule.
