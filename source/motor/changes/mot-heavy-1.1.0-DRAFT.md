Substitute vehicle hire (Linear PLT-1202, 21 November 2026).

- Substitute Vehicle Hire: a new optional section beside Downtime. While a Powered vehicle insured at the Comprehensive level is off the road after loss or damage we have accepted, we pay the reasonable cost of hiring a similar vehicle, whoever was at fault, up to $5,000 each vehicle each Occurrence and for up to 4 weeks by default. No excess applies. Fuel, tolls, driver costs and loss of income are not paid.
- No double payment: Additional Benefits, Downtime and Substitute Vehicle Hire do not pay for the same vehicle on the same day; where more than one could apply, you choose which one we pay.
- About This Policy, the Schedule and the default schedule values list the new section.
