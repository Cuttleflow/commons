This is the first release of the Meridian Private Motor wording. It is built on the Meridian Commercial Motor base wording version 3.0.0-DRAFT, so that cover levels, settlement, liability, exclusions and conditions read the same way across commercial, fleet and private motor. It differs from the base in these ways:

- It is a consumer insurance contract: the duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation (s 20B of the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth)) replaces the duty of disclosure.
- It is a retail product. The issuer must give a Product Disclosure Statement, make a target market determination and inform the insured of every departure from prescribed motor standard cover before issue. Those documents are not part of this wording and have still to be drafted.
- It adds a no claim bonus condition, a cooling-off period and the General Insurance Code of Practice in the complaints condition.
- It removes the fleet provisions, business-only additional benefits, the dangerous goods extension and the commercial endorsements. Endorsements E05, E06, E10 and E14 remain.
- Its defaults are set for private cars, in its own Default Schedule Values appendix.
