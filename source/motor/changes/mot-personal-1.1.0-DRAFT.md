Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. The Private Motor wording is now built on the Meridian Commercial Motor base wording version 3.1.0-DRAFT, so each change to the base listed in its own note applies here too, including the Insurance Contracts Act, sanctions, other insurance, cancellation, premium, joint insureds, control group, terrorism and Not at Fault changes. In addition:

- Who This Policy Is For: restated with the library's wording on retail clients, a Product Disclosure Statement and a Target Market Determination. An issuer must check any change to a Default Schedule Value against the list of differences from standard cover that it gives you. A tag asks the Wording Partner to complete that list and to confirm whether the Alcohol and Drugs exclusion, which now applies only where the Insured person knew or could reasonably have known of the driver's state, is within the exclusions the Regulations allow.
- Default Schedule Values: the Schedule may not set the third party property damage limit below the minimum amount that standard cover requires. A new default sets the notice period before a changed premium or changed terms apply (14 days).
- Tell Us About Changes: we may change the premium or terms only by written notice, from the date stated in it, not earlier than the notice period after we send it. If you do not accept the new premium or terms, you may cancel, we refund the unexpired premium pro rata from the date of cancellation, and the new premium or terms do not apply to you. A tag asks the Wording Partner to confirm the mechanism.
- No Claim Bonus and Additional Benefits: "we agree the driver was not at fault" is replaced by the defined test, Not at fault.
- Alcohol and Drugs, Unlicensed or Disqualified Driver: both now use the same knowledge test and are applied to each Insured person separately. An innocent Insured person is not caught by another person's knowledge.
- Joint Insureds: where more than one individual is named in the Schedule, one person's fraud, deliberate act or failure to comply does not affect the cover of another who did not know of it, take part in it or consent to it.
- Cancellation: the deduction for amounts paid or reserved for a claim is removed.
- Additional Benefits: the trailer benefit no longer depends on the trailer not being insured separately. Other Insurance applies.
