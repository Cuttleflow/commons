This is the first release of the Meridian Heavy Motor wording. It is built on the Meridian Commercial Motor base wording version 3.1.0-DRAFT, so that cover levels, settlement, liability, exclusions and conditions read the same way across commercial, fleet, private and heavy motor. It is written for owner-drivers and small transport operators with heavy rigid trucks, prime movers and trailers. It differs from the base in these ways:

- Retail position. Cover for a vehicle with a carrying capacity of more than 2 tonnes is not a motor vehicle insurance product under regulation 7.1.11 of the Corporations Regulations 2001 (Cth). Who This Policy Is For says so, and says that light vehicles on the same schedule may be retail. Two WP REVIEW tags ask the Wording Partner to confirm the test and to list the departures from prescribed motor standard cover for light vehicles.
- New definitions: Carrying Capacity, Combination, Gross Combination Mass, Gross Vehicle Mass, Load, Non-owned Trailer, Powered Vehicle, Radius of Operation and Vehicle Class. Insured Vehicle now includes trailers and fitted bodies, refrigeration units, tipping bodies, cranes and loading equipment. Permitted Use now includes carrying goods for hire or reward, and Hire, Fare or Reward excludes only carrying passengers for hire and hiring a vehicle out without a driver.
- Goods carried are not insured. About This Policy says so, and the vehicle cover and liability exclusions now name the Load. Goods in transit and carriers' liability need separate insurance.
- Additional Benefits are restated for heavy vehicles: towing, salvage and recovery; removal of debris; load transfer and journey completion; load restraint equipment; small trailers; and driver travel and accommodation. The child seat benefit and the trailer-while-attached benefit are removed: owned trailers are Insured vehicles in their own right. Glass claims on vehicles over 4.5 tonnes gross vehicle mass carry the basic excess.
- New Vehicle Replacement applies to Powered vehicles of any mass, not to trailers, at up to 110% of the Sum insured.
- The fleet provisions are removed. Newly Acquired Vehicles replaces automatic cover for new and replacement vehicles.
- Two optional sections are added. Downtime pays an agreed weekly benefit, after a waiting period and for up to a maximum period, while a Powered vehicle is off the road after loss or damage accepted under the vehicle cover. Non-owned Trailers in Your Control pays for accidental loss of or damage to a trailer you do not own, up to a limit for each trailer, whether or not you are liable to its owner.
- Trailers (liability) and What Is Not Covered for Your Legal Liability: damage to a Non-owned trailer itself is no longer met by the third party property damage cover. It is covered only by the optional Non-owned Trailers section, with its own limit and excess. This is a deliberate change from the base, for underwriter confirmation.
- Overloading: the exclusion now covers gross combination mass and the mass and dimension limits under the law, including Heavy Vehicle National Law permits and notices, and does not apply where the overload did not cause or contribute to the loss or where reasonable steps were taken.
- Tell Us About Changes adds changes of vehicle class, radius, load, multi-trailer combinations, restricted sites and two-up driving.
- Excesses add a combination driver excess (instead of the age and inexperienced driver excesses for prime movers and combinations), an outside radius excess, a tipping excess and a non-owned trailer excess. One basic excess applies where a Powered vehicle and its trailer are damaged in the same Occurrence.
- Endorsements E01, E02, E03, E08, E09, E11, E13 and E17 are removed. E02 Refrigerated Vehicles is removed because spoilage of goods carried is cargo cover, which this wording does not give. E18 becomes Owner-Driver. New endorsements: E19 Multi-trailer Combinations, E20 Tipping Operations, E21 Specialist Transport Operations and E22 Restricted Sites.
- Defaults are set for heavy vehicles in the wording's own Default Schedule Values appendix, including a $2,500 basic excess and a $5,000 combination driver excess.

For underwriter review before release: the non-owned trailer cover on an accidental damage basis rather than a liability basis; the 110% new vehicle replacement for heavy vehicles; and whether liability while tipping a Load at a delivery point should be written back from the Tool of Trade exclusion for vehicles under E20. The wording does not write it back.
