Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026. The template now describes Private Motor version 1.1.0-DRAFT.

- How This Policy Differs From Standard Cover: the table is rebuilt to list every term that gives less cover than a full indemnity, including the unsafe or unroadworthy condition exclusion, the unlicensed or disqualified driver exclusion, war, nuclear risks, deliberate acts, hire, fare or reward, the duty to keep the vehicle registered and roadworthy, no refund of premium for a vehicle after a total loss, and changes to premium or terms during the period. A tag asks the Wording Partner to check each row against the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017 (Cth).
- Is This Product Right for You?: the product is for a car only, as the wording says. Light commercial vehicles and motorcycles are not covered.
- Your Premium: the third party property damage limit cannot be set below the minimum amount standard cover requires.
- Paying by Instalments: the written-notice promise now also appears in the wording.
- Cancelling Your Policy: the deduction for amounts paid or reserved for a claim is removed. No refund applies to a vehicle after we pay a total loss.
- Telling Us About Changes: a changed premium or changed terms apply only after written notice, from the date stated in it, and not earlier than 14 days after we send it. You may cancel if you do not accept them.
- Making a Claim: the description of section 54 of the Insurance Contracts Act now matches the policy wording.
- Significant Risks, Excesses and No Claim Bonus: updated for the knowledge test in the alcohol, drug and licence exclusions and for the defined test for a driver who is not at fault.
- Notes for Issuers and the Wording Partner: updated for these changes.
