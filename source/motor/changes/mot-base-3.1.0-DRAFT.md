Remediation following the Claude pre-review of 2 November 2026.

- Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth): replaced with a general condition that states the Act prevails and applies section 54 to every condition, exclusion, limit and obligation, not only to the claims conditions.
- Sanctions: restated as a standalone condition naming the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011 (Cth), the Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 (Cth), and the sanctions laws of the United Kingdom, the United States of America and the European Union, to the extent they apply to us. The reference to United Nations resolutions is removed.
- Other Insurance: restated so that we do not refuse or reduce a payment because of other insurance, and may seek contribution. The Policy is excess of another policy only where the Schedule or an Endorsement names that policy.
- Substitute Vehicle, Substitute Vehicles, and Employees' and Directors' Vehicles: the limits that depended on other insurance ("not otherwise insured", "does not respond or is not enough") are removed. Other Insurance now applies. A tag asks the Wording Partner to confirm this against section 45.
- Alcohol and Drugs, Unlicensed or Disqualified Driver: both now use the same test. The exclusion does not apply to an Insured person who did not know, and could not reasonably have known, of the driver's state, or where the vehicle was driven without your consent. The note to version 3.0.0 described this test inaccurately; this release makes the text match it.
- Control Group (new definition), You, Your, and Who Is Covered: where the Named insured is not an individual, what it knows, intends or consents to is what a member of the Control group knows, intends or consents to. Knowledge, intention and consent are tested for each Insured person separately and are not attributed from one Insured person to another.
- Joint Insureds (new condition), Deliberate Acts, and Fraudulent Claims: where there is more than one Named insured, a fraud, deliberate act or failure to comply by one does not affect the cover of another who did not know of it, take part in it or consent to it.
- Not at Fault (new definition), Excesses, Uninsured Motorist Damage and Additional Benefits: "we agree that the driver was not at fault" is replaced by a stated test, decided reasonably on the evidence available, with a right to ask for a review and to complain.
- Uninsured Motorist Damage, About This Policy, and Third Party, Fire and Theft: the overview table and the cover now say that this extension pays for damage to the Insured vehicle at the lower cover levels. "Uninsured" is explained.
- Supplementary Bodily Injury: the extension pays only the part of a liability that a compulsory third party or statutory scheme does not cover. Defence costs are stated and are part of the sub-limit. A tag asks the Wording Partner to confirm how each State and Territory scheme interacts with it.
- Terrorism: the statement that motor vehicle insurance is not an eligible insurance contract under the Terrorism Insurance Act is deleted and replaced with a tag for the Wording Partner.
- What Is Not Covered for Your Legal Liability: a pollution and contamination exclusion is added, with an exception for a sudden, identifiable and unintended event. Liability from Dangerous goods stays within the Dangerous goods sub-limit.
- E02 Refrigerated Vehicles: now says which exclusions it displaces (breakdown, and loss of use and consequential loss) so that the spoilage cover works as its title promises.
- E16 Tow Trucks and Recovery Vehicles: now names the two exclusions it displaces for the vehicle being towed, and says every other exclusion continues to apply.
- Who This Policy Is For: restated with the library's wording on retail clients, a Product Disclosure Statement and a Target Market Determination. The statement that the Policy is "not a consumer insurance contract" is replaced by a statement of intended business purpose and a tag asking the Wording Partner whether a sole trader's policy is a consumer contract. The Duty of Disclosure clause says which duty applies if it is.
- Cancellation: you may cancel at any time and we refund the unexpired premium pro rata, less non-refundable government charges, with no refund for a vehicle after we pay a Total loss. The deduction for amounts we have "paid or reserved for a claim" is removed. Our notice to cancel is at least 30 days.
- Premium: before we refuse a claim or cancel for an unpaid instalment, we give you written notice that the instalment is overdue.
- Tell Us About Changes and Default Schedule Values: a changed premium or changed terms apply only after written notice, from the date stated in it, and not earlier than the notice period (default 14 days) after we send it. You may cancel if you do not accept them. A tag asks the Wording Partner to confirm the mechanism. Default Schedule Values also records that an issuer must check a change to a default against the departures it gives a person who holds a prescribed contract.
- Additional Benefits: the trailer benefit no longer depends on the trailer not being insured separately. Other Insurance applies.
